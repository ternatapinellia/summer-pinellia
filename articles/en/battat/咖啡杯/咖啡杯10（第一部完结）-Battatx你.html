Coffee Cup 10 (End of Part One) - Battatx You

Battatx you, OOC counts me as me

Alright, I want to write about the Xianghe San...... Alright, the first part is finished!! Yay (what does that mean)

——

You flusteredly took out the emergency umbrella from your bag, but luckily you brought one just in case. After all, there happened to be no buildings nearby to block the rain, and naturally, there were no eaves or anything like that. You pulled Battat, who was a bit dazed from the sudden rain, and huddled under the umbrella, your tone a bit anxious.

“…… I don't know why it suddenly started raining, and it's probably going to get heavier. We should hurry up and leave!" "And since this umbrella is originally meant for one person, standing together now would actually feel a bit crowded, so you naturally put your arm around Battat's shoulder, and now you two are pressed tightly together.

Battat was actually thinking about what to do. If it really didn't work, he could use a jacket to cover himself, and then you two would run together to the nearby street, or the nearest building to take shelter from the rain. Of course, both of them had to wear their jackets, but he was caught off guard by the fact that you actually brought an umbrella. His previously wandering heart suddenly calmed down, but at the same time, he felt a bit embarrassed.

…… So close! And your hand was still holding his shoulder. He could feel the warmth in your palm and your body leaning in close, as if so close. He could hear your warm breathing, which gradually grew rapid, because the rain was getting heavier and your steps quickened. Battat could even feel your breath gently brushing his face; he knew all of this was forced to do now...... But his face still couldn't help but burn hot.

And having you grab his shoulder or something like that made him feel a bit embarrassed. He knew it was just to get closer, otherwise the umbrella wouldn't hold two people. Even now, only the part tightly pressed together was still warm and dry. The part near the edge of the umbrella was damp from the rain, and the rain came in suddenly and heavy, not letting up quickly. The wind picked up, soaking his clothes roughly.

So he just listens nervously to the gradually accelerating heartbeat—he even worries if you can hear it, but at this moment, battat doesn't care about that; he simply reaches out and puts his arm around your shoulder as well.

You take a deep breath. Maybe your current state is just a bit of a romantic awkwardness, or maybe being so close fulfills a certain inner hope. In any case, despite the heavy rain and the pattering on the umbrella surface, your mood is actually quite good, even a bit excited. Maybe it's okay if this kind of time lasts a little longer, of course, as long as the rain isn't so heavy. You still don't want to endure your wet clothes sticking to you.

So when Battat placed his hand on it, you actually zoned out for a second—ah, you never expected you two to be holding that position. But maybe it's not so bad? At least the warmth of relying on each other brings you joy. At least it's like two souls coming close, and you hope Battat won't hear your heartbeat, because it's been trembling for a long time and you can't make out the exact frequency.

Quickening your pace, holding the small umbrella to shield yourself from the downpour, you huddled together, almost running away. Finally, you spotted a street not far away. We ran to the nearest building and found a seat to sit in. The shop was still warm, and at least the shivering from the rain suddenly eased—after all, it was already early autumn, not particularly cold, but once it rained, you had to change clothes, or else you'd just shiver.

You put down the umbrella, shivered, rubbed your hands, and the brightness inside made the rain and darkness outside seem even smaller, as if the earlier storm had been nothing but a nightmare. But your wet clothes remind you that none of this is a dream.

Luckily, only part of it was wet, and even so, you still complained: "...... So annoying, suddenly it rains or something. ”

"At least for now, we can sit down for now. Maybe we'll be better when the rain stops...... Or maybe we can take a taxi home? " Battat suggested.

You nodded in agreement: "That's all we can do. By the way, this is a ramen shop! Have a bowl of ramen to warm up!" ”

As you spoke, you shouted, "Boss, give me a bowl of spicy noodles!" From afar, you heard a "Okay!" Then you looked back at Battat.

"What do you want to eat? Maybe pork bone ramen...... This soup tastes a bit better. Anyway, it's just right to warm up with some soup." "Although the rain just soaked my mood, it doesn't stop you from feeling happy again. After all, just looking at Battat's face makes you feel happy, to the point that you hum a tune in your heart."

"Ah, then let's go with tonkotsu ramen. But we agreed that tonight's dinner would be my treat." "Battat didn't care what exactly to eat; as long as you feel happy, that's all that matters." He just hurriedly double-checked the treat. And you nodded indifferently.

"Yes, I know! Don't worry, this time I won't rush to pay. I'm giving you a chance to show off." "After saying that, you winked slightly.

Battat was a bit helpless, muttering quietly, "What's the show? Two bowls of ramen won't cost much......" He had originally thought about treating you to a French meal too, but the heavy rain dampened his hopes. For now, you can only stay in this small ramen shop.

However, the small ramen shop was warm enough—at least that's how Battat felt—so he thought it was all bearable. No, rather than enduring it, it was more like enjoying it...... Maybe the time we spent squeezing under the umbrella was just too unforgettable, though it was only for a short while.

So you raised your voice and reminded, "Boss, one more order of tonkotsu ramen!" ”

Before long, two servings of ramen were served, steaming clouds drifting around, temporarily blocking your view from overlapping together, so you could only clearly see the food in front of you.

You broke open your chopsticks and took a bite of the extra spicy ramen, then slapped your cheek and started sticking out your tongue: "Wow, so spicy!" ”

Battat hesitated for a moment, but still righteously pushed his tonkotsu ramen out: "Or, shall we swap the ramen? I'll eat the extra spicy one." Although he said this while feeling uncertain himself, knowing he couldn't handle spicy food and probably wouldn't get a few bites even if he got it,

You still stuck out your tongue, just shaking your head: "No. Aren't you totally uncomfortable with spicy food? I'm eating extra spicy ramen just to relieve the irritability of the past few days—anyway, just having a few drinks and alcohol will help you recover." ”

Just then, the owner brought over orange juice, cold and cold. You took several sips of food and set it down, then took a deep breath: "Phew, you're alive." Then you leaned back and leaned back in the chair.

Battat looked at your flushed face and hesitated: "Or should we order another order?" Can you really finish it? ”

You waved your hand: "Of course I can finish it!" Eating extra spicy ramen is all about challenge—it's just a bit spicy, and it's not enough to kill anyone! And the spiciness really warms up your body, right? Look, my face is warm right now. If you don't believe me, you can even touch it. "As you spoke, you pointed to your own face.

Battat cleared his throat twice: "Well, that's not necessary...... You can tell just by looking." At the same time, he complained quietly in his heart, How can you be so defenseless? Is your face something you can just touch like that? He had to pat his head before...... He really didn't know what kind of feelings you had when you said things like that.

But the good times didn't last. Battat had only taken two bites of ramen before he saw you coughing from the spiciness, and even after several sips of orange juice, it didn't work.

He started worrying again, stood up and came over to your side, gently patting your back: "...... Otherwise, maybe you shouldn't eat extra spicy ramen! What if eating it will only make you feel worse! ”

Battat genuinely worried about this matter.

You calmed down a bit before waving your hand: "It's okay, it's okay, it's okay. This is something that happens all the time when you eat spicy food...... I'll be fine in a moment!" You said this so confidently, and Battat could only glance at you helplessly and sit back down across from you.

He had already finished that bowl of tonkotsu ramen, drunk part of the soup, and it tasted really good. At this moment, he could only prop his face up with his hands and watch you still struggle with that extra spicy ramen. You've only eaten half of it now, your face flushed red, and some physiological tears welling up in your eyes—probably from the spiciness. But you're trying hard not to let those tears fall into the bowl. If they contaminate the ramen underneath, that would be bad!

He rests his chin on his hand, staring at your movements, while you show no sign of embarrassment, still battling with the spicy ramen. Battat looks at you, not knowing what you're thinking, or maybe not thinking at all—he's just lost in thought, staring at you like that, or maybe he's just daydreaming. At this moment, he can only feel his own heartbeat steadily, or rather, he can hear the sound of his heart beating for you.

Finally, after staring at you blankly for a while, you finished off the extra spicy ramen and leaned back happily, planning to rest for a while. After all, the heavy rain outside the window was still ongoing, and it looked like it would last a while before it would stop. The warmth and brightness inside the shop seemed to be from two different worlds compared to the damp, icy cold outside.

"Looks like I'd better sit a bit longer before I leave. My clothes are almost dry by now, and I don't want to get soaked again when I go out now......" you muttered, and Battat nodded in agreement.

Then, his heart raced. After hesitating for a moment, he asked nervously, "Then...... Can we just chat casually? I have some questions I want to ask you too. ”

You looked at battat with a hint of surprise, then nodded cheerfully: "Of course you can! I was thinking we might as well chat a bit, but before we could finish, a sudden downpour interrupted us...... So if you want to say something, just say it. ”

Battat took a deep breath, his hands clasped together on the table. He hesitated, secretly fiddling with his palm, lowering his head under your curious gaze. In the silence surrounding each other, he waited for quite a while before finally speaking: "Then...... Actually, I wanted to ask you, what do you do when a coffee shop closes? ”

You rubbed your chin to recall for a moment, then nodded firmly: "Ah, you can read, right? Usually, after the coffee shop closes, I go home. I always put out a disc with music playing, brew myself a cup of coffee, and sit on the sofa listening to music while reading. Oh, and I still have a gramophone at home now. I usually use that one to play music!" As you spoke, you gestured with your hand again, excitedly introducing the story.

"Anyway, and since my house is pretty big, sometimes when I get in the mood, I even dance along with the music on the gramophone...... But I don't look very good at dancing. I think I'm probably the kind of person who steps hard when dancing with others." You scratched your head awkwardly and stuck out your tongue.

Battat nodded thoughtfully: "Wow...... What a charming life." Saying this, his mood inexplicably sank, because he definitely couldn't live like this himself—every day was just too exhausting. At this moment, he seriously thought about whether their personal habits might not be quite right......

But before he could think further, you continued: "It's not exactly that stylish, I just have nothing to do. After waking up in the morning, I make myself a cup of coffee so I can happily go to work. And the house is too big...... I really don't like tidying up, so I just leave things around casually and I can see them. Just keeping the room tidy and tidy is exhausting. ”

“…… But I do prefer to use my free time to do something else and don't want to tidy things up. Mainly, being the only one busy is dull and boring, and no one else is watching. That doesn't feel accomplished, so it's better to be a bit more artistic in arranging things. At least I feel more at ease while watching. ”

Battat hesitated for a moment, then asked, "...... For example? ”

"For example, stacking a stack of books—like a triangle, stacked like a tower—I can even play a game of pulling out books and see when they'll collapse!" But the weight of books is different. Maybe playing cards would be better...... You excitedly gestured with your hands.

The conversation lasted for a while, the rain outside the window eased, and you said goodbye to Battat reluctantly, then you both headed off in different directions. Of course, you were planning to return home, and it was almost evening.

You walk toward home with new understanding and affection.
