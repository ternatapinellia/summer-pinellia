Coffee cup 2-Battatx you

Battatx you, OOC counts as mine

Some things are just me making things up, and I don't know if they're true. Anyway, this is a refreshing chronological account (I guess). There's no plot, maybe it's just a romantic exchange (hmm).

——

You absentmindedly wiped the cup in your hand, a bit distracted. Well, ever since Battat left, no other customers have come to this café, so—since he still said he'd come again, I was actually a bit curious about when he'd finally arrive.

Glancing at the cup with your name on it, now belonging to battat, you feel a bit troubled—this means you need to replace it—alright, you take a white coffee cup out of the cabinet, but it's completely empty and spotless, so you don't plan to write anything on it for now.

You just nod in satisfaction, pour yourself a macchiato, and add a beautiful latte art. Since you don't know which shape is best, you just draw a heart on it. The characteristic of this coffee is that it's relatively sweeter, with less bitterness and more milky smoothness and sweetness. Of course, the whipped milk foam itself is one of the sources of sweetness.

Just as you were about to take a sip of the steaming macchiato, the sound of wind chimes rang out again. Following the sound, you looked toward the door and saw Battat walk in. He had indeed visited the shop as promised. Today, he hadn't taken anything, and his spirit was a bit better than yesterday. He wasn't glowing, but at least he wasn't so haggard.

You put down the coffee you'd just taken a sip of, stood up straight, leaned against the counter, and looked at battat: "You really came. So, what do you think? The proposals I gave you yesterday were pretty good, right?" As you spoke, you lifted your head smugly.

Battat hesitated for a moment, then nodded: "Indeed, when I submitted it, it was the first time I'd seen the boss so friendly......" After all, this proposal was submitted under the identity of Pippins, not Mike. As for why? Of course, everyone had to submit a proposal. As for Mike? Haha, he didn't have to do that job.

Thinking of this, Battat was about to grit his teeth again, but you glanced at him. Of course, you were beaming with joy, a bit delighted: "See, I told you I wouldn't give you any bad advice!" Alright—since you're here, let's see what we have for today. It's on me! "You wave your hand generously, showing your generosity.

Battat was momentarily stunned, then quickly waved his hand: "Ah, no need, it's just a cup of coffee. I'll just pay for it myself—"

"It's okay, as long as I'm happy, so what do you want to drink?" "I'll give you a free meal today." You raised an eyebrow and picked up a packet of coffee beans placed nearby.

"Uh...... Then, macchiato? "Battat glanced twice, probably seeing the coffee you hadn't touched yet, and hesitantly placed the order.

"Hmm, then what about latte art? What do you need?" You softly hummed an off-key tune, then thought about bringing out some darkly roasted coffee beans this time. These beans had a rich taste, with a hint of chocolate or toasted bread, no acidity, and probably just right.

“…… Uh, heart-shaped? "He looked around nervously, clearly not having any good choices, so he could only grumble and follow your choice. After all, Battat can see the latte art shape on your coffee cup.

You nodded, "Alright!" Then find a seat, I'll bring it to you later. As you spoke, the coffee machine started to sound. You threw the coffee beans in, waiting for them to grind into powder and hand it to you. You hummed a tune, prepared hot water, put the coffee grounds into a cup, then poured in milk and hot water to brew. After a while, the coffee gradually turned light brown, and finally, you added a heart-shaped cream latte art on top.

You thought for a moment, then took out a tray and put on the coffee you had just taken a sip of and the coffee you had just made for him, along with a plate of macarons just taken from the fridge.

Then you carried the tray toward battat, and you could immediately see him sitting at the same spot as yesterday, staring out the window in a daze, his fingers seemingly unconsciously tracing something on the table.

You gently placed the tray in front of him, finally bringing Battat back to attention. He hesitated for a moment as he looked at the two cups of coffee on the tray: "What are you...... want to drink with me?" He raised his voice, probably a bit incredulous, or perhaps a bit embarrassed.

You nodded matter-of-factly: "Yeah, anyway, no one else is coming. I just want to find a seat for a while. Sitting here is just fine, and I can even watch you." Otherwise, just staring at the door out of boredom would be just too boring.

He seemed to have stammered for a moment, probably not expecting you to say that. Finally, he cleared his throat and said, "Ah, alright, is it okay if you sit here?" He wasn't sure when he said the last sentence, since it probably wasn't something he could decide.

"Hmm...... This shop is mine, so I can sit wherever I want. "You shrugged, placed the cup of coffee in front of him, the macaron on the table, your own coffee across from you, then returned the tray to the counter before coming back to sit down.

You nudged the plate of macarons toward the battat: "Dessert included—give it a try?" You blinked, tilted your head to look at him, propping up your cheek.

Battat picked up a macaron and put it in his mouth, then his face scrunched up—although the dice's face was a bit amusing, probably because he thought the expression wasn't very good, he quickly switched to a count emoji and put it on.

"This is way too sweet, isn't it?!" Although he disliked the sweetness, he still obediently ate the macaron. However, when he switched back to his expression, his expression wasn't very pleasant, the corners of his mouth curled upward, looking like he didn't dare to try it again.

You smile and say, "Well, so it probably tastes better with coffee, right?" If you eat it plain, it definitely tastes sweet. "But you didn't tell Battat about this earlier, after all...... Getting overwhelmed by sweetness and stuff is kind of amusing to look at his expression.

He hesitated for a moment, but finally chose to believe your words, so he picked up his coffee and took a sip. To be honest, today's coffee did taste a bit sweeter. Maybe it was because of the latte art on the top floor, but the whole cup had a soft, sweet sensation that actually washed away the coffee's original bitterness.

But even so, a bitter taste still lingered. Seeing your expectant gaze, he couldn't resist and still picked up a macaron and popped it into his mouth—seriously! You had just promised in your heart never to touch this again? But seeing you look at him so pitifully, Battat couldn't help but try again.

…… But the taste is still acceptable? At least this time it's not as sweet. The lingering bitterness in the mouth washes away some of the macaron's sweetness, while the excess sweetness takes away the original coffee-like bitterness. In fact, starting with a bitter bite and then tasting the sweet one is a good choice—at least now Battat doesn't mind this flavor.

But he put down his coffee cup for a while. After all, he wasn't that hungry and didn't really want to eat, so he just looked at you, suddenly feeling a bit curious about you—after all, you seem carefree, as if nothing matters at all. It's not like him, but while this kind of idleness is enviable, over time it just gets boring, right? Otherwise, why would you show such an excited expression right in front of him?

He thought for a moment, then couldn't help but ask with a question: "So, there are clearly no other customers, right? Why are you still opening a café here?" ”

You blink and sit up straight: "Is this a Q&A session?" Then let's ask one question after another—I'll answer you first! Well, honestly, I don't care if anyone comes. After all, I inherit the family business I left behind, but I also want to open a café like my parents. It's small, doesn't need many people, just purely handmade coffee, accommodating some regular customers, and as long as it can earn praise for the skills, that's enough. Of course—I also hope everyone can relax here. ”

"But my cooking skills aren't that great, and this café just opened. It's normal that no one comes—after all, there are already several famous cafés now." You shrugged as you spoke.

Battat nodded thoughtfully, then asked, "So...... Aren't you bored? ”

And you just smiled and waved your fingers: "That's the next question. Let me ask you first—well, I'm curious, what kind of work are you actually doing? Why do you look so haggard?" ”

"It's nothing really, I'm just working on the TV world side... it's just that the workload is a bit heavy. Anyway, I'm just a pippins..." battat smiled awkwardly, and under your gaze, his voice suddenly lowered, since he felt a bit embarrassed about this topic.

"So what if it's pippins? Pippins should have dice rights too. Whether it's using dice or anything else, everyone should follow the rules and regulations. If it's labor, there should be relevant regulations too, right...... You purse your lips, a bit dissatisfied. Actually, you've heard about the owner there before, but it doesn't really concern you, so of course you didn't care. It's just that during a meeting with the company's shareholders, there was discussion about whether to cooperate with the other party. However, one shareholder objected, saying the other party was already on the decline and that cooperating again at this point wasn't worth it.

But after that, you didn't listen. Forgive me, even though you inherited the company, you have absolutely no interest in running it.

"Really? But the boss probably thinks the pippins are just gambling and not doing their jobs...... Anyway, it doesn't really matter, anyway, they don't notice me. "Battat scratched his head in frustration, his clothes getting a little wrinkled."

You blinked: "Hmm, alright...... Let me answer your question first. Actually, it's not boring at all. Even though there are no customers, I'm happy to have some free time. I just need to sit there reading and don't have to socialize with anyone. It's just too convenient! But having guests come is a good thing, at least I can chat with you here." You blinked as you spoke, showing a playful expression.

"Ah, actually, it doesn't really matter. I can just leave me here alone......" he stammered again.

But you just shook your head in disapproval: "How can that be! I pride myself on making sure every guest feels relaxed and comfortable here, so of course I want you to experience it too!" As you spoke, you rolled up your sleeves, looking like you were following through on your word, feeling like you were burning with energy.

"Alright, maybe you just want to find someone to chat with?" Battat sighed and decided not to pursue the previous topic further.

"Hmm, I'll answer that later. Let me ask you first. I want to know why, since you're so tired, you haven't switched jobs yet. You seem to be working very hard, so other places shouldn't be rejecting you, right?" ”

As you spoke, you took a sip of macchiato, put a piece of macaron into your mouth, chewed on it, and asked unclearly.

"Honestly, I've tried before, but other places wouldn't hire me once they saw I was a pippins, they didn't even bother to look further—" At this point, battat seemed a bit indignant. He had been holding a coffee cup, planning to take another sip, but now he angrily put the cup down on the table, making a loud clinking sound, which even spilled some coffee foam onto the table.

You have to admit, you were startled by his actions, but you quickly recovered. You just picked up a macaron and held it to his lips, your eyes slightly curved: "Alright, don't be mad. Have something sweet, okay?" If other places don't hire you, it's their loss—if you want, you can apply at my company. I'll definitely read your resume carefully! ”

Battat hesitated for a moment, then took a sip of coffee, waiting for the bitterness to wash over his mouth before taking the macaron and putting it in his mouth. Carefully and shyly, he thanked him: "Thank you." ”

"It's fine. But well, I'm not the type to chat with people, and I'm not the type to chat just because someone comes. Most of the time, it's better to be quiet. It's just ...... I found it interesting to chat with you, so I wanted to talk with you." You thought for a moment, touched your chin, and said solemnly.

"Maybe I'm just a bit curious about you. After all, it's not easy to find this café so precisely—so, would you like to apply for a job at my company?"

Battat hesitated for a moment, then slowly shook his head, his voice a bit hoarse: "In that case, probably not." ”

"Hey—why!" You dragged out your words, a bit incredulous.
