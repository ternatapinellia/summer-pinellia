Coffee Cup 22 (Extra 2) - Battatx You

That day, he went to work as usual and went home after work. Even though he hadn't done much, Battat still felt exhausted. Maybe he sped up to finish those tasks so he wouldn't have to work overtime. It turned out he could leave work early and received envious, jealous looks from his colleagues, but...... He was exhausted too, feeling like he'd fall asleep on the floor in the next second, dragging his feet out in a daze. As for how his colleagues would see him, he didn't really care; he just couldn't wait to get home and see you, nothing more.

Just thinking of your smile made his tired steps, which had slowed down a bit, suddenly quicken at this moment. He was truly impatient. He wanted to go back and see you, to see your every smile, to hear your voice. Well, for Battat, this was just a brief break after a tiring day.

He pushed open the door, and you were still sitting on the sofa reading. Hearing the door open, you were startled and looked up at battat. After all, he did leave work early sometimes, but never as early as this day. Could it be because of working overtime recently? You couldn't help but start speculating, but you put down your book, quickly got up, walked over, took his briefcase, and set it aside, tilting your head to look at him: "Welcome home." ”

Battat finally felt a sense of relief when he saw you. He exhaled and smiled, nodding: "Mm, I'm back." ”

But he looked like he was just overworked...... After thinking it over, you took his hand and sat down on the sofa, complaining, "Oh no, you came back way too early today...... I haven't even prepared dinner yet, or maybe you can just skip the plate and eat takeout directly." You couldn't help but laugh again. You know, recently you'd deliberately prepared dinner just when Battat was about to go home. After all, you didn't want to be caught just ordering takeout. It wasn't awkward, but it still felt a bit embarrassing.

"But since it's rare to be back early today, let's sit on the sofa for a while before going for dinner...... You look exhausted from the whole day, so take a break. I didn't have many customers today, so I closed early and went home. Speaking of which, the coffee shop has had a few more regulars recently, which is great. Looks like more and more customers will get to know my coffee shop......" you muttered to yourself, pulling Battat to sit on the sofa and couldn't help but start sharing your day's trivial matters. In short, you can say anything—from simply clicking into a funny page full of ads when your phone malfunctions, or developing a new coffee product to give Battat a try, to saying you heard your fiancé's company recently went bankrupt and your fiancé was sent to a mental hospital. It feels like he might not be able to get out for a long time, maybe forever.

Battat just quietly gazes at your face. Honestly, he's exhausted—so tired that he doesn't want to speak at all, and so tired that he doesn't want to hear others' voices. But when he sees your mouth opening and closing, hearing your clear voice, he doesn't want you to stop. His soul only feels calm in that moment, or maybe...... Even if it's just trivial matters that probably no one wants to know, he wants to know and listen to you. Of course, he won't tire of you.

He just stared at your face, then suddenly said, "I like you." "Battat didn't understand this either; it was just an impulse in his heart, just a sudden suddenness. He wanted to tell you everything he was feeling, but all those surging emotions eventually boiled down to just four simple words—just four words that could sum up all his feelings."

So Battat just said, I like you.

You were stunned for a moment, and halfway through your sentence, you instinctively froze. You scratched your face, unable to hide the flushed blush spreading across your face. Your eyes darted around nervously, not knowing where to look. In short, you didn't dare look at Battat: "Why are you suddenly saying things like that...... Ah, I like you too! I really like you! ”

You said that last sentence out loud, even though you can't figure out why you did it—probably just suddenly feeling shy.

"I like you too, I like you the most." Battat quickly added, probably feeling that wasn't enough, and even added a 'most' comment. After saying this, he lowered his head and didn't dare look at you anymore, even feeling embarrassed for himself. Oh my god, why did he blurt out that sentence just now? Has it turned into some kind of mutual confession now? It's not that he doesn't like hearing that, he just thinks...... He just thought, oh my god, do you really need to do this?

You blinked, lowered your head, and didn't dare look at his face anymore. Maybe it was just to hide the blush on your face, or maybe you were afraid that if you saw Battat again, you wouldn't be able to say a word. You don't even know why you're doing this event now.

Anyway, you take a deep breath and add, "I only like you, you the most." Mm...... You could say I only love you, but that sounds a bit strange, as if I once loved someone else......" At this point, you couldn't help but laugh.

You don't forget to add, "Ah, anyway, I've never liked anyone else. I just gave my love to you." You didn't feel guilty about saying this, since you really did do it.

Battat's eyes darted away, but in the end, he slowly raised his head to look at you, meeting your sparkling, smiling eyes. Though his cheeks were shy, you couldn't help but feel a light sense of joy, as if your whole soul was delighted, and his heart was racing at that moment. In short, you couldn't wait to hear Battat say more...... Or perhaps, you just couldn't wait to do something else?

You don't know either, but at least for now, you still wish you could hear something more, even though before you hear it, your ears are burning hot and red.

Battat swallowed hard, but finally looked away, not daring to meet your gaze. He really was about to hesitate, not knowing what he was saying: "Mm...... I only love you. I've devoted my whole life to you. I will always love you......

Hearing this, you couldn't help but pat your face. Well, the heat on your cheeks just wouldn't go down...... Your heart was pounding and wouldn't stop, so you could only look at Battat with a hint of complaint, even though after that was filled with joy, and you thought Battat probably couldn't see your eyes.

So in the end, you just cleared your throat: "Alright. So I love you." After saying that, you probably couldn't hold back, or maybe you just didn't want to hear what he said next. You just felt this was enough, so you should just shut his mouth. So after saying that, you kissed him.

Just then, Battat looked up too. He was too shy and didn't want to hear any more. After all, his heart was beating too fast, and he couldn't control it. He was afraid it would break from beating so fast, so he thought, maybe a kiss could shut your mouth, so he looked up too.

So your lips met, and you widened your eyes slightly as you looked at Battat's tightly shut eyes and his hand holding your head nervously and awkwardly, and in the end, you closed your eyes.

So in that moment, you were simply immersed in the soft yet somewhat cool sensation, like a quiet and desolate lake. But when you reached deep into the water, you could feel an inner raging whirlwind. At least, that's what you thought before being kissed until you were dizzy. You were even a bit surprised, not knowing how Battat suddenly learned so much......

But there are times when you let go, so after this kiss, both Battat and you blushed and turned their heads away, not daring to look at each other for a moment. You just hurriedly spoke, pretending to be busy, and stood up: "Ah...... Ah, the takeout should be arriving. I'll go get ready first! ”

"Alright, thank you for your hard work." Battat replied stiffly. Out of the corner of his eye, he watched you walk away, then pressed his hands to his face and quietly buried his head in his knees. It would probably take him a while to fully wake up.

For dinner, you poured milk into two coffee cups as usual. This time, you didn't bother to plate it and simply served it in a takeout box, then you invited Battat over to eat. He finally calmed down and finished the dinner face to face with you, without mentioning what had just happened.

As for the taste of dinner, Battat didn't really care about that. After all, if you were willing to prepare dinner for him, it was already good enough, maybe even pleasantly surprised. In short, having takeout was better than eating fast food every day.

And he probably knows that preparing a lavish dinner is exhausting for you, or rather, tiring for everyone, and Battat doesn't want you to be so exhausted. If you have extra time, it's better to do more of your own things, even though you probably don't mind that.

After all, you'll definitely say that what battat does is exactly what you want to do...... Well, then you mutter to yourself that this sentence is probably not quite right, and tell battats not to cause any ambiguity. Of course, he knows exactly what you mean, and precisely because he knows, he never brings it up.

But after dinner, Battat sat back down on the sofa, as if he had exhausted all his strength, looking like he didn't want to do anything. You looked at him worriedly and said, "If you really feel tired, Battat, should you go back to sleep first?" ”

And he just waves his hand, leans back on the sofa, and tells you not to need it.

You hesitate for a moment, then sit on the sofa, reading the book you hadn't finished before, while Battat sits right next to you, his head pressed against yours, probably reading as well.

But after an unknown amount of time, you felt a heaviness on your shoulder. When you turned your head, you realized it was Battat who had somehow fallen asleep leaning on your shoulder.

You hesitated for a moment. Fortunately, the weather wasn't too cold, and the house was warm and windless, so you wouldn't catch a chill if you fell asleep here. But you gently laid him down, leaning him on your lap, letting him continue sleeping. Battat was clearly very tired and hadn't woken up, while you just gently lowered your head, kissed his forehead, and went back to reading your book.

But after reading for about an hour, you yawned, feeling a bit sleepy, and your mind was a bit unsettled while reading. Maybe putting down the book and resting for a while was the best choice. Then you lowered your head hesitantly to glance at the still-sleeping battat, then at the clock hanging on the wall. It was still before nine o'clock at night!

So you put down your book with peace of mind, reminding yourself to take a nap for a while, then lean back on the sofa and close your eyes, and soon fall asleep too.

When Battat woke up rubbing his eyes, it took him two or three seconds to realize he was resting on your lap. Instinctively, his face flushed, and he hurriedly got up, only to find you were already asleep. He glanced at the clock hanging on the wall—it was about a little past ten, not very early. Battat couldn't just let you keep sleeping here.

He hesitated for a moment, but finally bent down slightly, one arm wrapped around your leg and the other around your neck, carefully lifting you up, hoping not to disturb your sleep. Of course, you slept well, showing no sign of waking up. Battat took a deep breath, his steps light and light, trying not to make a sound, then walked to your bedroom and laid you on the bed.

Around ten o'clock, it was almost time to sleep, so after putting you on the bed, he gently crawled down beside you and lay down.

But you were a bit awake when you were put on the bed, or maybe you were just about to fall asleep again. You glanced at battat lying beside you, rolled over in a daze, hugged him, and muttered, "I love you, battat. Good night." ”

He's now with his back to you, so you can't see his sudden blushing face; you can only hear his stammering voice: "I love you too...... Good night. ”

You closed your eyes again with peace of mind, soon fell back asleep, and had a pleasant dream.

In your dream, you wander in that clear lake, cool and moist, yet not damp—just refreshing. Then, holding the crescent moon reflected in the lake, embracing the stars and raindrops flowing down, you fall into deep darkness once again.

The moonlight is truly beautiful.
