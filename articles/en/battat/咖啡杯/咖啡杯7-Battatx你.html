He coughed dryly twice, not knowing where to put his hands, his gaze wandering, so he could only scratch his head haphazardly. After all, things get busy when things are inexplicable, and there are always a bunch of little tricks and tricks.

You just sat down with a hint of confusion and looked at him, scooped a piece of tiramisu with your newly picked spoon, and stammered, "What's wrong with you?" ”

Battat cleared his throat twice more: "Ahem, it's nothing, I just suddenly remembered I still have some things to deal with, so I'll leave first." "Of course, he's definitely not running away! Well, maybe he really does need some time to think things through...... Now you're still sitting next to him, and he honestly doesn't know what to think. After all, his mind is full of you right now, and he can't think of anything else.

After saying this, he stood up holding the documents, and you nodded blankly: "Oh, then that's settled. See you next time!" ”

Battat seemed to nod behind that stack of documents, then pushed open the café door with his arm and hurried off. You even peeked at his back and couldn't help but marvel—wow, his footsteps are so steady, not a single document fell off. Of course, maybe it's because Battat is quite familiar with this kind of thing.

Fortunately, Tenna hadn't checked the documents and couldn't have noticed anything unusual. After arguing his case, Battat still got his salary. He counted the points he had and exchanged them for Dark Coins. Thinking he could finally treat him back, Battat visited the café again. As he approached, his mood soared, and he felt a bit nervous.

But when he reached the entrance, his heart sank again, because the coffee shop was pitch dark and there was a 'close' sign hanging there, clearly a closed place. He had deliberately chosen a time to come at the same time as his usual visits, so logically, he should be open. But today might just be your day off, since running a café is tough. Battat comforted himself like this and then left the entrance, though as he left, he couldn't help but instinctively glance back a couple more times.

However, on the second and third days, when he arrived at the coffee shop entrance, he still saw it pitch black and the door tightly locked. Battat couldn't help but feel anxious—although he thought he wouldn't let him get into trouble, he suddenly realized that apart from the coffee shop, he had no contact with you. You two were like strangers, only knowing each other's names and appearances, knowing nothing else, no contact information, let alone a deeper connection.

…… If he'd known, he would have asked for your contact info when they parted last time. Battat felt a bit regretful. Now, he could only wander off and keep visiting the café, only to be turned away.

Of course, this kind of absent-minded state didn't show while working. He was still seriously completing the work belonging to mike, but it was still noticed by pluey and jongler. However, it seemed battat didn't want to say anything; they just shook their heads, pretending not to notice, while actively helping him share some of the workload.

…… Of course, whether it was done well was another matter.

On the anxious fifth day, Battat finally saw the café open. He breathed a sigh of relief, pushed open the glass door, and walked in, nervously gathering his emotions and thinking about how to start the conversation.

When you first heard the sound of wind chimes, your body involuntarily trembled. Only when you saw Battat coming in did you breathe a sigh of relief, a smile forming on your face as you asked, "Ah, it's Battat. What would you like to drink today?" ”

Battat walked up to the counter, looking at your seemingly flawless but actually forced smile. After hesitating for a moment, he gritted his teeth and asked, "I...... You haven't seen the coffee shop open these past few days. Has something happened? Ah, I think I can ask. After all, since you're willing to listen to my troubles, I should be listening to your worries too." Battat said, patting his chest and putting on a serious and earnest look.

“…… After all, we're friends, aren't we? "When he said this, his tone rose slightly, with a hint of confusion and a hint of pleading.

You blinked at Battat, who suddenly took a serious pose, and put away the forced smile on your face: "Alright...... I'll tell you after I finish making coffee. I think my thoughts are still a bit chaotic right now. ”

Battat nodded, even feeling overjoyed and at a loss, since he didn't know he'd convinced you so easily. You have to understand, he probably wouldn't have shared his troubles before. Pretending to be Mike or something like that was better buried deep in his heart. But since you figured it out, he had no reason to hide it any longer, so he couldn't help but reveal more in your sincere eyes.

…… So, maybe you're the same—because of your seriousness and enthusiasm, you're willing to talk about locking your troubles. If no one asks, you'll pretend nothing happened and start thinking of solutions yourself, even if it's difficult. But once someone asks, you can't help but want to share your worries and confusion.

Actually, most customers who never come to me don't ask; they simply close the shop out of necessity, and only Battat asks.

You still made two caramel macchiatos and brought them over, because you wanted a sweet sip of coffee—that was just your personal desire. Of course, you also paid attention to the coffee cup with your name on it for Battat, and the cup with Battat's name on it was for you. This time, you wouldn't get confused, thinking excitedly. Besides that, you also brought a plate of macarons.

Battat is still sitting by the window, while you sit opposite him, put down your plate, and exhale.

"Alright...... Anyway, let's have a sip of coffee first. Let's both feel a bit better." You've finally combed yourself together a bit, and once again put on a faint smile on your face. It's just your habit. After all, your elders always teach you that smiling makes people feel better, and of course, that also means you're in a pretty good mood yourself.

But Battat just frowned and looked at you. To be honest, he always felt something was off about your smile, and couldn't help but say, "...... If you don't want to laugh, then you better not laugh." ”

Your smile immediately faded, and you just lowered your head, feeling helpless, holding the coffee cup with both hands, rubbing it. After a moment, you hesitantly asked, "So, are you really willing to listen to me?" ”

Battat nodded firmly: "Yes, so are you willing to tell me exactly why?" ”

You take a deep breath and then lift your head, probably to prevent tears from suddenly falling into the coffee cup. But you only keep your strength and won't do that.

"Well, actually, it wasn't that bad at first. I just planned to close the shop for a whole morning. After all, I told you before that the company executives suddenly called me over, saying it would be better if I kept the engagement we had already made. I really can't see what he meant, but I didn't want to fulfill that engagement, so I still proposed annulling it. After all, that engagement was a verbal agreement made while my father was alive, so of course it could be annulled, so the company executive did agree. But that fiancé didn't agree and insisted on continuing the engagement......" you rambled on.

“…… I guess it's because his family is on the verge of bankruptcy lately. After all, I heard their business line is on the verge of crisis. They've probably lost a lot of money on investment, so they can't wait to hold this wedding banquet. After all, if the engagement is finalized, they could get a huge sum of money. ”

Battat listened while frowning, his brows never lowered. In fact, his mood was fluctuating too—in short, it was unsettled—actually, when he first heard you had a marriage proposal, his mood suddenly sank, then when he heard you didn't want it, he suddenly felt joy, and later, when he heard your fiancé was still going to get married, he was furious. The origin of this feeling was elusive and unclear. Battat actually had a vague guess, but he also found it absurd—you had only met a few times, only for a short time, and didn't even leave your contact information. Why did this vague feeling arise?

He looks at your face seriously again, his thoughts drifting for a moment, pondering such a question. He doesn't know the answer either, but since that's the case, so be it. Maybe he doesn't even need an answer, since so-called answers are just to convince himself, and all he needs to know is the fact.

By the time I came to my senses, your rambling was nearing its end: "...... Then that fiancé, in short, was not very satisfied. I don't know how he found out where my coffee shop is. These past few mornings, he's been coming to pester me, demanding I go back and fulfill this engagement with him. It's just too annoying. To avoid his harassment, I simply closed the shop in the afternoon...... Luckily, he hasn't come over today. "As you say this, you let out a breath.

"Actually, if he were harassing me, it would be fine. But I'm worried he'll keep pestering other customers, making it so bad that no one else dares to come. If he wants to smash the shop or something, that would be even worse......" You pressed your brow to your forehead, and after saying the last sentence, you took another big sip of coffee before slamming the cup down on the table. Then, with a hint of nervousness, you looked at Battat.

"Come to think of it, I realize I've said so much. You don't find it annoying, do you? I just really want to talk about all these things......" you said, a little embarrassed, and pressed your fingers together.

Battat shook his head: "Of course it's fine, you ...... Uh, you can treat me like a tree hollow? Anyway, just say whatever you want to say." He said uncertainly, patting his chest again, as if to say he could definitely rely on him.

You chuckled, slumped over the table, turned to look out the window, and muttered to yourself, "Hey...... Saying it does feel much better, but I don't know what I'd do if that guy comes looking for me again......

Battat hesitated for a moment, then gritted his teeth and said, "How about we exchange contact info? If he shows up again, just call me directly. I think ...... If I can't help one of them, at least I can call others along. We can't just let this continue. Besides, it's not convenient to call the police for just harassment, so it's best if you have someone to protect you. Uh, I'm not recommending me. If you have a better candidate, you don't need to consider me......" By the end, he started stammering again, feeling his head burning—maybe his heart was burning too. Fortunately, he turned his face to look out the window, so he didn't see anything. Battat quietly breathed a sigh of relief.

You thought carefully for a moment, then turned and took out your phone: "Then let's add your contact info. Actually, we're all so familiar with each other, yet we've never added a contact info or anything...... Huh, why are you blushing? ”

You sit up. Honestly, you haven't really seen that expression on his face before. It was quite novel, so you couldn't help but take a second look at him.

Battat hadn't expected you to suddenly turn your head; he had just breathed a sigh of relief thinking you wouldn't see him! Anyway, now he could only lightly cough twice: "Ahem, it's nothing, let's add your contact info first." He also took out his own phone.

Then you swapped numbers. He saw you pin his number to the top and marked it with an asterisk. Somehow, he felt his chest throb twice more violently. He just hurriedly lowered his head, a bit shy, avoiding your gaze, and set your number to the pin. After hesitating for a moment, he added a heart to the end.

You let out a long breath: "Phew...... Next time he comes, I'll call you first! I think if he saw other men beside me, he wouldn't be so arrogant. But I can't really say what that kind of person might be thinking......

Battat glanced at the sky. He had come to the café early this morning, so it was still quite early. He looked at you, who was still muttering, hesitated for a moment, and offered a suggestion: "But that guy will probably find him later, right?" Why not close the café and go out first? The main thing is to clear his mind. Otherwise, being pestered by him every day would be uncomfortable. ”

You rub your chin, clearly convinced, and take a bite of macarons. Muttering as you nod, you say, "Alright, then let's go out and relax!" Good food lifts your spirits. I hope I can forget the unhappiness of the past few days! ”

You wave your hand to encourage yourself, and seeing you pull yourself together, Battat quietly breathes a sigh of relief.
