Coffee cup 4-Battatx you

Battatx you, OOC belongs to me

…… What's going on? Why does my mouth speaks so long? This was clearly only half the content I imagined this chapter should have (despair).

——

So you shake your finger and wink: "Of course you're a famous detective. After all, look, you already saw through my secret. You know, I never intended to tell you at first." ”

Battat glanced at you. Actually, he thought what he figured out wasn't important; to you, it was probably just part of the reason. And...... He had reason to suspect you were just deceiving him by saying that.

But since you said so, he might as well be proud at the right moment. So he raised his nose again, but still shyly touched it, probably afraid it would grow longer: "Uh, alright, then I'm a detective too!" He tried hard to put on a happy face, even though his raised pitch only made him seem a bit funny and also meant failure.

Moreover, the nose hasn't grown longer either; it stays perfectly there and remains the same length.

"Yes, yes, you are the detective." You even applauded him enthusiastically, watching Battat finally lift his head with a smile, and you were very happy.

"By the way, it's already dinner time before you know it. Let me treat you to dinner!" You suddenly said this.

Battat had just raised his cup to take another sip—even though it was already a bit cold, the cold coffee had its own unique flavor. Now, hearing you say that, he almost spat it out again, but his good personal manners made him drink it down, and what took his place was once again choking.

This time, you patted his back with practiced familiarity and said with a hint of concern, "Are you okay?" Don't keep daydreaming. If you're going to drink coffee, you should drink it properly. Look, you're choking again. ”

Although it was probably your fault—you turned your head a little guilty—you still said this with confidence.

Battat wiped his mouth: "It's all because of what you said—coffee and dessert are already enough, right? What's the point of treating him to dinner?" By the end, he was almost muttering, but his words were just right for you to hear.

You turn your head innocently to look at him: "To celebrate—and partly to thank you. You're my first guest, a repeat customer, and it's rare. You don't have work today, so you should just relax a bit!" ”

This time, Battat just gave you a strange look: "...... Do you treat all the returning customers to meals? ”

You pinched your chin and thought carefully, then shook your head: "Hmm...... No. I just... maybe it's because you're special! In short, I invited you because I wanted to invite you to dinner! "You said cheerfully, since you can't figure things out, just don't think about them for now. That's your way of survival."

He rubbed his head, seeming a bit troubled: "Alright...... What do you want to eat? Next time, I'll treat you back...... "Battat muttered under his breath, sounding a bit pained, probably thinking about money that wasn't even in his hands yet.

"Wait a moment, I saw a shop when I passed by nearby before. It seems pretty interesting...... Anyway, since no one else is coming, the café is officially closed today! "You cheered, ran to the café's entrance, changed the direction of the open sign to close, then turned your hands behind your back and looked at battat: "Alright, let's go!" ”

He stood up, hesitated for a moment, but still put the two coffee cups along with the empty tray onto the counter. Of course, the coffee had already been finished. It had to be said, the caramel macchiato really had a charm that made people enjoy coffee. At this moment, your cup was stacked with his. Battat stared blankly at the empty cup for two seconds until he heard your urge again: "Alright, I'll tidy up the cups and all that later. Let's go out to eat first!" ”

Only then did he snap back to reality, cleared his throat, and said, "Sorry, I'll come right away." Only then did he look away and quicken his pace toward you.

But the moment his gaze fell on you, he couldn't help but ask, "Aren't you going to write something on that cup?" ”

You hesitate for a second, then realize he was talking about your coffee cup. So you just happily shake your head: "I'm not planning to write anything for now. If I write my name again, it might easily confuse it with your cup...... That's not good, so let's keep it pure white for now. I'll wait until I get inspired someday. Maybe I'll get inspired and write a poem on it someday!" ”

Of course, you walk very fast, and Battat has to quicken his pace to keep up with you. Of course, for some reason, at times like this, he doesn't dare to look at you, so he just stares at the hanging sun hanging from the horizon...... The so-called sun.

And the orange and purple colors blending from the sky hanging on the horizon. Only then did he softly say, "Ah, then wait until you have inspiration before writing." ”

…… Although he just wanted to say that it was fine if you wrote your name again and then mixed things up, he didn't mind at all. But maybe you would, so Battat hesitated for a moment and ultimately didn't speak.

"Mm-hmm, of course no problem. Speaking of which, I'm planning to invite you to this place for dinner!" As you spoke, you excitedly pointed to the shop next to it with a French title. It was clearly a French restaurant, though probably not that upscale. But it still had the vibe of a grand Western restaurant.

Battat hesitated and asked, "Are you sure you want to go here?" ”

You nodded, "Mm-hmm, I'm sure." I've always been curious about how French cuisine tastes, and since we're treating you this time, I'll give it a try!" Saying that, you tried to pull Battat's arm into the restaurant.

Of course, he didn't have time to scold you for suddenly grabbing his arm. Battat could only frown and refute your point: "First of all, French food probably doesn't taste great, and second...... This kind of restaurant must be expensive! It looks so upscale. If you want to eat French food, we can go to another place, because this one might not taste great either, and they even brazenly advertise it so openly that people won't know what it's selling......

You hesitate for two seconds, then stop: "Uh, hey, but aren't Western restaurants always expensive?" I remember correctly. ”

“…… Not all restaurants are expensive, right? There are plenty of affordable ones, right? How about we change direction?" Battat rubbed his brow again, then grabbed your arm, trying to get you to change direction so you could leave in front of the restaurant.

You think about it carefully and think that makes sense, but you still want to go to this restaurant, so you just put on a serious face: "Alright, you make sense, but I think I'll stick with this place. After all, expensive has its advantages, at least the service won't be bad!" So today I'm treating you here! "Saying that, you grabbed Battat's arm and rushed inside. This time, Battat couldn't resist you and was pulled inside by you as well."

He felt his vision go black. Now he felt more money flying farther away from him, but ...... Forget it! He gritted his teeth; after all, he had to keep his word!

You pulled Battat down at a table, and the waiter quickly brought you the menu. Battat seemed a bit lost in thought—maybe he was just daydreaming? You waved your hand in front of him, but he still ignored you.

“Battat？ Battat？ So I've ordered my food, okay?" Just in case, you still asked again and called out to him. Of course, he still didn't respond, so in the end, you could only glance at Battat with a hint of concern, then happily hold the menu and start ordering.

Soon, you handed the menu back to the waiter, then rested your chin on your hand and watched the still-recovered battat, waiting for the waiter to bring the food. Soon, a double serving of creamy mushroom soup was served, along with a small serving of wine to accompany the meal.

Seeing battat like this, you thoughtfully pushed the creamy mushroom soup in front of him and said very thoughtfully, "battat, it's ready, wake up!" ”

Only then did he snap back to reality, slap his head with a smack, and look at you awkwardly and laugh twice: "Ahaha...... Sorry, I've been distracted for a while. Is the dish serving? "Absent-minded, he said as he picked up the spoon and took a sip of creamy mushroom soup. Then he burned himself and put the spoon down again.

Now he was fully awake, put down his spoon seriously, and pretended that the soup wasn't just like him, just placing his hand on the table properly.

You blinked innocently, still not telling him that you had just felt like he had left your body. You just gently blew on the soup on the spoon, then slowly put it in your mouth, squinting slightly. The salty, fresh cream flavor mixed with freshly stir-fried mushrooms, and the steaming soup had a hint of sweetness. It really was perfect as an appetizer, at least you were looking forward to what else they would serve next.

"You get what you pay for. Their taste is pretty good. Want to try some?" So you said.

Battat hesitates at you, then looks at the creamy mushroom soup with bitter eyes. To be honest, because he'd just been burned, he didn't dare to try it for now. And just thinking about how expensive the creamy mushroom soup might make his vision go black. Drinking it was like gold, so he didn't dare to take a bite.

Anyway, under your expectant gaze, Battat had no choice but to pick up the spoon and take a sip of soup. His eyes finally lit up slightly, finally freed from that lifeless state. After all, it really tasted good, and it was worth comforting himself after several days of hard work to finally get some rest. So he didn't mind that you spent a lot of money on the treat—forget it, how could he not mind?!

Wait until you come back with a proper treat. At worst, you'll just treat a few more times. I can't just let you spend money for nothing...... Battat felt a bit pained, but still made this decision. Of course, you across from you knew nothing, just swung your legs and finished your creamy mushroom soup, waiting for the main dish to be served.

Soon, a pan-seared squab along with seafood baked rice was brought to you. It was clearly freshly baked, still sizzling with hot oil. Of course, another portion was placed in front of the battat. The seafood baked rice was sprinkled with fresh shrimp and small fish, mixed with some chopped scallions and squid bits, and the aroma of the rice irresistibly filled your nose.

You nod in satisfaction, first tear off the pigeon's wings, then take a couple of bites. The skin is still crispy, and the taste is really good. And the meat is also tender and fragrant, showing that this restaurant has a keen eye for ingredients and is spicy...... Well, that's not the point—since you've already spent money yourself, of course they have to serve well! That's exactly what you think of as a matter of course.

Battat hesitated for two seconds, then used the provided spoon to scoop a spoonful of baked seafood rice and put it in his mouth. Well, it really was delicious. Now he was completely convinced.

Before long, you've finished the main course. Wait for the waiter to bring the plates down and bring out dessert and salad to pair with the wine. The meal is about to end.

The dessert was a freshly baked, delicately plated apple pie, fragrant, and steaming hot. The salad was rather ordinary, just a bowl of vegetables and fruits topped with salad dressing.

You use a fork to pick up some vegetable leaves and put them in your mouth. When the apple pie seems to cool down a bit, you put it in your mouth. The crispy crust and the fragrant apple flavor blend together...... It's truly sweet and delicious, at least you're willing to have another piece. Before you know it, everything on the plate has been gone.

Of course, at this moment the plates in front of you are all empty, which means you've finally finished eating. For some reason, Battat looks at the fact that no new plates are served, but instead lets out a sigh. You glance at him in confusion, unable to figure out what he's thinking.

You were the one paying the bill too. Battat propped his head up and glanced at the bill from the side. Even though you kept insisting Battat didn't need to pay, he still shook his head firmly.

"Anyway, when I get there, I'll definitely find another place to treat and invite him back." He still clenched his fist and said this, looking like he was burning with some fighting spirit.

Food...... You didn't mind, so you reluctantly nodded and agreed to battat. In fact, you were looking forward to what kind of shop he would take you to.

But after saying goodbye today, you haven't seen him for several days in a row, which makes you feel a bit melancholy and lonely, and also a bit uneasy. After all, you don't have his contact information. Although you took him as a friend without permission, in reality, you were just strangers who met by chance. At this point, you actually regret not leaving a single contact information.

However, the wind chimes occasionally ring, which means your shop door has been pushed open. At that moment, you look up in surprise, only to find it is a strange face, and then lower your head in disappointment.
