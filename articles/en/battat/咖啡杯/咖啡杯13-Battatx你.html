Coffee cup 13-Battatx you

Battatx you, OOC counts me as me

…… It felt like a random conversation started, and it ended with a hilarious ending.

——

You rubbed your chin, your tone a bit hesitant: "Really? Isn't this a bit too much trouble for you......? In the end, this kind of thing is still my problem. I'll handle it myself! I'm already very grateful that you helped me out these two times......

Before you could finish, Battat interrupted him. His brows furrowed tightly, and his voice grew urgent: "How can you say that? I don't think these are troubles at all, I...... Ahem, no matter what trouble it is, I'll find a way to help you solve it." By the end, probably a bit embarrassed, his voice gradually quieted down again.

You blinked, not sure if it was curiosity that got a good idea. Or maybe you just wanted to tease him. You pulled him to sit by the window, then propped your chin up and smiled at him: "Hey, why? I feel like these troublesome things shouldn't be left to you alone......"

He cleared his throat twice, still speaking softly, his voice almost murmuring: "Ah, because I really like you." ”

You curved your eyes slightly, tilted your head, and deliberately dragged out your voice: "What? I can't hear you. Say it again?" ”

Just as you finished saying this, you noticed Battat looked up at you intently, and for some reason, you seemed a bit guilty. Well, you did do it on purpose. After all, the shop was so quiet, you naturally could hear what he was saying. You just wanted him to repeat it a few more times, or rather, you just wanted to hear it a few more times. Every time you heard it, you felt overjoyed, and for a moment, nothing else was on your mind.

If only time could only stop at this moment, how wonderful that would be. Then you wouldn't have to worry about troubles or worries anymore; you just needed to enjoy the sweetness of the present.

Battat actually knew you were doing it on purpose. After all, your smiling face was full of ill intent. He was sure that even if your voice was soft, you could hear it. But he didn't mind repeating it a few more times. If it weren't for someone else present, he would feel embarrassed and want to say it out loud, repeating it over and over until your face turned red, until your lips touched again, and those warm, soft sensations came back to you.

Of course, that was just a fantasy. Right now, Battat didn't know why he had been so bold just now, and now he felt a bit embarrassed thinking about it. And seeing you propping up your head, leisurely waiting for his answer, somehow he suddenly had a flash of inspiration—maybe he had a bit of a mischievous sense of humor. Maybe he had never had such thoughts before, but maybe it was only after meeting you that Battat started having similar thoughts.

…… It was your presence that allowed him to see more colors in life.

So he coughed lightly twice, suddenly stood up slightly, and leaned close to your ear. Actually, at that moment, Battat blushed too. Luckily, from this angle, you couldn't see it, and he whispered in your ear: "Because I like you the most." ”

You paused, feeling a slightly cool yet slightly gentle breath brushing your ear. You heard the sentence clearly, so you couldn't help but blush. Your ears felt hot, so you covered your ears with your hands, but your heartbeat quickened. You could even hear the sounds in your chest. Fortunately, the battat sitting across from you couldn't hear a thing.

You watched him sit back across from him with a smile, his face a bit flushed, and couldn't help but glare at him. Although you knew your glance wasn't very harmful, you then coughed dryly and stammered, "Alright...... Alright! I understand, I like you the most! But if you have any trouble, you should tell me. If there's any difficulty, we should solve it together...... I can't just dump all the trouble on you. Anyway, I'll figure something out this time. I've decided to hire some thugs. Anyway, I don't need that money, but it might affect customer traffic......

You muttered to yourself, after all, you know those fierce, intimidating thugs might scare off a lot of people, but there's nothing you can do—for the peace of the future, you have to do it.

The other customers sitting inside the café were probably intimidated by the sticky atmosphere between the two of you. In any case, they tossed down their tips, pressed them under their coffee cups, and hurried away. You didn't really mind.

"Mm...... I'll still bring people over. If that's the case, let them be your guests then—they'll be the right way to hold the stage. "Battat suggested.

You glanced at him curiously: "Who are you trying to get here?" ”

“…… My colleagues. As for who they are, you'll know when the time comes." He just spoke vaguely, and you nodded knowingly, not asking further. Anyway, you'll find out when the time comes.

"But the timing is probably a bit tight, right? After all, I don't know when he'll bring people over. I plan to start hiring thugs tomorrow...... "You're still a bit worried. Maybe you're just being overly guarded. That so-called fiancé keeps coming to harass you and doesn't seem to care about anything else. You might think it's fine if he does something illegal, and might even be very arrogant."

"I think it will probably take three to five days. After all, he can't possibly find someone to come over that quickly, and after today's setback, it probably will take some time before he can muster the courage...... But hiring a thug first is a good idea. You have to protect yourself, and if that person suddenly goes mad or something...... Battat spoke very seriously about this matter, and even gave you a thorough reminder.

You look at your new boyfriend just nodding with a smile. You don't mind if it sounds a bit like lecturing. Instead, after Battat finishes, he feels a bit embarrassed and scratches his head: "Ah, sorry, I'm not trying to interfere with your decision, nor do I mean to make choices for you. As long as you feel comfortable, that's enough......"

"It's okay. I know you're saying all this because you're worried about me, and you really consider my feelings, so I don't feel uncomfortable at all. You're really nice, but you're right. I have to protect myself, and you have to protect yourself too. Otherwise, I'll hire some thugs for you too. What if he goes crazy and tries to attack you...... "You're seriously thinking about this issue, and you even carry a hint of worry."

Battat cleared his throat a few times: "I'll be careful these days, and I usually stay at the company, so it's not a big problem, right...... Sometimes I even have to work overtime because of another identity, and I think he probably won't find me either." As he spoke, he turned into mumbling.

To be honest, he was a bit annoyed at his current job, because work was holding him back. He couldn't see you all the time, but he felt a bit grateful for his job, because no matter what, the colleagues he knew now were doing things that helped him to some extent. Although exactly that kind of help was another matter, because whenever he talked about working overtime, he felt his life was tough.

“…… Uh, what you said does make some sense. "You cleared your throat twice and slightly looked away.

Alright, hearing him casually talk about working overtime and such, you even start to feel a bit sorry for him. It seems you won't have many chances to meet...... Wait, why are you starting to think about this? You shouldn't be thinking about this now, so you shake your head and throw your thoughts away.

Then you seriously suggested, "Hmm, maybe you should leave with your colleagues after work lately? Wouldn't that be safer...... I have no other choice but to hire a thug." "You said this with a hint of regret."

"You're way too nervous. Why are we making contingency plans for such possibilities...... battat muttered quietly, but he didn't think your explanation was completely impossible, so his expression grew more serious. "Alright, I'll be careful. We'll take precautions." ”

Then you exchanged glances and suddenly burst out laughing. You pointed at battat, panting and said, "Look at you, you're still too nervous." ”

Battat felt a bit embarrassed but retorted, "What's the big deal? Just in case, and you were the one who brought up this possibility first." ”

"Whatever, and you were the one who nervously warned me first. I just think if there's any danger, both of us probably need to be cautious."

“…… Ahem, it's just some emergency plans......

"Looks like work really is too busy. Even for something like this, you need to prepare contingency plans or something. Do you need to prepare several more plans just in case?"

You see Battat fall silent, looking conflicted, even a bit tempted, and you can't help but raise your eyebrows in disbelief: "No way, you really want to do this?" ”

He awkwardly cleared his throat: "I'm just thinking about it. I think maybe sometimes it can be used as a reference......"

“…… Thinking so much is exhausting. Sometimes you just need to relax and go with the flow. Constantly overthinking makes you anxious. "As you spoke, you lifted your body slightly, pressed down on battat's furrowed brows, and gently smoothed them out.

"Hmm...... Makes sense, but I often can't do that......"

"Then let's stop here. As long as you tell me everything, you'll be able to relax a little!" Oh, and after chatting for so long, my mouth was dry—I almost forgot to make you coffee...... You ran toward the counter, picked up two coffee cups, started pouring hot water and milk as usual, whipped the milk foam, made latte art, made two cappuccinos, and drew two heart-shaped latte art, each with your names written on it.

You handed the cup with your name on it to battat, the cup in your hand had his name on it, and then you sat across from him holding the coffee.

Drinking coffee is like quenching thirst. You seem to have endless things to say, but probably no clear focus. You just say whatever comes to mind, just say whatever comes to mind, then laugh together until both cups are finished, leaving the cup empty, and you finally say goodbye, still wanting more.

After Battat went home for the weekend, he went back to work. He had already decided to convert all the dark coins into points. Anyway, those pippins would definitely pass by when they heard about points being handed out...... He just gritted his teeth and used up the dark coins he hadn't used before and the points he had painstakingly accumulated. Now his expression was somewhat worn, because he was a complete pauper.

But it's okay, it's just spending a little money for you. If you lose it, you can save it again. He definitely doesn't want anything bad for you.

Naturally, since he used points, and hearing there was free coffee, those pippins were all very happy—uh, maybe because of the points. In any case, they had agreed to support Battat at the café, and if any annoying guy showed up, they'd definitely be the first to rush in!

Pippins just laughed and said in a slightly cynical tone, "Boss, we'll always follow you!" ”

Battat awkwardly tugged at the corner of his mouth: "Uh, I don't need you to follow me forever, and I'm not going to keep handing out points to you! My points are also given by Tenna! And don't call me boss. If you call me the coffee shop owner boss later, I'd be really happy......" He awkwardly covered his mouth, hiding his face that had suddenly turned red for some reason.

Those pippins seemed to suddenly understand something and excitedly said in unison, "Oh, I get it! That's the boss's wife!" ”

“…… No!! That's not it! "He waved his hands and began to explain weakly, but no pippins listened to him or cared about his explanation; they all laughed and scattered. Only Battat was still helplessly reaching out his hand, and then he could only sigh and let go.

…… Forget it, at worst I'll explain later, he thought with such a weathered mind.

Probably seeing the excitement, some Zapper and Shadow Guy wanted to join in, thinking they were there to hold the show anyway, so calling more people didn't matter, so Battat agreed. Pluey and Jongler didn't know if they wanted to watch the spectacle or really want to help, but they were eager to join in.

…… Anyway, the more people, the better! Battat simply agreed to everything, but all these just for fun, for free, Battat wouldn't give them any points...... He would just send those pippins over and use up all his existing points.

But to join the fun, they were even willing to skip work, probably because work was so boring that they had to go on strike every few days to show the boss a lesson!

In short, for several days after Tenna came, all she saw was the empty TV world, with only a few employees left, and Mike was gone.

…… The law doesn't punish the masses, that's probably how it is. At least they weren't punished afterwards, and even got more points.

But now, Battat was sitting in the café with a bunch of pippins, looking awkwardly at the packed café and you, who were suddenly so busy you barely had time to rest.
