Coffee cup 1-Battatx you

Battatx you, OOC counts me as me

The middle planning proposal is just my nonsense, since I don't study this.

——

Battat held the half-written proposal in his hand, hesitating as he glanced at the buildings on both sides. To be honest, he had his reasons for not daring to stay in the room he had been in before—how could he possibly hope others would discover that Mike was now wearing a disguised copy?

In short, besides, this proposal could only be left to him to write...... The other two guys were unreliable. He tapped his head and decided to find a place with few people to complete the proposal. Plus, he had stayed up late last night and had gotten up early again today to finish it, so he might need a cup of coffee......

In short, he walked into a quiet, seemingly deserted café ahead.

There weren't many people inside the café, and the seats were all empty, even giving off a fresh feel. Even the owner was dozing off at the counter.

But you still caught the sound of the glass door opening and footsteps. After all, you hung a wind chime on the door to keep yourself from noticing a guest had arrived. Now the bell was ringing, so you immediately woke up and forced a perfect business smile, pretending you hadn't fallen asleep at all.

"Welcome, sir. What would you like to use? The menu is clearly marked, and of course, you can also order at the counter." You said with a smile, placing the coffee cup you had washed that morning on the counter—uh, of course, you've actually washed it many times.

"By the way, we can freely choose our coffee cups...... Well, since you're the first customer in our shop, the cup you pick will become your exclusive cup, okay?" You blinked, somehow feeling like you're trying hard to promote the coffee business. Even though no one came to drink coffee, you were happy to have some peace and quiet, saving time and effort dealing with customers and brewing coffee.

After all, you're not short of money; opening a coffee shop is just for sentiment and personal interests. You still want to attract a group of regular customers with affordable coffee and simple yet delicious flavors, so they can rest briefly and enjoy comfort.

"Ah...... Is that so? "Battat was startled. Actually, when you were dozing off, he didn't think about waking you. He just forced himself to finish the proposal, and he didn't expect you to wake up.

Still, he cleared his throat and stepped forward, looking at the various coffee cups displayed on the counter. Some cups were simple, decorated with diamond-shaped or striped patterns, others had creative designs, shaped like peacocks or other species, and others used abstract colors and brushstrokes to depict abstract artworks.

There was a cup that looked quite odd, with only a name signed on it with a dark green pen. Battat couldn't recognize the name, at least he didn't think it was a celebrity's name. It looked like it was written casually, since some of the strokes had been washed away and some were slightly blurry.

He double-checked, "Can I pick any cup?" "After all, that cup looked like your own name, and Battat felt a bit embarrassed to pick one, but he couldn't help but look at the cup."

Following his gaze, you were startled and muttered, "Why did you take out this cup too...... I was planning to keep it for myself......

Then you closed your eyes for a moment and said with righteous determination, "Guest, pick whatever you like! Anything is fine—and I believe in your taste, I'm sure you'll have a good time here." ”

Battat hesitated for a moment, but finally reached out and poked the cup with only bare green handwriting: "Then this one, clean, and I like the color of the handwriting." ”

You opened your eyes, and good, you really picked out the cup you had forgotten at first, so you took it out together—you mourned in your heart, the one you wrote your name on for fun, and the cup that had gone out of glory after just two days, then continued to smile: "Okay, the color of this handwriting looks just like yours...... Hmm, what would you like to drink? I'll make you coffee right away. ”

"Huh...... Anything goes? I just want to stay alert. "As he spoke, battat shook the document in his hand. It was a proposal he had been halfway through last night, and now his mind was spinning, really not knowing what else to write.

You stroked your chin and thought for a moment: "Alright, I understand. I'll deliver your coffee to you later. Please wait a moment, sir!" ”

Battat let out a gentle breath and couldn't help but add, "Don't always use such respectful words to address me. I always feel a bit embarrassed...... Besides, my name is Battat, and I think I'll come to this place in the future too." He let out a breath. After all, it was really quiet here, with hardly anyone around, which was perfect for his work, and he didn't have to worry about whether anyone would notice him.

You blink and nod: "Alright! Well then, Mr. Battat, please find any seat to sit down. The coffee will be ready later!" ”

As you spoke, you picked up a bag of coffee beans, ready to grind them into coffee before brewing—since this process was quick. You decided to brew a cold brew coffee, which was refreshing, not acidic, and also had a refreshing effect. Since it was Battat's first time and the first customer, you decided to use only classic coffee beans.

So you can take out the Tampika—or call it Blue Mountain coffee beans? This is considered the oldest variety, a classic coffee flavor, very clean in texture, with floral notes, fruit acidity, and a refreshing sweetness.

The coffee machine rumbles. Once the coffee grounds are ready, you steep them in cold water and wait for a cold brew to finish. This process is a bit long—at least you yawn again. But the cold brew is still brewed. You see brown swirls gently spinning, and you think this cup must have a fresher taste, rich aroma, and sweetness.

Pour the coffee into that cup, carry the porcelain plate out, and immediately spot Battat sitting by the window, his head slowly moving as if he's about to fall asleep.

You walked briskly, deliberately softening your footsteps as you approached him, set down the cup of coffee, and then looked at Battat's head bit by bit, feeling a bit troubled. You saw the pen in his hand irregularly tapping the paper below, even with some scribble-like symbols in front—it was clear he was not fully conscious, but he came here to finish his work and even needed coffee, which probably meant he was urgently needed a document.

Anyway, you hesitate for a moment, but still nudge his shoulder and softly call out, "Battat, battat, wake up. The coffee is ready, and your work isn't done yet." ”

In any case, he suddenly woke up, his face still a bit startled. He didn't know what words had scared him out, but when he opened his eyes, he found you were looking at him innocently.

Seeing that he still looked a bit dazed after waking up, you pushed the cup of coffee toward him: "Alright, have this. The coffee is ready. I think it'll help refresh you." ”

Battat looked at it and still picked up the cold brew. To be honest, he didn't really care what kind of coffee it was—even if it was just a quickly brewed espresso, it didn't matter—but this cold brew still lifted his spirits. Of course, there was still a bitter taste at the mouth, but the acidity that should have come with the bitterness was almost gone, replaced by a soft, floral aroma and a subtle sweetness that seemed to envelop body and mind. Combined with the relatively cool temperature, it cleared his entire dice awake.

At least he couldn't say the coffee wasn't good; rather, it was rather novel. He hadn't expected such an unknown shop to have such outstanding craftsmanship.

"Hmm...... Thank you for this cup of coffee. I'm fully awake now. "Battat rubbed his head. Now he was looking at the proposal in his hand with some worry. He hadn't figured out what to write next, and now it was even better—he even wrote some scribbles on it. This paper was basically useless, and he would have to redo everything later.

"Although the coffee is delicious, I still hope all the guests who come here can relax. However...... Is this job really urgent?" As you spoke, you glanced at the document again.

"Yeah, I have to submit it tomorrow. If I don't write well enough, the boss might scold me. I'm still worrying about how to write next......

"Then, let me take a look. Maybe from a third-party perspective, I can offer better suggestions." You suggested.

Battat was stunned and stammered, "That's not really right...... This is my job, and you have your own job......

You just shrugged: "Oh, it's fine, no one else comes here anyway. Besides, I just hope you can relax a bit more here, so—let me take a look! Don't worry, I won't mess it up!" You even stuck out your tongue as you spoke.

Battat hesitated for a moment, then handed you the document that was definitely going to be discarded, then took out a blank one and began writing.

You took the document, sat across from Battat, and started reading, nodding from time to time. To be honest, what you wrote earlier seemed fine, or rather, it was good—well, although TV program planning proposals should be handed over to dedicated planning personnel, right? No matter how you look at it, they shouldn't be suitable for this, right?

But you do have some experience in organizing and planning—after all, your family has a company.

So you cleared your throat and started to look serious: "Ahem, I think what you wrote is fine, but I think before officially implementing this plan, we first need to identify each character's role, determine what they can do, and make sure they do what suits the job...... And for the upcoming project, why not include an audience participation segment? "You asked this question with some curiosity.

Battat hesitated for a moment, then looked up at you and whispered, "Because lately, the audience in the audience has been getting fewer and fewer." ”

You tapped your knuckles on the table: "Then there should be a segment like this—you have to attract some stable viewers to stay as a steady traffic. Of course, if you don't consider the audience, then your proposal should be a recommendation ...... for movies and TV dramas? So, you can compare and analyze similar films and provide in-depth explanations, such as studying some more specialized points. The mainstream tends to be watched by the general public, while those niche groups will naturally have more professional viewers or simply curiosity. ”

Alright, Battat thought your suggestion did use some wording, but he hesitated for a moment: "Is this really useful?" ”

And you nod firmly: "Of course it works! At least I really like this kind of more professional explanation...... Hehe. "As you spoke, you awkwardly touched your nose.

Battat nodded and began writing furiously. However, while writing, he could always sense a sharp gaze—not sharp, but feeling uncomfortable when someone was watching made him uncomfortable. After all, he came to this café hoping no one could see him......

So he still looked up a little awkwardly, looking at you, and found you resting your chin on your hand, watching him with great interest. When he stopped writing, he tilted his head in confusion: "Hmm?" What's wrong? ”

Battat cleared his throat twice: "Could you stop looking at me?" I feel a bit uncomfortable with this...... I can't keep writing. ”

You blinked, then simply closed your eyes, then leaned over the desk and muttered a couple of words: "Alright! Now I won't look at you, you can keep writing...... Just enough for me to sleep for a while......

As you talked, your voice quieted down, and soon you fell silent. It was obvious you were asleep, since Battat could hear the soft, even breathing.

He looked at you hesitantly, but in the end, he buried his head and started writing furiously. Whether it was the effect of that cold brew coffee, or the slight relaxation of his nerves during our conversation, or the suggestion you made that seemed a bit off-topic but actually useful, Battat felt his mind sharpened a lot at this moment, and he quickly finished writing the proposal. All that was left was to rub his aching hands, drink coffee, and stare blankly at your sleeping profile.

Coffee was just too useful. At least now, Battat didn't feel sleepy at all, and even miserably thought maybe he could write three more proposals.

When you wake up, the dice in front of you have long since left, leaving only an empty coffee cup and coins pressed underneath. You can count it—it's a bit more expensive than a cold brew coffee.

Besides that, there was a note under the coffee cup, probably written hastily by battat: Thank you.

You chuckled, put away the note, and picked up your coffee cup.
