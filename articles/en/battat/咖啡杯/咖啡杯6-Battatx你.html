…… It seems like a mere ledger......

——

Battat couldn't help but turn his head away looking at your expectant gaze. He didn't dare to look at you any longer, so he coughed awkwardly twice, struggling internally. To be honest, he was a bit hesitant about whether to touch it again...... Hmm, his hand was a bit restless, and he could feel the softness and fluffiness on top of his head now.

He hesitated for a moment, then squeezed his glasses shut tightly and steeled himself: "Alright!" He said this as if he had surrendered heroically, then lowered his hand and touched the top of your head, as if facing death fearlessly, and his movements were very stiff.

Blinking, you naturally notice his unnatural movement, so you kindly remind him, "That way, the pressure will only increase...... Relax a bit?" "This was obviously just a suggestion, but Battat immediately pulled his hand back, as if burned, clearing his throat to hide his embarrassment.

"Alright, let's leave it at that...... Let's finish the work first, work comes first! "Actually, it's fine to hurry up and finish it later, but now, whenever Battat thinks about the work left unfinished, that anxiety wells up in his heart.

You nodded knowingly, "Alright, then let me help you finish part of it. By the way, what kind of handwriting do you have? Maybe I can imitate it...... You hesitated as you glanced at the stack of documents. Clearly, they were all blank.

Battat hesitated for a moment, then pulled out a piece of paper and handed it to you, then divided part of the file out: "This one, right? I just happened to write part of it after I got these jobs yesterday, so you can just write it in this format...... Hmm, and then this part, not very important." He nodded.

He gave you his part, and whether it was out of trust or selfishness, since you wanted to imitate the handwriting, he showed it to you. As for the documents he traded with other pippins, he handled it himself.

Although he actually thought it didn't matter how you wrote it—after all, Tenna didn't seem like someone who would check these documents. To be honest, he hadn't even seen the boss name any employee's proposal adopted! But seeing how motivated you were, of course he wouldn't discourage you. He just thought for a moment and added, "Uh...... Actually, you can just write whatever you want? ”

You shook your head sharply: "That won't do!" Battat clearly looks like he's seriously completing these tasks. Of course I won't hold you back—I have to work hard too! "You seem to be burning with burning energy too."

Of course, for you, battat's handwriting isn't very hard to imitate. After all, it's probably to make it clear that it's not written in a complicated or hard way to read, just a bit of a hook at the end of the letter, probably a personal habit.

You carefully reviewed the documents Battat had written. There was nothing special about it, just drafting a speech that the boss might need. By the way, the speech was well written. If you could join your company just doing paperwork, that would be fine...... You were tempted again, but in the end, you regretfully gave up on it. You felt you couldn't persuade Battat right away.

You don't actually dislike his persistence; in fact, you quite appreciate it. In a way, he probably quite enjoys the job he has now? You don't really know either. Anyway, after reading this speech, you can guess you can put it down and start checking the requirements for other documents, oh...... There are also several dialogue proposals and program planning references, probably referring to the specific segments that need to be arranged in the program.

You see that the title is a detective show, think deeply, and then confidently write down some segments. Of course, first, you need someone to explore the scene for clues, then deduce based on the clues found and the intelligence provided by the NPC. Naturally, when questioning the NPC, you need some script; if the script completely fails, you get no answers......

You finished writing the document in a long way, glanced at your own words proudly, then compared it to Battat's speech. Hmm, you couldn't tell it was written by a second person—how nice.

But while you were studying this document, Battat had already completed three documents in wild cursive form. After all, he hadn't taken pippins' work too seriously, and wild cursive and similar styles suited their personalities better, right? In short, he wrote quickly, even after just a brief mental concept. Besides, Tenna probably wouldn't even look at pippins' files, which made Battat feel more at ease.

Seeing Battat finish a document so quickly, you can't help but feel a bit anxious and speed up your writing—haha, you just said anxiety is bad, but now you're actively anxious. But as long as it solves problems, sometimes being anxious is actually a good thing......

As you pondered, you couldn't help but take a sip of coffee, finally snapping out of the chaotic emotions. You diligently finished the documents again. Whether it was your imagination or not, battat's movements seemed to be just passing the job. You wanted to ask what the documents were, but seeing him start working on another pile and looking much more serious, his writing much slower, you couldn't bear to disturb him. So in the end, you remained silent, focusing on the document in front of you.

When you're finally done, you stretch lazily, watching Battat prop his head up writing the last document, quietly take a bite of the tiramisu that was brought out and hasn't been touched at all. Chewing on it, you ask unclearly, "Hmm, by the way, is this job for a senior employee really tough?" ”

As soon as he finished speaking, you saw Battat finally let out a long breath, slapped the last file on the pile beside him, and for once, revealed a look that could only be described as arrogant...... Sneering? Anyway, maybe he's just too happy about finishing his work, that's what you're guessing.

"Hard to do?" Of course it's hard. After all, although the boss has assigned almost the same content, each time he only carefully reviews the part the senior employee does and then takes their suggestions, praising them for a long time. But the ordinary employees have worked so hard to do all this, and who knows where he has thrown it away! "At this point, Battat was a bit frustrated. He reached out and gestured hard, not sure what he wanted to say.

In short, you just watch him vent his emotions, then take a deep breath, pick up a spoon, and scoop a mouthful of tiramisu. Of course, since you had already dug a piece, he avoided that side and only dug down a portion from the other end.

"So that's how it is...... I thought you should just quit your job already. Why bother working in a company like this? The treatment is just too bad! "You started defending battat.

Battat took a deep breath, pulled his chair closer to you, looked around, then lowered his voice: "Right...... If I were just an ordinary employee, I might have been fired. Although I don't know what other company would accept pippins, it's still better than being exploited. If I hadn't disguised myself as that senior employee, I definitely would have left......

He muttered, "You don't know if Battat is just looking for an excuse not to leave that company."

In short, you spoke up again, of course lowering your voice and with some concern for battat: "...Are you really not being PUA by the company you're currently at? Also, your sense of worth seems quite low? I think it has nothing to do with status or anything. As long as you have the ability, what company wouldn't hire you? If a company rejects you just based on status, I think such a company really has no vision and surely won't last long."

“…… But if you truly like your current job, I wouldn't insist you quit. I just think you really need to relax a little longer from time to time." You said with a cheerful smile.

Battat hesitated for a moment, pondering his true position, and finally hesitantly replied, "I don't know either...... But I guess I still like this job, right?" As he finished speaking, his voice rose upward, clearly showing that even he wasn't quite sure about it.

You just smiled and patted his shoulder: "Well, if you're tired, why not come here and rest?" You can talk to me for a bit. Of course, if you don't want to say anything, it's quiet enough here. You just need to come here and sit for a while. At least it should help you relax a bit, right? Besides, I said you can treat me like a tree hollow, so just say whatever you want! "As you spoke, you proudly patted your chest.

"Indeed...... It's relaxing here, but now that I'm relaxing, it feels like I'm just taking a nap. "In the end, he couldn't help but laugh and just took a gentle sip of coffee. The coffee had cooled a bit, but the rich milk and coffee flavors blended together still created a unique taste.

“…… Then sleep? "You asked, not quite sure.

"I just finished my coffee and still can't sleep." He answered matter-of-factly.

"Then just chat casually? Speaking of which, isn't there anyone holding this senior position? Why do you need to pretend......

Battat put down his coffee cup, resting his chin on his hand, putting on a deep expression in thought for a moment, then finally spoke softly: "Mm...... Actually, that senior employee also has a name—his name is Mike. But even though his room is there, it's empty. The key is, the boss always calls out Mike's name, and whenever Mike is gone, he gets really angry! Uh, part of the purpose is to keep him from taking it out on the employees below, but anyway, after we think about it, only the other two of us will take on the role of Mike. Of course, it's just a disguise......"

Your gaze shifted slightly, landing on the tall pile of documents: "Uh, so there are still so many documents to handle? So does that mean you're the one handling all the paperwork for Mike?" ”

Battat let out a quiet breath, leaned over the table, and stretched out his hands. It was clear he was truly tired.

"Yeah...... To be honest, at first the three of us shared the work, but it turned out the handwriting was different and the quality was different, so we decided to split up and finish it separately. Maybe this time it was my turn, and next time it was another. But this time, all the work piled up fell on me......

He seemed to be muttering to himself, but his volume was just right for you to hear. You nodded thoughtfully, scooped up another spoonful of tiramisu, and then, like Battat, lay down on the table and let out a quiet sigh.

"Alright, I understand. Although they don't let me do these tasks anymore, I've handled it myself, so I know this situation very well......" At this point, you start to feel a bit melancholy recalling the past.

Battat turned his head to look at you: "What is it...... Is there something you need? ”

"Well, it's not really that important, right? It's just that I'm of age and should inherit the company, so I've been involved in some matters. But my father happened to die in an accident, and the power has fallen to another elder on the board. I don't know what his attitude is, but I just didn't want to deal with those matters anymore, so I used the excuse of opening a coffee shop and left."

“…… Anyway, they won't be short of my supplies." You said this as if it were only natural.

"It doesn't sound like a great situation, but if that's the case, you could definitely try to reclaim your power, right?" Battat thought for a moment and said this, then sat up straight and scooped up a spoonful of tiramisu to eat. He was probably just daydreaming, so he didn't realize he had taken the wrong spoon, since when he put down two spoons on the same side earlier.

"But I still prefer my current life—it's easygoing, doesn't require me to think too much, and I can chat with guests I'm interested in...... Oh right, it seems you're holding my spoon." You scratched your cheek, still leaning on the table, just slightly looking up at battat.

His movements froze. He had already put the spoon in his mouth, but now he was unsure whether to take it out or not. Although it was strange to see the dice mysteriously turn red from bottom to top, you still sat up and said understandingly, "It's okay, I'll get another handful. It's also my fault for not paying attention and putting the spoon together......" You muttered to yourself, stood up, and walked over to the counter to pick up another spoon.

Of course, you glanced at the spot where the previous guest was, but you don't know exactly when he left. Anyway, he left a few coins under the coffee cup, probably as a tip? You guess.

Battat took out a spoon and stared blankly at it several times. Of course, he still felt a bit dizzy at this moment, and when you came back with a new spoon, you'd see him still like this. Of course, you took a different one—this time, you brought a small ceramic spoon.
