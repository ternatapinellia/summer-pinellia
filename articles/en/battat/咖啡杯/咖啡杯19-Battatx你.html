But before he could keep his wild thoughts, seeing his sudden blushing, you waved your hand and waved it in front of his eyes: "That's not what I meant. You lowered your voice to explain why you were doing this, and after hearing this, Battat gradually toned down his previously shy expression and became serious.

"I understand. But regarding this matter, haven't you considered calling the police or something?" he asked.

You shrugged: "Hmm...... I did think about it, but right now I'm just suspicious. Calling the police directly might not be the right idea. I still want to at least make sure this is done before reporting it. ”

“…… Alright, I'll go back with you." So you heard battat reply, lowering his voice. To be honest, he sincerely hoped all of this was just your imagination; battat didn't want you to actually run into danger. Things like stalkers are still a bit too scary.

Sigh, when will it finally be completely peaceful? Battat couldn't help but wonder.

You nod in satisfaction and return to the counter to continue making coffee and serving customers. Although there weren't many customers left, today was very leisurely. After finishing these tasks, you sat behind the counter dozing off until Battat walked up and gently tapped the counter, finally fully awakening.

Only then did you realize it was already this hour, and everyone else in the store had left. You shyly smiled at battat: "Ah...... Sorry, I just fell asleep. ”

Battat felt inexplicably embarrassed watching your actions, but when he heard the word 'together,' he felt as if his soul had fallen into a warm spring, soaking in the warm and sweet flow. For a moment, he just wanted to miss that feeling. But whenever he thought about maybe going home together in the future—words you often say and might say—he couldn't help but feel a sense of fulfillment.

"Come to think of it, this feels like it's the first time I've gone home with Battat. Want to talk about something?" You put one hand behind your back, while the other tightly held Battat's hand, gazing thoughtfully at the swaying sunset on the horizon.

Battat pondered for a moment: "That's fine, but lately it's been work-related matters, so I don't think there's much to say...... Anyway, he's been constantly dragged into overtime lately, so he's only had a bit of free time these past couple of days. He also wants to see you every day, as if all his fatigue melts away in that moment, but...... He usually doesn't get that chance. Usually, after overtime, he has to go back to his own place and feels embarrassed to ask for your photos or something.

Battat suddenly stopped in his tracks, making you stumble a bit. You turned to look at Battat, just about to complain, but then you realized his face was already bright red.

After hesitating for a moment, you didn't complain anymore. Instead, you walked back and gently grabbed his hand: "Alright, those are all unrelated matters. Let's go home first!" ”

Battat stammered, "Yes, yes, let's go home first......" He had just thought about what might happen in the future, so he couldn't help but feel embarrassed because of it. How strange. He should have thought about these things long ago. Battat couldn't help but feel a bit annoyed, or maybe he simply thought that maintaining the current state would be fine.

As long as they belong to each other, what kind of relationship doesn't seem very important. Battat couldn't help but think this way. So Battat lowered his voice: "It seems like someone is following us. Should you call the police first?" ”

You thought for a moment, gently shook your head at him, and said, "No need, I've thought of a good idea!" As you spoke, you flashed a sly smile.

“…… It's good that you have a solution. "Battat breathed a slight sigh of relief, but he still heightened his vigilance. If someone following behind tried to do something, something injure, it would be a serious problem.

You climbed the stairs of your apartment, and to let the person following you get out, you deliberately skipped the elevator today. Then, the footsteps of the followers became even more obvious. After all, your stairs are made of iron, and no matter how hard you try to hide them, it's hard to hide your footsteps on stairs like this.

At the corner, just enough to block the other person's view, you stop silently, listening to the other person's footsteps growing more urgent. Then, pulling Battat and turning around to look down, you see that fiancé is climbing the stairs.

You pouted, a bit annoyed: "Tsk, he really is a persistent ghost. Why is he so annoying?" ”

Battat also frowned and lowered his voice: "Maybe we should just call the police and have this guy arrested." ”

"But seeing his clothes getting even tattered, I'm afraid the family is on the verge of complete bankruptcy, with the whole family going to beg for debts not far off."

"What brings you here? Don't tell me you live here. The owners here can be found through the first-floor owners' register. I'm pretty sure your name isn't on the list. And don't say you're here to see some familiar person...... I know almost all those people. I don't recall anyone cooperating with your family's company; those who did stopped long ago. ”

My fiancé also stopped and stood on the stairs, revealing a look that was almost twisted, or rather, rather humble, and in short, even a bit frantic: "No, I'm here to find you." Then he suddenly sped up and charged at you. Fortunately, he didn't seem to be carrying any weapons, so you instinctively took two steps back. But the next second, you saw Battat suddenly appear from the side, blocking your way and grabbing the hand your fiancé was about to grab at you.

“…… What do you want from me? I've already told you, it's absolutely impossible." You poked your head out from behind the battat and looked at your fiancé.

"Haha...... As long as you're not married, I've always had a chance, right? Besides, being with someone like that isn't right for you at all. Marrying me is the right choice...... He looked crazed, as if he believed this reasoning was correct.

You can't help but wonder if you can send this guy to a psychiatric hospital.

If I just have my family put a bit more pressure on you, your company will go bankrupt completely, and you'll probably end up in debt. Maybe this evidence isn't much now, but by then it could be enough for you to never see the light of day...... So are you sure you want to keep this tangled up? As long as you leave now, I won't hold it against you anymore. "As you spoke, you took out the recorder and shook it, adjusting your hands and playing the recordings from the previous sessions.

Battat was stunned for a moment, instinctively turning to look at you, and you gave him a wink.

That fiancé's face turned deathly pale. He gave you one last resentful glare before hurriedly going down the stairwell.

You let out a breath. In fact, you weren't so sure. If your fiancé had gone crazy at that moment, there would have been nothing you could do. After all, you're just a wealthy young lady raised with no strength!

So you can't help but laugh out loud, and then a thought flashes through your mind, suddenly suggesting, "How about we go get our marriage certificate tomorrow?" ”

Battat hadn't fully recovered before your words snapped him out of his daze. Although he was still a bit confused, he pointed at himself and said, "Wait, what? Didn't you say there was going to be a formal proposal ceremony first?" ”
