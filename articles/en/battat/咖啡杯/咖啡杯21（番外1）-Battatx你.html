Coffee Cup 21 (Extra 1) - Battatx You

Speaking of when they first got married, Battat was a bit uncomfortable. Every day after work, he instinctively wanted to walk toward the staff dormitory. After thinking for a moment, he stopped in his tracks and remembered he should be heading in another direction—he should be going home, the home you share with him.

And every time he thinks of this concept, Battat can't help but smile.

Everyone at TV World also noticed that battat no longer lived in the staff dormitory. After all, every day after work, he was the only one who was completely different from the others! It wasn't exactly envy or jealousy, since he had gotten to know a bit before, so he just chatted a bit privately. For example, battat was beaming again today, casually showing off the ring he wore before coming over. Also, battat left a bit earlier than usual, probably to get home quickly or something.

Even though they chatted a bit privately, they were still good colleagues. Life was so boring that they had to find any topic to talk about, so the Battat incident had now become a new topic.

But Battat didn't know any of this. Even if he did, he probably wouldn't care much. He would only become more proudly showing off the ring on his hand. After all, he also wanted others to know about his situation, to know what kind of person you were to him, and how important you were to him.

On this day, Battat rarely worked overtime. He usually didn't like working overtime, since he wanted to see you early and spend more time with you. It wasn't because time spent made you more important. To him, you were his most precious treasure. He simply liked and loved the feeling of being with you, which completely relaxed his nerves and made him feel comfortable.

However, there was just too much work to handle that day. It wasn't just that he suddenly had a huge pile of work to handle; Mike's place also suddenly had a pile of urgent tasks, and they were urgent and time-limited. He couldn't even get them straight out of work to finish them and had to rush to the TV world.

By the time he tiredly walked home with his belongings, it was already late at night. Battat was unusually a bit guilty. He glanced at his phone—it was already past ten at night. He had already sent you a message when he was supposed to get off work, saying he had to work overtime today and couldn't be home on time. You replied with an OK.

And then there was nothing more, so Battat didn't know what he'd face when he got back. Would you be upset about this, would you throw a tantrum? Does he still have the energy to deal with these things? Battat was really thinking about these issues. Even though he was exhausted and wished he could collapse quickly and get a good night's sleep, he also hoped to handle the relationship well and not let you feel the slightest dissatisfaction. He hoped you could always be happy.

Until he opened the door with a nervous heart, what greeted him was not the pitch-black he had imagined. Only the moonlight outside the window was not the kind of reproachful or resentful look he might see. Instead, it was just steaming food, seemingly a bit cold on the table, not very lavish. There were two coffee cups on the table, but instead of coffee, they held milk, also steaming with steaming mist. You were lying at the other end of the table, looking like you had fallen asleep.

But when you heard the door open, you rubbed your eyes and climbed up from the table, smiling toward Battat, who had just entered but still felt a bit out of place with the warm atmosphere of the house, still carrying the humidity and coolness of the outside air: "Welcome home!" ”

Actually, when you saw him message saying he was working overtime, you guessed he probably wouldn't be eating properly anymore. You once specifically asked Battat how he handled lunch and dinner, but he stammered and said he mostly only ate a sandwich. You immediately realized how casual he was about to eat. But luckily, he still knew to eat on time and wouldn't get sick because of it...... In short, although your cooking skills aren't great and you don't like cooking, you still decided to prepare a meal for him.

So for lunch, you usually prepare it in a lunchbox: put cooked pasta, fried eggs and steak, a few cherry tomatoes on top, a slice of mint, and finally a sandwich on the other side. Occasionally, you stir-fry some seafood baked rice and add it, topped with shrimp and diced ham sausage. Or sometimes you make heart-shaped omurice, drawing a heart on top with ketchup. But you never use any spell to make it taste better. Does love really make food taste better? Actually, you're curious about this too.

As for dinner and such...... Actually, you were lazy. You just carefully prepared that meal for Battat, and your own lunch was just a casual leftover from the morning's meal. Anyway, it wasn't impossible. And dinner was always takeout you ordered. Although you bought it from outside, you still studied the plating carefully, not wanting your plating to be imperfect. This led to Battat seeing a beautifully plated dinner every day when he came home, and he seemed never to suspect anything.

Well, if you say he's skeptical, he still has a bit. Battat was just thinking, how could you have the leisure to fuss with these platings and such?

Today, you also prepared dinner for him, which might count as a late-night snack. Since tomorrow is a holiday, you can rest a bit later and get a good night's sleep tomorrow. To wait for Battat, you sat at the table the whole time, only reheating the food when it got cold, and flipping through a book in boredom. You said you were reading, but you were a bit distracted. Although Battat said you didn't need to wait for him, you had nothing else to do after working at the coffee shop, so you simply waited for him.

But you can't get through the book or think about anything else. All you have left in your mind is 'battat,' so you put down your book and lay down on the desk to fall asleep out of boredom, until you hear the door open, open your eyes, and see 'battat' coming home.

You're sure his first glance was at you, so you smiled and said welcome home, then stood up, pulled Battat down into the seat, and said, "Alright. You've worked hard all day. I guess you didn't have a proper dinner. Have something to eat, treat it as a late-night snack!" ”

Battat awkwardly scratched his head and chuckled twice. He really hadn't had a proper dinner, just a few simple bites of sandwiches to get by and then got back to work. You knew this all too well. Only now did he realize he was really starving. Although the food on the table wasn't very abundant and looked weak and not very tasty after being reheated many times, to him it was still a hearty late-night snack.

He picked up his knife and fork, ready to cut a piece of steak and put it in his mouth, but before he could do it, he couldn't help but ask, "Preparing such a lavish dinner and still waiting for me—you must be exhausted, right?" ”

You propped your chin up and watched Battat's movements. Since you had already finished eating, you couldn't help but glance at Battat in surprise: "Of course, that's because these aren't my job of making things. I'm just responsible for the plating...... You didn't even notice anything at all?" "Those were all takeout orders you ordered, but that also shows your taste in choosing restaurants is pretty good. Battat didn't even notice anything off."

Battat felt a bit embarrassed when he heard this. He scratched his cheek and thought carefully. He really didn't notice anything unusual—probably because every day was exhausting. Dinner and such, he could just take care of it casually. In fact, Battat was quite happy that you booked his lunch and dinner, since he really didn't have the energy to prepare any of this. He hadn't expected you to prepare something special for him.

Now that he heard you were only responsible for the plating, he actually wanted to say, 'You really put a lot of effort.' After a pause, he finally felt embarrassed.

So he cleared his throat twice: "Alright, I really didn't taste it. Maybe the taste doesn't really matter to me......

That's true—even though the food you prepare isn't very good, Battat still finishes it with gratitude. He's grateful for you for preparing lunch for him, and he loves you for making plans for him. Maybe love is like this—it requires both sides to give their all.

And he's not picky eater, and besides, the lunch you prepare is much more lavish than what he usually lazily eats with sandwiches.

"I knew taste wasn't that important to you. Actually, it's not that important to me either. Maybe my appetite isn't that strong......" You think for a moment and casually say, "Well, but it's actually convenient too, and takeout isn't a problem." ”

"The taste really doesn't matter, right? I'm actually curious about what kind of work you did to arrange the plates." Battat thought for a moment and decided to ask the question.

You thought carefully for a moment, then tapped your chin: "Mm...... For example, the steak you're eating now was taken out of the takeout box and placed on a plate, with my sliced cherry tomatoes and a slice of mint on it. The pasta roll next to the steak was also rolled out from another takeout box and placed beside it. ”

As you spoke, you pointed to the plate.

"Plating sounds like a very knowledgeable matter, but you really worked hard......" Battat couldn't help but sigh.

"Mm...... It doesn't matter. After all, since I'm back, I've been idle. To me, these things actually feel like cultivating my character—quite artistic. "You thought for a moment and nodded.

You really do quite enjoy this kind of time, and for you, what’s rare about this period is how very quiet it is. You don’t need to think too much; you only need to consider battat’s preferences and your own, only need to worry about how to arrange things so they look better, only need to ponder the texture of today’s food—just like tending to delicate flowers and plants. You are willing to set aside some time to think about these matters.

And now, by caring for Battat like tending to delicate plants—oh, these are living dice, not some kind of plants, and they'll probably be easy to care for, but you still hope they can live better, so you're doing these things voluntarily.

Battat hesitated for a moment, but in the end didn't dwell on the issue any further, and simply asked, "Then...... I wonder, since I came back so late today and made you wait so long, would you blame me? ”

At this point, he couldn't help but add, "Besides, I told you, you don't have to wait so long, don't wait for me. If you're tired, just rest first......"

You looked at battat in surprise and shook your head: "No, I was just waiting for you. I didn't do anything else. In fact, looking forward to your coming home is a unique experience, because I know I'll always wait for you to open the door and cross the threshold, so I'm willing to wait a little longer." ”

But as you spoke, you couldn't help but laugh again: "Besides...... How could I blame you? I won't blame you. You didn't do this willingly; you were forced to work overtime, and you're a victim. If anyone should blame you, it should be the one who made you work overtime. I wouldn't blame you over such a small matter. I considered this possibility from the very beginning when I chose you. ”

After a pause, you added, "Besides, you're tired too. If you don't want to talk, don't speak. Just finish your meal and go rest...... Oh, and you can't eat too much. What if you wake up hungry in the middle of the night?" You muttered to yourself.

Battat stared blankly at you, swallowing the rest of his words, then lowered his head to speed up his eating, swallowing the tears that were about to fall. He thought, unless it's necessary, it's better not to cry in front of you.

Although he was a bit moved by what you said, if he cried at a time like this, wouldn't that just ruin the mood......

You sat across from him, picked up a glass of milk, took a sip, and after a moment of slow thought, added, "Ah, remember to drink milk. I remember it's good for sleep, so I hope you sleep better...... Look at you, you've got dark circles under your eyes these past few days. When are you planning to change jobs?" You couldn't help but complain, but you still respect Battat's choice. If he doesn't want to switch jobs, then forget it.

Battat didn't say anything, just picked up the glass of milk and took a big gulp. After finishing this late-night snack, you quickly tidied up the dishes and planned to clean them up the next day. Then, after washing up, you changed into your pajamas and collapsed onto the bed with Battat.
