…… Customer: Am I part of your play?

Even so, when you see a new customer arrive, you instinctively smile, pick up another coffee cup, and place it on the counter to make a gesture of invitation to that customer.

"Hello, you can freely choose the coffee on the menu, and of course, you can also pick some desserts to accompany you and enjoy this leisurely time together. Sorry, someone has already chosen this cup, but if the guest wishes, we also have pure white cups to choose from. "As you spoke, you took another pure white cup from the cabinet. As for your own cup, it was still next to the coffee machine, since you wanted to make yourself a cup of coffee today.

The guest nodded, clearly very satisfied: "Then let's have this cup. Also, a latte and a ham sandwich." ”

You nod: "Okay, sir." After saying this, you take the cup and start brewing coffee. The coffee beans are still Blue Mountain Railcard, the most classic and convenient. For a latte, you need to add a bit of milk to the coffee...... After all, this is chosen because you like rich milk flavors and a mild taste. Of course, some might think the latte is too bland, but you just pour a large amount of steamed milk into the coffee, then whip some milk foam on top, and the whole cup shows a faint brown hue, carrying the sweet aroma of milk before serving it to the guest. Of course, it also comes with a fresh sandwich.

The customer nodded and accepted it, and you returned to the counter. This time, you looked a bit troubled by your blank coffee cup. Honestly, if you didn't write anything, you might get confused with the customer's cup, but if you wrote something, it might be mistaken for Battat's cup. Using other colored pens was one option, but unfortunately, there were no other colored pens in the shop. You should know, you usually only prepare one or two pens for emergencies; you're not the type to hoard colored pens.

Just as you were at a loss, you heard the wind chime ring again. Instinctively, you looked up—in fact, you hadn't expected much, since you'd been disappointed so many times over the past couple of days. But when you looked up, you couldn't help but be startled. It was none other than battat. Although his face was covered by a large number of documents, you could still tell by his figure and clothes.

Even walking was a bit unsteady, and the documents were swaying as well, looking like they might fall at any moment. I had no idea how he managed to open the café's glass door—maybe by pushing it open with his elbow? Anyway, you hurried out of the counter and went over to help him pick up some of the documents. At least now you could see the top of his head, though only a little bit of the top was visible. You hesitated, but still helped him carry the documents. This time, you got used to where he usually sits, so you walked straight to the window and put the files down.

Battat followed behind you. Because you helped him carry some of the documents, his steps were noticeably lighter than before. You could tell how hard he had been holding them all the way—after all, his legs no longer trembled, and his body no longer wobbled. Battat seemed his brain hadn't fully activated yet, or maybe he was just a bit out of the loop, just repeating instinctively, with a slightly rising tone at the end, probably puzzled: "Friend, ......?" ”

You immediately put on that somewhat hurt expression and looked at him: "What's wrong? We're not friends after all? I thought we were friends for a long time, but it turns out all of this was just my arrogance, just my one-sided wishful thinking. Battat waved his hand, trying hard to force a smile, but his expression was still very forced. It was better not to smile, since his smile was too stiff and almost intimidating, baring his teeth. Of course, you know he wanted to show a smile to comfort you, so you wouldn't feel scared.

…… All you can do is find it a bit funny that he struggles to show a desperate smile and still tries to cheer you up.

Of course, you have basic manners; you definitely won't laugh out loud at a time like this! So you just look at him seriously, waiting to see what else he can say.

"Of course that's not what I meant...... I just didn't react to it. Of course we're friends...... Battat waved his hand. Somehow, you could hear a hint of weakness in his voice. Of course, his expression was also haggard. The last time you saw him, you were fine, but this time he already had heavy dark circles under his eyes.

If only it could help him break free from this dead-like state. Unfortunately, it doesn't seem to be going well now—he probably took what he just said seriously. Hmm, maybe all serious people do this? You don't know, because it's the first time you've encountered someone like Battat.

"You looked at battat seriously, trying to persuade him.

But battat just glanced at you and turned away: "Ah, but all these paperwork are to be finished today...... That's why I planned to have a cup of coffee and then quietly finish these tasks. ”

You pouted your cheeks, a bit unhappy. Even though you knew he was here to handle these tasks and definitely wouldn't listen to you to rest, you couldn't help but complain, "What kind of boss is this? He actually assigns so much work!" If an employee died in front of him, would he compensate him? This counts as a work injury...... If you ask me, you might as well quit your job and quit. What is there to hide about that senior employee......

Of course, he lowered his voice when he said these things, since he had seen other customers inside the café. Although he probably didn't know him, it was better to be safe.

You glance at the height of his gesture, then open your mouth wide. Four-fifths of the work at his gesture is for regular employees, leaving only one-fifth......

So you can't help but mutter, "Uh, so the lives of ordinary employees aren't really their lives?" Are senior employees the truly valuable employees? ”

“…… Not really, the remaining one-fifth of the work is actually more complicated than four-fifths. "Battat rubbed his brow again, because the remaining medicine was for stage planning, writing preparatory scripts, planning the broadcast times for each scene on stage, as well as lighting and sound effects—all sorts of messy things had to be considered.

You closed your mouth and looked at him worriedly, paused, then lowered your voice and asked, "How about I help you handle part of it?" Since you said that four-fifths isn't important, does that mean it's okay to just do a little? ”

At this moment, Battat glanced at you with a hint of alarm and hurriedly shook his head: "No...... These are all my jobs. I really feel sorry for your help, and I'm afraid you won't fully understand what you're supposed to do......

You just raised your eyebrows: "Hmm, so you actually think I can get it done?" "It's fine, at worst case, just tell me exactly what you're going to do. I think it's fine. Anyway, besides sitting at the counter daydreaming, there's nothing else I can do." ”

Battat carefully examined your expression. Well, he could confirm that you were indeed saying such things, so it was unlikely he would change your decision, though it was fine.

But actually, Battat was just afraid you'd discover these two stacks of documents were exactly the same—he personally felt there was nothing to deal with, so he might as well just throw them away. But Tenna still had to check them, or else he wouldn't get paid. He was counting on saving up these salaries to treat you to a meal. As for whether Pippins would eventually hand over his salary, he wasn't sure.

So in the end, he sighed: "Alright, I understand. I'll pick out some for you later...... Alright, now, give me a cappuccino first. ”

You nodded, jumped to the other end of the counter, and started brewing a cappuccino with battat's coffee cup. This time, you added less steamed milk to balance the coffee and milk flavors, creating a smooth texture with the bitterness characteristic of coffee. You whipped some milk foam on top, then handed the cup of coffee to battat, and of course, placed a piece of tiramisu in front of him.

You said, "Hmm...... Tiramisu is the kind that mixes bitterness with sweetness, with a balanced flavor and not too sweet. I think you'll like this flavor? Anyway, I've prepared it for you. Actually, I just want to tell you that tasting sweets will lift your mood a bit. "As you spoke, you smiled and sat across from you with another cappuccino. But it was still a pure white cup before. Battat could only nod: "Yes, that's right, very smart." He looked at your proud head and couldn't help but reach out to touch it, but only after touching did he realize what he had done, so he stared blankly at his own hand.

After a while, he finally stammered, "Sorry...... Just now, I was rude. ”

"If you ask me, you're just under too much pressure. It's not even your business, but you still have to carry so many burdens...... Is it because your sense of responsibility is too strong? Now you took Battat's hand and placed it on your head. "Hmm, so this should be more stress-relieving and help you relax a bit. Let's try again?" ”

Your eyes sparkled as you looked at him with anticipation.
