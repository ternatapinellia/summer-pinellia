Coffee cup 8-Battatx you

Battatx you, OOC counts me as me

…… Feels like I could write a lot about this post, and I also tried to write about the feeling of a secret crush in this chapter (what does that mean?)

——

However...... Hearing you mention gourmet food, Battat decided to double-check, so he asked, "Hmm, are you planning to go eat something to relax first?" ”

You nodded vigorously: "Yes! I want mango brûlée, caramel pudding, chocolate brownie, chiffon cake, matcha mille crepe ......" You wanted to continue, but when you mentioned these foods, your mouth was watering. Luckily, you held it back—at least it preserved some of your image.

Battat could only helplessly press his brow: "If you order so much, you probably won't finish it." He was simply pointing out that fact. Although you probably don't want to hear this at this moment, since it sounds a bit disappointing at first.

You just stuck out your tongue. Although your mood was interrupted for a moment, you didn't seem too troubled. You just cleared your throat twice: "Oh dear...... If you can't finish it, just pack it up and put it in the fridge and continue eating tomorrow, or if you're hungry or craving, you can eat it. Besides, the snacks are stored in another stomach! Hmm, besides, you're here. If I can't finish it, I'll leave it to you! "As you spoke, you righteously patted battat on the shoulder, as if reluctantly letting it go, and even pretended to wipe away your tears.

“…… But eating dessert every meal isn't healthy. "Battat held back a bit and couldn't help but say this. Of course, he was seriously suggesting it, not deliberately disturbing your mood, just that he felt he should at least stop it at this moment to avoid binge eating.

"If it really doesn't work, you can try one this time and another ......next time," Battat couldn't help but ramble on with some suggestions. Only then did he realize you'd quieted down at some point, quickly turned to glance at your expression, and quietly breathed a sigh of relief. Luckily, you didn't look angry.

"Alright, you make a good point, so I'll reluctantly listen to that. But I'll still order two types. If I can't finish them, I'll leave them to you." You smugly waved your fingers, paused, and added another sentence.

"Yeah, but actually, it's because it keeps in the fridge and doesn't taste good......" you muttered quietly.

Battat scratched his head, a bit hesitant to ask: if you couldn't finish it for him, would you split it into two clean desserts first, or would you get stained on it...... Uh, fine, he didn't dare think any further, so he just cleared his throat and turned his head: "Alright, shall we head out now?" "I happen to have nothing else to do today." "He's been coming to see you these past few days after finishing his business. After all, he's already decided to treat you to a good meal, maybe there are other things to do too. In any case, it's best to finish things early and free up a whole period."

You thought about the question and hesitantly nodded, "Alright, let's head out now!" Hmm, but I'll treat you anyway. After all, I just want to eat, and I might have to trouble you to eat the ...... I left." You gave a couple of awkward laughs as you spoke.

After saying that, you got up to change the sign to 'close' first. Battat's eyes widened, a bit flustered. He had actually already planned to treat him, but hadn't known what reason to use before. But now, he had a ready-made one—

So he suddenly stood up and stepped in front of you. You looked at him in confusion: "Hmm, is there something else?" ”

"Uh, um, I think so. After all, I suggested it to relax and to lift your spirits, so this time I'll treat you." Otherwise, every time it's you're the one treating, he'll feel extremely embarrassed and always thinking about when he'll repay you.

You blink and shake your head sharply: "No, let me treat you. How can I let you spend money? After all, the dessert shop I'm going to is probably quite expensive." ”

Well, you really are thinking of him, and battat knows that. But he still took a deep breath, gritted his teeth, and insisted, "No, I absolutely have to treat this time. I can't just let you spend money, can I?" "He had saved up money specifically to get him back. If he couldn't repay this time and had to wait until next time, wouldn't that be a long-lasting entanglement?"

…… While maintaining this connection is fine, Battat always feels a faint sense of guilt and guilt because of it.

You muttered, "It's not like I'm the only one paying. Anyway, whatever, this time I'll pay. Let's just say it like this!" After saying that, you didn't care what Battat wanted to say and walked straight past him toward the shop entrance. Battat watched you walk away, but just as he reached out to grab your arm, he hesitated for a moment and let go, just following your footsteps.

You closed the café, wiped the nonexistent sweat from your forehead, then turned to battat: "Alright, let's go to the dessert shop. I've been eyeing their desserts for a long time, but I was busy opening the café back then and didn't even have time to try them. This time, I finally got the chance!" "As you spoke, you clapped your hands happily and started leading the way ahead of your own pace."

This was the last time battat had sighed since meeting you. Yet, for some reason, the corners of his mouth lifted slightly. When he belatedly realized this, he hurriedly reached out to try to pull the smile down, secretly relieved that you hadn't noticed anything. After all, since you were walking ahead, you naturally couldn't see what was happening to him.

Before long, you arrived at the dessert shop you mentioned. The decor itself exudes a warm and sweet feeling. After all, not every dessert shop cleverly writes its name in creamy font, decorates the interior with various creams and carvings, and even the top of the fork is shaped like a small candy.

You walked up to the counter and happily ordered two desserts. In the end, you chose the caramel pudding and chocolate brownie, since to you, it wasn't too sweet. You were still thinking about the battat—though for you, it wasn't too sweet, and you weren't sure how it would be for the battat.

This shop uses a prepayment system, so after ordering dessert, you have to take out dark coins to pay. Battat was still daydreaming, but when he saw you take out dark coins, he shuddered and quickly took out his dark coins, almost simultaneously handing them to the waiter.

"Sorry...... Uh, anyway, please accept the price for these two desserts. "Battat said dryly.

You widened your eyes, glared blatantly at battat, then looked at the waiter and handed your own dark coin forward: "Please accept mine! Don't mind the ...... that guy handed you over."

Battat looked away, pretending not to notice your gaze, but insisted on pushing the dark coin forward: "Please accept mine." ”

The waiter glanced at the two of you. To be honest, she felt a bit troubled, unsure which one to accept, though whichever one you took would only lead to an argument after you went down...... Since you took out the dark coins first, she hesitated for a moment and still accepted them.

You raised your eyebrows and made a 'yay' in your mind, then looked at battat and whispered a complaint, "Why are you fighting over paying? Didn't you say I'd pay for this meal?" ”

Battat actually felt a bit regretful. He put away the money, his gaze wandering, not daring to look at you, and simply said softly, "I just think...... Since I want you to feel better, it's necessary to pay for you. After all, I want you to relax, and it's my responsibility to make you happy." "It's not just because he wants to repay the money you paid before, nor because of the guilt and guilt—he sincerely hopes you'll be happy."

…… I hope you can truly smile from the heart.

You were stunned for a moment, then instinctively scratched your cheek, gave a couple of awkward laughs, and for some reason your face flushed a little, feeling as if the temperature on your face was on fire...... Ah, I just feel something isn't right! You know this is a reaction called shyness, and for some reason, your heart skipped a beat just now, as if it was beating for the battat in front of you. Immediately after, it was a pounding sound like a drumbeat, which probably won't stop for a while.

In short, you hope Battat didn't see you blushing, and luckily he turned his head at that moment.

After a moment of silence between you two, you quietly said, "...... It's nothing, but maybe next time you'll treat us even better. ”

You also want to be willful once, without worrying about anything or overthinking, just entrust your heart to the other person, trust that they can protect it, and you can simply enjoy happiness and let your emotions flow freely. But now, probably still can't do that, right? In short, you have just quietly buried this fluttering feeling deep inside.

"It's settled then. I'll treat you to dinner. Anyway, don't overthink things today—just relax." Battat said this quickly, as if afraid you'd change your mind. You couldn't help but chuckle, feeling relieved that this ease had actually faded the flush from your cheeks. After all, just then, Battat turned to look at you.

"Alright, no problem! Let's find a place to sit first. We can't just stand here like a block of wood forever." ”

You sat down by the window, and soon two desserts were brought over on plates. The chocolate brownies were topped with what looked like freshly made chocolate liquid, slightly cooled and solidified, yet still soft and liquid. The caramel pudding swayed with the shaking of the plate.

You handed a fork to Battat, hesitated for a moment, put your hands together and announced, "I want to eat pudding first!" ”

Battat nodded. He actually had no objections to your choice, but he still sat still, just watching you enjoy the dessert, since we had agreed to eat it first.

Instead, after a few bites, you looked up at battat in confusion: "What's wrong? Why are you so still? It's actually pretty awkward that I'm the only one eating......

He cleared his throat twice: "I'm waiting for you to finish eating......

He wanted to say more, but you just waved your hand nonchalantly to interrupt him: "It's okay, let's eat together. I don't mind. Anyway, two servings will definitely fill me up, and who knows if I won't finish it all, especially since I had macarons and stuff before...... Anyway, let's finish this pudding first!" ”

Battat hesitated for a moment, swallowed hard, and honestly felt a bit nervous, so it wouldn't matter if he dropped the word...... After all, if he dropped his spoon, it might end up where you ate. Is it really okay for two people to share the same pastry together? Only then did he belatedly recall the tiramisu from last time, but that time it was spaced apart because of the size of the cake, and the tiramisu wasn't finished in the end.

But seeing your completely unconcerned expression, he just feels a bit empty inside, or maybe a bittersweet feeling? Maybe he's just overthinking it, but maybe you simply don't care about it at all. Maybe today you're willing to share the same cake with him, and tomorrow you'd rather share the same dish with someone else. No, it's better not to torment your heart with these wild thoughts. He lets go of those thoughts and tries to focus his attention on the pudding in front of him, trying to focus only on this particular dish.

He raised his spoon, and to be honest, he ate the pudding without knowing how to taste it. But there was still a faint sweet and greasy taste lingering in his mouth, accompanied by a bit of caramel's unique burning pain and sweet flavor. You glanced worriedly at Battat, not sure why he was distracted again. But right now, your main focus was on other questions, so you asked, "Hmm...... How does it taste? I mean, it doesn't taste too sweet, right? ”

Only then did Battat snap back to reality, nodding nervously and shaking his head: "Ah, I can accept it. I mean, it's not too sweet. Anyway, it's still delicious......" As he said this, he picked up a spoon and scooped a bite. For some reason, that cloying sweetness stirred ripples in his heart every time he saw you. But within that sweet taste, there was a hint of sourness. Pudding shouldn't have this flavor, so why could he sense it?

In the end, you finished the pudding, and Battat gave up struggling, though he still felt a faint sense of anticipation and a hint of happiness inside...... When you shared the pudding, it was as if your souls merged at that moment, swapping your understanding—after all, when you were eating, you were already in the same state.

But you still seem unfazed, so Battat can only finish the remaining chocolate brownie with you, both hopeful and disappointed.
