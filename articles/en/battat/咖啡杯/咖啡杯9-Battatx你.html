Coffee cup 9-Battatx you

Battatx, you ooc belong to me, some of which are just my imagination and fabrication because I don't know either......

——

After finishing the brownie, you leaned back and sat down in your chair. You didn't rush to get up, just squinted slightly, as if you were satisfied. You put down your spoon, leaving only an empty plate in front of you. Then you looked at battat, who was sitting across from you. He seemed to be hesitating about what to say. After all, you saw his mouth opening and closing for a long time, and his face alternating between pale and red, making you curious about what he was planning to say.

So you sat up a bit straighter, resting your hand on the table with your chin on your hand, staring at Battat. Probably because you were staring at him intently, Battat's face blushed even more and he stammered, not knowing what to say.

In fact, he was indeed hesitating. He didn't know why he was considering so much now. He hadn't thought so much when he first made the suggestion, but now he had so many more concerns. Maybe he suddenly realized that he had always cared about your feelings? That's why he considered whether the plan was good or bad—after all, he was afraid you might not like it.

Although maybe you'll still agree with his proposal with a smile and approval, the more you do it, the more nervous he becomes, and the more he'll think about what you'd like more. That's when Battat realizes he doesn't really know your preferences that well, but he doesn't want to directly ask what you'd like. Maybe...... Should I hint a little?

Actually, he felt it was fine to keep sitting here daydreaming. Even though the environment was so noisy and irritating, in the moment you watched him, in the moment he saw you, he felt quiet and couldn't hear anything else. So quiet that all he could hear was his heartbeat.

But Battat still felt maybe he should go out for a walk and explore other places. After all, sitting here all the time might not seem a big deal, but it didn't quite match the original purpose of relaxing.

But when you watched him, all those thoughts melted away, leaving him unable to keep up and not even knowing what he was thinking. Battat was now so focused on his mind as if his mind was short-circuiting, avoiding your gaze—no, maybe nowhere to hide, since he was sitting right across from you.

And that was all he had been thinking about earlier. After struggling for a long time, he still couldn't come up with a conclusion; both answers and reasons were locked in his mind.

And finally, you couldn't help but speak. When you stared at battat for who knows how many seconds—maybe it was the ninety-second longing? You didn't really care about the passage of time. After all, you were willing to waste this time just sitting here with battat, even if it was just sitting there, even though you usually cared a lot about time and task planning.

“…… So, what exactly do you want to say to me? "You gently tapped the table with your knuckles and asked.

"Ah...... Ah, it's nothing, I just think we should go for a walk together, or maybe somewhere else too? Or maybe you want to keep sitting here? I just don't know where you'd prefer to go. "Battat stammered out this sentence, turning his face slightly. He knew the heat on his face hadn't died down yet, and you must have seen it clearly. But now, he was a bit ready to give up—if you saw him, so be it." He just scratched his head, pretending to be busy.

You tilt your head slightly, and you notice his face is redder, so you turn your face slightly and scratch your ear, knowing your ear is a bit hot now.

You just don't answer and ask back: "Then...... Do you want to go for a walk more? ”

Battat quickly waved his hand: "No, no, no, I'm fine. It depends on your mood. Anywhere is fine, as long as you like it." But at this moment, he didn't dare look up at you again, and naturally missed the red on your earlobe.

“…… Hmm, then let's go for a walk. We can chat a bit and such, which is better than going somewhere else. Right now, I just want to casually talk about painting and be with you. Ah, if you don't like chatting, then just quietly take a walk. "So you smiled and said, watching battat lower his head, feeling a bit itchy to get your hands moving, but in the end didn't dare to reach out. He just pulled his outstretched hand back, pretending he had something to do, then touched his nose.

"Ah, no, if it's just to chat, it's fine. I'm willing to chat with you...... No, that's not what I mean. Actually, I'm willing to chat with you no matter when. "Battat quickly waved his hand again, still not daring to look up at you. He didn't want you to see the expression on his face. After all, oh my god, he had no idea what the situation would be like. He just felt his face burning hot, and he didn't know what he was saying—maybe it was the truth, or maybe it was just nonsense.

And indeed, even if he might be tired and doesn't want to reply to anyone, he'll still be willing to chat with you. Perhaps through this conversation, he regains his energy and feels happiness and happiness.

…… Not at that coffee shop, just by your side, he feels relaxed.

You blink, looking at his still bowed head, then touch your nose, shift your gaze slightly, and cough twice: "Ah, then let's go for a walk. Say something casually, or if you don't want to say anything, just walk quietly." After saying this, you quickly got up.

Battat quickly got up too. At least his face wasn't so hot now, and he finally wanted to look up at you, catch your back, and follow behind you as you walked out.

You walked down the street, and after thinking for a moment, you suggested, "There's a garden nearby...... How about we take a walk over there? Anyway, that park has always been open to the public for free, and if you walk a bit further, you can even see the sea." "Honestly, you rarely go to see the sea. After all, where you live and the café are both quite far from the ocean. It's just that today you have some free time, and your mood is relaxed. Maybe going to see the sea is a good choice."

And you have a bit of selfishness—your mood is excited but also a bit nervous. You know Battat will probably agree, but what if? What if he refuses? Although you can still force a smile and make other suggestions, the disappointment at that moment will probably linger for a long time. In short, well, you want to go see the sea with Battat, or just take a walk in a quiet spot, just the two of you, and then you can ask whatever questions you want, because you want to get to know Battat better.

…… You also want to know his life, his habits, his hobbies—more and more, just out of curiosity or some special feeling. You can't say for sure, you just desperately hope for it.

Battat nodded matter-of-factly: "Ah, of course. If you want to go, let's go together. Anywhere is fine, and if you want to visit several places, that's fine too." He had no objections to this, and even hoped that this kind of time could last longer, or that time would pass a little more slowly. He hoped to watch over you longer, or maybe not at all, just knowing you were still by his side.

You relaxed and, with your obvious answer, headed to the park with Battat. There really was plenty of shaded space. Although it was loose, it was still a nice sight. The buildings on both sides were all vintage-style. If you wanted to take some photos here as souvenirs, it would be a nice atmosphere. Unfortunately, you're not someone who likes taking photos, and there was no one else in the garden at the moment. You didn't bring any professional camera equipment; the bag you took out only had an emergency water bottle, an umbrella, and a phone.

Unfortunately, you can't find anyone else to take photos of you both, so these tempting thoughts can only be put to rest.

Battat seemed to be lost in thought. You glanced at him quietly, waved your hand in front of him, and only then did he snap back to reality, rubbing his brow: "Ah...... I was distracted! Did you just say something? ”

You shook your head: "No, actually, I was just about to say something. I just saw you lost in thought, so I wondered if I could call your lost god back." As you spoke, you secretly chuckled a couple of times.

Battat felt a bit embarrassed and scratched his head: "Ah...... Ah! Go ahead, I'm listening! I'll definitely listen to you seriously. "He assured him, but also felt a bit guilty. Maybe it was because he was walking beside you that he couldn't help but zone out, because at this moment he didn't need to care so much, so it was fine to just think about something, because he knew you were right beside him. It was a kind of trust that was indescribable and even he couldn't understand.

"Ahem, then I'll say it. Actually, I wanted to ask you a few questions...... I want to know, besides work, what do you usually do after work?" You cleared your throat and asked solemnly.

Who would have thought battat's expression would go blank at that moment? You can't help but turn your head to glance at him a couple more times.

“…… It's nothing special, right? Usually, it's just work. Sometimes, after work, there might be some work to handle, so I have to work overtime. The little time left is mostly for rest, and nothing else to do. I'll just be spending some time at the coffee shop you run recently. "As he spoke, he couldn't help but cough lightly twice, and his voice grew quieter by the end. If you hadn't listened carefully to his answer, you might have missed that sentence.

You tilted your head slightly, knowing your cheeks were a bit hot at that moment. Then you scratched your cheek, coughed lightly, and couldn't help but ask, "It sounds tough usually...... Is it that your relationship with colleagues isn't great?" ”

Battat waved his hand: "I get along pretty well with my colleagues! Besides, there are other colleagues who occasionally help me share some of my workload, but...... After all, sometimes work just can't be finished. There's nothing I can do. ”

You nodded knowingly, and after a moment of silence between us, you spoke in a hesitant, surprised voice: "So, do you mean you can't do anything else besides that?" You must have some kind of hobby...... you muttered.

“…… Isn't there really not much time? The remaining occasional free time is also used to sort out problems...... "Battat continued to answer quietly.

You didn't ask what exactly he was sorting out, since his expression looked troubled and probably didn't like others asking him, so you just quietly waited for him to nag.

"However...... As for other things, I do collect some things occasionally, even if I might not need them. Besides that, I organize the items in the room, since things get messy every so often. I feel uncomfortable just looking at those messy facilities...... Battat said this while frowning, probably trying hard to recall something, so much so that his tone became unclear. It was obvious he thought for a while before giving a broken answer. Maybe it was simply because he had never paid attention to these things and didn't care much about his own life, so he didn't remember it either.

After all, caring about his own life was useless, and no one would pay attention to this matter. He just hadn't expected you to ask about it.

"Ah...... Do you have a collecting habit? If that's the case, do you need to buy a lot of storage equipment or something? "You asked casually. The shaded area was gradually left behind, and not far ahead was the ocean you mentioned. Above the ocean, on the ground you were stepping on, a path stretched out, crooked and crooked. In short, there was still some distance from the sea surface, so it was impossible to get close to the ocean.

"Not really, I just collect some small items occasionally? It seems like I only did this a long time ago. Now, because I'm too busy, I actually don't have much time." Battat sighed as he spoke.

"Oh...... Speaking of which, you don't really like sweet food? What about spicy food? Do you know how to eat it? Because I'm thinking about whether to go out for extra spicy ramen tonight, I wanted to ask for your opinion. "You clasp your hands behind your back, as if you're just asking casually, but in fact, your hands behind your back are tangled and picking at your fingers, sweating from nervousness."

“…… I can't handle spicy food; I can eat a little bit of other flavors, but it seems like I can't eat anything spicy, including drinks and such. I always get my face off with just one bite." Battat didn't hesitate much and quickly answered. Of course, he didn't think too much about what you meant based on your words.

You nod gently, just about to say something more when suddenly raindrops hit your face. When you look up, you realize the sky had turned gloomy at some point. At first, just a few wet raindrops hit the ground, wetting a patch of it. Then, the raindrops started to fall with a splash, growing louder, about to soak you both.
