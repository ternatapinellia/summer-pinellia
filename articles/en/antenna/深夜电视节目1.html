Late Night TV Show 1

Tennax, you, hmm, all character settings belong to me (? ）

With the Persona 4 fusion setting, it's okay if you don't know Persona 4! I'm just a pure Photoshop fan.

——

"At midnight on a rainy night, looking at my own face on the unturned TV, I would see another person's image." It is said that this is what the future lover looks like.

…… This is the rumor that has been making waves in the class lately. Although I don't believe it, it's been raining continuously these past few days, and my curiosity keeps growing. I always feel that even if I give it a try, it's no big deal—just satisfying my curiosity and thirst for knowledge. Even if I remain ignorant all this time, it's not a bad thing—but some questions always attract people to dig deeper.

In short, unfortunately, you really are a very curious guy.

Outside, a muffled thunderclap had been heard and the heavy rain was sparse. Raindrops from the eaves kept hitting the uneven ground, gathering into puddles. Perhaps in such weather, turning on the TV and then napping to the sound of the program was the best choice. But at this moment, you were just staring blankly at your own face reflected on the screen. In the pitch-black night, the second hand ticked, approaching midnight.

When the second hand hits exactly the number 12, you see a horizontal line on the TV screen that is tuned for FM to flash and light up, reflecting the image in your pupils. You widen your eyes, surprised that the rumor about late-night TV is actually true. You're curious about who will appear next, and you don't want to miss a single thing.

—So what is this? You fall silent as you watch an ad on the screen, accompanied by inexplicably explosive music. What appears to be a ...... The shadow of a TV person? Could it be some kind of headgear...... At the end, a scene like 'Welcome to Mr. (Ant)tenna' TV show was played.

It seemed like there wasn't much to see. You rubbed your temples, which were aching from staying up late, turned and went back to sleep, naturally missing the next scene. After the commercial ended, a scene of a female student appeared, then the TV screen closed again, plunging into a pitch-black silence.

The next morning, there was no rain, and since it was a rare holiday, you just zoned out. After all, for some reason, the fog in town had been getting heavier lately. Although it didn't really affect travel...... But it was still inconvenient. After an ordinary day, it started raining again at night.

Anyway, it's boring, and it's raining again. Should I watch some late-night TV? You hesitate for a moment, then decide to sit in the living room with your pillow hugging your pillow, staring eagerly at the pitch-black TV screen. Your face is reflected on the screen, the second hand hits midnight again, and it's the same ad as yesterday. You're a bit disappointed.

But you still insisted on watching it today. You're very persistent. What if the commercial is just an appetizer for some show preview, and the main episode actually comes later?

Unfortunately, after this commercial ended, the TV screen returned to pitch black, only reflecting your face. The lightning flashed by, making your face look deathly pale.

You scratched your head in frustration, not even knowing why it was happening. You felt frustrated, and probably subconsciously, felt it shouldn't be like this. So you took two steps forward, squatted down, and stared blankly at your face on the TV screen. As you looked, you noticed the screen had turned into a vortex—could it be malfunctioning?

You instinctively reach out your hand, but it passes straight through the TV screen and enters the whirlpool. Then comes an irresistible suction, as if trying to drag you in completely. You widen your eyes and try to pull your hand back—but unfortunately, you don't have enough strength and end up being dragged in.

When you cover your head dizzy and open your eyes, you find yourself sitting on the sofa, holding a controller, with a TV viewer sitting next to you, excitedly talking about something.

Oh, there's someone next to me...... Wait, TV person?? You instinctively tilt your head and look at him, confirming that this is the same TV person you saw in the commercial—probably, definitely.

Calm down and think about it: you crossed over to this world because you touched the TV. You can be sure this isn't your original world—after all, how could a TV person really appear? Haha. Is heaven punishing you for not sleeping properly to show up on late-night TV? That's not scientific!

So if he touched the TV screen again, he should be able to go back, and there happened to be a TV next to him...... Uh, it was the TV person, but he looked quite tall, so he probably had to climb up himself......

While you were thinking, the game character was, of course, AFK and untouched, and that TV person—probably a host—saw you stunned for a long moment without moving, hesitantly asking, "Uh...... My dear star, is there something wrong? Are you hungry or thirsty? Here is fragrant cake and drinks—everything you want—or if you need something else—"

Before he could finish, you dropped the controller and looked serious: "Wait a moment, I need to confirm something." ”

The TV quietly shut up, instinctively lifting the corners of its mouth high as if guilty. If it had eyes, it would probably be blinking wildly too. Unfortunately, it has no eyes, so you have no idea which way it's looking.

And you didn't care about his slightly guilty expression at this moment. You just tugged at his clothes to make sure they were still sturdy, then pulled his clothes up and tapped the TV screen with your hand.

In that moment, his screen turned completely black, and his nose retracted—or disappeared? You don't know, all you know is the hard TV screen beneath your hand, because after a couple of taps, it made a dull sound, nowhere near the alluring vortex you had when you arrived.

It means you definitely can't get back through this TV screen. You're a bit disappointed, but you didn't have high expectations at first. After all, if it were that simple, you probably wouldn't even be here anymore.

The TV crew's voice was stammering, tinged with a hint of panic, but they still tried their best to act as if nothing was happening, even speeding up their speech: "Oh—my little star, did you run into some kind of little furry problem? Some annoying but not big problems? It's okay, maybe you'll find the answer on TV. Trust me, TV has everything you want, and the show you want will be broadcast on TV—"

He was still muttering, "You think this guy talks a lot—judging by the ads you saw before, maybe this guy's name is Tenna?"

Anyway, you politely asked, "Mr. Tenna, I'm going to start playing this game. Could you please be quiet for a while?" ”

"Oh...... Uh, you actually know my name? Ah, I haven't even introduced you to what you need to do in the game yet—"

You interrupted him again: "None of that matters. Besides, I want to try it myself. Maybe I can figure it out on my own." ”

Tenna didn't dare make a sound. Anyway, he quieted down, probably staring intently at the screen in front of him. You didn't really mind.

You just feel as if a raging war is raging! In short, just treat this place as an RPG or adventure story game—just clear it and then reach the next scene!

In short, your expression becomes serious and you start to immerse yourself in the game, since those who want to win never have a smile on their faces.

For a small town lacking entertainment, this game is still eye-opening. At least it incorporates fighting and puzzle elements. Although the puzzle parts are simple, most of it is just Q&A.

In short, you quickly collect the first key, and with two left, you can open the door, something like that.

Tenna couldn't sit still and started applauding enthusiastically: "Look at our star, these problems are really easy for her!" She collected the first key so quickly—after that, I doubt anything will trouble you! ”

You coughed twice, touched your nose, and slightly looked away. Although it still felt a bit noisy, you couldn't really say anything when people complimented you. To be honest, you felt a bit embarrassed. At the same time, Tenna was praising you from the side, making you look like a peerless genius or a one-of-a-kind immortal with extraordinary abilities, which made you feel a bit embarrassed.

If you keep talking, it will get outrageous. Anyway, you cough heavily twice: "Alright, Mr. Tenna, I know I'm very strong, you don't need to say any more." ”

…… As for the previous ones, you just can't accept them without a conscience.

"Oh, anyway, you're amazing—no wonder you're my star." After saying this, he fell silent again.

You might actually be a bit curious why he keeps calling you that—but, well, so what if you know the answer? You also know, none of this is real. Maybe the happiness of this moment is real.

Because you really are happy now—in other words, you're feeling a bit carried away.

However, the next key is a bit trickier. You control your character to the front door, and opening it will probably reveal the key—though you don't know why the key is inside, probably for the sake of a puzzle.

There is a Huarong Road puzzle on this door. It takes you some effort to retrieve the key containing the door from the only exit, open the door, and then obtain the second key.

"Oh my god, you solved this puzzle in just a short time! It actually took me half an hour to solve this puzzle I had carefully designed!" Tenna's antenna twitched back and forth. I don't know if it's just your imagination or if he's just too excited, but you think he looks a bit higher than before...... A bit taller?

"Ahem, it's nothing to worry about." Honestly, if I keep praising you endlessly, you'll feel really embarrassed......

But you don't know what to say to stop him, so fine, just pretend you didn't hear him!

Ignoring your excitement, you're heading toward the next location. Honestly, you feel like this game is packed with a lot of content; otherwise, why would the next scene be a book, and you have to collect all sorts of materials to forge a key!

…… You suspect you've been set up by this game. It's a little trick to extend playtime and prevent refunds—definitely true. You glance at Tenna out of the corner of your eye. Unfortunately, you can't see anything—after all, he has no eyes and always wears the same smile.

Well, for some reason, even though you've probably arrived in another world, you don't feel sleepy, so just hurry up and finish it!

The game character runs toward the first collectible. This is fine—just help with the gathering task, and the NPC will give you the corresponding item, even though this NPC looks like just a bouncing dice.

The first collectible is in the backpack. When you get to the second item, the NPC regretfully tells you that this item is currently unavailable and may need to help him collect something first, then make another one.

So after collecting, is it still collecting? You sneered. Although you were holding back your anger, you still worked hard and went to collect. Fortunately, there were no more tasks this time, so you just had to run around and get the items properly.

With that, you can get the second collectible smoothly. The NPC happily gives you a saxophone segment, but as for collecting other items...... Actually, Tenna translated it for you.

He was probably watching your expression cautiously, while trying to keep his voice high: "My dear star, it's a pity he doesn't have any materials left! Maybe you'll need to find all the materials elsewhere first......"

In short, you didn't answer that question, just puffed out your cheeks and controlled the game characters.

The third collectible, which is the last one, is in a secret room, so at least you don't have to run errands for NPCs. But—why?! The door to this secret room is a rotating puzzle. Once you solve it, the next door is a piece-together puzzle, which isn't difficult. The next door is actually a pure white puzzle?

You have reason to suspect there are more puzzles ahead—not endless, but definitely draining your patience and energy.

You put down your game controller and sighed, "I'm a bit tired. Can I stop playing for now?" I want to rest for a while. ”

"Oh what—you don't want to play anymore? Is the game not fun? Should I switch to a different one for you? Or should I play a different show for a while? Anything is fine—" The antenna bounced again, and you thought it was actually pretty fun, like something startled and startled.

"No—I mean, I don't want anything, I just want to be alone, do nothing, find a sofa quietly? Or a bed? Lie down for a while." "You dragged out the sound, emphasized the pitch, emphasized those few words, you believe he definitely heard it, it was clear and clear."

He was quiet for a moment, then spoke rapidly: "Oh, sure, can I do something for you? As long as I can prepare anything, I've got it on my side. Just make a suggestion—"

You sighed, but patiently repeated, "I don't want anything." ”

"And, I don't know how I came to this world...... Playing games is fun, but I still have to go back to class...... You scratch your head in frustration.

Tenna's screen turned black again. This time, you couldn't see anything, but his voice sounded like he was still laughing, or maybe ...... A bit hysterical?

"You're leaving here? You just need to keep watching TV shows here and keep laughing—are you happy in that world? Isn't it better to stay here? ”

His hand gripped your shoulder tightly. Honestly, you didn't feel well, and you instinctively pressed down on that black screen.

Then you widen your eyes because you see very clearly that on the black screen, the area your hand touches has the same vortex, just like when you arrived. The vortex has the same attraction, pulling you in. You think, maybe through this vortex you can go back.

Tenna seemed stunned too, his hand instinctively loosened, and you gritted your teeth, pulled his hand away, and without hesitation, reached your other hand into the whirlpool.

Before leaving, you only had time to say one more thing: "I am indeed very happy, but—"

Before he could finish, the world spun around—he went back and sat down on the ground.

You press your temples and realize it's only been ten minutes—even though you feel like you've been in there for at least an hour or two.

Staring at the pitch-black TV screen, you still feel shaken. No matter what, you hurry back to your room and sleep.

Of course, the next day you go to class drowsy, and they seem to be chatting about the girl who appeared on late-night TV a couple of days ago, who seems to be a senior from the same school.

Then why is it that what you see is a TV commercial? You just can't figure this out.

Of course, you don't know that the TV commercial actually covered up the murder trailer.

After class, luckily, the high school course wasn't very long. You were about to go to Junis's store to buy something to cook. Just as you were picking out cabbage, you could see boys in the same class whispering something. In front of them was a row of large LCD TVs—after all, Junis was considered one of the town's big stores.

Even though there was no one else around, you still saw them dive into the TV, and the shape of the vortex was exactly the same as the one you'd encountered before.

After hesitating for a moment, you decide to check on TV. You also want to figure out what's really going on.

You found a TV in a different direction, just enough to block your view, and slipped in—it seemed to be the scene of the town, shrouded in white mist, nothing like what you saw that day.

If the TV comes in, you can get out relying on it, right? Turning around, you see three stacked TVs there, all old models. For some reason, you think of the TV at home and the Tenna from last night.

Maybe this isn't a dream, you think.

But when you get home and look at that TV, you hesitate for a moment, just wipe off the dust as usual, and pretend nothing happened.

What exactly are you thinking? You don't know either.
