Collar 19 (tennax you)

This story features a more S personality and a more M tenna. Some chapters also contain R18 content.

——

The next morning, you smell the aroma of breakfast—it's the one Tenna prepared for you. You get up, get dressed, and sit at the dining table, waiting for Tenna to bring the breakfast. He brings the prepared breakfast to the table, still wearing an apron, then leans on the TV, his nose still blooming, sitting across from you, looking at you with anticipation.

As you ate bread spread with blueberry jam, you said slowly, "After breakfast today, take me to the company with you." "As you spoke, you picked up another piece of bacon and put it in your mouth."

Tenna seemed even more excited. He leaned his head forward a bit and asked mournfully, "How is it...... How do we get there? ”

You glanced at him, not understanding why he could ask such a silly question, but you still kindly answered: "How you usually take me away, today you just take me there. Just carry me and leave." ”

Tenna quickly nodded. I wasn't sure if it was just your imagination, but you always felt like his screen suddenly seemed to brighten.

After you finished breakfast, Tenna waited at the door, half-squatting down, arms resting there, waiting for you to sit in his arms. You didn't hold back, straddled his arm, wrapped your arms around his neck, and sat on his arm. Then you watched Tenna lift his head to the TV, happily leading you out, your nose brimming with flowers. He wished everyone could see you two in the same state right now. And you just closed your eyes slightly, leaned against his screen, counting his steps, and only opened them when he was almost at the company entrance.

At that moment, Tenna was holding you hesitantly. You were at a corner, and the company's Dark People couldn't see you. Tenna quietly asked, "I...... Should I put you down here?" Although he asked that, he was still a bit reluctant. He didn't want to put you down, but wanted to show everyone that this was their relationship. He wanted everyone to know that you were a perfect couple.

You tapped his screen and said casually, "Just take me over directly." After all, the receptionist knows you're my husband. "So, it's okay to do this. You just hid a possibility and didn't tell Tenna—those Dark People all know Tenna is now your husband, and Tenna was their former boss. It's inevitable that once they learn Tenna got a job, they won't squeeze him out, thinking he's only staying here because of connections. However...... You also hope he can handle these troubles himself.

Either he stays by your side, just being a loyal dog. Although a bit mean and a bit foolish, this is a well-behaved and well-suited dog, or ...... Tenna is either able to accomplish something on his own and do something, proving that he is valuable. That way, even if he feels unwilling, he will still let the dog go out. However, if he likes the life he once had...... Then he will come back. If he doesn't like it and just walks away, then you'd rather get a new dog.

Even if you thought that way, you didn't tell Tenna a word, just waited for him to carry you to the company entrance. All the Dark People in the lobby looked at you with strange yet somewhat admiring eyes. Tenna carried you up the elevator, lifting his head proudly, because now the Dark People at your company probably knew the news, knew the sovereignty he had declared, and also understood the relationship between them.

Tenna walked into your office, put you down, and turned to leave. You flipped through the documents on your desk, then called out to Tenna: "Wait." ”

Tenna happily turned her head again, her nose blooming with flowers. He thought you had some sweet words to talk to him.

And you only handed Tenna a document: "This is a proposal. Your job is to revise and polish it, and to develop a plan to promote our company's new programs and productions." You said calmly. You watched as Tenna's screen went dark. He timidly took the proposal, then left your office and closed the door.

You calmly started handling the documents handed over by other employees, since you can't do nothing—you're very busy. You nod as Zapper revised the submitted proposal again. You agreed the script should have broadcast value, but still needed to be handed over to the project team evaluating the TV drama for re-review. So you put the file on the pending side. You looked at the document Pippins submitted. They proposed opening a casino in a rented shop near the company. You thought it was probably for their own use, and since casinos are a gray industry and not good for the company's future, you directly vetoed the proposal. Finally, there's the document Shadow Guy handed you over. This document mainly concerns the new show's plan, attaching a temporary stage venue ticketing map, a layout map, and a corresponding emergency response plan. You nodded and thought the plan was feasible, so you approved it. Finally, you took the proposal Zapper presented to you, made some suggestions and annotations regarding the broadcast of the TV drama, and finally called Battat. Your voice was calm: "Ah, there's a TV drama planning proposal here that needs your review. Come to my office and take this proposal now." ”

Soon, your door was knocked. You called out and invited in, watching as Battat, wearing a microphone helmet and carefully entering your office, bowed in greeting and then took away the proposal.

You look at Battat and sigh softly: "I've said it many times, I know you guys, you don't have to dress like this." ”

Battat hesitantly replied yes, then closed the door and walked out. You glanced at the time—it was time for the morning meeting. So you turned on your computer and started a video conference. Except for Tenna, who just took office today, everyone else participated.

You cleared your throat twice and started by explaining something: "Today, Mr. Tenna officially joined our company and will take on the role of content planner." You announced this calmly, and as you looked at the countless faces in the video conference, they were indeed starting to get anxious.

One of the zappes saluted you: "Boss, Mr. Tenna does mean something different to us. After all, it's because of him that we can survive in this dark world. But I don't think what Mr. Tenna is good at is suitable for our company......

An pippins also started to interrupt: 'Yeah, boss, could it be that guy only got in because of your connections? Can he really achieve anything?'

Shadow guy couldn't speak, just played a little tune on the saxophone, agreeing with Zapper and Pippins.

You cleared your throat: "First of all, I did hire Tenna partly because he was once the lord of this dark world, and because of his potential abilities. Second, his joining this company has nothing to do with me. I assigned him a project, and as long as he can actually do it well, it proves he has the strength to stay here. So...... There's no need for you to rush to mock him. "You said softly.

You know what these employees will probably do, so you hold a meeting at this time to remind them not to say anything randomly. Some employees probably understand, while others seem half-aware. You can only say this much; the rest depends on their choice and Tenna's choice. You simply announce the meeting is over, then close the video call.

The morning's work was almost done. You glanced at the time, used your personal phone to call Tenna, and called him up. Tenna came to the ground in high spirits. He thought you were going to give him something, but you just handed him a contract, your voice cold and indifferent: "This is the contract I drafted this morning. Take a look and see if there's anything wrong. I'm hiring you. You're working part-time, just come to the company for 24 hours a week, and the pay is clearly stated, along with vacation benefits and so on. I hope you'll accept it." "You rarely spoke so long to Tenna. Tenna stayed silent, took your contract, and started flipping through it. He had to say the benefits listed in the contract were very good, much better than the ones he had previously hired. In the end, he nodded and signed his name on the contract.

So you signed your name on it too. The contract took effect, and you put it away in a drawer. Then, resting your chin on your hand, you looked at Tenna, who was standing in front of you, a bit timid, and softly asked, "So...... Do you have any thoughts on the planning proposal I gave you this morning? ”

Mentioning this made Tenna excited, having something to say: "Ah, I've reviewed the polishing of that proposal. I think we need to reconsider the broadcast time slot—this time slot isn't very suitable for this show. Also, I want to share opinions about staff selection. I think using pippins on stage would be more suitable for this show......" He beamed with excitement, returning to his old profession similar to his previous job, looking energetic again.

You still propped your chin up, only nodding gently when you heard his words. Indeed, according to your idea, this proposal was revised the same way—this job was just a test you assigned to Tenna. So you nodded and generously praised, "You did a great job. That's exactly where the proposal needs revision. Next, I'll leave the revisions, implementation, and coordination of this proposal to you. I believe you won't let me down." After saying that, you took another sip of water to soothe your throat.
