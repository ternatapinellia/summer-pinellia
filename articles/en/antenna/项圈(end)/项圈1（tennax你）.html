Collar 1 (tennax you)

You rubbed your neck and shook your head. Honestly, recent work has been giving you a bit of a headache...... But it's still within your range. Luckily, you had to go home after working overtime, so you took out your phone to check. Hmm, the location hadn't changed...... Pretty good. You remember the location was briefly out this morning, probably just to some small supermarket. After all, the bug you installed on his collar could hear the sound of him talking to some vendor.

In short, you were very satisfied. You put away your phone and went home, took out your keys, and opened the door. As soon as you pushed open the door, you saw a tall figure rushing up to you, then obediently lowering his head again. Through this posture, you could see the ruby pendant hanging from the collar around his neck, inside which was a photo of you both...... Of course, you also put the tracker and bug inside.

You lift your head slightly. The height of his head is just right for you to look at without much effort. Clearly, he's used to living here for many days. The two antennas hang down, hanging beside the TV's head, while Tenna happily says to you, "Welcome home!" You're back so late today...... Did something happen at the company again? Is there anything I can help with? ”

You're just an ordinary citizen of light working in the dark world. Honestly, you don't really want to answer Tenna's questions. You always feel it's a bit tiring, but since he's so obedient today, you still change your shoes, casually put down your briefcase, close the door, and casually rub the top of your head that had been bumped by the TV. Honestly, it's hard, but when you touch it, it feels a bit warm...... Looking again, for some reason, the screen has turned a bit red.

You just casually reply, "Ah, it's fine, normally it's about this time...... It's just that working overtime today took up some more time. ”

"But...... But I miss you so much, every second feels like a year. "He trails behind you pitifully. Out of the corner of your eye, you notice Tenna's figure seems to shrink a bit, but you don't pay much attention. You just take out some warm food from the kitchen, sit at the table by yourself, and start enjoying your somewhat cold dinner.

You just casually responded, "Mm." ”

But he seemed encouraged, and started pouring beans as if pouring beans: "Ah...... Today is a day off, and you still have to work overtime......

You interrupted him: "If you work overtime on a day off, your pay is triple." If it weren't for our better life, I wouldn't have worked overtime. "You're just stating the facts. After all, ever since Tenna's arms were severed and you helped him find a doctor to reconnect him, he's no longer the controller of the TV world. He's now just an ordinary Dark Citizen. Even though he wants to return to his former status, there's nothing he can do.

—After all, in this world where the People of Light and Darkness coexist, you are the true controller. Still, you'd rather do some small work to hide your identity.

Tenna was interrupted by you, her antenna drooping, her screen pitch black, and she lowered her head, fiddling with two fingers awkwardly as she said, "Yes, yes...... I know you're tired, dear, but I'm not idle at home either. I tidied up the room in the morning, then took advantage of the time in the morning to go to the nearby market to buy some things...... These should be things you'll like. Oh, and I went to the nearby park this afternoon. The amusement facilities there are really interesting, dear...... If you have time next time, could you come with me to take a look? I'll go home tonight, waiting for you to come back all along...... But you only got home now......" he said, his voice fading, and his figure gradually shrank, becoming almost as tall as you.

You actually know all this. After all, you occasionally take out your phone to check the locator's location to see where he is. The headphones connected to the bug also take up your ears, so you're usually distracted while working while listening to his voice. I have to say, occasionally listening to his little noises makes you feel quite at ease, and your restlessness calms down a bit.

You picked up a napkin to wipe your mouth and glanced at him lightly: "Sure, but I don't have time this week. I'll go with you next week." ”

So his screen lit up again, the antenna straightened, and after a couple of shakes, he was excitedly watching you—even though you thought the TV shouldn't show any expression on his face.

"That's great—when's next week? I'll get ready in advance, are we going for a picnic? Oh...... Then maybe I should prepare something in advance...... He started rambling again, imagining plans for next week's outing, as if that day had already arrived.

You had to interrupt him again: "Just going for a walk, no need to bring anything." "Your original itinerary didn't include this; you just set aside a short period for him."

Tenna stiffened, the screen went dark again, the antenna drooped, and she softly said, "Oh......"

You close your eyes for a moment, then open them again: "Alright, if you want, that's fine too." ”

Then his screen lit up again, flashing again, but he kept staring at you. Helpless, you still gently kissed his screen as you walked by. You watched the white screen blush red, while you just expressionlessly walked past him with a plate and put the empty plate into the dishwasher.

You sat on the living room sofa, picked up the remote, and prepared to turn on the big LCD TV hanging in the living room to watch for a while. But Tenna suddenly leaned over, insisted on sitting at the counter, blocking the large LCD TV, and spoke somewhat reluctantly: "Y/N...... Just watch me, don't watch other TVs. Isn't having me enough?" You heard his voice sound a bit sobbing.

You sighed silently in your heart, just silently patted your side. Tenna immediately got up and sat up closer, practically wanting the entire TV to press against you. He was tall himself, and the metal shell felt a bit cold against you. I just don't know why this guy kept getting hot, making the places you touched feel a bit warm.

You frowned slightly, but didn't say much. You just gently patted Tenna's head and said casually, "Just sit here and watch a movie with me. Shrink a little." "There was a hint of commanding tone mixed in."

Tenna didn't do as you said, still asking with a bit of grievance, "...... I can hold you, so we can watch a movie together." ”

You were really getting a bit impatient, but you just patiently patted Tenna on the head again and repeated, "Shrink a bit, don't make me say it a third time." ”

In the end, he did as you asked, making it the size you wanted—about the size of a pet dog. His screen was pitch black, probably because he wasn't in a good mood. You just picked him up in your arms, picked up his dropped phone—or a senior phone—to check the apps he used today, then opened his contacts list to check the messages from today, nodded barely noticeably, and then you picked up the remote, turned on the LCD TV in front of you, and started playing a movie.

But then you heard Tenna's sobbing: "Y/N...... If I show it, I can watch a movie with you too. Can you turn off that TV?" As he spoke, he sneakily grabbed the corner of your shirt. You touched it, and the TV screen was already covered in tears. You sighed again. Dealing with your lover's inexplicable emotional issues every day is really a hassle.

"Are you stupid? The movie I want to watch isn't even on your old TV. Do you think I'm supposed to sit here and stare with you? Or should I praise your shows and movies just out of consideration for your feelings? No one should always consider your feelings." You said it directly. You think Tenna should have understood this long ago, but unfortunately, he seems unable to figure it out. You did consider his feelings before and didn't say it directly.

…… But if he keeps dwelling on the past, there will be no future between you. You are completely rational, even coldly calculating this, so you think you have to—or should tell him—about it.

Tenna fell completely silent. You touched him, and the small TV screen was already covered in tears. You couldn't even count how many times you sighed today. You just turned him around, picked him up, and gently kissed him, or rather, licked the tears dripping from his screen. At first, the screen was cold, then you felt the screen gradually warming up. You thought, he must be getting better. So you looked up and saw his screen lit up. Tenna was grinning foolishly, but tears still hung on the screen, making it look a bit comical.

You looked at him silently and said, "You can't rely on nostalgia just to hope you'll always be the supreme figure. You should take a good look at the future." "Although you know what you say probably won't help. He'll probably just stick to his own ideas and stick to his ways. But that's okay...... If he stays the same forever, that's fine. At least you don't have to worry about him becoming a completely different person in the future.

And sure enough, he neither nodded nor shook his head. He just turned away, nestled in your arms, leaned against your chest, and said gloomily, "Darling, let's watch a movie." ”
