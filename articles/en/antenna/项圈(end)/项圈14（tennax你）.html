Collar 14 (tennax you)

In this story, the Yn personality is more S, and the Tenna is more M. Some chapters also contain R18 content. This chapter mainly focuses on Tenna's psychological portrayal.

——

As he passed by, those Dark People would look at him with a look that he couldn't quite understand, just a slightly ambiguous gaze, as if he were acting like something truly disgraceful. Tenna just straightened his antenna, glared fiercely at the pitch-black screen—well, even though he had no eyes at all, he felt that gesture sounded more like a glare. In short, after he looked over, no other inexplicable gazes came his way.

Tenna only remembered after stepping out of the company gate—he seemed to have planned to wait here and pick you up after work, but hadn't told you about it beforehand. You didn't know Tenna's plan. Tenna hesitated whether to go upstairs and tell you again, or just wait outside the company—he knew exactly when you got home each day, because everything was fixed except for overtime. But he didn't know how long he'd be waiting here, or rather—Tenna didn't know if he had the right to wait for you and then walk back with you. But...... Since you let him have that kind of relationship, that means he had the right to do it.

Tenna walked back and forth outside the door holding the empty lunchbox. He knew that quite a few Dark Citizens passing by and those in the company lobby were looking at him with strange expressions. He also knew that for you...... Maybe whether he was waiting for you or not wasn't that important. His antenna couldn't help but hang down again, and in the end, he hesitantly walked into the company building, went to the front desk, and asked the pippins, "Your boss...... I mean Y/N. What time does he usually get off work? ”

Pippins looked at Tenna with a very strange expression. After all, this was the first time he'd seen his former boss so humble, and he was just flipping through the booklet, pretending not to hear Tenna's question: "Oh, sorry, what are you talking about, sir? Could you please say it again?" ”

Tenna couldn't help but bare his sharp teeth, but he just gritted his teeth in anger. He knew exactly what these guys were doing—to get back at how he treated them before! But he had always looked down on these pippins, and he didn't know why he would let them take on such a position. But now, he could only politely repeat, "May I ask, what time does Y/N get off work?" Every word was spoken through gritted teeth. If he were still in his former identity, he could have forced these pippins to speak the truth long ago—unfortunately, he wasn't anymore.

But the red pippins seemed finally satisfied, jumping up on the spot: "The company's uniform closing time is 5 p.m. ”

Tenna responded and then walked out with her lunchbox. He was certain that his humble voice had probably been heard by everyone in the company lobby. He never wanted to lose face in front of the Dark People he once looked down on, but...... In short, for Tenna, that bit of dignity was nothing more important than you—at least in his heart. That's why he was able to speak so humbly just now.

Outside the company entrance, there was a bench. Tenna sat on it, hugging a small lunchbox. The bench actually seemed a bit small to Tenna. Tenna shrank a little, took out his phone, and with a bit of awkwardness glanced at the time. It was 1 PM, which meant he still had to sit here and wait for four hours—it didn't matter. He had once waited for the People of Light to come see him, for countless years, until in the end, all that awaited him was abandonment and death.

So it was only four hours...... It was okay, he comforted himself in his heart, adjusted his posture, and made sure to see him as soon as he walked out of the company. He knew you would always see him, just like before—seeing him damaged, then taking him to a proper repair, and bringing him back into this somewhat different dark world.

He heard whispers and laughter coming from the company lobby—probably mocking him—but Tenna just lowered her head, the antenna drooping, swiping the phone screen, counting the seconds hand as it spun again and again, until a minute finally passed.

…… It was only four hours, much less than the time he had waited for the Light People to turn on the TV before. This time, he had a clear expectation, but he felt that even a second was unbearable. He wished he could be by your side every moment. He had only been apart for a short while, and he already missed you so much. Yesterday, the day before, the day before that, he missed you the same way, but he never said it to you.

He scrolled through his phone screen, and at that moment, he saw a message flash at the top of the screen, showing it was from you. Tenna happily clicked in.

However, the message contained only one sentence: [Working overtime tonight, coming home late.] 】

Tenna's antenna, which had been raised, lowered again. He stared blankly at the line of text, then swiped through the small phone interface to exit the text message page. The contact list showed only you, ...... He had never tried calling you. First, he was just not used to this kind of electronic device. If he knew a bit of a phone, you would give it to him. Your exact words were probably that if he needed anything, he could contact you.

Secondly...... He's always nervous, afraid of disturbing you. Tenna lifted the screen and glanced into the distance. Indeed, it had been snowing lately, and by the way, Christmas was approaching. It's completely understandable that you've been busy lately.

But his heart was still heavy, and he planned to go home first...... Tonight, he was the only one sitting dazed across from that table, facing off for dinner.

But at that moment, Tenna's phone vibrated again. He hurriedly opened the message and saw another message: [Sorry, the project manager notified the wrong person. You need to work overtime tomorrow.] 】

Tenna let out a big breath and sat back down. Although your chat only had messages you sent, mostly just about working overtime and coming home late, as long as Tenna saw your own messages, Tenna was very happy.

He smiled, resting his elbow on his thigh and chin, a little flower appearing on his nose. The whole screen was sparkling, and the antenna was swinging back and forth. He started looking forward to seeing him as soon as you left work and left the house.

…… So he sat there, just daydreaming. He sat there for a full four hours, constantly checking his phone time in anticipation of five o'clock. When the minute hand passed by, it was truly five o'clock. Tenna stretched her slightly stiff body, planning to give you a big hug when she saw him later—after all, it was a surprise.

Tenna started staring at the company door. But after a minute, two minutes, he saw those pippins and zappers eagerly coming out, still nowhere to be seen. Tenna comforted himself, thinking you probably needed to pack up before taking the elevator. That would take some time in the end. He tried to calm his anxiety and fear, still staring at the company door.

…… But you waited until 5:30, and it seemed the last Zapper had walked out of the company, yet he still didn't see you. Tenna's antenna drooped again.

The zapper looked at Tenna, who had been sitting here waiting, wanting to say something but holding back. In the end, he said nothing. Tenna just anxiously picked up his phone to check the time, then to see if he had received your message. His screen went black and lit up, and after hesitating for a while, he finally walked toward the company building, then entered the elevator. His fingers trembled, and he finally pressed the button on the rooftop. He planned to go see for himself what was really going on—just to take a look.

The elevator dinged and arrived. He stepped out of the elevator doors, nervously straightening his clothes, afraid that what you saw might not be his most beautiful self. He slowly walked toward your office, slower and slower, his footsteps growing softer. He nervously swallowed hard, and wild thoughts began to surface in Tenna's mind. He wondered how he would explain it if you happened to see him as he walked out of the office. He was thinking that you happened to take another elevator down, and he happened to miss it. What should he do then?

Unfortunately, all these wild thoughts were shattered as Tenna approached the slightly ajar door of the office. Through the gap, he happened to see you talking to Zapper. He saw you smiling, handing the pile of documents in his hand to Zapper, and shook his hand, saying, "You did very well. Overall, this project is fine. I hope you will work hard together with the other staff under your team." ”

Tenna's antenna drooped, and he instinctively hid in the next room—after all, this zapper had hopped out and seemed to be in a good mood. Jealousy, even a kind of jealousy, an emotion he couldn't put into words. For a moment, Tenna even wished this zapper would disappear immediately—he thought, why not smile at him? He should have received your smile, your hug, your warm touch, but why let the other dark people get it too?

…… Why, not just for him? Why isn't your beautiful smile just for him? Why, why, why...... Can't you keep smiling at him?

Tenna's figure shrank bit by bit, his antenna drooping, the screen turning black and wet, leaving only large teardrops rolling down. He hurriedly took the elevator down, ran out of the company gate, then curled up on that bench, curling up into a tiny ball.

He thought, well, maybe you don't care about him that much. Maybe you don't care about him at all.
