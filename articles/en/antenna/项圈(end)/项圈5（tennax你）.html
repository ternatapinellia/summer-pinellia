This story features a more S personality and a more M tenna. Some chapters also contain R18 content.

A night without dreams.

When you come to your senses, you find yourself still curled up in Tenna's arms, just a little more cautious and his hand relaxed, while his head is still pressed against your neck, making you feel a bit uncomfortable.

You sighed, gently pulled his arm up, and stood up—it was time to get up and prepare breakfast for work—even though your body was still a bit sore.

But as soon as you got started, Tenna grabbed your wrist. When you looked back, you realized his screen had lit up at some point. It seemed he had been startled awake by you, his voice still a bit sleepy and cautious, and even the force he gripped your wrist involuntarily grew lighter.

He just asked softly, "Today...... You don't have to work, right? Can you stay with me a little longer? "You paused for a moment. You remembered today was Saturday, so you really didn't need to go to the office, but you're a bit hungry now, so you can't just lie here like this.

So you just patted Tenna's head and softly said, "Mm, but it's time to get up for breakfast. I'm hungry." With that, you found the clothes you had taken off yesterday and put them on yourself, then got ready to get out of bed. In short, you could eat anything you wanted, and you only planned to bake some toast.

Then you hear rustling sounds, and then you feel yourself being embraced in a warm embrace. But his arm doesn't touch you, just gently wraps around you, while his head rests on top of yours. Tenna's voice sounded a bit muffled: "...... I—I'll help you prepare breakfast. Can you ...... Stay a little longer? "What he actually wanted to ask was if you could spend a little more time with him, but he didn't dare ask, so he had no choice but to ask.

You gently scratched his TV's head: "Alright, I'll wait for you." "After all, it's only for a short while, and since there's nothing to do today, you're willing to spend a little more time with him." After all...... You were thinking that you should give him something he was hoping for. It's just a pity you can't give him much.

So you sit at the dining table, watch Tenna happily wear an apron, then walk into the kitchen to get busy. You smell the toasted aroma of bread, you smell the sweetness of jam, you hear the crackling of fried eggs in the pan, you smell the unique scent of morning.

And you just sit at the table, waiting for Tenna to bring the food over. Breakfast is simple: a few slices of freshly baked soft, slightly crispy bread, served with blueberry jam set aside, and two golden fried eggs on the plate. In addition, the plate has a few slices of freshly fried bacon and two steaming hot sausages.

You spread jam on the bread, took a bite, then squinted happily. Sure enough, having something steaming hot for breakfast is truly comforting.

Tenna nervously wiped her still oily hand over her apron, a bit timid but full of anticipation, and asked, "What do you think...... How does this taste? ”

Since you've said this much and he asked, you stopped and said directly, "I...... I don't like bread that is slightly burnt. Next time, don't set it to level 2—just use level 1 directly." "Although some toasted bread tastes great too, you told him earlier that speed 2 is enough...... But it really does depend on luck.

Tenna hurriedly wiped her hands with her apron: "Alright, I remember what you said...... It's just, just ......"

You glanced up at him, tilting your head slightly: "Just what?" ”

He finally took a deep breath, closed his eyes, and told you honestly, "I just opened the fridge and found there wasn't much left in stock...... Could you—can you go with me to the bakery?" "He wants to buy you some bread. Of course, he has his own reasons—he wants you to spend a little more time with him, and occasionally, he hopes you can go with him for a dessert meal, whatever it is."

You pulled out a napkin to wipe your mouth, thought for a moment, then nodded gently: "Alright, I'll go with you. But I'm a bit tired......" Before you could finish, Tenna couldn't wait to interrupt you.

"Darling, what do you want? I can do anything." "He's willing to do anything for you. Even if ...... is impossible, as long as it's your command, he'll be willing.

You propped your chin on your hand and thought carefully. You felt this proposal was a bit bold, but you did want to experience it. After all, he was so tall, so you suggested, "I wonder, could you carry me over there?" ”

Tenna's antenna suddenly stood upright, shaking excitedly. He propped his chin, and even the mouth on the lit screen curved into a smile: "Darling, of course I'm willing! You can tell me as much as you want, I'm only afraid of you...... Maybe you don't want to ......" Because he could tell you were a question, maybe you still didn't trust him that much, maybe...... Maybe you didn't love him as much as he loved you, but he was always comforting himself that loving you was enough...... But he still wanted more. From that moment that belonged only to him yesterday, he seemed even greedier. He hoped for more moments when you could belong completely to him, just...... That seems completely impossible.

You slowly swallowed the egg. It was soft-boiled egg. Tenna really remembered your preferences, so at least you were very satisfied with this breakfast. Hearing this, you were a little stunned: "No, I'm not unwilling. Then just carry me over there." You said this calmly, as if giving orders, and Tenna clearly enjoyed it. After all, you could see the two antennas above his head swaying happily again.

As you spoke, breakfast was finished. Tenna put all the plates away in the kitchen and brought you a glass of honey water. This is your morning habit—adding two drops of honey to a glass of water makes the water taste better. You just took a gentle sip of the perfectly warmed honey water, squinting slightly, listening to the splashing sound of water coming from the kitchen. It was probably Tenna cleaning those plates.

After you finished the honey water, Tenna came out, took the cup, and rubbed his hands together. You saw cold sweat dripping down his screen, probably...... Is he nervous? That's your guess, and as you expected, he said, "Darling...... I've packed up, shall we head out? ”

You softly hummed and bent down slightly. You glanced up at him and saw the ruby pendant gently swaying around his neck. You remembered the photo of you two hidden inside the pendant, but in the end said nothing, just gently grabbed the pendant with his hand and then tugged at the collar. You felt Tenna's body stiffen for a moment, but in the end, he managed to squeeze out a few laughs: "Haha, darling, get ready, we're about to go!" As he spoke, he reached out to support your hips, easily picked you up, and even bounced it in his hand.

You frowned slightly, but in the end, you wrapped your arms around Tenna's neck and hugged him. Your cheek was pressed against Tenna's screen, and his body stiffened for a moment, then his heart burst with joy from those subtle touches. He likes it when you approach him on your own, and he likes to approach you too. Whenever he turns his head and sees your face, he feels immense joy, and his heart in his chest is pounding wildly. Fortunately, you can't hear the sound of his heart right now. He just needs to see you stay by his side and feel content, but he also feels anxious, because being close to him is your body, not your heart...... If, if ...... One day, you can truly tell him that you love him with all your heart. But Tenna always felt that such a day would never come.

So he carefully cherished this small moment, cherished the present moment, and didn't want to be able to have it even now.

You leaned against Tenna's slightly hot screen and glanced at him with some puzzlement. For some reason, Tenna walked with a bit of a hand and foot, and seemed a bit light. If you hadn't subtly pushed him, you might have crashed straight into the utility pole ahead.

"If you feel uncomfortable, then put me down." You said.

But when you hear this, Tenna suddenly hugs you and shakes her head: "No...... No, I won't let you go...... No, I'm not feeling uncomfortable, darling, you know, I'm just too happy...... "Oh my god, what on earth is he spouting nonsense? He doesn't even know. But when he says this to you, he seems a bit pitiful, a bit cautious, and the screen goes black, as if the next second the screen will get wet again, with big droplets rolling down.

You were silent for a moment, feeling that he was holding you too tightly, but you didn't say anything. You just reached out to touch the head of the big TV, then wrapped your arm around him and gently pressed it against his screen. But his screen seemed to be getting hotter and hotter.

Tenna holds you, walking down the street with enthusiasm, or perhaps almost like a drifting haze. He lifts his head toward the TV, as if wanting everyone to know that his lover is always clinging to him at this moment, holding his lover—if only it had lasted a little longer, he just wanted to hold you forever, always be with you.
