This story features a more S personality and a more M tenna. Some chapters also contain R18 content.

——

You saw his antenna still hanging down, his screen still pitch black, and that pitch-black screen staring at you. You saw Tenna's hand reach out, as if wanting to hug you, but then shrinking back and pulling it back. You pulled his hand over yourself, then moved a little closer to him and asked again, "Is there anything else you want?" ”

His screen lit up for a moment, then went black again. Maybe Tenna was struggling to hold back her emotions, while you just quietly watched him.

Tenna's hand gently wrapped around you. After hesitating for a long moment, he said, "You...... You said before that your company still needs a ...... content planning or something, I want ...... to join your company...... Although after arriving at your company, his colleagues were all once his subordinates, but...... But if that means he could look at you a little longer, then he didn't mind. As he spoke, he swallowed hard, not daring to look at you anymore, but his screen was pitch black, so you couldn't tell what he was looking at.

You hesitate for a moment, considering whether you might install pinhole cameras in his office—you're a bit anxious too, since if he goes out, there are many things you can't control.

Tenna probably noticed your hesitation; his voice grew quieter and more timid: "I...... This is the ...... you promised me before."

You sighed and gently tapped Tenna's screen: "Mm, I understand. I'll let you onboard, but before you start, I want to have a psychological counseling session with you...... Just like what we did before, hmm? ”

Tenna's antenna lifted slightly. These conversations usually took place once a month, sometimes once a week—when you first brought him home, almost once a day, and you usually just listened quietly to his statements. Tenna knew you would definitely listen carefully and seriously at this time, so he happily shook the antenna and nodded, "Well, I...... I definitely won't let you down!" ”

His voice was a bit stammering, but it was tinged with anticipation. You know, he probably had shaken off all his negative emotions, but Tenna leaned over again, rubbing your cheek with the screen and whispering, "So...... So I hope you...... You can stay with me a little longer...... He seemed to understand what he was talking about, but it was just his simple wish. His screen finally lit up, and a flower appeared at the tip of his nose. You sighed, took off the flower, put it on your head, and softly responded.

They said it was for psychological counseling—but in reality, it was only done on weekends. But these past few days, Tenna had been in a great mood. He would go up to the rooftop to bring you a lunchbox, then sit on a bench outside waiting for you to walk out of the company, so he could carry you home with him. Although his former subordinates would mock him when they saw him sitting there waiting, Tenna would just pretend not to hear, pretending not to care about anything—but he heard everything. His chest always felt aching and a bit numb, so that every day after work, when you saw him, the screen was always pitch black, and the screen was damp.

You have no choice but to wipe his screen, gently kiss him, comfort him, and feed him desserts you bought on the road. Every day. You roughly understand why he has such feelings...... But this is your lover. Although you feel a bit annoyed, you still patiently soothe him until his screen lights up again and a little flower appears on his nose.

—It wasn't until the weekend that Tenna's long-awaited psychological counseling finally arrived.

You sit at the dining table, facing each other. You look at Tenna, and Tenna hesitates, finally speaking the words she's been wanting to say but never knowing when they're right to say them: "I...... I'm jealous, I even want your employees to disappear. I don't know why you can smile at them. If only you could smile at me more...... Can't you just smile at me...... Can't those words be just for me...... You know...... I, I...... I hope you can show a little more care about me......

You softly responded with a hum, clenched your fists on the table. You didn't look him in the eye, just softly said, "I've already said, my praise is just hoping the employees will help me." ”

You look at Tenna's antenna, which also droops: "Then ...... Can you praise me a bit more, I...... I've helped you out too...... You feel his screen seems a bit red.

You think for a moment, then reply in a flat tone, "Hmm, then...... Tenna is awesome. "Forgive me, you really can't find many words to praise him." You see Tenna's antenna lift for a moment and then lower again. You probably feel a bit satisfied, but not too much.

Because you heard his voice choked with tears: "Then...... You, can't you say a little more? ”

You stared at his darkened screen, hesitated for a moment, and said, "Then, Tenna, you understand, you should understand, today I love you, tomorrow, and even the day after. Every minute and every second from now on, I love you too." Is this the answer you want to hear? ”

You listen to the sound of both crying and laughing, and you look at Tenna nodding and shaking her head.

"No, that's not enough......" His voice was soft. You looked at him quietly and just sighed.

"So what exactly do you want?" You ask again, just like the one you asked a few days ago.

"I...... Will you, will you love me forever? "He seems to be just fixated on this answer.

“…… If every minute and second I say I love you, then those words aren't enough. I'm sorry, I won't say forever." Your voice was still calm, just rubbing your temples with a slight headache.

"Why...... Why? Why won't you love me forever? "Tenna slightly raised the dark screen, looking rather oppressive, while you quietly looked at him, saying nothing. You don't know why Tenna keeps struggling with this answer. Even though he knows that even after asking it, he will never get a satisfactory answer—what else does he want? You sigh, and in the end, you have forgiven your lover.

"If you won't, then you won't. If you really want an answer...... It's because I've only ever seen the separation after forever. "As you spoke, you seemed a bit distracted. You remembered the family who once promised to love you forever, who eventually gave up on you and said they would love another sister born a little later. You remembered the friend who once said they would love you forever, but in the end, they betrayed you for their own interests. Actually...... On that day, before seeing Tenna at the edge of the cliff, you had chosen death.

“…… So, can you act like you care about me a little more? I don't want to be looked down upon by those former subordinates...... To be honest, at the time, I thought I was going to die...... But you saved me. I once thought I would stay like this, I thought everything was within reach...... But now, but now...... I am the one who looked down on me now...... Why should I......" he murmured, probably resentful, or perhaps a bit uneasy. Unable to get a satisfactory answer, he felt uneasy and could only find another way to ask you.

“…… Because they're more successful than you now, and they're doing better than you—that's why they look down on you." You spoke calmly, and then you heard Tenna's somewhat anxious voice.

"Then...... As long as you show you care about me enough, they definitely won't dare criticize me...... "That's just Tenna's feelings, and you're almost amused by what he said, almost bursting out laughing."

You gently shake your head: "None of this will change unless you prove it to them." ”

Tenna's urgent words were interrupted by you. His antenna drooped again, and he only murmured softly, "...... Is it only if I do well enough?" ”

"Mm." You replied softly, as if it were a response.

Tenna just lifted her head and looked at you again: "...... Then, what do you think I can do well enough?" "It seems too much time has passed; he no longer knows what kind of show the People of Light prefer."

You gaze at him, think deeply, then gently answer his question: "If you stay here all the time, in my eyes, you're already good enough. If you want to go out and stand in front of everyone, then...... You have to innovate. Old and outdated won't let you stay a star forever. ”

Tenna's antenna drooped again, shaking as you saw his two fingers tangled together, probably hesitating and conflicting, before finally cautiously whispering, "Then...... Then I shouldn't be worthless to you. Why did you save me in the first place?" His voice was once again tinged with tears.

You look at him, think for a moment, then whisper, "Precisely because you had nothing and nothing at the time. You had no support, no future." "So...... He would be someone who belongs completely to you, someone who can never escape the prison you control. But of course, you can't tell Tenna that everything is under your control.

“…… Then do you love me, who is worthless?" Tenna didn't understand what he meant, so he could only cautiously ask again.

And you nodded and, without thinking, said, "I love you." ”
