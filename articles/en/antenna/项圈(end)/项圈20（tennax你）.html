Collar 20 (tennax you)

You looked at him, seemingly overjoyed by your praise, Tenna was overjoyed, her nose blooming again. You just calmly asked, "So, regarding the proposal I proposed to have you write from scratch, what do you think about it?" ”

When this question was mentioned, Tenna stammered again; his screen went dark again, his hands behind his back, and you just quietly looked at him.

After a while, after a moment of silence, Tenna finally spoke. His voice became less confident again, perhaps realizing that no matter how hard he tried, the plan he came up with was still a bit old-fashioned: "I...... Um, the plan I have is to take promotional photos for them, and then promote them during the TV show......

You nodded. This was a standard plan, not too bad. You tapped your knuckles on the table and added, "You're right. Overall, your idea can be implemented, but you also need to understand the current situation. The promotion of the new play can't be limited to TV shows; it needs to be promoted across major media platforms, and we need to filter which media outlets are more suitable for our promotion, and which might not be very effective even if they do it. I've also assigned you to work on this planning proposal. I believe you'll do it well." As you spoke, you gently nodded at him. The office door was knocked, and you saw a zapper walk in. So you asked him to wait outside the door for a moment.

Finally, you look at Tenna again and nod at him: "I believe you'll do well with the proposal I hand over to you later. I hope you can create even better results for my company—hmm, so if you have anything else, you can leave now." As you said this, you still wore a smile, while Tenna, somewhat unskillfully, glanced at the time on her phone and finally tentatively asked, "It's almost noon. Can I invite my wife to lunch?" As he spoke, he took out another lunchbox and placed it in front of you, eagerly scooping it over the edge of the table, just raising the screen to look at you.

You hesitate for a moment, then see Tenna take out another lunchbox and place it beside you, then nod to him: "Alright, come sit here." ”

At the same time, you shout toward the door: "Sorry, it's lunch break. Come over when you start work in the afternoon!" The zapper outside responded, and you heard his hopping footsteps away.

Tenna was a bit unhappy and muttered, "Why don't you just have him wait outside the whole time...... "The last time he came, he could only sit on the sofa watching Zapper report the mission to you."

But you just shook your head and said softly, "I can't let my employees feel disheartened." ”

Tenna fell silent. He wanted to question you, but could that really make him feel disheartened? But...... But he also knew that whatever he made could already be fulfilled, he seemed a bit greedy. But even then, he would never be satisfied, never satisfied, even though he didn't even know if he could reach eternal length with you.

So he quietly opened the two lunchboxes, eagerly handed you the utensils, and you ate the takoyaki and pork rice balls prepared in today's lunchbox, then leaned in to kiss Tenna's screen.

"Today's food is really good, I really like it." You said lightly, and Tenna just felt her body become lighter. He chuckled foolishly as you calmly continued eating the rice ball. By the time you finished, Tenna hadn't even eaten a few bites.

So you thought for a moment and reminded, "Then sit on the sofa next to you and continue eating." Tenna didn't understand but still sat down next to her as you instructed. Then you dialed the phone and called the Zapper, who you had just called down, to come back and report.

Zapper bounced open the door to your office, glanced discreetly at Tenna, who was still sitting on the sofa eating lunch, then placed the documents on your desk and began to report to you.

"Boss, regarding the tour you mentioned to our project team before, our team has already discussed it again. This production was a huge success at first, so we're preparing to start a tour. However, the actors will probably need to be replaced, and there are also stage design plans and stage prop selection plans...... Take a look." He mumbled as he finished speaking, handing you the documents, anxiously waiting for your answer.

You took the plan and glanced over it. There were no major issues. Just as you were about to take a closer look, you noticed a resentful, burning gaze. You looked up and saw Tenna hadn't continued eating, just staring at you with her antenna hanging down. You sighed softly, really not understanding why Tenna was acting like this again.

So you could only skim through the proposal again, quickly nod, and give a conclusion: "The plan is well done, generally fine. Just continue with this plan. If there are any minor issues, contact me or ask other colleagues—some of them are more experienced in this area." After saying that, you handed the file back to Zapper. He nodded and happily jumped out of your office again. You glanced at Tenna, who still exuded a gloomy aura and a pitch-black screen. You reluctantly sat down beside him and gently stroked his screen.

You asked, "What's wrong with you now?" ”

Tenna just shrank a bit, gradually becoming about your height. He tilted his head, staring at you through the pitch-black screen. It looked quite frightening at first glance, and you calmly looked back at him. In the end, he seemed deflated, the screen wet as it rested on your shoulder, soaking your shirt: "I...... Why do you still call someone in during your lunch break...... I just want to spend a little more time with you......" As he spoke, his heavy, huge TV screen rubbed against your shoulder.

You sighed and gently patted his head: "Because he came to see me earlier, and it wasn't lunch break yet. I finished lunch with you, so of course I had to finish the work I had delayed earlier." He explained patiently, reaching out to gently wipe the tear stains from his screen.

Tenna sniffled, her sobs growing quieter, and his voice was also very soft. He just asked in a low voice, "During lunch break...... Can't it be just the two of us together? "Can't ...... Can't it always be just him and you? Can't it just be like this? It felt like some monster was baring its fangs in his heart, but he could only force that monster to retreat.

You took out your phone to check the time and calculated: "You just called me to dinner twenty minutes early, but I only took ten minutes to settle this work matter." "In other words, you even made an extra ten minutes to spend with him."

"Lunch break is a time to rest." You added, telling Tenna that he could come anytime during lunch break whenever he wanted.

But he seemed to misunderstand and sniffled again: "So...... Except for work, I don't come to see you during my breaks. ”

You sighed and poked his screen: "During break, you can come see me whenever you want." ”

You don't know why Tenna sometimes acts a bit silly, but that's okay—you're patient. Tenna's screen lit up again, and a flower appeared on his nose again. He picked the flower and placed it on your head. That way, anyone who comes in will see the flower and know you belong to Tenna.

You let him lean on your shoulder and let him do as he pleased, just leaning on the sofa, resting your head against his, taking a nap during the lunch break. Then, as the break was about to end, the alarm rang, you woke up, and gently nudged Tenna: "Alright, it's getting late. You should go back to your post." ”

Tenna stood up, his antenna still drooping, looking a bit gloomy. But when he saw the flower on your head, his mood improved a lot, and he happily strode out of the office.

You sighed, still slightly pulling open your collar, revealing the bite marks he left on your neck yesterday. You don't want to cause any trouble, but...... A little showing off can satisfy the anxious guy, right?

So this afternoon, everyone who came to report saw the flower on your head that clearly belonged to Tenna and the bite marks on your neck. They hesitated, and after finishing their report, hurried out. You sighed silently in your heart, hoping Tenna could face the trouble ahead.

As for Tenna...... He returned to his small studio to start handling the plan. You turned on the pinhole camera's monitor and absentmindedly watched Tenna's actions. Then you saw Tenna push open the door and walk out. You switched to the regular camera in the hallway and continued watching Tenna's actions. Shortly after he left, you saw him run into Pippins, who was bouncing and talking about something. The monitor didn't have recording function, so you could only see Tenna's screen turning red. He seemed to be arguing with Pippins.

You look at the screen, black-and-white and red-blue lights shining on your face, flickering on and off.

Tenna felt wronged, because Pippins probably came because he used to look down on them—he still looks down on them now, but clearly, Pippins probably also looks down on Tenna.

The pippins bounced and said sarcastically, "Are you really not the employee the boss squeezed in?" "I think you're close to the boss, and you even left her with a kiss mark." ”
