Collar 24 (tennax you)

After the holidays, you held another online meeting, but this time still no Tenna—after all, he's not very good with such equipment.

You cleared your throat and calmly announced, "The reason I'm holding this meeting is to announce a promotional proposal that Tenna has completed. In my opinion, this proposal has already been done very well. From the selection of performance materials, the schedule, and suggestions from the performers and organizers, to the feasibility of implementing this proposal and any potential risks that might arise during the performance, everything has been implemented. Next, I'll send this proposal to the media department first. You will handle content selection, content organization, and selection of performers. That's it. Meeting adjourned." After saying that, you planned to close the meeting, but before that, pippins spoke first.

"I have doubts. I don't think that proposal was entirely written by Mr. Tenna...... Is there any evidence? To be honest, in my opinion, his abilities still don't suit our company. "He raised his hand and shouted loudly.

You furrowed your brows and thought for a moment, then continued calmly and seriously, "First, the original proposal is with me. Tenna wrote it out carefully." After saying that, you displayed the original proposal in front of the video camera, flipping through the pages to show them one by one, so they could clearly see Tenna's handwriting.

"Secondly, after this proposal is set up...... I think everyone knows I have installed surveillance cameras in the office area. If you still don't believe me, you can come find me in person to retrieve the backup footage and check it. "You rested your chin on both hands, speaking leisurely.

Those pippins probably looked at each other in their own office area for a while, but in the end, they chose to trust you, and so the crisis was resolved. No other employees approached you all morning. You let out a gentle breath, thinking that Tenna would be allowed to take the stage at the next meeting, and that most of the conflicts needed to be resolved would be resolved. Now, no one should doubt Tenna's abilities.

Just as you were thinking this, you heard a knock on the office door. You shouted loudly to come in, then saw Tenna push your door open and walk in. You signaled with your eyes what else he wanted, and Tenna swallowed twice and whispered, "...... You said that if you complete the task well, you can freely claim a reward. ”

You smiled and nodded. You really said that, and it turned out Tenna did an excellent job too.

Tenna took a deep breath; he knew...... If he ever asked you to love him forever, you'd just regretfully tell him you couldn't fulfill that promise. But now he knew that he was the only contact on your phone, and you only had him, and probably only him in your whole world. So Tenna was already very satisfied.

So this time, his request was: "I...... I want to go to Castle Town and see Chris again." His voice was a bit muffled, probably a bit cautious.

You raised your eyebrows and waved, "Of course. You just need to go to the train station here and catch a train there. It's pretty fast." ”

Tenna quickly nodded and thanked you. He hadn't brought anything except a bit of Dark Coin, and was just set off enthusiastically. But when he arrived at the train station security checkpoint, he dazedly heard the alarm at the gate. Tenna knew that since he was a mechanical creature from head to toe, passing through security wouldn't be easy. But the security checks in the Dark World were carefully designed—unless there was something he couldn't bring and everything was made of metal, since the Dark People working at the station could see it.

After checking the scan, the staff member waved his hand: "Take off the collar from that neck." ”

Tenna took off the collar in confusion and handed it to another staff member. He opened the pendant to check it and told Tenna, "There are trackers and bugging devices inside. These can't be brought on trains." ”

Tenna was stunned when he heard this. At first, he hadn't thought about what these two things actually did. He just glanced at the collar again, then at the two small items in the staff member's hands. The screen suddenly lit up: "Oh—then could you please help me put these two things back?" "I'm not taking the train for now." His antenna was also swinging, and he was almost ecstatic, almost unable to figure out what he was supposed to do. He didn't know where to put his hands, but after the staff put the two little items back in, he happily held the collar and waved it, then walked toward the train station outside.

His screen was bright, and flowers popped up from his nose, as if petals were floating all over him. He just held up the collar and danced happily down the street, casually circling around. Passersby looked at Tenna with strange eyes, but he didn't care at all. He just hugged the collar and murmured to himself, "So Y/N cares about me this much...... So she's been watching me all along, turns out ...... Turns out she's always known where I am, basically I'll always be by her side...... Tenna felt her whole body feel light. He remembered there was a bug in the collar and couldn't help but bring it closer to his mouth, then repeated.

"I like you...... I love you so much, oh Y/N...... You have no idea how much I love you. I thought, if only you could keep looking at me...... Turns out my wish had always come true. I thought it was impossible...... Darling, I really love you so much, I love you so much...... He was a bit incoherent, while on the other end, holding up his phone, you listened to everything through the bug and remained silent.

Tenna's screen was wet again, tears streaming down her face. But this time, her screen wasn't pitch black—it was bright. Tenna was looking forward to it, or perhaps completely joyful, looking forward to everything beautiful that would come. He thought, he knew everything, knew just how much you cared about him, so...... So why didn't you tell him before? Tenna was lost in thought, quickening her pace as she walked toward your shared home.

…He noticed it. At first, you were a little nervous, but then you became slightly calm. After all, if he couldn't accept it, he could leave, but he seemed to be able to accept all of this.

You stayed at home, slowly closing your eyes to rest. Until there was a loud bang, your door was pushed open, and then Tenna carefully closed the door. He leaned in beside you, lying on your knee. You felt those tears wet your knees again, and when you opened your eyes, his screen lit up, and the flowers protruding from his nose were falling one by one onto you.

He stammered, "Y/N, darling, so you put the locator and bug in the collar, and I'm the only one in your contact list. You only care about me...... Why couldn't you have told me earlier? I've been anxious all along. If you had told me earlier...... How happy would I be? Darling, you would be happy too, right? Oh really, I'm so happy to see you care so much, darling. I like you so much......"

You interrupted him: 'So how do you know that you are the only one in my contact list?'

Tenna stiffened for a moment, and his previously upright antenna now drooped. He whispered, "I...... Um, I secretly checked your phone during Christmas......

However, he became self-righteous again: 'But you’ve also been keeping track of my movements all along, so in this way, we’re even!'

You silently watch Tenna say nothing, and Tenna quietly turns off the screen. In the long silence, he feels a bit uneasy.

And you're thinking that maybe checking and invading someone's privacy is something hard to accept. You don't think there's anything wrong with him checking your phone, but...... But since he equates this with checking and monitoring his whereabouts, maybe he thinks he doesn't like checking his phone, which means he wouldn't like it either...... But you don't know, you only know Tenna looks happy.

So you just asked softly, 'Are you really feeling happy?'

Tenna just rested her head on your lap, staring at you with the dark screen, her voice also trembling a little: 'You... what do you mean?'

You just shook your head: 'I don't know... do you really like it, or rather, do you really like me?'

As soon as she finished speaking, Tenna interrupted her anxiously: "Of course I like you, I really, really, super like you!" ”

“…… Okay, but you have to understand, I can never give you equal feelings, because in my heart, the most important thing is myself, and you comes second. And if you want to become my possession...... Trackers, bugs, cameras...... Everything like that will surround you. So...... If you're not sure you can accept this collar, I can leave my side. "You look down at him, then turn away and stop looking at him, just gently clenching your fist. You think, this is Tenna's last chance—if he leaves this time and never comes back, you'll find the next more obedient dog, a better one to control. But if Tenna chooses to stay by your side—he won't have another chance.

Tenna also lowered his head, his antenna drooping down and landing on your legs. He felt a little heartbroken, with a heavy feeling in his chest, as if falling into an ice pit.

His voice trembled as he slowly asked you, "So...... So you mean to drive me away?" Tenna couldn't be sure he could accept this kind of love, because in his heart, you were the most important thing, and only himself came second. He ...... He even began to doubt this kind of love himself.

But you didn't answer him, just turned your head away, closed your eyes, and didn't let Tenna see the struggle in your gaze.

Tenna lifted his head, then lowered it again, slowly picking up the shattered mood all over the floor. With heavy steps, he opened the door and gently closed it; he left your home.
