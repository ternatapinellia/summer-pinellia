Collar 13 (tennax you)

This story features a more S personality and a more M tenna. Some chapters also contain R18 content.

——

It's a new week. After finishing the breakfast Tenna carefully prepared, you didn't comment, just hurriedly grabbed your briefcase and headed out. After all, you have to go to work.

As for Tenna—he was curled up at home, sitting on the sofa, dark on the screen, and then a cooking show started playing automatically on his screen, already thinking about what kind of meal to prepare for you.

And you...... Sitting at your own desk, watching reports submitted by others. The company you currently work for—or rather, the company you run is a media company—and you are reviewing the promotional and performance planning proposals submitted by your subordinates.

You tapped the paper and said emotionlessly, "The overall number of people planning was wrong. In my opinion, this performance should be reduced by one thousand people—just report it to three thousand when reporting. Also, the safety planning plan isn't detailed enough, so keep revising it." After saying that, you handed the reports back to Zapper, who was bending down to show you. He bowed toward you and then hopped away, while you just pressed your brow with a slight headache, closed your eyes and pretended to nap, since you're waiting for the next report to be presented.

However...... Probably they're still revising the plan, so at least you haven't seen it before noon.

Tenna carefully plated the lunchbox, placing two small strawberry sandwiches in the lunchbox, then several sausages shaped like little octopuses—and a few cherry tomatoes piled together. After closing the lunchbox, he carefully carried it and walked toward your company.

Tenna walked into your company, and the front desk pippins asked who he was looking for—Tenna's feelings were complicated, since these Dark People were originally employees of his world, but you saved him and took over the position of lord of this world, and he had no complaints.

His antenna bent, trembled twice, the screen lit up, and a little flower appeared at the tip of his nose: "I—I'm Y/N's husband! I'm here to deliver lunch to my dear wife!" He shouted this loudly and proudly, and the front desk pippins gave him a rather flirtatious glance, then finally let Tenna take the elevator upstairs.

Holding the lunchbox, wondering what to say to you, Tenna's mind was always active. He was hoping you'd praise him, or perhaps the smile you'd show after tasting the lunchbox, or maybe you'd give him a kiss—or a hug, even if just a word? He imagined what you'd say when you saw him. As he thought, another flower popped up at his nose. He carefully picked one and stuck it on his strawberry sandwich, hoping you'd see his joy and embrace his heavy feelings.

Thinking this, he stepped out of the elevator, lightly and swiftly heading toward your office. Finally, he stopped in front of a room, which was also his former room—thinking of this, Tenna couldn't help but feel a bit bitter, but he quickly composed himself. He knew that being saved by you and receiving your love — even if only for this very moment, it was already very happy. He didn't ask for more. Because...... He knew very well that maybe the people of light would still look at him seriously, only you, and only you.

Tenna took a deep breath and gently knocked on the door. Then he heard your voice, indifferent, saying, "Please come in."

He pushed open the door, holding the lunchbox in his hands, standing tall and shouting loudly, "Darling—"

But before he could finish his sentence, he died because he discovered there was someone else in your office—a Shadow Guy, the same person he had been hiring as a fan before. His screen went black, his hand lowered, and of course, you noticed Tenna's voice, tapping his knuckles on the desk: "Okay, put it on the desk first." "You need to finish your current tasks first."

Tenna silently shrank a little, walked over, placed the lunchbox on your desk, then sat down on the sofa nearby, the screen dark, antenna hanging down. He waited for you to finish talking, and also for you to notice him. He hoped you—even just glancing at him would be nice. But you didn't look at Tenna. You just looked at the proposal Shadow Guy presented, flipped through it, and then commented, "Hmm, this proposal is pretty good, but you need to make sure you can find an agent who can provide it, or at least hire one." Also, since the plan is finalized, you should discuss what kind of script you want to produce these next few days and give me a rough look. After you finished speaking, you handed the proposal to Shadow Guy, who gave you a slight bow before happily leaving your office.

Tenna hadn't expected you to be completely unaware of him, and only after the door closed did you have the energy to deal with lunch and Tenna's matters. You glanced in his direction and saw his screen was covered in tears, wet, even the sofa was a bit wet. His body had become very small, probably about the size of a pet dog. He hung his head, looking as if he had no other reaction.

Tenna wondered, when will you finally look at him seriously? He felt a bit wronged. You and those employees have plenty to say, so why are you so reserved with words when facing him, saying just a few words? Tenna doesn't understand why you're like this, nor does he understand how you treat him, and he doesn't dare disturb you. So he can only quietly cry here, and he can only feel sad about it all, but no matter what, it's hard to let go.

You sighed, not knowing what was really going on with your lover. There was always a first-come, first-served order. You must have handled other people's matters before taking care of your own lunch. But you still sat down beside him holding the lunchbox, then sat on your lap holding him. You opened the lunchbox and began to enjoy today's lunch.

You first take a bite of the sandwich, then say seriously, "Hmm, the sandwich tastes great. Tenna put a lot of thought into making it, right?" Thank you. "But you know that saying these things probably works, since you see the hanging antenna slightly upright, and you don't see his tears dripping down soaking your clothes anymore."

Then you used your fork to pick up a small sausage and chewed on it, softly saying, "The shape of the sausage is quite interesting. Tenna probably decided after watching a lot of cooking shows, right?" As you spoke, you gently touched his screen again. It was still icy cold, but it didn't feel damp anymore.

In the end, you ate the cherry tomatoes again and softly said, "That's great. Eating fruit after meals is a good habit. Thank you, tenna, for reminding me." As you spoke, you gently patted Tenna's head, and then you watched as his TV screen lit up again, but...... Probably because he was still in your arms, he hadn't grown bigger.

You heard his voice sound a bit muffled and a bit cautious: "So...... So do you like this lunchbox? "You like it too...... Is he the one who went to great lengths to prepare your lunchbox? But Tenna didn't dare ask that question; he just leaned closer into your arms, the head of the TV resting against your chest.

You gently stroked his cheek again and said casually, "Mm, I really like it. Tenna's bento tastes really good." Then you held him and spun him around. You could feel the screen suddenly getting hot, and Tenna seemed to be getting bigger. But since he was still in your arms, Tenna held back the urge.

You gently hugged him again, then placed him on the sofa beside you, returned the lunchbox to him, and smiled slightly: "Tenna can take this empty box home now. I'm very satisfied." "As for exactly what you're satisfied with—of course you wouldn't tell him."

Then, you see Tenna suddenly grow bigger, and then he hugs you tightly, kissing your face wildly, or rather, an overwhelming wave of kisses falls like that. As he kisses, he licks and entwines yours, making you a little dizzy, tears welling up at the corners of your eyes, while your gaze remains clear.

Tenna finally let go of you. His hands carefully wrapped around you. You sighed softly and stuck the little flower from the lunchbox into your head, as if announcing Tenna's possession. You felt Tenna's screen brighten even more—probably a surprise. Anyway, he stammered, "Darling...... I love you the most. I will always love you the most......

You gently reached out to tap his screen, didn't say much, and didn't respond. You just nodded, responded with a hum, then tilted your head to look at him and kissed his screen. You felt his screen getting even hotter.

"Go back, lunch break is almost over." You said, which meant work would start soon, and others would walk into the office. Only then did Tenna reluctantly get up, glancing back at you every few steps, while you simply sat back down at your desk, starting to clear the morning piles of work and messy documents, without looking up at him.

But as soon as Tenna saw that flower still hanging on your head, she was overjoyed. He knew that all his longtime employees in this company were his longtime employees, so—all the Dark People would know that the flower belonged to Tenna, and that you belonged to him. With this indescribable yet elusive secret thought, he happily went down from the elevator.
