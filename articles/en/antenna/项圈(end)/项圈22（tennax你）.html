Collar 22 (tennax you)

Fortunately, the next few days were relatively safe. Thinking about it, it was time to reach the day before Christmas, Christmas Eve. You originally didn't want to celebrate, but since you gave your employees a holiday for Christmas, you and Tenna stayed home to rest.

You were sitting on the sofa, casually taking out a book to read, while Tenna sat right next to you, cautiously moving closer to you. You could feel the warm body leaning against you. It was just right for winter, but in summer, you could see it...... You couldn't help but keep your distance from Tenna.

But seeing him hanging his antenna, hesitating to speak, you still looked up at him and softly asked, "What's wrong?" ”

Tenna stammered, "Today...... Today is Christmas Eve...... "His voice grew quieter as he spoke.

You tilted your head slightly, put down your book, and looked at him, softly replying, "Yes, yes, today is Christmas Eve." ”

His screen was lit, but for some reason the white screen had turned red. His figure shrank a bit, leaning right next to you, resting on your shoulder, still stammering, "Christmas Eve...... Can I make a wish to you? ”

You gave Tenna a slightly strange look and said, "I'm not Santa Claus." "The implication is that he shouldn't make wishes to you; making wishes with you won't help."

He just pressed his fingers to the other hand, the screen went dark, and cautiously spoke to you: "Mm...... But I think only you can make my wish come true. "His voice was also very quiet, sounding a bit muffled. You were a bit uncomfortable with Tenna leaning on your shoulder, but you still let him do it. After all, your body was just a bit stiff, and it would get better after a while.

Tenna's screen rubbed against your shoulder, and you saw his screen was wet again. I imagine if you didn't agree, he'd cry out loud too. You could only sigh, gently wipe his screen, then turn your head to kiss his screen, then kiss his re-upright antenna, and softly ask, "So...... What is your wish? ”

"I...... I wish I could watch you forever. "Tenna raised her voice slightly, and you patiently responded with a hum, still gently stroking his screen.

So Tenna's voice grew louder: "I hope...... I hope you can always care about me. ”

And you just gave a soft 'mm', signaling Tenna to continue.

His voice grew a little louder until it was almost as loud as usual: "I hope...... You can always love me. ”

You didn't answer him, just watched as Tenna's figure grew a little bigger, leaned in again to kiss his screen, and his screen finally brightened again, with a little flower appearing on the tip of his nose.

He held his head high and said proudly, "I hope I can always be by your side! Forever!" ”

After saying that, he brought the screen close to your cheek, smiling as if asking for a reward. You leaned in and kissed him on the screen, softly responding with a soft 'mm.'

Finally, Tenna quietly and cautiously asks you, "My wish...... Can it come true? ”

He doesn't ask for much, but if only you could look at him, if only your eyes could be with him, and your heart was only with him, that would be great. Tenna knew it was just a wish, and he might never live to see this day, but as long as ...... Even if you gave him even a little false hope, he would be happy to accept it, but he also knew you wouldn't lie.

He looked at you expectantly, and you just gently rubbed his antenna, softly saying, "I told you, I'm not Santa Claus. Nor can I guarantee your wish will come true." ”

He shrank again, just curling up beside you, his voice muffled and probably tinged with tears. He knew very well that you probably wouldn't say it out loud, and that you wouldn't give him even a single false promise, yet he was still so heartbroken and helpless. Tenna thought, he should have gotten used to it long ago, and he had asked for answers again and again, but when he heard your denial, that part of his chest still ached unbearably, as if a dense, biting pain was biting away. It was as if the heart of the Dark People was shattering once again, and Tenna could only carefully piece it together again and again.

So he cautiously asked in a low voice, "So...... So do you care about me?" He hoped for an answer that could bring him a little comfort, even if it wasn't the answer he truly wanted to hear, but just getting a little from you would make him satisfied.

He just wants a little love you can give him with all his heart; he's not greedy—but in fact, Tenna knows he's greedy, wanting the present yet forever, wanting part but wanting everything. Yet he thinks he's not wrong—after all, he loves you wholeheartedly. In his world, there's only you, and only you are what matters most. So he always hopes you can love him wholeheartedly, if only he were your whole heart in your world.

You remained silent, gently stroking his head, but didn't answer his question, only giving another answer: "If I didn't care about you, even if you were in danger right in front of me, even if you died before my eyes, I wouldn't have looked at you again." Your voice was somewhat cold, and Tenna's body stiffened. He probably didn't know you would give that answer, nor did he know you would say that. Seeing tears about to roll down his screen again, you sighed, held the head of the TV, and looked at him, repeating yourself once more.

"So, you clearly know that I care about you, I love you, so why do you still ask?" You don't understand, so what's the point of repeating these two sentences over and over?

Tenna stammered and didn't answer your question. He just gently rubbed against your palm, savoring the warmth those two sentences brought him, the comfort they bring, and even the love that was just right now.

You didn't understand what Tenna was really thinking. You just scratched his chin and withdrew your hand. You picked up your phone to check the time, then casually placed it on the coffee table and stood up: "I'm going to take a shower. Let's talk later." With that, you walked to your bedroom to start tidying clothes. Tenna watched you walk into the bathroom, followed by the sound of rushing water. He imagined your figure in the shower, his face flushing again. He hurriedly shook his head to stop his thoughts. Then, Tenna looked at your phone casually placed on the table. He had seen it countless times before, but ...... He never opened it, because he knew you wouldn't like him interfering in your affairs.

But tonight, whether he's lost in thought or for some reason, Tenna sneaks a glance at the bathroom. He estimates you'll be out in ten or twenty minutes, so he picks up your phone and sneaks a look. He tells himself that just one glance is enough.

The unlock password for the phone was theirs...... On the day they met, Tenna didn't expect that you would always remember. The lock screen was open, and he couldn't quite understand the other apps. He just clicked on the exact same Contacts app as on his phone, and then he discovered—or perhaps as if ecstatic—that in your contacts list, there was no one else except him, and everything else was blank.

Tenna thought, maybe...... Maybe the wish he once made in his heart, a wish he thought was impossible to fulfill, was so easily fulfilled. To you, he truly is the one you look at wholeheartedly, the only one you care about. For you...... Is it like being the most important person in your heart? Is he the only one in your whole world?

Tenna's nose couldn't help but bloom with more flowers. He couldn't wait to share in this joy, but he could only suppress his joy, because he couldn't possibly ask you about it, he couldn't ...... tell you about peeking at your phone. He doesn't know how you'll react, and he doesn't dare gamble on that possibility.

Tenna just acted guilty and put her phone back on the coffee table, comparing it to try to keep it in its original spot. Then Tenna sat upright on the sofa, wanting to stop the endless blooming of his nose, but the flowers kept popping up. Helpless, Tenna could only cover his nose and try to keep his screen pitch black. He nervously glanced at the bathroom—you were still inside. Tenna made up his mind, went into the kitchen, grabbed a piece of cake, and then entered their shared room and closed the door.

He hesitated for a moment, then his nose started to bloom again, and he slowly typed a text message for you: [Dear, please come to your room with your eyes covered, then take it off. I've prepared a surprise for you!] ]

He touched his chest, which was pounding violently. Tenna took a deep breath, then spread the cakes on himself, then stuck the fruit from the top of the cake onto the cream on his body. Afterwards, he smiled and lay back on the bed, letting the flowers on his nose pop up and then fall. Around him, many flowers gathered, and petals kept falling, making it look like a fairyland.

After showering, you put on a towel and come out. Casually picking up your phone, you glance at the message and see the message. After hesitating for a moment, you pick up an eye mask from the coffee table and cover your eyes, heading toward the room. You're curious about what kind of surprise it is.
