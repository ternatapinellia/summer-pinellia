[Merge] Collar Three

This story features a more S personality and a more M tenna. Some chapters also contain R18 content.

You woke up groggily, still exhausted and sore, and you weren't exactly very clear-headed. But...... The light was on, and you smelled the aroma of toasted bread—probably Tenna got up first to prepare breakfast. You sat up, holding your forehead, changed your clothes, and tried hard to prove you could still get out of bed. Then you got up and slowly walked toward the dining table, sitting down in your usual spot.

Tenna placed the baked bread and jam on the table, along with freshly fried eggs still sizzling and two slices of bacon smoking. She placed the knife, fork, and plate in front of you, then sat down opposite you. You saw his screen lit up, and the antenna was upright—probably hoping you'd give him a compliment.

You leisurely spread jam and put bread in your mouth, the heat just right. It seems he listened to what you said yesterday and calculated the events you'd likely wake up for—after all, your wake-up time is quite fixed.

You put down the bread in your hand, nodded for once, and gave Tenna a gentle smile: "Yeah, well done. The taste is exactly what I like." Then you saw Tenna's antenna wobbling unobtrusively, her nose sticking out, and a little flower sprouting from her nose tip. You actually smiled inwardly, but you didn't show it on your face, just slowly eating breakfast.

Your movements were elegant yet quick. Soon, you finished breakfast. Tenna took the dishes, knives, and forks to wash them. Of course, he had already had breakfast. You squinted at the table and rested for a moment. Hearing his cautious footsteps, you opened your eyes and looked at Tenna, who was cautiously approaching you. He smiled awkwardly at you: "I...... I thought you were asleep. I was even thinking of carrying you to bed to sleep. ”

But you just shook your head softly, "I still remember, today I have to go out with you." ”

Tenna immediately straightened up. Whether it was just my imagination or not, you always felt his screen was a little brighter. Of course, soon it was about the same brightness as usual. His voice was a bit excited but also a bit cautious: "Then...... Then, this time, can we go to the park together...... Shall we have a picnic? "He wants to spend more time with you, and he wants to ...... He wants others to see that he is loved by you, at least ...... He wants others to think he is.

You looked up at him. Tenna's screen had gone dark, and the antenna was drooping. Probably because he hadn't received your reply, he felt you didn't agree, so he hurriedly said, "Alright, actually I just wanted you to stay with me a bit longer—"

But before he could finish, you interrupted him. You just nodded and said softly, "Sure." ”

Tenna's voice stopped. He probably hadn't expected to hear the answer, so his screen slowly lit up, the antenna stood upright, and you see another little flower sprouting from his nose, while he was just busy with excitement.

"Oh...... First, you need to bring a basket. Darling, what do you think of this? "Tenna said as she excitedly picked up a basket. You glanced at it quickly—it was probably something everyone else would use for a picnic. So you nodded and responded with a hum.

He enthusiastically went to pick out the rest again.

"How about that...... Dear, do you prefer red tablecloths, yellow or blue? "He put down the basket, picked up a few tablecloths, and showed them to you. You glanced at them and thought the color didn't really matter, but today's weather was pretty nice, right?

So you think for a moment and say, "Then, the blue one." ”

He muttered as he opened the fridge again, folded the blue tablecloth, and put it in the basket: "Oh...... First, the red bean paste bread and daifuku I bought yesterday, plus some bread and snacks from the bakery...... Put on a few plates, and prepare hot water and tea set...... He was busy tidying up all sorts of things, and you just rested your chin on your hand, yawned, and watched him busy.

Hearing your yawn, Tenna's body stiffened. He turned to look at you, his voice a bit stammered: "Dear...... Darling, do you want to rest for a while? Can't we go together...... "Well, it's okay......" As he said this, his head lowered, the flower on his nose withered and fell, the screen went pitch black, the antenna drooped, and his whole body shrank a little.

You sighed softly and rubbed your temples: "I'm fine. You've been waiting for a long time, haven't you? Let's set off." ”

Tenna just let out a muffled 'oh'. His body had grown a bit larger, but the screen still didn't light up. Just like yesterday, he naturally picked you up, the antenna still hanging in front of the screen. His other hand was holding the picnic basket, but his movements were slow. After a long while, he still hadn't left the house, as if waiting for you to regret it.

You yawned again, kissed his antenna, then pressed your head against his still cold screen and closed your eyes. Soon, your breathing became even, almost like you had fallen asleep.

Tenna, on the other hand, became at a loss. He carefully held you, bent down as he walked out of the house, then gently closed the door as if afraid of waking you, and walked out onto the street.

His screen lit up again, becoming somewhat warmer. You muttered and rubbed against his screen. Tenna's body only stiffened further. Then, his heart burst with joy, feeling that light happiness again. He was certain he was very happy now, and Duan Duan could never be happier. He just gently rubbed your cheek with the screen.

Then, cautiously, a smiling face appeared on the screen, a flower blooming at the tip of his nose, leading you forward at a pace that wasn't exactly fast. Of course, there would be passing Dark Commoners, some even knowing Tenna. After all, it's not just about making money—they also have to go out to prepare meals for you. Those Dark Folk wanted to greet Tenna, but Tenna always gestured to silence with her index finger in front of her mouth, then pointed at you sleeping soundly in his arms and shook her head. Of course, his face was still full of happiness, especially about your slight dependence on him...... He just felt a bit flattered.

The Dark People understood, also nodding at Tenna with a smile, watching her figure as she led you away.

Tenna always thought that if only this time could be a little longer, if the road could go farther and have no end, how wonderful that would be...... From time to time, he would lower his head to look at you, as if if you could disappear from his arms for even a second.

But the road does have an end. Tenna saw the park too. His antenna drooped slightly, and he slowly carried you inside, then found a patch of fresh grass—not far from people, but definitely not disturbed—and put down the picnic basket.

Then he gently poked your cheek with his finger and softly called out, as if he couldn't bear to wake you up: "Wake up, Y/N, we've arrived." ”

Actually, you haven't been sound asleep at all—just a light sleep. You can't just let yourself sleep outside without rest. Even though you're in Tenna's arms, you can feel his carefulness. You know how gentle his breathing is. Only then do you rub your eyes, pretend you've just woken up, look around, and see Tenna patiently waiting for you. Then you tap Tenna's screen: "Put me down." ”

Tenna obediently put you down. He carefully laid out the tablecloth—or rather, your picnic cloth—then laid out the tableware, arranged the bread and snacks he had brought from home, and finally set up the tea set. He brewed a pot of hot tea with hot water, poured a cup, and eagerly offered it to you.

You took a gentle sip of the hot tea. The temperature was just right, and you sat down in the empty spot on the picnic cloth, right next to Tenna. You slightly lifted your head and saw Tenna looking at you with concern. The antenna hung down, and you gently stroked it and asked, "What's wrong?" ”

He nervously tugged at his clothes, seemingly adjusting his tie again, probably to look a bit better, and softly asked, "Sorry...... I just want to know, darling, what would you like to eat first? ”

You tilted your head slightly and glanced at him, then at the pastries on the tablecloth. At that moment, a good idea popped into your head. You winked at Tenna and said, "Mm...... I want to have some sandwiches now. ”

Tenna seemed stunned for a moment, then hurriedly picked up two slices of bread. For some reason, his picnic basket actually contained some sausages, bacon, lettuce, and a jar of salad. He carefully arranged the two slices of bacon, sandwiched salad in the middle, and stuffed some sausage slices. Finally, somehow he got the knife and cut the sandwich in half, cutting it diagonally, turning it into a simple sandwich.

You took a bite and gave Tenna a thumbs up. No matter how many times you try, you have to say Tenna's cooking is really impressive. The sausage and bacon were probably homemade by him to suit your taste; they weren't too salty, and the salad dressing helped balance the flavor. Overall, you enjoyed eating them with great enjoyment.

And Tenna, who got your thumb, was happily shaking the antenna.

Of course, you wouldn't be stingy with praise, but since you're not good with words and don't say much, that's all you can say. Yet Tenna was very happy; he knew this was your approval of what he had done—before, it seemed he had never lived like this. He only remembered the expectant glances he cast at you again and again, but you remained indifferent, while Tenna...... could only feel disappointed, but despite his disappointment, he could only revolve around you—or rather, he was willing.

…… After all, just calling his name is enough to make him happy for a long time, let alone watching him all the time. Tenna propped her chin up, just sitting on the tablecloth, beaming with a smile, a little flower blooming from her nose, just staring at you the whole time. Of course, you could sense his gaze, but you didn't care. You simply picked up another red bean bun from the plate, slowly stuffed it into your mouth, didn't look up at Tenna, and just took another sip of tea.

Tenna, however, was already satisfied. His antenna flickered, and he just stared at you. This place was quiet, but occasionally people would pass by. When they saw you and Tenna sitting close together, they all smiled knowingly, and Tenna felt happy. He only needed to look down at you and already saw all his own happiness. He knew...... No matter what, in the eyes of outsiders, you were probably a perfect couple. He desperately longed for you to admit something, but as long as you tacitly accepted...... That would be enough.

Lost in these wild thoughts, Tenna didn't react for a moment when you handed him a large fortune cake. Until he heard your leisurely voice: "Alright, have something to eat too. Weren't you the one who called me here for a picnic?" "Even though you know very well that he probably doesn't really want a picnic, he just wants to spend a little more time with you. You don't quite understand why he stays by your side all the time, but maybe...... That's fine too.

After all, you really do need someone who is wholeheartedly yours.

But in any case, no matter what, you still have a bit of tolerance toward your lover. Seeing his smile, probably lost in thought, with a hint of teasing, you watch Tenna hurriedly bite down on the daifuku, then leisurely take another sip of hot tea and softly ask, "What are you thinking about?" ”

But just those few words startled Tenna like a sudden thunderclap. His antenna instinctively trembled a few times, the screen flickered on and off, and finally his antenna drooped gently, looking a bit aggrieved. You couldn't understand what he was really upset about, even though you had already fulfilled the request he made.

As for Tenna...... He really feels awkward asking you, asking if he will be part of your life journey, asking you...... Are you really a real couple? If so, why have you made no commitments until now? He is clearly your lover and has done all those things with you, but in the end, what does he mean to you?

He hugged his knees tightly, and the head of the TV hung down. You looked at him, waiting for him to speak. He would always say something, so you heard him speak cautiously, almost trembling: "I...... I'm wondering, what will my dear Y/N become in the future? Will ...... What will you do in the future? "Will he be there in your future?"

You tilted your head, not realizing he was thinking about this question, so you thought carefully before answering, "Hmm...... My future? I hadn't thought about that before; after all, the most important thing is the present. "You said slowly, and it was rare for you to say something this long.

"However...... If you truly want an answer. I will do my job well, use my savings to buy a new home, and the clothes and furniture I wear. Maybe, maybe I will still have the free time and energy to travel again. "You spoke calmly about your original plan. You are very confident you can achieve this goal, and by then, you will be free.

But as Tenna listened, his figure suddenly shrank even smaller, and the antenna completely drooped. When you turned your head to look, you saw his screen was wet again, and big tears began to fall again.

You sighed silently, picked up a large fortune cake, and brought it close to his screen. He opened his mouth and ate it, while your voice remained calm: "What's wrong with you? If you hadn't told me, I would never have known." Your lover always seemed to like to wash his face in tears. You didn't know why he was like this, but you knew he was sad. You couldn't control all his emotions, which made you feel a bit irritated—though maybe your lover is just such a troublesome creature.

So you just watched his screen glow slightly, probably chewing on that daifuku, while you gently wiped his screen with your hand, then leaned in and softly kissed the TV casing. You felt the screen seem a little warm, at least not as cold as before, and you just quietly watched him, listening to his sobs. He whimpered a few times before finally speaking.

"Then...... What about me? "He doesn't dare ask why he's not in your future, nor does he dare ask...... Will you even consider him in your own future? He really wants to question you: what does he really mean to you? Is he just a trivial plaything? Or an insignificant object? But he doesn't dare to ask you. If, to you...... He really isn't important, then he hopes that at least, at this very moment, Tenna is an important presence to you, not something to be ignored.

You tilted your head, a bit puzzled, but in the end, you still fed him a large fortune, and he obediently ate it. You simply said coldly, "That's your future, it has nothing to do with me." ”

After a pause, you hear your lover's sobs grow louder. If no one else were around, you'd be afraid you'd be met with a bunch of condemning looks. He wailed loudly, like the sound of a kettle boiling......

You had no choice but to add, "But since you said you'll never take off the collar...... Then now, take me home and hold me." As you spoke, you reached out your hand to him. Tenna's crying gradually faded, but the screen was still damp. He obediently began to tidy up the napkin, finally picked up the basket again, and looked at you. You walked toward him, naturally reaching out both hands. Tenna held the basket with one hand, while the other picked you up and slowly walked forward. His posture remained unchanged; your toes almost touched the ground.

But you just leaned in, gently kissed his screen, and spoke softly, almost as if inviting: "You promised you wouldn't throw away the collar, and you wouldn't let me lose yours." So, what do you think your future will be like? ”

His screen was still wet, but his figure had grown a bit larger, and his voice sounded a bit muffled and damp: "...... I'll always be by your side." Tenna said. Although he still felt a bit dissatisfied and anxious, more than anything, it was a sense of joy...... Or perhaps a feeling as if dust had settled. At least, Tenna knew that to you, he wasn't insignificant.

You reached out satisfied, patted Tenna's TV, then pressed your cheek against his wet screen and whispered softly, probably not hoping he could hear clearly: "Mm, as long as you don't throw away the collar." ”

And then you stopped talking. Although you didn’t manage to give him a completely satisfying answer, Tenna also understood… or perhaps, he only realized after experiencing countless times that these words from you were already very rare, so his antenna no longer drooped, and the screen became slightly warm, at least when you leaned against him, it just felt cozy. You closed your eyes and started dozing against Tenna, your breathing becoming steady, and Tenna thought you were asleep, lowering his footsteps and not daring to make a sound.

But you remembered how far his steps would reach, and you quietly counted them. Only when you were almost home did you rub your eyes and, pretending to just wake up, lightly straighten in Tenna’s arms, then pointed to the shop on the street corner: 'Tenna, stop there for a moment.'

Tenna held you as she said and stopped in front of the pastry shop, then bent down slightly so you could talk to the old lady inside.

The old lady just smiled and said, "Oh my, the young couple is here again." ”

You just smiled and said, 'Mm. Five cream puffs, five matcha daifuku, all to go.' Then you took out your wallet and paid. The old lady packed the pastries in paper bags and handed them to you.

Tenna carried you back toward home. You also took out a cream puff and stuffed it into his mouth, then leaned in and kissed the corner of his mouth.

It wasn’t until you reached your front door that all the pastries were finished, and Tenna’s screen lit up again. He happily set you down, took out the key, and opened the door.

After dinner, you pulled him along to play video games to pass the time, then after washing up, you lay down normally on the bed and closed your eyes.

Tenna carefully pulled the blanket back and lay down next to you, snuggling up close. It seemed that even just sleeping next to you would make him happy for a long time. You gently rubbed in his direction, then tilted your head and rested your head on his chest. Tenna's body instantly stiffened, but hearing your steady breathing, he thought you were probably sound asleep. So he carefully placed his arm on top of you, maintaining a half-hugging posture, awkwardly falling asleep.
