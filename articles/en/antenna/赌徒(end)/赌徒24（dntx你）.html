Gambler 24 (dntx you)

…… You really don't know what love really is. Is love careful? Is it always wanting to look at the other person? Is it always hoping the other person can look at you? Is love possession? Is love the hope that the other person can become happier, better? Is love companionship? Is it a form of protection?

Or is love hurt, the feeling of pain and pleasure when your soul is torn apart? Is love destruction? Is it the same as what your parents said and brought to you? You don't know, so when you heard Tenna's words, you were stunned for a moment.

"Yes...... Is it love? Do you really love me? "You seemed uncertain, your voice trembling as you asked this question over and over.

"I love you." His voice was faint, as if whispering, but you could still hear it clearly.

"Do you love me?" He keeps asking you this question over and over. You both search for the answer you want or don't want in each other, yet both feel empty and unsatisfied.

"I love you...... But what kind of love is yours? "Is it care, is it possession, is it companionship, or is it hurt, destruction, and hatred?

"Why don't you just feel it yourself and you'll know?" Tenna's voice returned to normal. He still seemed a bit embarrassed and simply turned his head away, while you quietly looked at Tenna, staring at his pitch-black screen, tears uncontrollably rolling down your face again.

Tenna leaned over somewhat awkwardly, licking the tears from your cheek with the tip of her tongue. Then he cleared his throat and helped you clean up. Now you both sat silently on the living room sofa, seemingly feeling a bit awkward. Neither of you had the intention to speak first.

After a while, you quietly reached out your hand. You quietly reached out and gently placed it on Tenna's hand. Compared to that, the difference in hand size was huge. Tenna only quietly turned her head to glance at you. He could only see the top of your head, and he silently turned his palm and gently held your hand. His movements were light, but he held your hand tightly and refused to let go. You were silent for a moment, as if you both had to maintain this position for a while, so you whispered, "I...... I ordered you to watch a movie with me. ”

There was also a projector hanging on the living room wall. Tenna looked up, not resisting you watching other electronic devices...... After all, you were watching with him, so he just gave a soft hum, feeling only the joy of being able to watch a movie with you.

You randomly pick out a film on the projector, and in the end, you pick a story that seems to be a suspense film...... This movie is called "Class 3-A," roughly telling the story of a girl in her class who commits suicide. The teacher uses public pressure to force all those who bullied that girl to come forward and clarify their actions.

You were enjoying the film with great interest, since you really liked the genre and used suspenseful foreshadowing quite well. Tenna just tugged your hand and whispered, "Why not watch a romance movie?" ”

You paused, looking at Tenna's cold face, and belatedly realized he probably didn't like this genre—it was you who liked it, and you invited him to watch a movie with you, and you chose this film on your own initiative.

So you hit pause, lowered your head, and mumbled softly, "Sorry, I wanted to watch this one. I didn't ask if you liked it, so I made my own decision......

Tenna just held your hand tightly, her voice very soft. Maybe it was the first time he'd done this. In the past, he would take others' apologies and what others did for granted, but today he felt a bit flustered. His fingers couldn't help but pinch your palm and whispered, "No need to apologize. You won today, so it's only right for me to watch a movie with you." ”

Your face was just a little flushed, and you stammered, "I...... Well, well, what do you prefer to watch? "You gave Tenna the choice, you think about it, you really forgot to ask, you need to remind yourself...... You have a lover, so you don't have to think only of yourself anymore.

Tenna just silently watched the movies displayed on the projector. He pressed the remote slowly, watching the movies until he finally picked one and said, "Then let's watch this." ”

You watch the movie he chose, called "Journey of the Heart." According to the synopsis, it tells the story of two childhood sweethearts who quarrel because neither of them wants to tell each other their feelings, leading to countless funny incidents, but in the end, the distance between them still grows closer.

Your fingertips also dug at Tenna's palm. You didn't say much, just controlled the remote to play the show. Meanwhile, Tenna turned off the living room light.

For a moment, apart from the intertwined breathing and the sounds coming from the film, there was no other movement.

You see, this film—as it plays, you realize that both the male and female leads have suffered other hurts, which is why they became childhood sweethearts, supporting each other. But because they've suffered so much, even though the atmosphere between them is ambiguous, neither is willing to take the first step, leading to frequent funny incidents over their identities. In the end, it's a game of truth or dare held by mutual friends that finally reveals their feelings.

You silently watch the film, tears streaming down your cheeks. Then you feel a pair of hands—Tenna's—clumsily wiping away the tears from your cheeks. But when you look up, you see that although his screen is off, through the reflection, you can still see wet marks on his face.

Until the film ends, you two sit there motionless until Tenna turns on the living room light.

He said nothing, just walked straight to the kitchen. You stared blankly at his back, wanting to say something, but couldn't get anything out. You just sat blankly on the sofa, letting tears stream down your cheeks.

It wasn't until Tenna walked over to the table with her tray, then came up to you, and half-squatted down. His voice was dry and a bit stiff: "Don't cry, let's eat first." As he spoke, he handed you a cup of hot orange juice. You took the glass and drank a few sips. The warm juice really helped calm your body.

You broke into a smile through your tears, just looked at him and smiled: "Alright, let's go eat." After saying that, you sat down at the dining table. Only then did you realize that Tenna had prepared an omurice for lunch today, and while you were still dazed, he walked over to you with a blank expression, picked up ketchup, and squeezed a big heart into your omurice. Of course, there were also Tenna's and your names below.

You paused. You remembered the fireworks on the day of the summer festival. Even though he showed no expression as he spoke, you still saw the flowers popping up from his nose. Looking at the omurice in front of you, even though Tenna said nothing, it seemed like he had said everything. You just smiled, stood up, picked up the ketchup, and drew a heart on his omurice.

Then you all sat down, almost as if in tacit agreement, not looking at each other anymore. You probably knew that when facing each other, you only had rosy cheeks, and you knew that when you looked at each other, you could only see rosy faces.

You enjoy your own heartfelt omurice. You don't even know when Tenna has become so familiar with your taste. This omurice is neither too salty nor too bland, and according to your taste, it's made with vegetable curry. There's not much spiciness, just a light flavor. On the side are slices of broccoli and potatoes, along with a piece of chicken cutlet, fried to a crisp and just the right amount of flavor.

You finished your dinner contentedly. Tenna carried the plates to the kitchen as usual to clean up, then took off the childish apron with a heart. He pulled you back to sit on the sofa. You still held hands and wouldn't let go. Your mind went blank, since you didn't know what to do next. You just sat on the sofa, lost in thought, and Tenna didn't know what to do either. He just held your hand tightly, refusing to let go, as if once he let go, everything that happened today was just a dream for him.

His thoughts were racing, never stopping for a moment. His mechanical heart was pounding, roughly ...... He was savoring what had happened that morning, enjoying your hand holding his, enjoying this unknowingly unknown love. But he knew that at least—even just a little—you cared about him. And precisely because of this, his heart was beating greedily, telling him he wanted more, needed more, more emotions to satisfy his desires and fill his heart. So Tenna couldn't help but turn back and sneak a glance at you. You seemed unaware of his gaze, just lost in thought. Tenna felt a bit delighted that you hadn't noticed, but then he felt a bit dissatisfied—he hoped you would notice him, that you could always see him.

Then he started to feel a bit down, his antenna drooping, and he began to suspect that you didn't love him as much as you claimed. After all, what you said yourself was unclear. He started to suspect that maybe you didn't care about him that much, just because he seemed to care, and you seemed to care in response to him too. He started to worry...... Maybe you say you love him, but your mind is unclear. Maybe you're thinking about someone else—he can't guarantee you're thinking only of him. But Tenna has to admit, no matter what, he always subconsciously thinks of you—every frown and smile, your red eyes and cold tears when you cry, your vivid words, your calls, your fair skin, the fireworks explosion of excitement and heartbeat when he hears you say 'love', and your sincere smile.

He couldn't help but squeeze your hand a little tighter. You frowned, instinctively a bit dissatisfied. He gripped a bit tightly, making your wrist hurt a little. But looking at his pitch-black screen and his drooping antenna, you didn't know why he was upset—you, well, actually worried Tenna might not care about you that much, but that thought quickly dispelled by yourself. After all, nothing can be fake on the day of the summer festival. He definitely cares about you, but you have no idea how much he cares.

You just ...... gently lifted up, leaned in to kiss his screen, and his screen became wet. You just lowered your head, a bit nervous, and said, "Um...... I, I ordered you to go to my room, lie down on my bed, and then ...... and sleep with me. ”

Tenna was stunned for a moment, then his screen lit up again. You seemed to see the corners of his mouth curl up in a quick smile. He seemed to smile, but soon after, his expression cooled again. He simply nodded calmly and then quickly walked toward your room.

After you washed up, you lay down on the bed. Of course, when you finished tidying up, you would find Tenna already lying there, arms crossed, his mouth curved downward, still wearing a sour expression. But flowers were blooming from the tip of his nose, drifting down one by one, leaving the bed covered with petals he had fallen.

You couldn't help but smile, climbed onto the bed, looked down at him, and tapped his nose: "Now the bed is covered in petals, how am I supposed to sleep?" ”

Tenna just let out a muffled 'oh', got up, and swept the petals that had fallen from the bed onto the floor. Then, his screen closed again, leaving only a pitch-black patch.

You lay helplessly beside him and whispered, "Turn on the screen. I actually quite like how you look—you're really cute." ”

You don't hear Tenna's answer, just ...... You see him turn the screen back on, his nose still blooming with large clusters of flowers, yet he remains expressionless.

You just grabbed his arm, gently nudged him into his embrace, and buried your head in his chest. You felt his body stiffen for a moment, then slowly relaxed, turned sideways, and hugged you tightly, pulling you into his embrace.

…… You're dreaming again, but this time, you didn't wake up scared. After all, in the dream, Tenna with all kinds of expressions surrounded you, saying they liked you as if hoping for your answer. But in the dream, you couldn't say a word.

Until you wake up from a deep sleep.
