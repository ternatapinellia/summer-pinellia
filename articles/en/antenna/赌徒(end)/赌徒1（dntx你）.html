Gambler 1 (dntx you)

Heaven have a mercy—you were just wandering out for a walk after settling in the Dark World. How could you see a TV person lying on the edge of a cliff, looking like he's lost his legs?

Moreover...... It looked like he was left here and no one was going to care about him anymore. Although you don't like meddling, you can still be considered kind-hearted. After thinking it over, you went home and brought out a cart to move him onto it, then struggled to pull it carefully. After all, you saw this was a TV person, who knows there might be many intricate parts on his body. You wouldn't dare to bump him around.

After finally carrying him home, you look at the large TV he's unconscious and the two broken legs you brought back, feeling a bit worried. Fortunately, your parents forced you to study science, so you know how to repair computer circuits, so naturally you can repair TVs too. So you dig through the CRT user manual you've collected out of boredom, connect the wiring on his broken leg, finally put on the insulation tape, and then clapp, lie back on the bed, hum in satisfaction, and you're done! Reward yourself with a nap!

Half-awake, you frowned because you heard a sobbing sound like crying—not loud, but definitely enough to keep you awake. You angrily opened your eyes to see what was disturbing your peaceful sleep, but you realized the sound was coming from your side. Turning to look, you realized...... It was the TV person you brought back, now shrunk into a tiny ball, big enough to hold in your arms, and still whimpering continuously.

You gently moved a couple of times closer. He was probably still lost in his own emotions, still sobbing and sobbing, curling up into a small ball. The pitch-black screen was wet, with tears still dripping down.

You're a bit worried. You know about the existence of the People of Darkness, but no one tells you what to do if someone else cries. You know, your parents will just slap you, complaining that your crying is too loud, that you've cried too much and that your family's good fortune is gone. But you won't do that, because hearing those words won't make you happy, so you won't do that.

You racked your brains for a long time, but in the end, you gently placed your hand on his shoulder and tentatively asked in a low voice, "Hello?" ”

The TV seemed to pause for a moment, but it was still sobbing, its voice broken and intermittent: "I...... I can't give you anything, why, should ...... Save me? ”

You look at him in distress, so you reach out and pat his head again. You think, when you cry, getting a pat on the head would make you happy—maybe he would do the same.

"Hmm...... I don't want anything, I just don't want to see a perfectly fine person die there...... Ah, for the Dark People, it's damage, right? "You nodded, your voice very soft. You really didn't think about anything when you brought him back.

"But if you say that, does that mean there's nowhere to go...... Then you can stay here for now, pay me the rent first, and pay me later, that's fine......" You kept nagging, thinking about how you'd always behaved, probably because you had no way out, and you would rack your brains trying to figure out what to do next. But you saw that he didn't make a sound, just curled up and sobbing, while his screen was flooded with even bigger tears.

You scratched your head, so you took a piece of candy from the bedside table, spread it to his lips, and whispered softly, "Don't cry, eat something sweet. You have to be happy." ”

He didn't answer your question, just silently swallowed the candy. You don't even know where his screen opened to swallow the candy, but you saw him stop whimpering, though the screen was still a bit damp, and you finally breathed a sigh of relief.

You see this little TV person sit up from his soaking wet bed, the dark screen staring at you, showing no other reaction. You simply sit cross-legged on the floor too, because squatting all the time would be exhausting.

You waved a finger in front of him: "Alright, you should be more alert, right? Now I'm going to start questioning." ”

You see the little TV guy nodding.

You hugged your knees and asked first, "So who exactly are you, and how should I address you?" ”

“…… Anton Tenna, you can call me whatever you like. You...... Where did you find me? "His voice trembled slightly.

You shrugged: "Ah, on a cliff. You should be able to tell I'm a citizen of light, or a human, right?" ”

Tenna nodded lightly, but then asked, "Then...... Why does that Light Tribe choose the Dark World? His voice was muffled, the antenna dropped, and his body seemed to tremble slightly. You don't understand why he reacted this way. Is it strange for the Light Tribe to appear in the Dark World?

But you just smiled indifferently and shrugged: "Ah, how did it turn into you asking me a question?" Alright, I have to tell you, I ...... It was because I didn't want to have any more contact with my parents that I went to the underworld. ”

Your words were relaxed, but when you said it, you couldn't help but zone out a little. You remembered that day—the day you completely decided to move to the Dark World. After all...... You're not short of money. You can go wherever you want; you belong to yourself.

"But what exactly happened to cause you to be so seriously injured?" You looked at Tenna's legs with a curious look. Clearly, they were now able to move freely.

“…… I struck a deal with the Howling Knights, the deal fell through, and that's it. "His emotions seemed to have stabilized somewhat. In any case, the screen was still pitch black, but he wasn't crying anymore.

You sized him up, then nodded thoughtfully: "...... Who is this? Never heard of him, but he probably wouldn't chase you to kill you, right? ”

Tenna shook her head, but then asked urgently, "Have you heard of TV WORLD?" ”

You frowned and thought for a moment, then shook your head as well: "I haven't heard of it. All I know is that the place where we live now is called Castle Town. Is that a place very important to you? If so, I'll help you find out later." ”

Tenna nodded but shook her head again, her tone gloomy: "...... That world has probably already been sealed. ”

You didn't quite understand the word, but seeing his downcast expression, you didn't press further. Instead, you opened a nearby drawer and took out two more candies—one opened and put in your mouth, and the other was opened and fed to Tenna. This time, he ate them without hesitation.

You smiled helplessly: "Alright...... Although I still don't know exactly what the situation is, I think you need to stay here for a while. Wait until you find your next place before leaving. ”

As you spoke, your mouth was mumbling a bit hard to hear because you had that candy in your mouth. You felt Tenna's body tremble, then he nodded stiffly.

You stood up and gently picked him up. Well, he really does feel light in your arms. The apartment you rented happens to have a guest room, and you moved in a couple of days ago just finished tidying up. I didn't expect you'd need it today. Tenna seemed even stiffer in your arms. You didn't pay him any mind, just put him on the bed and softly said, "Alright, I think you need to rest for a while. I'll be leaving now." Then you gently closed the door.

You don't know what Tenna made again, but you happened to be a bit thirsty, so you made yourself some floral tea. After a few sips, you put the teapot and teacup on the table, walked to the guest room door, and shouted loudly, "If you're thirsty, there's something on the table to drink!" ”

You heard Tenna respond from inside the room, then ignored him and went back to your room to lie back on the bed and go back to sleep. When you woke up again, you rubbed your eyes and glanced over. You realized it was almost dinner time, so you quickly opened the door and went out to make a simple dinner. Although you couldn't cook, you could at least heat up the semi-prepared dishes.

But as soon as you reached the living room, you heard the sounds of stoves and smoke coming from the kitchen, along with a faint smell of cooking oil. You hurried to the kitchen—what if this guest temporarily staying at your house blew up your kitchen?

But your worries seemed unnecessary, because as soon as you walked into the kitchen, you saw Tenna, now about the same size as before, wearing an apron and heat-resistant gloves, carrying a plate of roasted lamb leg.

You stopped in your tracks, stared at him wide-eyed, and said, "Huh?" You instinctively let out a soft "ah."

Tenna bypassed you and placed the grilled lamb chops on the table. You instinctively turned to stare at him, and Tenna looked back at you. His screen lit up, though his expression seemed a bit cold, and for some reason, his voice sounded a bit tough but weak inside: "What, can't I go into your kitchen?" ”

You shake your head: "That's not ......." But as you say this, all you see is Tenna's back. He walks into the kitchen again, while you sit at the dining table, a bit confused, feeling that something is off.

Next, Tenna placed several plates on the table and brought over the knife and fork. He sat across from you. Neither of you made the first move, but instead fell into an eerie silence.

While you were still struggling with what to say, you heard Tenna's voice: "This is ...... To repay you for saving me today and making you dinner, don't count on me anymore. ”

You sighed, thinking to yourself that you never intended to have him make dinner, and you didn't expect any payment, but on the surface, you just nodded: "Alright, alright." ”

You hesitated for a moment, but finally decided to cut a piece of roasted lamb leg and eat it. You bought this and kept it in the fridge, worrying about what to do. Today, Tenna finished making it, and of course you gladly accepted it.

“…… Also, for the next while, I probably really will be staying here. "Tenna hesitated for a moment, or perhaps hesitated, but his voice still sounded cold.

You just nodded again: "Alright, alright, I know. I'll pay rent for now......." But before you could finish, Tenna interrupted you.

"No, I think...... Although you saved me, I shouldn't repay you like a beast of burden. You're the one who should be a beast of burden. "He said seriously, his face grave.

You whisper, "I didn't make you work like a beast to repay me...... but you felt like Tenna had just glared at you.

So you changed your tune: "Alright, alright, then either I'll be like a beast of burden, or you'll be a horse, right? Let's play a game." As you spoke, you put down your knife and fork, clapped your hands excitedly, and said, "Heavens have had it—after all these years, you've never played a proper game with anyone."

"What game?" He asked briefly. He considered himself a game show host, skilled at any game.

You waved your finger as if teasing her, feeling like Tenna glared at you again, then cleared her throat and said, "It's simple. It's just a bet every day. Whatever you want, the winner can order the loser to do anything, and within that day—sounds fair, right?" ”

"What if the loser refuses to comply?" Tenna crossed his arms, probably thinking the proposal was feasible, but he needed to clarify everything.

"That's simple. If the loser refuses to comply, then the winner will have to work like a beast of burden for the rest of their life." You clapped your hands and announced. Honestly, you never came up with such a shameless idea before. But Tenna insisted on saying something like that. How could you not play a game with him?

Tenna seemed to hesitate for a moment, then gritted his teeth and nodded at you: "Alright." ”

You tilt your head: "Then this game officially starts tomorrow. As for today—well, I saved you, so I asked you to help wash the dishes and such. Isn't that too much?" ”

Tenna gritted her teeth, looking a bit furious: "...... Not too much. ”

"It's settled then. I swear—swear it's a suction swearing, and you won't change for ten thousand years!" As you spoke, you extended your pinky to Tenna. Tenna hesitated for a moment, then extended her pinky too. Compared to him, his body was really big—even his hands were several times bigger than yours. You just smiled and pinky promised him, then began enjoying the dinner.

After eating, Tenna would clean up after all—so you just messed up mutton fat and similar items on the table, watching Tenna grit her teeth as she tidied up, while you just leisurely drank a cup of floral tea, tidied yourself up a bit, and after passing the night, you went back to your room.

You're still smiling when you sleep, after all—you've already started looking forward to what tomorrow will be like.
