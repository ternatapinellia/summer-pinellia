Trainee Boyfriend 14 (tennax you)

You don't have time to savor what breakfast tastes like. You just stuff those two slices of bread into your mouth and hurriedly eat the fried egg. Then you look at Tenna, who is sitting nervously hugging her knees at your door, and finally nods, your tone still soft: "Yeah, it tastes pretty good, but I have to go to work, so I won't have time to savor it carefully." ”

Originally, you planned to throw him out of your house as soon as he came in the morning, but you knew very well that a TV person who looked completely different from others would probably only be sent to a lab for dissection or something if caught. Plus, surveillance is very advanced nowadays; you might accidentally find your own home while investigating, and then you might be arrested and questioned, which would make things especially complicated...... It might even delay you for a long time. After thinking it over, you decided to leave Tenna at home for now.

…… It's definitely not that you've softened your heart; you just think that throwing Tenna out would cause more trouble.

His screen lit up, and it looked like he was in a good mood because he had received your comment...... Well, you don't know how this guy can be so easily happy, because at that moment, you see flowers blooming again on the tip of his nose, and he must be in a good mood.

You shrugged, then walked to the door, picked up your handbag, put on your shoes, and got ready to leave—but before leaving home, you deliberately opened your bag to take a look, then grabbed Tenna, who had somehow slipped into your bag, and placed it on the counter just in case. Before leaving, you checked yourself again, then pulled the shrinking Tenna off your pant leg, sleeve, and even the one sitting on your shoulder. Before closing the door, you glanced at it to confirm he was indeed staying at your home, finally breathed a sigh of relief, and was about to close the door.

Just before closing the door, you heard Tenna's words, and he timidly asked, "Um...... Dear, I, can't I go to work with you? ”

You took a deep breath and tried to stay calm, saying, "No. If you come with me, you'll only cause me trouble...... There have been a lot of people at the company these days, and I don't want you to be caught and taken to the lab for dissection." "That's true. There are indeed a lot of messy people in the company these days, so you won't be causing trouble for yourself."

Tenna's antenna drooped, and he looked as if he had been flattened, just staggering in place. Even his voice became hoarse: "Alright, I understand. My presence is a trouble for you. I can't go with you and cause trouble anymore......" With that, he flattened his way into the room.

You have no idea how he's ended up like this again. Helplessly, he scratched his head and said, "I'm not saying you're a nuisance to me...... It's just that some things are better left unaddressed if I can, you understand? ”

You don't know if he understands, but he seems to not, since he's still a bit flat, just nodding.

You sighed, not knowing what to say, just closed the door and left. You rushed to work by subway, but because talking with Tenna delayed you for a while, you almost arrived at the company right on time to check in. There were a lot of people visiting the company today. After all, the higher-ups were signing some big contract, so you didn't have much work to do. At least you didn't work overtime today and left on time.

You stretched lazily, walked tiredly to your doorstep, opened the door, and yawned. As soon as you opened the door, a wave of aroma greeted you...... It smelled like grilled lamb chops, but you actually had no appetite after a day at work. Then you noticed a gift box on the entrance counter. Curious, you picked it up, and Tenna, still stained with a few drops of oil on the screen, poked out from the kitchen. You saw him wearing a pink heart-shaped apron, though you had no idea where he found it.

And his voice was delighted: "Darling, welcome home!" I ...... "That, that's the gift I prepared for you, because I'm still very sorry about what happened last night......" As he spoke, his voice gradually quieted. You noticed his screen brightness seemed to be dimmed, probably not very enthusiastic. Anyway, he hurried back into the kitchen. Curious, you opened the gift box and took a look. Inside was a piano-style music box, which was indeed quite delicate and beautiful...... Hmm, although you didn't particularly like this thing. After turning the sound, you could see the piano keys were pressed down, which was quite nice.

You sit at the dining table, casually setting the music box aside, while Tenna, wearing large cooking gloves, excitedly brings your grilled lamb chops to the table. Then he places a plate of crispy, fluffy pie on the table and sits across from you, looking eagerly anticipating your evaluation, since he is holding his head with both hands and grinning at you.

You swallowed back what you wanted to say and tried a piece of grilled lamb chop first. It really tasted good, very light, without much pepper or chili, just to your taste, with just a bit of cumin on top. So you gave Tenna a thumbs-up, showing you were very satisfied with the taste of the grilled lamb chop. You watched Tenna's antenna sway, and then a tiny flower appeared at the tip of his nose again. He was grinning foolishly, probably very happy.

You took another bite of the pie, and once again you couldn't help but furrow your brows—oh my god, it's hard to say what this tastes like, it's heavy, a bit sweet, even a bit sticky. Seeing Tenna's expectant expression, you feel too embarrassed to spit out the sweet pastry and swallow it with difficulty.

Then you gave Tenna a thumbs-down and said with difficulty, "Hmm...... Here, um, it's really bad. I should have told you, I don't like sweets anymore, right? ”

Tenna's screen instantly went off again, his tone a bit panicked: "But...... But this is my specialty...... In the past, my subordinates would always praise my delicious food......

“…… But what if they say your cooking isn't good? What would you do?" Your tone was flat.

"Then I'll dock their salaries heavily, and their monthly performance evaluation will be zero." Tenna said without hesitation. He really would do that, since he never thought anything he made tasted bad, since he had done it using exactly the same steps as Torriel...... He just added a bit more sugar.

"See, that's exactly why anyone dares say your cooking isn't good?" You sighed and shrugged. Maybe after being lectured a couple of times, Tenna felt a bit guilty, but Tenna involuntarily shrank. He lowered his head, his hands obediently resting on his knees, anxiously waiting for your evaluation.

"Very sweet, very greasy, terrible, really not to my taste...... But that doesn't mean your cooking isn't tasty, it's just that it doesn't suit me. Maybe next time you can add less sugar. "You made your judgment fairly and objectively, while Tenna just let out a gloomy 'oh,' probably still not very happy.

You blink, pick up the music box beside you, and with a hint of teasing, ask, "Also, who told you I like music boxes?" "Although it's actually quite fun, and you really like it just as a decoration...... But that doesn't mean you really like music boxes.

Tenna said dryly, "But in the game, when asked what you like, you say you like music boxes...... So he spent a long time searching and found scattered woodworking tools in your room. He painstakingly checked tutorials to carve this music box, and he even broke several prototypes.

You couldn't help but laugh and cry: "That's because none of your four options are what I wanted to choose. I can only pick the one I like the most...... Think about all your options, right? Books? Forgive me, I stopped liking reading after I started working. Game discs? Hmm...... I'm not some game-loving otaku, why would I like this? I like anything Tenna gave me? It's not like I like something you gave me...... There are some things I just can't like, no matter what. "After you finish, you sigh, and as you spoke, Tenna's figure shrank smaller and smaller, until finally, he curled up in his chair like a tiny one, looking very dejected.

You don't want him to end up like this; you just want to make things clear...... You couldn't help but sigh again, stiffly picking him up and lightly patting his head: "Alright, it's not that I don't like your gift. Actually, I do quite like it. So, my favorability has changed to Zheng Ju." You glanced at the guest room next to you. You didn't tidy up, so I guess Tenna hasn't tidied up either...... You don't really need to tidy up for him. That's probably how it is. You walked into your own room, put Tenna back on the floor he had made, looking expressionless, while you just got into bed. Turning around, you said dryly, "The guest room isn't tidyed up. You should just sleep here for now." ”

It's definitely not that you've softened your heart.

You turned off the light, and Tenna curled up there tiny. To be honest, after hearing your answer, he was still happy...... Listening to your gradually gentle breathing, he couldn't help but hold his breath. His body returned to its original size, and then he couldn't help but gently climb onto your bed, hands on his chest. He nervously lay in the corner of the bed, but his tail involuntarily reached toward you.
