Trainee Boyfriend 1 (tennax you)

You rested your chin on your hand, watching the CG on the screen—Tenna proposing to the protagonist...... After quickly finishing the story, you closed the game. Honestly, this was a very fun otome game recommended by your best friend to you recently. Although your interest in otome games isn't great, you downloaded it after her strong recommendation.

But you regret playing it for just a while...... After all, it doesn't seem much different from other otome games, except for an extra option to input personal dialogue. You guess the built-in AI might have replied to your words, but you quickly lose interest.

After all, playing otome games is always like this: choosing the right options, the right gifts, the right actions, and coincidental timing can smoothly boost affection. After all, every game character set up has their own set of values and preferences. Rather than saying the male lead in an otome game falls in love with you, it's more accurate to say he falls in love with the self who disguises himself and matches his preferences.

So you quickly got bored and figured out the preferences of this TV head named tenna, quickly finished the strategy, and planned to quit immediately. Although you don't know why if you choose to type dialogues yourself, no matter what you say, it will increase your favorability, but later you don't bother to talk anymore. You're naturally a quiet person who doesn't like to talk much.

So you choose the option based on the preferences you previously guessed. And now, you close the game window and plan to uninstall it. You uninstall successfully, then you hold your phone, planning to go out for lunch and complain to your best friend that the game isn't that fun at all.

Before leaving, you glanced at the dusty appliances sitting in the corner...... You bought them out of boredom, probably with a CRT TV or something else. You originally planned to play games on it, but it turned out computers suited your preferences better—at least they weren't that heavy.

You lowered your head and typed rapidly on your phone, then walked out of the room, casually made yourself some instant noodles, and brought it home, planning to sit in front of the computer to eat.

You were startled as soon as you walked into the room, nearly spilling your instant noodles. Fortunately, you remembered that the mess would be hard to clean if you did, so you didn't let go. You just pretended to stay calm as you looked at tenna, who had suddenly appeared in your room, crouching yet still looking very tall, and seemingly feeling wronged.

…… And when you walk in, it shrinks to about the size of other pet dogs, then slowly walks up to your side, seemingly wanting to rub against your leg. In the middle of summer, your legs do feel like a cold metal sheet now. But actually, it does dissipate heat.

You cover your forehead with your free other hand, feeling a bit of a headache and trouble. In fact, at first you wondered if you were dreaming, but it turned out you pinched your own face—it hurt.

So it probably wasn't a dream. You kept a calm face, but your mind was blank. You brought the noodles over and set them on the table, then lowered your head to look at Tenna.

“…… Mr. Tenna, I think I've already unlocked your ending—it's a perfect ending." "That's exactly what you don't understand the most. After all, you have OCD. You haven't cleared any of the other bad endings, only this one perfect ending."

Tenna looks up at you. Actually, he has many questions he wants to ask, like saying that at first you were still talking to him and he was happy to be able to communicate with you, but why did you end up choosing those options instead...... He can only watch you through the narrow screen, unable to take any action. Even those conversations are preset, so all he can see is your increasingly uninterested expression, feeling inexplicably anxious.

He wants to ask why you stopped talking to him, why you only quickly got through the ending, or even stop looking at him, he wants to ask why you just uninstalled the game......

But in the end, You just asked, "...... You just uninstalled the game, so what about our married life? ”

While eating the noodles, you almost choked up when you heard this. Luckily, you swallowed it with difficulty, or you would have sprayed the noodles on the computer screen. But the price was that after eating, you still coughed dryly many times.

A glass of water appeared beside you. You glanced at Tenna, who had suddenly grown larger, but said nothing more. You just took the cup and drank a sip, barely suppressing the choking sensation.

Only then did you clear your throat and say, "Does this game system even have a post-marriage mode?" "You're genuinely confused. You didn't see it when downloading, nor did you see it when you cleared the ending. You just returned to the big screen after the happy end message appeared."

He fell into an awkward, subtle silence, and you patiently waited for a moment before he finally spoke: "No, but can you come back and see me a bit more?" "Just being watched a little longer by you makes Tenna feel satisfied."

You looked at him carefully for a while longer, eating instant noodles while mumbling a bit unclearly, "Why should I go back to see you more often?" ”

“…… I thought you liked me a lot." He lowered his head, clearly thinking so, since his voice sounded very dejected and his figure was shrinking.

You were genuinely surprised at this moment. Although watching him shrink made you feel a subtle pity, you still asked, "Why should I like you?" ”

Honestly, you just find this character fairly cute and don't have much to dislike, so you're willing to understand his ending. But if you say you like him, you can't quite say how much you like him.

But you can also understand why he has that idea, so you say, "Well, I understand why you think that way, but I don't think it's necessary." ”

"After all, in the end, the choices I made were just options that matched the character's preferences and pleasing them. I just created a virtual and hypocritical image you might like. You don't need to question the real me, nor do I have to like you." You said calmly, taking another sip of water.

You see his screen suddenly turn pitch black—maybe he's lost in thought. But you're not in a hurry. After waiting a while, his screen lights up again. You see his lips seem to move, but after a while, he finally utters a complete sentence: "But...... I've always seen it. I like you, I like the real you. ”

"I don't understand why you like me...... There must be some reason for this kind of longing, right? "You tilted your head and asked this question.

Tenna hesitated for a moment, but finally shook his head. In fact, he didn't understand the reason either. He only knew that he loved the feeling of being watched by you. As long as you kept watching him, he felt satisfied. But over time, he wasn't satisfied with just watching. You looked at him, and he looked at you. He wanted more touch, wanted to touch the real you, wanted to talk to you, not just watch you through a narrow screen.

In fact, it was only after you uninstalled the game that he suddenly realized he had this privilege. Using that old CRT as a medium, he came into this world, sat across from you, and had this conversation with you.

You found it a bit amusing, and for some reason, he looked a bit nervous at this moment. You clearly couldn't see his eyes, but his fingers resting on his knees kept fidgeting, probably feeling uneasy.

Then you cleared your throat and said solemnly, "Alright, I still don't understand why you like me. But I want to tell you something seriously...... Just because you like me doesn't mean I have to like you. Whether I like you or not is my natural and genuine choice. In fact, I chose you as my game strategy target just because you look pleasing to the eye. Maybe I have some affection, but it's far from liking you. ”

You watch Tenna lower his head, lowering it more and more, and you watch him shrink again, eventually becoming the size of a pet dog, silently pressing against your calf. You seem to feel a bit moist...... Probably from the direction of the screen. So even TVs can cry? You wonder this in a daze.

"But the fact that you like me has nothing to do with me, so I won't ask about it anymore............ But I think I can still give you a chance." "You thought about it and felt a bit unwilling, because for some reason, you can't be sure that this affection and love truly exist. You only want a definite love that can be confirmed. If not, then it's better never to have it.

After hearing this, Tenna grew bigger again, sitting across from you, looking at you longingly. You just felt that at this moment, you should describe him that way.

You solemnly reached out a hand to him: "You can try and make me like you too. Or at least, let me feel your love...... Come to think of it, I don't even know what this counts as. Maybe it's called an apprentice boyfriend? "You thought for a moment.

Tenna quickly reached out a hand, his palm large, tightly wrapping your hand inside, unwilling to let go.

"Of course, I'm willing to do it." He said this, and you couldn't help but laugh a little, feeling like he spoke like a wedding vow. Then, as if noticing he was holding too hard, he hurriedly let go again.

But you just took his hand and gently shook it, looking relaxed: "Alright, then, trainee boyfriend, nice to meet you." ”
