Trainee Boyfriend 8 (tennax you)

Of course, you didn't notice this. You hurriedly put on your shoes, slipped on your bag, and headed out, squeezing onto the subway to get to the office. The subway was very noisy inside. You took out your phone and Bluetooth headphones to listen to music, closed your eyes for a moment, and didn't notice any strange movements in your bag. Tenna curled up inside your bag, listening to the sounds outside, feeling a bit nervous—he quietly poked his head out to take a look, but it turned out he didn't recognize this kind of thing...... What on earth is this? It seemed to be something shuttling through, but it was so crowded that Tenna didn't know how much time had passed before he realized you had lifted the strap of your bag and gotten off the train.

You hurried to the office, clocked in for work, and greeted colleagues passing by: "Ah, good morning." ”

The male colleague nodded as well: "Mm, Y/N, good morning." ”

Listening to you greet your male colleague, Tenna instinctively felt a bit wronged—he, he...... Why can't you get such a gentle response from you? Why is your attitude toward him always a bit cold? Why does he now feel even softer when he listens to your voice? Why can't you talk to him like that?

The colleague just continued, "The boss demands that we see that planning proposal today. Y/N, I'm afraid you'll have to work overtime again." ”

You rubbed your shoulder that felt a bit sore for some reason, forcing a smile: "Ah, I know, it's fine, just changing the plan...... I'm already very skilled, I get it. Thanks for the reminder." ”

After greeting and taking the elevator, you arrived at your workstation. Your workstations all had separate partitions. Although there were no private rooms, the partitions were already very focused on privacy and privateness. You opened the computer at your workstation and turned it on. Just as you were about to take out your bag and take out the USB drive saved from some of the weekend planning projects, you saw the little TV person in your backpack. You paused for a moment, then met the black screen on the little TV's head. Your expression was a bit cold and speechless. Tenna hesitated for a moment but decided to pretend to be a doll and remain motionless, just sitting there as if it were a toy you had casually brought out.

You couldn't help but laugh a little in exasperation—you remember very clearly that you never took such a stuffed animal out; rather, you never brought anything extra.

So you just reached out and pinched it, staring at it expressionlessly. You were sure it was metallic and very hard when squeezed, and after you squeezed it, Tenna was like a tiny voice doll, softly pinching her voice and saying, "I LOVE YOU."

You shivered involuntarily and looked around. Fortunately, there weren't many people on your floor. You took out Tenna and placed it on the table, gritting your teeth as you said, "Stop pretending, you must be Tenna." You lowered your voice to avoid attracting attention, and then you watched as the tiny Tenna, who looked like a doll ornament, silently turned his hands behind his back, lowered his head to the TV, and drooped the antenna. You saw tiny droplets of water fall onto your desk—as if he was feeling so wronged. You silently glanced at him, but finally reached out to pat his head. Your hand was now much bigger than his—you said dryly, "You can stay here, but if someone else comes, you have to pretend to be a toy ornament...... Don't let anyone notice you're alive, got it? ”

He nodded eagerly, then sat down on the few books stacked on your desk. You saw the little screen light up again, a tiny flower once again appearing at the tip of his nose, but it didn't fall. You glanced at him worriedly, making sure he wasn't moving much and looked like a decoration, and only then did you finally get back to your work.

You open the PPT you're halfway through, start modifying it, add new pages, and soon you're back in Excel to start creating data reports...... Of course, you still need to create bar charts, but fortunately, with Excel, you'll be able to do it quickly. Tenna watched your fingertips move rapidly across the keyboard, then control the mouse to click all sorts of buttons. He was dazzled and couldn't understand a thing—Tenna couldn't help but feel a bit frustrated. He had thought, if you were here to work, maybe ...... Maybe he could help in some way? But as it turned out, you probably didn't need his help—you could do it well on your own, and he couldn't understand at all.

If he can understand even a little bit...... And then help you with a little thing, that's fine, but he can understand every word, yet when put together, he can't fully grasp what it means.

He sat awkwardly on those books, watching you type away. He wanted to say something, but then a colleague suddenly poked his head over, so Tenna had to keep standing still, pretending she was just a doll ornament.

"Sis, I have a bit of a problem I don't know how to solve it. How should I prepare this year's earnings report...... Hey, when did you get such a little thing on your desk? It's so cute." "It's a little girl, with a slightly round face. She looked at Tenna on your desk and couldn't help but poke her. She remembered your instructions and just sat here without moving."

You casually said, "Consolidate the revenue data, tally each department's earnings separately, and then make a report—ah, I brought this ornament today. After all, work hours are too boring, and having a stuffed animal with me makes me feel better." As you spoke, you poked Tenna too, then tapped his TV head with your fingertip. His antenna instinctively relaxed naturally—but your colleague was still watching, so Tenna had to keep her stiff posture. He was very happy to be touched by you, but now he could only sit there motionless.

"Alright, I get it now. Thank you, sis—this little ornament is quite interesting. I want to buy one for fun too. Sis, where did you get it?" The little girl, quite curious, poked Tenna twice more cautiously. Fortunately, Tenna was still perfectly still and hadn't given away.

"And it actually feels like it's metal, not plastic...... If it's metal, it should be heavy, right? Hmm...... That's rare. I remember most ornaments like this are made of plastic. ”

"After all, plastic is convenient for coloring and patchwork...... There are also metal ones, but they're rare, since metal is much harder to cut and polish compared to others. I just bought this randomly on Tao. If you ask the store, I can't remember either...... You smiled and made up a lie on the spot. Of course, this thing isn't some toy ornament, but your TV person's turn into a character—and not only that, this TV person actually came out of a game. If you tell the truth, you're afraid Tenna will soon be taken away for research.

That little girl still looked like she wanted to buy the same style after taking a photo, but you just put on the usual smile you usually use at work and put away the tenna: "I don't really want to use the same style as others...... You'd better go look for it yourself on TaoTao. ”

So she put away her phone in disappointment and sat back down. You quietly exhaled, took out Tenna again, and then took a photo to check the search results on Tmall for reference. After all, if someone really came to question you about this later, you'd have to come up with some excuse. The search results showed that Tmall really did ...... There were similar ornaments, probably merchandise from that game company, but made of PVC plastic. You glanced at Tenna sitting on your book, pretending to be obedient, and fell silent for a moment...... At worst, you could tell someone you really like this character, so you customized it specifically according to the in-game version.

Well, in any case, it shouldn't cause much trouble...... You put your phone away again, simply immersing yourself in your work once more. But this time, before you could get busy for long, you heard Tenna's voice, faint and tinged with caution: "Um...... Darling, can I help you with anything?" You glanced at him. The little TV guy sat there cautiously, the screen lit up, but his nose was crooked, and the antenna hung askew. He hesitated nervously, his hands seemingly gripping the cover tightly, and you could see that he had already scratched the cover with fine creases. You hesitate for a moment, think about how he behaved with his phone yesterday, then glance at Tenna, and finally sigh slightly irritably.

You stretched lazily to relieve your fatigue, but said emotionlessly, "...... You don't know how to operate a computer, do you? I think it's fine if you don't cause me trouble...... I'll check tomorrow, and you can't go out with me anymore. Also, you struggled so hard trying to learn to use your phone yesterday...... I can't guarantee you won't forget anything again, just don't cause me trouble, okay? "The life you're looking at now is already troublesome enough, and you really don't want these things to get worse...... Sigh, although you didn't mean to blame him—you were just stating the facts—Tenna still looked even more downcast, lowering his head once again.

“…… I understand, I'm useless to you...... I'm nothing worth mentioning. Indeed, you probably need someone who can help you share some of the burden...... I—I'm not suited for ......."

…… I feel like if I didn't say anything more, this guy could run up to the institute platform by himself tomorrow. You scratched your hair, sighed, and patiently said, "I told you not to cause me trouble, but I never said I didn't want you...... By the way, it seems you never tacitly agreed to our subordinate relationship...... Forget it, anyway, as long as you sit here, I'll feel much better. Just remember that. ”
