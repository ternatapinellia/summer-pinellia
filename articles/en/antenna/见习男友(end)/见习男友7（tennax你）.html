Trainee Boyfriend 7 (tennax you)

…… You don't quite understand why a stranger who is just a familiar one would want to share a room with you. You can't really treat those things from the game as reality, can you? Although you wouldn't be scared by his appearance, and you didn't think it was a big deal to suddenly have a TV person at home...... But you still gave Tenna a strange look, scanned him up and down again, then repeated: "The guest room isn't ready yet. How about you rest on the living room sofa for the night?" You pretend you didn't hear that sentence, but Tenna just lowered her expression, lowered her head, and let the antenna hang down, looking rather pitiful.

After a moment of silence, you simply raised your hand, first showing one finger, and said, "First, quickly tidy up the guest room and go to sleep first." Then you raised a second finger, "Two, sleep on the living room sofa," and finally raised a third finger. "Third, I have specially granted you the privilege of sleeping on the floor in my room." "As you spoke, you held your head high—after all, you truly thought this was your remarkable gift. You thought it over for a long time before finally giving this merciful answer!"

Tenna's antenna was set up again. Without a moment's hesitation, he hurriedly spoke, as if afraid you'd take it back any moment and regret his proposal: "I...... I'm going to sleep on the floor in your room, darling, I don't want to be too far from you......" His voice was a bit sticky, and you weren't quite used to it. You just gave him a rather strange look, walked into your own room, and pulled out two quilts—one laid on the floor, the other covered with him. Tenna followed closely behind you into the room.

Just as you turned around to say something, you almost bumped straight into Tenna. He was right behind you, almost bumping into his abdomen—yeah, after all, he's much taller than you. You pouted, hurriedly stepped back two paces, rubbed your ears that had turned red for some reason, and put your hands on your hips to announce, "Alright, then you'll rest here tonight—well, I'm warning you, don't come near my bed even a single step!" You flicked your fingers as you announced, then sat down on the bed, turned your back to Tenna, changed into pajamas, and casually flopped onto the bed, closed your eyes, and got ready to sleep.

Tenna's nose couldn't help but bloom with flowers again. Just being able to stay here and see you was enough—he was sitting on the floor, just enough to see you clearly. Maybe he felt uncomfortable facing his screen, so you muttered and turned over again. Tenna turned off the lights for you, and he also turned off his own screen, just cautiously staring at you in the darkness—he didn't want to sleep, as if he had, and when he woke up the next day, he'd realize it was all just a dream, that you had never truly appeared before him. In the darkness, he couldn't help but stare intently in your direction, even though the darkness made it impossible to see anything.

It wasn't until a while, after quite a while, that Tenna finally caught your silhouette again in the darkness. Listening to your shallow breathing, he guessed you were probably sound asleep—even though you had warned him not to come near your bed, he couldn't help but want to get closer. So he first secretly moved his bed. Originally, it was just from a distance, just close to the window, but now, he was getting closer and closer to your bed. Although he could still only see your back and couldn't see your expression, he ...... still satisfied.

But in such a pitch-black night, he couldn't sleep. He stared quietly at your screen, maybe only a few dozen seconds, maybe just a few minutes—after all, Tenna didn't know how much time had passed; he only knew he had been watching your back, listening to your shallow breathing. Even if...... He didn't know if he would wake you; he still instinctively softened his movements—because he didn't want you to wake up. Tenna slowly stood up, trying to crawl onto the bed.

But just as he made a move, his legs landed on the bed, causing the bed to sink in. You muttered, not knowing what he was saying, and rolled over directly. Tenna instinctively stopped moving, holding his breath. He was afraid you would wake up, but maybe he was also somewhat hopeful, because if you did—you'd realize that he really was the husband you had seriously won over, and of course he should hold you as you fell asleep.

But you just turned over and continued to immerse yourself in your sleep, never waking up. Tenna felt a bit regretful, but also a little happy. He climbed onto your bed completely, though still a little distance away. He just lay by the bedside, watching your peaceful sleeping face, instinctively curling his lips in a silly smile, while flowers appeared at the tip of his nose, but no one noticed.

Your sleeping face...... Your cheeks were fair with a faint blush. He noticed your eyelashes occasionally fluttering unconsciously, and your bright lips gently parted and closed, as if mumbling something. Tenna swallowed hard, wishing he could bite your lips right now...... He thought, that must be his favorite scent. But he didn't dare to do it yet. He only dared to quietly shift a little toward you while still sound asleep. Seeing you still unconscious, he moved closer to you, just a few steps away, until his screen was almost pressed against your cheek. He closed the screen again, just staring blankly at you, waiting for your warm breath to hit his screen—then he felt the entire TV screen grow hot, the antenna bent unnaturally, and finally it cautiously hung down on your head. Trembling, he reached out and hugged you. He didn't use any force, just held you weakly. Watching you sleep soundly in his arms, Tenna already felt deeply satisfied. He felt the mechanical heart in his chest floating lightly, almost leaping out of his chest—he was willing to give his heart, willing to give you everything, as long as you were willing to accept it.

You didn't wake up. Your eyelids blinked twice. Tenna nervously pulled her hand back, but it quickly turned over and lay flat. Only then did Tenna dare to put his hand on you again. He wanted to hold you tightly, but only half-heartedly. He carefully watched your expression. Seeing no reaction, he relaxed a bit and gently placed his arm on top of you. His screen lit up again for a moment. The corners of his mouth lifted into a smile he hadn't before, and he quickly turned off the screen. Afraid the screen would wake you, he just lay on his side beside you, awkwardly wrapping one arm around you, neither loose nor tight...... But he couldn't help but tighten a little more so you wouldn't escape from his embrace. Tenna leaned in, his screen pressed against your cheek, and he felt the mechanical heart in his chest beating faster—and he just leaned in, daring to lean in, gently pressing the screen against your cheek. He looked at your lips, and in the end, he simply let himself fall asleep holding you in his arms with satisfaction—his hands instinctively tightened, afraid that if he let go, everything would be gone.

As for you...... You had a nightmare all night. At first, you just slept in a daze, but at some point, you had a dream—a dream where you were wrapped tightly in a warm, heavy blanket like a zongzi. Instinctively, you frowned and turned over several times, trying to escape this environment, but unfortunately, you couldn't. You were still tightly wrapped in these scorching leaves.

When you struggle and finally wake up from the alarm ring, you groggily open your eyes and find Tenna tightly holding you in an awkward position. You laugh at his exasperation—no wonder you have those dreams, it's all because of this guy. You clearly told him not to come near your bed, but you don't even know when this guy got on top.

Anyway, you shoved Tenna's screen and arm, threatening as you said, "Get up! Didn't you say you wouldn't let me sleep in my bed?! I'm going to take off your affection points!" "Just thinking about your favorability makes you more alert. At least you have this way, otherwise you wouldn't even know what to do with this guy."

Of course, Tenna was already awake; he was just pretending to sleep...... He was anxious but wanted to hold you a little longer, and now he let go, feeling wronged. The screen became wet, the antenna drooped, and he muttered to himself, "Um...... I, I miss you so much...... Dear ......" he said, but he still hadn't gotten out of bed. You were furious, pushing his chest to keep him away from you, and then your body stiffened—because you felt something hot, hard pressing against your thigh. Your face flushed red. You grabbed your clothes and stood up, glaring fiercely at Tenna: "You, you, you, you...... Your favorability has hit zero! ”

With that, you dashed to the bedroom door, slammed it hard, got dressed, and got ready to head to the office to continue your busy day's work. Tenna carefully opened the bedroom door and came out. He saw you had already packed your things, a slice of bread in his mouth, a bag slung over his shoulder, and was about to pick up his shoes and leave. He didn't know where you were going; he was just a bit flustered—he wanted to regain the favorability he had just lost, so he took advantage of your distraction to become a small one and slipped into your bag.
