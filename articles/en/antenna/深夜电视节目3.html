"What's wrong, my dear star? Is it that the bed is uncomfortable, or is there noise disturbing you? Or is the light disturbing you? I knew it—I'll make them move faster—"

You rubbed your temples, reached out and gestured to stop: "Neither, I just can't sleep, so—maybe you could let me walk around here freely?" ”

It's only half-truth, half-fake that people can't tell. At least Tenna glanced at you a few times and didn't notice anything unusual, but you looked back with confidence.

"Oh, oh...... If you're tired, come back early and rest! Let me lead the way, or else! ”

"No need, I'll go alone." You waved your hand. If he goes too, how are you supposed to find a way to escape? Of course not!

After saying this, you ran off into the distance.

However... the part of the lounge that connects to the back really isn't much to see. It's more like the distance is too short, with only the end where a sneaky pippins is standing, seemingly up to some kind of reselling activity.

Hmm...... The other direction seems to be backstage? It wasn't a big deal at first, but Tenna mentioned it again. You really have to go take a look, so you leaned against the wall and cautiously looked over there, not knowing how things were going.

Good, Tenna isn't here; he's probably still backstage, and no one was guarding the front door before. You can definitely take the opportunity to get there.

So you walk openly in that direction, confirm that no one is looking in that direction, and then you open the door.

When you push open the door and see the huge back of Tenna standing there, you choke up.

…… Is that right! Didn't he catch him red-handed! And just now, I saw how he was flashing over from the backstage—it can't be some kind of video!

You wanted to close the door and slip away right away, but before you could close it, Tenna had already turned to look at you.

Well, now there's no way to escape—probably because the door opens a bit! You gave up and stood where you were—after all, there's no way you'll be playing an escape game here.

Even so, you still swallowed hard, extremely nervous, watching Tenna walk toward you step by step, and the screen went dark again, making you see nothing.

So you choose to strike first: "I'm thinking, since I'm going for a walk, I should walk everywhere, right?" You even give an awkward smile, silently shouting in your heart, what am I even saying?

“…… I know, you want to leave here, right?" But Tenna suddenly shouted those words, her body trembling continuously, just walking high, step by step, then enveloping you.

For some reason, that kind of pressure really does feel pretty strong...... Haha, you're a bit guilty, so you puff up that V-shaped cat mouth again.

"Not really, I was just looking around!" You're still trying to argue for yourself.

"After all, I just finished playing a horror game, and I can't sleep right now!" You close your eyes, turn up your voice, and just tell the real reason—just give up.

Tenna had originally wanted to point at the three small TVs stacked at the finish line and tearfully say, "If you want to go, then go," then drag you back with you. Hearing this, she suddenly fell silent.

So that horror game actually works? Now he was a bit overjoyed, nervously rubbing his hands behind his back. To avoid giving away anything on his face, he forcibly suppressed the urge to start laughing recklessly.

For a moment, things quieted down between you. You mustered your courage and opened your eyes to take a look, only to find Tenna still using that pitch-black screen, showing no reaction. So you simply spoke up: "So can I keep walking now?" ”

“…… Well, if you're scared, my dear celebrity, you can definitely watch TV a little longer! ”

So how did things end up like this?

You lay on the bed expressionlessly. Although the room was pitch black, you could still see Tenna lying beside you. You were even covered with the same blanket, and to avoid squeezing you or something, he had deliberately shrunk down. Now he was the same size as you. Compared to that, you felt warm all over him, leaning against you, making you sweat.

Alright, you close your eyes and prepare to nap for a while. At least now you won't feel fear or unease, only embarrassment. But it turns out you're still unable to sleep because it's too embarrassing, so you open your eyes expressionlessly and staring at the ceiling again.

Then you tilt your head, probably to avoid poking your nose. Now Tenna's screen is still pitch black, unlike when you first suggested it, when tiny spots appeared on your nose. You really don't understand why the TV can still bloom.

After a moment of silence, you think for a moment and think to yourself, 'He's probably fine. Even though it's a death warning, it's been a few days and nothing has happened to him, right?' And you can't stay here forever. If you find the culprit, maybe everything will be safe.

So, after much thought, you make up your mind. You reach out your hand to the screen, see the familiar vortex, feel the familiar gravity, and once again travel back to your own world—this time landing at your own home.

Well, you're pretty sure this is your TV, especially since you just crawled out from the screen.

Make the most of your time to get through dinner and finish your homework before you have the mood to watch TV again.

By the way, is there a sleep aid music channel on TV? You start to think about it. Logically, there shouldn't be one, but you still turn on the TV and start switching channels with FM, all the while mumbling about sleep aid music channels. In the end, you actually find one.

However, this channel number is quite far back, so you might suspect this is a recently launched program.

No matter what, the music is truly soothing. Your tension and unease have faded away. Perhaps those past memories are just a wisp of mist. At least at this moment, you don't need to think or reflect. Right now, all you need is a peaceful dream.

He slightly raised the volume to make sure he could hear it in the room, then yawned and walked back to his room. Hmm, I hope everything goes smoothly tomorrow...... Although you still feel a bit guilty thinking about Tenna lying in bed, you don't even know if he's asleep.

If he fell asleep, wouldn't he wake up to find the person gone?

After thinking it over, you feel justified again. After all, you just want to return to your original world. From your perspective, you have absolutely no mistakes or omissions.

So you comfortably pull yourself in, close your eyes, lie on the bed, and faintly hear soothing sleep-aiding songs coming from the living room TV. Scenes from another world flash through your mind, and then you drift into a dark, deep dream.

A sleepless night, in short, you sleep soundly, get up, get dressed, turn off the TV, prepare breakfast and lunch for yourself, and then head out to get ready for school.

Only when you were leaving, you leaned on the doorframe and glanced inside, your gaze faintly landing on that TV.

Then you said, "I'm heading out." ”

Only then did he turn around, close the door, and leave.

Class goes as usual, lunchboxes are served, class is over, school is dismissed, shopping, and you bring the ingredients for dinner home. You hum a tune and open the door, feeling pretty good today. After all, although yesterday was a horror game...... you still find it quite exciting and fun.

…… Honestly, you might think that if the next person appears on a late-night TV show and that person is still missing, then Mr. Tenna, who appeared before, should be fine. But it turns out you were too naive.

Although you may not have the sense of responsibility or empathy to meddle in others' affairs, still...... After all, this is still lively in front of you just yesterday.

Still holding on to a bit of luck, you walk over and press a button, but naturally, no screen appears. You guess the circuitry inside is also broken.

You took a deep breath and forced yourself to calm down. You remembered there was an appliance repair shop in town, not too far from here, and it was still possible to get there now. You found a small cart and painstakingly removed the broken screen, the antenna, and the battered TV inside, placing it on the cart, then pushing it out to find a repair technician.

The repairman tells you it will take about two or three hours, and it might not be fixed. You don't dare imagine what might have happened in another world, so you just make yourself dinner in a daze, finish your homework, and eat in a daze, unable to swallow, still thinking about what happened earlier.

Is it regret? Probably regret, probably thinking, if I had stayed here all the time, maybe I could have avoided his death? Maybe...... At least it's an appliance, and it can be repaired, so there's still a chance for a new life.

But you can't be sure what will happen to all of this.

You force yourself to stop thinking, to suppress all these thoughts, to hide all your hopeless ideas, and after finishing your meal and tidying up the dishes, you run over quickly.

Fortunately, the repair technician did give you a TV that was still in good condition. At least it looked fine on the outside, but both antennas were wrapped with tape, as you know, after all...... When you gave it to the repairman, both of his antennas were already broken.

You move that TV back home, put it back on the counter, and you try pressing the power button—it really works—so now it's time to check on its condition in the other world.

The Junis store wasn't closed yet. You placed your hand on a TV displaying it, and a familiar whirlpool appeared. You thought about the world Tenna was, closed your eyes, and when you opened them again, you saw a familiar, narrow passage before you.

You walk down this road, you start running, after all, you have no idea where he will be. The lounge lights are completely off, and it's as if the whole world has fallen into darkness and dead silence. Rather than deliberately closing it, it's more like a quiet that makes people uneasy.

You run forward, passing through the passage that originally blocked you, running downward, your lungs tightening, your heart pounding violently, twitching—this is probably a sign of oxygen deprivation, but you keep running.

Until you arrived at a silent snowy plain, with towering cliffs above, you didn't know where you found the strength to run to the mountains, but you saw Tenna lying quietly in the snow. Judging by its appearance, it was indeed perfectly fine, probably without any problems.

Run over and squat down, and you'll find that for some reason it's shrunk back to about the size of a pet—just enough for you to pick up.

You hesitated for a moment, then tapped his screen. This time, you deliberately held back to avoid the same vortex when you shot.

He didn't respond.

So you pat his shell again—maybe a little pat will wake him up? Of course, that's just a matter of not having high hopes.

He still didn't respond.

…… Alright, you should pick up this little iron lump, find the way you came, and slowly walk back. This time, you're not in a hurry.

But standing in front of those three small TVs stacked together, you hesitated for a moment. Actually, you didn't know if Tenna could even go out.

Hmm...... You held his hand and pressed it against the screen, only feeling relieved after confirming a vortex had appeared. If you couldn't take it out, you wouldn't know what to do.

Closing his eyes, he slipped out again and reappeared inside the Junis store, sneaking a glance around, then at the Mr. Tenna in his hand, who had somehow grown smaller and could easily fit into his pocket.

You put a small TV in your pocket, then openly walk back to the store and home, though at this moment you feel a bit uneasy.

After all the fuss, it's about time to rest...... Wash up, get into bed, and you place the still-awake tenna on the nightstand, then lie down and close your eyes to sleep.

In a daze, you frowned. For some reason, you felt a bit heavy—could it be that you've been paralyzed by a ghost? You opened your eyes. Just moments ago, you were vaguely wondering if you'd wake up, but the next moment, you were completely awakened by fright.

…… You almost cursed, but luckily you have good manners! After all, who wouldn't be scared to death seeing a TV person in the middle of the night with a pitch-black screen and arms on it!!

You think you're definitely being gentle, and there's no sign of anger at all. For some reason, he still looks scared, shrinks down again, then lies down next to you, pulling the blanket over himself. Great, now you're sharing the same blanket again, and he's really warm. You don't even know why you drifted off to sleep.

When you wake up the next day, you can even smell the aroma of breakfast. Honestly, you're really shocked—you never imagined he would do that.

Eating with a conflicted face, packing your backpack, you find he's even prepared lunchbox. You know he's definitely observing your life every day, even knowing exactly what you like to cook.

You scratched your head in frustration and saw him nervously with his hands behind his back. Guess it, maybe he was picking at his fingers behind his back again.

Clearing your throat, you solemnly announced, "Alright, then, Mr. Tenna, can you make it roughly the size of my pocket?" ”

"Ah, of course you can, but my dear star, what are you trying to do? Are you dissatisfied with something?" He kept muttering to himself, but still kept quiet as he was told, so small that you couldn't even hear what he was saying.

You're very satisfied and have stuffed him into your own pocket, just enough to show your head.

"Of course, you came with me to school."

Tomorrow, the day after, next year, the year after, and even longer, you might think you'll go to the shrine to make a wish.
