Reverse Care 24 (dntx you)

Tenna also fell silent, not knowing what to say for a moment...... After all, if he still struggled to get up, you would definitely sense something was wrong. But if he stayed here all the time, he felt tormented, lying here all he could hear was a faint sound outside, and beyond that, he couldn't do anything. He didn't want this either.

But how is that possible? Tenna think this kind of thing is a bit funny...... But what he longs for now seems to be all he wants.

"I can't just enslave a patient like you to play a show, can I? That would make me look so heartless. At this moment, you just felt like he was a bit confused or hopelessly ill, so he started relying on you...... You know his fever has subsided, so you don't bother to check his temperature or anything like that. You just feel like he's somehow inexplicably relying on you too much, which probably isn't a good thing...... But undeniably, your mood has improved a lot because of his dependence. So now you just shrugged: "Do you need any more reason? I just want to save you, so be it. “…… You're really utilitarian." tenna just let out a mocking laugh, because he didn't know what to say right now. So, he just mocked you a little.

"Not really. This is my world, but I'm willing to give it to you." tenna's tone was flat. To be honest, at first he was a bit reluctant, but then he thought that you'd be by his side forever. Thinking about this made him feel a bit tempted.

"Then that's it...... Alright, is there anything else you want to ask me? There's nothing else. I'm about to head out, and it just so happens that the show I most want to watch is playing." As you spoke, you glanced at the clock hanging on the wall.

“…… I want to know, why did you come to the Dark World? What are your plans for the future? "He asked.

“…… So, are you thinking about settling in some dark world? Like the TV world? "He hoped your answer would be yes. At this moment, he felt his mechanical heart beating faster—he, tenna thought, he needs you, he needs you to always be by his side, so of course he hopes your answer is yes, that's all," he told himself.

You hesitated and glanced at tenna again. To be honest, you couldn't see his eyes, so of course you didn't know what he was thinking...... Well, you had the same idea about settling in the TV world. You just felt tenna's mood was starting to feel a bit strange: "What's wrong with you? Since you said you would give me the lord's position, of course I'll settle in the TV world too...... Yeah, but staying in the same place all the time seems a bit boring......

But tenna interrupts you: "Y/N, I'm the lord of the TV world, and of course I can take you elsewhere." ”

"Oh, oh...... Wait, why did you just assume I was going to leave with you? Am I really that important, or do you like me? You shook your head twice, frowned, and looked at tenna. tenna had turned off the screen, and you had no idea what he was feeling right now. However, your heart couldn't help but pound violently...... Indeed, it seemed you didn't mind him insisting on staying with you like this, of course...... Actually, not being together didn't really matter; you'd long been used to living alone in silence.

Just, just...... If you could always have someone you could fully control, if you could always stay with someone who makes you happy, that wouldn't be so bad either. Honestly, you do feel a bit tempted.

Tenna just turned off the screen and didn't answer you, so it seemed like it was a bit down.
