Reverse Care 1 (dntx you)

You're basically a scavenger. The reason for this is because...... Well, after coming to the Dark World, you didn't know exactly what to do for a while, so you were temporarily like this. After all, your specialty is chemistry, but it seems the Dark World doesn't have any labs or similar things. After you moved to live there, your specialty was temporarily neglected. As for why you came to the Dark World...... It's because you don't want your parents to keep asking for money to take care of your brother. The reason is simple: the Dark World can block all signal bombardments, and you really enjoy this environment.

But today was a bit different, because as usual, when you went out, you saw the lord of the TV World with his legs severed on a cliff not far away—you knew him, after all, when you lived in the Dark World, you had dealt with several lords to some extent. He just lay there lifelessly, looking lifeless, as if no Dark Folk cared about him anymore. He lay there all alone—just like you, all alone. With a thought, you realized you'd been considering whether to change your profession lately. After all, if you wanted to be in the Dark World, there would be plenty of Dark People who still needed to repair themselves. And coincidentally, this TV world lord could let you experiment—hmm, so you dragged his body and those two broken legs back into your own house. You found a screwdriver and other tools, checked the circuit board, and first swapped the circuit to ensure that even if the leg was broken, the circuit would still be smooth and running. Then, you left some extra wires for convenience, and after reconnecting them to the broken leg, you temporarily repaired the circuits on both sides. You placed it on your bed while you started tidying up the equipment by yourself.

When Tenna woke up, the first thing he heard was a buzzing sound—probably the lingering power on him. Then, immediately after, he heard some rustling sounds. He struggled to turn on his screen, and the first thing he noticed was the bright, incandescent light above. Then, he twisted his TV's head—at least it really was ...... movable, and then he saw you lying by the bed, packing your things.

You heard his movements too, and without looking up, said, "You're awake. I've temporarily fixed your circuits, so at least you can wake up. But I haven't reattached your legs yet...... You suddenly feel a shadow appear in front of you. Instinctively, you look up, then you feel your arm being pulled, tenna pull you onto the bed. His body presses heavily against you, and his voice is hoarse and low: "A citizen of light?" Why save me? ”

…… Although he said this, in reality, he was indeed a bit anxious. After all, now that he had lost both legs, he could hardly do most things—he could no longer rely on his usual advantages to suppress others.

You couldn't really say. You just wanted to study his circuits for experiments, so you cleared your throat twice, tapped his dark screen with the screwdriver you were holding, and half-jokingly said, "Why would you need a reason to save someone...... Ah, even if you're just a TV, fine, put me down now." "If he keeps doing this, then you might be thinking a bit about how to destroy his circuits, since right now, the circuit on his chest is still exposed to you."

“…… Why?" He seemed to still be fixated on this answer, because tenna knew very well that he was usually terrible to everyone, so he originally thought he would never wake up again. You got a bit annoyed by this and simply tugged at the exposed wires on his chest. You knew very well that those wires only short-circuited his entire body's electrical circuits for a short time, with no other effects—they just left him temporarily weak after passing the electricity. Then you rolled to the side, and his body collapsed onto the bed, unable to move for a moment. You got out of bed, just crossed your arms and looked down at him. tenna struggled to turn over to face you, his screen still pitch black. But his antenna was upright, looking like he had become very alert after being startled, like some fluffy kitten. You couldn't help but sigh inwardly.

In short, at this moment you just stood with your hands on your hips and looked at him: "Alright, I have plenty of ways to calm you down. Just don't make a fuss. It'll probably be quite a while before I finish fixing your leg. You'll be staying here for now. Come on, let's get to know each other." My name is Y/N. "As you spoke, you reached out a hand to him, signaling him to shake your hand.

Tenna gritted his teeth. Although he didn't like that feeling of helplessness, it seemed this was all he could do now. Other than that, he had no other options—ah, he really hated the feeling of having the power lines grabbed, like being slaughtered. But now, all he could do was reach out and hold yours, waving it up and down: "Alright, I'm MR.(ANTON)TENNA." But now he was so powerless, unable to do anything. He could only put his hand down and lay back on the bed, motionless.

You shrugged and said cheerfully, "Oh, tenna, I'll get you a wheelchair. If anything happens, you can handle it yourself. I don't want to take care of you." "Given your ability to take care of yourself, you think letting someone else take care of you might be more reasonable. But you think sending tenna to cook would be a bit too much for a patient, making it seem like you're exploiting him. So in the end, you didn't let tenna cook."

You used leftover materials—circuit boards, leftover wires, and some wood you had earlier—to piece together an electric wheelchair for tenna. Hmm, your skills are pretty good! Maybe you should just open your own shop later. You looked at your handiwork with satisfaction, nodded, and pushed the wheelchair into the room. Although it was an electric wheelchair, to be honest, the power didn't last long; most of it still had to be done manually. When you got back to the room, you saw tenna struggle to sit up from the bed, and he even looked like he wanted to get out of bed. You pushed the wheelchair to the edge of the bed and patted its handle: "Alright, you want to get out of bed, right? Just put on this one." "You're quite satisfied with the wheelchair you made, but tenna look at it, his face dark and gloomy, which always makes people feel a bit uneasy. You look at him in confusion, even though you can't see any emotion on the pitch-black screen.

“…… What, are you not satisfied? If you're not satisfied, you have to sit here. I don't have any other materials. You can sit on it yourself. You're too heavy, I don't want to help you." You said this with a curled lip. Although you were a bit dissatisfied and wanted to quit—back in the Light World, no one had ever shown you such a face! Those people were all very cautious, making you feel a bit bored. But since he was now your only test subject, you were still quite patient and just crossed your arms and looked at him.

“…… I can't go up alone." In the end, you only heard tenna sound like he was gritting his teeth. He was indeed holding back a breath, because right now he could only live under someone else's roof, at their mercy. Other than that, he couldn't do anything. You just shrugged and adjusted the wheelchair position: "I don't think your arm strength is that strong. Why don't you just prop yourself up and sit on it—oh right, I forgot you still have half a leg left." Your tone was so casual, but in the end you adjusted your wheelchair, lowered the armrest beside you, and then raised the seat to keep the wheelchair at the same height as the bed where tenna was sitting.

"Alright, that's fine now. Go ahead." You clapped your hands and said casually, tenna was silent for a moment, then finally gripped the handrail beside you with one hand and struggled to move your body onto it.

He sits in the wheelchair, and you lift the armrests again, changing the wheelchair height. Then you clapp your hands, looking a bit proud: "Alright, this wheelchair has an electric function. You can try it yourself. I'll go prepare dinner first." Saying that, you walked toward the kitchen. You glanced at the fridge and assessed your messy cooking skills...... Uh, it seems like it's a bit difficult even to cook a normal bowl of noodles. You're thinking about what to do tonight, when you hear tenna's voice. He seems to be shouting loudly, making your ears hurt. You pinch your ear, lean out to see what tenna is wrong, and say with some dissatisfaction, "What do you want? Don't shout so loud next time. I can hear you. You're making a lot of noise." ”

"Why is this wheelchair out of power?" tenna just pressed the button on the wheelchair's armrest, asking anxiously. Of course, at this moment, his voice was instinctively lowered.

"Oh, I made it casually using some materials I had on hand. That battery isn't great, probably dead by now. If you want to just look around the room, just push the wheels by hand." You shrugged casually, then returned to the kitchen to decide what to eat tonight. Of course, you deliberately ignored tenna cursing outside, treating it as background music—background sound!

In the end, you decide not to make things too hard for yourself—just cook some noodles...... As long as you can eat it, that's enough; you don't have any expectations for your cooking skills.
