You close your mouth, widen your eyes at Tenna—oh my god, oh my god! Is this door just like that?

"Is it really a high note? You didn't even sing it when you sang it for me...... you muttered, doubting the accuracy of his words.

"Of course, definitely high notes—I just haven't sung in a long time, so it's just a bit unfamiliar!" Tenna hurriedly gestured and gestured, and finally his shoulders drooped, his antenna drooped, and he turned away in dejection.

"Uh...... It seems I made a mistake during the recording. When recording the password here, I used the same pitch as before, so...... So the system only recognized that pitch...... "That's why it opened the door for Tenna."

Your eye twitched, and in the end, you just shrugged helplessly: "Alright, alright, I get it, I get it...... Uh, so as long as we move forward, that's our ultimate goal, right? ”

At this, Tenna immediately perked up, waving his hands once again: "Yes, yes, yes! Our reliable hero Y/N overcame five trials and defeated six enemies, finally defeating the super evil Great Demon King, defeating the four cruel and evil disciples under the Demon King, liberating the people of TV City, and finally about to unleash the sacred relic of TV City—that is—the TV antenna!" He gestured, and the red arrow appeared again, pointing ahead. You sighed helplessly and stepped forward...... After some distance, you finally saw the exact same stone platform, but unlike before, this time it was a TV antenna placed on top. Tenna stepped forward first and raised the antenna.

He spoke in a very proud and confident tone: "Ah, this is it—our greatest sacred relic, the television antenna! With it, we have hope for television worldwide. With it—we can revive the era that belongs to television!" ”

He wanted to keep talking nonstop, sounding like a beautiful dream, and you couldn't help interrupting him: "Stop, stop, stop, so...... You said before that when you reach the end of the journey, you'll discover...... What is the way to leave this world? ”

Although this doesn't necessarily mean you will leave this world, you still have many things to do, you have family and friends, you are not alone...... So, it's okay to go out and then come back.

Tenna stopped moving, stopping the originally intense and extravagant performance, and his screen went black again...... You always felt he seemed unhappy, or rather gloomy, and instinctively moved closer to him, but Tenna simply turned and walked out of the vault.

“…… Go further and you'll see. His voice was so deep you could barely hear it. You thought this was the exit...... But there was still ...... A door was open.

You hesitated for a moment, then followed Tenna out. You stood again in the forest exactly like the starting point, and you saw the small cabin in the corner...... Well, at this very moment, not far away, you also saw a black ...... rising into the air. What did it look like? A fountain? You didn't know what it was, just instinctively looked at Tenna.

At that moment, Tenna turned around and stood in front of you. Immediately, battle music played—you had entered the fight, your pupils contracted as you instinctively drew your sword...... Uh, the staff sword. But your hands trembled, unwilling to cast a spell on Tenna, and you didn't understand what was happening.

Tenna's voice was choked with tears, hesitant and slow...... But in the end, she was resolute.

“…… I'm the last boss! Yes, yes—after the adventure, our hero Y/N discovered that the companion who had followed him all the way had actually chosen to turn around and face him, no longer letting Y/N go forward or go home. For our hero Y/N, this was probably a very annoying obstacle—but only this last boss remained, and the only one standing in Y/N's way was me! "As he spoke, he summoned the red arrow aimed at him.

You instinctively want to say something more...... But Tenna kept talking, seemingly trying not to cry: "And the condition to defeat me is—" He seemed to be teasing on purpose, not finishing right away. He raised his voice, only to lower it the next second.

His voice was even a bit muffled: "...... Say you love me, Y/N. Please tell me, you love me—that's the condition for clearing the level." "As long as ...... As long as you say that, he's willing to let you go back. He lowered his TV screen, not wanting you to see the tears falling from his screen, and even turned his back to you...... But you still saw it.

You open your mouth, then close it again, and you really don't know what to do...... Do you love him? You haven't thought about this question. You're just certain that you do like Tenna, but does liking mean you love? You...... You don't know, you don't know, but...... Maybe, at least at this moment, you really want to shout those words, at least at this moment, you're certain this is your true feeling, this is what you genuinely want to tell Tenna.

So you hesitated for a moment, but finally softly said, "...... I love you. ”

He stood still, as if savoring it for a moment, but in the end he didn't dare face you, just stepped aside and said in a muffled voice, "...... Yeah, I love you too, so...... So you should go. Hurry up. "He knows, Tenna knows, he'll definitely regret it soon. You silently passed by Tenna, who was standing sideways and refusing to face you, and silently walked toward the pitch-black fountain ahead...... A flash of white light flashed by, and you finally snapped out of this long sleep. Only then did you realize you were sleepwalking again—you were at the junkyard. Instinctively, you took a step and immediately kicked a CRT TV that had popped out of the garbage station. You looked down and realized, this must be Tenna—no, it must be Tenna.

You carried the TV home. Although your parents were a bit surprised that you suddenly brought a CRT TV back, they didn't ask much. You turned on the TV, and the screen displayed your previous game world. The only difference was, Tenna's image didn't appear in the screen.

You sighed, smiled, and hugged the TV...... Eventually, you fell asleep, thinking, maybe you could see Tenna again in your dreams.

You woke up from the dream, stepped out from the darkness behind you, and saw Tenna still standing in the spot where you had left earlier. At this moment, he was crouching down, probably muttering to himself...... But as he wiped his screen, it looked like he was crying.

You quietly walked forward, then hugged him from behind, your voice tinged with laughter: "Alright, guess who came?" ”

He didn't even turn to look at you—but his voice was full of surprise: "Y/N! I thought, I thought...... You'll never come back......" he whispered, not daring to look back at you, afraid you were just his imagination.

"Yes—get married! Y/N, my dearest one, I like you the most. Please marry me!" Tenna's antenna straightened up again, gently shaking it. He turned around and tightly held your hand. His screen lit up again, and a large flower appeared at the tip of his nose—though it sounded like a question, he simply pulled out a ring and slipped it onto your hand, with a TV-shaped diamond at the tip...... Hmm, it looks a bit strange, but it suits Tenna's taste quite well.

You sighed helplessly and hooked your finger at him: "Mm, mm, I'm marrying you...... But I didn't bring any rings or anything. "After thinking for a moment, you simply wove a small wreath ring from fallen branches, leaves, and petals for him, then slipped it onto his finger.

"Alright, now our wedding is complete! Speaking of getting married in the game...... Hmm, is there any bonus? ...... "You start overthinking, wanting to pull up your own panel to check, but this game is so basic, it doesn't even have a single panel!"

Tenna's body stiffened, and he hurriedly added two attributes: "Yes, yes, after marriage, physical attack increases by 15%, and magic attack increases by 15%!" He added these two attributes to the pause screen display in the game...... You shrugged. You were just saying it offhand, but you hadn't expected Tenna to add it to you. You held Tenna's hand and walked forward.

"Alright, attributes and all that don't matter anyway. You're here anyway—hmm, shall we continue our adventure? By the way, where are we heading to our next stop...... By the way, did you not even finish the map? ”

"Y/N, where do you want to go—I can make a map on the spot!"

"Alright, alright, leave it to me to do it...... Don't worry, I come every day, though I only come in my dreams......" His voice gradually faded, probably walking away.

Your journey seems to have never ended.
