Tenna's antenna drooped again, and you couldn't help but widen your eyes when you heard the clause: "You...... So now, I have to stay in this game forever. Can't I leave?" His antenna drooped, and he left the cabin gloomily. Naturally, he saw you in the distant grass...... And he instinctively walked toward you.

He is the creator of this game, this world, and of course won't be trapped by this little ghost wall. Tenna stands behind you again, hands behind his back, a smile on his face once more. "I haven't refused your clause that I want to be with you forever—in fact, I don't reject being with you, nor do I hate the world you've created, nor do I hate you. “…… I don't need your apology. After all, I've signed that contract, and there's no way I'm leaving here."
