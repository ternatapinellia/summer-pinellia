Adventurerror19 (tennax you)

"Ah, that Fourth Apostle......" Tenna's voice suddenly became stammering. You looked at him in confusion, and then, as if he had made up his mind, he leaned close to your ear and whispered in a very quiet manner, "Ah...... That Fourth Apostle usually doesn't leave seclusion. He's the top fighter under the Demon King, and most people are defeated by the first three apostles, so—there's no need for him to show up. ”

You glanced at Tenna, tilted your head, crossed your arms and looked at him, a bit speechless: "Tell me, did you just make up these settings?" ”

Tenna really did lower his head at this moment. Feeling a bit guilty, he clasped his hands behind his back. Cold sweat was dripping from the screen, but he had to keep his smile on his face.

"Uh...... Alright, you're right! "He looked around but still refused to look at you, "After all, I haven't finished this game yet...... But I definitely want the People of Light to experience my game!! So, there are some things I had to make up on the spot...... The more he spoke, the quieter his voice became, and you understood why the later stages were so simple, with basically nothing at all...... The truth is, Tenna didn't write it.

So you can't help but show a half-moon eye—fine. If he didn't write it, so be it; you're just here to play casually—fine! But if he really can develop a truly exciting game, you'll really like it! It's not that you don't like the current game......

You move forward two steps, and naturally, you enter the battle with those two dice. You pull out that sword again, after all...... Hmm, if you cast the spell directly, it's a bit more convenient.

So you first cast a Fireball spell—though for some reason, it was designed as a turn-based battle. The red die got injured, and then the green die seemed to do something, and all the injuries on the red die disappeared—at the same time, the red die's head spun to a six, and you were attacked too...... Well, anyway, it was an indescribably intense attack. At this moment, Tenna waved his staff and automatically restored your health bar.

You show your crescent eyes and choose to switch to another attack method. You slash at the green dice, and then the green die is injured, but the red die continues attacking you—you more or less understand that the green die is for healing, so it's no wonder the Second and Third Apostles stay together, since one of them has almost no combat ability. You choose to use slashing to kill the green die one by one, about three or four times. The red dice hits you quite painfully, but Tenna heals you, so you can hold on. After the green die falls, you use fireball spells and similar spells to defeat the red dice.

Now it's clean in front of you.

You beat those two dice, and as they escaped, two sheets of paper were left behind. They looked like they could be pieced together with the notes you got from the first apostle you defeated earlier, but still, I couldn't figure out what was on them...... Well, in the end, the words you couldn't understand were mixed together.

You were a little confused...... But because of the recent battle, you weren't cold anymore. You looked back at Tenna, who looked cheerful and was still recording the narration for this part of the story.

"Our brave and great warrior Y/N fears no dangers ahead. After solving the puzzle, he defeated the two who appeared together, determined to do everything they can to defeat Y/N's Second and Third Apostles! With my help, Y/N indeed defeated these two terrifying creatures! And next—Y/N must keep moving forward, our journey goes on, and the Fourth Apostle and that evil demon king are waiting for us ahead!" He gestured excitedly...... Or rather, gesturing and gesturing, and with his voice, a red arrow appeared once again ahead...... Pointing straight ahead of you. You half-moon looked at Tenna, who only shifted slightly, a bit guilty as he clasped his hands behind his back, but not much, since he was now showing a bright smile.

He reached out his hand to you: "Alright, my dear star, shall we move forward now?" ”

Although you always have a lot to say and want to complain about this game, in the end, you still end up with Tenna...... You keep moving forward.

Tenna grinned, and you suddenly had a bad feeling—then he pulled out another big black sign with ten days to go...... Alright, that replaced the cutscene. Then he kicked the sign again, sending it flying, and then you switched to another map. You stared at the familiar grassy field before you and fell silent...... Ah, the only difference might be that this is just grass, and you don't see that cabin.

You don't even know what to say...... Oh, and one more difference is that the sky here looks darker, you ...... Well, anyway, it's different from how it looked in the forest before.

"Is there any difference between here and the places we've been before?" You couldn't help but ask the question—oh my, is there really any difference between these two places? You must think Tenna couldn't come up with any extra maps, so she simply started reusing the previous maps.

Tenna just cleared his throat and said solemnly, "Ahem, of course it's different. After all, this is—the Dark Forest!" Legend has it that this forest is terrifying. When night falls, you hear terrifying howls, and even wolf packs appear and attack humans...... But that's just legend! To let our stars enjoy this game, we only recreated the Dark Forest scene model, not the wolf packs from it! " He said, gesturing and gesturing, yet cold sweat kept dripping down his screen...... To be honest, you're already used to it.

You just put your hands on your hips, looking at Tenna, tilting your head slightly. Maybe because you two had been quiet for a while, Tenna once again brought up the red arrow, this time pointing only ahead.

“…… I think the only difference between this place and before is that it's daytime here, and this is nighttime...... "You still can't help but complain, and for now, you still don't dare to go forward. After all, the map is the same. Who knows if this place will be the swamp from before!"

"So...... Is there still a swamp in this grassland? "You decided to ignore that question above. It won't really affect your journey anyway, probably......

"Of course, you can walk on this grassland with peace of mind. I guarantee there's nothing left! And this isn't just switching between day and night; I did do a little extra or something...... Tenna patted her chest and assured you, but as the time went on, his voice sounded a bit aggrieved...... His antenna drooped, and the screen drooped, so for a while he didn't dare look at you.

"Alright, alright...... Oh, I get it. Adjusting the time and scenery of the entire game scene is really tough, isn't it? That way, Tenna is really impressive! "You said with a smile. By now, you were used to this old guy always having some inexplicable moods, so you simply stood on tiptoe and patted his head, then cupped his TV head and gave him a kiss on the screen."

Then you quickly put your hand down, walked past Tenna, and to avoid being too embarrassed, you made up for it, saying, "Ahem, hmm, anyway...... Yeah, as a reward and compliment, I gave you a kiss! "Now you don't just laugh it off like last time, after all, last time you moved quickly, but this time...... Yeah, it's obvious, it's exactly what happened right in front of you.

So Tenna eagerly followed behind you, mumbling, "Mm...... Um, that...... I... I want to ask for another reward, is that okay? Can you give me another kiss...... "Oh my god, he really loves that soft feeling on your lips. He really likes you so much that his heart is pounding so hard that the mechanical heart in his chest is pounding right now...... So much so that he can't wait to lean in and kiss your cheek.

You didn't respond to him, and Tenna just leaned in and kissed your cheek...... You turned your face away, pressed your lips together, and said nothing. Alright, you know your ears are burning now, and Tenna must have seen it too...... You're also panicking, thinking, Oh my God, how did you just get so bold and do something like that?! Oh my god...... Could it be, could it really be that you have fallen for him? You don't know...... You don't know how you feel right now—it's a complete mess, absolutely terrible.

And now, the two of you have fallen into an awkward silence. Tenna, who didn't get your answer, lowers her head in disappointment, and you turn your head away, not daring to look at Tenna for a while.

Breaking this silence was the sudden sound of battle music—looking ahead, it was the same dark monster you encountered when you left the spawn point, playing strange saxophone music.
