You look around at the surroundings. After leaving the cabin, you're greeted by a vast forest...... Hmm, it looks pretty well in line with the setting, but when you turn back to look at the cabin, you can't help but twitch your mouth, because you see that backstage and cabin are still mixed together as if they've been molded, making it look like Tenna is doing her best to modify the code—just don't know what she actually made.

So you can't help but complain: "Do you still need to keep modifying the spawn code code?" It looks like you've already written a mess. ”

That voice quickly answered you: "I...... Uh, of course, I'll be able to fix it soon! Don't worry, Y/N! "As he spoke, you felt as if his typing speed on the keyboard had gotten faster...... After all, his speed had been very slow before, sounding like he was typing letter by letter, and you didn't even know how long it took him to finish writing this program.

"Alright, then what do you plan to change it to?" You crossed your arms and asked leisurely.

"Change it to ...... Change it to, still the little cabin from the beginning." You seem to hear him sigh...... Probably in a bad mood, feeling a bit down, as he pressed the last button, only the cabin remained before you.

You shrugged, turned around, and walked toward the forest...... It looked like there was only a single passageway, and as you set out, you asked, "Well, this looks like an RPG, so what's my mission?" "After all, you've looked around now, and it doesn't seem like a typical game with a guide box or anything...... Your field of vision is empty, nothing at all.

Normally, the game would at least display a quest guide in the top left corner, right?

"Ah...... Ah, right, I forgot to tell you the task! Why doesn't this scroll guide pop up on its own? I've clearly written the program...... Tenna's second half of his sentence sounds like he was talking to himself, and he really did call a...... A scroll that looks like a hologram for you.

You show your half-moon eyes and watch the scroll slowly scroll upward. The story is quite old-fashioned, probably about a long, long time ago, there was a saint or something...... Then the scroll got stuck.

It's stuck?? Does the holographic image get stuck too?? You widened your eyes in disbelief, pointed at the scroll in front of you, and began loudly questioning Tenna: "What do you mean? Why is the holographic image still freezing? Hey, hey, is the network not working well or what? It can't be that your code is wrong again......"

You hear him chuckle awkwardly: "Uh, that's right...... I wrote it wrong again, but I designed this holographic screen to be touchscreen! So you can scroll down yourself, as long as you finish reading this scroll, that's enough! "His voice sounded a bit guilty, but more likely...... Excited? You don't know exactly what he's getting excited about, just speechlessly showing half-moon eyes and manually dragging the scroll down. You don't know what kind of spelling he made a mistake, but the area that should be displaying the image next is completely gone, just a bunch of mosaics...... And for some reason, the scroll has all sorts of messy, even constantly jumping WordArt and bizarre puns...... In short, you put in a lot of effort and half a day of time, and finally managed to interpret this story.

Probably a long, long time ago, the land was ravaged by the Demon King—then heaven sent a saint to descend to earth, seal the Demon King, and leave behind a sacred relic to protect the survival of the kingdom. But recently, the seal was loosened and the Demon King emerged. The Demon King had four great apostles under his command, and one of them stole the kingdom's sacred relic. The monsters began to invade the human world. At this moment, a hero stepped forward, about to reclaim the relic and eliminate the Demon King...... And the hero entrusted with a great mission and obtained the holy sword is you, the participant in the game.

After reading this scroll, you pulled out your sword and looked at it. No matter how you looked at it, it was just an ordinary sword...... You couldn't help but ask, "So where is the Holy Sword? Don't tell me...... Uh, the sword in my hand is the Holy Sword? And where am I going next? Could it be that I'm going straight to the Demon King to fight? Where is the Demon King? ”

As you spoke, you swung the sword in your hand again. Clearly, it was just scrap metal.

"Uh...... Wait, I didn't give it to you directly? "His voice was a bit flustered, and you heard the sound of typing on the keyboard again, probably ...... Yeah, he was probably checking the code he had written. You ignored him and just walked forward on your own, then you heard his voice again: "Oh, I get it...... The scroll I gave you seems to be incorrect. That holy sword wasn't handed to you directly; you had to visit the king first. The king only handed it over to you after he confirmed your abilities. After all, the holy sword is a treasured treasure among them...... He rambled on, and you nodded absentmindedly, continuing forward through this seemingly endless forest.

"Alright, so which direction is my current destination?" You haven't even done a single quest guide, so having no map function is one thing...... And according to what you said, the Holy Sword is also a sacred artifact? So what exactly was stolen from them? Or am I the Demon King? "Your questions are a string of things, since you really can't understand what he's thinking...... If you had to judge, it's that the game code and story he wrote are basically just bugs.

You hear that voice and cough twice: "Well, actually, it's been stolen...... It's part of a TV, which is part of me! ”

…… For a moment, you don't know whether to first complain that the game's creator is just a TV, or if the stolen part of it is just a part of it.

You just keep your half-moon eyes and dryly ask, "Alright, so what exactly was stolen?" ”

"It's the antenna on top of the TV—that would make the signal reception better." In fact, this country is actually technologically advanced, and everyone in the kingdom loves watching TV. If they can't watch TV, people in this country would feel life is worse than death. And the king preserved Fa Zheng, one of the top technologies in television development. Without this antenna, the country would be doomed! ”

…… You feel like this story was just made up by him, and thinking this way, you simply ask: "So this story was actually made up by you, right?" ”

You hear him seem to choke for a moment, then awkwardly chuckle twice: "How could that be?" ”

But in fact, Tenna was a bit guilty; this story was just something he made up...... But he did have a model of this country; he just needed to modify the treasure, turn it into a TV...... Yes, he really hoped everyone could love TV and watch it. Isn't that a very simple wish?

While thinking this, he revised the code to replace the traditional medieval country that existed in this RPG world with a model of a modern city he had just collected.

"Alright, then tell me, without an antenna, scientists in this country can still rebuild it, right?" You shrug.

He paused, then spoke rapidly, "It's the Demon King. Another apostle under the Demon King has abducted all the scientists in this kingdom!" ”

You honestly don't know how to comment on this story, just complaining, "Ah, then honestly, just rescue all these scientists, right?" Speaking of which, for scientists, is that really ...... Uh, is there really no security at all? Logically, scientists with such top-notch technology should be well protected, right? ”

"Uh, one of the Demon King's apostles is skilled at disguise, taking on the appearance of another person, and even mimics every move...... He transformed into a high-level security staff member at the institution where the scientists worked, then issued an emergency evacuation order and immediately took all the researchers away! "It sounds like he's just racking his brains to write stories, his voice stammering. You've walked a long way forward before he finally answers your question completely.

For some reason, you think listening to him make up stories like this is actually quite fun...... At least you want to tease him a little, so you continued, "Alright, so what comes next? What's next?" ”

Maybe it's because you didn't question it...... Tenna stopped racking his brains to make things up; he just continued stammering, "Uh...... Well, that, anyway, the Demon King kidnapped these scientists too! That's about it, Hero, let's set off now!! ”

"Yeah, I'm on the way, but you don't have a single task guide. Where am I supposed to go?" You shrugged again. Tenna glanced at the code he wrote and your current state, hesitating for a moment...... Uh, he really didn't write a task guide. He awkwardly tapped the code and added an arrow...... He was manually controlling it.

"Yes, let's head in this direction!" He declared confidently, the arrow pointing toward the kingdom. He thought, this is definitely the right thing to do!

And you glance in the direction the big red arrow points to...... Well, very good, it's actually tall bushes and trees.

With crescent eyes, you point at these plants: "Do you mean I want to fly over them?" ”
