Adventurerror1 (tennax you)

You feel dizzy and lightheaded; you clearly remember that you seemed to have just fallen asleep...... Logically, you should have fallen into darkness and then woke up, but why is it that now, when you fall into the darkness, a new light appears before your eyes?

It's simply baffling...... You muttered, thinking this way, but you ended up saying it out loud. After you said it, you were stunned. You tentatively moved your arms and legs, and it actually moved freely according to your will. It didn't look like a dream; instead, it felt like you had entered another world, because...... Because when you looked down, you realized you had changed into a different outfit.

It was a set of light armor, looking like medieval knightly decorations, mostly white with some deep blue fabric accents...... It felt like some hero's outfit had been combined with the noblewoman's attire, which looked a bit odd. Then you noticed a sword sheath at your waist, and when drawn, it was a rather rustic longsword. Not only that, you also heard surrounding music that seemed to be Celtic-style surrounding view?!

Obviously, your complaint was overheard, and then you heard a 3D surround sound echoing endlessly, kind of ...... It was like some kind of broadcast voice, probably trying to sound tense, but still carrying a slightly sticky smell. You even seemed to hear a hint of grievance: "How is this weird...... This is a scene I carefully crafted! ”

You startled and instinctively look around, trying to figure out where the voice was coming from. Unfortunately, it looked like some kind of initial character spawn location...... It was just an empty wooden cabin with nothing at all.

“…… Who are you? "You tried hard to stay calm, and now you don't even know where you are.

And that voice was very lively, seeming somewhat excited: "Ah... this honorable, shining people of light, I am MR.(ANT)TENNA!! Very pleased to meet you, I just knew that there would definitely be people of light who would like my game!"

You feel like he can keep rambling...... For some reason, that's just how you feel, so you quickly raise a hand to make a pause gesture: "Stop, stop, stop...... Stop talking, now tell me, where am I? ”

The voice faltered for a moment, sounding a bit frustrated because you interrupted it, so much so that the volume was lowered: "...... This is the dark world generated from the game I developed. I sincerely thank the guest who created this dark fountain—namely—you!" As his words fell, I wonder where the spotlight inside this cabin came from, shining on you.

You show a half-moon eye, point at yourself: "Me?" And what's with your sudden light? This doesn't make sense at all......

As your words fell, the surroundings seemed to quiet down. You almost thought this guy had disappeared, just as you were about to say something...... The voice rang out again, this time with a tone of panic: "It seems the data was called incorrectly...... This should have been the backstage for welcoming guests! Not this little cabin, where did this cabin even come from...... His voice grew quieter, sounding a bit like a rambling. You wanted to ask more questions, but you couldn't help but say, "Is your game reliable? Do you really know how to design games?" ”

The volume suddenly rose: "Of course I will!! How simple is that? It's just integrating several data points and calling them up......! ”

In fact, Tenna...... was sweating profusely as he accessed the data in front of him, which he had no idea what it was. Well, although he could understand a little, he was not sure about the actual effect. He was only judging what the code was used for based on the situation on site.

He didn't follow the purple girl's advice, was taken to their territory, nor sent to a new home. He just ...... Stayed here, ignored by anyone. But he couldn't let himself remain unnoticed forever, so he started trying to piece together those seemingly old-fashioned codes. Maybe after the new show airs, the People of Light will return. Look, a Citizen of Light has already appeared!

“…… Alright, you said you could do it, so why do you say this dark world was created by me? "You sighed, deciding not to dwell on the game's issues, since your initial spawn location has already ...... Uh, it's changed. Specifically, the backstage scene Tenna mentioned is stuck with the current cabin scene, and it looks like it's been molded through. Although you can write code...... But you wouldn't tell Tenna exactly what he wrote wrong.

"As long as you pierce the earth with willpower, you can create a dark fountain...... That's probably what it looks like, and you did it in your dream! "His voice sounded cheerful, but after you listened, all you knew was...... Uh, you really brought yourself into this place now.

Your mouth twitched as you coldly watched the scene before you freely mold the mold, then turned into a cabin. In the end, you couldn't help but say, "Oh my god, do you even know how to code?" ”

"I will, of course I will! Just change this string of code!" ”

Along with his words, you also hear the sound of keyboards tapping, followed by a puzzled face because he probably pressed Enter, and the small cabin around you disappeared...... The next second, you fell into the dark underground with a puzzled face. And he probably didn't set up an air wall, nor did he know what he did. In any case, you kept falling, the surroundings pitch black, and with gravity, you kept slowly spinning around.

You couldn't help but raise your voice: "You—this—fixed—a bug, right!!" "You really doubt how you could create such a dark world, even though you really want to play games, because being alone at home every day is too boring, and you really want someone to play with you...... But, but no one told you you'd end up like this?! You don't want to play a game full of bugs either?!

"I-I'm fixing it...... Uh, um, what does a bug mean? "That voice still lingers around you, making you feel like some kind of game narration, and his voice sounds a bit panicked.

You cross your arms, this time showing a deadpan gaze: "You don't even understand what a bug is?" You...... Are you an antique? ”

…… It felt as if the other side had been stabbed, and for a moment, they didn't say a word.

You take a deep breath: "Alright, a bug is ...... Hmm, it means an internal fault in a program. Because a bug itself means a little bug, it's like a bug inside the program that has bitten a hole. You should understand this meaning, right? ”

Tenna's voice still sounded a bit disappointed: "I...... Got it, well, really got it...... Right, um, what's your name? "He spoke a bit awkwardly, changing the subject, and you still fell expressionlessly into this pitch-black pit.

"Me?" Y/N。 Isn't it more important to get me out of this state than the trivial issue of my name? Is your game just a program that keeps making me fall? That's really boring." "Being stuck in this situation all the time has made you a bit angry, and your words have become a bit stiff. After saying that, you start to regret it again; your tone seems a bit too harsh, and the other side ...... It has nothing to do with you, you don't need to say that.

But before you can make amends, Tenna has already spoken. His voice sounds a bit awkward, yet he deliberately laughs loudly: "Ahahahaha...... This is just a small accident! Dear star, our game is definitely not like this; there are still many exciting things ahead worth exploring! "Along with this sentence was the sound of him rapidly tapping on the keyboard.

"Since it's just a minor accident, could you put me back on the ground again?" You said calmly.

“…… I don't know how to do this, what should I do? "At this moment, his voice was so soft, it was as if he was speaking right next to your ear. You instinctively turned your head to look, but you didn't see anyone beside you. You really wondered where Tenna was.

"You can just design a death routine for the drop, or send my character's target directly to the starting point. Your voice is still calm, and you even yawned...... Wait, can you yawn even in your sleep?

You listen to Tenna tapping on the keyboard, then his voice turns frustrated: "Seems useless...... I clearly wrote death programs for you. ”

You press your temples, feeling your head throbbing up and down...... You honestly have no idea why this guy writes game programs—it's obviously bad.

"Alright, if it doesn't work, just rewrite my coordinates to the starting point. You know how to write this, right?" But you were still patient, since if this was a dark world you created, not just a dream...... Uh, then you probably don't need to expect to wake up right away. If you can move freely, that means your old practice of forcing yourself to wake up through physical movements is no longer effective...... So you have to find a way to find the end, so you should be able to leave.

You didn't hear his voice, only a string of keystrokes. Then, your vision blurred, and you glanced over. Finally, you stopped falling. Behind you was that wooden house, and you seemed to have returned to your birthpoint.

…… Well, the adventure has probably officially begun.
