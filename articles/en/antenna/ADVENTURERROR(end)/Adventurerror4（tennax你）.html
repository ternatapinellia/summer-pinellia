Adventurerror4 (tennax you)

A gust of wind blew by, only ruffling your messy hair and the small stones in the forest that, for some reason, were carried by the wind... And yet you heard tenna's voice acting, and you felt he was hardly even acting. His voice was exaggerated, with an inexplicable sense of joy: "Y/N, with the help of MR.(ANT)TENNA, successfully defeated the monster in front of them! As expected, tenna, who gives all the instructions, knows everything, has foresight, and is certainly a master in this world! And Y/N is also wise and mighty..."

You can't help but interrupt him: "Stop, stop, stop, stop bragging like this. Can't we just focus on solving the challenge in front of us?" ”

“…… Oh. "You see a muffled text appear in the air, and his voice sounds muffled, probably...... It's because you interrupted him, and he seems unhappy.

You shrugged, not really minding the matter, just sheathed your sword and continued forward. On the road ahead, you didn't encounter any other monsters. The arrow pointed straight ahead, and the area ahead grew even more spacious, until you left the forest and stood at the edge of the soil...... Because you saw a city ahead, but you had to first overcome the obstacle before you could get there.

Calling them obstacles is just a few jumping fun. You used to always play 2D parkour, but this is your first time playing 3D...... You feel a bit eager to try, but just as you walk toward the edge of the cliff, you see the arrow suddenly descending for some reason.

You silently stared at the arrow, then silently at the city ahead. Finally, you pointed at the arrow and asked, "What does this mean? Does it mean I'm going into the water?" Although my armor isn't heavy, I can't float up in the water. Wouldn't I be drowning...... You muttered to yourself. Tenna, who had been busy rewriting code for the objects in the city you were about to reach, quickly turned to look, only to realize that the code he had written for the arrow was timed...... When the time came, the arrow naturally fell.

Tenna cleared her throat twice, choosing to manually hold the arrow to direct the signal. The arrow pointed forward again: "Ahem, this was just a small accident. It's fine now......"

Then he suddenly raised his voice, and his voice shifted to a loud voice: "Y/N knows the arrow's direction is absolutely correct. As long as you follow the arrow's direction forward, you can reach the kingdom, obtain the holy sword, and then set out to defeat the Demon King—"

You raised a hand to ask, interrupting him: "Wait, so I'm the only one going to hunt the Demon King? Do I not even have teammates?" ”

Tenna's voice faltered for a moment, then he changed his pitch, switching to a low, sneaky tone and quietly asked you, "Uh, then, what other team members does a hero need?" He whispered, probably not wanting to ruin the grand atmosphere he had created in his previous broadcast voice.

"At best, it's a swordsman responsible for physical damage, a mage for magic damage, a heavy knight for holding damage at the front, and a mage for healing. That's how this is usually done in typical RPGs...... Uh, of course, I can handle the first three tasks by myself, but I don't have healing spells, so at least give me a healer teammate, right? "You sound pretty convinced and analyzed it for him. Anyway, you're a swordsman who deals magic damage now...... Uh, I'm not sure if this is a universal weapon.

"Uh...... Alright, I understand. "Tenna's voice sounded a bit guilty. Listening to him tapping on the keyboard, you couldn't tell what he was doing.

But tenna...... In fact, he was working on the town code he hadn't finished writing earlier. He was turning all the paintings in the townspeople's homes or the wall decorations into TVs...... Hmm, he would definitely fix them before you arrive in town!

Then he cleared his throat and continued in that broadcast tone: "Our warrior Y/N will be appointed by the king in the kingdom about to arrive ahead, and will have his own teammates in the hero squad—this is also the second member of our hero squad. Let's look forward to it!" ”

His voice had a hint of cheering, and you were indeed curious about who the team member he mentioned was...... Is it a Light Clan from the same world as you, or, like Tenna mentioned, a Clan of Darkness in the current world, or is it just a character from the game?

But regardless, the most important thing is to enter earlier and reach the opposite bank of the river...... Arrive at that city. So you look at several grassy platforms above the rushing water ahead. Although you don't know exactly how Tenna did it, you still take a deep breath, move forward, then jump and land—even though it's your first time playing 3D parkour, you know you can land precisely above your destination.

But right after, your vision went black, and then a bright red message 'GAME OVER' appeared before you. Your temples twitched involuntarily. In fact, you didn't fall into the river. Who knows how you died?! Then your vision blurred, and when you looked again, you returned to the spawn point, still in front of the same forest. Looking back, it was the little cabin from the starting point...... Yes, you were instantly back at your starting point.

Take a deep breath, then another deep breath, trying to stay calm. You tell yourself not to get too worked up, that getting angry isn't good for your health...... Well, then you hear Tenna being very cautious. A nervous apologetic voice: "That ...... Y/N, I'm sorry, the code you used to fall in the cabin earlier—I wrote 'death' as 'whereabouts', but for some reason, it didn't work at the time, and instead it worked now...... So you just died......

You pat your forehead and exhale, saying, "It's nothing, but why did I come back here after resurrecting?" "Damn it, this is already the third time you've set out from the spawn point!!

"Uh...... "Hey, I clearly wrote a save point ...... at that spot just now." It was Tenna's voice muttering to himself, along with the sound of typing. You crossed your arms and waited for his answer, then you heard Tenna's voice: "Ah, I wrote the code wrong. I have to stay there for 5 minutes to save...... Sorry, I'll make a timely save for you. If you need to save, just tell me, and I'll save it for you......" His voice sounded a bit submissive, but he also put up a holographic screen floating in front of you, ...... Above it was a strange image of a TV screen, no eyes, only a strangely long nose that looked like it was sticking out its tongue.

You fell silent, not knowing why you thought this image resembled the face you had for lunch today...... Uh, maybe Tenna just liked this look, and you convinced yourself like that. Then you sighed, put your hands on your hips, and asked, "Alright—So, could you take me to the cliff from earlier?" I don't want to run any further, and I think I should have dinner now. Look, my fullness has dropped again. "As you spoke, you pointed to the hunger indicator in the lower right corner of your vision, which showed you are currently at 50 fullness.

You didn't hear Tenna's voice, only the sound of keyboard typing, then your vision blurred, followed by the gentle sound of flowing water—well, you opened your eyes and found yourself back at the very spot where you had just been about to jump.

Immediately after, a table and a chair appeared in front of you, with a steaming hot dish on top. It looked similar to the meal you had at noon, except this time it wasn't shaped like a nose, but a big smile. You took a bite; the dish was salty and tasted pretty good—after you finished, your fullness level returned to 90. Looks like you'll need to eat properly three meals a day, even though eating in the game doesn't feel much of a big deal.

You rub your stomach, stand up again, and look at the small platforms ahead, just in case you ask again: "I'll ask again, I have a save here for a save file, right? And you changed the code that dropped it to be dead, right?" ”

"I've changed, I've changed. This time there will definitely be no problem, just rest assured...... You hear Tenna's voice almost nagging.

Then he raised his voice again: "Y/N has once again arrived at the edge of the raging stream. She is certain that following the arrow's guidance, after she jumps over, she can reach the other shore, reach the kingdom, and begin her true adventure—" As you spoke, you also heard the sound of the arrow...... Uh, I don't know if it's just my imagination, but you keep feeling that the arrow's nose just got a bit bigger. You sigh and still jump forward. Luckily, this time you didn't fall and die. Unfortunately, your running start wasn't enough, and you fell straight into the river, then were washed away by the river...... GAME OVER。

You fell silent, but luckily the save point was here. Then you heard Tenna's voice again: "Oh my god, we really weren't paying attention and fell into the river...... Hero, you have to remember, if you run out of money, you can jump twice in the air, as long as you keep moving in the right direction—"

But you interrupted him again, rolling your eyes: "Got it, got it, you really don't feel pain talking while standing...... This time, there's absolutely no problem!" You half complained, then started running up again, jumping off, and this time finally managed to cross the raging stream and reach the other side.
