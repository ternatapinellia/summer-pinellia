You grab Tenna's hand and run ahead, but he's still muttering, "But if something unexpected happens...... The CRT TV will definitely stand in front of the owner, the first to protect them! Whether it's a fire, typhoon, flood, or a natural disaster, or if a stranger breaks into your home or encounters a murderer or robber, the CRT TV is definitely the most reliable assistant...... "He rambles on, and you can't help but interrupt him: "Alright, alright, what you're saying sounds like you're cursing the family that owns a CRT TV...... Can't you say something nice? ”

"Besides, it's just a bulky TV...... Where does the owner get the ability to protect the owner? The owner should at least protect the CRT TV from harm, since repairing the appliances would cost a lot of money." You couldn't help but keep complaining.

Tenna was quiet for a while, so long you thought he couldn't even speak...... Or maybe he disappeared, or if they weren't still holding hands, you'd really think so. You turned to look at Tenna in confusion, startled—for some reason, his screen had turned dark at this moment. Although he obediently followed behind you, ...... But his antenna drooped, and his head hung low, clearly unhappy.

Just when you want to say something else—he suddenly looks happy again, just excitedly saying, "Alright, alright—yes, a CRT TV is very useful for the families who own it! It can play programs during family gatherings, liven up the whole party atmosphere, and give families with only children a chance to play—the CRT TV is their best toy! Of course, if needed, anyone can find their favorite programs on the CRT TV—" His screen was lit, but you always felt he was forcing a smile—after all, cold sweat was dripping down his screen, and his volume suddenly turned up, his voice somehow sharp and even harsh—after all, he spoke very fast.

You interrupted him helplessly: "Alright...... Alright, well, I know CRT TVs are useful, everyone needs them, I get it." That sentence sounded more like a perfunctory explanation—well, you already found what he said a bit funny, after all, nowadays...... Times have changed so fast, old TVs have long been replaced with LCD TVs. Of course, you don't really need TVs anymore; you mostly use computers or mobile phones...... These facilities are far more advanced than CRT TVs.

But think about it, Tenna probably doesn't want to hear such things.

You pulled Tenna into the palace, but you heard Tenna's seemingly cautious voice: "Th-then, people will always need a CRT TV, right?! Including you?" "It seems a bit urgent again...... That's probably how you judge it.

You didn't turn around, just nodded helplessly and answered Tenna's question: "That's right...... Although CRT TVs are already outdated for us, having a TV like this at home and occasionally watching nostalgic shows isn't a bad idea—though some people probably don't like it. But...... Well, if it were like you now, I don't think anyone would dislike you, right? At least I don't dislike you. "You really mean the truth...... Well, although he is a bit noisy, you don't dislike him. After all, you do enjoy lively moments, and it seems that after hearing your answer, he was in a better mood. Anyway, you saw flowers blooming at the tip of his nose again.

"Oh...... Oh, thank you, Y/N. I knew there would still be some of the People of Light who needed an old TV—like you! "He was very happy, but still let you pull him toward the throne—you just vaguely saw someone above. Of course, the castle isn't very big, but before long you arrive before the king, and then you watch the person on the throne fall silent—well, this person, uh, maybe not human, you don't even know what it is, looks like a microphone—or rather, a headpiece with a microphone.

You were silent for a moment, tugged at Tenna's sleeve, and quietly asked, "Are you sure...... Is this guy the king? ”

"Of course! Mike is my best partner, my best subordinate—he certainly has no objections to being king! "Tenna was proud, although ...... Although none of his subordinates were here now, and everything here was just his fiction.

You glanced at Tenna with a complicated expression, then stepped forward two paces, hesitated to speak, knelt on one knee, and said to the king: "...... Honorable king, I heard that the nation's sacred relic was stolen, so I came specially to prepare to defeat the demon king and reclaim the relic. ”

The person on the throne—just like Tenna—began to shout loudly: "Oh, dear hero—we've been anxious for days, and finally you've arrived. Now, we won't have to fear anymore! Next, I will bestow the holy sword upon the only hero—of course, before that, you must prove you are a hero worthy of great responsibility! ”

"So, how do you prove it?" You were quite patient in asking this question, and this ...... The microphone head didn't keep you waiting long; he just asked loudly.

"What is our slogan? Once you pass this test, you can prove you are the tru, only brave one! ”

And your mouth twitched—Oh my god, you never expected the so-called trial to be so simple?! Or rather...... Hmm, you never expected the so-called trial to be so ridiculous. Anyway, you twitched your mouth as you remembered what you'd encountered since you entered this city...... This is completely different from the Celtic-style forest you saw at first—this place is just a TV town?!

In short, in the end, you still stiffened your face and read out the lines in a dry tone: "...... I love TV." ”

The microphone head above nodded, hands on hips, looking very satisfied: "Excellent, that's it! We love TV—you've passed the test, come and take your holy sword!" He snapped his fingers, followed by the rumbling sound of the door opening—you stared at the door behind the throne as it opened, and then the guy with the microphone hood led you around.

"Let's move forward. The Holy Sword is just ahead. I'm looking forward to hearing the good news that you have successfully defeated the Demon King and brought back the sacred relic!" He announced, making room for you, so you and Tenna headed toward this ...... You walked toward the pitch-black so-called treasure trove, with only a CRT TV-shaped chandelier hanging on the wall. Other than that, there was nothing else. You had no idea how far you had gone before finally reaching your destination—in front of a large door.

Then you see...... Uh, the door has a password, still a four-digit electronic screen, and next to it is the same pigsty code marker, except this time there is no note on the door.

…… You look at the electronic screen, dig through the numbers 3712, and then the door opens with a bang. Of course, along with the sound of the door opening, you also hear Tenna clapping. You can't tell if he's doing it on purpose or genuinely—"Y/N is amazing, cracking the password so easily!" Effortlessly, I feel the journey ahead won't be difficult at all! ”

“…… Your hint is on the wall ahead, did you forget? Of course it's easy, not difficult. "You whispered, cleared your throat twice, and moved forward, then you saw...... Hmm, the ...... stuck in the head on the stone platform. A staff???

Wasn't it supposed to be a holy sword? Why a staff?? You pulled out the staff with a TV-shaped tip and instinctively opened the skill list to glance at it...... Hmm, normal attacks, heavy attacks, spiral spinning slashes...... Alright, it's the sword skill list.

“…… I know you wrote the code wrong, but can the code still turn a sword into a staff?" "That's way too illogical...... Uh, it's not that it can't be used, but you casually swing the staff and leave sword marks on the stone platform.

…… Hmm, looks like it can still fire sword and blade damage from a distance...... You hold this staff and choose to replace the sword you're carrying first.

Tenna chuckled awkwardly twice. You turned to look at him, and cold sweat was dripping down his screen again: "Uh...... Ahem, isn't this just perfect? After all, a hero should use a sword! And I'm also good at swordsmanship! I've already maxed your swordsmanship to LV.999! ”

You checked your skill panel...... All the slashes and charged attacks were maxed out just as Tenna said, but you still showed half-moon eyes and shook your staff: "Hmm, hmm...... But this is just a staff, not a sword. Instead of using a staff instead of a sword, you might as well swap the skill lists for these two...... Well, this is pretty fun. ”

As you spoke, you swung your staff and unleashed a slash that struck the wall opposite, leaving a deep sword mark...... Then you drew your sword and slashed at the stone platform ahead, using an ice spell. The stone platform also left a sword mark, which was frozen in place.

"Hmm, I won't change it. This way works pretty well." You put away the sword and swapped it back for a staff, nodding in satisfaction, while Tenna just stared blankly on the screen, expressionless.
