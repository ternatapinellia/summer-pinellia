Did the archaeologists dig up a real CRT?

The inspiration comes from SG's digging (? ) But it has a bit of a snail girl vibe (hmm...... ）

——

Resting your chin on your hand, you look at the replica archaeological soil blocks you take out of the box in front of you, feeling a bit troubled. To be honest, you don't even know why you impulsively bought this—maybe it was because of the description on the box. Although you've always kept your distance from them, this time seeing those exquisite vintage items on them still couldn't resist buying them.

Well, after all, they all look great. You really like collecting these useless but beautiful little items. As for where to store them and where to tidy them up later, that's a matter to decide later!

So when you roll up your sleeves, you feel like you're wearing those red ribbons that represent hard work and hard work. Of course, you really do want to work hard now.

You pick up the small chisel that comes with the soil clod you, and then start digging. But it's really exhausting, and the area is a mess. You think you'll have to clean thoroughly once you're done. Now you regret it—if you'd known, you would've just bought one—it wouldn't have cost so much. Why waste time and energy here? You don't really like this kind of work either.

But hard work pays off! After your relentless efforts, you managed to dig open that inexplicably hard clod of earth, revealing the object buried inside.

You take out that tiny item and take a look, then fall into deep thought. Honestly, it's nothing like the dazzling look on the promotional image. Stunning and extraordinary, it's just a grayish, shrunken CRT model. To be honest, you even feel a bit disappointed, but you still neatly place that tiny CRT model on the table, clean up the useless dust, wash up, and go to sleep, but...... You always have the illusion that the CRT model you placed is flashing when the lights are off?

You glanced over there, but unfortunately, you didn't notice anything unusual. You had no choice but to close your eyes and quietly lie down on the bed and fall asleep.

A dreamless night, but you feel like you always feel restless. This description doesn't seem quite right. Basically, you feel like there's a heavy sensation on your body? It's as if something is pressing down on you, or maybe it's just something heavy holding you? But when you rub your eyes and sit up in bed, there's nothing on the bed or beside you, so you start to suspect you're just feeling allergic.

And maybe it's just your imagination, but when you get dressed and stand up to walk past your desk, you always feel that the CRT model is no longer where you originally placed it. But it's a subtle feeling—after all, it's still there. You just always feel as if it's been displaced a bit.

If all of this was just your imagination, and you were just overthinking it, now you can't fool yourself. Actually, you smelled a delicious aroma outside in the bedroom just now, and at the time thought it was just a hallucination caused by hunger. Now you can't convince yourself anymore. You stare blankly at the bedroom door and look outside, only to find breakfast already set on the table—a meal that looks beautiful, aromatic, and tastes delicious. Probably not knowing your taste, you even prepared a little of everything.

Now it's great—you don't have to prepare your own breakfast, and you can even finish it quickly before heading to school. But who exactly made this breakfast? You sniffed carefully, and the fragrant aroma filled your nose—probably non-toxic. Only after confirming did you feel at ease and start eating. Your stomach, which had been flattened and even aching from hunger, was quickly warmed up, and you even felt a bit full. Reluctantly, you put down the sweet bread and grabbed your backpack to go to school.

…… At noon and evening, when you come back, the food is still piping hot on the table. Could it be that some kind snail girl has come to help you with these things? It's just that you don't believe in such myths, but after thinking for a long time, you can't think of anyone you know who might do such a thing.

After all, you don't have many close friends, and your parents are generous enough to let you rent a place elsewhere. But you don't really know how to cook, so just heating up a meal for yourself is enough.

Now it's great—you don't even have to worry about what you should eat—just follow the dishes this unknown person makes and decide your day's meal! This even cures your decision paralysis. In short, you gratefully put your palms together and say goodbye, though you don't even know who you're praying to.

Only then did you start and happily enjoy the meal.

…… Now you look at everything before you in a somewhat flustered way. You realize you were just thinking too well. Cooking and cleaning were one thing, but why did she even wash your underwear? You watched the underwear hanging on the rack, swaying in the wind, slowly petrify. Then you patted your face, trying hard to wake yourself up.

Alright, so be it, so you don't have to do anything yourself. That's actually a good thing......

But then something even stranger happened. After you finally got used to the food that appeared on the table for no reason, to the inexplicably clean and tidy floor and clothes, and after a while, the weather turned cold. You were anxiously calculating whether you should add more clothes and lay out a thicker quilt. But by the next day, you found the clothes folded and placed at the bedside. When you came back, you found that all the sheets and duvet covers on the bed had been changed—obviously thicker.

You fell silent for no reason, instinctively looking around, but unfortunately, you couldn't see anything unusual—only the CRT model on the table was suspiciously and subtly shifted.

Taking a deep breath, you pat the trench coat you just put on today and decide to hold off for now. At the same time, that curiosity grows day by day. Before, you could pretend not to know and calmly enjoy the service, but now—well, you really want to know who is doing this.

So, even though it was the weekend and you didn't have classes, you still pretended something was going on, grabbed your backpack, and headed out, then hid not far away, quietly watching your house.

Through the window, you see the CRT model on the table jump off the table...... Then it turns into a TV person who's unknown tall, almost reaching the ceiling. Wait, ...... TV person?

You thought blankly for a moment, then quickly accepted the setup. After all, this incident was less impactful than the reality of the CRT model coming to life. In other words, maybe you just misread it. Unfortunately, you rubbed your eyes and realized there was really no difference—everything was the same.

Alright, maybe you just got the hidden model! You comforted yourself like this, then Kan Ze, that TV guy, walked out, picked up a broom that was very small to him, diligently helped sweep and mop the floor, and then went into the kitchen, looking like he was hesitating about what to do.

After hesitating for a moment, you tiptoed back and suddenly opened the door. The TV guy who had been in the kitchen was caught red-handed by you. His body stiffened, and then, trying to run straight back to your room, he turned back into that tiny CRT model, pretending nothing had happened and that you hadn't seen anything.

You hurried forward, then grabbed his arm, trying not to let him slip back—forgive you, his hand was just too big, you couldn't hold on.

"So, you've been the one doing all these things these days?"

He hesitated for a moment, but still turned his back to you, but nodded.

"Oh...... So, what's your name? "After all, you can't call her that by 'TV person' or something, that's just too rude!

"Ah, me? I'm Mr. Tenna, the most popular host! Uh...... Although I probably don't think that's the case now. "When asked for his name, he instinctively straightened up, his voice rising, and turned to face you. Maybe it was just my imagination—otherwise, you'd think he could hold a microphone in his hand."

You nodded: "Alright, I understand. So why have you been doing these things these days, Mr. Tenna?" Although I also like the feeling of these things being mysteriously done, don't you need nothing in return? ”

He was probably stumped by your question, not responding in the first few minutes, and you patiently waited for his answer. After all, you still had plenty of time and didn't mind spending that little time.

“…… After all, I have to thank you for rescuing me from where I used to be! You know, I was trapped there for a long time......

As he starts rambling about his past experiences, you get a bit distracted and realize, 'So he really got a hidden version for himself.'

But driving the hidden model feels pretty good?

"But what kind of return do you think you need...... Maybe I don't need too much? I just need you to keep looking at me, always watching me. Always ...... Always letting your gaze be on me. ”

Originally, you were supposed to look up at him, but now, Tenna is looking you in the eye. I don't know why he can still shrink back, but he's probably saying things with utmost seriousness.

You tilted your head slightly, as if thinking for a moment, then watched him grow more and more nervous and helpless, his hands behind his back probably pinching his fingers or clothes, feeling uneasy.

So you smiled faintly, held his hand, and shook it twice: "Sure, of course you can. I'm watching you all the time, and I will in the future. Actually, I've been watching you all along...... Actually, I've been watching you all along. ”

Now, you are seated at the table and begin enjoying today's lunch. It will be like this in the future.
