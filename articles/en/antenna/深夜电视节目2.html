Late Night TV Show 2

Tennax, you, hmm, all character settings belong to me (? ）

With the Persona 4 fusion setting, it's okay if you don't know Persona 4! I'm just a pure Photoshop fan.

——

The next day, you still go to school as usual, no different from usual. The only difference is that the senior who appeared on the late-night TV show was found dead hanging from a utility pole, just like a female anchor from a while ago.

Your hand holding the lunchbox trembled, and luckily you didn't fall.

—You recall the completely different world you saw on TV. If it were that foggy world, this would be understandable.

So, did the commercial I saw on TV before, Mr. Tenna, also die because of it? Did he also die because of the late-night TV program previews? Do you think the answer to this question is certainly no? Come on, these are two completely different worlds—how could this happen?

But you wavered, hesitating. Because apart from the game room that day, you hadn't seen anywhere else, and you couldn't be sure the two worlds were completely different—maybe you just hadn't seen them yourself. Honestly, you don't know. To know the exact answer, you'd have to go to that world again.

…… Haha, I guess I was overthinking it. It must be two different worlds. Why did I think he was in danger, and why worry? After all, he definitely wasn't a real person.

Probably.

I worked hard to adjust my mood, spent my class time unremarkably, participated in club activities, went home, had dinner, finished homework, washed up, and went to bed.

This has been the case for several days in a row. Even though you've tried hard to adjust and told yourself that none of this is a problem, you still become increasingly anxious.

…… After all, it's better to fear the unlikely outcome than the ten-thousand-case. So, what about the unexpected? You feel restless because of this.

Another girl in the class has disappeared. You planned to stay out of it, but while shopping at the Junis store, you see your classmates slipping into the TV.

Biting your lip, you take a deep breath. You walk forward in a daze, only to bump into the shelf ahead. Your body falls forward with inertia, and everything goes black before you. In the final scene, you only notice yourself falling into the whirlpool inside the TV.

Cabbages were scattered all over the floor. When you pressed your temples and stood up, you noticed the cabbages were still scattered beside you, and behind you were the same three TVs as before.

Rubbing your tailbone, you look around and prepare to leave, but suddenly pause.

…… This was not the foggy space. Unexpectedly, there was only silence and a faint howling cold wind, and along the narrow road, the phantom of a TV floating in the air.

Maybe it's intuition? You guess you've fallen into that world again. You walk forward, gently push open the door crack, and the blinding light makes you squint your eyes first.

When you recover, wipe away your tears, and look forward again, you only see that huge TV person—well, Mr. Tenna—standing in front of him are a bunch of NPCs you've seen in the game before, and it looks like Tenna is loudly scolding the staff.

Should I leave on my own? Or should I push open the door and walk out? No, no, no, no, I can't really choose to open the door—

You try to close the door and leave, but at that moment, a red one—you think it's probably called pippins—suddenly jumps up, then runs toward the door and pushes it open.

And so, you suddenly appeared out of nowhere.

Run away and then return to those three TVs. Is it still possible to return to your original world? You don't even need to think, just turn around and run. In short, you even covered your face, though doing so probably doesn't help......

But in any case, you start hypnotizing yourself that everything is fine. Unfortunately and desperately, just as you are about to reach the finish line and hope and endless anticipation arise, your shoulder is grabbed, and then hope shatters crisply, with a snap.

You are desperate, truly desperate as a wolf or tiger, always following you like a shadow.

…… Or maybe you just feel a slight fear at this moment? Why is it fear? Panic, confusion, or embarrassment?

You don't know, but you just froze in place, not daring to move, and of course, didn't dare to turn around. You instinctively showed that V-shaped cat-like mouth, feeling cold sweat dripping from your forehead onto the floor. No need to think about it—if he let go, you'd definitely be collapsed on the floor.

No, no, no, this isn't some horror game at all. What exactly are you afraid of, why do you feel guilty? You're still lost in your thoughts, when you hear a somewhat muffled voice behind you.

Or maybe there was a faint, barely noticeable tremor? Normally, you wouldn't notice, but now your nerves are tense, so it's easy to notice.

“…… Now, can you turn around? ”

Out of the corner of your eye, you catch a Pippins making faces at you. You want to sneer, and if you heard correctly, those guys behind you must have let out a sigh of relief!

Helpless, you sighed, puffed out your cheeks, and your voice was muffled, clearly not very happy: "Then could you please let go of my hand first?" ”

You feel the heavy pressure on your shoulders disappear. Even though you haven't turned around yet, you can still imagine the unease and confusion behind you, accompanied by anxious voices: "Oh, I'm really sorry, my star, I just didn't expect ......"

For some reason, you feel like laughing, and luckily you didn't really give out and sit down!! Your image has been preserved, and you can still put on a calm and serious persona in front of Tenna!

You take a deep breath, close your eyes, and suddenly turn around.

You hear his stunned, held breath, the fading sound, the fading footsteps.

You feel the footsteps grow louder and draw closer to you, then your body is tightly embraced, and you fall into a heavy yet warm embrace.

You feel like you can't breathe, open your eyes expressionlessly, and it's wonderful.

His hand seemed to be trembling. You don't know why this happened—can it really have such a big impact after just a few days away?

In any case, you still spoke. Although you coughed several times before speaking, you were so oppressed that you couldn't say anything.

Before you could speak, Tenna's hand loosened and she took a few steps back, unable to tell what he was feeling because the screen went black again.

"Oh, sorry, sorry, it's just that too much time has passed! The game time is about to skyrocket. Hurry up and finish the last round, my star?" ”

That deliberate, high-raised smile again. You can't quite tell what's going on, but since you're here, it's already here...... Let's double-check if there's anything wrong.

Heaven and earth, honestly, you're just afraid that death warning will happen right before your eyes, Mr. Tenna.

No matter what, you don't want to see someone you've interacted with die right in front of you.

Really, that's all.

"Then let's go to the game room first. But let me make it clear: if I'm tired, I'll come rest in the lounge." "Look around here, at least there's a sofa here where you can lie down—you're not picky anyway.

You can clearly see Mr. Tenna, walking ahead, stiffen for a moment, then speed up his speech: "Oh, that's not it—I promise this will definitely be an amazing experience!" You'll get so excited you won't want to stop at all, let alone be so tired you can rest! ”

…… But gaming always gets tiring, right? You really want to say this, but after thinking it over, you might as well forget it and just quietly follow behind.

Haha, at worst it's just a matter of death—better forget that.

Soon, you arrive at the game room. You find the game controller you used last time still there, the walls around you seem a bit old, and you have no idea how much time has passed. The game screen does show a certain duration, which is truly sky-high. At least, you have no intention of calculating how long it is.

Picking up the controller, you still stop at the piece you had puzzled last time. But for some reason, the puzzle you unlocked earlier also went back. You diligently solved the earlier puzzles and looked at the next one—hmm, this time it's the Huarong Road puzzle, it's fine, you're really used to it.

Fortunately, after solving this puzzle, there are no more doors ahead. However, as you control the character and move forward, you'll notice there's still a long road ahead. It's obvious there are other gates ahead, but now all these doors are open. The truth is, it's hard to guess what they're actually going on.

No doubt, Tenna definitely deleted the puzzles from the end. Got the keys. Now there were three keys. Go back and open the tightly locked door, and the game character would enter a new world. This level was basically complete.

The further you go, the darker it gets, but the further you go, there is light. As you control the game character and walk forward, standing at the only bright spot, you can see the torch hanging on the wall. It seems to be a cave, and you can even hear the dripping of water.

Then, not just an illusion, the game's speakers echoed with the roar of monsters, the approaching footsteps, and suddenly a loud, tense and thrilling music playing.

Out of the corner of your eye, you can see Tenna nervously uneasy, probably wondering if you actually like this kind of game. However...... But a chase isn't easy! Anyway, the lights around here are so bright, and there's a Tenna sitting nearby—what is there to be afraid of!

You confidently control your character to run, but there are too many forks ahead, and you always end up in dead ends. Then comes the ghost face and the jumping face. Honestly, at first you were startled by that ghost face, your heart pounding hard many times. But after dying so many times, you become numb. You just memorize the route skillfully, hoping your next escape will go more smoothly.

Haha, being so skilled is truly sad. You're finding joy in hardship thinking that.

Tenna, standing nearby, had been playing on a pitch-black screen since earlier, and I don't know if it's just your imagination, but do you think his figure is ...... Seems to be gradually shrinking?

After you finally get through this chase, the game prompts you to complete the QTE within the time limit and seal three demons, or you'll die.

You ignite the flames of battle with a blank expression. Although it's not overwhelmingly excited, your stubbornness is just here. You must pass this level!

But unfortunately, you have met death once again.

You grit your teeth and prepare to try again. Although you're startled by the ghost face attack once more, it's all fake anyway—it's nothing, right?

Suddenly, your shoulders felt heavy. When you turned around, you realized that Mr. Tenna, who was originally twice your size, had somehow shrunk to your size. The TV's head was resting on your shoulder, and the antenna was drooping. For some reason, it felt like he actually didn't dare to watch such a terrifying game scene.

But wasn't this game prepared by him? You really wanted to ask about it, hesitated for a moment, looked at his pitch-black screen to avoid the same situation as last time, and still just tapped his shell.

After all, you're not in such a hurry to go back just yet.

It's fine to stay here a little longer, that's how you tell yourself.

But the next attempt failed, and in the end, you pressed the wrong button, feeling excited, but all your efforts were wasted.

The ghostly face flashed again on your screen, accompanied by a piercing roar and chilling music. Just as you were about to muster up the courage to try again, all the lights in the game room suddenly went out, leaving only the screen below. The pale ghost face reflected your pale face.

Your teeth trembled. You tried hard not to let out a ghostly scream, trying hard to stay calm, ignoring the fear rising inside you, and slowly turned your head: "...... Mr. Tenna, the lights in the game room seem broken. Should we go to the lounge and sit for a while first? ”

"Huh?" Haha, it's fine, I think this light will be ready soon! The staff just informed me; they're just maintaining the lights! "You saw Tenna's antenna tremble a few times in the dark. And you can tell his voice is trembling almost imperceptibly.

…… You remain skeptical, and you notice that in the dark, Tenna seems to have shrunk by two sizes. Well, if he says it's fine, then it's fine!

“…… Haha, really? Then let's finish this scene first." "Your teeth trembled again. You really couldn't think of any more words, so you simply didn't say anything. You definitely don't want Tenna to see that you're actually scared."

"I believe my star will definitely achieve great results!" Tenna was quite supportive, even applauding him, though now his applause sounded much softer.

"Maybe if I try again, I'll succeed!"

He kept muttering and loudly announcing something. Why not just go all out and try again? This time, it really worked!

You exclaimed excitedly, then instinctively raised your hand to high-five, and then you noticed Tenna was standing there, raising her hand to high-five you.

…… What? You can't help but laugh.

After passing through this room, the game character can finally move to the next room. However, the rules here are even stranger: when the lights are on, you can't move; you can only watch monsters approach, and you can only move when the lights are dim. You also have to navigate through the maze.

You tried once or twice, but each time the monsters successfully chased and blocked you. You couldn't take it anymore! You couldn't take it anymore. Oh my god, playing horror games in such a pitch-black environment—what on earth were you thinking!

You scooped Tenna into your arms, carrying the now shrunken Mr. Tenna to about the size of a pet, and ran out of the game room, panting heavily. Fortunately, the lights outside were still normal, and there was even very cheerful music, forming a stark contrast to the eerie atmosphere inside the game room earlier.

Haha, how could something like this happen? You wiped your face, still holding this hard, little iron lump in your arms, and, lost in thought, walked unsteadily toward the lounge.

"Next time, let's try a different game, okay?" You wiped your face again.

"Oh! Of course no problem. You can play whatever you want, or you can choose any show you want—" He got excited again, growing two rounds bigger. You couldn't carry him anymore, your arm was sore, so you put it on the floor.

"Hmm, but I don't want to play games for now." You squatted down, feeling like he was mumbling something just now, but you didn't catch it clearly.

Actually, he had been muttering very quietly just now, deliberately not wanting you to hear: "Mike clearly told me this trick would definitely work...... How did it end up like this now...... Alright, next time I'll practice my courage first......

"I have to go back, I still need to rest, I still have homework to finish, I haven't eaten dinner, I haven't previewed my homework yet, and I'm about to prepare for the midterm exams. I have classes tomorrow too......" You started counting on your fingers about all this. Looking at it this way, you were quite busy, even though this kind of life was quite boring.

"But that kind of life is boring, isn't it! You can definitely enjoy more interesting programs, play more meaningful and fun games, and enjoy relaxing time!" Tenna's antenna trembled, her tone quickened, and she grew bigger. Now you have to tilt your head back—and you're standing up.

"Yes, but that's real life. No matter how happy I am now, to me, it's just a false moment." You repeated calmly, after all, people have to face reality. It's impossible to stay immersed in a dreams that are both false and joyful. If you don't move forward, you'll never have a future.

"Fake?" How could it be fake—all this happiness is real! Your emotions don't lie! You must be too tired! Better rest first, get some sleep, and then get up to talk!" You felt him trying hard to force a smile, even if only to force a smile, and probably to prevent you from going back like last time, this time he wouldn't show that pitch-black screen again.

Honestly, you still have some regrets. If you'd known, you would've gone back earlier.

However...... It's no big deal to stay a little longer, since you have to admit, happiness is real.

So in the end, you still nodded: "...... Alright, I'll rest for a while. ”
