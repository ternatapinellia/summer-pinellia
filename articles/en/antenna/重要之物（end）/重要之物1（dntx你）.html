Important Thing 1 (dntx you)

You leave the world of light because...... You feel you can no longer stay in that world, or rather, there is no longer any need; the things that exist there are completely unnecessary. So you come to the dark world...... You beg the Happy Gang to find you a place to stay in Castle Town, so you settle down there. This is just right—you don't want to go out, don't want to eat, don't want to see the sun, don't want to talk to anyone, don't want to ...... to live.

You just sit dumbly in your room, hugging your knees with both hands, dumbfounded, showing no reaction or wanting to have any reaction. The dark world lords who were also left by the Happy Gang to live in Castle Town also saw you, a little citizen of light...... Of course, only Tenna still remembers you. He remembers seeing you before. Before that, he was your family's TV, and of course, it was only when you were very young...... He doesn't know how you became like this now. Although it was just something you said casually as a child, Tenna remembered it. He still remembered what you said: grow up well and stay with him forever—of course, you might not remember, but he remembers the promise you once made. Although he never mentioned you again, in fact, it was ...... He always remembered you.

He doesn't understand what exactly is going on with you. Of course, after all these years, he's no longer the same as before, and it's only natural that you will change. But in the end, he just ...... Silently knocking on your door.

You heard a noise coming from your door, your eyelids twitched, but you showed no intention of getting up to respond, and you didn't say a word. Tenna knew you were in the room, but you didn't respond. His brows furrowed, but he forced himself to suppress his irritation and knocked on your door again, showing some patience. But...... It was very quiet inside, and there was still no response.

He took a deep breath, suppressing the urge to smash the door open, and patiently asked, "Is Y/N inside?" ”

…… How would he know your name? You're a bit curious, but you really don't have the strength to respond...... If only no one would disturb you, how wonderful that would be, but...... But that's probably impossible. You stared blankly at the door, completely in a dimly lit room, then lowered your head, pretending not to hear.

Tenna knew you were inside the room, after all, you hadn't gone out at all these days—but it was still very quiet, as if no one was there; all he felt was emptiness. Tenna's expression turned cold; his patience was running low...... Even if he broke down a door, the Happy Gang wouldn't dare say much, let alone anyone else. So he clenched his fist and smashed it against your door—the door shattered with a crack, and you were startled, your body instinctively shrinking back. You curled up and looked toward the doorway...... It was a tall TV figure standing against the light at your door. Although the dark world had no sunlight, your room was so dark that when you looked at that TV person, your pupils involuntarily contracted slightly.

You're pretty sure you don't know this guy...... But Tenna just fumbled around, found the ceiling light switch in your room, and then walked straight up to you, looking down at you. You closed your eyes uncomfortably because the light was suddenly on, oh my god...... You really can't get used to this kind of light; you feel like you haven't seen a light in a long time.

And this TV person stood right in front of you, his tone cold and a bit commanding: "Come out, you can't stay here forever." ”

…… You don't want to talk, but you still have some strength, so you shake your head.

Tenna clicked his tongue irritably, torn between leaving you behind or continuing to care for him...... Hmm, the latter option was very appealing, but he wouldn't be willing to accept it. He definitely didn't want the person he once fantasized about spending the rest of his life with to end up like this. He crouched down to look at you. Now he finally looked you in eye contact, and your vacant eyes stared at him. Tenna gave another order: "Then come out and eat something. Don't die here. This place is near my room. If I die, those Lightfolk will come after me again." ”

But you just shake your head again; you don't feel any hunger; sadness has taken over most of your senses...... Other than that, you really have no other feelings.

He irritably grabbed your wrist. He didn't understand how you had become so hard to communicate with—completely different from the little girl he remembered who dared to scribble on his screen. He looked at you, and you just stared blankly at his screen. In your pupils, your TV head was also reflected. Tenna clicked his tongue, grabbed your head with both hands, and you didn't resist at all. Only a hint of confusion flickered in your eyes. You were indeed a bit curious about what Tenna was planning.

But he simply said coldly, "Now, look at me." ”

You looked at him as he said, and then you saw, a black-and-white whirlpool appeared on his screen. You saw...... You saw that faintly glowing, spinning thing, making your consciousness dizzy, even completely disappearing. You looked dazed at Tenna. Even though you had been hypnotized, you looked no different from one who hadn't.

Tenna looked at you like this, unsure if he had succeeded or not...... But this was the best solution he could come up with. He still held your head and looked you straight in the eye, though his voice softened a bit: "Alright, now, call me Tenna. You know me, don't you?" ”

“tenna…… I, should I know you?" You just answered his question blankly, following his words in a daze, even though you ...... probably really don't remember.

"You've seen it, the TV that used to be in your house," Tenna didn't want to say much about it, just glossed over it with a single sentence. Then, he began giving new instructions, "You have to eat properly, get out more and move around, and live seriously." He didn't know if this would work for you, so he could only try it for now. And sure enough, you just shook your head, tears streaming freely from your eyes.

“…… No, I, I can't ...... I, why do I want to live? "Your voice is soft, you say this, your hands involuntarily hugging your own head, you, oh my god...... Your head is just a mess, but that sadness still weighs heavily on your heart, making it impossible to relax even a little.

“…… Is there no point in living for you? Nothing matters to you, so there's no need for you to keep living. Is that what you think?" Tenna still remembered some of the psychological counseling shows she had watched before. Although his voice was now cold and stiff—he was forcing himself to say each word—while he stared at you intently, watching to see how you would react.

You shook your head and nodded: "...... I don't matter to anyone. Everyone will hate me, they'll despise me, so no one matters to me...... Anyway, in the end, I won't ...... either." You whispered honestly...... Even though you said these things, so coldly, you were still crying, your body trembling. Maybe those words truly came from your heart, but do you really want that?

Tenna was silent for a moment, his hand stiffly wiping the tears from your cheeks...... To be honest, when you cry, he felt like he understood why you were crying, but he didn't fully understand either...... Maybe it was because their situation was similar, but now he was also a TV discarded by others who were considered unimportant, so any Light Citizen was simply unimportant to him. But, but—you are different, you are different, at least that's what Tenna believes.

He hesitated for a moment, but finally cupped your head with his hands, his tone commanding: "Look at me." ”

And you really did look up blankly at Tenna.

“…… You might not matter to anyone; after all, you're just a bunch of useless people of light. He clicked his tongue lightly as he said this, and as he looked at you, his tears began to fall like they were free.

He roughly wiped the tears from your cheeks, making your cheeks a little red from his actions, while Tenna just said sternly, "No crying. Anyway, those are all useless humans, but you have to understand, you are very important to things." "For example...... He—of course, he wouldn't reveal this to you, and you sniffled, still sobbing, though not completely stopping your crying...... You just looked at Tenna with your eyes blurred.

"So, you'll think I'm important, got it?" As he spoke, he finally let go. At least that way, you'd probably pay attention to him instead of just staying in this room waiting to die.

You obediently nodded: "...... Yes, Tenna is a ...... very important TV to me. "Although you can't remember most of it clearly, you still remember some things, like that old TV.
