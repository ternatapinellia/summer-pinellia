Important Thing 3 (dntx you)

He only felt his antenna jumping, as if electric currents were running wildly throughout his body. He only knew he was overheating—in fact, Tenna just felt his body was probably a bit too hot. Of course, there was a reason for being too hot. Hearing what you said made him even angrier, a feeling he couldn't describe. He didn't know what had happened to you all these years, why you ended up like this. He also wanted to know what had happened to you over the years, even though those things didn't matter to him.

But he cares about you, so those things are exactly what he needs to know. And now, he's just holding your hand, holding you down as you squat down to wipe the floor. His tone is harsh, his face unpleasant, his screen dark, not letting you see his screen, and his tone is only a bit eerie: "I didn't let you do this kind of work. It's not like I'm short on people here. If you want to work that much, why not serve me?" Of course, his tone carried a hint of sarcasm, and you stared blankly at Tenna and asked blankly, "Is that really okay?" How am I supposed to serve you? "This is probably your habit over the years. You subconsciously think that if someone treats you a little better, you should repay them double...... Even if you don't want to do that, everyone tells you it's the right thing to do. Your parents tell you you should repay their kindness in raising you, your teachers tell you to be grateful, your friends tell you to repay the kindness they have given you.

“…… Eat me well. That counts as taking good care of me." Tenna snorted coldly. He hadn't expected you to actually respond, and his tone grew even more fierce, because his mood was clearly worse now. He really couldn't imagine that such a confident girl from before had become like this. He was clearly just saying things out of anger, but you didn't understand, which made Tenna even angrier.

You stammered, not daring to look up at him, just repeating, "Sorry, I really can't eat, so I wasted your good intentions......

Tenna took another deep breath, trying to calm herself down a bit. He looked at the plate of pasta left on the table, frowned, and finally used a fork to roll up a few pieces and brought them to your mouth, his tone cold and stiff: "Eat." ”

You shivered, but still followed Tenna's instructions, carefully swallowing the pasta. You chewed, then swallowed. Then your face turned pale again, because you almost wanted to spit it out again...... But seeing Tenna sitting opposite you with a dark, gloomy screen made you feel a bit uneasy. You didn't dare spit out what was in your mouth, even though what you had just swallowed was now mixed with the sour liquid in your stomach and returned to your mouth. So you just sat there. Tenna brought the fork back to your mouth, but you just shook your head and gave Tenna a pale smile, as if telling you you'd be fine in a while.

“…… Spit out what's in your mouth." Tenna was truly speechless. He didn't even know why you endured such things, endured things that would make you feel painful. Even if it seemed you couldn't endure even a bit longer, you would still endure it.

You didn't dare disobey his orders, so you leaned against the table and tilted your head to the side, vomiting out everything in your mouth. No one had come to deal with the vomit on the floor, so you felt a bit uneasy. Tenna looked at you like this and felt a headache coming on. Obviously, whenever you're in a bad mood, you can't eat anything, and even if you do, you'll vomit it out...... No matter how you feel, even if he hypnotizes you, it won't make your mood better. So thinking about this, he actually feels a bit irritated again. He put down his fork again. He wasn't sure if his words would make you feel better, but he could only do that. He remembered those employees really liked hearing him speak, so maybe it was useful.

“…… Hey, I really don't understand why you're in such a bad mood. How about this: let me help you relax, how about that?" He hesitated for a moment before saying this. You looked up at him in confusion, and Tenna gave that standard smile of eight teeth, pulling out a kitchen knife stained with blood—was it freshly coagulated blood, or bright red with the taste of rust.

"Let's play a game. I'll be chasing after you, and your mission is to run forward. How about that?" Maybe letting the sadness in your heart be replaced by other emotions might help—at least that's what Tenna thought. Whether it actually works or not, he wasn't really sure. Anyway, when he appeared in front of the employees like this, he just felt that those people were much more efficient, and he was quite satisfied.

But you just stared blankly at Tenna, then closed your eyes. You exposed your snow-white neck to him, your voice soft and crisp: "No need, I already know my fate. Tenna, just put the knife down and snap it, and it's over." You even felt a bit of longing, you longed for the end of death—but at this moment, you forgot something: Tenna's face darkened, his smile fading away, and he also put away the knife. His hand gently gripped your neck. If he wanted, he could snap your neck in the next second. Of course, he wouldn't do that. His tone only grew more sinister at this moment: "I told you, don't die in front of me." ”

You nervously opened your eyes again, then nodded. Tenna also turned on the screen, sizing you up, and finally just snorted coldly: "Anyway, no matter what, you have to finish this meal today. I don't want you to starve in front of me. Besides, you're dirty too—how many days have you been without a shower? I'm afraid your little arms and legs will break at the slightest movement. Eat something, or you won't be able to run back." As he spoke, he again showed a blank expression and pulled out that knife. You instinctively wanted to say he could do anything, but before the words could come out, you remembered, he said you shouldn't die in front of him, so...... So you really need to eat something to be able to run, right?

You didn't say anything, just propped yourself up on the chair, staring blankly at the plate of pasta on the table. Tenna just rolled up a spoonful of pasta with her fork and brought it to your mouth. Your mood was still bad, but at least you remembered—you couldn't die in front of him...... If you didn't eat, you know, your body was weak right now. He wouldn't even need to chase after you. If he just threw that knife in front of you, you'd faint immediately.

So in the end, you opened your mouth and ate that mouthful of pasta. Maybe it was because your emotions had at least calmed down a bit. Although your body still felt a bit uncomfortable, your stomach still ached, and you didn't feel much nausea. You just took one bite and then stopped wanting to eat—that kind of calm, almost like you were full...... Even though you knew you weren't really full, and your face was still a bit pale, you didn't spit it out. Tenna looked at you and looked at you, seemingly finally able to eat. He snorted coldly. Now he was a bit more satisfied, but seeing you like this, if he didn't watch, you probably wouldn't eat anything. So from now on, he could only stay by your side. Logically, he should have felt unhappy, but in reality...... He actually felt quite satisfied with the result, at least which meant he was indeed important to you, and that he was still a useful TV. Although he didn't need others to accept this, it was undeniable and somewhat sad that at this moment, he did have this thought.

In the end, he just rolled up another fork of pasta and held it to your mouth. He said nothing, and you obediently opened your mouth and ate the pasta. As you chewed, your stomach, which had been hungry for so long, started working again. The dizziness caused by low blood sugar in your mind vanished, and at least you felt a bit better—probably because of eating, and your mood improved a bit. Tenna looked at you now, barely satisfied. He just snorted coldly, then shoved the fork into your hand. His tone remained icy, but now you were somewhat used to him speaking like this: "Alright, you eat by yourself from now on." ”

You silently twirled the pasta on your fork and brought it to your mouth in small bites, while tenna sat across from you, arms crossed, watching you, probably thinking about something, though he didn’t say a word. He just stared at your movements, and you had no idea how much time had passed. In any case, you ate very slowly, and finally managed to finish the entire plate of pasta. Only then did Tenna lift his TV-like head, and then casually summoned a pippins to clean up the vomit left on the floor. He glanced at the time, then gave you an order: 'It’s already past three in the afternoon. You should go out for a walk.' His tone carried an air of authority that left no room for doubt. You hesitated for a moment, and finally just shook your head softly and said, 'I don’t want to go anywhere... tenna, can’t I just stay here in your room?'

You're really, really tired of a monotonous life.
