Greedy Nightmare 1 (tennax you)

The setting of Fork YN and cake tenna.

————

You hold your resume, looking gloomily at the ...... written on it. Yes, you're a fork, so it's hard to find a job in this world. After all, as long as there's cake at the workplace, if they hear you're a fork, they'll be on high alert and won't even let you in...... But, but becoming a fork isn't what you wanted...... A few months ago in college, you were fine, thinking you'd be like most of your peers—becoming cake, or just an ordinary person—but instead, you became a fork. Now look, even classmates who were close to you started to distance themselves, afraid you'd bite them right away.

But...... But you have absolutely no desire. If the test form didn't clearly state you were fork, you'd think you're just an ordinary person.

No matter what kind of company the Light World is, they don't want to hire you. Although your resume is impressive except for being a fork, they don't want to take the risk of having a fork. Your family isn't wealthy, and your parents can only sigh when they see you. There's no other way, you came to the Dark World—maybe, maybe things will be different here! After all, the Dark World doesn't even bother to check resumes carefully...... And once they learned you were a Citizen of Light, the Dark Folk here just passed by and put you up there, without even checking your identity.

Until the end, you inexplicably stand at the door of the boss's office they mentioned, swallow hard, and wow, you never expected things to go so smoothly. Of course, maybe it was because they hadn't looked carefully...... They hurriedly let you in. You had just noticed that this place was either an ordinary thing or a cake. Although you didn't have much desire to eat, you instinctively had an instinct to recognize food.

You took a deep breath, held the resume in your arms, walked up and knocked on the door, and then you heard a cheerful call inviting you in.

You push open the door and walk in, and almost instantly, you catch a rich fragrance enveloping you...... With its sweet aroma, it rarely whets your appetite. Almost instantly, you realize that the TV person in front of you is a cake. No wonder the staff here are cake and ordinary people, and since you're a fork, of course you won't be hired. Instinctively, you step back, wanting to leave the office, awkwardly saying, "Um, that, sorry......"

And Tenna saw you too; he noticed you, which was only natural. A Light Being—no matter where in the underworld, he always draws attention. He looked at you, resting his chin, "Oh my god, you're a fork...... Because in that moment, he actually felt a slight urge to be devoured by you.

"Wait, you're here to apply. Come in quickly. How can a Light Citizen just leave like that? Oh my god, you're the most welcoming person in our Dark World...... Come over quickly." "You heard the TV guy's voice and hesitated a little...... Rejecting physical desires and accepting a good job is now swirling in your mind, and Tenna looks at you, watching your expression, knowing you are hesitating...... Even though it seems you still have the urge to retreat and leave. But he can't just leave a Light Citizen directly, and besides, you've piqued his interest—oh my, oh my god, if only he could use him to keep you, wouldn't that be great? Tenna hesitated for a moment, then rummaged through the drawer and pulled out a small knife, gently cutting her finger.

He felt his mechanical heart pounding faster at this moment—oh my god, he simply had no idea, couldn't understand why he would do something so bold...... But it would succeed, it would definitely succeed. He put the knife back into the drawer and looked at the blue-red blood dripping down his finger...... It could probably be considered blood, since it was the substance in the body of the Dark People, but that thick, somewhat cold liquid didn't really look like blood.

It's just that you smelled this increasingly rich and sweet aroma… That is the kind of cloyingly sweet smell unique to desserts, even a bit overwhelming. Before differentiation, you could still taste the flavor; after differentiating into fork, you would never be able to taste food again. It's the body's instinct. Even though you don't want this, your body still drives you, temptations lure you, and little by little you move closer to the source of the fragrance… You feel frustrated, your rational alarm keeps sounding, urging you to leave immediately, but in the end desire wins out. You walk to tenna's desk, struggle, but ultimately put your resume on his desk and slightly bow: "Hello, I am here to apply for a job. Here is my resume." As you say this, reason gradually gains the upper hand again, and you feel annoyed that you were actually tempted by this scent… And tenna can't help but let out a sigh of relief. Luckily, he bet correctly.

You nervously clasped your hands behind your back, fiddling with your fingers. Naturally, you didn't see Tenna's excited smile; you only heard his pretentiously deep voice: "You're fork...... Logically, you'd be in danger, but you're a citizen of light, and your resume looks great, so I've decided to make an exception and hire you as my secretary! "You heard his eyes full of excitement, but of course, he was lying. He couldn't understand what kind of resume you had interned at which company you had on your resume...... He just knew you were a citizen of light, and he was attracted to you. He just knew he couldn't wait to keep you, to get close to you, no matter what...... Touching you, being devoured by you, or just touching them brings an unconscious joy to his heart.

"But—" He dragged out his words, and you still couldn't get back from that sentence. Seriously...... So, what is it? A place full of cake, even the boss, shouldn't keep your own place, right? You instinctively followed up on his words and continued, "But?" ”

"But since you're a fork...... To prevent you from attacking other cakes, we can only arrange a special workstation for you! In other words, right here in my office, what do you think? Dear Y/N, our soon-to-be-appointed superstar—" He said, hands clasped together, dragging his head, looking quite happy. You opened your mouth but closed it again. What do you want to say...... That would put him in danger, but he clearly didn't intend to let you continue; he just waved his hand lightly.

"Alright, the interview is over, so Y/N, you can get ready to start work tomorrow—of course, our company provides free accommodation, so Y/N, you just need to move here, it'll be more convenient!" His warm voice sounded in your ear.

"I am MR.(ANT)TENNA! Of course, you can just call me tenna… I look forward to seeing you in the office tomorrow!" For some reason, you feel he seems a bit too enthusiastic toward you… You dazedly reply that you received it, then take your resume and leave, returning to the world of light. Well… you didn't expect that you successfully found a job. It seems you can just go to work normally tomorrow? Although having your workstation in his office and everything seems strange… hmm, you think, since it’s here, you might as well be at ease. After all… he himself is a cake, he should probably know what to watch out for, right?

On the other side, Tenna was very happy because you agreed to his request, and even when the employees came in, he was beaming with joy. Zapper looked at the boss's nose bristling with a smile, hesitated for a moment, and finally nervously reported: "Um...... The Lightfolk we let in earlier seem to be fork, very dangerous......

But just as she said this, Tenna waved her hand and interrupted: "She passed the interview! She'll be working here tomorrow." ”

"But to you, boss, and the other employees, she's a very dangerous presence...... But before he could finish, Tenna interrupted him, and this time Tenna impatiently waved her hand.

"If I say I'm hired, then I'm hired. If this keeps up, I'll deduct your points—yes, yes, I know she'll be in danger, so I specially arranged her workstation in my office so she won't threaten you, right? I've made a huge sacrifice for you employees—so now, get back and work hard!" As he spoke, the antenna bent, then verted up, and his screen went black. His tone was lowered, as if threatening as he slammed the desk.

Zapper hesitated—was the boss planning to let that Light Citizen eat him? Forget it, this was probably not his business, so after bidding farewell to Tenna, he left the office. As for you, after telling your parents the news, you packed your things and carried a big suitcase back into the TV world...... Oh my god, this suitcase is just too heavy; you can't even carry it. After all, you're just a skinny girl, someone who would just look like a weak cake.

Just then, you hear Tenna's voice, which sounded a bit mysterious: "Oh, look at our new superstar. She seems to be in trouble—of course, the helpful Mr. Tenna will certainly assist Y/N!" "The voice rang out, and you noticed Tenna had come over from somewhere. As he spoke, he helped you carry the heavy suitcase. For someone as big as him, the suitcase that used to be quite tall for you now seemed small.

You feel a bit lost; you don't know why Tenna suddenly appeared here...... You're already prepared to carry the box by yourself.
