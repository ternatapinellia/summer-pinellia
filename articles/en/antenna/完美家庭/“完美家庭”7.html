——

You let out a gentle breath, put on a smile again, and finally let go of him. Because this time you were kneeling on his lap, you could maintain a position of looking up at him.

You softly said, "Then let's settle it this way. Trust me, maybe this could actually be a miracle." Maybe this is already a miracle now, isn't it? This is our good fortune. ”

"Maybe...... But how can you guarantee that one day I won't lose you because of this? "You waited a while before hearing Tenna's muffled voice. Clearly, he had been struggling with this issue all along, but now much better than before, no longer so anxious.

You pretend to be deep and think carefully about this question, then say, "Well, actually I feel that words can no longer persuade you, and I can't prove anything with weak words, so let's see what I do!" I will still prove it to you! ”

You said this with conviction, yet you just stared at you like Tenna's pitch-black screen, never stopping. Maybe he was staring at you, maybe he was judging your words, but you didn't really care; you just wanted to say what you wanted to say.

Actually, Tenna had been thinking about what he should do since things were in this situation—should he let you stay outside and keep facing potential dangers, or let you stay in a place absolutely safe where he can see you at all times? He had to admit, the latter thought really tempted him, because now he wouldn't have to worry about not being able to follow you all the time.

After all, Tenna still needs to watch over you every moment to feel at ease.

So he hesitated for a moment, looked at your serious and serious expression, and still asked, "Then, dear, what if I still think you should stay somewhere absolutely safe?" ”

You raised your eyebrows, a suspicion forming in your mind, but you couldn't help but ask, "What place are you talking about?" ”

Tenna's screen was still pitch black. You couldn't see his expression clearly, nor did you know what he was thinking. In fact, you could say he was openly staring at you right now. You stared at his screen, and he was staring at you; neither of you knew what the other was thinking—no, maybe you already knew what the other was thinking and doing.

So you look at Tenna, who remains silent but still determined, and scratches your head in frustration: "Ah...... I know, you're just talking about the capsule shell, but I don't think locking me up would make it a completely safe place. But if that's what you think, I'd agree to give it a try. ”

"Well, but this time I did it voluntarily, so I can't possibly do anything suicidal...... Don't worry! "You smiled as you spoke, pressing your forehead against Tenna's screen.

His screen lit up again, and you could see a small flower even sprouting from his nose. Clearly, he was extremely excited right now.

"Oh, darling, you're serious, aren't you? Then let's go over now!" Tenna was truly excited. When he made this suggestion, he already felt it was absurd and had a premonition. You might even firmly reject him, since you really dislike that kind of place. But in the end, you still made your choice and willingly followed his ideas and suggestions.

To be honest, he even felt a bit flattered......

You stared in shock, eyes wide open. You hadn't expected Tenna to be so excited—he was even standing up right in his arms holding you! And he was taking big steps—he figured he'd be in a moment to reach the pile of gacha balls. You felt a bit of a headache and tapped Tenna's TV screen.

"So you're going to just use it as my commuter......?" You asked, a bit uncertain.

"Mm, of course, it's not impossible! That's a good suggestion, sweetheart. After all, if I let you down, you might face danger, so maybe not letting you leave my side is a better choice...... "Unexpectedly, Tenna didn't even need to think and gave this answer, and he was even quite pleased about it. Maybe your greater dependence might actually be a good remedy for him."

"Alright, so are you going to keep holding a capsule toy shell and go around with you?" You imagined that scene and couldn't help but burst out laughing, covering your mouth and laughing, gasping for breath. It was rare for you to be laughing so happily.

Tenna was stunned, her steps pausing for two seconds, muttering to herself, "I never thought about this at all......"

"Then you can think about it now...... In short, I think this is absolutely inappropriate! Maybe you could think of another way? "You suggested.

"Uh...... So maybe, when I can see you, I'll hold you together, and when I can't see you, I'll stay inside that capsule shell? His voice rose up, clearly not sure about the answer himself, or maybe he was just asking for your opinion. In fact, he was still a bit dazed. He never imagined you might agree to his request, but you did agree—and without hesitation.

"Hmm, so you're really busy right now, so busy you can't even see me before you plan to put me inside the capsule toy shell?" You asked with a smile. In fact, you know Tenna probably has nothing going on right now. If he hadn't gone out this morning, he'd have stayed by your side all day. That's almost an accepted fact for you, even though we've never spoken about it.

"No, uh, I'm not busy at all! I can be by your side all the time, but I always have busy moments, maybe tomorrow......" His voice stammered again, and his steps slowed, clearly hesitating about whether he should go.

"Hmm...... Alright, whatever you think is up to you. But since you don't have anything to do, could you put me down for now? Actually, as long as you can see me all the time, then it's okay if I don't stay inside that capsule shell, right? "As you spoke, you poked Tenna's shoulder, then tapped his screen, and asked with a hint of curiosity and confusion.

"Uh...... Alright, my dear sweetheart, but you have to make sure I can see you at all times. "Tenna was eventually convinced by you. Actually, after thinking it over, he realized that doing that wasn't realistic. After all, as long as he wasn't seeing you all the time, he felt uneasy, or even if we were apart, he would get irritated. Before, when he stood on stage, he would also be separated from you and feel irritated, but it was just because the lively atmosphere and music on stage kept covering up those restless thoughts.

But maybe it's just that you're just confused by you. Even though you didn't say anything, he would still respect your opinion. So Tenna easily listened to your opinion.

So he squatted down and laid you on the ground. Before he could stand up, you actually leaned in, first wrapped your arms around his neck, then gave him a hug. Although maintaining that hug posture would be a bit odd.

"Alright...... Then I'm going to give you a big hug. Well, whenever you need it, I'll always give you it. If possible, I want to give you a basket of hugs! ”

Tenna hesitated for a moment, then hugged you back, then lifted her head and kissed your forehead: "Then...... Then I'll give my sweetheart a kiss in return. As long as you need it, it will always be yours, and I'll give you a basket of kisses. ”

Although he didn't really know what that sentence meant—maybe it was just a scene from a movie you saw on TV before. But that restless, beating heart did feel much better.

At this moment, Tenna just wanted to maintain this quiet posture with you, yet you could hear two hearts beating. But obviously, this couldn't last long, because lunchtime was almost here!

For the next period, just as you agreed, Tenna didn't mention the capsule shell anymore. But he also felt uneasy when you disappeared from his sight. So now, your anxiety and restlessness have become his regular audience. Now, as long as he stands on stage, you have no choice but to sit in the audience. But his show is indeed interesting, at least for you. Maybe it's just your personal opinion.

After all, if you feel good watching him, watching everything he does feels good. So every time the show ends, you give him a warm round of applause. As long as Tenna sees you in the audience, eyes still sparkling as he watches him, following every move he makes, he feels very happy, even his heart swelling and feeling happy because of it. Although he was anxious about whether you might not like these things, seeing you look at him with such interest every time made Tenna stop thinking about those questions. He just thinks about doing something else to make you happier.

You don't really know what Tenna is thinking; all you know is that every day after coming back, his mood seems better than before, so you feel happy for him.

Until one day, you see three ...... No, one of them is the People of Darkness, so only two of the People of Light stand on stage, while you stay below watching Tenna host the show, watching from the beginning to the end until those three disappear into the capsule toy shells you know.
