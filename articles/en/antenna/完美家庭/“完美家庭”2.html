"Perfect Family" 2

Immersed in reading, the morning quickly slipped by. After lunch, I took a short nap, nestled in the arms of the giant TV person and rested on the bed for a while. Drowsy and drowsy, time passed unnoticed, and soon it was afternoon.

You rubbed your eyes and saw Tenna still had a pitch-black screen. You couldn't tell if she was staring at you or something else, but you gently tapped the screen with your knuckles: "Oh...... Get up! We promised to go out for a walk this afternoon! After saying that, you actually sat up energetically, threw on your coat, and got ready to go out for a walk.

Tenna seemed to mutter a few words, but you didn't really hear what he was saying. You just tilted your head in confusion and looked at him: "Huh, what are you talking about?" ”

You really didn't hear clearly, and of course, you were genuinely curious about it.

But Tenna quickly shook her head: "Oh no, no, nothing—darling, just pretend I didn't say anything, it's nothing important." He clasped his hands behind his back, then gave you a smile. For some reason, you always felt like he seemed a bit nervous, but when you turned your head to look at him, you couldn't see anything. In any case, you just shrugged, then took his hand and gave a sincere smile.

Of course, his palm is huge, so you can't hold it. You just slip your palm into his palm, and then he naturally grips it tightly. It's warm and cozy, and not too light, not too heavy. In short, you've used this handshake posture countless times and are used to it.

Turn off the lights in the room and step outside. Outside is a bright lounge, where some employees are sitting on the sofa playing games, including the weather couple leaning together. When they see you, they smile and nod. You nod happily too, then pull Tenna out. He slows down a little with your pace, otherwise you won't be able to keep up with him.

However...... Out of curiosity, you still asked, "Tenna, are you not working today?" I remember you usually don't see you in the mornings and afternoons. "Although it's not very useful, probably, at these times you'll see Tenna active on stage, the spotlight shining on him, as if he never gets tired. Only the events of lunch and dinner mysteriously appear at your table. Sometimes it's steaming hot seafood baked rice, sometimes fennel paired with grilled lamb chops and a serving of pasta, and sometimes a chocolate brownie or caramel pudding on the side......

Well, you're actually curious about what time he actually cooks...... But that's his secret, and you won't ask too much! Some things just stay under the beautiful lie of the past, after all, things always seem this perfect on the surface.

Tenna seemed to stiffen for a moment, then you heard him give a couple of awkward laughs, but it was very fast, as if he didn't want you to notice: "Oh...... Oh! Honey, why are you asking that? Of course, I'm about to give my employees a break recently, so I took some time off myself. Plus, I get to have some free time to be with you. Don't you like this kind of thing?" As he spoke, he lowered his head, the pitch-black TV screen facing you, looking a bit aggrieved. The antenna drooped, and you felt as if he was staring straight at you.

—Of course, he knows he's just lying, and of course, he also knows that no matter what he says, you'll believe his words.

So you couldn't help but stand on tiptoe and pat his machine's shell: "Ah, of course I wouldn't like it! Actually, I really like these days...... As long as Tenna is always by my side, I feel great. I really like it, really enjoy being with you." Then you gently hugged him, lightly kissed the food on the dark TV screen, let go of him, and then slipped your hand into his palm: "Alright, let's not talk about that! Let's take a walk first. Standing still isn't really a walk......"

As you spoke, you quickened your pace and walked out, taking longer steps. Tenna followed right behind you, probably with a long gaze fixed on you. Although you were a bit puzzled why he wasn't sitting side by side with you, you know, he used to squeeze into a row with you, and sometimes even shrank down because holding his hand would be inconvenient.

Although you were a bit uneasy inside, it worked. In any case, you didn't say much. As usual, you walked around the corridor twice. Up ahead, it seemed like a concert was underway. Although you couldn't hear the singing, you did hear instrumental music—probably saxophone? You didn't know much about music or similar things, just curiously glanced over there and prepared to walk back.

"Let's head back now. Does it feel like it's about time?" You suggested, glancing back at him while you were at it.

Tenna nodded at you. Of course, the screen was no longer pitch black; she even showed the signature smile of a host. Maybe thinking about watching TV soon made him excited. In any case, you couldn't help but chuckle, probably unable to help yourself.

You shake your gripping hand: "Alright, we still have a lot to do anyway. It's okay to walk back slowly, right?" Oh...... Let's hurry up, I want to sit down and rest. "You mumble, grabbed his hand and walked back, then threw yourself hard onto the sofa, blinking as Tenna sat in front. In short—probably to protect your eyes, he was even known for his serious reputation, insisting on keeping some distance before watching TV. Although you wanted to say your eyesight wasn't a problem, he insisted on doing it. Well, you have to accept that.

The TV screen starts to change, you hug your knees, watching a movie play — "The Bridges of Madison Dream"? It's a romance film that's been around for years, you feel that way, but I have to say it's still quite good, though it struggles between family and true self love...... Forget it, anyway, this kind of feeling is beautiful, isn't it?

After watching this film, it's almost time for dinner. You all enjoy a red wine stew with beef together, then share a fruit tart on display. After dinner, you relax a bit, and it's time to sleep.

You lie on the bed, and the slightly smaller Tenna hugs you. You say goodnight to each other, turn off the desk lamp, and the room falls into darkness, and you both fall asleep.

—That's how it should be. When you wake up the next day, it's supposed to be the same routine. But after finishing breakfast and admiring the potted plants as usual, sitting on the sofa, you instinctively pull out the book you've been reading recently to continue, but a piece of paper falls out from the open page.

You picked up the paper in confusion and looked at what was written on it.

[1. Do not leave your room after 10 p.m

You cannot leave your room before 7 a.m

Medication must be taken at least half an hour before going to sleep

You must drink water first, then take the medication with the water

You must maintain a seated posture to drink liquids and take small sips

Posture must be maintained properly

Avoid the following ingredients: pepper, Sichuan peppercorns, mustard......

Do not touch objects with sharp properties

You can only leave the bedroom to eat at nine, twelve, or eighteen

Without anyone following, dangerous movements cannot be performed

No one follows strenuous exercise that cannot be done: sprinting, swimming, ......

A five-minute stand-up relaxation activity must be used at every hour of reading

Exercise should not be less than half an hour each day

You can't watch electronic devices for an hour before bed (not even TV!) (By the way, after this sentence, there's a little TV emoji showing tears)]

…… You pause for a moment, flipping through the paper to make sure there's nothing written on the back. Uh, obviously this looks like some kind of rule-based horror tale? But some of the words and sentences are a bit rambling, almost like some kind of reminder, making you want to laugh. And for no reason, the handwriting is a bit like Tenna.

Anyway, pick up this piece of paper and stand up, ready to ask Tenna.

Although he had written the paper based on his mental impression, he hesitated and hadn't decided where to put it, nor did he know if he needed to add more. After all, no matter what, the rules written on this note couldn't help you avoid all dangers, nor could they be all the instructions he could remember. He wished you wouldn't encounter any danger at all. If you got even a little hurt, Tenna would get nervous, even though those wounds might heal in the next second.

In any case, this tension was unlikely to dissipate, and Tenna decided not to take out the note for now—maybe he needed to add something to it. He just carefully hid the note, as long as it wasn't seen by you.

But...... What other way could he avoid danger from you? After all, Tenna wanted you to avoid these possible injuries without knowing anything, or maybe his protective instincts were just too strong. In any case, he did seriously think about what to do with the props he had placed in the background.

…… Oh, he just thought of it! Tenna picked up an empty capsule ball and happily raised it, even thinking he was a genius! To think of such a great method!

Isn't it enough that you can never get hurt? Isn't it enough to never let you leave his sight and to ensure you're always in a safe place? As long as you stay locked here, you'll never leave, and of course, you'll never encounter danger.

So he enthusiastically took advantage of your sleep to carefully put you into the capsule ball. He even lined it with soft plush so you wouldn't hurt yourself when you woke up or struggled. Then he went to prepare breakfast, humming off-key songs, imagining what would happen next. In short, he thought everything he had done was the best choice.

…… However, when he came back carrying the tray, he saw you sitting there holding the blanket, looking at him with a hint of disappointment and a cold gaze. Although he had long prepared himself for this, even though he had warned himself about possible reactions, seeing your look still felt like falling into an ice cave. It felt like a hole had broken through his heart, with only the cold wind rushing in, and his body stiffened in place. After a while, he forced a smile no different from usual: "Oh, darling, you're awake." ”

You nodded, "Yes, I'm awake." After that, you didn't say anything more. It seemed you had no intention of saying anything more.

…… He silently put down the plate, even set a table in front of the capsule toy, and then placed the steaming hot breakfast on it.

"Darling, I think you should understand what I'm doing...... I'm just, just afraid you'll walk into death somewhere I can't see, or encounter some other danger. I'm just afraid you'll be outside my protection. I don't want to lose you......" Tenna explained nervously. He knew this excuse sounded a bit forceful, but in such a short time, he couldn't come up with any good words.

You just nodded absentmindedly, probably not paying attention to what Tenna said, so Tenna quieted down, but the antenna hung down, and the dark screen pointed in your direction.

You picked up your knife and fork and ate a simple breakfast—still fried eggs with bread, with freshly fried bacon on top. Of course, there was a glass of milk nearby, but you didn't touch a bite, instead gnawing on the bread and fried egg as if venting frustration.

Tenna watched all this in a panic, of course he was uneasy, having no idea what the outcome would be.

Then, you collapsed headfirst onto the table. The scene shifted, and this time the time rewind was still very long, roughly ...... It was when he was about to pull you out and put you into the capsule capsule shell.

He stood by the bed, and even in the darkness, he could see your face clearly, your slightly fluttering eyelashes, because he had long since adapted to the darkness.

After hesitating for a moment, Tenna decided to try again, so he carefully picked you up, led you back to the capsule toy shell he had prepared, and then placed you inside.

This time he changed his story. When you woke up inside the capsule toy, Tenna squatted in front of the capsule and looked at you, saying seriously and persistently, "Darling, I think maybe if you step out of my sight, you'll somehow run into danger, so maybe this place is more suitable for you?" I promise, I'll do anything for you, and I'll make your life here as comfortable as possible. ”

Naturally, he still failed, the same ending, the same look. But this time, you gulped down two mouthfuls of milk before preparing breakfast. Then, for some reason, you suddenly started coughing, leaning on the table and coughing repeatedly. Tenna patted your back, trying to make you feel better, but it was useless. The scene blurred again, then reversed, and he stood by your bed once more.

Honestly, he knew that if he tried again, the result would be the same...... Tenna was troubled by this, but honestly, he no longer wanted to see those cold, disappointed eyes. Two times was enough; he no longer could handle a blow.

He still hopes you'll always smile, looking at him with warm eyes, and saying childish words like hugs and kisses. Tenna still feels that these ordinary yet somewhat childish moments of life are actually your whole life.

After some hesitation, he gently slipped into a corner of the blanket, hugged you, and smelled your faint scent—maybe the scent of shampoo. Tenna wasn't quite sure what those you occasionally used were, but in any case, he drifted off to sleep. But this time, he was at ease, in a sweet sleep, without those cold gazes or terrifying, possibly revived memories.

Naturally, you don't remember everything inside the capsule toy shell, and you still get up, eat, and laugh with Tenna as usual, deciding the day's schedule. That's the best thing you can do—you remember nothing, and Tenna hopes you remember nothing. But he also feels a subtle sense of disappointment, because you remember nothing, which means you have no idea what might happen, and he tries hard to hide it all—anyway, well, Tenna still hopes you know nothing, and he'll find a way to solve it properly.

All you need to do is keep that smile forever, gaze at him, and when he looks over, hold your face and look back happily.

So he hesitated for a moment, but still slipped that note into the book you've been reading recently. Of course, last night he had added a few more on the spot.

Then, with a hint of nervousness and unease, he started scrubbing the dishes. Fortunately, the sound of running water drowned out the noise outside, easing some of his inner anxiety. But waiting was the hardest time to endure, because Tenna knew you would definitely come over with that piece of paper to ask him. He didn't even hide much—he had written all those words himself, and you had definitely seen his handwriting.

But Tenna would never admit that she wrote it herself.

Oh my, he'd rather you think those are rules you really have to follow and enforce than think it's just a joke. Tenna was already trying her best to figure out how to avoid the dangers you encountered.

Then, he turned off the faucet, because he heard footsteps behind him. He knew you were coming, so he took off his gloves, took a deep breath, and turned to look at you.

You raise the paper and ask, "Tenna, this handwriting looks just like yours. You must have written it, right?" ”
