"Perfect Family" 1

Tennax, you, OOC is mine; the tone of this post will be a bit dreamy and unrealistic

There are private settings because I don't want to explain (。 ), and the idea of time rewinding after death is a meme I came across while reading novels before

…… By the way, the inspiration comes from friends who worry every day that I might die one way or another.

——

The room was still somewhat dim. You groggily opened your eyes, and the first thing rushing into your nose was a fragrance with a hint of sweetness. You thought it might be jam and bread, maybe other desserts, some oil aroma, or maybe it was freshly fried eggs. After all, you could even hear the faint sound of oil splashing and dancing.

Next came the crisp sound of a porcelain plate clashing against a glass tabletop. But the sound was very soft, clearly meant not to disturb you, who was dozing off in bed. Still, this meaningless yet subtle noise actually made you feel more at ease, or rather, you could immerse yourself in these sounds and fall back asleep. The fresh aroma enveloped you, and the occasional crisp sounds sounded more like a lullaby. You groggily closed your eyes, turned over, and decided to continue sleeping.

But while you lay in bed with your eyes closed, you felt a warm hand covering your eyes. Earlier, you were startled by the faint light shining through the curtains, but now it was pitch dark.

Then you hear Tenna's voice—gentle, and of course, with a touch of everyday uplift and joy: "Darling, it's time, time to get up...... I'll open the curtains. ”

You muttered softly from your throat, "But I still want to lie down a little longer......"

"You've been lying down long enough. If you don't eat soon, lunch will be postponed again." Tenna's voice sounded a bit troubled. In fact, you don't even know how he actually judges time; it's just that at some point, a set of rules was established here. At least when you follow these rules, you don't feel there's any problem.

"Hmm...... Then get up! "You tried to raise your voice to sober up, opened your eyes, and your eyelashes were still brushing in Tenna's palm, but what you saw was still a dull white, after all, your eyes were still covered."

It wasn't until you heard the curtains rustle and a faint light peeking through your fingers that you were truly sure it was time to get up.

…… After all, it seems that the brightness outside also symbolizes different times.

His hand was still on your eye, and after a while he carefully let go. You blinked a few times to get used to the light before putting on your clothes and stepping outside, heading to the dining table to eat.

The temperature of the food on the table was just right—clearly having calculated your wake-up time precisely. You picked up two slices of slightly crispy bread, spread blueberry jam, and slowly began to savor it. You squinted, savoring this simple yet warm breakfast, then saw Tenna sitting across from you, resting his chin on his hand, seemingly looking at you, though you couldn't catch his gaze.

His apron hadn't been taken off yet. Imagine whether you could still smell a bit of oil smoke on it, or maybe the sweet aroma of dessert. You picked up a scone with your fork, but instead of rushing to eat it, you hesitated for a moment before handing it to Tenna.

"Thanks for your hard work. You should have a bite too. You always have to watch me eat, and I feel a bit embarrassed." You stuck out your tongue a little embarrassedly.

Tenna's tone sounded as if she were flattered: "Oh? Me? Actually, it's okay if you don't have to consider me. This was originally prepared for you—" He even waved his hand to refuse, basically explaining everything.

You tilted your head slightly and looked at him: "I can't eat it anyway...... Besides, breakfast and such—shouldn't we all enjoy it together? ”

"Alright, alright, I'll have a bite." He sighed, followed your fork down, and bit into the scone. Still, that small piece was somewhat trivial to him, but he still chewed hard twice before swallowing.

"But it really does taste great." He coughed twice as he spoke, probably not wanting to praise himself. Sorry, maybe it was possible.

"Of course, I really trust Tenna's cooking. It's definitely delicious." You raised your eyebrows proudly.

Then the dining table fell silent again, with only the sound of knives and forks clashing on plates. Chewing on roasted bacon, you glanced at the black tea placed on the table, your eyes darting around. Then you picked up the teapot and poured a cup for Tenna, then a cup of black tea for yourself, along with two sugar cubes.

You stand up and push the cup of tea toward Tenna.

"Alright, stop staring at me while I eat! Have some tea!" You pursed your lips, a bit slurred, since you were still chewing on bacon.

Tenna picked up the cup of black tea and took a sip. He seemed a bit hesitant, but still chose to speak: "Your tea brewing skills are quite good." Of course, this sentence sounded sincere, though it sounded a bit forced.

"I didn't brew the tea, I just poured it. So judging by this, Tenna's cooking is the best!" As you spoke, you took a small sip of black tea. Honestly, this kind of daily routine played out in this room every day.

After dinner, you listen to the sparse sound of water and the clinking of bowls and plates coming from the kitchen. You know Tenna is probably cleaning, while you sit on the sofa, leaning your back against it, deciding to enjoy your own time—you casually pull a book from the nearby shelf, not even paying attention to its name, and just open it to look at it, since there are bookmarks inside.

But just then, Tenna suddenly poked her head out from the kitchen, mixed with the sound of running water. The emotion in his words was hard to discern, but you could still hear the sentence clearly: "Honey, don't sit down and read right after dinner!" You can start with something else! ”

You sigh, "Alright, you've actually heard this many times before. You can't sneak a book for a while."

Alright, alright, you know he's thinking of you. Reading directly would definitely be bad for your health—so you closed the book and placed it on the coffee table in front of you, tapped your waist, stood up, and chose to stroll around the room first...... Well, although there's nothing much to look at, you still find it especially interesting to look at the potted plants on the floor every day. Even though the changes aren't significant, you still look at them seriously, and at least you can spot some clues—this leaf has lifted by one millimeter compared to yesterday's waist, and new leaves are about to sprout on the branch over there.

You squatted down with interest to watch for a while, and only when you got tired did you knock your legs and stand up, choosing to take a casual walk in the room. At this moment, the sound of running water in the kitchen stopped. Tenna came out carrying a rag and wearing an apron. Although the dining table wasn't dirty, he insisted on wiping it down.

Seeing you, he instinctively asked, "Are you going out for a walk later?" Anywhere is fine. ”

You hesitated for a second, then shook your head: "No way, I still want to read a little first......

"Oh...... Then in the afternoon? Maybe you could watch some TV shows too? Some of the book's plots have already been made into movies. Can I help you search for them? ”

In this regard, you still insist: "No, some things can't be captured in movies!" Reading paper books is better, but if we go for a walk together in the afternoon, that's fine, right? Then come back and watch some TV shows, just treat it as a way to relax. ”

You think for a moment, then naturally plan the time ahead.

Tenna's voice was filled with joy: "I knew it...... You really won't give up watching my show! Don't worry, today will definitely be your favorite TV show too! ”

You smiled helplessly, estimating that time was about right, you jumped back onto the sofa, letting the soft sofa collapse wrap around your body. Then you leaned back against the sofa backrest: "Ah, of course, I believe in your choice!" "After all, we've lived together for so long, how could I not know each other?"

And you really like this kind of life. Even though you say nothing, you know deep down that this is your "perfect family."

Tenna felt that this kind of life was so wonderful—so good it even felt dreamlike and not real. Sometimes he wondered if this was some kind of fairyland or fairy tale that would wake up after the bell tolled. If that were the case, he would rather remain immersed in this dream forever.

He still did his usual routine, preparing meals at fixed times for three meals a day, calling you up to eat at fixed times, resting his chin on his chin with joy and happiness as he watched you eat. Of course, occasionally he enjoyed your feeding service—though all of it was made by his own hands, for some reason, the food you personally fed him always felt a bit sweeter. Even the bacon sprinkled with salt and pepper had a hint of sweetness in that moment.

Meals at fixed times, occasional schedule changes, and day-after-day sharing a bed, quietly getting up in the morning, carefully tucking you in, looking back every few steps to watch you lying peacefully, then wearing an unconscious smile as she prepares breakfast.

Then, when you're quietly reading or doing other things, he'll keep staring at you in a similar posture—since you can't notice his gaze, that's why he's so confident and bold.

Perhaps this is his "perfect family." After all, Tenna never dared to dream of such a life before, until you entered his life and felt that his former irritability and irritability could be easily soothed. As long as you were still by his side.

As long as you are still by his side.

—So actually, he was still a bit troubled. This feeling of distress had only recently emerged. The reason he felt distressed was actually because he realized your lifestyle habits weren't very good, which made him anxious.

But if that's all there is, he just needs to give appropriate reminders—when you instinctively sit down to read or go straight to sleep after eating, or when you sit in the same spot all day without leaving anything, or when you've stared at his screen for too long on a TV show.

All this requires just some appropriate reminders. After all, although it's not very healthy, it's okay to do this once or twice occasionally, and he is happy to indulge your behavior.

But what if—and that's not all?

Tenna knew your sleep wasn't very good, so she prepared medicine and capsules for you. He knew you liked reading and put all the things you loved first, knowing you weren't very fond of exercise, so his usual workout routes were quite short.

He noticed that your movements were always a bit unsteady, like accidentally bumping into the wall next to you, getting bumped by the table in front, or simply not noticing there was another layer on the bookshelf—or maybe you just didn't remember at all, but you always bumped your head up when picking up the book.

He would subtly grab your arm, pull you away from the wall, or let you stand steadily in the chaos. He would place his hand on the shelf so your forehead would only bump into his hand instead of hitting it directly. Later, he would simply place all the books you read on the upper shelf, clearing the bottom shelf so you wouldn't bump into it again.

But beyond that, it was far more than that. Although he was so careful and cared for this "perfect family" every day to keep it going, he could only watch helplessly as you swallowed that capsule, took a sip of water, and then probably choked—in short, he kept coughing, clutching his chest, bending over, showing no sign of stopping. All he could do was anxiously pat your back, watching your persistent coughing, and only feel anxious about it.

—Then, when your vision blurs and you still don't know what happened, the scene shifts to Tenna watching you pick up the capsule, ready to swallow it.

He was startled, snatched the capsule from your hand, and forced a couple of laughs: "Darling, I just remembered the capsule's efficacy might be a bit lacking. Let's start with another one today." ”

You glanced at him in confusion, but still nodded obediently, since you thought there was nothing wrong with his explanation.

Seeing that you showed no sign of doubt, Tenna finally let out a careful breath in his heart. Although he didn't know if switching to something else would lead to the same outcome, nor was he sure what had just happened—no matter what, he had to give you another one.

So this time, he brought you a steaming, freshly brewed potion, which even smelled a bit sweet.

You carefully swallowed the medicine, and this time there were no other problems. Tenna finally breathed a sigh of relief. After finishing the medicine, she lit the bedside lamp and read for a while before falling asleep. He stared at your quiet expression, watching your eyelashes flutter unconsciously, and looking at your face, gradually falling asleep as well.

At least, today was a perfect pass, right?

But the next day, as usual, he was preparing breakfast and was about to call you, when he saw you rubbing your eyes and walking out of the room. Maybe you hadn't seen the way clearly, or maybe you had misjudged the distance. In any case, you didn't see the threshold at the bedroom door. When you walked over, you didn't think to cross over and your body tilted forward and nearly collapsed, leaving you stunned as you stared at the ground so close to you.

Tenna fumbled over to catch you, but with a second left before you hit the ground, he was still a few steps away.

—Then, as the feeling blurred again, Tenna realized she was standing at the dining table again, still holding a plate. This time, she didn't hesitate again, put down the tray, and walked to the bedroom door to catch you, who was about to leave but was about to fall.

You looked up shyly and smiled at Tenna: "Sorry...... I didn't react ......"

As you spoke, your voice quieted.

"Of course it's okay, darling. No matter what, whenever it happens, I'll catch you." Tenna looked at the hair swirling on top of your head, gently stroked your head, and said this very softly.

No matter what, he breathed a sigh of relief in his heart. It seemed that this kind of time-reversal-like feature was indeed very helpful to him—at least he could confirm your status. And if he didn't make it in time, he would have other chances.

But he also felt a lingering fear—what if one day this ability suddenly disappeared? What if one day he wouldn't have time to hold you? What if, one day, relying on this ability, he could only watch you stop breathing in front of him? These were things he dared not even imagine.

But now, he could roughly tell that this regression would only trigger when you encountered danger, or even death. Either way, he was relieved, even joyful, because this way, you wouldn't have to experience pain—this kind of anxiety and fear would be better if only he could experience it.

The reason is that he confirms you actually remember nothing.

After all, if you remembered, you wouldn't look at him with that admiration, a bit of confusion, and a wet gaze. But he really enjoyed that look in your eyes.

Everything in the "Perfect Family" can still go on as usual, except for the tenna that silently relies on time rewinds to save you a lot of pain when you are completely unaware.

At least you know nothing, yet still think everything is as perfect as before, and that's enough for Tenna.

However, he still needs to think about what to do next; relying on time rewinding all the time is unrealistic...... He can't become dependent on this, and besides, there's always a sense of fear that he might lose you because of it.

After hesitating for a moment, he took out paper and pen, wrote something on the blank sheet, and then looked at the paper and nodded in satisfaction.
