Tenna instinctively let out a couple of awkward laughs, wanting to put his hands behind his back. After all, it seemed like cold sweat was forming in his palms. And you just wait patiently, since you're not in a hurry at all. You're not very tired or want to go back to sleep; in fact, you're excited. You think, maybe it's okay to just wait here until dawn. You have plenty of patience, and of course, you still have time.

Alright, the moment he said it, he regretted it. He really shouldn't have said that. What exactly was he saying!

You couldn't help but laugh, probably amused by his frantic excuse and finally coming up with a clumsy excuse: "Hmm, I have to admit, the environment here is pretty good...... But I definitely wouldn't like it, because this place is too cramped. It's way too lonely, and there's no freedom or personal rights left. If I were to live in a place like this, I'd rather end my life—in any case, I probably won't have any desire to survive anymore." ”

As you spoke, you shrugged and patiently explained to Tenna. After all, you were worried he wouldn't understand, and if he really lost his mind and tried to do something, it would be a disaster if he had to figure something out. After all, you didn't want to see this guy end up in a state where no one believed anything others said—that would be a real problem.

Tenna opened his mouth and stammered. Now he understood why everything was rewound when he locked you in the capsule shell before—it turned out it was because you no longer had the will to survive. So, how exactly is this so-called death rewind mechanism determined? Tenna didn't understand either.

Hmm...... I actually know you're hiding something from me. I don't really care. I originally thought you would be willing to tell me one day. ”

So you have no choice but to crouch down and look at your boyfriend who has suddenly shrunk for no reason, softly saying, "Are you feeling sad?" Or you can choose not to tell me yet...... It's okay for me to guess a little longer for a while, since our relationship is still the same as before. ”

You've softened your heart. Seeing him like this, you really think it's okay not to press further. After all, you still want to see the person you love happy every day, just to be happy. As for everything else, you can't worry about it and can solve the problem slowly later.

Anyway, problems can always be solved.

Tenna's voice now was tinged with sobs: "Oh...... But since you're asking like that, I have no choice but to tell you......

You were silent for a moment, then raised your tiny tenna and looked you in the eye. You lowered your voice, softened your tone: "Mm...... Alright! Then just tell me. Anyway, I respect your decision. Do whatever you want. If there's a problem, we have to solve it together, right? ”

As you spoke, you reached out and pressed the head of the shrunken TV. Well, you could rub it around as you like, so while daydreaming, you pressed the antenna several times.

"Well then...... I think, first of all, darling, you have to make sure you believe what I say. "The crying and sobs have quieted down a lot. Tenna's emotions seem to have calmed down, but he's still a small one. Maybe he's afraid that if he grows bigger, he'll suddenly crush you."

And you still look him in the eye, repeating once more, emphasizing: "Well, I know, I said it too, no matter what you say, I will believe it, I believe everything you say." ”

He seemed to have finally stopped sobbing, and maybe he'll continue this process later—you don't know.

Tenna stared at your serious expression for a while. To be honest, he was a bit hesitant. He didn't know if laying everything out was good or bad, but now that he had chosen to tell you everything, he had no other choice.

So after hesitating for a while, he finally spoke: "...... So, darling, what if I told you that when you run into danger, my time will turn back? I can only prevent you from getting into danger. I'm really scared. What if you slip out of my sight and something unexpected happens? What if I can't always protect you......"

You raised your eyebrows. Honestly, at first, when he said this, you really didn't want to believe it, but since you had already promised to believe him, you still took it all seriously. Since that's the case, the capsule shell over there has room to explain.

So you nodded: "So that's why you prepared that capsule shell for you?" ”

Tenna's voice was a bit stammered: "Yes...... Yes. I think if you had stayed there all the time, maybe you wouldn't have encountered danger. ”

And you calmly explained, "It's that there's no more danger from outside, so at this moment, I am the greatest danger. I wouldn't want to live in a place like that. Even if it cost me death, I would leave it." ”

"Yes...... Yes, I understand that now. "Tenna's antenna drooped again, looking like he was about to sob again.

You pressed your forehead a bit troubled: "Alright, anyway, I'm still standing here perfectly fine right now. I haven't encountered any danger you mentioned, and nothing unexpected has happened to take me away from you. Even so, I will try my best to get back to you. Of course, I believe you will try your best to come to me, don't you?" Anyway, stop being so downcast. ”

He seemed to be still sobbing: "Oh...... Sweetheart......"

You patted his head again: "Anyway, don't be too nervous, don't be too anxious, don't age even faster. Of course, even if you age, I won't abandon you anymore...... But still, I hope you can stay well and always be fine." After saying that, you carefully placed him on the ground and only stood up after squatting down to make sure he was okay.

Just as you were about to walk back, Tenna suddenly grew huge and suddenly hugged you tightly.

His voice still carried a hint of tears, but now it carried a hint of joy and happiness, as if he was floating in bliss: "Oh, sweetheart! I really like you...... I love you so much...... Anyway, I really like all the things you say......" He seemed a bit incoherent.

And you just couldn't bear it anymore, nudging his tightly embraced arm: "Yeah, yeah, I know I love you too, so...... Let go first...... You're holding me too tight! I'm struggling to breathe! "You screamed out loud in the last two sentences.

Only then did Tenna react, hurriedly let go of you, then pressed your shoulder, anxiously sizing you up and down: "Oh, no, are you okay?" "She looked her up while nervously gesturing with her hand."

"Phew, of course I'm fine, and I didn't trigger any time rewind just now...... Anyway, let's go rest first and study this tomorrow, okay?" You rubbed your brows, then grabbed Tenna's hand and led him back. Of course, rather than holding hands, it was more about putting your hand in his palm, and Tenna happily held your hand as we walked back.

Now, you've returned to your room again. You lie on the bed and have been feeling sleepy for a while. Honestly, getting up in the middle of the night is a bit of a stretch. In short, you don't actually feel much sleepy right now, so you regret having a sudden outburst earlier. If it weren't for that, your drowsiness would have been even more obvious.

Tenna shrank to about the same size, then hugged you tightly. Luckily, this time you didn't feel suffocated, only the warmth of your body remained.

You sighed, then turned around to face his screen, then patted his head: "Alright, I'm right here, I won't go anywhere else. Are you really that nervous?" ”

In the darkness, you barely managed to regain your sight, then you looked at Tenna and hesitated for a moment, nodding.

"So are you going to keep watching me and make sure I'm here? Don't you even need to rest? Oh my god." You couldn't help but laugh. But you didn't know exactly where his eyes were, so now trying to close his eyelids was actually a bit difficult.

…… Well, and you don't know if he's actually asleep, or if he'll keep staring at you and keep that posture.

So you just tapped his screen and gently kissed him: "Mm...... Alright then, if you want to maintain this state, I can't stop you, but you have to understand, you also need to relax and rest, okay? ”

"Of course I know, it's fine. I'm Tenna, so even if I stay on standby all night without rest, it's fine."

You patted your head: "Alright, then I guess you used to be on standby and never rested, just staring at me the whole time." Alright, alright...... Even if I pin you down on the bed, you wouldn't get a proper rest! ”

After saying that, you take a deep breath: "Alright, anyway, I can't sleep right now, so why don't we just talk about that question from earlier?" I think...... Is it really that simple? ”

You feel Tenna's body stiffening, his hand tightly gripping your shoulder, clearly not very happy for you to discuss this issue.

"Hmm, maybe on the surface, things really do seem simple...... Alright, anyway, you have to understand, I don't die just because I encounter a little danger, otherwise I wouldn't be alive until now. Of course, I don't always run into danger, and besides, isn't it because you're here? I believe you'll definitely find a way to eliminate those dangers, and of course, I'll be careful too. I don't want you to keep worrying about me. ”

As you spoke, you let out a slight breath, but you still couldn't read Tenna's expression, because he was still a pitch-black screen. His body seemed to tremble, as if he was struggling to endure some emotion.

You hesitated for a moment, then finally spoke: "Alright, so when I wake up, do you want to give it a try?" What you mentioned about rewinding—after all, I really have no memory at all. I'm afraid I won't be able to preserve that memory myself, so I'll have to rely on you. ”

“…… No, can we ...... Don't try? Really, dear, as long as we maintain the status quo, just follow the same steps as before. If there are any other problems, let me solve them all, okay? ”

"Do you still want to maintain a superficial perfection? Actually, so-called perfection has never been something that can last forever," you chuckled, "So, if there's a problem, solve it, because we both have responsibilities to each other. I can't just stand by and watch you fall into anxiety, panic, and unease, just as you can't just watch me get hurt and face danger. Our family doesn't rely solely on you to sustain, so when necessary, rely on me too." Trust me, maybe when we face problems together, problems can be easily solved." As you spoke, you took his hand, cupped it with both arms, and then placed them on your chest.

You solemnly said, "Whether it's love, passion, or simply responsibility and habits, in any case, I want to maintain our connection and hope this family can always survive." ”

“…… So, get some rest. No matter what, when I wake up the next day, I still hope to see a tenna who is forever happy and joyful. "You said softly.

He can easily feel the sound of your heart beating in your chest. That passionate heart may now be pounding passionately for him. His tears dripped from the screen again, soaking the pillow below, and his voice was choked: "Alright...... Alright, then I'll try tomorrow. Anyway, good night......"

You looked at him with satisfaction, smiled, and gently wiped the tears from his face with your hand: "Well then, good night. I hope you don't think about anything, don't be nervous or anxious, and at least for now, rest well." ”

Then you gently hugged him back, burying your head in his shoulder. Honestly, after saying so much, you were actually a bit sleepy yourself, so after a while you fell asleep, and all he could hear was your shallow breathing.

After a moment of silence, Tenna still held you tightly, then also fell into a pitch-black sleep.

By the next morning, still at a familiar time, you groggily woke up, hearing the sounds of cooking outside and the faint sound of running water. You knew Tenna was making breakfast for you. Probably because you woke up in the middle of last night, you were still a bit sleepy. In any case, you still wanted to rest a little longer, so you mumbled as you hugged the blanket and turned over, half-closing your eyes in a daze, planning to rest a little longer.

But after lying down for a while, you hear the noise outside quiet down, and after a few minutes, you feel a hand covering your eyes.

"Time to get up, my sweetheart." You heard Tenna's soft yet slightly excited voice.

"I haven't slept enough, I want to sleep a little longer......" you muttered and complained in a low voice.

Tenna seemed a bit troubled and didn't speak for a long while. You had no idea what he was doing, but you just rubbed your head against his palm, planning to sneak a little more sleep while you could.

But your little thoughts were interrupted by Tenna: "Alright, sweetheart, there's definitely enough time. You can't sleep anymore, you'll just get sleepier the more you sleep......" Saying this, he couldn't help but reach out and pat your head. Of course, his other hand still covered your eyes, but he mercilessly opened the curtains.

Now you can only enjoy the dazzling lights. Reluctantly, you get up, rub your eyes, and grumble as you change your clothes before leaving the bedroom, sitting at the dining table watching the steaming hot breakfast.

Today...... Well, besides the usual bread, jam, and bacon, there's also a small plate of koluli, probably baked in the morning—at least that's what you can guess.

You should just obediently finish the bread and sausage bacon before picking up a fork and taking a piece of it. Keluli popped it into her mouth, chewing while showing a delighted look and squinting her eyes slightly: "Hmm, delicious...... I didn't expect you to have time to prepare this. I thought you might actually be in the mood......

You see Tenna's fingers moving a few times on the table, probably nervous and a little spasm: "Of course, this was specially prepared for Sweetheart, though I really don't feel like it...... But after hearing what Sweetheart said, I couldn't help but feel overjoyed, so I thought, I must prepare this for you, just as a gift. ”

You thought for a moment, then put down your fork: "Actually, it's okay if you don't prepare anything. That's exactly what I wanted to tell you, after all, I've always loved you. So no matter what, it doesn't matter. No matter what you do, I still love you...... Well, if it's too much, then it's not counted. ”

You couldn't help but laugh as you spoke.
