"Perfect Family" 8 (end)

Tennax, you, OOC is mine

This chapter has ended, and it's quite short, so in any case, the writing style might have changed a bit (maybe?) ) Also, the logic is probably a bit weak

Also, this YN really will sacrifice himself ()

——

Somehow, you always have a bad feeling. Of course, even in this situation, Tenna hasn't forgotten to call your name, so you still follow him in the end. You just keep quiet, wanting to know what exactly that ominous feeling in your heart is all about.

You followed Tenna past the front stage, seeing the stage fall into darkness and silence after it closed—you were very familiar with this silence, because it had been like this for a long time. Tenna kept glancing back at you from time to time to check if you were following, to confirm he could see you all along.

Although you're curious how he'd react if he suddenly disappeared at this moment and couldn't see him, you didn't intend to do so.

You don't want to proactively cause him to feel anxious, as that would only lead to guilt and guilt, so just maintain the status quo.

Until you pass through door after door and enter the snowy field where the cold wind howls and snowflakes drift. You wrap yourself tightly and stand beside the tree, looking up at the sky—it's just pitch black. Then you look toward that similarly dark, unknown doorway. Out of the corner of your eye, you notice Tenna is also uneasy—or rather, he actually wants to escape from here. He may not want to face what comes next, but he knows he has to do it, that he has no choice.

So you quietly took two steps toward Tenna's direction, then slipped your hand into his tightly held palm as comfort. You saw that although he was bowing his head, he still held your hand tightly, wrapping it tightly in his palm.

…… Then, you saw the figures of those three people appear at the doorway, so you quietly pulled your hand out and just hid behind the tree. Because you felt that maybe you shouldn't be standing here. To be honest, you don't have any other abilities, so it's better not to get in the way here.

But maybe it's only natural...... Tenna still failed, maybe he had his own sense of self-awareness—this was a doomed failure, so before it all began, he wanted to escape. And you saw it all in full, and belatedly realized that maybe leaving with him back then would have been a better choice? No, maybe he didn't want to leave either.

Because you see him so happy now to hand out the ultimate prize that was supposed to be on the treetop—then you widen your eyes, unable to believe what you've just seen. You see a pitch-black shadow appear behind Tenna at some point, raising its blade and pointing it at him.

You have to do something. Whatever it is, but maybe everything will be too late in a flash. Suddenly, something comes to mind. You pull out the knife you've hidden on yourself when you've been feeling uneasy, and don't dare hesitate for a moment, slashing straight toward your neck. As you raise the blade, you run toward the distance, still too close to it.

Because only death brings rebirth, and at this moment, you overcome the fear and pain brought by death, so you know none of this will happen again, only rebirth. You even do all this with a smile and relief. You remember the pain of dying and the feeling of near suffocation, then your vision goes black and blank. When you come to your senses, you are standing not far away, still holding the knife to your neck, blood dripping from the scraped skin, wetting the blade.

You look up in a daze, still unsure if you've truly succeeded. If not, you'll have to try again—then you see Tenna's screen go black again, with tears falling continuously. He kneels in the snow, hugs you tightly, and knocks the knife from your hand.

Tenna, in fact, from that moment of blurred before his eyes and the moment he felt the familiar distortion of time and space, suddenly understood that you had encountered danger again—no, more like choosing death, because in that instant, in the pitch-black reverse, he truly saw your fate, smiling as blood flowed, soaking every inch beneath you, staining the pure white snowflakes red, while you lay there peacefully, your body gradually turning cold. Then, in the very next second after rewinding in time, he noticed the knife you were holding against your neck. He couldn't care about anything else; his only thought now was to run over, hold you tight, and stop your movements.

He was even a bit annoyed that he had prepared this dagger for you earlier, since he was afraid you might be in danger and gave it to you. But now, it was you who brought danger upon yourself.

The falling blade missed, and then the trio shouted something and rushed forward. You weren't interested, and you didn't listen anymore. You just looked at Tenna, who was hugging you tightly, gently wiped the tears from his face, and then smiled with pure joy: "So I succeeded on the first try? That's great." Don't cry, look, we're all standing here perfectly fine now, nothing happened at all. ”

"But what if that time-space rewind function just fails? What if it happens to ...... What if all of this fails? I can't bear losing your future......" His voice was still choked with tears, confused, and even gasping for breath—clearly, he was crying even harder.

You could only pat his head: "It's okay, didn't I succeed?" Besides, I can't bear to lose your future. If I hadn't done anything just now, maybe I would have just watched you die—just thinking about it scares me. That's why I thought of doing this. Maybe my sudden death alone could actually bring us a future where we both survive together. ”

"But if you do that, nothing happens, and you die for no reason—" Tenna was still shaken, emphasizing the possibility.

But you just interrupted him slowly: "Is that so?" Alright then, let's both step into death together. Maybe a shared death is another kind of rebirth. I don't care about that; I just want to always be with you. "As you spoke, you cupped Tenna's head, pressed your forehead against his screen, and looked at him intently. Even though you didn't know if you were looking into his eyes, it didn't matter.

Tenna seemed stunned, or maybe just silent. In any case, he didn't speak for a while, leaving only silence surrounding you. Of course, there was also the distant sounds of battle as background music.

After a long silence, he finally tugged at your collar: "No matter what, let me see your wound first......"

You let him do as he pleased, though honestly, you still feel a bit guilty.

Tenna was stunned once again. He saw the uneven colors of skin beneath the newly formed bloodstains. Some of the skin tones were already very close to the original, but near the scar, there were still quite a few that were clearly recognizable—they were newly formed skin. Or rather, the wounds had already endured countless times, the cuts had been cut countless times, leaving deep wounds. Only this time the wounds were shallow, and they would probably heal in a little while.

He began to blame himself, wondering why he had never noticed before—yet he never had such a close look at your neck before, nor noticed skin color, so naturally he wouldn't notice the patchwork of skin marked by death and rebirth.

Maybe, without his knowledge, you would have died countless times already, yet now you still stand in front of him, even looking at him with a puzzled gaze.

So he starts to feel afraid. Tenna hugs you tightly again, but you don't understand and just hug him back.

You ask softly, "What's going on?" ”

He hesitated for a moment, then hesitated deeply, then asked in a trembling voice, "Without my knowledge...... Have you died many times? ”

You cleared your throat twice, feeling a bit guilty, and replied uncertainly, "Didn't I say before? Trading my own death for a new life for us is a very worthwhile thing......

So what you said about experimenting before was just a disguise; you can't suddenly show that you already knew about it. But you do fear the pain that death brings. You really didn't have memories before; you just guessed through subtle shifts in the position of things and started trying on your own. So every death feels like an adventure to you. And now, you don't know why Tenna entered this cycle with you, still retaining memories.

But all these adventures are the sacrifices that should be made to protect this family, to maintain perfection.

“…… Th-is it worth it? "You heard Tenna's voice. Although he probably didn't want to accept it, he still trembled as he asked, perhaps confirming something.

So you nod without hesitation: "Of course it's worth it. Because I love you, I can't bear a future where we can't coexist, I can't bear to see our current family destroyed. If it's just a possible adventure, and there won't be any other danger besides that, why not give it a try? I don't think there's anything wrong with that. Of course, maybe it's just that I've been working hard for the future where we coexist, even if it's just a risky death. ”

Maybe you just piled up countless deaths just for this one success? Honestly, you don't even remember. You can't even remember why you died, only barely remembering it was probably for Tenna. But now it's best to succeed on one try; otherwise, you'd have to pile up new wounds on top of the same ones.

In short, you are now holding each other tightly in this snowy field, leaning on each other's warmth, and then celebrating the birth of a perfect family.

End.
