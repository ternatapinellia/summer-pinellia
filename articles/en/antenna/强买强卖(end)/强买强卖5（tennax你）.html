No matter what, you couldn't break free from Tenna's embrace.

You pulled out your phone again and turned on the electricity bill—luckily, the electricity bill here could check real-time usage. You checked the electricity bill after Tenna appeared, and it suddenly increased—he didn't need to charge it by any other means, because it was already drawing electricity from the house!

You waved your hand and slapped Tenna's screen on top, saying in a deep voice, "You little liar, you're lying! You've been stealing electricity from my house all along!! "Shut up! From now on, you're going to be muted. When will I allow you to speak? Then speak up. Now, just sleep properly. I have to get up early tomorrow morning!"

Tenna turned off the screen feeling wronged. He really did say to you and stopped talking. Seeing Tenna finally quiet, you finally breathed a sigh of relief and closed your eyes, ready to sleep...... Uh, wait, he's still holding you, clinging to you. You can't push him away even if you want to...... Forget it, the bed is gone anyway. Consider it your kindness and let him sleep on the floor for a night. It doesn't matter. After thinking it over, you close your eyes and fall asleep again.

Well, of course it wasn't a dream. After all, you were now watching Tenna rush to your bedroom door with a blank expression, looking like he had done something bad. But when he got to your bedroom door, he acted as if nothing had happened, just gave you that silly smile as usual.

"Oh, ah, Y/N, good morning! I've already prepared breakfast. Let's finish breakfast first. I believe you'll really like what I make. You sat down and looked at the dishes before you, which were truly delicious—perfectly baked bread, jam on the side, and of course, grilled sausages and bacon. Maybe because you didn't know your taste, there were even steaming scones, apple pie, and tiramisu on the table...... Besides that, there were two clearly freshly made sandwiches, and on the plate, there was a steaming hot and fragrant pie.

…… Oh my god, you think breakfast is way too lavish, you definitely can't finish it. Thinking this, of course you raised your objection: "You've made so much, I definitely can't finish it. I'll take care of the parts you can't finish—ah, but I think you should try that pie first. That pie is definitely delicious, and anyone who's tried it will praise it endlessly—" He started promoting himself first, and you shrugged without saying much. Of course, you took a bite of the scone first...... Sweet and crispy, the taste was great, you thought to yourself, then looked at Tenna again, speechless for a few seconds, and covered his chattering mouth with your hand.

"Stop, stop, stop, then I'll give you another command to mute—without my command, you can't speak. I'm telling you, at least let me finish this breakfast quietly, okay?! It's not that I find you annoying, I just prefer quiet while eating and don't like listening to others talk—" After saying that, you took a deep breath. Since Tenna kept praising that pie, you might as well try it. Actually, you don't usually eat pie, so you don't really know how it tastes.

In short, you carefully cut off a piece and put it in your mouth with a fork...... Hmm, you couldn't help but squint. The taste was indeed very good—at least you really liked it. The flavor was just right, not overly sweet, so you could accept it. The pie itself was fluffy, but not overly puffy—it had a bit of a crispy, fragrant texture. In short, you enjoyed the pie, while Tenna sat beside you, obediently accepting your mute order. Now he stopped talking, just drooped his antenna pitifully at you, and the end of the wire behind him drooped as well...... Well, you only gave him a thumbs-up. It's still comfortable that no one speaks quietly. Thinking about it this way, in short, you happily finished the breakfast. Tenna, having received your praise, seemed a bit carried away. His antenna flickered, and the screen showed a silly grin again, but he just happily went off to clear up the leftovers on the dining table.

After you've almost finished packing everything and rested, you stretch lazily and head toward your study. Today's work is about to start again—well, just turn on the computer and open the design software as usual. But when you reach the study, you find the desktop completely empty, nothing at all. You pause, thinking back to last night—well, you just turned off the computer and put it on the desk as usual, but now the computer has mysteriously disappeared? You searched the study briefly but really couldn't find your computer, so you shouted, "Where's my computer?!" You held your head in your hands, looking very anxious—well, you can probably guess who got your computer, after all, isn't Tenna the only one in this house besides you?! You turn your head to look toward the living room. At that moment, Tenna is sitting on the sofa, and his antenna can't help but stand upright...... Hmm, you look at Tenna with an expression that's hard to describe—he clearly looks guilty, since cold sweat is dripping down his screen!!

You put your hands on your hips and walked out of the study, then stopped in front of Tenna. You wanted to look down at him, but when he sat down, he was a bit taller than you, and you even had to tilt your head up to look at him...... How infuriating.

But you still keep your hands on your hips, at least that still gives you some confidence: "Tell me, did you take my computer away?" ”

But Tenna just looked at you with an innocent expression, still maintaining that smile, though the cold sweat streaming down his screen grew even more—he pointed to his mouth, signaling that you hadn't even lifted the mute order for him.

You couldn't help but roll your eyes and mutter, "This morning you ignored the silence and argued with me, but now that you made a mistake, you know not to speak anymore. You did it on purpose, didn't you? So where did you put my computer and give it back to me? Oh, and now I've revoked your mute order—so if you don't say anything, you're in for it." You even swung your fists twice. Alright, even though hitting him would hurt you first, that didn't stop you from threatening him first.

But Tenna just turned her head away without looking at you, pretending to be innocent and said, "Tenna doesn't know." ”
