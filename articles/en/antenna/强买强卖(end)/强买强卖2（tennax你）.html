Forced Selling and Buying 2 (tennax you)

You couldn't help but roll your eyes: "If you don't have money, what are you talking about...... Honestly, if you're out of money, can't you go out and earn some money to bring back to me, or just buy a lottery ticket and win a big prize to give it to me? Then I wouldn't hesitate to keep you. Ah, it's because you're useful, definitely not because of money...... "You're patching yourself up, after all, saying that makes you seem very greedy, and the TV guy in front of you listens to it and looks pitiful and pleading...... His screen goes black, and all you can see are tears falling down, while he grips your arm and shakes it back and forth, his voice tinged with tears.

"Don't abandon me...... I don't have any money right now, are you still willing to keep me here? Honestly, I'm very useful! I can play you a weather forecast, all kinds of entertainment programs, and even a six-o'clock show I can even set it up to five for you—" But before he could finish, you interrupted him.

"Ah, you can now watch the weather forecast on your phone, and you can even see reports on the weather for the next ninety days, entertainment programs, and so on...... Now you can get early screenings. Even if the theater hasn't shown it yet, as long as I buy it, I can watch it in advance on my computer. Of course, I don't like watching movies or TV dramas, nor am I really into variety shows—after all, they don't seem very interesting. Ah, this probably isn't the main point...... You said this while taking out your phone and turning on the weather forecast to show him. You saw the TV guy wilted again, his antenna drooping, clearly not very happy, and his figure was swaying.

You just turned on your phone's calculator and said, "Hmm, but that way, it looks like I can save you the money I give you every month. Then you have to cook for me and clean up. That way, I can save time, and with that time, I can do more orders and buy more groceries. I guess you can afford the appliance maintenance costs......" You mutter, staring at the small screen on your phone, while he keeps staring at you, watching the faint white light shine on your face. Suddenly, you feel a bit annoyed—oh my god, since you've already agreed to let him stay, he must be the only appliance in this house! He can do weather forecasts and stuff too, so why should you look at that phone?!

So he slapped the small electronic screen to block it. You were a bit dazed, instinctively looking up at him, while he gripped the small phone with both hands and took it from yours. His voice was a bit stammering: "You...... You can't stare at this screen anymore! You just need to look at my screen. I can do anything, I'm very useful! I'm well-behaved too—really, you can have only me, just me! "He looked a bit tough but weak inside, but excitedly turned the screen back on, the antenna shaking as he smiled at you foolishly. He knew his actions were a bit out of line—but, he really couldn't stand you staring at something else.

You blinked twice in confusion and reached out your hand to him: "Uh." I get it. Since you say you're well-behaved, can you give me back your phone? Also, I can keep you, but you can't take my phone—and you can't take any other appliances either! After all, you can't replace a rice cooker for cooking, can't replace a refrigerator to freeze food, or replace a washing machine to wash a lot of clothes at once...... You grumbled, wanting to snatch the tiny phone in his hand, but he raised his hand so high that you couldn't even touch it even if you jumped up.

"I-I-I...... Of course I can! I can cool and heat, I can help you do laundry, I can do many things! So it's enough that I'm the only one in your home! "He said anxiously, the antenna shaking as he raised his hand not wanting you to touch him, but now you're on the sofa, and you collapsed onto him, pressing down on him to stand up on the sofa, then naturally took back your phone—but for some reason, when you looked down, this guy's screen wasn't black or white, but instead turned red??"

You looked at him in surprise, but in the end, you reached out your hand: "Alright, then give me all the money you spent on those appliances, and I'll move them all away. You're the only one left at home." Of course you know he definitely can't come up with the money—besides, you can't possibly do this kind of hard work. How heavy are those appliances? Why would you move them out for no reason!

So his antenna drooped again: "...... I don't have money, you know ......"

You counted on your fingers, then used your phone's calculator to calculate: "Okay, so you have to be my free laborer...... Uh, since you just took my phone, I have to add a rental fee for you. So for the next week, you have to listen to me—whatever you want to eat, you can do it, got it?" As you spoke, you patted his chest and chest without hesitation, then finally got off his body and sat cross-legged beside him. For some reason, you felt he seemed a bit unhappy, especially since his screen was dark again, and he was still trying to get a little closer to you. You didn't even know what he meant, so just pretend you didn't see it at all.

"Alright, of course I'll listen to my new master. I'm very well-behaved...... I'm obedient and capable. Trust me, I will never let you down, and I will never make you regret it!" He patted his chest and promised. You exhaled, and the matter was almost resolved. It was only then that you remembered you might have forgotten something.

So you slap your forehead: "Ah, I almost forgot. Now let's formally introduce myself. I'm Y/N, and I don't even know your real name...... Alright, although it's fine if I keep calling you TV person, I don't think you like me calling you that. Of course, if you're happy with how I address you, you can tell me, but I have to say, I definitely won't use some overly harsh forms of address. And of course, don't call me by nicknames, and don't expect me to call you by them...... We're not that close yet......

"So, does that mean once we're closer, I can use other names for you? Ah, Y/N—it sounds like a good name, I really like it, and I believe you'll like my name too, I am MR.(ANT)TENNA! Your best, most caring TV, your best friend, partner, and family! Well, of course, you can just call me tenna directly, and if you like, it's also okay to call me by other names!" tenna... hmm, this television person, whose name you finally now know, has finally told you what his name actually is.

Ah, wait, it sounds like an antenna person...... Thinking this, you said it out loud, frowning in confusion: "Your name...... Can I call you antenna person? That sounds like the word 'antenna' just has an extra 't'...... Honestly, I'm curious why you have that name, but thinking about it, you're a CRT TV makes sense—after all, there are indeed two antennas above your head for receiving signals. Ah, wait, I got off track, that's not the point...... You gave an awkward smile, and only when you came back to your senses did you realize you had just talked to Tenna for a long time.

You cleared your throat, ready to change the subject, but then you heard Tenna speak proudly: "Yes, yes, the antenna above my head. I'm proud of that. I use these two antennas to receive wireless signals. Of course, if someone wants to contact me, they can send signals directly to my antenna, so I can receive signals freely—" He said, excitedly gesturing his hand. You looked at him, not knowing why he could always maintain such high energy. Although you talk a lot because you're always alone, you're actually not someone who likes socializing...... You rubbed your forehead and made a gesture to be quiet, signaling him to quiet down. Tenna really did quiet down and didn't say anything more, just timidly looking at you—probably looking at you.

"Alright, I understand. But if I were to call you, which number should I dial? That way, would your antenna hear it?" Speaking of which, the signals we use now are all connected to the base stations. Can you connect with the signal base stations in this world? Well, anyway, when you appeared here, I knew you weren't from this world. After all, TVs don't turn into people in this world...... You muttered to yourself, feeling a bit hungry. You couldn't help but open your phone and glance at it. Hmm, it was indeed close to dinner time. Honestly, you didn't expect to talk for so long; it seemed time passed without you noticing.

"Ah...... I'm not really sure either. After all, I've never given my employees their phone numbers or anything. In short, they all act like they already know...... "Tenna got stuck, and you dug out an old phone from the coffee table drawer—maybe a senior phone—since it only retained the function for making calls and texting. You handed the phone to Tenna."

"Alright, here is my contact info, so we can make calls. Anyway, the SIM card is my secondary card, so you don't need to recharge it...... Oh, but remember to charge it. If you don't charge it, your phone won't work—it's just a brick......
