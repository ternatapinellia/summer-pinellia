Forced buying and selling 1 (tennax you)

You usually stay at home and seldom go out... Anyway, the salary you earn is enough for you alone, but occasionally earning a little extra money wouldn't be bad — that's why you started thinking about that CRT TV lying in the junkyard. After all, even though the wires seemed broken and were only fixed later, most of the internal components are probably fine, and if you take them out and sell them separately, you could still make some extra income!

This CRT TV is just way too heavy... Anyway, you can't move it on your own. Luckily, your home has that kind of small trolley used to make it easier to carry packages regularly, but even so, you still had to put in a lot of effort to get the TV onto the trolley, and then spent quite some time dragging it all the way back home. You wiped the sweat off your forehead and decided to rest in your own room for a while; what to do with the TV can be dealt with later... You dragged your tired body back to your room, then collapsed directly onto the bed, scrolling through your phone to pass the time while resting.

Just lying there like that, maybe an hour passed, or maybe two hours... You didn't check the time; you just heard a sudden crackling noise coming from the living room, and it was quite loud... and it was still continuing. You figured, could it be that a mouse got into your house to gnaw on your appliances? So reluctantly, you got out of your bed and walked out of the bedroom, not wanting to spend a big sum just to fix an appliance... But the first thing you saw as you stepped out of the bedroom door was someone standing in your living room, next to a messy pile of scattered little things from your house... Above his head—roughly above, or you might say above the TV—was the blanket you draped over the sofa, and he had unfinished ribbons you bought hanging on him. This guy looked very tall; standing up, he almost reached your ceiling, and he could probably still be considered a living creature... You looked up to see, was this a TV person? His head looked like a television, and when you turned, the CRT TV you had painstakingly brought back was indeed gone!

That blanket was probably a bit in the way. At least at first, the TV person had his back to you, then after fiddling for a bit, he took the blanket off from above and then revealed a silly grin looking at you… or probably looking at you, since by now the TV head was bent down towards you. And he had no eyes, which was fine; that way he wouldn’t be exposed to radiation… you thought absentmindedly. Then you glanced at the little cart next to you—empty, without the CRT TV you had brought back hoping to make a little extra money. Then you looked at the big guy in front of you, clutching his head and letting out a heart-wrenching scream: 'My money!!'

The TV just happened to start talking, and its tone sounded quite cheerful: 'Oh, are you my new owner? I'm glad you could bring me back from the junkyard—' but its voice was drowned out by your heart-wrenching screams.

He was stunned for a moment, then turned around, his whole body becoming shriveled, roughly about your height, even slightly shorter than you, because he kept flattening more, swaying from side to side, his tone filled with a sense of frustration.

"Oh... you don't welcome me coming to your house?" As she spoke, her voice grew weaker and gradually quieter. You were curious whether this TV still felt as heavy even when it became smaller... but you didn't want to try it yet, after all, if that happened, the one who would become all flat would be you.

Anyway, you automatically got the equation. This guy is the CRT TV you brought back earlier, after all, you don't have a TV at home! Not at all!

"Uh, of course I’d welcome a TV into my home… after all, selling it could earn some money, but in your current state, it looks like you’d just end up costing me money…" You said, taking out your phone, opening the calculator, and starting to calculate how much more it would cost for daily meals and transportation if the other party were a human like you, how much it would cost to maintain if it were an appliance, and if it were both human and appliance at the same time—such expensive costs! Much more expensive than raising yourself! Oh, no, it’s impossible for it to be both a human and an appliance…

You were just babbling: 'If I had known, I wouldn't have picked it up. Being greedy for small gains leads to big losses... Now it will cost a lot of money to properly fix a TV...' You weren't thinking about calling the police; after all, the police can't do anything about a TV person anyway... You were just sincerely looking at tenna, who was still standing in place, deflated, staggering slightly.

"Can I ask you to leave my house and go somewhere else? I can't afford a TV. Ah, if you want, you could go to the police station and get some kind of vagrant allowance, or you could go out and look for a job, maybe find one with food and lodging provided..." But before you could finish your rambling, this TV person, like a small cannonball, grew a little, becoming just big enough to cover you, and then held you in their arms—you hadn't even reacted to what was happening, instinctively wanting to push them away, but they were way too heavy for you—oh my god, oh my god, you were now forcibly hugged by this TV, and tears were dripping from their eyes, quickly wetting your shoulders. You were a little stunned, only able to hear their sobbing, and it was super loud, hurting your ears—oh my god, you hadn't even tested the soundproofing of your house, but now you definitely need to buy some soundproofing material or something..."

"Oh my god, my new owner, are you going to abandon me?! You can't throw me away. I thought when you picked me up from the junkyard, it meant that you wanted a TV... You definitely need a TV, I'm very useful! I can report the weather for the next week for you, I can broadcast entertainment programs for you, I can keep you warm when you're cold, and I can play all kinds of music and various programs, surely there will be something you like!" He cried and sobbed, holding you tightly, not willing to let go at all.

"You... you let go of me first..." You pushed against his chest, standing firm, hoping he would let go on his own... Unfortunately, he seemed to hold you even tighter.

"No, I won't let go. If I let go, you'll definitely take me to the police... I, I don't want to be a stray TV. I'm very useful, you can take me in, right? I'm very useful, you surely need a new TV, I'm very useful... Don't throw me away. Since you picked me up from the junkyard, you have to be responsible for me, be my new owner..." He cried while holding onto you, and you didn’t even know where all his tears came from. In any case, you felt that a thin layer of water had already formed on your floor... Yes, it was all from his crying.

You feel like it's getting a bit difficult to breathe, but he probably hugged you while crying and got a little overwhelmed. He even held you while sitting on the sofa, hugging you tightly, and that heavy TV head buried into your shoulder... your shoulder... it hurts so much... You take a deep breath... alright, you couldn't do it. Breathing is really difficult, and you can only say with effort, 'I... I can't catch my breath...'

Only then did his hand loosen a little, but he kept repeating over and over: 'I am useful... don't throw me away, don't abandon me, you will definitely like me...'

You pressed your forehead and felt a bit of a headache... Oh my, it seems this TV is speaking nonsense and its state of mind is a bit abnormal... Let's let it calm down a bit first. So now you can finally take a deep breath: 'Alright, alright, I won't send you to the police... But honestly, you really could try to get a job and support yourself...' Before you could finish speaking, he hugged you tightly again.

"...I'm just a television, I can't find a job. Yes, I can work for you. You must really need a television, right? You definitely won't refuse, right? You surely need me to work for you, right?" His antenna was also wobbling, and now it drooped again. Clearly, he was probably nervous.

"...Can you... let me go first, this thing I don't even know the name of... oh right, I don't even know your name, but never mind, it's not important..." You feel a bit dizzy, probably because you're a little short of oxygen.

"No... no, you will definitely throw me away..." He even looked wronged... You had no idea what exactly he was feeling wronged about, so you could only painfully reach out and pat his TV head... My god, it looks like he's determined to stick around with you, and you don't even know who he really is.

…And probably he won't let you go unless you agree with him.

“...I understand, I will hire you, including food and accommodation, but your workload will be very heavy. And I won’t pay you extra wages! I don’t have money, so if you want money, go find another job!” You said this all in one breath, then gasped for air. At this moment, his antennae stood straight up, and his nose even poked out... a flower??? Anyway, he seemed very happy, and finally he let you go.

"Oh, I knew it, my new master must need a TV—it's okay, it's okay, I'm happy to work for you! Even if I have to pay you, it's fine!" As he said this, he raised both hands and started cheering. And you did catch the key words.

"You have money? Give it to me." You reached out your hand rudely, and this unknown television person turned their TV head aside, probably feeling a bit guilty.

"...I have no money, haha."
