Forced Selling and Buying 3 (tennax you)

Tenna, however, was unwilling to accept the phone, which surprised you. Instead, he sounded confident: "I can reach you just by my antenna. My antenna is my best achievement, uh...... One of them! In short, I don't need your phone. Of course, when we're together, I don't need your phone. As long as you have me, that's enough! "He argued confidently. You glanced at him speechlessly, shrugged, and put the phone back into the coffee table.

"Alright, if you don't want to, then don't. But for now, go cook first. It's almost dinner time. Oh, and by the way, today I want to have red wine stewed beef. You can look for some ingredients in the fridge—we've got these ingredients anyway...... You directed Tenna to cook for you. He walked toward the kitchen in the direction you pointed, and you naturally returned to your room. Oh heavens, this order is almost done! You really don't want to ...... Hmm, postpone all this until tomorrow, because you have a feeling that if Tenna gets a bit free, she'll definitely come bother you, and then you won't be able to do your job well.

You hurriedly finished the work, and after finishing, you stretched lazily and headed outside again. Just then, Tenna, wearing an apron and heat-resistant gloves, came out holding a tray, looking very virtuous. You looked at the delicious-looking dinner he set out on the table with great interest, and Tenna immediately asked you to take credit, hands on hips, looking very excited.

"Look, Y/N, I did pretty well, right? I told you I was very useful and wouldn't cause you any trouble. Try it, it will definitely taste great!" He puffed out his chest as he spoke, looking very proud and confident. You were amused by him and indeed tasted a flavor to see how it was. Then you nodded seriously, "Hmm, it really tastes great. I like this flavor." "As you spoke, you even gave him a thumbs-up, and Tenna was clearly pleased with your praise. You watched him jump up and down with joy, as if he didn't even notice how heavy he was. The floor started pounding, and you worried it might suddenly collapse. Luckily, it didn't, and ...... Hmm, a flower even bloomed on the tip of his nose. You don't even know why a TV blooms, but it looks like a real flower. Forget it, he's not from the same world as you anyway, so why care so much? Thinking this, you can rest easy and continue eating your dinner. Tenna paces around on his own, probably feeling he has enough, then puts on a silly smile, with that flower still on his nose, and then sits down across from you, holding the TV head with both hands, smiling at you. At first, you intended to ignore him, but his gaze was so intense that you felt like you were sitting on pins and needles, so you couldn't help but look up at Tenna: "...... What's wrong with you? Why are you staring at me all the time? I don't think there's anything good about my face, and besides, you don't need to eat? Even though you don't look like you're alone...... Uh, you're just a TV, but running a TV also needs energy, right? What exactly is your energy? "Your questions are one after another, and Tenna just looks at you with a silly smile. Hearing this, his head tilts slightly, then his antenna shakes excitedly, and his smile grows even wider.

"Ah, Y/N, are you really concerned about me? No, no, you really are caring about me. I knew you definitely cared about me, I knew I was different to you, I knew I was very important to you—" And you were speechless, and you just casually said you cared about him, so how did it turn into definitely caring about him? He was very important to you. You didn't know what kind of logic he had, but you just couldn't understand it. But to say he didn't care at all was impossible, after all, this was a living TV that suddenly appeared in your home! Of course you would be curious!

You couldn't help but interrupt him: "Stop, stop, stop, stop, I don't care what you're thinking. I just want to know if you've eaten. If you haven't, what is your energy source...... Honestly, after all, you're not a person but a TV. It's normal for me to be curious, right? ”

He finally stopped talking, but still repeated as if unwilling to give up: "Ah...... But Y/N, you really do care about me, otherwise why would you ask this question? Of course, alright, I'll tell you the answer—I already ate it in the kitchen, when I tried making this dish! Y/N, don't worry, of course, my energy comes from food, but most of the time I still need to be plugged in, when I was a TV. Of course, I'm not just a TV now, so I don't need to plug it in! He happily rambled on, and you had no idea why he spoke so much. Anyway, you rubbed your brow in frustration and quickly finished the dinner, wanting to hide in your room for some peace.

"Alright, alright, you talk a lot...... I'm done eating, then I'll head back first. Remember to tidy up these dishes and make sure the house is clean. Other than that, there's nothing else. After you're done with these things, it's your time to rest. You can do whatever you want! "Of course, you didn't realize that because you haven't communicated with people outside for a long time, you can't help but talk a lot. In this regard, you and Tenna are just as good.

Tenna just cleared her throat: "Mm, I understand. I promise to complete the task, Y/N!" But my dear star, don't you like me talking...... Uh, although I probably can't change it anymore, if you don't, I can talk less often. I don't want you to feel annoyed......"

Unfortunately, by now you had already entered the study, and of course you didn't hear Tenna's voice. Tenna's antenna, which didn't respond, drooped. Although he knew you just hadn't heard, that didn't stop him from feeling wronged...... Besides, talking too much wasn't his fault; he just stayed in the garbage dump with no one talking to him, so he was bored—and you talked a lot. He guessed you probably wouldn't talk to anyone else anyway, but that was fine, so he could enter your life with peace of mind.

So he pulled himself together again, diligently tidying up the dishes and cleaning the room. It was only about nine o'clock, which was resttime—meaning his new little owner had time to watch TV! Tenna's antenna was reinforced. He tidied up, uh, then excitedly dashed into the study—uh, but before entering, because he had walked too fast and not high enough, his TV's head hit the doorframe with a loud bang. You startled him, instinctively turned to glance at him, then just saved the current work on the computer and walked to the door. You saw Tenna rubbing her own screen, her lips drooping as she looked pitiful. Only then did you barely breathe a sigh of relief: "Looks like you didn't hurt yourself, but it must have hurt a lot. I just heard your footsteps walking over. By the way, how did you deal with your injuries before?" Normally, a wound like this should have turned a bit bruised, but you didn't look like you were hurt at all, just fine...... "Yeah, at least you don't have to pay for appliance repairs. As for the door frame...... You turned to look. Luckily, Tenna hadn't hurt it and was still there, perfectly fine. You breathed a slight sigh of relief.

Tenna rubbed her TV screen, pitifully crouching in front of you. Now that he's squatted down, he's about the same height as you, and he just mumbled vaguely, "Ah...... In my world, healing magic existed, so when you got hurt, just one healing spell was enough! Hmm, unless it's something like a broken arm or leg, then you have to reattach that part first...... Ah, right, I heard that just blowing on it will make it hurt, so could you blow on me for some breath? "Even though he has no eyes, you always feel like he's looking at you with anticipation...... Your lips twitched, and Tenna pointed to the spot where he had just bumped into the screen. Then his antenna drooped, and he obediently rested his hands on his knees, looking at you pitifully. You hesitated for a moment, but in the end, you couldn't take that intense gaze anymore. You had to bend down slightly, then cupped his screen with both hands and blew a couple of breaths on that spot where you couldn't see the wound but Tenna kept saying it hurt—hmm, his antenna was standing upright again, and for some reason, the tail of his antenna was wagging behind you, provocatively...... He seemed to be in a good mood, almost hugging you and rubbing against you...... You first took two steps back when his hands reached out, but that didn't stop Tenna from hugging you all at once. His TV head rubbed against your shoulder—this TV was just too heavy...... You felt the weight on your shoulder, feeling a bit overwhelmed, but Tenna just cheerfully said, "Oh thank you, Y/N, my dear star, now I don't hurt anymore—oh my god, seriously, I really want to thank you. My injury healed in no time, so I wanted to invite you to watch the show on my TV. I promise you'll love it!" ”
