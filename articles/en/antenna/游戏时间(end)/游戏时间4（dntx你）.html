Game Time 4 (dntx you)

When you fully wake up, you rub your eyes and stared blankly at the TV plush toy tightly hugged in your arms. You don't even know what you're thinking, anyway...... You're probably just daydreaming. Are you wondering if Tenna specially arranged this room? After all...... Other than him, the other Dark People in this TV world probably aren't so familiar with your room's layout.

…… After all, you only occasionally feel a bit weak and empty because the house is empty and empty, then drag the TV into your room, then sit on the bed hugging your knees, just watching the show absentmindedly, or rather, you just need a little sound to dispel your fear and loneliness when you're alone.

You get dressed and eat the steaming hot breakfast on the table, then walk out of the room. For some reason, Tenna is already sitting on a chair in the lounge. His screen is still pitch black, looking like he's resting with his eyes closed.

You hesitated for a moment. To be honest, you hadn't figured out what you should do today, nor had you figured it out...... What kind of attitude should you have when facing this guy? After all, although what he said was really harsh, the room was decorated and there were other aspects...... You still felt a bit grateful.

But you don't know if he'll actually agree to your invitation. After all...... What if you're just delusional? What if you just assume he likes you? That's a huge misunderstanding that absolutely must not happen—

So you sat on the sofa opposite him, hesitated for a moment, then cleared your throat twice and said, "You...... Want to play games with me? ”

His screen lit up. He seemed to be looking up at you, or maybe just looking down at you with a half-smile: "Oh?" Didn't you think my words were disturbing you? ”

You always feel like Tenna is being sarcastic...... Hmm, maybe this isn't just your imagination.

When he brings up this topic, you feel a bit awkward too, lowering your head and not daring to look at that TV again. You just think about it and feel that giving him a chance to play with him is a win—others might not even get the chance to be your playmate! Of course, that's just because you don't have friends.

So you put your hands on your hips, lifted your head, and deliberately spoke with a high vantage point: "Hmph...... This is an opportunity that others can't even dream of. Why don't you cherish it?" After saying that, you swallowed hard; you really couldn't get used to this way of speaking.

So you sneak a glance at Tenna out of the corner of your eye, only to see his screen go completely black again. He just coldly responds with an 'oh' and then ignores you.

"What do you want to do today?" He just casually switched to the next topic.

You thought he agreed, and excitedly ordered a two-player cooperative puzzle game. Instead, you watched Tenna hold his TV head, projecting the game's image onto the screen.

“…… This is a two-player game. You can't just let me play with two controllers by myself, can I?" You pointed at yourself in disbelief.

"Didn't you say playing with you was a chance that no one could even dream of? Just find someone else to play with." Tenna lowered her voice. For some reason, you always felt he was a bit sarcastic...... But you couldn't see anything from this screen right now.

If they go out to find other Dark People...... Most of them probably wouldn't dare come over, since most are still afraid of Tenna, the big boss.

So your remaining choice is either to pick the lock and go to the Light World to find someone to play games with you. But that's too difficult, since the town's Light Folk are all in small groups with their own companions, and you...... still have nowhere to gather together.

Or have Tenna play games with you...... No, you're still a bit unwilling, so you got up, holding back a breath, cheeks puffed up, and ran toward the lounge: "Alright, I'll go find the other Dark People right now!" ”

But for some reason, when you walked out the corridor, you didn't see any other Dark People along the way. Either they saw you and then hurried away, looking flustered, as if they saw someone they shouldn't have. It took a lot of effort to finally catch a Zapper. Before he could jump away, he had to hurriedly salute you.

You kindly and smilingly asked Zapper if he could play games with you, and Zapper was so startled he gave you a big bow—almost to the point of kneeling on the ground, though you weren't sure if he could do it. Then he dashed off in a flash.

Helpless, you could only storm back to the lounge, only to find Tenna sitting leisurely on the sofa with his legs crossed. You rolled your eyes inwardly, then walked over to Tenna and reached out a hand: "...... I—I invite you to play games with me......" Your voice softened a bit, just quietly finishing the sentence. You can't be sure if he did it on purpose, but right now, he's the only gaming partner you can choose from.

Only then did he leisurely take your hand and then stand up. Of course, he's much taller and more impressive than you. Then Tenna took your hand and sat back down. He snapped his fingers, and the wall opposite was a large projector—game screens displayed on it. You stared at everything in shock, then looked up at Tenna sitting next to you and couldn't help but raise your voice: "So why does it seem like you're the only TV in the TV world with other audio-visual devices?" ”

"After all, what you want is a TV. In this world, I am the only television." He answers your question calmly, and you just feel he's being sophistry—although the pristine white wall in front of you is just a projection device, it can display images, so in a broad sense, it's still a television!

For some reason, you always have some suspicions about this—you feel a bit uneasy, a bit doubtful that you're mistaken. After all, suspecting Tenna is deliberately doing this just to make you look at him or something, maybe it's just your own imagination. Better not think about these things and just enjoy it first.

You had just started the game for a short while before you yawned, hurriedly ran out to find other Dark People, and while passing the gate to the world of light, you tried to force it open. It really drained your strength. Last night, because of that dream, you didn't sleep very well...... Now you're feeling a bit sleepy.

Tenna lowered her head to glance at you, her lips straightened: "What, are you so bored?" ”

Maybe it's just ...... Maybe it's an illusion that arises when you're half-asleep. You always feel that his words are rarely taken lightly, and of course, you still seem a bit dissatisfied.

You yawned again, forced yourself to perk up, and waved your hand: "No, no...... I heard this game is really fun! I just didn't sleep well, it doesn't affect my gameplay! "After saying that, you sat up straight and stared intently at the screen.

Tenna didn't say anything more. Each of you controlled a character into the game scene. It was a cooperative escape room game—because two people were locked in different rooms. Some puzzles could be solved by themselves, while others required cooperation from each other.

You control the character, quickly piecing together the ship model in front of you, and then you feel a bit troubled—after all, even though you've done it, the ship's route is still unclear. It seems you have to wait until the ship reaches the destination before you can get the sealed key.

You fiddled with the boat for a long time without starting it, so you urgently urged Tenna: "Do you have any directions or something?" I need this over here. "For the fun of the puzzle game, you tacitly avoided looking at each other's screens. Tenna glanced at your screen and started mocking you.

"Are you blind? If you don't need your eyes, you can donate them." As he spoke, he took your game controller and pressed a button. The sails on the boat were lowered, and finally, they could be moved.

You paused, pouted, and looked a bit aggrieved: "I was just too impatient...... What right do you have to scold me! Besides, I was probably just a little sleepy...... A little groggy...... Your voice gradually faded, just weakly making excuses for yourself, and Tenna didn't refute you.

He just put the game controller back into your hand: "Mm, next time I suggest you play games with a clear mind." ”

"Then do you want me to catch up on sleep here? I'll just sleep on the sofa for a while. I promise I'll wake up soon." You couldn't help but choke at him, feeling a bit unconvinced, thinking you'd have to get back at it later.

Then Tenna nodded. You looked him up and down in disbelief, and he grew a bit impatient: "If you want to rest, just do it quickly. Don't stare here with your eyes wide open. I don't have a blanket on me." ”

You glanced at him skeptically, then closed your eyes and leaned back on the sofa. You have to say, the lounge sofa is really perfect for lying down...... It's soft and perfect for sleeping—at least you fall asleep after a while. Then, unknowingly, because Tenna sat on the sofa next to you, the sofa sank in one spot, and you also leaned in that direction, landing on top of Tenna.

His body stiffened for a moment, and the screen went dark, lowering his head. Maybe seeing you were sound asleep, he carefully picked you up and leaned against his chest. He held you, and you were sleeping soundly against his chest. Tenna just gently rested the TV's head on yours, not sure what he was thinking. The controller tossed nearby had long since disconnected and automatically cut off power, and now they were leaning against each other.
