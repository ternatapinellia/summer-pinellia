Game Time 7 (dntx you)

You seem to have had a nightmare—bizarre and bizarre, as if a fire had broken out, and your parents were so focused on escaping with your sister, leaving behind you, the sister who never had their concern, leaving everything at home. Little you could only helplessly lean against the counter, against that TV—the one that had suffered a mouth injury, was damaged, had tape wrapped around its antenna, and its screen was slightly cracked, until its once cold mechanical casing and glass were now tinged with warmth.

Further on...... What comes after that? You can't remember clearly...... Whether this really happened, you can't recall either. You only remember that when you were about fifteen, you had a serious illness, and after that, you basically forgot everything from before. Even your own parents—when you saw them again, you didn't feel much.

They anxiously call out to you, call your name, and embrace you with tears in their eyes, but you don't know why they hold you. Finally...... Perhaps because they never got the response they wanted, they left disappointed, settled elsewhere with your sister, leaving you alone when you were only sixteen.

It's okay, you can already earn money to support yourself, and being alone won't be boring because they find it useless. Leaving that old TV behind, you can still have a short moment of entertainment.

…… Was it really a dream, or did it really happen back then? You can't say clearly, or rather, memories before eighteen are shrouded in a fog, and you can't recall clearly. You seem ...... Only clearly remember what happened after you arrived in the TV world.

And you woke up from that dream, startled again when you woke up, because you found everything pitch black before you—and when you looked closely, it was Tenna's pitch-black screen. He didn't turn on the light, just propped up his arms, probably watching you.

You swallowed nervously: "So...... Why didn't you turn on the lights? ”

"Because you've been sleeping all along, Lord Sleep God. How could I dare wake you up?" He said sarcastically.

You puffed out your cheeks and tapped his screen. For some reason, you felt like you weren't as afraid of him anymore—you didn't know why, but you said ruthlessly, "What a harsh way to talk—alright, turn on the lights!" The pitch-black room was terrifying! ”

Tenna said nothing, just snapped her fingers, and then the lights in the room came on. Suddenly touching the light, you instinctively squinted, tears streaming down your face, followed by darkness before your eyes, with only a faint light filtering through the gap.

You realize it was Tenna's hand. And he just said with contempt, "If you can't accept it, you'll blame me again. Your Majesty, what will it take for you to be satisfied?" ”

You coughed dryly twice, your face instinctively turning red: "...... I—I wouldn't do that...... "You're not some arrogant person. If anyone is self-centered, Tenna would be that kind of person."

"Mm." He replied briefly, then let go. You blinked twice, and you had to admit, you really were used to it now. You tugged at the hem of your skirt, a little embarrassed, but in the end, you spoke softly.

“…… Thank you. ”

"I can't hear clearly." He said lazily, leaning back on the sofa.

Your face flushed red as you glared at him fiercely—hmm, he probably didn't see it.

"I said, thank you!" You raised your voice and shouted—hmm, but it seems the TV doesn't have ears, so what exactly does it use to listen? It can't be a mechanical shell conduction, right? You suddenly start thinking about it.

"Mm-hmm, I heard it clearly, you don't have to shout so loudly." Tenna started to irritably pull her antenna, pulling it down, as if it were the structure for receiving audio.

You quieted down, touched your belly, hesitated for a moment: "...... I'm hungry, I want to go back to the Light World to eat. ”

Spending all day in the TV world eating TV meals is pretty boring—you're getting tired of them.

"Is it that the TV food isn't good? Or you can try something else—" His speech inexplicably sped up as he began to introduce it, while Tenna stood up, clearly preparing to bring out something.

"No, I'm tired of the Dark World's food. I want to go back and eat." You persisted—well, even though you were the only one cooking and the food you cooked wasn't very tasty. But you wanted to go back. No matter what, you missed home, you wanted to go back there, even if no one was waiting for you. The only thing waiting for you was the bulky TV form of the TV person in the world of light.

"Your cooking skills are indeed a decent dish." He seemed to be sarcastically teasing you, or maybe you just misheard...... Just pretend you didn't hear it, just keep pretending to be strong.

"So what? I just think the food I cook is really good, so I have to go back and make it myself. Some ingredients aren't ...... from the underworld." You checked the kitchen a couple of days ago, and sure enough, there aren't any ingredients you'll need.

Tenna's voice was still cold: "What do you want to eat?" ”

You paused, almost unable to believe your ears: "...... What? ”

And he patiently repeated, "What do you want to eat?" ”

You blinked, unsure if it would happen, but since you were so, you started listing dishes: "Then I want garlic pork with clear pork knuckle sauce pig's trotters sweet and sour pork ribs, Farewell My Concubine braised pork with quail eggs, roasted pigeon, jade jade soup—"

You listed the names of the dishes one after another, then Tenna covered your mouth. You blinked innocently and looked up at him. His screen was still dark. You couldn't tell what he was feeling, nor did you dare to cause trouble, so you simply reached out and removed the hand covering your mouth.

"If none of these works...... Then stir-fried pork with chives—that should be possible, right? "You tilted your head, and this request was probably reasonable.

He walked out without saying a word. When you saw him walk out of the lounge door, you quietly followed him, slowing your pace so he wouldn't notice you following behind him.

But in fact, Tenna had already noticed this; he just hadn't exposed it. He knew maybe you were just ...... for fun, that kind of thing. He also knew you probably wouldn't be at ease, but he couldn't just let you leave here.

Not just because of him, but also because of your request—of course, he knows that even for himself, he won't let you leave the TV world. Anton Tenna won't let the prey he sets his sights on run away.

So...... Even though he hadn't tried it much, Tenna still made a detour and walked into the kitchen. And as you watched him walk into the kitchen, you instinctively widened your eyes and tried to close your gaping mouth. Oh my god—Tenna actually cooks himself! You don't even know if he can cook, but...... You remember several TV channels are cooking shows, so Tenna is probably very skilled in the kitchen.

Obviously, you were too relieved too soon. Because you sat in the corner sneaking around, staring toward the kitchen, and after about ten or twenty minutes, you saw wisps of black smoke rising from the kitchen doorway, and you heard the clattering of bells and a violent explosion. It felt as if the entire TV world was shaking, and you felt your body shake off the floor and fall back to the floor.

You swallowed nervously, quietly peeked your head out from behind the wall toward the kitchen door, and then saw a figure walking out of a TV covered in black and gray on both his head and clothes. Unexpectedly, his screen was lit, which made the black gray look so out of place.

…… You might as well take this chance to find a way to return to the world of light—after all, Tenna had already left. So you quietly and steadily walked to the locked front door. You hesitated for a moment, took off the hairpin from your head, stuck it inside the lock, and studied it for a long time. It turned out your lockpicking skills were still insufficient...... The door wouldn't open. Although you couldn't quite remember why you actually picked the lock.

You try again to see if you can brute-force pull open the lock or break the door, but obviously, it won't work...... Your hands and legs hurt, and you feel like the door and lock are very solid.

You looked gloomily, then glanced over the room—ah, there really were other rooms, but one of them was sealed off for some reason, sealed with countless strips. You tried to tear open those seals to see if you could get in, but those seals were very secure.

And in another room...... Unexpectedly, a door sign was hung that read Anton Tenna.

It's Tenna's room...... You hesitate for a moment, then press the doorknob. The good news is, the door isn't locked. Feeling guilty, you glance to the sides. It's clear that no Dark People usually come to this place, so you breathe a sigh of relief, press the doorknob, and enter the room.

You turn on the light, blink to adjust to the light—otherwise, you wouldn't be able to see anything in the pitch-black room.

Well, actually, you just want to see if you can walk into the rooms on either side of the tightly locked door...... Is there a connecting passage or something?

Tenna seemed to live in this place most of the time, since it was cleaned very thoroughly, with no dust left behind.

You casually scanned his room, and if there was no passage, you turned off the lights and left—after all, suddenly coming to someone's room always made you feel a bit guilty, as if you were inspecting.

Then your gaze froze, you froze in place, eyes wide as you stared at a wall—the wall was covered with photos of you, from childhood to adulthood, and ...... even, even photos of you bathing and getting out of the bath?!
