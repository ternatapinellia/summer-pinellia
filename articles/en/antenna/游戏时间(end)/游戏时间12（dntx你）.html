Game time 12 (dntx you)

You widened your eyes and looked at Tenna, who was expressionless beside you, then at the scene before you. For some reason, Tenna always felt like she was watching a show...... And somehow, he pulled out a voice recorder. At this point, this memory ended. For a moment, you didn't know whether to cover your ears or your eyes, because Tenna pressed the recorder and started playing the same sentence.

"This is my only family." And after finishing that sentence, he pressed the recorder to play it again. Your face flushed red as you tried to jump up to snatch the recorder, but unfortunately, compared to Tenna, you were just too short, so no matter how you tried to reach out, you couldn't get the recorder and were forced to listen to this embarrassing remark several times.

In the end, you have no choice but to cover your ears, grab Tenna's hand with your other hand, and quickly move into the next photo—you definitely don't want to hear that sentence again.

Indeed, once he entered a new memory, he stopped playing that recorder. It was just a randomly chosen photo, and you weren't sure what period it was. You just looked very young, probably from before that fire.

You stared blankly at the elderly lady with a head full of white hair but a gentle and kind smile, sitting in a rocking chair knitting a sweater. For some reason, you couldn't help but feel wronged and missed—a deep longing. Tears unknowingly streamed down your face, but you remained unaware. Tenna silently wiped the tears from your cheeks and said nothing.

Then you see yourself. As a child, you sit seriously in front of a TV, holding cheap diamonds and a curtain you don't know where you bought them, trying hard to decorate the TV, even though it looks a bit out of place.

The old lady smiled, "Nannan, yesterday a few kids came to play with you. Do you want to go out and play?" ”

The little girl said without turning her head, "I don't want to! It's not fun to be with them either. I'd rather dress up my TV here......

"The TV can't talk, and it can't play with you."

"That doesn't matter! I want this TV to be my bride! ”

“…… You can't get married yet. ”

"Hmm...... Then from now on, I want the TV to be my bride! The little girl raised her arm and shouted, radiant and excited.

"Alright, sweetie, let's eat." Until the grandmother called the little girl to go eat together, and the little girl happily took her hand, and they walked together in a certain direction. You smelled the familiar aroma of food, just like the dishes Tenna prepared for you at noon.

By the time you come to your senses, you've already left that memory, and before you know it, tears are streaming down your face. You instinctively grab Tenna's hand, choking up for a long time but unable to say a word.

He also rarely mocked you, just quietly glanced at you and asked lightly, "Do you want to keep watching?" ”

You shook your head, instinctively refusing the offer, just sniffling: "I...... No need to look anymore. ”

"So have you remembered everything now?" He continued to ask, but for some reason, you sensed a hint of nervousness in his voice.

You honestly shook your head: "I don't remember." But maybe...... The things you can't remember probably aren't that important either. "After all, the memories from the past fifteen years mostly come back after seeing that photo. You remember buying Tenna all kinds of decorations, like veils and headbands, and you even hung necklaces and bracelets on his antenna. You also recalled those familiar but gone-of-a-kind scents from memory, and remembered the time when your home was full plus a TV, and you only had two family members.

And after the fire...... The memories that continued up to entering the Dark World remained blurry, but just from those few fragments just now, you could sense that these were probably not the memories you wanted to recover.

Besides, it probably doesn't really matter.

"So, what are you going to do next?" Tenna's voice was a bit tense. He knew that if you insisted on returning to the Light World, no matter how much he tried to stop you, it wouldn't work—you wouldn't listen. So, if you wanted to return to the Light World, he could only follow your wishes.

“…… Can you play games with me?" You just tugged at Tenna's shirt and gave him a pitiful look. He clearly couldn't stand you like this, and his screen suddenly went pitch black again. Then he turned his head around, unable to see his face clearly.

You blink, your eyes darting around in your sockets. Somehow, you feel like you've suddenly found a way to handle him.

But you just tugged at the hem of his shirt again, your voice a bit pitiful: "...... I'm not in a good mood. Can you play with me for a while?" ”

Tenna finally turned her head. Her screen was lit, but she still showed no other expression, and her voice was tense, a bit stiff: "Fine." So, what are you going to play? ”

You kept blinking at him: "Hmm...... I bought an RPG game disc before. I couldn't get past one myself...... Your skills must be much better than mine, right? "You said that on purpose. Since he mocked you like that before, it means he's really good at gaming—so playing games with you and helping you clear levels is something he has to do!" You thought this with confidence.

Tenna glanced down at you. Of course, he could tell there was a hint of provocation in his words—but it didn't matter, he didn't care that much. Besides, that provocation had indeed stirred his fighting spirit.

So he simply said in a cold and resolute voice, "Of course, I'm good at any game." ”

Your eyes darted around as you asked, "Then...... Can you play horror games with me? I'm afraid of the dark." ”

This is true. Because of your fear of blacks, many of the games you buy end up unfinished, but you also like to buy puzzle games. Because many of these games are puzzle-themed but have a terrifying nature, you don't keep playing.

Then Tenna fell silent. He didn't answer you. You just gently tugged at his shirt with your hand, softened your voice, and whispered, "Please, I really think that game is probably fun, but I don't dare to keep playing......

“…… Of course, no problem. His voice was still icy, and you couldn't hear any extra emotion. Then he scooped you up and hurried toward the lounge.

From initially feeling uncomfortable to now somewhat enjoying his embrace, after all, he has treated you this way these days.

So at this moment, you just put your hand on his shoulder to keep your balance, then leaned in to look back. From a distance, you could see some pippins whispering as they walked by, and when they noticed your gaze, they sped up and ran away, making you puff out your cheeks in frustration. You never knew why those pippins were so afraid to get close to you...... Including the other employees of the TV world—could it be because of Tenna's orders? Thinking that way isn't entirely impossible.

In a moment of distraction, Tenna had already taken you to the lounge. He put you on the sofa, sat down next to you, and then handed you the game controller.

You walked over and stuffed another CD into the TV in front, then went back and sat next to Tenna, unconsciously leaning a little closer to him—almost sitting right next to him.

Tenna turned his head again. You couldn't see what expression he had, but only Tenna knew. He lowered his head and covered his nose, unable to hold back the flowers sprouting from his nose. If you saw it, you might think he picked some flowers and planted them there, since you used to do this often as a child, under the guise of dressing up for TV.

It's just a pity you didn't see it, and he absolutely could never let you see it. Since that fire, you haven't dressed him up again, so...... He could never be shown to you.

But you just stared intently at the screen, tapped the keys, opened the game, then poked Tenna, your eyes sparkling: "Time to pick a character! I'm going to pick the assassin, it looks really cool." "You've always liked the assassin class.

Tenna didn't argue with you; he just glanced around and chose a defensive tank character.

“…… Then I'll have to hide behind you and deal damage, so you have to protect me!" You swayed your legs and shook your head as you spoke.

…… Actually, you only chose to come back to play games to ease the confusion, anxiety, and sadness you felt after learning about your memories. Because you know that playing games here, at least someone will accompany you, and at least you won't have to return to the world of light and sit alone in front of that TV.

"What, you can't possibly be this weak, can't you not dodge at all?" Tenna couldn't help but mock you, then you looked at him, tears naturally welling up in your eyes, but you gazed at him with tear-filled eyes.

Tenna's antenna was hanging down, and you just shook it slightly, almost like a rogue as you said, "I don't care, you have to protect me! If I die mysteriously, it will definitely be your fault!" ”

After saying that, you start to feel a bit guilty. After all, you know what you're saying is completely nonsense, completely pointless. If you're prepared, Tenna might still be able to mock you again.

But to your surprise, Tenna was silent for a moment, then softly said, "Alright." ”

You widened your eyes in shock.
