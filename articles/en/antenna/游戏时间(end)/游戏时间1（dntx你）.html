Game Time 1 (dntx you)

Maybe there's OOC, dntx you. By the way, I was really so angry I cried while writing......

——

"Oh dear...... So boring...... You lie on the sofa complaining, swinging your legs casually, then bury your head in the sofa.

Of course—no one cares about you, or rather, the Dark People don't care about you. After all, you know what kind of person Anton Tenna is in this world. If she allowed you to stay here and cause trouble, that's more or less. Although you don't even know why he allowed you to stay here, it's probably because the Dark Folk are naturally caring for the Light Ones.

You didn't care about any of that—since no one answered you, you simply looked up, turned over, and sat back down on the sofa. Then you immediately saw Tenna sitting on the leather chair on the other side, the screen pitch black, as if she hadn't noticed what you were doing at all.

You couldn't help but feel speechless, walking over with your hands on your hips and looking down at him—well, that's not possible, since he's only about as tall as you when he's sitting down. In fact, you need to lift your head slightly to see him clearly.

You stand on tiptoe, a bit annoyed, trying to grab his antenna, but the next second his screen lights up, and the two antennas shake and slip away from your hand.

He looks at you expressionlessly: "What do you want to do?" ”

You saw his expression shrink slightly. Whether you said it or not, you were still a little afraid of him—after all, you knew he wasn't easy to talk to, but you still straightened your back and stared straight at his screen—after all, you couldn't see his eyes.

"Hmm, ahem, because it's too boring, I've decided to requisition your TV screen for gaming! After all, besides your TV, there's no other TV in your lounge that can be used." You put your hands on your hips and announced, even though you didn't know why, despite the room being meant for rest and entertainment, there was nothing else except the sofa—oh, and there's this one you instinctively found a bit annoying.

"What game?" His voice was cold and indifferent, and you couldn't help but take two steps back—after all, you started to regret coming to him directly. If you'd known, you should have looked for a TV somewhere else first. Anyway, there must be other TVs with big screens in this world, so why face such a ...... A guy you can't even quite put your finger on.

You pull out those few game CDs on you, hmm...... A fighting game, a two-player game, no need for this, you can't find anyone to play with you. A two-player survival game...... It's probably a drifting adventure on the sea, kind of like Life of Pi...... No, single-player is a bit too difficult, you can't play it. Two-player cooperative puzzle game...... Actually, you're quite tempted, but you sneak a glance at the guy in front of you and finally snort coldly in your heart, not asking if he wants to play games with you! He's really annoying; who knows what he'd say if we played together......

In the end, you chose a single-player adventure game—a simple Japanese RPG, a classic Dragon Quest story. After searching for a long time, you finally found a game disc you thought was quite interesting, then raised it high, your eyes sparkling: "This one!" "I want to play this!" Here, here you go. "You hand it to Tenna, looking at him expectantly.

Actually, Tenna had already noticed your sneaky glance earlier—he really wanted to say something more, but held back. After all, he knew that what he said was probably something you didn't want to hear, and for now, he didn't want you to leave here angry.

Tenna took the disc. I don't know exactly how he operated it, but he handed you a game console, then sat down opposite you on the sofa. He held the TV head and started projecting the game screen to you, while you happily sat cross-legged on the sofa and pressed buttons to start your adventure story.

Although there is a lot of textual storyline, this game still tests puzzle-solving thinking and skill, since it's somewhat of a 2D platforming puzzle-solving adventure game, and there are quite a few small monsters to clear along the way.

You seriously solve the puzzles along the way; those levels are a piece of cake for you—but in terms of combat controls, it's unclear whether your reflexes are slow or ...... The combat system in this game isn't very well designed. After all, you don't feel any impact at all, and the controls feel a bit sluggish. You even suspect it's a bit of a transmission issue, since pressing a button usually delays a little before the character actually acts.

Once again, the character dies. After the familiar GameOver CG, you purse your lips but don't lose heart. Instead, you regroup and decide to do it again. After so many times, you have to remember the boss's move moves and what you should do next.

But—while you were focused on attacking the boss, you heard Tenna's voice, casual and tinged with mockery: "...... Looks like you're bad at fighting games, no real skill. ”

He just watched you die several times, and even he knows how to get through it.

You choked for a moment, distracted by what he said, your hand instinctively trembled. The character who could have perfectly dodged the boss's finishing blow and landed the final blow shifted, landing in an inaccurate spot, and was killed by the boss's strafing fire. The gameover CG reappeared on the screen.

You angrily slammed the controller onto the sofa: "Yeah, I know I'm terrible. I've never been good at combat—but, but I was about to clear it just now, and it was you who suddenly said something that distracted me!" You started to blame him. After all, you were very self-aware, and you knew you were bad at playing. But as long as you enjoyed the game, that was enough. You didn't mind trying a few more times to clear it.

But you were originally quite interested, but after hearing that one sentence, your enthusiasm suddenly faded. Tenna just snorted coldly: "I've been watching all along, and your gaming skills really aren't great." ”

You glared at Tenna with red eyes—well, you don't even know if he actually saw it. You just raised your voice and said, "Bad at it, that's bad! If I try a few more times, I'll clear it. If you keep talking......"

After saying that, you still press the confirm button and decide to restart and challenge the boss again. But for some reason, after this restart, the screen actually starts to garbage, black-and-white noise appears on the TV screen, and even—intermittently and suddenly goes black. You try to control your character forward, triggering the boss again, but this time, because of the garbled screen and black screen, the character dies quickly.

Accompanied by the sound effect of death, Tenna's slightly mocking voice said, "I thought after so many times, you would remember it completely." ”

“…… The boss's jumping and moving positions are different! Even if I remember his attack order, I can't always guess where he moves! "You said angrily, feeling like your rage was almost full, but you still held back and started another round. This time, the screen was completely guttered. You didn't know if he was deliberately going against you. If he hadn't finished this boss and cleared the game, your intuition would have been that he wouldn't hand over the game disc so easily. You'd have already run the entire dark world looking for other TVs."

You puff out your cheeks, holding your breath as you control your character forward. Indeed, you've fought many times, so you have memorized the controls and map—you cleared that boss with a garbled screen and no visible scenario.

Tenna also raised her voice, speaking in a slightly surprised tone: "Oh? Isn't this something you can do?" ”

“…… Yeah, but if I hadn't died so many times before, how could I have done it! "You dropped the game console. Anyway, you've cleared this game, and you don't plan to play anymore. Besides, you just wanted to pass the time, but now you've been holding in all your anger, so you're completely fed up and not bored at all.

You feel like you're about to cry from anger—but in the end, you just step forward and pound the TV's head hard to vent your anger, your eyes red. You don't want to cry in front of him anymore, but you still have some shame: "Broken TV!! I'm not playing with you anymore, I'll go find another TV to use. I refuse to believe there are no other televisions in this TV world!" ”

You looked at the decorative screen in front of you, seemingly getting even more imposing, and you didn't care what else he would say—in short, it was probably something unpleasant. Anyway, you rushed out the door—yes, you pulled down the lounge door too, and you wanted Tenna to sit there and enjoy the cold wind!

But after running a short distance, you stop, panting, sniff twice, wipe the tears streaming down your eyes, and sob twice. You feel wronged, but you also know that if you really tell Tenna, at most he'll just apologize stiffly. But—but you don't want that. If things end up unpleasant, why not just do it well from the start? If you end up going our separate ways, then not meeting at first is probably the best choice—at least that's what you think.

You don't want a rigid apology, you don't expect that kind of apology—but if he comes after you to apologize, it's not that you can't forgive him for now.

You just ...... You just want to give him another chance. Even though you always tell yourself that this is the last time, there's nothing you can do—who told you not to really be ruthless with him? Who told you to ...... to like this guy? You don't even know why you like him; maybe this is just the origin of unpredictable love.

So your steps slowed down more and more, curling up in a corner and quietly crying. You felt a bit awkward, hoping Tenna would catch up and find you, but also wishing he never came back, so you could tell yourself that it really was time to give up.

Until you sneak around this corner, sneaking around and spotting a deep blue figure.
