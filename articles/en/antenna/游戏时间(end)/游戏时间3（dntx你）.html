Game Time 3 (dntx you)

You glared angrily at Tenna sitting there, then banged his TV screen twice.

“…… Let me out!" "You don't want to make any more excuses, so just be honest. Anyway, you're not in the mood to play games right now...... Even if he's already marked the path for you, you still don't want to keep playing.

Tenna really did turn back into a pitch-black screen. He stood up, looking down at you, and you instinctively took two steps back, thinking you really didn't need to be afraid—after all, control of this TV was still in your hands! So you pouted and looked at him with your head held high.

"Then I'll go with you." His voice was hoarse, a bit cold, and deliberate.

You can't help but feel a bit scared, since such a big TV is following behind you, but soon, you can't care about those things anymore. You run to the kitchen not far away and grab a box of fresh yogurt, while Tenna follows behind you at a leisurely pace. His footsteps are especially clear, making it impossible for you to run quickly or shake him off, because he always manages to keep up with you.

You slurped your yogurt and tried several twists and turns, finally reaching the entrance where you once came from the world of light. Although the door was tightly shut, you still happily stepped forward and pulled the doorknob—the door was locked and wouldn't open.

You pause for a moment, and Tenna steps forward, standing in front of you to block the door.

“…… No, you can't go back." He thought for a moment, but finally spoke so firmly. To be honest, Tenna had thought about quite a lot. At this moment, he should have begged the Lightfolk to keep watching him, to beg the Lightfolk to stay here and immerse themselves in the fun of the game. He could no longer tolerate losing—but he didn't want to do it either, so all he could do now was look down at you and say those cold, cold words.

You glared at him angrily: "Why should I! Why do you make decisions for me on your own...... I—when I get back, I'm going to replace your broken TV!" This statement sounded a bit uncertain, since you didn't know when you'd finally return to the world of light, to go home—even though you were the only one at home, that was a place where all your sense of security existed, a place where you could feel at ease. As for family...... You no longer had that kind of thing, ever since they wanted to abandon you for your brother.

Tenna stepped forward, closing in on you two steps. You instinctively swallowed a couple of mouthfuls, feeling a bit guilty, and stepped back two paces, but still looked at him with a stubborn attitude.

His voice seemed a bit urgent—maybe it was just your imagination, your fantasy, since you were nervous right now.

“…… Change the TV? Do other TVs really know your preferences so well? He finished speaking rapidly, then fell silent again. You don't know why the screen stayed black, or why those two antennas seemed to have just dropped for a moment before quickly rising again.

"Anyway, you can gradually learn about your preferences. I usually play those games often, so I'll know if I switch to another TV." You muttered softly, your voice growing quieter and quieter, because for some reason, he was bent over. The pitch-black screen leaned close to you, and you couldn't help but feel scared.

"Do other TV shows understand your hand fetish?" For some reason, you find his voice a bit eerie, eeri.

"So what...... If you lose, you'll just start over again. At least you won't be like you, saying a few words to me just because you can't beat me!" You glared at him twice, then covered your mouth. You know you've been speaking quite boldly lately—after all, this scary-looking TV wasn't trying to stop you.

"You only want a silent TV?" For some reason, after saying this, he fell silent as well.

"Right...... That's right! I want to play the game properly, I don't want to be disturbed—oh, even if it's a TV person! "You muster up the courage and shout loudly, closing your eyes.

“…… You don't need to return to the world of light. "After the unsettling silence, you only hear these words, then you feel yourself being lifted up, and when you open your eyes to look, you find yourself sitting on Tenna's arm.

"Put me down! It's not like I can't walk on my own!" You tried hard to kick your legs twice, but it didn't help, while Tenna just talked to herself.

"If you go back, you'll be alone too. Why not stay here...... Don't you think that's great?" It sounded like a question, but he just spoke in Chen Dataset's tone, very certain. Probably because he's certain you'll stay here.

"So what...... I'm doing fine on my own. "You muttered quietly, not knowing why he was walking so fast, but once again returned to the lounge and put you down on the sofa. He handed you the game controller, then sat at the door again, propping up his head on the TV and reopening the game screen.

You threw that box of yogurt on the ground during your struggle. You felt a bit regretful you hadn't finished it, so you smacked your lips, propped your chin up and boredly controlled your game character to reach your destination. After pondering for a while, you finally deduced the truth behind the incident and successfully solved the case.

You set the next side quest as a tracking item, and immediately a navigation path appeared before your eyes. Only then did you realize that Tenna had already marked the destination markers on the map. You cleared your throat twice, wanting to thank him, but...... He was so quiet it was almost eerie, making you hesitant to speak.

Although it was a bit bumpy, you eventually reached your destination. This case was relatively simple, you solved it quickly, and then it seemed to trigger the main quest—well, the marked points still appeared on the map.

I have to say, even though you're bored and playing games, time still flies by—it's just that you're not used to it. After all, Tenna has been quiet for so long that you can't help but ask, "Why did you suddenly stop talking?" ”

"This is your request." For some reason, you somehow sensed a hint of sarcasm in that sentence.

“…… Anyway, thank you." You cleared your throat twice and touched your nose. Although it was indeed your request, suddenly losing your voice made you feel a bit lonely.

After all, in this dark world, no one plays with you. Except for this TV—only this one. The other Dark People somehow run away as soon as they see you.

"Oh." He answered you very quickly in a short voice.

You tapped your controller; the game screen was still loading. Next up was a cutscene CG, but you didn't want to watch. You hesitated for a moment and asked, "But in the dark world, I'm also alone......"

Just being honest, or maybe you're just making things up.

You hear Tenna snort coldly, half mocking: "Are you ignoring something that exists?" ”

“…… Yes, yes, you are here, but days like this are no different from when I am in the Light World. "Because there's nothing to do, you play games every day in the Light World. Anyway...... Let's talk about the future later. When you were in the Light World, you were clearly facing this TV every day too.

Oh, you just said in a fit of anger that you want to replace this TV when you get back.

"Oh, so what are you talking to me now?" His voice returned to a cold, rigid tone, and you started to doubt yourself, because you strangely felt he seemed angry.

“…… Didn't say anything. I just found it a bit strange that you hadn't spoken for a long time. "You whispered, watching as the main storyline animation finally finished, you began to control the character to move.

But Tenna suddenly closed the game screen, and what appeared immediately was that face that had never been happy, with a downward curve at the corner of her mouth.

"Because it's bedtime now." He said as he stepped forward, took the game console from your hand, and then pushed you into a room. This room had been your residence recently—very simple, with no decorations, except for a poster of Anton Tenna stuck on the wall.

But today, when you lay down on the bed, for some reason you found a TV plushie on the bed—just a black screen, with a bent wire wrapped in tape, exactly like the TV in your Light World. Looking around, you realized the room had become exactly the same as your bedroom in the Light World.

…… When did he do it? He's been with you all along...... Could it be when you ran out of anger earlier? You don't know, you just hugged the stuffed animal in your arms tightly—by the way, if you sleep holding the TV at night, would it be radiated? But originally, it was just a furry stuffed animal, how could that be? Unless you have a plush allergy.

Thinking this, you can't help but yawn, rub your nose, and quickly crawl under the covers. Alright then...... Better get a good sleep first.

You closed your eyes, but couldn't help but smile. Indeed, perhaps the world of the dark would be warmer, since it felt more like home, much better than the empty houses in the world of light.

But you can't give up! After all, staying in the dark world all the time is nothing!

In a daze, you drift off to sleep—in your dream, you're sitting on the sofa playing a game, seemingly in the dark world. You must be playing some game that requires skill. Tenna is still holding his head on the TV, seemingly mocking you a bit. You start arguing with him, get so angry you try to run out through the lounge door.

He wrapped his arms around the waist and lifted you up, the screen pitch black, and said slowly, "I know you love me." ”

"But you know—"

Before you could finish the last half sentence, you woke up from the dream, gasping for breath, thinking how he could possibly know what you were thinking. Uneasy, you turned over and fell back asleep again.
