You paused, not understanding why he would say this at this moment—this was completely out of place! Honestly, you don't understand.

So you were silent for a while, but just tapped the dark screen twice with your hand, then asked, "Why do you like me?" "Even though you know you love him or something...... It's completely for no reason.

"Then why do you love me?" He didn't answer your question, but simply asked in return.

You're a bit stuck. To be honest...... You really can't give a specific reason. After all, you can't tolerate his words either, can't tolerate his occasional behavior, or rather...... You can't fully tolerate him.

But if you want to find a reason, it seems you can find some. You think hard and honestly answer: "Hmm...... Because you've always been with me? Because you quietly help me decorate the room? Because...... Well, I don't know why, I just like you. ”

You scratch your head irritably, and it's precisely because of this affection that you subconsciously treat him a little better.

Tenna slightly lifted her posture. You could finally sit up a little straighter.

"No reason. That's the answer I'm giving you." He snorted coldly. If he had to give a reason...... There should indeed be some of them, but he didn't want to tell you. Some things, he just needs to know himself.

You blinked, pointed at yourself, then at Tenna: "...... So you chose to tell me now...... I—can I pretend nothing ever happened? "That way, everything will develop exactly as before.

Habit is a scary thing. At least, now you seem to be somewhat used to what Tenna says, and a bit used to losing your temper when dissatisfied—because you find Tenna doesn't do anything about it, so you've become bolder.

Tenna just shook his head, then pressed down on your face and bent down to bite your lips. His large tongue scraped along your lips, wet, entering your mouth, filling your entire mouth, making only some stammering sounds as he pushed you, tears welling up involuntarily.

You think—you didn't agree either! How did you suddenly start doing intimate things that only men and women can do? You propped up your arms, trying hard to push him away, but unfortunately, for Tenna, your strength was still a bit too soft.

Fortunately, he finally let go of your mouth, but the screen was still pitch black, and he didn't look very happy.

You swallowed twice and didn't dare say anything more, just stared at Tenna in fear.

But he spoke slowly: "...... Of course not, I want you to agree." At this moment, his words carried a hint of assertiveness.

"What if I don't agree?" Maybe you wanted to try it out, and you couldn't help but ask.

Tenna watched you in silence for a moment, then let out a cold laugh and leaned in close again. His voice suddenly became soft, so soft it was almost eerie.

"You'll agree, okay?" Even the words entering your ears carried a haze and dizziness, making it hard to read. Your mind seemed a bit dazed, and in a daze, you seemed to see some dizzying pattern appearing on the TV screen in front of you—you quickly shook your head to clear your head, hoping not to lose your mind at this moment.

“…… But I don't want to agree. After all, you—you're a broken TV! "As you spoke, you reached out and tapped Tenna's TV casing again.

Although you may forgive him, that doesn't mean you can forget them after hearing those words.

So you start counting on your fingers: "For example...... I remember you've already said 36 bad things to me! You've blocked or rejected my requests 9 times, and then, ...... You crushed me 12 games in the game! That one I don't know but felt uncomfortable about was 1 ......."

He listened quietly. In fact, he knows your characteristics...... That's why he wanted to hypnotize you just now, because after that, you'll probably completely forgive him.

But at this moment, he simply sat back down beside him and spoke casually: "If I don't say something, you won't remember at all." ”

Because...... When he praises you or treats you kindly, you can hardly remember them; instead, when you're hurt, you always remember them. So he hopes you can remember him forever, even if in this unpleasant way.

You choke up, thinking he really is telling the truth, after all...... Happiness is a fleeting thing; only pain will make you remember it forever.

You lower your head a little dejectedly: "But I don't want to think of you anymore, and all I think about is pain." Even if ...... Even if the impression of happiness isn't deep, you still vaguely remember what has happened.

"Hmm, then tell me, how many happy things did you remember before?"

You opened your mouth, then closed it again. Yes, you really don't have any happy memories...... But that clearly doesn't matter.

"Then let's work backwards...... The most recent thing was that I found you helped me tidy up my room, and the layout was exactly the same as my old bedroom. "You took a deep breath and said.

Tenna nodded, "And then?" ”

Then...... Then you can't remember. Only then do you realize—or rather, you have to admit—that in your life up to now, you don't have many happy memories.

Going further back, probably...... You happily sit at home, playing games in front of this TV. But you quickly become unhappy, because your parents, who promised to come home, have broken their promises just like countless times before and haven't come back, just letting you stay here. You know, after all, the sister who has always stayed by their side to raise is the most important thing to them.

So you fell silent, then weakly spoke: "...... I... I don't want to say it anymore." "Happiness always comes with pain, and you don't want to open your own wounds."

“…… If you can promise that the memories you'll bring me from now on will be joyful memories, and that I'll never suffer anymore, then I'll agree to your request." You took a deep breath, finally looked up at him, and said your request. Maybe this was the first time you'd ever spoken your request so seriously.

This time, Tenna was silent. He couldn't guarantee you'd always have only happy memories—he knew very well what he might and was destined to bring you.

So he just said stiffly, "I can't guarantee it. Some happiness is mixed with pain. ”

“…… Hmm, I understand. Then when can you promise me? I'll promise you then." You gave a bitter smile, after all, you knew this would be the ending. My heart seemed a bit sour, but it made me beat violently, probably because of it.

You don't know...... Is love always like this—sweet yet mixed with pain? If so, what is the so-called love you seek from him?

Tenna looked down at you, probably pondering the same thing. His screen lit up, as if ready to say something he promised, but before he could speak, you covered his mouth with both hands: "Don't tell me right now, please." Otherwise, you won't believe it.

Then...... You felt your wet, slightly cool tongue lick across your palm, your eyes widened, and you quickly withdrew your hand.

"You, you...... Rogue TV! "You don't even know what to say. Good manners make you mentally unable to curse, so you just bangbang the TV's casing a couple of times."

He didn't answer you, just said, "I'm tired." ”

His screen was off, and the entire TV crew looked completely drained. His heavy body suddenly leaned against you, nearly pinning you down on the sofa.

You glared at this baffling guy, not knowing why he suddenly acted like this, so you just muttered, "If you're tired, go back to your room and rest...... What's the point of falling here? I'm not your bed......"

He didn't seem to have been woken up by your voice, or maybe even if he did, there was no response, because you can't tell if he's awake or not, and you don't even know if the Dark People actually need to sleep.

But leaving it alone isn't a good idea. After all, you feel your shoulders are being pressed down and sore. In the end, you still carefully hold his head, move your body to the side, then rest the head of the TV on your own lap so he can lie down and rest.

At least, he probably didn't disturb him...... That's what you think.

But Tenna actually hasn't fallen asleep at all. With outsiders around, it's hard for him to fall asleep. He naturally senses your movements, knows he's resting on your lap, and he silently enjoys the moment.

You puff out your cheeks, hesitate for a moment, and finally carefully reach out to touch the TV's head—oh my god, he's forcing himself not to let his screen light up suddenly, he's going to pull back every flower that would have appeared at his nose.

You pick up the remote and switch the TV in front of you to a movie channel. You turn off the sound and watch silent movies, yawning—probably to get used to Tenna's state, but before you know it, the lights in the lounge have also been turned off, leaving only the flickering screen.

You leaned back on the sofa watching the movie and couldn't help but yawn again. This time, you fell asleep too.
