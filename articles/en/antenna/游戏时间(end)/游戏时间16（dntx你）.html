Game Time 16 (dntx you)

You seem to be walking through the corridors of memory.

"Today, it's just me at home again...... You sat cross-legged on the wooden floor, mumbling as you fiddled with the game console in your hands. Grandma left early in the morning, saying she was going to the market and wanted to sell more eggs from the hen she laid yesterday to earn some money. Your family doesn't have much money; the little money is mostly spent on buying things for you. Grandma always thinks about earning more money to help you live a better life. To avoid disappointing her, you always work hard to get better grades. You want to move to a big city, live with Grandma, and bring this second-hand old TV and that second-hand game console over, because they've always been with you.

Muttering to yourself, you turn on the TV. There aren't many games—most are game discs that grandma picked up from trash, unwanted ones, or you rented from the county town. But even with these few games, you're already having a great time.

You open a game you haven't played before, this game...... Playing alone isn't very fun, so you guess you haven't opened it until you have no one to play with you. In the end, you have no choice but to open the game alone.

This game basically involves entering an abandoned factory to collect items that can be exchanged for money and other resources, while also avoiding the monsters inside. Honestly, entering such factories is indeed quite scary, and you heard in the county town that some of the ghosts there are quite scary.

But you still took a deep breath, mustered up your courage, controlled your character, and started the game, stepping into the factory alone. Honestly, pitch black is pretty scary, but luckily you found a flashlight that made it easier to see what's ahead. After some picking, you managed to find some things, like surprise boxes thrown around or old batteries...... You didn't know how such things could be here, but you still huffed and took them back and put them on the spaceship.

You looked at the value of these items and hesitated—should you escape now while there's no danger, or seek fortune by risk—go again? Seeing that it was already afternoon, you hesitated for a moment, but in the end, you grabbed the shovel you had just bought for self-defense and then got off the spaceship.

Trembling, you enter the abandoned factory again. At first, there was nothing, or rather, it was empty—after all, you had already taken out something valuable. Then you continued deeper until the character's movements noticeably slowed. Your flashlight shone down and saw a ...... Transparent, or rather green, writhing thing, seemingly called a slime. Instinctively, you opened your mouth wide, wanting to shout out but lost your voice. You instinctively stepped into the Slime King's interior. But beyond that was a dead end. You tried to circle back the way you came, but only to find it was a vicious loop. No matter what you did, you couldn't get out; it seemed you were lost.

You puff out your cheeks, tears quickly welling up in your eyes. You just sniff, not giving up completely, still trying to get out, but...... It's all in vain. The flashlight is out of battery, and even a shovel can't kill the slime. You're trapped in this place and can't get out. In the end, you can only put down the game controller and watch in vain as the slime wraps around your character, while your character collapses to the ground, dead.

No one restarts the ship, and no one brings back your corpse, so...... You can only restart the game, and this game counts as your loss.

You sniff, trying to comfort yourself that it was all okay—after all, this was only the first round, and you just needed to start one more round. But the next second, you noticed your perspective changed to another character, who pressed a button, transported your corpse back to the ship, and started the ship, which meant you could proceed to the second level.

But...... Wait, where did this person come from? You widened your eyes, your body trembling uncontrollably. Could it be haunted? You couldn't help but ask, "Who... who are you?" Your voice was still trembling.

Maybe it's just your imagination. You seem to see the TV's antenna slightly shake, and when you rub your eyes and look again, the antenna remains the same. Instead, the TV screen goes black, then a mosaic flashes, and then the screen lights up with characters of varying sizes, like characters taken from various news reports and other programs. You barely piece together the words the TV shows you: "anton...... tenna？ ”

You seem to see the TV's antenna trembling slightly. If it's a ghost, then you're really quite scared. After all, you've heard plenty of stories, and you definitely don't want to be involved with ghosts. Otherwise, what would Grandma do if she was left here all alone? But if it were just this TV...... Although the phenomenon was a bit strange, you could still accept it, since the TV had been with you for a long time—at least that's what you think.

"So...... Are you the TV you have? "But to be safe, you still asked a bit more. What if it's just a haunted trick trying to trick you? Even though if the potion is sincere about tricking you, you probably have no way to do it......

The TV screen was black-and-white mosaic again, and then a big hook pattern flashed on the screen. Anyway, you breathed a sigh of relief and were quite excited—your TV was conscious! Of course, you knew this couldn't be told to others, but since it was a little secret between you and your family—you had to admit, you still felt a bit nervous.

So you ask again, "Can you play games with me?" This time, the TV just redraws the matching hook. You excitedly shout 'yes,' and then the TV screen switches back—it's the same game as before. The difference is you have a teammate, but they probably won't talk to you.

You still chose the factory from earlier. You still have some resentment toward the slime that trapped you. This time, Tenna was the one who got off the ship with you, but you entered the factory directly from the entrance, while he detoured outside the factory and entered through another secure entrance.

You excitedly start licking the room—no, it's just collecting supplies. This time, you find the same surprise box as last time, an abandoned TV, and a countdown that starts counting down as soon as you pick it up...... It shows something called a bomb.

Then, before you even realize it, there was a loud boom—your character was blown to death.

You widened your eyes, staring blankly at your fallen body and the changing camera angles, quickly shouting, "Tenna!" Tenna！ "Hurry back to the spaceship, I'm already dead. You can't die anymore......"

He also carried a few things down and returned to the spaceship. It looked like a toy dog and a battery box. Then he finally started the ship and flew away. His corpse was summoned back by him, and it was time to choose the next location.

After a glance, you still feel a lingering fear, and decide not to choose a factory again. Instead, you choose another place with a higher mortality rate and resource rates—an abandoned villa.

When you arrive, you excitedly rush down: "Alright, I don't believe there's anything in this room that can stop me!" You grab the shovel and run off excitedly. Tenna's character follows you calmly into the villa.

Hmm...... Gift boxes, discarded cards, battery boxes, and even an abandoned TV here...... You muttered, but in the end, you struggled to carry the valuable abandoned TV outside. Then, you heard a clicking sound from the first floor of the villa, like some kind of wood twisting. You went out to take a look and saw it was a soldier patrolling there—apparently called the Nutcracker. You were startled and instinctively stood there, but the Nutcracker aimed its gun at you. Before you could react, you saw Tenna already behind the Nutcracker, swung a shovel a few times, stunned him, and knocked him to the ground, ending your crisis.

You exhale, beaming with joy: "Thank you, tenna! I thought I was going to die there...... Good thing you're here! ”

The screen didn't switch, only showing lines of character by word after a long time. I don't know if it was some kind of good word pieced together from some news report: "It's okay." This is what I should do. ”

“…… Then you have to keep protecting me all the time. I really don't want to die for no reason anymore." You said coquettishly. While you were there, you ran out with Tenna and threw the things back on the spaceship. You don't want to die and still have nothing to retrieve, making a wasted trip.

The TV was quiet, with only the hum of the heat. You might think it wouldn't answer you, but in the end, a line of text slowly popped up on the screen: "Mm, I will always protect you, forever." ”

Forever.

You don't know, you just gave a silly smile to this TV: "Yeah, it's a deal!" Pinky swear, hanging for a hundred years, no change—wait, you can't pinky that. "You stuck out your tongue, a little embarrassed.

At that moment, your ship started up again, and the TV screen went pitch black again. Then, a grin flashed across the screen, leaving you a bit stunned. Instinctively, you leaned over and gently patted Tenna's shell: "Stop laughing, it's so scary!" ”

So the TV put away that smile, leaving only the game screen, and the antenna seemed to bend down a bit...... Maybe it felt a bit wronged?

Either way, you remember that was the first real conversation between you and your home TV.
