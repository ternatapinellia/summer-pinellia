I don't even know why they're constantly chasing you to kill...... Is Tenna's gaming skill really that high?

"No." Of course, all he got in return was a cold reply.

With no other choice, you switch to another character to play this fighting game, but every time you lose completely. You sniff, because you've been losing all the time, and tears welled up in your eyes. You really felt wronged—why? This wasn't a game, it was clearly playing you.

“…… I don't want to play anymore. Why are you always the one winning? W-bad TV!" You tossed the game controller aside and sobbed as you said this. You had originally planned to repay Tenna when you left this morning, but now—you just want to get back at him. Why does he keep winning? Isn't this really unfair to you? Even though you know your skills aren't great—why is he always crushing you? You barely have a chance to fight back!

You sobbed, wiping tears from the corners of your eyes, wanting to go back to your own room, at least your stuffed toy wouldn't bully you. But just a second before you got up, you felt something cold, glass-like pressed against your cheek, followed by a warm touch—you opened your eyes and realized it was Anton Tenna pressing close to your face, kissing the tear stains on your cheek.

“…… I know you love me. "He spoke softly, yet in a voice you could hear clearly.
