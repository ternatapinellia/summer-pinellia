Tenna didn't respond. You glanced at him again and found he was still the same size as before. He clearly claimed to be good at any game, yet he was still so afraid of horror games...... You pursed your lips and had no choice but to lower your voice: "Alright, how about we redo this level?" ”

Tenna still didn't make a sound, but you noticed out of the corner of your eye. He quietly nodded, so you took a deep breath, chose the same level, and then entered. Well, this time you learned from last time and knew exactly what kind of ghost was inside this house. So you grabbed your flashlight and the items needed to seal the ghosts and rushed in.

At the end, you reminded Tenna, "Just stay in the car, protect yourself, and don't go down." With that, you rushed out of the car, arrived at the pitch-black house, pushed open the front door, gritted your teeth, turned on the front hall light, then placed the candle on the table, manipulated the character to draw a formation, and rushed to the bedroom to grab a random piece of clothing to burn. But just as you rushed out, the front hall light went out again, leaving only the candlelight faintly burning.

Tenna had reverted to his original size. He controlled the character, looking at the dashboard and said with a hint of schadenfreude, "Ah, the ghost just passed by you. Your HP dropped by 20%." ”

You pursed your lips, and now he was as mean as he always was. I really didn't know what was going on with this guy, but...... After all, he was the one you liked. Even though you were a bit annoyed by hearing that now, you just controlled the character to burn that piece of clothing, then recited the spell as needed to seal the ghost.

You won this round. You excitedly grabbed Tenna, who was about to drop the controller, and started the next round: "Don't leave yet, let's clear this round too!" ”

Tenna was almost forced to start this round of the game with you. Once again, he kept his mouth tightly shut and said nothing, so quiet that it felt strange to you. And this time, you couldn't just leave Tenna alone to run into ghosts. As for going alone or something—that was even more impossible!

So you nudged Tenna with your elbow: "Let's go into the room together this round. We'll walk together, so we won't be so scared, right?" As you spoke, your character picked up the camera and flashlight, while Tenna's character picked up another flashlight.

You took a deep breath and got out of the car. After a few steps, you realized Tenna hadn't followed, so you turned back: "What are you standing here for? Come down together." ”

"I think you can clear the level alone, so why bring me along?" His tone was half mocking. Of course, if you think he did it on purpose, he was just...... He just didn't want to run into ghosts. This is the first time you've noticed Tenna is so afraid of ghosts. Next time, just pretend to be one and scare him!

Your character pushes and pushes back and forth, and finally manages to fill his role. You just say as if it's only natural: "Of course the second place should go with the first. Otherwise, if the second place gets left out, what if they can't catch up to the first place?" ”

You hear Tenna seem to sneer, then simply say, "Alright." ”

He finally manages to control the character to move forward, but the speed is very slow. Your character circles around his character, and in the end, with no other choice, you still stand behind him, pushing the character forward: "You should be in second place, you should be standing at the front!" ”

"Why should I?" He was extremely stingy with words, but you actually felt he was just scared, because it seemed like his body had shrunk a little more.

"Because you're in second place! If the second place doesn't steal the credit, they'll never beat the first!" You still said with confidence.

But his character still moves forward slowly. You try hard to use your modeling to push him forward, and it took a long time to get him to the door. You're so patient, since you don't know how much time you've wasted on this trip. Honestly, you're a bit tired and don't really want to play games anymore.

"Alright, open the door. Last round, you successfully entered the room and searched the room, didn't you? This round won't be a problem either!" You encouraged Tenna, and he finally reluctantly controlled the character to open the door, though he still refused to go inside.

Honestly, you're a bit angry at this point. You were supposed to play games with you, but he ended up like this. You might as well call another Dark Civilian to be your playmate.

So you dropped the controller, got up angrily, and tried to leave: "Looks like you don't want to play games with me anymore. Then I'll call other Dark People to join me." "Anyway, you can't just refuse to cooperate like this."

But you had barely taken two steps before Tenna grabbed your sleeve and dragged you into her arms, her voice cold: "Don't look for other Dark People." ”

"Then why don't you just play your game properly? You even said you wanted to play with me? Look at how much you've gotten into it!" You turned around and poked Tenna with your fingers, but Tenna just lowered her head, staring at you in the darkest light.

His voice was still emotionless: "...... Alright, I'll finish this round seriously." He finally started running around with his game character, quickly turning on the lights in the room. At some point, he even brought a portable thermometer to measure the temperature in each room. Finally, he controlled the character to run to a small bedroom and said dryly, "The ghost is in this room, the thermometer is malfunctioning." Then he placed you on the sofa next to him.

Only then did you notice he had turned into a tiny ball, about the size of a pet dog, and the trembling in his voice became even more obvious. Helpless, you had no choice but to hold the little tenna in your arms, then tightly shut the room with ghosts, hanging a cross at the door to prevent this type of ghost from escaping. Then you lit a candle at the door, drew a formation, and chanted incantations as you sent the ghost away.

The screen showed you had won. You tossed down the game controller and stretched: "Hmm—finally cleared it!" I'm tired. I'll keep playing later. "You said matter-of-factly, since you can't do anything else right now except play games. You also disdainfully tossed Tenna, who had just finished playing and was slowly growing bigger and heavier.

"Hmm, then what do you plan to do next?" His voice was quite calm, so unfamiliar that it didn't even feel like Tenna, and when you looked up, all you could see was ...... His face still wore that cold, indifferent expression, as if nothing had happened.

You were silent for a moment. Even though you knew you shouldn't make this proposal, you wanted to ...... You couldn't just stay ignorant like this. You couldn't just stay in this state, constantly avoiding past realities. Escaping is shameful but useful, but...... Just a little while, just a little while. You know that only when you stop running away can you truly grow up.

So you take a deep breath and calmly say, "I want to return to the World of Light." ”

Then, you hear the machine rattling, and you wonder if you're mistaken, but then you realize it's Tenna making the sound. You watch the TV as your head slowly turns around, then lowers your head to face you.

“…… Isn't it better to stay here forever? Only by staying here will you truly stay happy forever." he said. His screen was pitch black, and you can confidently hear a faint tremor in his voice.

"Of course it's good, but...... I can't deny or abandon my past self, nor can I abandon the old you. I want to go back...... Find the traces you once left behind, I want to reclaim all the memories. I shouldn't get lost in this. So...... Tenna, trust me, okay? Because I love you, and I want to love you even more, that's why I want to do this. "As you spoke, you lowered your eyebrows, your voice trembling a little.

You really think so, but this is also the first time you've admitted that this matter really exists. Before that, you never dared to admit it, because you were afraid that Tenna wasn't as you wished, because you felt timid, uneasy, and uneasy about this relationship. You couldn't confirm whether this twisted, anxious, and sometimes aggrieved emotion was still love.

But you also feel joy and excitement about this, and feel peace and happiness beside him, so you think, perhaps that's how things are. After all...... Before that, there were no more people in your household.

Tenna remained silent. He knew you probably wanted to find some evidence to prove that you two truly loved each other, but...... In the current house, there probably isn't much more evidence left. The original house...... Has long since gathered dust in my memory.

But in the end, he still chose to give in, and he also felt hopeful for it. If ...... Even this house now bears traces of love, it means you have always loved each other. He was moved because he could never be sure if you still loved him after that fire.

So he relented: "...... Alright, you can go back, but only for one day." His voice was icy cold. Even though you knew what he said was completely useless, since Tenna couldn't come to the Light World to catch you—he was just a TV. Still, you smiled and gave him an OK gesture. You wouldn't break your promise.

"Yeah, I know. I'll definitely be back within a day." Saying this, you still feel a bit nervous, since you don't know what will happen after you get back.

And Tenna watches you step through the gateway to the world of light.
