Game Time 9 (dntx you)

You are placed at the end of the table by Tenna, just looking at him in frustration.

“…… Why don't you just ignore me? Who told you to mind me, who told you to care about my thoughts, who told you to ...... "Who told you to love me?" You wanted to say this, but when you opened your mouth, you couldn't get it out. You just awkwardly closed your mouth, lowered your head, and stared blankly at the steaming hot food in front of you.

Tenna sat across from you, handed you the bowl and chopsticks, and said coldly, a bit annoyed, "Yeah, who told me to love you?" ”

He could say that out loud...... Because you probably wouldn't care, but after hearing his words, your body just shrank back, and you still showed no other reaction.

…… See, you really don't care. Even if he says it so confidently, he doesn't know if you truly feel that way, if you truly love him, or if you ...... You still care? He sneered inwardly, as if mocking, but deep down, there was still a bit of grievance he couldn't put into words.

“…… Why do you love me?" You whispered, unconsciously poking the plate with your chopsticks—this was your habit when daydreaming. Tenna just used her other pair of chopsticks to serve you food, and soon, your bowl was piled high with food, as big as a small mountain, full and full.

Looking at this bowl of food, you can't help but get a little distracted, feeling ...... It seems that a long, long time ago, someone would have done this for you, but who exactly was it? No matter what, you just can't remember.

And Tenna just said, "I don't know. Are you satisfied with this answer?" ”

His voice was still icy cold, like dark clouds hanging over you. You didn't dare look up at him and just mumbled, "...... That's not really a reason, right? So do you have a reason?" ”

Maybe...... Maybe love doesn't need a reason, just as you love Tenna, it seems you don't need a reason either. Even though you never directly admitted it, nor agreed to it. You just...... Still staying here.

Because it seems you have nowhere else to go besides this.

"So if you love me, is there a reason for that?" he said.

You thought for a moment, put down your chopsticks, and started counting on your fingers: "I think there is. You'll always be with me. Even if it makes me angry, it's reluctant, and I can forgive you. Also...... Hmm, the food you make looks really delicious...... But aside from that, you couldn't find any other reason, so you timidly looked up at Tenna.

But—but why did he subconsciously say that Tenna would always be with him? You clearly felt that you only saw Anton Tenna after entering the Dark World, clearly ...... He remembered that there really was such a TV in the Light World, but it seemed only in the past two years.

"Is that a reason?" Tenna asked.

You were silent for a moment: "...... Maybe that's not really a reason." "Love seems to be something that doesn't need a reason, and the so-called reason is just evidence you find to convince you that you love him, or that you discover he loves you."

Maybe, in fact...... That's not really that important.

You pick up a slice of garlic pork, dip it in sauce, and put it in your mouth. It's a familiar taste...... You seem to have tasted this sauce for a long time. Why do you feel like crying the moment you taste it? That flavor makes you nostalgic, as if recalling the taste that was once beautiful and warm, as if ...... Being able to let go of all pretenses and throw yourself into this warm embrace, letting go of all sadness and sensitivity.

You bit your lip, tears uncontrollably streaming down your cheeks. You sniffed, staring at the screen that was lit up, but still wearing a sour face waiting for your evaluation. Tenna cautiously said, "This smell...... Could it be that you're my mom? ”

As soon as you say it, you regret it. How could a TV person be a human mother? What are you thinking! You even want to punch yourself so you can lose your memory from earlier.

Tenna couldn't help but sneer, "What do you mean I'm your mom?" ”

He just made the whole table of dishes the way you used to love, and didn't expect any kind of comment—even though he was indeed looking forward to it. But he never expected to hear you say that!

"No, it's just, this smell is very familiar......" It's as if, long, long ago, this was the very scent that had accompanied you...... As you spoke, you picked up another bite of eggplant and put it in your mouth. Indeed, it tasted exactly like the one you remembered, but no matter how you thought about it, you couldn't retrieve even a tiny bit from that foggy deep sea of memories.

"I can't possibly be your mother." He said, unusually calm, his tone still fairly calm—uh, of course, you can't ignore the suppressed anger beneath his words, seemingly ...... And a bit aggrieved.

You fell silent. But no matter how much you searched your memories, there was nothing more to say. You only remembered that in these years, the TV was still with you at home.

So...... "Did we know each other before?" "You think about this question, but memories can't give you the answer."

"Does it matter whether you know him or not?" Tenna just asked you back.

Maybe it's important, maybe not. In fact, one of the reasons you want to return to the world of light is that you believe that once you go back, you can recover those memories from the past. In some way, you always have this feeling.

You slowly gnaw on the vegetables piled up like a small mountain, pondering for a moment, then nodding and shaking your head: "Yes, memories of the past...... will be important. But the present and future matters are also very important. ”

If you are someone with no past, could you just be a hollow person?

"I... I want to recover my past memories. No, I absolutely must recover my past memories, so I must return to the Luminal World once more." After quickly finishing your meal, you put down your chopsticks, looked directly at Tenna, and mustered the courage to speak. This time, you have truly made up your mind; you want to reclaim your memories.

Whether painful or beautiful, that is your past. If forgetting them is just a temporary escape... then now you should remember, you should face them.

Tenna stared at you, and even if you couldn't see his eyes, you could feel his burning gaze fixed on you.

His tone was heavy, tinged with some disdain: "Even if the memories you recover include things that caused you unbearable pain, things you would rather forget?"

You hesitated for a moment, but ultimately nodded firmly: "Yes. If it truly is very painful... then I cannot forgive the perpetrators. But I must put these things down and move forward." At the end, you let out a soft laugh.

Choosing not to forgive is okay too.

"Even if... you’ve once experienced happiness, only to eventually lose it?" He said these abrupt words, and you nodded and shook your head at the same time.

"Yes... joyous memories cannot last forever; a brief moment of happiness is enough to savor for a lifetime," you said softly with your head lowered and a smile.

But Tenna only stared at the top of your head. He wanted to question you: what about him? What about the old TV you once left behind in your memories if you move forward? What about the old TV that has always been with you if you forgive those past events? If... you are willing to accept your current family, were his words about being the only family left to you a complete lie?

He wanted to question you, to stop you at any cost, to stand between you and the truth. But... perhaps deep down, he was still unwilling to do so.

So in the end, he only took a deep breath: "...If you really want to know the truth, then come with me. Of course, I don't think relying on your fragile psychological endurance, you can fully accept that truth." He couldn’t resist mocking you again. Perhaps, with this sentence, it could make you hesitate for a moment, delaying your pursuit of the truth.

You just puffed up your cheeks and, pouting, poked him on the back: "No way, I'm not as fragile as you say — and, and! Even if I can’t accept it right away, maybe after some time, I will."

After all, time always washes away sorrow and grievances, even though there will still be sadness and grievances, more and more memories will eventually fill in the old pain.

"You really are fragile," Tenna repeated, saying little else. He walked ahead quickly, and you hurried to catch up, nearly unable to keep up with him. But in the end, he suddenly stopped. You, rushing forward, collided into his back with a thump, and physiological tears welled up in your eyes. You covered your forehead and looked at Tenna turning around with grievance: "It hurts..."

He let out a cold snort: "Endure the pain. Are you blind? Don’t you realize we’ve arrived?"

"How would I know? You didn’t say anything. Should I just guess what you’re thinking?" You glared at him angrily.

Tenna said nothing, turning away, and dryly said: "Next time I’ll tell you."

Then he pushed the door open and walked inside. You recognized it as the room you had come out of not long ago. You took a deep breath and followed. Tenna was standing in front of the photo wall, his back to you.
