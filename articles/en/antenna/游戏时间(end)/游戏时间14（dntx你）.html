Game Time 14 (dntx you)

You can't help but get excited: "Finally, we've reached the final level—once we clear it, this game is completely over!" ”

The healer's character stands in front of you, taking a massive damage attack from the boss, leaving only a health skin hanging there. Tenna seems to be casually asking this question: "So after this game is over, what are we supposed to play?" ”

You focus your attention on attacking the boss while casually replying, "What are you playing?" Hmm...... For duo, I have some action or puzzle games, but I still prefer to play horror games! "After all, you're curious how Tenna would perform in a horror game.

"Alright, I understand." He didn't say much, just answered you concisely. Although you were a bit puzzled, in this situation, you didn't have the right to ask questions, because BOOS's attacks were coming at you in full swing. You fled in a panic, your health bar dropping but only to be pulled back up, all while trying every possible way to attack. Unfortunately, BOOS was really tough, and you spent a long time in this level.

Finally, BOOS takes the final blow, wailing and roaring as he collapses. You wipe the unwarranted sweat from your forehead and excitedly raise your hand to high-five Tenna: "Yay, we finally cleared the level!" ”

After saying that, you raised your hand, and Tenna did the same, but his hand covered yours and didn't high-five you. You stared at him in shock, pouted, and said a bit aggrievedly, "Why didn't you high-five...... We went through so much trouble to clear it! ”

"Yes, but most of it is thanks to me, right?" After this sentence, the damage and other damage settlement panels were displayed for you. Indeed, because you really like assassins, and Tenna didn't deal a single bit of damage, your damage share on the panel shows 100%. In contrast, both damage absorption and healing are 100% for Tenna—well, some of the damage absorbed also hits you. You know that without Tenna, you might not even get past the first two stages.

You can only clear your throat and cough twice, then look up: "So what! Anyway, I'm the one dealing damage, I'm really strong!" ”

Tenna just nodded perfunctorily: "Mm-hmm, you're amazing, you're strong, you're the best." ”

Even though you could tell he was just brushing you off, you were still happy. Your eyes curved as you grabbed his hand and shook it side to side. Your small hand tried hard to hold his: "Mm, I'm the best, so Tenna is the second best!" "You speak with such confidence, as if the whole world is made up only of you." Tenna's screen was still pitch black. You felt him turn his head to glance at you, then turn his head back. His screen lit up again, his voice a bit teasing: "Alright, then the world's number one, the strongest Y/N, will surely protect me, the fragile second place, right?" ”

You're stuck. Honestly, of course you can't guarantee it! But soon, you cleared your throat and put your hands on your hips, saying, "How can I protect you? The second place should be protecting the first place!" After all, the second place is strong too, but the reason the first place is the first place is simply because she doesn't want to work hard! ”

For some reason, you always feel Tenna giving you a half-smile. You touch your nose, feeling a bit guilty. After all, these things are just your nonsense. You never intended to convince Tenna; these are just twisted logic.

For some reason, you always felt his expression was a bit smiling, half-smirk, and rather creepy. Anyway, you couldn't help but shiver, but you still stubbornly refused, patting your chest with your other hand: "But if the second place is that weak, then I, the first place, can protect you either!" "From now on, just follow behind me and watch me show off my skills!" Saying that, you happily opened the next game, which was a horror game.

Tenna simply said softly, "Mm, you must protect me. I'll be watching closely." ”

Honestly, you're still afraid of the dark, so after buying this game, you never played it once. Today, thanks to Tenna here, you finally reopened the game. Soon, you chose the simplest level and entered together with Tenna's controlled character. The car had various dashboards that let you see data from another character entering different rooms and check their safety. You immediately decided, "Go down and check that haunted house. I'll watch the data here for you." ”

"Why aren't you the one going?" You all know that cars with their lights on are absolutely safe zones; as long as you stay here, you won't encounter any danger.

And you just say seriously, "Because I'm first, I have to stay here and see how things go. I'm working hard too!" I need to analyze based on data, and you just need to risk going into the haunted house. Besides, isn't it only natural for second place to work for first? ”

"Then the first place should be taking the risk, right?" His voice carried a hint of sarcasm.

You shook your head seriously, waving your fingers and said, "No, no, no. First, if we go on a risky trip and something goes wrong, what then? I'll stay here and see how things develop. You go downstairs!" ”

Of course, this was just an excuse because you were number one and didn't have to work. You didn't believe it anyway. You just didn't want to go down and encounter ghosts; you're very afraid of the dark.

Tenna turns to look at you, then finally gets out of the car with the camera and flashlight, entering the haunted house. You carefully look at the dashboard, confirm the data, and dutifully start announcing: "The temperature and humidity here are normal, no ghosts. You can explore this room with the lights on as usual." ”

For some reason, you seem to hear the sound of your teeth chattering, but when you turn your head and look around, you can't find the source of the sound, and the strange sound has disappeared. You have no choice but to give up and continue directing Tenna: "Keep moving forward. There is no abnormal data on the dashboard yet. You can explore normally, just turn on the lights." ”

When the room was brightly lit, the eerie grinding of teeth stopped. You suspected Tenna was making that sound, but since you had no proof, you could only turn around in confusion and glance at him again. You wouldn't know until you looked, but when you did, you were shocked. You guessed that somehow, or at some point, Tenna's body had actually shrunk! Just now, you had to tilt your head up to see him, but now you only need to tilt your head slightly to see him clearly. You blinked, a bit stunned, your mouth agape, and then Tenna's cold voice came through: "What are you looking at?" "Look at your game screen carefully. I don't want to be inexplicably dead just after entering." ”

You let out a couple of 'ohs', scolding yourself in your heart, determined never to look at Tenna in a daze again. Then you shifted your attention back and stared intently at the screen. The dashboard had been normal before, but now it started fluctuating wildly, with the temperature fluctuating wildly. You shouted urgently, "Ah, the ghost has passed by!" The data on the dashboard has moved! "Just as you finished speaking, a sudden click came from the house. The game screen went dark, and the entire room where Tenna was was lost power. After a brief adjustment, you could barely see your surroundings, and the dashboard returned to normal."

Although you haven't played this game before, you still have a rough idea of the process: "That ghost should have already left, and the power in the room should have tripped. You can go to the basement and push the switch back up." ”

But when you finished speaking, Tenna didn't move for a long time. You turned to look at Tenna in confusion, only to find his screen was still pitch black. The difference was that his figure seemed to be slowly shrinking. Gradually, you could see Tenna clearly just by turning your head. You nudged him with your elbow: "Why aren't you moving? If you stand here and don't move, that ghost might come looking for you soon." ”

Unfortunately, he still stood there, not moving. And his body kept shrinking, not saying a word. You figured he was definitely afraid of horror games, but you couldn't just leave him there, so you gritted your teeth, casually guessed the type of ghost in the house, then grabbed a candle and flashlight and rushed into the dark room.

Your teeth were chattering uncontrollably. Now you could be sure that the grinding sound you heard earlier was coming from Tenna. You lit the candle and burned out the only piece of clothing you had just found in the wardrobe with the candlelight. Then, following the notes in your notebook, you drew a formation, placing the candle in the center. You prepared to perform a ritual for the ghost, though you didn't know if your approach was right or wrong, and you were very nervous.

But just as you set up the formation, a cold wind blew by and extinguished the candle. You had no choice but to try every possible way to relight the candle with matches, but no matter what, there was no flame. Not only that, another cold wind howled by, your screen turned pitch black, followed by a loud crash. It looked like your character had died under the ghost's interference.

Although I still felt shyness and lingering fear, when I turned around and saw Tenna had already tossed the handle aside, now only about the size of a pet dog, I couldn't help but laugh a little.

You couldn't help but tease, "I didn't expect you to be so afraid of the dark. Didn't you say before that you're good at any game?" ”

Unfortunately, before he could answer and you hadn't said anything more, Tenna's game screen went black. With another bang, he fell to the ground, followed by the payment screen.

You can't help but let out a sharp crackling sound: "No—I was almost clearing the level—it's clearly the kind of ghost I guessed!" ”
