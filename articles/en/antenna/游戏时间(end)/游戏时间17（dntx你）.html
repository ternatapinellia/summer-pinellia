Game Time 17 (dntx you)

Since then...... You always take advantage of grandma being away to talk to the TV by yourself. After all, you don't dare talk when someone else is around—what if they think you're crazy? But when you're bored, you still turn on the TV and play games with Tenna. He becomes your silent companion, and all of this feels like a secret between you two.

However...... It still happens occasionally

After all, even though you're a bit weak, you love playing and really enjoy puzzle games. Many single-player puzzle games are played by just one person. You struggle to control the console and spin the screen, but no matter how hard you try, you can't find a real way to clear the level. At this point, Tenna seems to be unable to take it anymore. Somehow, he picked out a red circle and placed it in the spot where you couldn't find the right spot after searching for ages.

You were a bit unhappy, and grandma wasn't home either, so you pouted and spoke up: "Why do you need to hint me!" I just wanted to think it over myself, and only when I figured out the answer would I be the happiest...... If you hint me now, wouldn't that ruin everything! ”

The TV screen went black, then turned bright white, struggling to type one character after another: "But...... I think you should need help? If you can't see it all along...... I think I should have hinted you instead. ”

At this moment, Tenna spoke in a polite tone, but you pouted, threw away the game controller, and started rolling on the floor throwing a tantrum: "I don't care, I don't care, I don't care, I don't like it anyway, it's all your fault! Why did you suddenly give me a hint? I never asked you for it!" ”

The TV was silent, only the hum of the cooling was heard. You wondered if you'd gone too far, and feeling guilty, you straightened up and was about to apologize, only to notice its antenna was bent. The screen was trying hard to type a line as fast as possible: "...... Sorry, next time I'll wait until you ask me." ”

You blinked, not expecting him to say that, and couldn't help but feel even more justified. You nodded as if it were only natural: "Yes, that's right! Don't give me a hint before I asked, or it makes me look silly......" You whispered, "Tenna might have heard that, or maybe she didn't. You're not sure."

This little episode passed quickly. You continued playing the game with great enthusiasm, eager to know how the game would end. But after smoothly clearing the room, you got stuck again. Holding the prop in your hand, you didn't know what to do for a moment. You clicked on each object carefully and tried to observe it, but still couldn't find the right direction. The game had no prompts, and the TV was quiet, just like an ordinary object.

You're a bit annoyed and can't help but start pointing and pointing again: "Tenna, why didn't you give me any hints? I just searched for ages and still couldn't find the answer......

The TV screen flashed for a moment, then slowly typed a line: "But...... You just told me I didn't need to remind you? ”

You cleared your throat twice, realizing you did say that earlier, and changing it now would seem a bit shameless—but you don't care! Anyway, this is your TV, so of course you need to know your preferences!

So you pursed your lips again: "I don't care, you can tell, right? I'm not in the mood to study this now, so you should give me a hint, right?" Just now...... Hmm, I was still interested in continuing to study that puzzle, so you shouldn't have given me a hint! ”

You spoke so confidently, but the TV probably didn't know how to respond. For a while, no new text appeared on the screen, almost making you anxious enough to go back up and film the TV again, until it slowly pieced together a yes.

So you nod in satisfaction, but still pat the TV with your hand: "Yeah, I knew it—you're my best friend!" Hmm...... Probably the only friend? ”

The TV's antenna stood upright again, slowly typing some characters on the screen, finally piecing together into one word: Forever?

And you definitely nodded: "Forever!" Hmm...... Speaking of which, "forever friends" is a bit strange, isn't it? I've never had friends...... But as long as we can always be together, that's enough, right? I roughly know—friends, family, lovers, all that...... As long as we can always be together, that's enough. "As you spoke, your eyes curved with a smile and you reached out both hands to give the TV a hug. You really don't know how to distinguish these words, nor how to properly express this kind of feeling.

But you know, at least you feel joy for it, and that's enough.

As for everything else—you don't care at all! No matter what your identity is, you'll always be together, right?

You shout that TV should be your bride because you hear other classmates say that if you get married, you'll be closer and spend your whole life together. You think you'll be together for life...... But you're curious—you really want to be closer, which is why you say that.

In short, you always like to act mischievous with Tenna, venting all the childish charm you've hidden from being mature onto him. You're happy, and Tenna seems happy too—well, you can't really tell the mood on TV, but every time you play around with him, you always lean over and kiss his screen.

Grandma occasionally kisses your cheek, so you copy the example and kiss Tenna too. But every time you kiss him and touch his screen, you'll find it gets even hotter.

Over time, Tenna became skilled at reading your expressions. He didn't even need you to say a word to know roughly what your next move would be, and skillfully displayed the prompts you needed on the screen.

Occasionally, when rushing homework and encountering problems you don't understand, you pester Tenna to show the solving process on the screen, assuming he can solve these childish problems.

…… Then, at that moment, a line of text will appear on his screen, blankly asking: "Why don't you solve it yourself?" ”

You confidently put your hands on your hips and said, "Because I don't know how!" ”

Ahem, although you say this is just child's play, it's precisely because you can't solve it that you ask Tenna!

The TV fell silent for a moment. You stared nervously at the screen, and then, Tenna, somehow managed to extract those symbols, actually displayed the problem-solving process on the screen. You scanned it and immediately understood, so you quickly wrote it down.

"I knew you could solve problems! Next time I come across a question I can't handle...... I'll come to ask you directly! "You said happily, writing down the answers to the last few fill-in-the-blank questions. Honestly, it's actually okay to take it over and ask your other classmates, but for you...... Building good relationships with others is really hard. You don't really want to talk to others, but just sitting here talking with Tenna is a very comfortable thing.

Sometimes you wonder if it's just your imagination, but when you look at the problems that can only be solved under Tenna's guidance, you firmly believe they are not hallucinations.

You originally thought that such a relaxed and joyful day, with a bit of monotony, would keep repeating.

Until that day.

Grandma...... Suddenly, that day you collapsed. When you cryed and rushed your grandmother to the hospital, you found she had already passed away. The hospital also sadly notified your family that your loved one had passed away.

You carried the small urn home, crying until you fainted. When you woke up again, a strange couple came to your door, saying they were your parents and wanted to take you back. It turned out your grandmother knew she didn't have much time left, so she went to the place where she found you, asked around for a long time, and finally found your biological parents. She handed you over to them.

You hold the TV, unwilling to leave alone with them, insisting on bringing it with you.

“…… A broken TV? What's the use of this thing? "Your mother raised her eyebrows high, looking surprised.

"This is my only family...... you sniffled and cried.

"Family?" "Is this the only thing?" Are you ......?" Your mother hesitated, your heart sinking. You thought, I really did think you were crazy.

Your father tried to smooth things over: "Forget it, if the child likes it, just take it home. It's just a TV after all. The child doesn't have many companions here, and after going back, he'll be fine for a while." ”

In the end, you still bring the TV back with you and solemnly put it in your room. You still chat with Tenna, but you have to be very careful when you do, after all...... You definitely don't want others in the family to find out.

But no matter how careful you are, you can't stop the child's curiosity. Parents usually go out to work, and you relax a bit while chatting with Tenna. After transferring to this school, you still don't have many friends, and even after entering high school, it's the same—you seem to be isolated without leaving a trace. Thin threads don't matter, as long as Tenna is with you.

You hugged your knees and sat on the floor, watching the door warily to make sure that little sister wasn't eavesdropping at the door before daring to talk to Tenna, quietly complaining about her recent life: "...... Honestly, the recent physics test is way too hard. I can't even answer the last two questions......

After a moment, the TV screen slowly typed a line: "So you admit it. Did you make yourself stupid before?" ”

“…… I didn't admit it! Besides, my liberal arts grades are always very good, so maybe I'm ...... Just not really suited for calculations or anything...... You blushed and denied it, then your voice grew quieter and quieter.

But suddenly, a voice came from the doorway, filled with surprise: "Wow, sister is actually talking to the TV!" ”

Your pupils contracted slightly. You instinctively stood up to stop her, but she had already run to the open-door door and threw herself into mother's arms: "Sister is talking to the TV she brought back!" ”
