Before he could finish, your father interrupted him. He frowned, "Enough, forget about whether you have it or not. Don't you know how to pretend better?" ”

Your face turned pale as you stared in disbelief at your father and mother, while they simply gathered around your sister and started caring for her, slamming the door shut with a heavy thrust. You slid slowly to the floor as if exhausted, kneeling on the ground, your knees aching, but you couldn't care about that—after all, the grievances and pain in your heart were far more painful than your physical pain.

You don't know why your parents do this, you don't understand, only the TV, still keeping a bit of warmth behind you, lets your cold body draw some strength from it.

Gradually, you shift into sitting on the ground with your hands hugging your knees, chatting idly with the TV: "I...... Are you really sick? ”

Tenna was very excited, typing several big words on the TV screen quickly: "No, you didn't!! ”

The room was very hot; you could almost see waves of heat flowing, but there was no fire burning inside—it looked like it was burning outside. It looked like there was a raging fire outside the door, and you had no chance of escaping from this room. As the carbon dioxide increased, your vision darkened. You could only hold the still-cold Tenna to try to cool down, but amid the thick black smoke, you still lost consciousness.

Gradually, the TV also quieted down, no longer making any noise.

Then you slightly bent down and hugged the TV.

“…… Tenna, long time no see." Your voice choked with emotion. You sniffled, wanting to cry, but mostly guilt. So you had ignored Tenna for so long, missed so many words from him in the past, but you felt his antenna gently touch you, then you lowered your hand and looked at his screen.

“…… Just a minute ago, we parted ways. Did your memory have trouble again? ”

"I didn't, I just ...... Talking about myself at fifteen, and with you, long time no see. ”

“…… Long time no see. So, are you here to fulfill your promise back then? ”

This is a mismatched bride TV, a wedding that is also out of place. After all—this isn't a proper marriage, nor a serious audience, only you two take it seriously.

You cleared your throat and simply went ahead to deliver a piece of completely made-up wedding vows: "Whether in health or in sickness, whether in wealth or in poverty, whether in happiness or in sorrow, I will always be by Tenna's side. I vow to present all the beauty and joy to you and me. I vow to cherish this relationship, whether it be friendship, love, or family affection. And you just nodded, because you had known this truth for a long time, and you smiled as you said: "I love you too."

Then you touched the TV's antenna, finally reconciling all your inner knots, and walked briskly toward the entrance of the dark world. It was time to go back—it was time to see the true other side of your lover.

With this thought, you quickened your pace and ran through the gate of the dark world, then fell into an embrace.

You looked up to see it was Anton Tenna, still with a tense, expressionless face, and you noticed that his left ring finger was wearing that somewhat large ring. You thought these plain rings were probably bought by your fifteen-year-old self during a youthful crush—ah, indeed they were, because back then you were nervously planning how exactly you would marry the television.

You just smiled and tiptoed. Tenna still looked unhappy, but he obediently lowered his head. You put the veil on his head, looking a bit mismatched.

You just smiled, lifting your eyebrows: "Tenna... how about we have another wedding?" This time there would be an audience: all the employees of the TV world would be spectators at this wedding.

Tenna just silently nodded, staring down at you, then coughed twice. He lowered his head to bite your lips, lightly sucking twice before letting go, and hugged you tightly, as if you might run away in the blink of an eye.

You, on tiptoe, held the TV's head in both hands and kissed the screen, smiling: "Mm, I love you too."
