Honestly...... Actually, you don't really know why you agreed. The purple monster girl in town told you that a new psychiatrist had arrived in their castle town, and it might be useful to you. She strongly recommended you try telling it.

…… You don't know; you only know you have a serious mental illness. You have no interest in anything, as if everything in your eyes is black and white. You can't stand the noise of the city and eventually move to this small town. At least it's quieter here to help you relax a bit, but there is no suitable psychologist here...... Moreover, outside psychologists are powerless to treat you. No matter how they intervene in your mind, you can't muster even the slightest interest in the existence of this world, as if there's nothing worth remembering. You originally wanted to refuse, but after all, she meant well...... In the end, you were brought to Castle Town with complicated emotions.

But you never would have imagined that the new psychologist they mentioned...... It was actually a television?! It can't be that they're playing you a TV program to ease your psychological problems...... If that were the case, it would be no different from those so-called sandbox treatments or other things outside. You immediately wanted to get up and leave. After all, this was just a very simple room, and right now...... Although this was a pristine white office, it was only white enough. At least you could immediately see the person sitting there in a dark purple TV ...... Yes, even the clothes he wore weren't the usual white coat of a doctor, but a gray robe draped over his body, probably to cover up the fact that his legs were broken. At least from your perspective, he was still sitting in a wheelchair, and the place where his two legs were connected was just wrapped in gauze.

But you were only called out, a slightly hoarse, low voice—of course, it only sounded like that. You weren't sure what his real voice looked like, but you knew it wasn't. It was precisely because you could see through everything at once that you lost interest in the world. You felt that everything was so boring, dull, and uninteresting.

"You're Y/N? You're the patient today, aren't you?" He just said firmly, and since you've been called out, you have no choice but to turn around, okay...... At least let's talk first before leaving, you thought this and sat down at the seat across the table.

"Yes, Mr. TV man, I'm Y/N." You answered his question and took a look at him: the broken leg, the dark world, a TV—looks like this is the TV you mysteriously saw a couple of days ago that they sneaked to school. In theory, his dark world should have disappeared, so he was brought here to exist as an ordinary Dark Citizen. He wasn't wearing a white coat, and the room's layout was very casual...... He didn't truly want to be a psychologist; it was more likely he was just pushed over by those Light Citizens and temporarily took over.

It seemed there wasn't a psychologist to begin with; the room was newly cleaned...... So, why did you use the title of psychologist to see yourself? You looked at the TV person, giving him a serious assessment, and your tone was equally certain: "You wanted to see me, so you called me over...... Why use the title of psychologist? I don't remember seeing you, so why did you want to see me?" You were puzzled; you had no impression of this TV person.

Tenna snorted with a laugh. He hadn't planned to hide his intentions from you, but he really did want to be a proper psychologist—the reason was simple: he wanted you to live, he wanted you to still stand in front of him safely. He had indeed met you, once, back when he was at the Dream Chaser family.

He simply said, "Yes, at the Dream Chaser House." Of course, he didn't know if you still remembered it, but you recalled and realized you had indeed seen it back then...... Hmm, although you were just bored watching TV shows, no matter what it was, you could immediately guess the ending. It was really boring.

"Alright, then why do you want to see me?" You didn't hold back, just asked directly about this matter, and of course you were curious...... This was something you didn't expect, and you felt you shouldn't have any interaction with him.

"I am mr.(anton)tenna, and I have indeed come to be your psychologist. I suppose you have already seen many doctors, but to no avail. Perhaps my hypnosis will have some effect on you; of course, I cannot guarantee that it will be harmless for you." Perhaps because he no longer holds the status of a lord, his tone was considerably more gentle, though there was still a hint of superiority... enough to make you slightly frown.

A stranger from the Dark Tribe, a television—in theory, you shouldn't trust him...... But emotionally, your mind is urging you to make a decision, to agree. After all, this matter has sparked some interest, and you might think, maybe this is the best start to cure your illness.

You pondered for a moment, then placed your hands together on the table, and Tenna's hands rested on the table. He was much taller than you, just quietly looking down at you—he hadn't told you his full purpose. He found you interesting because you were the first person he had seen in so many years, so he wanted to get close to you...... Or rather, to have you, so he had ownership of you...... Although he didn't understand what unnecessary desire was, at least he knew his body wanted to get close to you. Tenna didn't stop his body's desire, so now he stood before you.

And you quickly made up your mind—you'll still take psychiatric medication, but coming for counseling every day for a period doesn't really matter to you. So you propped your chin and asked, "Alright, Mr. Tenna, from now on, I'll come for counseling every afternoon...... I hope the hypnotherapy you mentioned is effective. Of course, I'd like to know, what is your fee standard? "Anyway, you can afford it.

"I don't charge any extra fees." He simply said this—anyway, whatever he wants to collect, you will hand over to him during hypnosis, since the only fee he wants is you. Exploring you, demanding you, longing for you...... Tenna knew that this was the only desire he had now. He wanted to see other expressions on your face, as dull as stagnant water, and now, you indeed showed a look of surprise.

He doesn't want anything, which means if he wants something...... He'll take it himself, meaning maybe there's something he wants on you...... It doesn't matter, you think nothing matters to you, and you can give anything. It's okay, nothing matters.

You originally wanted to say more, but in the end, you just nodded to show you understood.

"Alright, then let's start today. Let me be clear: if I feel it doesn't work, I won't be coming back again." You cleared your throat, and Tenna nodded. Then, you saw his originally white, expressionless screen change...... On his screen, a black circle appeared that kept rotating and contracting, ring after ring, as if it was about to draw someone in. You stared at his screen for a while, letting your consciousness drift away, away from ...... Then, you lost consciousness.

Tenna watches you keep seeing circles in your eyes, but the light in your eyes has vanished, leaving you just staring blankly at him. Tenna put away the whirlpools on the screen, staring at you, clearly ...... At this moment, all you need to do is make some subconscious changes to you. Of course, this kind of thing can't be rushed; he'll just slowly lure you deeper inside.

"So, Y/N, what is your impression of this meeting?" He decided to start with some basic questions to get you to let go of your guard against him.

"It's trivial, it's not important." You simply answered him like that—yes, you really do feel none of this matters.

"So, Y/N, what impression do you have of me?"

"A somewhat strange TV guy who's been injured before. I don't know why he's interested in me. I need to be cautious." You honestly shared your thoughts, and Tenna, hearing all this, just let out a stifled laugh. Clearly, he didn't like your answer.

This time, he switched to a tone tinged with a hint of inducing emotion and said, "Then, Y/N, you need to think I'm a qualified therapist now...... You should be willing to tell me your true thoughts. Don't you think the doctor who provided you with treatment feels a bit close to you? ”

"I'm willing to tell the therapist any of my true thoughts......" Then, you stutter, even showing a somewhat confused expression...... Maybe your subconscious is thinking, Tenna grips the armrest, looking at you, ready to hypnotize you again at any moment. Then, your expression finally calms down again, "Yes, although you're strange...... But it seems like I feel a bit close? ”

"Yes, I'm someone who makes you feel a bit close...... Do you like the feeling you have now? "Tenna's tone still carried a hint of seduction, but this time, you nodded decisively.

"Yes, you make me feel a bit close...... Now I feel very calm. ”
