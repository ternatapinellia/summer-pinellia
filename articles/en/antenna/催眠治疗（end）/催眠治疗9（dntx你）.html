Hypnotherapy 9 (dntx you)

Drinking coffee, yet your thoughts differ, but you aren't really thinking about anything; you're just thinking about what tomorrow will bring. Tomorrow is a very interesting word for you—tomorrow, tomorrow. Maybe everyone looks forward to tomorrow, everyone imagines what tomorrow will be like, everyone thinks that maybe when tomorrow comes, something will come true, but you don't believe in it, or rather, it means nothing to you.

It's just ...... Maybe something unexpected will really happen tomorrow. You look at Tenna thinking that way, and you're curious about what else he'll do tomorrow—you're just curious about that.

So, after finishing this afternoon tea, you said goodbye to him. Your tone was light, though that was mostly just an act. The real you were just expressionless: "Well then, Mr. Tenna, see you tomorrow." You said this, stood up, and prepared to leave. Tenna opened his mouth, trying to persuade you to stay. Of course, at this moment, he couldn't find any excuse; he couldn't find a reason to keep you going—so in the end, he just watched you walk away unwillingly, away from the café. He watched your back, but you didn't even glance back at him. He could only watch your back until your figure disappeared from his sight. The other Dark People in the café became noisy again, making Tenna's mood terrible. The noise made him even more irritable, but he didn't say anything more. He just sat in the dark, slammed his coffee cup onto the table, and then drove his wheelchair out of the café himself. The other Dark People were startled by Tenna's sudden move, but they only dared to look at Tenna's figure and didn't dare say another word. They simply watched as Tenna left. Only after Tenna's figure disappeared from their sight did they dare to start talking loudly again. Tenna, too, just sullenly steering his wheelchair back to his room—or rather, the consultation room—even though you had already confirmed that that room wasn't the clinic, he wasn't a real psychologist, and he was just an impostor, nothing more. Yes, he's just a fake trying every possible way to get close to you. In his heart, he spat himself on himself—and felt a bit smug. At least he succeeded, didn't he? That's enough—it's much better than trying so hard but still unable to get close to you.

In short, no matter what you were thinking, the next day quietly arrived, and you visited that room again—the room where Tenna was, and once more the so-called psychologist. You were still doctor and patient, and maybe this would never change, but you sat across from him, just watching him, or maybe you even had a bit of anticipation—you were curious about what Tenna would do today, to be honest, he really did make you curious.

So you spoke with a hint of anticipation, "So, what's the treatment plan for today, doctor?" You sized up Tenna. He still looked the same as in the past two days, except now his legs were covered with a blanket, as if this could cover up the fact that his legs were broken.

Tenna just chuckled, took a candy from the nearby candy box, and handed it to you as usual. His voice was still stiff and cold, with a hint of awkwardness. Of course, you didn't really care about that—you just took the fruit candy, and Tenna's cold, hard voice echoed in your ear: "Today is still hypnotherapy, but I think you could relax with a candy first. I think you're really tense right now." That's how he commented. Of course, you're indeed tense right now—part of it is nervousness, since you don't know what else the hypnosis therapy will do today. And part, of course, you're excited—actually, you're still looking forward to what Tenna might do next, and you're really curious about what he'll do next.

“…… I understand, thank you, Mr. Tenna. Also, this candy is really delicious—I really like it." You nodded, giving your own evaluation. Tenna nodded back. Anyway, it seems the hypnosis I gave you yesterday did have some effect. At least his judgment now that you were truly sincere is true.

His voice remained cold and hard. Ever since you exposed him yesterday, he stopped pretending in front of you. No matter what, you'd see through him, and you might even mock him. If that's the case, why did he keep pretending?

"Alright, so does hypnotherapy start now?" Although it was a question, he spoke in a declarative tone, showing no sign of confusion. You tilted your head, and it seemed that no matter what you might answer, Tenna had already confirmed what he was going to do. Now he was going to hypnotize you, so you just nodded noncommittally.

Tenna's screen shifted again. At the top of his screen was a black-and-white rotating circle, swirling like a vortex, constantly drawing your attention. You stared at the spinning circles, and before you knew it, your consciousness seemed to drift and disappear again, as if you were in a dream. Tenna looked into your vacant eyes and knew the hypnosis had succeeded. He nodded in satisfaction, then leaned back against the back of the wheelchair, still in that commanding tone, simply commanding: "Alright, now, stand in front of me. I have a question for you." ”

You stood in front of him as he said, but you were standing while Tenna was sitting. Although Tenna was much taller than you, you were still looking down at him. This gesture annoyed Tenna a bit because he had to lift his head to properly look at you. He clicked his tongue in annoyance and continued issuing orders: "You should sit across from me. Standing there is really too much of an eyesore. I don't like that." Then, you have to keep looking at me the whole time. "As he spoke, he gestured for you to sit back down in the chair opposite you. You sat back down on that chair as he said, maintaining the posture of looking at him."

Tenna sized you up, and now he was looking down on you openly, which satisfied a certain feeling of his own. So he snorted softly, sounding like he was in a good mood. He then lay comfortably on the back of his wheelchair, gazing at you and leisurely saying, "How does it feel to go out with me?" He wanted to know your thoughts on what happened yesterday.

“…… Not really, or strictly speaking, whether you go out or not has nothing to do with you, because who you go with doesn't matter to me, I don't care." You didn't hesitate at all, just answered Tenna's question directly. After hearing your answer, Tenna's screen went black again. That wasn't the answer he wanted to hear—but he also knew that right now, he might not be anything to you.

He just kept asking, "That's not the answer I want. Besides that, do you think there's anything else?" ”

“…… It's interesting. I think your presence is interesting, so I think everything related to you will be interesting. But......" At this point, you pause again, as if you deliberately keep something hidden from the lips.

"But?" Tenna was just eagerly asking for the answer. He was eager to know what you hadn't finished saying, and he even felt a bit impatient.

"But I'm disappointed. I feel like going out with you or with others makes no difference—it just seems the same." Your tone was flat, and when Tenna heard this, her heart sank. I can't believe you felt this way...... He felt unwilling, so he insisted on doing something unexpected to show you—otherwise, would you be disappointed? How could that make sense? He can't possibly be like a comic artist trying to please you all the time, right?

"But it's just an ordinary feeling, which doesn't seem so bad, because your very existence is interesting." But you immediately added another sentence, which lifted Tenna's previously angry and somewhat gloomy mood again. He turned the screen back on and looked at you, this time asking a completely different question, the same one he was very curious about—"...... Yesterday I saw you feeling unwell, so I quieted down all the Dark People present. Do you know about this?" "That was also the question he asked in a fit of embarrassment and anger, not daring to smell it while you were conscious.

And your tone was very cheerful: "Well, of course I know. No matter what, I still have to thank you, Mr. Tenna." ”

"Since you know, do you just not care at all?"

"Of course, I don't care at all...... This matter doesn't matter to me. Whether they're noisy or quiet doesn't disturb me, at most it just affects my mood for a moment. And whatever you do, it's just your own choice—what does it have to do with me? ”
