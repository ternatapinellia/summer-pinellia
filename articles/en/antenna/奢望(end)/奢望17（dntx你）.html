Luxury Desire 17 (dntx you)

You two got divorced, so logically, everything should have ended—at least that's what Tenna believed. So he just pretended nothing had happened, and that everything was just as before, living as usual no matter what. But...... Tenna never went back to his old house; he just casually found a place to live near the company. He thought everything was ordinary, but...... When he got off work and returned to his current residence, he would instinctively glance up at the sofa, and only then did he realize that this was no longer his old house, and you were no longer there.

When he went to sleep, even though he had turned off the screen, he still instinctively reached out to scoop up the person beside him—of course, all that remained was a patch of icy air. He turned on the screen, then irritably closed it. He really didn't understand what was going on, but the emptiness in his arms only made him instinctively feel annoyed and anxious, which made the employees all anxious lately. After all, the boss's mood seemed even worse than before—of course, there were quite a few Tenna's 'poisonous Wei' who actually liked this way. But Tenna didn't really know what was upsetting her; she only vaguely realized that all of this seemed to be related to her as well.

After all, whenever he thought of you, remembered the smile that instinctively appeared on his flushed, drunken face that day...... Tenna, sitting in the office, couldn't help but smile a little, but then he looked at the cold home and quickly put away his smile.

…… He would always instinctively make an extra serving when preparing breakfast in the morning, then expressionlessly toss those things into the trash. He always remembered buying a bouquet of flowers when passing by a flower shop, but now there was no vase in the living room. The desserts he bought were always cut in half with a knife, then he silently stared at the pastry, finally eating it himself. Yet, for some reason, the originally sweet pastry had taken on a slightly bitter taste...... Or was it simply because his mechanical heart was aching?

Tenna didn't know that he had just decided to visit the Cyber World tomorrow after work. Of course, tomorrow happened to be a holiday. Of course, he planned to renegotiate cooperation with the Queen—not just to see you.

Although when he arrived in the cyber world, his gaze couldn't help but follow you as you stepped off the bus, and for a moment, he even forgot to lift his foot. And standing beside you was a pink Addison, probably talking to you. In short, the two of you should be having a good conversation—because Tenna saw it, and a smile appeared on your face, a pure, unmasked smile, and you were just looking at that Addison...... You didn't notice the other Dark People nearby, nor Tenna.

He genuinely felt jealousy—why could you smile at others like that, and he himself hadn't even seen it a few times? Why was it that after leaving him, you were still so happy, even smiling so recklessly? It shouldn't be like this, it shouldn't be like this—aren't your emotions his own, and also his? Tenna only felt a deep sense of unwillingness at this moment. He couldn't help but take a step, naturally ignoring the other Dark People around him, who were trembling at the sight of another deadlock lord. He simply strode toward you.

You naturally heard the commotion behind you, then glanced back—ah...... It was Tenna. You instinctively wanted to shrink back, but then you remembered—you were now in the cyber world, and you were no longer his wife. So you took a deep breath and gave Tenna a polite smile: "Mr. Tenna, welcome to the cyber world." After saying that, you turned your head, not wanting to pay attention to him for the time being. You originally ...... There was no need to keep being together; you were doing everything you could to forget him, trying your best to drive him out of your mind. But when you looked at him, the feeling of being suppressed by you surged in your heart, and now, in the chaos, it rose up again. You feel as if your heart is being squeezed, and you just quicken your pace and move forward.

Tenna watched your back, recalling your expression just now, recalling your words. That mechanical heart ached—why did it hurt? He only knew that his heart was beating because of you, joyfully beating faster because of you, and a desperate pain because of your indifference—he, my goodness, at that moment only quickened his pace to catch up with you. Tenna's body was much larger than yours, and his stride was naturally much larger. Before long, the pink Addison beside you had left early. To be honest, you didn't really want to face Tenna, but he stuck tightly by your side. Finally, you sighed, composed your expression, and gently asked, "Mr. Tenna, is there something you need from me?" ”

…… It's not like that, it shouldn't be like this. He doesn't want to hear your voice, but he can't deny that even if he only hears your voice right now, that mechanical heart that barely controlled by him is still screaming. Getting closer, hearing a bit more, that beating heart urging him to touch you, while Tenna just lowers her head, looking at you expressionlessly, her voice a bit dry: "...... I've never been to the Cyber World before. Can you show me around?" ”

"Then you should go find the guide, not me, Mr. Tenna." You politely declined him, but Tenna still stayed by your side, her voice still stiff.

“…… The only person I know in the cyber world is you, so you have to be my guide." His tone was a bit gentler than before, but still carried that self-righteous tone. You instinctively wanted to hear him again, but then you thought about it—you no longer had any connection with him, so why care about that—so you just smiled and shook your head.

"I'm sorry, Mr. Tenna, but I don't know how to be a tour guide at all. I suggest you go find someone else instead." You politely refused again, and then you stopped caring about him. You kept reminding yourself that the only person you should care about is yourself—what does that have to do with you? Even though you can't help but inquire about the TV world, what does that world really have to do with you? You force yourself not to think, not to care, not to recall everything there. You just unconsciously walk toward a nearby bookstore, and just a short while further is the shopping street. It's normal for a bookstore to be here.

Tenna didn't say anything more. He knew he was talking to you, and from now on, he probably wouldn't get a response from you either. He just quietly followed you beside you, watching you walk into the bookstore, so Tenna followed you in—the bookshelf wasn't that tall, but even if you stood on tiptoe, it was hard to reach the book on the top shelf. You bounced in place a couple of times, barely reaching the edge of the shelf. Just as you were about to grab a ladder nearby, a hand reached out and handed you the book you had been trying to take. You glanced over—it was Tenna. He silently maintained the posture of handing you the book, ignoring the hand reaching in front of you, just went straight to the ladder, climbed up, and took another book down.

Every bookshelf that followed looked like this...... You ignored Tenna, as if he didn't exist at all, just grabbed the books you wanted, went to check out on your own, and paid with your own dark coins.

Tenna lowered her head to look at the handful of books in her hand, and in the end, she just stuffed them back onto the shelf. He ...... He didn't understand. Clearly, this was more convenient, so why didn't you take the books in his hands, but instead went out to carry a ladder? Why did you pretend not to see him? He was very unwilling, but as you hurried out of the bookstore, he still followed behind you, and then walked side by side with you again. You were naturally tired carrying so many books, and Tenna simply reached out his hand to you, though his voice was still dry.

"These books are too heavy. Take them for me." He said this, which was only natural for him—the husband should be helping his wife carry things, but they were no longer husband and wife.

You just glanced at Tenna in surprise. You really didn't know why he came to the cyber world to find you, but you didn't mind—after all, it was his own business and had nothing to do with you. You just forced a smile and shook your head: "Thank you for your kindness, Mr. Tenna, but these books are mine. I have no reason to give them to an outsider." You deliberately emphasized the words "outsider," but Tenna acted as if she hadn't heard him, just persistently reaching out her hand toward you.

"Not an outsider. No matter what...... We all, at least have some connection. "He doesn't know how to refute you; he's never managed such a relationship, and he has no concept at all. Of course, he doesn't know...... Now that you're saying this, you're putting him in a position where even a friend can't be made. Indeed, you just want to stay as far away from him as possible...... You don't care what you two have left now.
