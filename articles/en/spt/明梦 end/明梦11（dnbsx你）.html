Bright Dream 11 (dnbsx you)

"If you pray to the Virgin Mary to protect yourself, it might still work...... The believers were still chattering at once. Spamton just stood quietly to the side, listening to their argument, not intending to interrupt—after all, he knew nothing about this underground church and needed to investigate thoroughly.

"What's the use? Didn't the Bishop say before that people who believe in religion tend to bring more sins?" Another Dark Folk lowered his voice and spoke urgently, sounding anxious, and speaking softly, as if he didn't want anyone to hear—like Spamton.

Spamton didn't react much, just a more obvious smile at the corner of his lips. He pretended not to hear, just waiting for the followers' reaction, while he just stood there pretending to be confused: "So...... What should I do next? The Bishop, my friend, told me that the Dark People with nothing to rely on are more suitable to join here...... He decided to take a gamble. After all, if the few Dark People who died before heard news about something like a sin or something like a possible death, then Duan would never join here, let alone leave a mark belonging to the followers.

The Dark People looked at each other, probably half-believing that Spamton hadn't heard anything. Only then did the leading Dark Folk beam and say, "Ah, that's right. Because the Dark Folk here are all very kind, homeless people who come here can treat this place as their own home...... After all, even the Dark Folk still need the warmth of family. I think any Dark Folk would agree with that......

Spamton nodded in agreement: "Of course, even someone as popular as me would agree with that...... Although we are just people of darkness, we also need care from those of darkness, and naturally, attention from them...... Hmm, if possible, it would be great if more and more of us noticed our people of darkness and light. " His tone sounded especially innocent, and he must have heard the gritted teeth of those people of darkness, probably saying that he still wasn't satisfied enough to satisfy that kind of ...... However, although the leader of the dark folk gritted his teeth, he still nodded in agreement.

"Yes, it's said that as long as we believe in the Saintess, she will listen to our wishes...... The Saintess will make our wishes come true, and it's said that this statue of the Saintess is once the incarnation of a Citizen of Light." As he spoke, he nodded devoutly and knelt down on the ground again...... It seemed he truly believed it.

Spamton thought, this case probably isn't that simple...... He wondered if these Dark People might be accomplices or something, which is why they were also involved in the case. Moreover, they weren't telling the truth; instead, they were trying to lure him into joining the church as well.

Although Spamton was thinking this...... He just knelt on the ground like a fellow believer who had been deceived, praying together with the other Dark People. Of course, he saw those Dark People whispering among themselves, then their faces lit up with joy—hmm, maybe he was glad he actually took the bait. In fact, Spamton had already guessed the possible culprit. Normally, the Dark People of Cyber City wouldn't dare make a move against him. After all, no matter what, he had won the Queen's favor and was still admired. No matter what, if he suddenly disappeared, the Queen would surely be furious.

So, anyone who dares to make a move on me...... Or they've been blinded by jealousy, just like the one who was caught at the previous banquet and stuck to the wall, and have framed me. Although the technique isn't clever, if he really succeeds...... That definitely wouldn't be a good thing. It would indeed cost him the queen's favor, and then he might not even be able to keep his current position. But this is absolutely unacceptable. No matter what, he must remain this popular figure. No matter what, he has an even bigger and swelling ambition. He wants the fame and status he currently holds to reach new heights. He wants ...... to become more popular, more noticed, bigger and more famous than he is now.

So, this was indeed one of the reasons he initially wanted to come to you—to become more famous through the People of Light. But now, this simple desire is mixed with indescribable emotions, because he is curious about you, because he likes being with you. No matter the reason, he likes you, the Citizen of Light. Likewise, no matter what, Spamton knows you also enjoy being with him...... Spamton knows very well that, although he doesn't know whether this ambiguous feeling should be called love, he really likes you, truly enjoys being with you, indeed...... He really likes being by your side, likes touching you, likes looking at you, likes everything about you, likes you.

So what does it matter whether it's love or not—after all, he really likes you, loves you, and that's enough.

Although these thoughts made him feel a bit distracted, in reality, he was still properly imitating the prayer lines of other Dark Folk reciting, while his mind was filled with other thoughts.

Getting back to the topic, he likes being with you, and that can help his career advance — of course, there are also people jealous enough to want to kill him, like the addison in the top shop. Spamton knows very well, so currently the only person he suspects is this addison. Other than that...... Maybe other addisons also want to make a move against him, but they haven't found the right opportunity yet.

You nervously watch everything happening inside the church. Honestly, you're worried that Spamton might be in danger...... Well, although it seems nothing happens inside this underground church, and you think it's better to worry about others than about Spamton getting hurt...... Even though you say that, you're still worried about Spamton. It's probably just a nonsensical thing, simply worried about your lover's safety.

It was at this moment that you subconsciously realized you were indeed very worried about Spamton, no matter the reason—even if it was just because he was your assistant, that worry had already gone beyond what was necessary...... And it was not just worry about the assistant; more importantly, it was indeed a concern for your lover.

You also start to zone out, lost in thought, while Spamton inside is also pondering a question—since you choose to join the church and choose this faith, that faith will surely leave a mark on you. It might look like a tattoo that looks inconspicuous but stands out here very noticeably. Maybe it's because the candlelight here is special.

…… He didn't want the tattoo, but to pretend that believers were hiding in the church, maybe he still needed it. Spamton began to think about how to keep the tattoo somewhere on his body without actually damaging his own skin.

Finally, after reciting the Lord's Prayer, he rolled up his sleeve, revealing a layer of white tight clothing, or rather, artificial skin, on his white arm below. After becoming a popular figure, Spamton often did this, because he wasn't sure if someone would suddenly target him or try to erase him. At least this fake skin could give him a chance to escape, although he never imagined it would come in handy—well, at least it was useful. He frankly stretched out his arm, and the Dark Citizen didn't notice, simply placing the pattern of faith on Spamton's arm. Everyone gathered around Spamton, congratulating him for joining the church, congratulating him for becoming a member, and for having a new home. You watched those people celebrate around Spamton for quite a while. Finally, Spamton shook them off, left the church, and walked back to you. The Dark People probably didn't realize what was happening...... Nor did they notice a group of people hiding outside watching them, and as soon as Spamton came out, you grabbed his arm and pulled him aside. You nervously sized him up: "Are you alright? Those Dark People probably didn't do anything to you...... You got that tattoo...... Does it hurt you badly? ”

Spamton was stunned for a moment, looked you up and down several times, then said with a mischievous smile, "You're really that worried about me?" That's truly an honor...... But don't worry, that pattern didn't actually catch my scent; it just so happened that I was wearing bionic skin today, just in case...... I didn't expect to actually use it, look. "As he spoke, he pulled aside the tight bionic skin, revealing the truly pale skin underneath...... You touch it casually—it's cool, yet a bit warm. You can't quite say what it feels like—it's a bit like a doll, but also like a puppet. But you clearly remember Spamton isn't a doll or a puppet of the sort...... Alright! Anyway, whether it's a puppet or a puppet, you don't really know what kind of existence the Dark People will become—just like the Sakaka butler next to you. You can't figure out what that thing is. In any case, you withdrew your hand, nodded gently at Spamton, then pulled him back onto the ground.

At this moment, Addison was still sitting there. You didn't say anything. Instead, the Color Card Butler began searching his shop and finally found a set of clergy clothing. The Card Butler sentenced him to the crime: "This Addison has opened a church underground without permission and held church activities, without reporting the location or related activity plans. He is hereby detained for one week for disturbing public order and fined 10,000 Dark Coins." ”

Addison nodded reluctantly and was taken away by the color card butlers. You were stunned for a moment, not expecting it to end like this...... The real culprit still hasn't been found. Spamton just gently tugged your hand and said softly, "Alright, let's head back to the detective agency first. I've been running all day, I'm already hungry...... I suppose you're very hungry too, right? His voice was soft, with a hint of coquettishness, so you couldn't help but nod along, and together with Spamton, you walked toward the agency. Soon, you were back in front of the Mingmeng Detective Agency. You took a deep breath, pushed open the door, and walked in with Spamton. Then you collapsed onto the sofa—even though Spamton was basically in charge of everything today, you still felt tired.
