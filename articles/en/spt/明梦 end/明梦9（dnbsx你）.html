Bright Dream 9 (dnbsx you)

Even though you feel completely burned out, the case shows no sign of letting you off. Probably because ...... What happened yesterday made your detective agency stand out. After all, you've solved ten cases in a row without making any mistakes, and you can even give some justified reason. Anyone would care about this, and the next morning, the color card butler came knocking.

You lay listlessly on the table, letting Spamton greet the guests...... Oh my god, you wished the case had come knocking a little later, but you never expected something would happen the very next day.

The color card butler respectfully addressed you, "After hearing about the reputation of this detective agency and the presence of the People of Light, we have unanimously agreed to seek your help...... There has been a case that has troubled us for several days. We hope you can assist us in solving it. ”

As he spoke, he bowed deeply to you again, respectfully, and of course, didn't forget to bow to Spamton. You yawned, feeling a bit lazy. The exhaustion in your body kept weighing you down, but your curiosity about the case and the excitement you felt from it were involuntarily stirred. You looked up and asked.

"So, could you first give us a general overview of this case? What kind of case has troubled you all for so long?" "You can't help but be a bit curious; you don't know what these people really are...... Hmm, what exactly is going on?

The Seka butler bows to you again, then begins to explain the general situation of the case: "Here's what happened. During this period, we've been discovering their own bodies in different households...... Because their bodies had decomposed and deformed, their neighbors held them tightly, because they noticed a strange smell. However...... What's quite strange is that some of the Dark People were missing parts—some lost legs, some lost arms, and one was a Dark Commoner...... All the skin on their bodies was stripped off, along with their hair. ”

Just hearing the descriptions makes these people seem strange. You fall into deep thought, but the saka butler continues, "We're not sure if any other cases will happen next, nor who the suspects are. These three dead Dark People have nothing in common." ”

"So, you can confirm their identities and their social networks?" You confirmed, and the color card butler nodded.

You pondered for a second, then asked, "So, can't you find a single suspect, even if it's only related to one of them?" ”

But the color card butler just shook his head: "We can't find them. These three dead Dark People are all out of touch with society, with little social connection. That's why their neighbors discovered them and reported it." ”

You fell into deep thought, while Spamton was the first to stand up, smiling as he said, "We roughly understand some details of the case...... You still need to go back and take care of other things, right? Why don't you go first? Later, we'll head to the police station. Don't worry, we'll definitely help find the culprit." ”

So the color card butler walked out. Of course, he was probably still a bit uneasy, glanced back at you two more times, while you rested your chin on your hand, staring at the desk, lost in thought. Spamton walked back over to your side, pulled out a chair and sat down. He smiled and said, "Sweetheart, do you have any thoughts on this case?" ”

You scratched your head: "Hmm...... The killer must target people who live in isolation and isolation. Anyone can see that, so there's no need to say more." Spamton nodded, his hands also resting on the table, waiting for your answer. You're not very clear about the distribution of people in the Dark World; Spamton probably knows more about that.

So you looked at Spamton with extra sincerity: "This time, my assistant needs to make a big contribution too—I'm not very familiar with the distribution of these dark people in the Dark World, so I guess you'll need to step in." ”

He just shrugged as well: "Well...... I'm not really that clear either. If anyone knows about it, no one knows this better than the police, right? Anyway...... I understand, I'll help. Of course, I think in some respects, I still understand this dark world better than the police. ”

After some discussion, you packed up and headed to the police station. Of course, most of your stuff was packed by Spamton, and before leaving, he stuffed a lollipop into your mouth. Then he gently held your hand, and you got into a taxi to the police station. You were smiling the whole time, secretly sneaking glances at Spamton, while he just turned to look at you, even making a wink gesture at you—you instantly blushed, quickly turned away, and patted your cheek. You felt so embarrassed that you felt embarrassed.

Spamton paid the taxi driver, then pulled you out. Throughout the whole process, you were still a bit dazed. Spamton helplessly shook your hand: "Alright, fresh and awake now. We've arrived, we've arrived at our [destination]." As he said this, you finally snapped back to reality. But even after you did, you still looked very embarrassed, stammering and not daring to look at Spamton anymore. He walked ahead of you, grabbed you, and led you into the police station. When those color card butlers saw you walk in, they all bowed respectfully toward you, and you nodded awkwardly. Spamton greeted them with a bright smile.

Then he asked, "Could you first show us the condition of the victims' bodies?" We need to understand the details more clearly before we can conduct a reasonable analysis. "The color card butlers naturally nodded and led you to the entrance of the autopsy room. You pushed open the door and walked in. The first thing that hit you was a cold air—probably to help preserve the bodies. The cold air made you shiver instinctively before entering. Spamton followed behind you, facing the three corpses you had described earlier. Even though you had prepared in advance, you couldn't help but retch twice. Although unlike the bodies of the People of Light, the limbs of the People of Darkness do not bleed. Unlike the People of Light, what flowed out was a thick, swirling liquid with alternating yellow and red alternating forms. At this moment, the two corpses with severed legs and arms still bore the marks of circular drawings, while the last corpse, stripped of skin, was completely deprived of intact skin, with only the thick, blood-like liquid continuously oozing out.

You frowned and stepped forward, carefully sizing up these Dark People. To be honest, you could hardly tell who they really were—maybe that wasn't important, since the forensic report pages weren't anything special...... Their deaths were all due to external injuries and excessive blood loss, with their bodily functions unable to keep up.

You squatted down and carefully examined their wounds; there seemed nothing special, as if they had been severed with sharp blades...... Then you looked at their pale skin, then at the broken area, and immediately frowned.

You quietly beckoned to Spamton, and he squatted beside you. Then you quietly pointed to the marks on his skin and said, "His ...... There is this strange mark on the skin of the broken wound." If it weren't for the slight reflection, you probably wouldn't have noticed it, since it's a pattern subtly outlined through the texture of the skin.

It seemed to spread outward along the surrounding lines, and at the wound site, only half of an eye-like pattern remained, with the other half probably cut off, along with the severed leg. After examining the wound, you began examining the next corpse. Sure enough, at the severed arm, you found only a small fragment of a damaged mark. It didn't look like a tattoo pattern, since it wasn't very obvious...... It just looked like a randomly drawn pattern. As for the third corpse, it had no skin left, so naturally there was nothing to look at.

You furrowed your brows and thought for a moment. You did have some guesses about this eye-like design, but you weren't entirely sure. You just whispered to Spamton, "In the world of light, these eyes look like patterns surrounded by knots and vines, representing faith in Pan...... If I remember correctly, Pan is also another name for the Black Holy Mother. It's said that some believe she can bless them and resurrect their loved ones...... That's probably how it is. ”

But you know very well that this belief probably never reached the Dark World...... If that's the case, who exactly is spreading this belief, or even using it?

“…… The Dark World had never heard of such a thing, or rather, the Dark People simply didn't believe in it. Spamton just shrugged; he really hadn't heard of this, of course...... He didn't pay much attention to it, but this case was probably related to these marks.

"Hmm...... There are two possibilities now. The first is that someone spread the teachings and beliefs of the Black Madonna in the dark world, then recruited followers and eventually killed them. That's why these people's social networks seem completely unrelated. The second is that when they killed them, they also painted these patterns on their bodies...... I don't understand why they would do that. ”

As you spoke, you felt a bit lost. After all, you really couldn't understand why the killer did this, and you couldn't come up with anything in a short time.

Anyway, you still stood up: "Anyway, let's first tell them the results of our investigation." "But maybe you stood up too quickly, your vision went black and started to sway—you felt dizzy, then fell into Spamton's arms. He happened to stand up too, watching you slovel and almost fall, instinctively supporting you, then softly asked, "You...... Are you okay? My sweetheart. ”

What you see is Spamton's face, looking at you with concern. You instinctively blush again, grab his arm, and reluctantly stand up to steady yourself. Then you turn your flushed face away from him to keep him from seeing, just waving his hand at him, your voice as cheerful as ever: "What could possibly happen to me?" I'm fine—alright, let's go out and tell those color card butlers what we've found! "You shouted loudly, walking out hand and foot in hand, while Spamton looked at you and couldn't help but chuckle.
