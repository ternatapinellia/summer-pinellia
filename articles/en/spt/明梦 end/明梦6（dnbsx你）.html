Bright Dream 6 (dnbsx you)

You pulled Spamton back to the detective agency...... Of course, before that, you first saw a roadside grocery store, so you went in and started buying all sorts of snacks. You bought two bottles of grape soda with sugar, two bags of strawberry fruit gummies, then you crawled into the shelves to grab a few Alps lollipops, grabbed two bottles of chewing gum, and finally, because it was fresh, bought two more spicy strips. After that, you stuffed all these things into Spamton's arms, while you took two popsicles from the freezer in front of the store, stuffed one into your mouth, and then stuffed another into Spamton's mouth.

You kept biting on the popsicle, mumbling unclearly, "Mm...... Let's head back first. I think this popsicle tastes pretty good. Maybe we can buy a Xiaoice box at our office to display these things......

Spamton glanced at you holding a big bag of things, then smiled and said, "But isn't there already a big refrigerator inside the office? Just put everything there...... Well, of course, if you want to buy a separate refrigerator, that's fine too, as long as you like it. ”

"Not really, but you're right. If you put nothing in that fridge, leaving it there would just be a waste...... Hmm, I'll buy some more things to put in there!" You said enthusiastically, and once you got back to the office, you could just calm down this big pile of stuff and then drag Spamton to the supermarket.

"By the way, you can cook, right? What are you better at? I'll buy some vegetables to put in the fridge. Hmm, I also need to buy some frozen dumplings and pizza and the like...... Ah, these tangyuan look good, and that bun is ...... "You curiously study the pre-made dishes in the storage cabinet, even though you don't really like the taste of thawed frozen foods...... But it's okay to eat once in a while, and you're quite curious about what these strawberry-flavored buns actually taste like.

But just as you reached out to put these things into the shopping cart, Spamton first stopped you, smiling and saying, "You only need to buy a few cents for an emergency...... Besides, I can cook, so I don't need to keep a lot of frozen food at home." He naturally called the agency 'home' since it's where you two live together, and now you've acknowledged your legal relationship with him...... Of course he would say that, but Spamton didn't admit it was just a little trick of his own. As long as you don't mind, it's fine.

In fact, you really don't care—probably, well, you think the agency is home anyway, and you two live there, right? It seems like hardly anyone comes to your office usually, so it's fine to be casually a little extravagant...... Anyway, you pulled your hand back from trying the strawberry bun and pushed the shopping cart forward.

"Then, it doesn't matter what you eat. As long as it's something you like, I can learn the recipes...... It doesn't matter. Well, as long as you think it's tasty, that's all that matters. " he said.

And when you hear this, you perk up, your eyes starting to light up: "Really?! Then I want to eat cauliflower...... Before, I wanted to try it, but I could never find a proper Chinese restaurant...... In short, in the end, I could only grit my teeth and eat a big lump of something that didn't taste great...... You couldn't help but feel a bit down, but honestly, you really did want to try it...... In the end, you gave an awkward smile.

"But I've heard that growing cauliflower is hard to learn. If you really can't learn it, forget it. I'm not particularly interested in eating it......" you patched up yourself, while Spamton just smiled and listened to you, asking.

"It's fine, it's not very difficult for me—so what do you want to [taste delicious] more?" He had also looked at some Chinese recipes, and for him, it wasn't particularly difficult; he just needed to spend time trying a few more times...... Well, it really depends on what flavor you prefer.

You thought for a moment, hesitated for a moment, and said, "Um...... Strawberry Mapo Tofu? ”

Spamton's expression stiffened for a moment, but he forced himself to keep his smile and said, "...... Strawberry Mapo Tofu is just an invention by other Lightfolk people. It's not a proper Chinese dish. The proper one is Mapo Tofu, but this one is spicy. I wonder if you can accept that...... He started explaining to you at length, maybe because he usually liked spicy food. You got a bit tired of hearing it, couldn't help but cover one ear, and whispered, "Alright, alright, I know...... But I really don't like spicy food; I prefer something lighter, sweeter, and tastier...... I like sweet things. ”

Spamton opened his eyes slightly, glanced at you, and smiled, saying, "Are you a child?" You actually prefer sweet ......" Speaking of which, you just bought a lot of candy, and it seems you really do prefer sweets.

Your cheeks flushed, but you muttered, "Yeah, I just prefer sweet things! I like sugar, cake, milk tea—what's wrong with that!" Eating sweets releases dopamine and makes you feel happier—hmm, of course, eating carbohydrates and such can also help regulate your mood...... In short, eating sweets is really good for your health! "You spouted a bunch of twisted logic, sounding so convinced, then stood on your hands with your hands on your hips, looking at Spamton with your chin slightly raised, as if he'd make a scene if he didn't agree."

"Besides, I'm just a kid anyway...... I just graduated from college! It's fine to call yourself a kid. Oh, right...... I want to try what would happen if chicken, duck, fish, and meat were made sweet. Let's make fish for today. "The topic jumped quickly, and you quickly decided what to eat next. Happily, you instructed Spamton to pick out food for you.

After you've finally finished buying everything, you go home and slowly fill the fridge with all sorts of things. Spamton looked at the top shelf and thought for a moment: "Vegetables can't really be put in here. Tomorrow I'll make a soup with all these vegetables for you." ”

You were exhausted, sitting on the sofa. Hearing this, you glanced at the fridge and looked a bit puzzled: "Hmm...... Let's stew soup together? Will it taste good...... "From what I remember, the last soup you drank was Russian red soup. As for the taste, there's no need to say more—it's just very warm."

Spamton just nodded firmly, closed the fridge, and sat down across from you: "Of course, leave it to me. Don't worry, my sweetheart. Tomorrow, just enjoy my cooking." He smiled at you, and as he looked at his face, you couldn't help but blush a little—well, you really think beauty can be misleading! You hurriedly grabbed whatever was in the bag on the table, opened it, and took a bite. Then you hurriedly pulled open the soda can lid and gulped down several mouthfuls, but unfortunately, the spiciness lingered in your mouth and wouldn't fade. You coughed twice, the blush on your face refusing to go down. Looking down, you didn't know how you had just picked up a bag of spicy strips. You have no choice but to open it and pop a lollipop into your mouth to calm your nerves. Even so, your mood isn't great, since it's just too spicy—plus the soda's stimulation, making you feel like something is jumping in your mouth, constantly stimulating your nerves.

…… You don't know how to describe this feeling. With reddened eyes, you look pitifully at Spamton, who is trying to hold back a laugh and somehow brings you a glass of frozen milk. You unceremoniously bring it over and gulp it down.

…… Hmm, now your mouth feels much better, just a little sticky and rich sweetness. You smile again and sincerely say, "This feels much better...... Thank you, spammy. I like the taste of this milk. Maybe next time we go to the mall, we can buy a little more...... "I was just planning to have a few sips at breakfast to get by, but it turned out to be pretty good. At least you really like it." You don't know if Spamton will like this taste.

Spamton took a couple of sips from the glass of milk you'd already drunk, nodded in agreement: "It really tastes great. Next time you go to the mall, you can buy more...... Hmm, if it's just to relieve the spiciness, drinking milk will have a pretty good effect." He said this, and you just nodded thoughtfully. You really don't understand a lot of everyday knowledge...... After all, you're just a weirdo immersed in your own world.

Then you look at the can with a loop on the table, your eyes darting, and suddenly an idea pops into your head. You lean closer, a bit mysterious and somewhat anticipatory, and say, "Stretch out your left hand." ”

Spamton blinked twice in confusion, then you picked up the iron ring and slipped it onto his left ring finger, hands on hips as you looked at your masterpiece, excitedly saying, "Ta-da-da~ Now you're marrying me, spammy! So, do you like this ring?" ”

Spamton was stunned for a moment, glanced at the ring on his finger, then at you, and finally just curved his eyes and said, "Of course, I like everything about you. Whatever you give me, I'll like it, my sweetheart." ”

You blink, your eyes darting around. You pat your cheek to calm yourself down, then simply sit down on the sofa next to him. You hug him hard, hugging him hard and hard, even rubbing against his neck. You think about a movie you've seen before and imitate a line from the movie: "Then I'll give you a basket of hugs—"

Maybe he'd seen that movie too, maybe he hadn't. Spamton just turned around and hugged you back, gently pressing his cheek against yours, then softly kissed yours. So barely noticeable, his lips brushed against the corner of your mouth. You felt your ears start to burn, your arms went weak. You didn't push him away, or rather, you didn't have the strength to push him away—or rather, you simply couldn't bear to push him away.

You feel the breath from his mouth brush against your ear. Beside your ear, he softly whispers in a faintly electric sizzling voice, "Then I want to give you a basketful of kisses." As he spoke, he leaned in and kissed your forehead again.

Then he looked at your lips and softly mouthed: So, can I do it?

You blushed and nodded, the smile at the corner of his mouth seemed to grow bigger and more obvious. Then he cupped your cheek, and you could feel that warm, slightly cool yet moist touch on your lips.
