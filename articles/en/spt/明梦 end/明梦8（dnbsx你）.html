Bright Dream 8 (dnbsx you)

This time, the person who walked in was a love-sticker who loved to stick against the wall...... You glanced briefly and thought, hmm, this time it was a murder case. The victim was a manager named Ren Wu, and the crime scene was a small alley. The injuries on the victim's body hadn't been thoroughly investigated, but she still had this stick-in-the-wall token on her, which is why he was brought in as a suspect...... You glanced over the file and then looked up at the love-sticker in front of you. His eyes darted around, not daring to look at you, and his face seemed a bit pale compared to his, as if he couldn't see clearly—except he was slowly floating here, looking indeed weak or guilty.

You just put down the materials and asked a question, which seemed unrelated to the case—except the current estimated time of the incident was around this time: "Between nine and ten the night before last...... How was the performance at the dance hall? I heard the wine there was really good. ”

The one who loves sticking to the wall instinctively said, "That day, we happened to invite the famous diva from all over Cyber City...... It was just too beautiful. As for the wine there, I was responsible for transporting it over, and my family is responsible for brewing and making the liquor...... He seemed about to keep boasting and continuing his conversation, but you just interrupted him.

"Alright, he's not the culprit in this case...... But he's probably the culprit in another case. Let's take him to the side for now." Only then did the wall-hungry man realize what was happening. He shouted that he was innocent, but no one paid him any attention. He was handcuffed and escorted to the side by Spamton, then welcomed the next suspect into your venue.

You checked the records, and this time it was the same ...... Well, the church children's disappearances probably happened around evening, when the children playing near the church all disappeared together. A group of anxious Dark Folk called the police. As for the one being detained, it looked like an Addison dressed as a priest...... You weren't interested in understanding why they were dressed like this. You just flipped through the documents again, then looked up at the Addison in front of you. He had his eyes closed, still smiling, holding a cross—uh, his expression somewhat resembled Spamton's, making you turn to glance at Spamton, who just tilted his head curiously as if looking at you. You quickly turned your head again, looked at the documents again, hesitated for a moment, and then gave your judgment: "This guy is innocent." He didn't kidnap these children...... As for where the children went, let's look again; maybe they're nearby. "Of course, that's just your speculation. You know kids like to run around everywhere, maybe they just got lost.

The case was solved, the suspect was acquitted and walked out, and then another suspect was brought in. This time, it was a virus...... Well, it's not very common. When you glance at the files in your hand, you can't help but feel exasperated. Invic is a virus, and the victim's computer was hacked, so they sued their neighbor's virus...... The virus looked completely justified, as if it didn't matter at all. You looked at him, unable to tell if he was guilty or not, so as usual, you asked a question.

"What do you think about being accused of hacking your neighbor's computer?" Because you really can't think of anything else to ask...... So that's all you can ask. I hope he can give you an answer that satisfies you.

"Him?" His computer's firewall is so bad that I wouldn't even bother attacking him—there's absolutely no need for that. Why would I attack his computer? It wouldn't do me any good, so this definitely wasn't my doing. Maybe that guy just misread things! "This Dark Citizen said with absolute certainty.

You nodded and waved your hand: "This guy isn't the culprit in this case, but probably someone from another case...... Detain him and take him aside for now, next person! ”

The next to walk in was Manager Ren Wu, whose face was full of anger. You hadn't even had time to read the materials, and she was already rambling: "Honestly, I'm just a front desk manager. How could I possibly be involved in the poisoning case? I didn't even go inside—isn't that just slandering me?!" She was indignant, but when you heard her words, you perked up and picked up the documents to glance at them.

This case was a nightclub poisoning incident. Reportedly, there were no casualties, but all the Dark Citizens present who had been drinking were poisoned. So they jointly reported the nightclub staff, and in the end, the receptionist was charged.

You glanced at her twice, then at the documents, and finally at Ai-Wall-Sticker, who was being detained by Spamton: "You really aren't the murderer of the poisoning case, because the killer is someone else—this Ai-Wall-Sticker...... As for you, you killed your good colleague in the alley behind you, another Ren Wu Manager Ji, right? "You lifted your eyes slightly, resting your chin on your hand as you said with great interest, and Ren Wu's expression immediately changed. She was naturally taken away, and as for the Ai-Wall...... Yeah, she was taken away too. Of course, although they weren't the murderers of the accused case, they were the ones in another case. You weren't interested in figuring out their motives and methods, since you were dealing with the next case.

The next person to come in was an ordinary Addison, blue in color. Take a look at the accused case—it was actually a bakery bread that had spoiled...... The victim insisted she ate expired bread and demanded compensation, and even found a necklace inside. This is simply outrageous!

Your expression suddenly became hard to describe...... Well, in short, there was nothing else to say. You looked up at the addison, who took the initiative to speak: "Ah, all the bread in my house is freshly baked every morning, and I check each ingredient one by one to ensure there's no problem, so this case has nothing to do with me." "And as you looked down at the documents in your hand, you naturally didn't notice the undercurrents between Spamton and this addison.

You just tapped your knuckles on the table and said, "Mr. Addison, now you're not only charged with spoiled bread, but also with jewelry theft. You are the culprit, so—next!" You waved your hand to beckon him, and Addison's face turned pale, and Spamton grabbed him, handcuffed him, and took him away. And the next one who walked in—or rather, the one who drifted in was also a virus.

You first checked the information. In this case, the Queen's villa's security system had completely failed, and everything was hacked. Because of this, the Seka butler caught the person acting suspiciously near the villa, accused him, and brought him before you as a suspect.

You roughly take a look—it's about the access controls—each room's own restrictions, the basement lock, and the office access controls—all have been breached...... The queen was furious, and the remaining color card butlers naturally followed her orders to quickly find the culprit. However, there was currently no other evidence to help accuse the virus.

Ni Kan paused for a moment, then asked, "Do you know what the access control inside the Queen's Villa is like? Is it easy to conquer?" ”

His voice trembled as well: "I-I don't know! To me, that would be like a natural chasm—it's just too dangerous...... Even if I had a hundred times the courage, I wouldn't dare do something like this! Miss, you probably misunderstood me. I'm a citizen of the Three Good Darkness...... I would never casually hack into someone else's equipment, unless it's absolutely necessary...... He rambled on, and you found yourself getting a headache coming on. You gestured for him to be quiet, then looked at the virus imprisoned nearby and asked with a smile.

"So, what do you think of the access control inside the Queen's Villa?"

The virus quickly replied, "A piece of cake, it's a piece of cake. It's really easy, not difficult at all." After instinctively speaking, he realized what he'd just said in his rush, hurriedly shut his mouth, and then you turned to look at the virus in front of you, equally terrified, and couldn't help but laugh.

"Don't be so nervous, they're just accomplices—though I don't know why you broke into that Dark People's computer, maybe something important. In short, one of you is responsible for hacking into that poor neighbor's computer, the other is in charge of hacking into the security system inside the Queen's villa, and in the end, the virus pretends the person involved is caught, so you can both escape charges at the same time...... Unfortunately, you were caught together anyway. Forget it, take them down, next one! "You wave your hand, Spamton also handcuffs the virus, and then both viruses are escorted out together, looking utterly defeated. You wait for the next person to come in, and take a look at the files for the next case.

The case was simple, except it also happened in a nightclub, where the Dark Folk were accused of poisoning and murdering another Dark Citizen. You don't even need to look closely to know this guy is innocent, since it was the same poison he had stuck under the wall before. The Dark Folk probably died from the poisoning because they drank a bit too much—sure enough, the documents clearly state that the victim was extremely addicted to alcohol and drank excessively that night.

So when the next defendant walks into the detective agency, you simply wave your hand directly: "Alright, you're innocent. Go home, next ......."

Honestly, although you really enjoy solving cases, solving ten cases in a row is just too exhausting. You've gone from being passionate at first to now just lazily sprawling on your desk, always feeling like your brain has been drained...... Of course, it could also be your imagination.

The last Dark Clan who walked in seemed to have stopped acting. You could still see the unique bloodstains of the Dark Clan on him—like yellow-purple vortex-shaped marks. Of course, the area wasn't very large, and he was holding a small knife. Oh heavens, this knife is still dripping blood?! Where did you find the murderer?! You command Spamton in terror: "This guy is the murderer! Hurry and seize him and take him out!! ”

Spamton agreed to your words and took the guy out, and when he returned, he saw you sprawled over the table, sighing, "So tired...... Don't take on so many jobs next time, I wish I wouldn't open for a whole week...... you muttered plaintively.
