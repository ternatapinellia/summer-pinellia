…… He didn't dare imagine this possibility, couldn't imagine that he might never change from beginning to end, while you would have to age with the passage of time. Originally, Spamton hadn't considered these things—maybe he just thought none of it mattered, or maybe he just blindly believed he had a way to solve it. But now, Addison's words laid the problem right in front of him, and he had no choice but to think about it...... Think about the possibility that you might die before that. So he kept staring at you, a bit scared, afraid that maybe somewhere he couldn't see, you might suddenly face death.

Before leaving, Addison gave him a rather provocative look. It was clear there was only one intention: ...... His provocative meaning was that Spamton was also falling in love with the Citizens of Light, so one day, the situation Addison faced was exactly the same one Spamton would face.

And you probably haven't noticed this. You're just busy contacting the Scard butlers to confirm they have indeed caught the culprit and taken him back to the police station. Meanwhile, the Sakaka butlers who were staking out have also caught other Dark People who came to commit a crime—the same believers from that small church that once began. Now they've caught all the other believers, and it seems the matter has indeed come to an end. You put down your phone and let out a long breath. Only then do you notice Spamton was staring at you for some reason. You turned to him in confusion and tilted your head slightly: "Hmm, Spammy, what's wrong? Why are you staring at me all the time?" I'm sitting here perfectly fine, and I won't suddenly get hurt or anything. You said half-teasingly, leaning over and leaning against Spamton, gently patting his head.

"Do you have something troubling you? Why not tell me? I'm a great detective who can definitely help you solve it!" You pointed at yourself with a cheeky grin. Clearly, you hadn't realized that Spamton was thinking about something that might be very serious.

Spamton wanted to say something. He instinctively wanted to tell you what troubled him, but then he stopped—well, it's fine if he was worried about this. He shouldn't have told you about it, he ...... Telling you would only add to your troubles. Do you really know how to handle it?

So he just gave you a smile as usual and winked: "Ah, it's nothing major, just thinking about that arrested Addison...... Well, but there's no need to think too much about it. ”

You glanced at Spamton hesitantly, feeling like he was hiding something from you, since his smile looked a bit fake. Anyway, it was just your intuition guessing. Maybe it really is just as Spamton said.

So you didn't dwell on it any further, just shrugged: "Ah, that Addison?" There's nothing much to say about him, right? Anyway, his crime is already settled, so there's no need for any more evidence. We've solved this case so it's basically closed...... Ah, I really need to rest well these next few days. "You yawned as you spoke. It was clear you were a bit tired from everything that happened these past few days. Spamton knew very well that we shouldn't be looking for any more cases for you at this point."

Spamton just nodded and then shook his head: "How should I put it? What I'm thinking about is the statue of the Virgin Mary enshrined in that church...... That is, that Lightborn. If a Lightborns appeared in Cyber City, I would definitely not know, but I have no impression of them at all......" He hesitated, unless this Lightborne came to the Dark World a long time ago, when he was still a small white Addison, before he became a popular figure. In that case, her arrival would be considered quite early.

So he changed the subject: "Unless she came to the Dark World long ago before that." "That's the only possibility...... Spamton couldn't think of any other possibilities. And you blinked, reacted for a moment, and rubbed your chin.

"So, when I first started high school, I did hear about a missing person case, but that family had no intention of searching further, so the case was dropped. But now that I think about it, the description of the missing person was similar to the statue of the Virgin Mary enshrined...... "You recalled events from a few years ago. To be fair, your memory is pretty good. You remember everything that happened back then, and your description of that girl, you remember everything clearly."

“…… How did you get to the Dark World?" Spamton suddenly asked, his question sounding completely unrelated to what he had just said. You paused, thought for a moment, and replied, "Hmm...... One day, I saw a door that suddenly appeared in my house, and the door was painted with a cyber city pattern. Out of curiosity, I pushed it open and then entered the Dark World. As for how that Citizen of Light ended up in the Dark World...... I don't know about that. "As you spoke, you shrugged and casually explained where you came from.

"Then my sweetheart, take me there to see it. How about it?" He was still smiling, but only he knew that deep down there was a sense of tension. In fact, he wasn't as calm as he appeared on the surface. He was thinking, he really was thinking that if he could travel between the light world and the dark world through that entrance, if he lived with you in the light world, would everything change? Then...... Would he, like you, grow old together and then pass away together?

Although you didn't understand why Spamton suddenly wanted to see the passage you came from, you still held Spamton's hand and went along. That place was in a rather remote alley in Cybercity. You clung to Spamton, winding through twists and turns before finally entering and reaching the destination.

You point to a door on the wall in front of you, with a sign for your bedroom drawn on it: "Yes, this is the door. I came here from here." As you spoke, you stepped forward, wanting to open the door and show Spamton, but Spamton grabbed your arm, his face looking even paler—even though he was already bloodless.

“…… I didn't see the door you mentioned." He said softly. He had thought that once he found the passage, he could leave. He tried his best to ignore the things he had heard before, but...... But just as he had heard before, the People of Light possess souls, or rather light, so they are free. They are born in the world of light, completely different from those born in the dark world. After all, in the end, they are just ordinary objects...... They are just ordinary objects transformed, carrying their own thoughts and ideas.

The foundation of this dark world is probably the laptop in your room, after all, he still remembers a few times...... He once saw you on that computer screen, witnessed everything in reality.

Although this was something he once didn't want to admit, now he had no choice but to admit it. He ...... It's just a collection of spam emails your computer receives.

He was just an ordinary Dark Citizen, or rather, he could never become like the Light Citizen—unable to have freedom, autonomy, or to become completely famous. What he called popularity and fame now was only built upon the foundation of this dark world.

…… Spamton can't be with you forever; he fully understands this, then feels anxious and helpless, and tightly grabs your arm.

You frowned and looked at his hand, muttering, "You scratched my arm in pain." You don't know what's wrong with him, why he's suddenly using so much force. You don't know what he's thinking when he frowns, but it's definitely something bad.

Spamton instinctively relaxed his grip, but he still gently held your hand and softly said, "...... Sorry. I hurt you by grabbing you. I was just distracted. ”

You stared at him carefully, stepped closer to him, and stared intently at his face. Spamton, however, couldn't help but take a step back. He stared at you, gave an awkward smile, and asked, "What's wrong?" "Although he lost his composure a bit, he believed his disguise was flawless and shouldn't arouse your suspicion."

You just nodded and shook your head, looking at him and softly saying, "It's nothing, it's just that you look ...... a bit strange. Rather, ever since that killer was taken away, you've been acting a bit strange. What's wrong with you? ”

Spamton just showed his usual smile, squinting so you couldn't tell what kind of mood he was feeling. You stared at him, seeing that Spamton probably had no intention of answering. You took a deep breath and stepped closer to him again, this time your face was even closer. Spamton instinctively took another step back, then leaned against the wall, with nowhere left to retreat.

You rubbed your chin, stared at him, and narrowed your eyes slightly: "The questions you asked today all seem strange, and you yourself seem a bit off...... Let me guess—are you thinking about something related to that Citizen of Light?" His expression didn't seem to change much, but it seems that's not the case. You came up with it.

So you shrugged and continued to ask, "Then...... Is it something related to the People of Light? Or is it related to me? "This time, you noticed Spamton's expression change slightly. In short, he blinked slightly and looked at you like that. I think you probably guessed part of the correct answer."

So you keep asking, "That's about me, and also about the People of Light...... You hesitate for a moment, unsure whether to continue, because you always feel your guess is a bit absurd.

But to figure out what Spamton was really hiding from you, you continued to voice your suspicions: "Hmm, so probably...... Because you're different from me, you're just a Dark Citizen. Can't you become a Light Citizen? "You said this as you looked at Spamton.

Spamton sighed, raised both arms in a surrender pose: "I really lost to you...... You're pretty much right. Since that's the case, I'll tell you the truth. ”

As he spoke, he cleared his throat and decided to tell you everything that had just made him uneasy.
