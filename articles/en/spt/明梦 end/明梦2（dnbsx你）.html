Bright Dream 2 (dnbsx you)

Now those scan card butlers are all looking at each other, not daring to make any further moves, and you can only scratch your head...... Uh, after all, you can't give any other clues. You can only quickly determine who the culprit is by relying on speculation and the criminal psychology knowledge you once studied.

So for a moment, the arena fell into an awkward silence. Perhaps even the Color Card Butlers didn't know what to do. Originally, they just needed to take away the suspect, Spamton, but now the esteemed Light Tribe stepped forward and said things weren't like that. No matter what, they couldn't refuse to give the Light Tribe some face.

And you hesitated, looking at the Dark People present. None of them said a word, just stared at you, waiting for you to say something next—which made you suddenly tense up, because you really didn't have much to say to these people...... Uh, or maybe you don't even know what to say. Yeah, that's just how it is.

At that moment, you suddenly hear Spamton's voice. You look at him in surprise, but Spamton simply replies, "Hmm...... You've heard the facts this Miss of the Lightfolk stated. I now request to join her in the investigation, so I can clear my name. Secondly...... A noble Lightborn is my guardian, so you can feel at ease, don't you? "He said slowly. Although his words might sound a bit off, he was indeed very clear. At least you don't think there's anything inappropriate."

The slot managers exchanged glances again, but in the end nodded in agreement. They released Spamton's grip and brought her back to your side.

You were still a bit stunned, but Spamton simply walked back to your side with his hands behind his back, smiling as he said, "Next, I will be your [temporary assistant]. Please take care of me, Miss Y/N." "It's also the first time you've heard his voice mixed with this sizzling sound, as if it's just a piece separated from some recording—uh, although you can still understand, right? And you really do need an assistant. You're wondering what to do, and then Spamton comes knocking—you really need an assistant, preferably smart and capable, because you can only name who the killer is, but can't provide complete evidence."

So you looked gratefully at Spamton and whispered, "...... Thank you for your help, Mr. Spamton. I happen to be short of an assistant. ”

His smile only deepened, as if it had become more sincere: "No, I should be the one thanking Miss Y/N, thanking you for clearing my name—though this freedom is only temporary, limited edition." ”

You cleared your throat twice. In any case, you were used to his tone, so you simply said, "Then let's start now, no time to waste." Then you looked at the slot butlers and asked politely.

"Is there a map of the banquet hall? Or could you tell us which locations this banquet hall is divided into?" ”

The color card butler who seemed to be the leader bowed slightly: "This banquet hall is divided into two floors above ground and one underground...... However, during the banquet, only the first floor of the main hall is open to the guests. Moreover, the basement contains warehouses and wine storage, containing only the necessary and backup facilities for the banquet. The second floor of the banquet hall has four separate private rooms and three servants' rooms. The other servants are all hired externally. ”

You nod, there's no time to lose...... You should investigate quickly. You can't stand being stared at by these dark citizens here, so you hurriedly grabbed Spamton's hand and headed up the hallway: "Alright, then we'll start the investigation now. Your banquet can continue." ”

Even though you said that, no one seemed to have any intention of hosting a banquet anymore; they just sat in small groups, drinking and chatting casually. And whether it was your imagination or not, you kept hearing Spamton chuckle behind you, and when you looked back, all you could see was his smiling face...... Hmm, maybe it was just your imagination. Thinking this, you followed Spamton to the second floor. The signs on the second floor were quite clear: to the left was the servants' room, to the right were the small private rooms reserved for distinguished guests.

You looked around and expressed your opinion: "I plan to go to the servant's room first." ”

"Of course, no objections." Spamton looked at you with interest, noticing the nervousness on your face and the youthful vibe that radiated vitality. In fact, he didn't care at all, because he knew very well that his crime was falsely accused. The Queen certainly wouldn't deal with him, a hot figure in Cyber City, just that with the skills of these Color Card butlers, they couldn't find the real culprit.

It's just ...... You stepped forward. He originally approached you just because of your identity as a Light Citizen, at least leaving you with some impression, for better or worse—as long as you remember him, and if you remember him later, you can offer him a little help. But now, you've stepped forward. Although he's still a suspect, he won't be taken away directly. He's very curious...... How exactly did you figure out who the real killer is? And he's also curious if you can find any evidence. So he chose to be your temporary assistant.

Because he was just too curious.

You really don't know how many things Spamton has on his mind, because he's always smiling. You can't see his shadow, and naturally, you can't understand what he's thinking. You just dragged Spamton into the first room. This was the butler's room, not very big...... Well, it didn't seem anything special, just a pocket watch hanging on the wall.

So you took Spamton's hand and led him into the second room. This was the maid's room. Unexpectedly, it was a large communal bed with about ten beds arranged here. You pressed your forehead, reluctantly feeling through each bed, then checked the nightstands. Finally, you found the same mail-shaped badge on one of the nightstands, identical to the one Spamton's chest wore—also gold. You weighed the badge in your hand; the weight seemed a bit off, as if it was a bit light...... But before you could think it over and examine it carefully, Spamton took the badge. He weighed it twice as well, then glanced at it in the light—it was somewhat translucent. Spamton calmly told you, "This badge isn't made of the same material as mine; it's probably a replica...... It has a translucent property, while my badge is made of pure gold. ”

Finding a counterfeit badge here means the one downstairs might also be a forgery. You glance at Spamton: "So, do you think the badge they seized is real or fake?" "You can't figure it out, so you might as well throw it to Spamton to figure out the answer—well, you're not very good at this process of gathering evidence, and Spamton just smiles."

"That one is fake, it's a replica, absolutely authentic." Because at the moment he was captured, he saw the light shining through that badge, so...... That badge was made of exactly the same material as the one he was holding.

"Also, I'm afraid there are at least two real culprits behind the scenes...... Or maybe that guy wants to frame someone." "He reminded you.

And you just shook your head without thinking. You'd seen all the dark people present in the banquet hall at the time, unless there was someone you hadn't noticed: "There's no other culprit, he's the only one." Hmm...... After all, he—he showed the kind of expression and gesture only a murderer would have...... "As you spoke, you gestured, unsure how to describe your intuition, but in any case, it was a feeling you had."

You were troubled that you might not be able to explain yourself, but Spamton just smiled and nodded: "Mm, I believe you." ”

You stop what you're doing in surprise and look at Spamton—does he really believe you so easily? You were racking your brains trying to figure out how to gain his trust. So you can't help but ask, "Do you really trust me that much?" ”

Spamton just tilted his head innocently, still smiling brightly. Your attention flickered for a moment, and you thought, he really looks good like that...... Ahem, beauty can be misleading, and you've sworn to be a towering detective—don't let beauty fool you!

So you shook your head twice, trying to shake the thought out of your mind. Spamton watched your actions and found it a bit amusing. He said nonchalantly, "Hmm, [absolutely true], but Miss Y/N, what's wrong? Are you feeling a bit of a headache?" ”

But you just shook your head in a panic, hurriedly turned around, and patted your slightly flushed face twice, warning yourself that she was just here to help you investigate clues and clear up suspicion, not to smile at you because she likes you. Don't overthink it! There will never be an ending between the People of Light and the People of Darkness! After reminding yourself like this, you felt much better, but hurriedly walked toward the servant's room, mumbling a bit carelessly: "Ah, it's nothing...... I just think you look a bit good...... Uh, no, no, no, there's nothing interesting in the maid's room, so let's hurry to the next room and take a look! ”

As you spoke, you hurried toward the servant's room. Spamton just chuckled softly and slowly followed behind. The smile in his eyes grew even wider—Hmm, you look like an interesting little girl, a very interesting lightborn. He even wants to spend a little more time with you, because pretending to be innocent teasing you a couple of times is especially fun.

But he still followed you into the room. This time, you entered the last servant's room, which was also the waiter's room. This room also had ten beds, but all were bunk beds, making it feel even more crowded. The nightstand was basically full of items, so you had to search one nightstand at a time, rummaging through the messy items, but after looking for a long time, you couldn't find anything suitable as evidence. By the end, your mouth was a bit parched, and you reached the last nightstand. Instinctively, you wanted to grab the glass cup filled with a clear liquid on the table, but before you could act, Spamton's expression changed and he knocked over the glass in your hand.

The sound of glass shattering echoed clearly through the room once again. You were a bit stunned, still standing still, and you heard Spamton's slightly reprimanded voice: "Don't rush to drink every liquid, like this one...... It must be poisonous. ”

You nodded blankly, and you broke out in a cold sweat belatedly—as expected, your social experience was still too limited.
