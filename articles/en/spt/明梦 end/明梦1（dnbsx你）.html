Oh heavens—

You stood with your hands on your hips, looking triumphantly at the building before you—a detective agency you had just rented and renovated. You dreamed of owning a detective agency of your own, but unfortunately, this was too troublesome in the Light World. It might take you several years to have such a small office, so you came to the Dark World. All you needed to do was spend some money and say a few nice words to the queen, and she agreed to lease you part of the territory.

You're really happy, but the agency can't be run by just you; at the very least, it needs an assistant or something. After all, you can't let the detective handle all the work alone! You looked around, looking at this small office. After hesitating for a moment, you decided to go out and look around. After all...... Right now, no Dark People have come to your office, and you have no business at all. You have to go out and work hard to find some cases and bump into them, so you have to make the detective agency's reputation known.

As for the agency's sign—you've already thought it through, it's called Mingmeng Detective Agency. It helps people see through the darkness hidden beneath the light, discerns the reality within dreams, and distinguishes between dreams and falsehoods, and falsehood from reality. Just then, you heard the Queen tell you that there would be a large banquet in Cyber City today, with famous people attending. You were no less enthusiastic, using your identity as a Light Citizen to make others yield, and you walked straight in.

But when you arrive at this lively ball, everything puts you in a bit of a dilemma—you've never attended such a banquet before, after all, you're just an orphan. Although you have good grades, you've never been involved in high society in the Light World. But in the Dark World, as a Light Citizen, you are naturally treated with great respect. As an honored guest, you were invited directly to the seat of honor. Not only that, but you heard them bowing and bowing to welcome another person to take their seats. You looked curiously at the Dark Folk sitting next to you, named Spamton, who seemed to be called Hot Shot—probably a popular figure in the cyber world. Spamton also looked at you curiously. This Light Citizen, said to have arrived in the cyber world yesterday as a human, had even opened a detective agency in the Dark World with the queen's approval...... It seems to be called Mingmeng? But that has nothing to do with me.

You sized him up a few times, noticed his gaze, and met his gaze. But the next second, he smiled again, raised his glass to toast you, and said, "Welcome to the Dark World, Miss of the People of Light." ”

You glanced at your glass, which was filled with the orange juice you specifically requested. After all, you're not good at drinking, and you just hurriedly raised your glass and bumped it with him: "Um...... Thank you, I'm very happy to see you, Mr. Spamton. ”

You heard him chuckle softly: "I wonder if I have the honor to know your name?" You always felt his tone was a bit odd, or maybe the order of words was a bit odd, but you could understand it, so you didn't pay much attention to it.

You just said dryly, "Ah, my name is Y/N." After saying that, you hurriedly swallowed the orange juice in one gulp. You seemed to hear his laughter again, but when you turned to look at him, he was just smiling, as if everything just now was just your imagination.

But you're sure he just smiled, since the smile on his face now was genuine. Even though the curve of his smile and the angle of his eyes looked fake—you knew very well, it was real. Even though you couldn't see his pupils, you were clear about it. You glanced at him thoughtfully, but said nothing. After all, it was just your feeling, and it wouldn't be appropriate to say it out loud.

The banquet went on as usual, and soon you started to get bored. You thought there would be something else at the party, but you saw many Darkfolk going straight to the dance floor after finishing their food and drinks, while you sat there bored, propping up your head and yawning—you definitely don't want to see any of this. Is there any kind of heart-wrenching case happening right in front of you?

At that moment, Spamton turned his head to look at you: "I wonder if I have the honor to invite you to dance with me?" ”

You shivered and looked at the smiling Spamton. You thought he probably had ulterior motives, but you didn't really care what he was up to. You just said dryly, "I, uh, can't dance." After saying that, you gave a couple of awkward laughs.

But Spamton's smile deepened: "It's okay, Miss just needs to follow my movements." "As he spoke, he reached out his hand. You hesitated and looked at him. Well, he didn't mean to embarrass you...... So you held his hand and let him lead you away from your seat.

You walked to the center of the dance floor, the spotlight shining on you. Only then did you feel panic and call out to leave—you didn't want to embarrass yourself, nor did you want to be famous in public! You shouted, "Wait—"

But Spamton was already holding your hand and swinging it in the center of the dance floor. In a panic, you stepped on his foot. You felt a bit embarrassed and was about to say something when the lights in the banquet hall suddenly went out. You heard the panicked voices of the Dark People, the struggling and shouting of each other, and your eyes lit up—you smelled the smell of a case! This must be a case that had happened!

You tried to walk toward the place where the loud noise and glass shattering were coming from, but suddenly landed in a fairly warm embrace, almost a bit fluffy: "Miss Y/N, the lights suddenly went out. I'm afraid something might happen to you...... Sorry to offend you." It was Spamton's deep voice. You blinked and hadn't even said anything more when you saw, not far ahead, beside the banquet table, a Dark Commoner collapsed to the ground, cheeks flushed, even his skin touching the floor completely red...... Beside him fell a broken glass, the green clear liquor soaking the carpet. This Dark Folk seemed dead. In any case, the Dark Folk nearby were all exclaiming, and some were whispering among themselves. They were reciting the identity of the Dark Folk on the ground—it seemed to be an Addison. Then they quietly glanced at Spamton beside you. The moment the light came on, he had already let go of his embrace, but still gently held your hand.

"Quiet, quiet!" A few of the Sakaka butlers you saw in the Queen's villa came out, since this unusual banquet was organized by the Queen's order.

The color card butlers pushed aside the onlookers of the Dark Folk, then began checking the condition of the fallen Addison. After a brief investigation, they seemed to find nothing unusual. The slot stewards looked around, then looked at Spamton: "Mr. Spamton, you are currently the prime suspect among the Dark Folk." ”

Spamton just kept the upward curve of his lips, pointing to his tilted head, looking a bit confused.

But those color card butlers just continued, "This dead Addison is holding the badge on your chest. If you hadn't committed the crime, how could he have gotten your badge?" ”

Spamton just smiled: "But my badge is still hanging perfectly fine on my tie. How can I prove that the badge in his hand is mine?" He shrugged, looking innocent. And you just scanned the crowd, scrutinizing who was more suspicious. Hmm—the killer was hiding among the Dark People, trying to fish in troubled waters, because you had already seen that guy. But...... Right now, you have no evidence to identify the other party, which is your biggest headache. You can identify the culprit at a glance, but you can't provide solid evidence to identify them.

"No matter what, you here are the most suspicious Dark People, so we need to detain you for now." Those color card butlers said. Well, after all, the Dark World doesn't have a strict system like the Light World—there are no police. Instead, it's the butlers in the Queen's Mansion, who take over the police responsibilities.

While you were still lost in thought, you noticed that the Spamton beside you had been taken away by those color card butlers. Before leaving, he mouthed you: Miss Y/N, I believe you can prove my innocence. Your detective agency isn't just a free one, right?

You were stunned for a moment. You hadn't expected Spamton to trust you like this. You have no achievements at all right now. Surely it's just because you're a citizen of light? Although it's not impossible, you sighed, straightened your trench coat that had just fallen apart from dancing, and casually pulled out a chair to sit down.

You clear your throat: "Quiet, listen to me first!" ”

They all knew you, a Commoner of Light, a highly sought-after honored guest of the Queen, so all the Dark Ones quieted down, including the slot stewards escorting Spamton. You glanced around again, then nodded gently: "First of all, Mr. Spamton has always been with me. As the person sitting beside him and his dance partner, I know this very well." ”

"—Secondly, the killer is right in front of you, yet you caught the wrong person. It's just too funny." You said this and couldn't help but shake your head with a smile.

"Then, Miss of the People of Light, tell me, who exactly is the culprit?" One of the color card butlers asked. When he heard Spamton had no time to commit the crime, his mother looked at each other and decided to let him go. After all, he was a well-known figure in the cyber world, and if they really caught the wrong person, it would be extremely awkward.

You glanced at those color card butlers and casually commanded, "Yes, the killer is right next to you—it's that guy." ”

As you spoke, you pointed to one of the Love-Sticker Walls, who was dressed as a waiter and dressed as another Dark Commoner. You brought him a glass of wine. The Dark Commoner noticed where you pointed and trembled, shattering the glass in his hand.

"Do you have any evidence?" The color card butler asked.

Here it comes, and this is also your biggest headache—evidence. Of course, you're not good at that, since you're only good at analyzing the psychology of criminals. You barely studied those courses back then.

So you just shrugged: "Evidence...... I, uh, suggest you send this Addison for a body examination...... Well, I don't know if there's such a test in the Dark World. Anyway, I have no other evidence, but this guy definitely touched Addison's wine glass lying on the ground. "You said this with conviction. Indeed, on the surface, there seems to be no evidence for this case. The Saka's butler, who collected the alcohol for the examination, quickly returned to report. The alcohol only contains trace amounts of absinthe's own toxins, which aren't fatal at all—at most a bit addictive."
