Of course, you didn't notice his laughter, because you stepped out first, and his laughter was hidden behind it. Spamton gazed at your back, and in the end, he followed your steps out the door. He knew very well that these things needed to be explained to those scan card butlers, since it was up to these guys to figure out who the suspects really were. He really wasn't lying; in this dark world, it must be these guys acting as police officers who know who exactly is there.

You walked out, and naturally, all the color card butlers inside the police station looked at you. Faced with so many stares, you instinctively took two steps back and hid behind Spamton—oh my god, you really can't get used to having to talk after facing a crowd of people. You hate this attitude, or maybe you're just just nervous...... Besides, the police station is very noisy. Think about it, it's probably easier for you to solve a series of problems in a quiet environment.

Spamton turned around, gently patted your head, and said softly, "Alright, it's okay. I'll go tell you about what you just discovered...... No need to be so nervous, no one will eat you." He said half-jokingly, and under his reassurance, your mood did improve a lot, though you were still hiding behind Spamton, tugging at the hem of his clothes...... Hmm, think about it, maybe you were just too nervous. Now, with his reassurance, your mood was clearly much better.

You take a deep breath. Spamton takes your hand and leads you to the scan card butlers. You widen your eyes, trying hard to maintain a serious and focused expression, while Spamton smiles and says, "We just checked the condition of these bodies...... To be honest, the situation is truly tragic, and I feel sorry for it first...... Secondly, the wounds of these people all have half-scar marks, like one eye with knots or vines spreading out. If you don't look closely, you might not notice it...... I think you should know this clue. ”

The color card stewards looked at each other, probably talking for a while. Finally, one of the stewards seemed to be the leader...... Stepping forward two steps, it signified that the sectarians nodded at you. Seeing the other scheat stewards leave, you finally dared to speak: "I'm not sure if that mark has other meanings in the Dark World, but in the Light World, it represents faith in the god Pan, that is, the Black Holy Mother...... I suppose the Dark World probably doesn't have such a thing, right?" ”

Naturally, the color card butler shook his head: "In the Dark World, there are no such ...... Or rather, we have no faith at all. After all, everyone in the Dark World is originally Dark People transformed from objects. Those so-called gods and the like also become ...... along with the incarnations of objects. Hmm, some other types of Dark People. "The Color Card Butler explained to you what form the Dark People actually take in the Dark World.

You nodded: "The third corpse was skinned and can't be clearly seen, but the first two bodies have this mark near the wound section...... I'm sure the third corpse has a similar mark as well. Also...... I think this mark can't just exist out of thin air in the Dark World, so I want to ask, have there been any new churches lately? "As you spoke, I recalled the priest I saw yesterday while handling a case...... Anyway, there really are some alternative beliefs in the Dark World.

The color card steward thought for a moment, then shook his head: "Any facility requires registration here for any setup. As far as I know, there hasn't been such an organization recently...... Of course, there wasn't one before. ”

You blinked, secretly tugged at Spamton's clothes, and quietly leaned close to his ear to ask, "If that's the case...... Where did that priest Addison from yesterday come from? ”

“…… There really are churches like this in this dark world. Have you really not received any reports?" Spamton thought for a moment, then double-checked with the color card steward. This time, the sword steward still shook his head.

"We have surveillance footage that proves that Addison yesterday was indeed a clergyman's outfit...... So, they must be either a secret society or some kind of underground organization...... We have requested to search the shop where Addison operates. "As he spoke, Spamton took out his phone, which had real-time records of all the surveillance footage. Then he switched to that time yesterday and showed the Seka butler the footage of him entering the detective agency dressed in priestly robes.

The color card butler's expression grew even more serious—you think that's about it, but he's actually quite fast, probably because the Dark World doesn't really have many rules. After all, it's more like he's just imitating the Light folk playing pretend toy, nothing more. The search warrant came down quickly. The two color card butlers followed you, while you and Spamton walked ahead. He was clearly familiar with Addison's usual habits and walked straight to the door of the addison to hand him the search warrant. Although somewhat unwilling, he could only step aside to let you in and start searching the shop. This addison mainly sells shoes. Most of the items inside the store are all kinds of shoes, including various shoeboxes and full-length mirrors for customers to check how the shoes look when worn. Besides these, there are quite a few tall boots and similar items...... There are also rain boots.

Of course, because of all this, there are still a few raincoats and other items on the shop racks, along with all sorts of cloaks that can leave you a bit stunned.

Spamton, on the other hand, accepted this very well. He directly examined the wall and floor tiles, tapping them to check for any gaps and to see if there was a switch. You reached out and gently tugged at him, softly saying, "Usually...... Suspects wouldn't hide their mechanisms in particularly conspicuous places, though sometimes the most dangerous place is the safest place...... Kind of thing, like this. "As you spoke, you glanced around the shop, finally fixing your gaze on a small plate placed on the cashier counter ahead.

It seems to be a small sample or something, with a bamboo skewer stuck in the top and a shoe that looks very, very small...... Uh, it looks more suitable for eating rather than trying it on to see how it works.

You walked straight over and twisted the little shoe. Sure enough, you pressed it and spun it. Then, a rumbling sound came from somewhere—it was loud and quite frightening. You saw Addison, who had been smiling, turn pale. You looked him over and finally gestured for Spamton to help. He easily lifted the counter and set it aside, revealing a staircase leading downward—half a solid floor, and the other half a deep, invisible passage leading down. You turned on your flashlight and eagerly walked down, while Spamton turned around and waved to the two color card butlers to come down together, following your steps down.

The further you go, the wider your view becomes, and it's no longer gloomy darkness. Instead, candles are lit around you. You turn off your flashlight, instinctively tighten Spamton's hand, and then move forward...... Further ahead, a vast expanse opens up, with candlesticks burning all around. Directly above, a crystal chandelier hangs from the ceiling, and you see the sparkling stained glass windows and the silver-white interior of the church...... It seems to be just a small chapel, with many benches, but at this moment, there are no Dark People on the benches. Directly ahead stands a statue, which looks like a goddess...... But this doesn't match any statue you've seen in the world of light. The goddess held a mirror in her hand, and at her feet were several goat statues beside her, seemingly spinning around. Directly ahead was probably the priest's alms platform for preaching...... On top lay a messy book half-turned. You don't intend to look at what the book is, nor do you intend to make a sound, because before you stand countless Dark People kneeling on the ground, praying something...... It sounds like they are murmuring something.

You and Spamton exchanged a glance, still hesitating about what to do, but Spamton just winked at you, signaling you to leave everything to him. Then he shoved you and the color card butler to the door, telling you to stay outside, while he just walked in with a smile and spoke lightly, "Everyone...... What are you doing? My friend invited me to join this church...... But he dropped me off and left. I really didn't know what to do, so I had to ask you all. ”

His tone carried a hint of pitifulness, and as you looked inside the church, those dark people instinctively became wary upon hearing Spamton's voice. But then they realized he was a popular figure in Cyber City and also an Addison, so they didn't think much of it and excitedly introduced Spamton: "Ah...... It's you! It's simple—just pray to the Holy Mother, pray for our long-lasting peace, and for the loved ones to be safe for long! The Goddess will see your sincerity and help you fulfill your wish...... The Holy Mother really will manifest! Otherwise, how could there really be a resurrection happening before us! ”

Their voices were feverish, and Spamton watched their waving hands, naturally seeing the eye-shaped marks on their arms or other parts of their bodies...... Well, they were exactly the same as the marks left on the corpses.

Then Spamton pretended to be troubled and said, "I see...... But I heard that a few regular visitors have gone missing recently? He told me that a few regular customers haven't come lately...... He suspects those people have gone missing. He said these people are also faithful followers of the Virgin Mary. ”

As soon as these words were spoken, the Dark People exchanged glances, whispering among themselves, probably pondering what to say. Finally, the first Dark Folk at the front spoke: "Here's the situation...... Recently, three people who regularly visit the church have indeed gone missing. No one knows where they really went......

"They probably just don't believe it, that's all. What's there to talk about!" Another Dark Civilian was indignant.

"Anyway, the three of them are all alone, so praying to the Holy Mother won't help much......" some muttering sounds. In short, everyone started talking at once.
