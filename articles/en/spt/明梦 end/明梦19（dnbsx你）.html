Bright Dream 19 (dnbsx you)

You subconsciously furrowed your brows—you hadn't considered that something like this could happen before. Logically speaking, to commit a disappearance case, the perpetrator would definitely find a way to kidnap the victim and then take them away. But you hadn't considered that something like this could happen, that such a thing could actually… happen, where the victim is unconsciously being controlled to walk towards the destination.

"What about that Dark One? Has he been brought under control now? Does he still have his own consciousness… If he does, does he even remember why he acted that way?" You furrowed your brows and spoke faster, just hurriedly asking about the current situation.

"We've got him under control. Now he has regained his self-awareness, but he himself doesn't know why he acted that way at the time. He has even lost the memories from before the incident and has no recollection of why he behaved like that," the Cieca butler explained. They had also suppressed the man for quite some time before this Dark Clan member finally came to and revealed the information he knew.

"And moreover, after our people blocked him, he still tried to walk toward that wall — it was only about fifteen minutes later that he finally came to his senses. We don't really know why, but in any case, we’ve confiscated all the suspicious items from him. Right now, we and this Darkfolk are still staying in this alley. Are you coming over?" The Color Card butler reported all this information to you. You hesitated for a moment, glanced back at Spamton, and then nodded in agreement. "Alright, then I’ll bring Spammy over now… It shouldn’t take too long, probably about ten minutes. But… won’t you first take that Darkfolk to the police station?" You responded, but also raised your own question. The other side seemed to go silent for a moment, and then another Color Card butler took over the call and spoke — presumably the overall person in charge of this incident.

"We hope you could take a look to see if there are any clues that we might have missed... that's about it. We hope you can provide the necessary assistance." His voice sounded quite polite, and after thinking for a moment, you still nodded in agreement in the end. Although you knew it was just a phone call and the person on the other end probably couldn't see your actions, you still did it out of habit.

"Okay, I understand. I will do my best... but I also don't know if there are any clues that I might have overlooked." After saying this, you hung up the phone. In fact, you were a little nervous. This case was different from the previous ones. In those earlier cases, the culprits were all members of the Dark Folk, and the clues were visible to everyone. Even if you didn't notice them, your reliable assistant Spamton would remind you and directly help you fill in that piece of evidence, allowing you to confidently deduce the culprit.

But this time is different. This time, the culprit is probably one of the people of light, which makes the situation particularly complicated. Moreover... some clues can only be seen by you, a person of light; no one else can see them, which makes things even more complicated. In this case, if you wanted to fabricate clues and accuse someone of being the culprit at random, wouldn't that become incredibly easy? Of course, naturally, you wouldn't actually do such a thing.

But... but you were still nervous. After all, you couldn't guarantee that you could truly meet the expectations of those color card butlers, couldn't guarantee that you could truly live up to the title of a great detective, and couldn't guarantee... that you could really catch the culprit. So you and Spamton hailed a taxi by the roadside, and after getting in, you still had that somewhat sullen expression. Spamton, smiling kindly, leaned close to you and asked softly, "My cute little sweetheart... what's wrong? Why do you look so down?" Even though he was actually nervous and uneasy too, Spamton didn't want you to develop any negative feelings because of it; he still hoped that you could be happy and joyful at all times.

You glanced at Spamton and quietly replied to him, "I'm a little worried... I'm a little worried about this case, you know, the clues are only visible to me, but what if I can't handle this case properly? What if I fail to find the crucial clue and waste everyone's time... what will I do then? Of course, such a thing might not even happen, but I'm just too scared, too scared, what if it really happens... I, I don't know..." As you spoke, a look of nervous unease and some guilt appeared on your face. You did feel a bit apprehensive, after all, you couldn't really be considered a truly skilled detective... It's just that because Spamton is by your side, solving the case seemed so smooth, and you were well aware of that.

"...It's okay, at least I will always believe in you. Even if we fall into the worst situation, I will still believe in you." Spamton froze for a moment. To be honest, he didn't expect you to have this kind of thought... You were actually worried about something so trivial in his eyes. After all, Spamton had done plenty of scams and tricks like this before. He had deceived others more than once. Of course, with you, he still maintained honesty. And precisely because the others he had tricked in the past, the Darkners, either didn't notice anything wrong or, even if they did notice, could only grit their teeth and swallow it, he simply didn't care about those things.

But... but you are probably too young, or perhaps, you are just too upright. Only people who are too upright would worry about possibly not doing well, only people who are too upright would worry about unforeseen events in the future, and only people who are too upright would worry about potential changes that might occur because of themselves.

You weren't particularly happy because of what Spamton said; you just sighed and said in a low voice, 'I know… of course I know, my spammy will believe me, but, but I don't believe myself.' Saying this, you gave a bitter smile… To be honest, the reason you came to the Dark World to open a detective agency was, first, because it was easier to set up such an institution in the Dark World, and second… because you had repeatedly messed things up in the Light World before, so no one would believe in your detective agency anymore.

After all, you only have the ability to see the murderer, but cannot directly prove it. As a result, once, twice, three times, the friends and relatives who originally supported you to become a detective drifted away, and no one was willing to pay attention to your detective agency that sounded like a scam.

However, you still opened a detective agency, and you still chose the name Mingmeng, because you hope that, with your help, those who might not have seen the truth will eventually be able to see it clearly.

So... so, although at first, your relationship seemed like a joke, as if it wasn’t very serious, you admit that the moment you touched your own heart racing, you told yourself that you truly liked Spamton… After all, after all, now he is standing right beside you, fully supporting all your decisions, believing in every word you say, and you can't help but be moved by this.

"But it's okay, really, spammy, you don't have to worry about me—I can take care of myself." You gave spamton a bright smile. Although that smile looked fake and you didn't seem genuinely happy, you didn't want spamton to be affected by your emotions. You... hoped that he could be happy, joyful, and content.

"No, it matters. How could your matters have nothing to do with me? Although I might not be able to help much, I will also do my best to assist you... because you are our famous detective." He gave you his usual smile. You blinked in response to his smile, your mind drifting somewhere for a moment, but in the end, you simply held Spamton's hand tightly, gave a faint smile, and then nodded firmly.

The taxi quickly arrived at the destination. You and Spamton got out of the car hand in hand. Spamton naturally paid the fare, and then you both walked toward the scene. You took a deep breath—you saw a Darkling sitting nearby wrapped in a blanket; it was Wireless Fast Charge. Then you saw the door on the wall, which was now half open… Your expression grew serious. You let go of Spamton's hand and walked toward the door. On that large door, you saw marks that weren’t there yesterday. So you turned to look at the others, standing there were Color Sample Butler and Spamton: 'Did you see anything?'

But they just exchanged a glance, then shook their heads. They really didn't see anything, only a wall, and you standing in front: 'We didn't see anything.'

You looked at the mark that appeared above the main gate... From the center of the circle as a starting point, three crooked lines extended outward. You, of course, knew what it was, but you just prayed that Kalksha wouldn’t appear in your dream... You hoped not.

You took out your phone and aimed it at the wall, wanting to capture the mark along with the half-open door, but strangely, you could only photograph the half-open door and not the mark—well, at least half of the tracing work was done.

You turned your head again to look at that member of the Shadow people who was sitting on the side, shivering. He was tightly clutching the blanket draped over him. You hesitated for a moment, half-crouched, and asked, 'Did you see anything on that wall?'

And the Dark Folk simply shook their head quickly: 'No.' You frowned, always feeling that he wasn't telling the truth... but for the moment, you didn't know exactly how to press this person for answers, and Spamton certainly noticed this too. He lightly furrowed his brows; he could tell that this Dark Folk was lying, but he had said too little... and there was no way to refute what little he had said.

"Alright, then his personal belongings should all be sealed as evidence, right? Can I take a look?" You chose to give up continuing to ask the witness for information for now, and instead turned around to first look at the color-coded housekeeper, who is the person in overall charge, and reached out your hand toward him.

And that color card steward also nodded, signaling the other color card stewards to bring up everything that had already been sealed in the evidence bags.

You glanced over it once, and at a rough look, there was nothing unusual. But why would something like... gold appear on a person who should be an ordinary Darkfolk worker? Even if it was just a small amount. You examined those tools, and besides that, there was something else rather suspicious: a leaflet that was entirely bright yellow, so bright it was almost dazzling.
