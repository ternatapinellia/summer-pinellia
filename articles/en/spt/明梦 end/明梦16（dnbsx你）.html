Bright Dream 16 (dnbsx you)

You blinked at him, making a serious look as if you were listening, while Spamton just sighed. He took your hand and slowly walked toward the detective agency. For a while, he didn't speak, and you waited for him to say something, but didn't take the initiative either. You didn't know what he wanted to say—was it about the case just now, about him, or just about the underworld, or ......

Your wild thoughts haven't even reached a conclusion, but you've already heard Spamton's voice. His voice sounds a bit light, as if ...... It's not meant for you to hear, but you can hear it clearly. You turn to look at Spamton, but he doesn't look at you, just ahead, though his gaze seems unfocused...... At least, you can't see where his gaze is falling. You look at his profile, and it's just the same as usual, with an elusive smile...... But you know that smile is fake.

"You know, I'm a citizen of darkness, and you're a citizen of light, right?" He didn't speak with a questioning tone, but with certainty. You tilted your head in confusion, but still responded with a 'yes.'

“…… The People of Darkness are all transformed from objects. In essence, we are not true living beings, so when we go to the world of light, we only take on the form of various objects, because we do not truly exist." Spamton's tone was very calm, but you could sense that none of this was as calm as his voice suggested; he was just...... Probably just pretending to be calm.

You don't know what to say, nor what kind of answer he wants to make, but no matter how he seems to be emotionally at the moment, it doesn't seem good...... You say, "Well, I didn't know before, but you told me, so now I know...... Alright, so what does it matter? Although you don't really exist...... Maybe that's how it is, but the feelings we feel right now aren't false—that's what is real. ”

"Yes, I know, we're still standing here, so we are real. After all, at least feelings can't be faked...... But, you know, we're objects, so unless we've suffered great damage, unless we can't restore ourselves, we can't die easily. "Spamton stopped, turned to look at you, and unlike usual, there was no smile on his face, replaced by ...... It seemed to be a bitter taste. He slowly smiled again, but in the end, only a faint bitter smile appeared.

“…… However, there was a recent news saying that some of the dark people from the neighboring dark world really turned to dust, and it was impossible for them to become dark people again. "You followed his words and naturally continued. Of course, you didn't consider what Spamton meant by saying that; you hadn't thought about yourself at all.

You look at Spamton and sigh helplessly. He doesn't seem to want to say much more about this, but he goes along with your words: "Yes, that Dark Folk will never be able to come back to life. He must have suffered a very serious injury. And my own carrier is mail...... So, it must be very difficult to suffer this level of harm. ”

Before you could speak, he changed the subject: "But that's not what I really meant. What I really want to say is, in theory, as long as the carrier of the object in the world of light hasn't been suddenly destroyed, the people of darkness cannot truly die...... Unless they have suffered excessive harm in the dark world, I still have reservations about that. As for the people of light, it's clear that you have specific bodies, specific ages, and specific ages...... living beings. When you are hurt, all you can do is wait for treatment. As time passes, you change, and as time passes, you ...... In the end, you will face death. " He said this, his tone somewhat heavy. Clearly, he was not prepared to accept your possible fate.

But you already knew this...... This matter didn't matter to him, so you just smiled and said, "Ah, I knew I would die one day—in fact, if I hadn't discovered the Dark World, if I hadn't discovered my dream here, maybe I would have chosen death long ago," your tone was cheerful, as if you weren't discussing something big, just the usual jokes, "after all...... In the world of light, some things I see through too clearly, it's just too boring! So living and dying probably make no difference. But in the world of the underworld, it's different. I've encountered all kinds of new things, and there are so many mysteries here I just can't figure out...... Ah, I even met you, just like you said. I like you, I like you, I like you, so I like being with you, so I like staying here...... That's all! "You rambled on, your voice cheerful. This matter probably isn't a big deal for you.

Spamton was stunned for a moment. He lowered his head and stopped looking at you, only speaking in a low voice: "...... The People of Light possess souls, but the People of Darkness do not, so I can't see the door you came through, nor can I go with you to the world of light. This means I will always be a citizen of darkness, and you will always be just a citizen of light...... Your lifespan and mine are not the same." His tone became somewhat obscure.

You had just lazily yawned, but when you heard him say this, your expression grew more serious. You looked at him: "I understood this when you just said it." So what? I don't feel regret. Even though as time passes, my figure changes, my voice probably won't be clear, and I won't stand by your side like this anymore, I won't regret it. I'll only be proud of having solved those cases, and I will only be happy because of your growth...... I will be moved and surprised to see you like this when I'm older, as if time has never passed. But what about you? Do you regret it? Because my youth is gone? ”

You look at him seriously, but Spamton's expression only grows more complicated. He sighs, wondering what exactly he is afraid of. He ...... It seems he is afraid that he won't grow old with you, or even die, that you will be afraid and hate him. He probably worries that you will despise him because of his identity as a Dark People...... He, well, to be honest, is selfish. He cannot accept your departure; he will probably keep missing you for years to come. He probably ...... He is not worried about your appearance or other things no longer as before, but that your inner self is no longer what it used to be. He longs for the times when he could walk side by side with you, not just to look down at you.

"I...... I'm not worried about that." His voice sounded a bit hesitant, but under your gaze, in the end, he could only answer your question. He felt a bit uneasy. Spamton hoped you wouldn't keep asking, but he knew very well that you would only keep asking. The moment you said it, he knew this wasn't the answer you wanted.

"Then what are you worried about again? Will you regret the possibilities of the future? Will you regret things that might or even never happen?" You keep asking, really curious about what Spamton is struggling with.

“…… But that's not a future that might not happen. From my perspective, it is probably a future that will definitely happen." Spamton said, unsure how to express his seemingly selfish thoughts...... To be honest, he didn't really want to show you his darker side.

But your eyes are so bright. When you look at him, your pupils are as clear as a mirror, as if they can illuminate everything in his heart. You look at him, you see the struggle and pain beneath his expression. Even though he squints and you can't see his eyes or his specific emotions, you still speak up based on your guess: "...... You think I will definitely die before you, and you will see my aging and my helplessness. Will you feel unwilling about this? Because you can no longer share those youthful and joyful years with me? Because I grow old with time, yet you still maintain your usual appearance? Will you miss the past because of this? So you are afraid, afraid you won't dare imagine the future anymore? As you spoke, you moved closer to him, staring at him intently, your breath swirling around you, as if you could see every detail on his face.

“…… So you regret it. Do you regret dating me?" You asked him in return, and your speech was fast—or rather, it was more like a pressing question. You stared at him intently, not wanting to miss any expression on his face. Then you saw traces of struggle and struggle on his face. You saw that he finally opened his eyes and stared at you intently.

His hand gripped yours tightly, his voice trembling: "...... Yes, I admit, I'm a selfish guy. After you die, I will find a way to make sure you can appear before me again, for a long time, and if that doesn't happen, I'll be trapped forever in those memories of the past. If only there was a way, 'forever' would be enough. "In the end, his voice was almost a sigh...... He just looked at you with longing, and he realized that when he said those words, he didn't seem nervous at all; instead, he felt a sense of relief. Instead...... He thought, looking into your clear eyes, thinking, maybe you already knew.

Sure enough, you just shrugged: "Ah, spammy, I knew you were a selfish guy all along...... Otherwise, why would you be so close to me? Even if you're selfish, despicable, and mean...... I just really like being by your side. Well, after all, you said you like me, I like you. I just want to ask you, do you regret it? "You said this and lowered your eyes. In fact, your mood kept dropping after you asked that question. You know maybe your answer will decide everything between you two, so you slowly loosened your grip, wanting to let go of his.

But Spamton just squeezed your hand in return, you looked up in surprise, and he just cupped your head with one hand, biting your lips. In that tangled moment, you heard his breathy voice: "I never make a deal I regret." ”
