Bright Dream 12 (dnbsx you)

You lay down on the sofa, but still felt that posture was uncomfortable. So you changed positions, and now you lay comfortably on the sofa, lying flat—no need to think about anything, just lying there blankly. Time was peaceful, and for a moment, everything around was very quiet. You truly enjoyed the environment, squinting slightly and softly sighing. Then you turned over and saw Spamton sitting there leisurely, smiling at you.

You gave a couple of awkward laughs, which was quite unreasonable, and said, "I'm so tired, and I don't have any cases right now. I should be fine with a break, right?" You said confidently, and Spamton just nodded and smiled.

"Of course no problem, my sweetheart. You can rest as long as you want." Spamton pondered. The Addison store had been temporarily sealed off, meaning this case should be closed for a while, with no further follow-up...... So during this time, you can rest as much as you want. Of course, he too.

"However, sleeping on the sofa can easily catch a chill...... My [suggestion] is that you go back to your room and rest. " he added.

To be honest, lying here makes you a bit lazy to move, since lying on the sofa is quite comfortable...... Well, but maybe it's better to lie back in bed. You rolled around on the sofa a few times, rolled your eyes, and said with a cheeky grin, "No. I'm too lazy to move anymore, I don't want to walk back...... Unless you carry me back." As you spoke, you laughed and watched Spamton's reaction. You thought he would do as you said, but in reality, you weren't entirely sure...... So you were still a bit nervous—what would Spamton's reaction be to be nervous?

Spamton did indeed walk up to you, then slowly bent down. A smile hung on his face, looking no different from before, except that his smile was even more sincere. Then he gently lifted you up from the sofa, bent your legs, and wrapped it around your arms. It was a classic princess carry. You instinctively exclaimed, wrapping your arms tightly around his neck. Your cheeks were so close that you blushed red. You couldn't say a word for a long time, while Spamton just smiled and leaned in to kiss your lips.

You pressed your lips together, your eyes darting around. Finally, when Spamton carried you to your room, you leaned in and gently kissed the corner of his mouth, very quickly. After all, you were still somewhat shy, and you hadn't really been a couple for long—but Spamton just leaned over, laid you on the bed, then rested his arm next to your head, deepening the kiss. He traced your lips, gently sucked, then bit your lips, then bit your tongue. It seemed like you were exchanging saliva. You were dizzy from the kiss, and even as he let go, you still looked at him with a hazy gaze—Spamton instinctively wanted to pull away, but you just instinctively grabbed his arm, because kissing it felt a bit cool. It felt so comfortable in this summer, you couldn't help but lower his head and then lean in to kiss him. You bit his lip, savoring the slightly cool taste almost in bliss.

At first, Spamton just bent down to let you bite your lip. In the end, he lay on the bed too, the summer blanket rolling beside you, and you wrapped around him like an octopus, savor the coolness from his body, blissfully nestled in his arms.

Spamton hugged you tightly in return, though he still pulled a thin blanket over you both...... Usually, when you covered yourself with this blanket completely, you would feel warm and want to kick it away. But today, you lay under the blanket with Spamton, feeling a bit intoxicated. You couldn't help but think this was just right—oh my, you can't even bear to leave Spamton's side or this bed, which is usually the feeling you only experience in winter.

You nestled in his arms, hugging him tightly with both hands and feet, then burying your head in his chest, half-asleep and half-awake. You really did feel exhausted, but to be honest, handling ten cases in one day in the past two days was practically draining you dry. You think you'd need several days to recover to your best condition, otherwise you'd probably be so listless these days.

You were drowsy and about to fall asleep...... You imagined lying here for the next few days, nestled in his arms, and enjoying the coolness of summer. But the good times didn't last long. A rapid knocking on the door woke you from your sleep. You opened your eyes unhappily and glanced at Spamton. He seemed to have never fallen asleep, just kept watching you. He looked at you a little embarrassed, nudging you, his voice soft and soft as his hazy pajamas: "You...... Spammy, go open the door. I wonder what exactly is going on this time. Let me lie down a little longer......" As you spoke, you turned over, kicked off the blanket, and only covered your belly. Then you closed your eyes and pretended to sleep.

Spamton sighed helplessly, reluctantly letting go of you. He really wanted to hold you tighter a little longer, to look at you a little longer—he didn't know who was disturbing their sweet dreams, but Spamton, feeling a bit annoyed, walked over and opened the door.

But when he opened the door, he found it was another color card butler, and this one looked quite anxious. Spamton's expression turned serious. He didn't know exactly what had happened, but it seemed like a serious problem—he asked, "Did something happen?" ”

The Seka butler nodded, looking panicked, and spoke rapidly, "Just now, we received a report that another Dark Commoner with no relatives or friends was found dead at home...... The time of death wasn't very long; it was probably an hour before we went to Addison to investigate." ”

Spamton's expression grew serious. In other words, killing three Dark People wasn't enough; maybe he needed more bodies...... But what use was he having so many corpses? Spamton couldn't figure it out, but he knew that if you were to handle this, you'd probably figure it out quickly...... However, he didn't want to disturb you right now. After all, he already had a suspect in this case. As long as he caught that killer first, it wouldn't matter. The remaining evidence and testimonies could be found slowly. He knew what you needed now was to rest for a while, not anything else...... He knew all you needed was rest.

So Spamton said, "I get it. I think that Addison is probably the killer. Have you managed to control him?" ”

But the Seka butler just nodded and shook his head: "He did pay a fine of 10,000 dark coins according to regulations, and after that, he left the police station. After that, we don't know where he went...... No matter what, we couldn't find his whereabouts. ”

Spamton pondered for a moment: "I remember there are surveillance cameras, but I can't find them either?" ”

The Color Card Butler nodded but shook his head again: "Indeed, but he doesn't carry any internet-connected devices, so we can't locate him or check the upcoming surveillance data. We've already searched every possible direction he might be heading, but haven't found any trace of him." ”

“…… What about the sewers? Spamton asked again. He remembered that Cyber City's sewer system was quite complex, and because of that, many passages were dirty. But for easier cleaning and maintenance, the Cyber City's sewers were enough to stand for a Dark Civilian and, of course, enough for Addison to escape...... Coincidentally, there was a sewer not far outside the police station.

The Color Card butler's expression also grew serious: "We haven't investigated along the sewers yet...... But the cyber city's sewer pipeline system is very complex, with countless forks, making it really hard to find targets or confirm where they actually go......

“…… Hmm, let's search along the line first. Maybe we'll find some clue left behind. "Spamton nodded and said.

Maybe the discussion had been going on for too long, and the noise was a bit loud. Even after tossing and turning, you could still hear faint sounds outside. You couldn't fall asleep, so you straightened your clothes, pushed open the door, and walked out as well. You yawned, "What exactly happened?" Tell me so I can hear it. ”

The Seka butler and Spamton exchanged a glance. Spamton hadn't intended to wake you, but now that you were awake, he could only sigh and say, "It's nothing serious, just that the possible culprit from that case this morning escaped—that Addison." ”

You stopped yawning, seriously thought about the morning discovery, then nodded: "Hmm, that guy really is the mastermind, but there are accomplices...... We just need to catch those accomplices first." You said.

"Accomplices?" The Seka butler clearly didn't understand, and you finally came to your senses a little. You began gesturing with both hands and said, "Yes, it's all the Dark People in his secret church. All those Dark Folk are his accomplices. Hmm, although their hands might not be stained with blood...... But you can arrest those Dark Folk first. ”

"As for the mastermind...... In any case, he did this with a purpose, probably for that purpose. So, if we can't catch him, we can first think about where his next destination might be......

“…… The newly discovered victim's body also lost both legs, so what exactly is the mastermind's goal? "The Seka butler bowed slightly and asked you for advice.

Think about it: the faith of the Black Holy Mother, the goals behind it, and the statue of the Saint in the small chapel that looks like the People of Light...... Ah, and the mark near their wounds......

You shared your idea: "I think the killer collecting these broken limb parts probably wants to resurrect that Lightfolk. After all, there's a legend that says six legs can grant immortality or something...... Although this doesn't mean six legs—he's probably a bit uneducated. But I think once the killer's purpose is confirmed, it'll be easy to find." You said confidently, and the color card butler and Spamton didn't notice anything wrong. They exchanged glances, then nodded, and Spamton pushed you back to the bedroom.

"Alright, my sweetheart, detective, get some rest. Your assistant will be able to solve this case...... You've already told us all the necessary parts of the case, so for now, you can relax and enjoy your leisure time. "Spamton told you to stay and rest, and you naturally collapsed onto the bed.
