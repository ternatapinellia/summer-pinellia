Bright Dream 17 (dnbsx you)

You smiled softly, then turned around and hugged him tightly: "Well, of course I won't regret it either—so let's go home." Your voice was soft. You felt Spamton nod gently, then you took his hand and walked toward the detective agency. The way back didn't seem very long; anyway, neither of you made much noise, just headed in that direction. When you pulled Spamton into the detective agency, you let go of his hand and walked straight into your room, lay down on the bed, yawned, and let out a long breath: "Oh no, I'm exhausted. I hope I can rest well these next few days......" You mumbled, probably because it really wasn't very comfortable.

And just as you wished, there haven't been any cases these past few days. While you were playing Gomoku with Spamton, you suddenly received a call from the Seka butler—well, it's been almost a week since the last case, and you happened to be looking for a new one, so you answered the call.

"Is there something you need?" You asked casually, still playing Gomoku with Spamton while distracting yourself to the voice on the phone. Sure enough, you did lose because of it, since he was fully focused on playing against you.

“…… For the past week, there have been daily disappearance cases. Most of the missing Dark People are young and middle-aged people working hard outside, some have wives and children, but for some reason, not a single Dark Citizen has reported it. The relevant units only reported it after discovering that the Dark People hadn't come to the company for several days. Moreover, the missing persons are still happening. Just now, we received a new report...... We hope to receive your and Mr. Spamton's help. ”

You raised your eyebrows. To be honest, you hadn't expected this case, like the last one, to be a serial case. Although you're not very good at this kind of case, since it's not convenient to find the culprit all at once, ...... Well, you're still very interested in cases like this, so no wonder the Seka butler came to you. After all, you're the only detectives making a big splash in the dark world right now.

So you simply responded and hung up, then pulled the spamton, gave him a kiss as a reward for the winner of the game, and then excitedly pulled him outside.

You said enthusiastically, "Hmm, there's a new case this time, probably a serial disappearance case. Once we get to the police station, those color card butlers will explain the situation to you!" But I don't know how those victims are doing now, and ...... Hmm, I don't know why the victims' relatives are in such a state. ”

"In short, those people have disappeared, but their relatives haven't reported it; instead, they have kept it hidden...... I think there must be more to the story, and maybe those relatives benefited from it...... Well, of course, there's also the possibility that they were threatened and didn't dare to speak up, but I don't think it's that simple...... "In short, you talked on and on for a long time, looking quite interested, while Spamton just smiled quietly as you chattered, only speaking again when you were almost silent.

"I wonder, do those missing individuals share any common traits?" Spamton asked. Of course, you're currently taking a regular taxi in Cyber City. To avoid leaking case information, you speak in a low voice.

You hesitated for a moment and answered Spamton's question: "I don't know. I didn't ask the color card butler about that. Let's ask carefully when we arrive later, okay?" ”

Spamton nodded, then took your hand proactively. Although you didn't know what he meant, you still shook his hand in return...... After a while, you finally arrived at the police station.

Spamton took your hand and got out of the car, then you entered the police station one after the other. You cleared your throat twice and put on a serious expression: "So what exactly is going on? Do these cases have specific details, and do you have statements and statements from those involved?" ”

"Of course, I think the most important thing is to show us the case summary first." Spamton followed you and said, and upon hearing this, you crossed your arms and nodded vigorously.

The color card butler bowed to both of you and then handed you a set of documents. I suppose this document was probably the case summary—but since there was only one summary, you and Spamton had to huddle together to look at it. Halfway through, your heads almost bumped into each other, but your furry heads were still leaning together.

The case summary is very simple, showing that over the past week, a total of seven Dark People have gone missing—exactly one per day. According to the current timeline prepared by the Color Card Butler from the parties involved, these seven Dark People have disappeared at different times.

The seven missing Dark Folk were listed in order: wireless fast charging, color card butler, Renwu Admin Ji, mouse, virus, wall attachment, and an orange Addison...... It was still unclear which Dark folk would disappear next, but none of the missing ones had repeated, and their habits were completely different. For a moment, it was hard to guess what they had in common.

"Hmm...... Is there any similarity between the last sighting locations, the last witness, and their outfits when they disappeared...... "You start to interrogate them, though you think it probably won't help—after all, the Dark People have different builds, so it's hard to find any similarities, and their clothes are hard to find similarities either."

Sure enough, the color card butler shook his head: "Their outfits before disappearing aren't much in common. After all, the clothes of these Dark People are different, and the last witness was also different...... But the places where they were sighted seem to have some similarities." ”

"So where are the similarities?" Spamton pressed.

"Hmm...... It's all a remote alley, so I don't think there's much to say about that. After all, it's common knowledge that cases are more likely to happen in remote alleys. ”

"So, is there any surveillance footage near the spot where you witnessed it?" Spamton pondered for a moment, then asked another question, while you blinked and just curled up beside him, quietly observing the color card butler's expression. Well, he wasn't lying; he really believed it...... Well, it seems there's no need to pursue this clue further.

But you still follow behind Spamton to check the surveillance footage from that time. The equipment looks a bit old, since the footage is even a bit blurry in black and white, which isn't very suitable. Curious, you watch these images. These footage are usually from the alley entrance, and after the missing Dark People are captured entering the alley entrance, there are no other footage.

"We've investigated those alleys; they're all dead-end roads, and we haven't found anything around them...... So, maybe those people just vanished into thin air. " the color card butler added.

Spamton stared at the surveillance footage, rubbing his chin. Maybe he was lost in thought, but you looked at Spamton curiously, gently tugged at his sleeve, and softly said, "What are you thinking about now? Do you already have some thoughts about this case?" ”

Spamton turned his head and nodded gently at you, softly saying, "Yes, I already have some ideas." Then he pointed to the surveillance footage, having the color card butler bring him a map of the cyber world, and with his pen, circled the locations of the seven alleys on it.

Looking at the positions of these seven alleys alone, there was no repetition, or rather, they seemed chaotic and completely unfounded; it was just that coincidentally, these alleys were all close to one of the locations...... Hmm, or rather, a certain place. When he drew them on the map, you could tell. You pointed to a dead end alley in the center of these alleys, a bit puzzled: "So will the next missing person case happen here?" ”

Spamton, however, shook his head. He drew the location of a door on that dead end and said softly, "No, do you remember? This is the location of the gate leading to the World of Light that you took me to last time...... I think maybe our movements were noticed by someone, and then that person tried to use these Dark People to get to the World of Light." ”

"Ah......" You opened your mouth wide, looking a bit dazed, "So when we get to that spot, is there a chance we'll find something?" ”

At this moment, the Seka butler chimed in again: "We've also investigated this dead end, Miss Y/N. As it turns out, we found nothing. It's just a dead end, nothing worth seeing. But just in case, we've left the police nearby to prevent further disappearances. So far, nothing has happened over there." ”

You scratched your head, looked at the color card butler and Spamton, and finally suggested, "Um...... After all, I'm a citizen of light. What I see might be a bit different from yours. Anyway, let me take a look—maybe I'll discover some new clues. ”

So, at your request, you took the police station car and returned to that alley. You went in to see if there were any clues nearby that only you could notice.

Well, the facts proved...... Only you could see the door on that wall, and none of the surrounding Dark People could see it. You felt a bit frustrated, but still walked up carefully to observe, then your eyes widened—because you noticed that beside that door was a bloodstain unique to the Light Tribe. The color of the blood had already darkened, clearly not fresh enough, probably from several days ago. Not only that, you also saw the kind that only the Dark People had...... Yellow and red mixed together, swirling shapes, blood-like but also like slime.

You point to the marks and ask Spamton, "Can you see those marks?" "Those marks remain by the door, seeming to seep out from inside or simply splashing onto the door frame."

But Spamton just shrugged, shook his head, and told you he couldn't see at all. To be honest, he was starting to feel a bit restless at this point...... Why is it that you can see it, but he can't? What if this happens more often, and what if he has no chance to protect you—after all, he can't see at all?
