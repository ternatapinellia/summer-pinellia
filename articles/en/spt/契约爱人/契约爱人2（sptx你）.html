Contract Lover 2 (sptx you)

You instinctively waved your hand, because the other hand was holding Spamton's hand—so you only waved one hand, even though you were somewhat lacking in common sense, you knew it was wrong.

“…… Living together is fine, but we shouldn't sleep in the same bed, right?" Your voice was still a bit stammering, and there was a burning sensation on your face...... In short, your heartbeat seemed to quicken, but you didn't understand why. All you knew was that this feeling was terrible, very bad, so bad that you wanted to escape.

So you instinctively take two steps back and try to let go of Spamton's hand: "I...... I'll just sleep on the floor. I don't mind where I sleep, or the sofa downstairs is fine......

But Spamton interrupted you, gripping your hand tightly to keep you from retreating. He just kept smiling: "Ah...... But, Miss, according to the way love works, we should share the same bed. ”

Your eyes widened slightly. Indeed...... If you had needed this according to your initial terms of the deal, and he also provided you with a method, then ......

You no longer hesitate, just took his hand back and said softly, "Ah...... I understand, then...... I, I will share a bed with you. ”

As you spoke, you scratched your cheek, feeling for some reason it only felt hotter and hotter. Maybe it was just your imagination? Spamton looked at your flushed face, just smiled and said nothing.

"Then ......" He was about to say something more, but you suddenly stopped in front of him as if remembering something.

"Well, that's right...... Although I know what you want is important, it's not good to just eat and drink freely at your house all the time. I'll give you money. "You thought about it. Although the operation is complicated, it's true that currency in the Light World can be exchanged for Dark Coins, and you'll probably be living in the Dark World for a long time, so exchanging a bit more isn't a problem.

Spamton opened his mouth, wanting to reject your proposal. After all, he doesn't need your money. He just needs you to step in occasionally. With the Light Founder's hype, he can make several times over.

But you ignored his hesitant words and continued on your own: "And then...... So, do you need me to do anything else? Because if you calculate it this way, I'm your lover now, so I have to help you too...... Right? "You're not sure, so your tone ended with a hint of doubt. You've never been in love, just know some knowledge from books, and know that both parties in a relationship should give to each other. But come to think of it, you did only say you wanted to learn about love, never what kind of relationship you would have with Spamton.

But now, Spamton just smiles and silently watches you.

You cough twice, your voice growing weaker with a hint of uncertainty: "We're ...... now Are you lovers? ”

Then Spamton nodded seriously: "Yes, because of the [Love Transaction], we are now in a [lovers] relationship." ”

You quietly responded with an 'oh,' feeling a fleeting melancholy in your heart for some reason. But you quickly pulled yourself together and eagerly asked, "Is there anything else I can do for you?" After all, as lovers, doing something for you is something I should do quickly......" Before he could finish, Spamton extended his index finger to cover your mouth.

He tilted his head slightly and smiled, saying, "Ah...... I only need to attend the ceremony; I don't need to pay extra. ”

You had your mouth covered, so you could only blink and look at him. Spamton coughed dryly too, belatedly realizing how soft the lips under his fingers were, so he quickly withdrew his hand.

"And [housework: from Baidu Encyclopedia], I'll take care of everything, like [kitchen operation services] and [daily household management services]...... What you need to do is [enjoy life]. "He's explaining to you very seriously, and you look at this guy, who is a bit shorter than you, listening in surprise. You really hadn't thought about housework, but now that he's brought it up, you just remember.

"No need, I can leave these things to me. I'm used to it...... How could I trouble you? "You waved your hand again. But Spamton just held your hand and walked down the stairs.

"I think, Miss Y/N, what you need is [enjoying love], [enjoying life]—and since I have made a deal with you, the [heart-shaped item] is a valuable item, so you don't need to make any more [redundant steps]." He spoke softly, slowly explaining everything to you. Although he didn't need the heart-shaped object for now, he had a feeling he might use it someday—maybe someday in the future.

You pressed your lips together, suddenly feeling uneasy. You weren't used to this kind of life. After all, in the past, what you truly had—was only obtained through genuine effort. The clothes you wore, your school tuition, your school supplies—all were earned by doing housework at home and by your parents. And now, it's just a transaction, yet another person is willing to give so much. What is it all for?

…… Or maybe that deal really does mean a lot to him, you think. And that unrealistic, somewhat light, even floating feeling like floating on clouds kept going until Spamton pulled you into the car and stopped at the entrance of a store. He politely held your hand and let you get out, then led you into the store.

"You should be missing [personal items]?" Please [spend as much as you like]." He nodded slightly at you, then pointed to the clothes in the store cabinet.

You hesitated, pursing your lips, your toes spinning on the ground.

"I...... Can I pick whatever I want? "You asked, not sure—except for the necessary change of clothes, there weren't many extra clothes at home. You could only go to school wearing faded clothes. Even though you didn't think it was a big deal, inevitably some eyes that were hard to ignore were directed at you.

"Of course, feel free to choose your hobbies." He said.

Even though you still find his way of speaking a bit obscure and hard to understand, after half understanding it, you finally get what he means. He gently squeezes your hand, and you take a deep breath before heading straight into the shop. The colorful look dazzles you, but for you, as long as the clothes are comfortable, that's enough. Many of the clothes you don't need are really needed. So, based on the current weather, you pick out two sets of white shirts and black sweatpants, plus a jacket—these are enough for you to change into. Turning the jacket inside and out will last another month, and it'll be about time to change clothes.

Spamton stood at the counter, watching you. The owner of this shop was a yellow Addison, if you didn't see it wrong...... He seemed to have just hidden something inside? But you had no interest in pursuing it. You just walked over to the counter with those five pieces of clothing and cautiously glanced at Spamton: "...... I've picked out. ”

Spamton flipped through the five pieces of clothing you brought, then fell silent. You swallowed hard, almost thinking Spamton thought you had chosen too much. Just as you were about to ask to put two more pieces back, you heard Spamton's somewhat puzzled voice: "Just bought [daily necessities]?" "[Miss]'s [Aesthetic Concepts] need to be updated." ”

You hesitated and blinked, and the few pieces of clothing in your hand were placed on the counter. Then Spamton pulled you aside and started sweeping through each shelf one by one. He asked about your size, then held up the clothes matching the size and gestured toward you. At first, it was just a regular shirt and pants, just a matter of patterns and patterns.

"I...... I don't need that many clothes in daily life...... You try to stop Spamton, but he just waves his hand casually: "There's [extra space: walk-in closet] at home. I'm not just picking out for you, but also my personal [picking preferences]. ”

As soon as you say that, you shut up, but Spamton keeps holding the clothes and gesturing at you. No matter how you look at them, these clothes must have been chosen for you, right? White, blue, and pink shirts; green, blue, yellow long-sleeved shirts; and some pink, white, and beige short knit sweaters. He just picked out each one and tossed them toward the cash register. In no time, the clothes piled up into a small mountain.

And from start to finish, you're just a clothes hanger. Spamton gestures the clothes around you, thinks it's about right, and then buys them with a wave of his hand. He shows you through everyday clothes and finally stops at the front of a series of Lolita clothes. These dresses are generally quite expensive, the kind you wouldn't even dare to look at. In fact, you hardly ever wore a dress in the world of light—because it's extremely inconvenient, inconvenient for your movements, for housework, or for working outside.

And you don't dare to dress too pretty...... After all, your parents have told you about many girls being harmed by bad people because they dressed too revealingly and too pretty. You don't dare to gamble on the possibility that you won't encounter danger.

But Spamton casually took a dark green dress from the shelf and gestured on you: "Hmm...... This outfit is just what I want." As he spoke, he tossed the dress to the cashier, about to get another set, but after hesitating for a long time, you finally mustered the courage to speak: "Mr. Spamton, you don't need to buy me a dress or anything...... I probably wouldn't wear it either. ”

The previous clothes were still suitable for everyday wear, and you'll wear them to some extent.

"No need to add 'Sir'...... Also, these [incredibly beautiful dresses] I think suits you very well. "He didn't look up at you, just casually rejected what you had just said.

"But...... You wanted to say more, but he interrupted you again.

He smiled warmly: "We...... Now we're lovers. So...... I think I need "pleasing to the eye," and I also need you to be "joyful." ”
