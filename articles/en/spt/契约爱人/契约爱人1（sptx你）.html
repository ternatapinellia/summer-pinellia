Contract Lover 1 (sptx you)

I didn't have an outline, so I just wrote it casually...... Busy with my graduation thesis and such, which is tiring, so I wrote something short to make some adjustments.

——

You wander aimlessly down the commercial street.

To be honest...... You really are just in a tangled mind, wandering around casually. You really are, even if you just wandered aimlessly along this street, you wouldn't want to return to the world of light. Or rather...... For a short time, you don't want to go back.

You don't know who to look for, or rather, who to explain your inexplicable panic and irritation—even though your mother has painfully scolded you for having no compassion, and you don't even feel sympathy for her. Yet in the end, you still hope you can change.

After all, after hearing those words...... You felt as if you had fallen into an ice cave, instinctively running away from home. You sincerely hoped to change yourself, to change her perception of herself. Maybe then she could continue to love her.

But what exactly is love? You don't understand, as if you have never experienced it yourself, nor have you ever given such feelings to others. You are just a creature loved by no one, loved by no one, and not loved by anyone.

After taking a few deep breaths, you finally stopped. At that moment, you looked up and paused, only to notice that almost everywhere on the street were advertisements—the same one.

[Any trade is possible!] Call SPAMTON G. SPAMTON now! He will fulfill all your needs. Start watching now and become a big shot! 】

You look at the phone number written on the ad. Honestly, you don't really believe all of this...... After all, how could any wish come true? Is it a universal wish-granting machine? That possibility is way too low.

But with a 'give it a try' attitude, or maybe just a bit lost, you dialed the phone and listened to the beeping on the other end. You swallowed nervously, feeling inexplicably nervous.

Immediately after, the call was answered.

Through the phone, the voice crackled with electric currents and a few random pauses, but you still heard him clearly: "Welcome to [call] [Hot Topic] big shots! Please [inquire] about your specific precautions?" ”

You didn't expect him to speak like that, but after being stunned for two seconds, you still understood what he meant, so you just cleared your throat and tried to stay serious, but still stammered a bit from nervousness: "I...... I want to know what love is. ”

The voice on the other end paused for a barely noticeable moment, then returned to the same tone, carrying a sense of excitement—or rather, full of vitality—almost jumping: "Ah...... Miss, what about your [Transaction Terms]? This Miss [Citizens of Light]? ”

In fact, he almost understands the voices of everyone in the cyber world, so when he hears your voice, although he can't be 100% certain, there is a 99% chance you are a citizen of light.

It turned out he had bet right.

You paused for a moment, but didn't ask how he knew you were a citizen of light. You just softly said, "Conditions...... Anything is fine." "It doesn't matter what you give, because you never really cared about all of this. In fact...... Maybe finding love is just giving yourself a reason to live. The love you long for from your mother may not be what you want.

"Anything is fine...... So, a [heart-shaped object]? Spamton drew out his voice and asked you. He only planned to retreat to advance. If you refused, he could naturally make the following demands, even thinking through the content.

But you only think about what the heart-shaped object he mentioned is, and you realize it's probably the one stored inside your body, dancing with every beat of your body, shining with light...... The soul. If you lack it, although your movement isn't much inconvenient, you might still feel a bit empty.

But at this point, you only agreed to his request: "This one? I agreed...... So does that mean the deal is done? ”

Spamton didn't expect you to actually agree; he originally only intended to ...... to make you agree to a trivial request. After all, if the People of Light stepped in, his value would definitely rise even further, and all he needed to do was tell you some trivial things. After all...... What is love? Thinking about it carefully, he couldn't explain it clearly, nor had he ever felt it specifically.

And now, once you agree, his thoughts will have to change accordingly. Spamton squinted, still smiling: "Alright, [deal confirmed]...... "In that case, please go to [this location][Baidu Encyclopedia: Meetup]. I'll let you [experience love]." ”

Since you agreed to give him a heart-shaped object...... He had no choice but to try hard to make you experience love. He told you a location: a property he had purchased outside the Queen's mansion. He originally planned to sell it within a few days, since he was already living in the Queen's mansion and didn't need another place.

And now...... He planned to temporarily move out of the Queen's mansion. Fortunately, there were still some basic daily necessities stored in that house, so there was no need to add anything extra.

You responded with a 'yes,' and after asking countless people of darkness, you finally arrived at that place. This residence looks like a double villa—not particularly large, but it's more than enough for two people to live in.

You take a few steps forward and start staring blankly at the keyhole on the door. Right, you don't have the key, and you can't get in. In the end, you have to crouch down, prop your chin up, and wait for Spamton to come.

Fortunately, before long, a red car with its paint reflecting the glow stopped in front of you. Then, a person got out of the driver's seat, wearing a red suit—because he was all white, the red suit stood out even more. He smiled warmly, slightly tidying his hair that was actually not messy. When he got out of the car and stood in front of you, you realized he was about the same height as you, or even a bit shorter...... Slightly shorter than you.

And he smiled and reached out his hand to you: "Are you Miss Y/N? I'm SPAMTON G. SPAMTON! Very happy to meet you for the first time." ”

You hesitated for a moment, then shook his hand as well, then shook it up and down: "Mm, nice to meet you...... What should I do next? ”

Spamton stepped forward, took out the key, opened the door, then bent down slightly and made a gesture of invitation to you, holding your hand and leading you inside: "Well then, Miss, please start with the [sightseeing]." ”

You walked in—the house really looked big and empty, but it was deserted and had little daily necessities. It looked like no one had lived there for a long time. Although it was a bit strange why Spamton insisted on calling you here...... But you usually wouldn't be confused by others' decisions, since you'd long been used to your parents and those around you never making decisions based on your opinions.

You were led inside, yet still stood at the doorway, your body slightly tense—you turned to look at Spamton, your voice a bit weak: "Mr. Spamton...... So, could you please show me around? ”

Spamton opened his eyes, glanced at you with those different-colored pupils, then nodded: "Well then, [Miss], please [keep pace]." ”

He said this, gently holding your hand, sounding a bit cautious. After all, he wasn't sure if you would accept this kind of behavior—someone of the opposite sex suddenly grabbing your hand or something.

But it seems you don't feel much about it. His hand feels a bit cold to you, even a bit novel, so you can't help but gently hold his hand...... Well, you know it's not good to have too much contact with the opposite sex, but just holding hands is pretty normal, right?

But Spamton froze, a bit surprised—after all, your warm hand gripped his. He subtly turned his head to glance at you, his Adam's apple moving slightly. He didn't know what you were thinking, he just felt ...... At this moment, his chest felt tingling and ticklish, something strange.

But in the end, he just smiled, held your hand, and slowly walked around the living room, introducing the facilities to you.

"So, this side is the [backup sanitary facility] room. Also, there's a [dedicated sanitary facility] room on the second floor." He pointed to a small cubicle near the door and said to you. You nodded to show you understood, so Spamton took your hand and carefully continued walking forward.

"This is the [Kitchen Safety Facilities] room. But [Miss] doesn't need to go in; I'll prepare [reasonable and tasty meals] for her." "He pointed to a room blocked by a glass door, where you can see things like the refrigerator, oven, stove, and sink.

You hesitate for a moment and ask, "So do I have to do nothing?" This actually makes you a bit uncomfortable, since when you're home, you often have to prepare breakfast, lunch, and dinner just because you're the only one around...... Sometimes, even when your parents are around, you still have to do this. Not only that, but sometimes you get all sorts of chores at home. No, it's often.

Spamton, on the other hand, just nodded and shook his head. His voice was light and a bit sweet: "No, [Miss], of course you have [necessary things], and your [essential things] are to feel love." "Although he wasn't really sure what love really felt like...... But probably, at least this is how it should be done? He had at least read some novels and short videos about the world of light, so he knew a bit about it.

You stared at Spamton for a while, until her body stiffened, and you really thought you'd said something wrong. Then you slowly nodded, your tone tinged with hesitation and disbelief.

"Alright...... I understand. ”

He breathed a sigh of relief and pointed to a small door behind the kitchen: "That door leads to 'Ruins Beautiful.' You can go there for 'relaxation and entertainment.'" ”

You tilted your head, not quite understanding: "What is ruin beauty?" "Although you can mostly guess based on the actual objects and context, you really don't understand this."

Spamton coughed dryly twice, his voice growing quieter: "...... Garden. ”

You blink, about to say something more, but Spamton just pulls you in another direction, and you enter a spacious room.

"This is a [leisure and entertainment facility]. [Miss] can enjoy [your free time]." He explained that you noticed the living room had a long sofa, a coffee table, and a bookshelf. Other than that, there was nothing else to do. Spamton then pulled you out. It was a small corridor with a spiral staircase leading to the second floor. He held your hand and led you up. The second floor, besides the large bathroom he had just mentioned, was also quite simple. There were two rooms of moderate size, except one had a tightly locked door.

Spamton glanced lightly at the room, then pulled you into a relatively large room, which had a large double bed. Spamton opened his arms and introduced you: "This is our shared residence. This house has only a [single residence], so the lady wants to share some rest time with me." ”

For once, you felt a bit stuck. You blinked at the big bed in the room, imagining what might happen the day after tomorrow, and blushed belatedly.
