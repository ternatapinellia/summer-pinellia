The Dark World School's class schedule...... The schedule is pretty relaxed. You double-checked and found that there were basically only one or two classes a day, or sometimes none. You weren't sure if this was a special treatment for the People of Light, and the course content was quite relaxed. The Light People you exchanged for were in a separate class.

But every time you walk into the classroom, you can immediately see those four Spamton G. Spamton guys sitting in the same row, even elbowing and joking at each other—probably to save your seats? But when you look at the empty classroom, it's obvious they don't need to take seats. But their bickering is so obvious that the other Light People all look at them, even the newly arrived Dark People teacher has pupils in shock, pointing at them in silence.

You feel a bit embarrassed, although you only need to pretend not to know them — but as soon as you walk into the classroom, those people of darkness spot you, and immediately spamton neo start calling out loudly to you: 'Y/N, my [beloved]——'

It's so awkward, you almost don't want to bother with them, but then Big Shot smiles and says, "Y/N, there are [reserved seats] here. You can come and enjoy the [Privilege Aura]." ”

You felt even more embarrassed—because now the rats and classmates' gazes were fixed on you again, forcing you to grit your teeth and walk toward those four Dark Citizens. The other three wore smug expressions, but White was a bit uneasy. As you approached, she whispered, "Y/N...... Are we the [Troublemakers]? ”

You looked at him, sighed, and just rubbed your forehead: "...... No, don't overthink it. "Thinking about it, you agreed to it yourself, so there's nothing to regret. So you just walked over, pushed them aside, and sat down in the very center, trying to be perfectly balanced.

"Also, you don't have to reserve a seat for me...... Didn't you see that not many people came to class? "You took a deep breath.

"But I want to meet Y/N at the first sight...... I want to meet my wife at the very first sight. "Spamton whispered.

You can't do anything about them. In short, the class doesn't forbid others from coming to audit, so the four of them openly sit beside you, occasionally switching seats, because Spamton and White, who sit on the outside, will protest. You won't just see the poor little one being bullied like that.

The classroom atmosphere was also very relaxed; the teacher basically didn't bother with you. Fortunately, the exchange students who could come were all good students, and the class continued like this.

However...... Sometimes you do get drowsy because the teacher's lectures are very boring, and you don't take notes carefully. But when class finally wakes up, a notebook is pushed over—sometimes big-shot, sometimes white—depending on who sits next to you, and all the notes you haven't recorded in class.

Every time, you opened your mouth as if wanting to say something but held back, so you had to lean in and kiss the person taking notes on the forehead. This caused Spamton and Spamton Neo to compete to take notes for you, and in the end, because the handwriting wasn't nice enough, you sent them back.

Since you are the only one in the dormitory, you don’t really mind them coming in and out of your room. Occasionally, when your homework is really overwhelming, big shot will directly grab your computer to help you with your homework, and spamton neo will forcibly push you onto the bed to make you rest, because they say that people of darkness don’t need to rest, it doesn’t matter to them, but people of light must rest.

White carefully covers you with a blanket and puts you in an eye mask. As for Spamton...... It serves as a pillow in your arms.

They all envy Spamton for receiving such treatment, while Spamton, on the other hand, always fears that this privilege might disappear at any moment. All they can do is open your eyes after you fall asleep, repeatedly sketching and tracing your brows in your mind, recording your quiet posture in your sleep, carefully smoothing your forehead when you seem to have a nightmare and furrowing your brow, and remembering your smile during sweet dreams.

So, when you wake up, he will smile but reveal a subtle cautiousness and say, "Y/N, are you satisfied with the [companionship service]?" ”

You yawn, still a bit half-asleep: "Of course I'm satisfied...... It's nice to have someone to hold you. "You mutter, to be honest, you usually have some anxiety, but when you hold Spamton, that feeling disappears, and you get a good night's sleep.

Only then did Spamton let out a barely noticeable sigh of relief, but at the same time, the others seemed dissatisfied. Spamton neo came over, lowering his head to look at you. The slight pressure made you instinctively widen your eyes and shrink back. Perhaps noticing your movement, spamton neo slightly raised his body again: "...... [no intention to intimidate], but I can also [provide services] [companionship services]." His voice was sincere, tinged with a hint of urgency.

You paused for a moment, then Big Shot and White, who had just been helping you tidy up the room, also came over and said in unison, "I can also provide [companionship services]." ”

Spamton glared angrily at them, and you glared at them, hesitated for a moment, and looked at them. Seeing Big Shot's deliberately put on smiles and the inexplicably lively face, you couldn't help but get distracted and cough twice.

"Then...... Then let's take turns and try with someone else. Let's start with Big Shot. "As you spoke, you were still staring blankly at Big Shot's face.

Big Shot's smile grew even brighter, leaning in even closer to you, while you stared at him blankly.

He softly asked, "Do you really like looking at my [face]?" As he spoke, he deliberately leaned close to your ear, his voice mixed with warm breath reaching your ear. You felt your ears a bit damp and a little hot—no, it seemed like your whole face was a bit hot, so much so that you couldn't help but cover your face, leaving only your eyes visible as you looked at them.

The other three Dark People glared angrily at Big Shot, who was smugly apparent, while you just mumbled and replied, "It's a turn...... Take turns, anyone can ......" Your voice grew quieter and quieter.

Luckily, the Dark World School has prepared single dorms for you, and no one is coming for rounds. The beds are relatively large, so you and another Dark Citizen can fit in.

They behaved quietly, just resting with you in their arms. To your surprise, when they held you, your inner irritation seemed to calm down. So every morning when you woke up, you would snuggle tightly into their arms, then blush, push them away.

For some reason, it seemed they weren't arguing much anymore, as if they had tacitly agreed to the same goal.

Before class every day, Spamton Neo will hand you a cup of milk tea, all with your favorite flavors. Spamton will place your desk in advance with the books and notebooks you'll use in the class, Big Shot will find the relevant content for your review, and White will help you tidy up your clothes for the day. If the temperature suddenly drops or rises, he will remind you to change—you feel like you've been turned into a loser by these four Dark Citizens, and every morning when you wake up, you see them greeting you together.

Maybe after some time, you've gotten used to enjoying it all, and getting used to kissing them—at first just the forehead, then the cheek, and finally the ...... the corner of the mouth, and finally the lips.

You usually get pulled hard until your lips are bitten until they're swollen and red, and then you let go. This always makes you feel embarrassed, but...... You have to admit, that unfamiliar warmth and sense of comfort wraps around you, making you feel like crying and becoming increasingly reluctant to part with them.

You usually don't check the phone brought from the world of light, because most of it doesn't have content you're interested in, and it even has contact information for people you dislike but have to interact with—your parents.

To prevent them from causing trouble with the school, you actually charged your phone and didn't set it to silent.

But when your phone rings, your body instinctively stiffens, your face turns pale, your hands tremble, and you don't know what to do for a moment.

At this point, Spamton Neo rushes over to answer your phone. You don't know who is on the other end or what is being said. He doesn't turn on speaker and covers his phone a bit, not wanting you to hear.

And all you can hear is Spamton Neo's voice: "I'm Y/N's [legal lover]. She's been [in good shape] lately and doesn't need [extra attention]. ”

Then he hung up the phone and looked at you as if asking for a reward.

You paused for a moment, then slightly curved your eyebrows, leaned in and tiptoed to kiss the corner of his mouth, then hurried off to the classroom for the next class, leaving spamton neo standing there grinning foolishly and touching the corner of his mouth.

But a year is neither too long nor too short. Soon, it's time for your exchange student life to end, and you should return to the World of Light.

The other classmates left early. Some were eager to see their parents, some wanted to rush to see their loved ones, and some were just there to watch game updates. In the end, only you remained, holding your suitcase and standing before the beam of white light, hesitating.

Those four Dark People stand behind you, watching your back, anxiously waiting for your decision.

After hesitating for a moment, you finally put down your luggage, wiped away the tears streaming from the corner of your eyes, and then turned to walk toward them.

“…… I'm not leaving." You opened your phone, and for the first time mustered the courage to type for your parents, saying: You don't love me, I don't love you. I won't go back again.

Afterwards, he blocked all their contact information.

White once again opened an ad pop-up that read: "We love you."

Big Shot somehow pulled out 99 roses and scattered them at your feet.

Spamton cautiously leaned over and grabbed your pant leg: "Can you apply for remarriage? Let's start with a [lover's relationship]?" ”

Spamton's neo just rushed over and hugged you, shouting with excitement, "Y/N, I'm your 'forever lover'!" ”

You didn't push away Spamton Neo's hand, but instead hugged the other three Dark People who had come closer: "Mm...... I love you too. As for remarriage and all that...... Let's think about it again! ”

Actually, you still don't quite understand what love really is, but you want to dedicate all the experiences you have to them...... This is probably love, right? But it's okay, you still have a lot of time to think about this question, and you think they will always be with you.
