spamton.zip (1)

Yawning, thinking about the homework the teacher assigned, you reluctantly search online for pirated software—the genuine version costs you about a hundred or two hundred silver dollars, and you wouldn't be happy about it! And the teacher said you should go online and search for it yourselves, just find a pirated copy to use. Unfortunately, the teacher didn't give you an installation package for the pirated software, so you could helplessly search online with your limited computer skills.

After skipping several pages that looked like paid ads, you finally found a page that looked ad-free and, surprisingly, even quite clean. Of course, you also cautiously avoided the security installation requirement to download some software manager, choosing a normal installation.

The mouse clicked, and you finally downloaded the installation package—though for some reason, what was the name of the package? spamton.zip you had a bad feeling about it. Why did the name look like it...... What kind of spam did it look like? Did you download it by mistake?

Haha, you can't just put a virus on your own computer after all this fuss. You gave a couple of awkward laughs, but still tried it out and extracted the installation package. Next, in the folder that opened automatically, you saw four exe files filled with garbled text and a bunch of json files. Besides that, the file your teacher requested indeed popped up.

At the same time, your computer flashes a virus warning, warning that those garbled files are all infected and may harm your computer. You click the mouse, block those files, frown and drag them to the trash to delete them, but for some reason...... Once those files are deleted, the JSON files in the folder and the pirated software you want will disappear together.

You frantically struggled for a long time and even downloaded powerful uninstall software, but it still didn't work.

With no other choice, you click on the pirated software, hoping it will work.

But no matter how much you tapped, nothing happened—just as you were feeling discouraged, you saw another prompt pop up in the center of your computer, saying the software was trying to launch other apps at the same time, and the system had blocked them from running.

You searched for ages and finally found this pirated software! With no other options, you have to go into Windows Security System and pull out those four garbled software programs, setting them to run safely.

This time, the pirated software finally got running. However...... The four garbled files also started running. This time, the filenames were no longer garbled; as the pirated software unfolded, the four garbled files also gained their own names.

It seems to be arranged from old to new, according to the change time: the first file is white, the second is called big shot, and for some reason, the third and fourth files look very similar—one is Spamton, the other is Spamton Neo. Meanwhile, several little figures who look completely different but have similar hairstyles appear on the screen. They call them people...... They don't really look alike, since people aren't this color scheme and don't have long noses.

You tried to turn off the four gadgets that appeared, but even if you opened Task Manager and forcibly shut it down, it would restart the next second and appear on your screen, looking like it had been infected with a virus. You pinched your nose and had no choice but to hurry and finish the homework left by the teacher first. After all, you didn't have the money to buy another computer, nor did you have time to reinstall it.

I don't know what crazy the teacher is, but lately I've been assigning homework related to this software, leaving you no time to think about the little gadgets on the computer screen.

However, aside from staying on your computer screen to block your view a bit and occasionally moving around, they don't do anything particularly distracting you, so you temporarily forget about it.

…… Besides when you're scrolling through short videos on your computer and that big shot suddenly raises the webpage pop-up and throws it out of the screen, making it impossible to find it. Besides that white, who especially likes to run ad pop-ups on your computer screen, as long as you're not doing anything serious, it will pop up ads to block your view or even block the videos you're scrolling through. Besides Spamton, which suddenly throws out some blue egg-like things that make you unable to open game software, forcing you to click on the blue egg and then the computer is forced to open a large, explicit photo that looks like Spamton is showing off itself. Except for Spomton Neo, which occasionally wraps a green cable around your shutdown button, forcing you to keep your computer open every day, everything else is fine, right?

Well, if it weren't for the occasional time when you can't finish your homework, Big Shot controlling your mouse to finish the design drawings assigned by the teacher, White proactively popping up ads for hacking software when you need to download software, Spamton organizing the document formats for assignments, and Spamton Neo proactively throwing the priority project software pop-up at the very front, you would definitely have reinstalled your computer long ago!!

However...... You're still used to having these four little things at the top of your screen. You know now: the one in a red suit is Big Shot, the one with pure white hair and body is White, the tiny one is Spamton, and the big one with wings flying around your screen every day is Spamton Neo.

You feel like they must be the same person from different periods...... But after all, they're just virtual personas—what does it have to do with you? Thinking this, you can't help but feel a bit disappointed. You laugh at yourself, thinking it's not just because real life is too lonely, that you imagine these are real beings that can accompany you, right?

That's just wishful thinking...... But sometimes you just stare blankly at the screen, watching Spamton hang up your father's video call, then Spamton neo typed and fired, saying a series of sarcastic but polite remarks that knocked your father back.

You might also think, if only they really existed...... That way, you would never have to fear going home again.

You blinked, trying hard to keep the tears from flowing, just controlling the mouse arrow to pat Spamton's head and tapping Spamton Neo's wings.

They seemed very happy. You saw the four little ones on the screen huddled together, saying something unknown, then White jumped up and waved to greet you, and you had no choice but to pat his head.

White seemed satisfied and dragged out your parents' chat boxes, while Big Shot opened the software you were about to use and started drawing the work diagrams for your homework.

You couldn't help but move the mouse back and forth, patting White's head and poking Big Shot's head again, while White ...... You don't know why, but you could see the blush in that little white shadow. As for Big Shot, his movements seemed to pause for a moment, then he worked even harder and faster to draw your work.

You lowered your eyes, leaning in front of the computer, looking at the four little things on the screen. Big Shot was still controlling your mouse to help you finish your homework, and your eyes reddened, but you couldn't help but say, "If only ...... If you really existed, how wonderful it would be to always be with me...... I really like you all. ”

You were just mumbling this sentence to yourself, but for some reason, those four little things on the screen seemed to have heard you. white jumped up, probably blushing, to greet you once again, while spamton danced joyfully in place, its little short legs moving up and down. As for spamton and neo… a small, connected, five-featured blue heart flew out from the chest, only to be stuffed back in by him.

Big Shot just finished drawing the work-to-finish drawing quickly, and countless ad pop-ups flooded your computer screen. All the pop-up messages were 'I love you,' and all the options below were 'yes.'

You were startled, clicking your mouse on the screen. At the same time, a pop-up was thrown by white, and your mouse happened to land on yes. Immediately after, all the pop-ups on the computer disappeared, as if it had just been your imagination.

Staring blankly at the computer screen, you shake your head and decide to ignore everything that just happened—after all, it was probably just your imagination. After all, these are just programs running on your computer, and for some reason, these four little ones are so vivid. Other than that, how could they possibly be real? Besides that...... There could be no more unexpected things happening.

You think, you're just too lonely, so you place your feelings on it. You're just too lonely, so you fantasize that they are real. You just ...... Too much, too much desire, loved by someone, even if you don't even understand what love feels like, and what it's like to be loved.

Yet, you still sincerely hope someone can give you that feeling.

You still live as usual, spending three years of university with these four little ones on the computer screen. Until your senior year, you all need internships. The teacher also proposed an exchange student project, sending a few students from your world—the Light World—to study in the Dark World. Correspondingly, this is also a practical project. Exchange students need to observe the ecological environment of the Dark World and collect relevant information about the cultural industries of the Dark World.

It was only then that you realized...... Besides this world, there is another world that exists. And since your grades have always been the top in your major since university, you naturally have an exchange student spot.

For some reason, when you were muttering that you were going to the Dark World as an exchange student, the four little ones on the screen were all very happy.

And you pack your bags, take a deep breath at the agreed time, and muster the courage to step into the whirlpool at the boundary of light and darkness, into that unknown dark world.
