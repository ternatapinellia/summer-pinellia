So your hands are just fiddling with the hanging rope, absentmindedly thinking about which method would be better. Honestly, you don't want to suffer too much.

But then, you suddenly hear the music stop abruptly, stopping at a high note. The food in your hand has a slightly teasing flavor. You think the music player has suddenly stopped, so you look up at the computer, then freeze for a moment.

…… It won't open. Well, that's not surprising. You shrug, drag the app icon to the trash bin, and then plan to delete it.

The trash bin did show a 'filled' icon, but after a while, the same icon appeared again on the previously clean desktop.

You hesitate for a moment, open the trash can to confirm, confirm that the big head Spamton is still there, and then confirm that the desktop has the same big head. This time, you drag the icon over again, confirming that there are indeed two identical icons in the trash can, then breathe a sigh of relief. But before you can fully relax, you notice another identical icon pops up on the desktop.

This time, you completely felt a heart attack, rarely feeling a bit annoyed, and of course, a strange curiosity and excitement followed. After all, this was indeed an unexpected event, and you really wanted to know what was going on.

So you thought for two seconds, considering what exactly to do—after all, the extra spamton icon on the computer was obviously some kind of rogue software! You couldn't delete it, you couldn't open it, which was the most annoying part, so you decided to just open the Deltarune yourself to see if anything had changed.
