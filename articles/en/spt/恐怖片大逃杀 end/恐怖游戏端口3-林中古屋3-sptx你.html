Horror Game Port 3 - Old House in the Forest 3 - sptx You

Sptx you, ooc count me

…… Poe got hooked, so the style of this series leans a bit more toward Poe's writing style. Also, since it's a horror game with a genre similar to infinite stream (? ) It's not entirely based on the original work. It might be quite long.

——

Those red bloodstains stared straight at you. You just managed to hide the Spamton inside your clothes—just right on the end of your belly. After all, it wasn't impossible to camouflage, and the size was just right, though it wouldn't look good in emergencies.

Fortunately, the killer only roughly dragged you out, seeing you clutching your stomach with both hands, your movements a little gentler, but only a little. He didn't listen to your explanation, just locked you in an empty cage and locked it up. You saw the nearby cage was empty and thought those people had all been escaped by the killer—probably a hunting game, giving them time to run, giving them enough tension and hope to exhaust them and then slack off, only to face another chase and disappointment—finally, they stared wide-eyed at their bodies being cut in two, staring at the shadows in the forest without light, dying with only blood flowing.

You thought absentmindedly, hugging your knees tightly and pretending to tremble as you hid in the corner of the cage, hiding the spamton hidden inside your clothes during the murderer's hurried glance.

Spamton wasn't feeling well either. To be honest, this place was just too stuffy—though dark yet safe, it was especially warm and soft. After all, at this very moment, he was pressed against your belly, just a layer of clothing, so he could naturally feel the skin on your belly rising and falling with your breathing, and through the dim clothes, he could faintly see the light outside. Now he could hear footsteps so close by, and he thought, those must be the people who had just captured them.

He was a bit annoyed by your choice. At that moment, there was clearly a better way—even if it was to climb over the bed and sprint through the window, it might not have been impossible to escape. Wasn't that exactly what happened last night? You could have shaken off the killer tracking you, but now you willingly get caught. Of course, calming down a bit and discarding the fear is just an objective analysis. Spamton also knows your decision is the best choice. You naturally have more choices in this situation, can learn more clues, or rather, you are the one in control.

—But if he needed clues, wouldn't he still need to help search and analyze them!

Spamton could only feel you pressing him against his own belly again. Good, he thought expressionlessly, now everything was even more stifling. But he heard rustling sounds, chewing sounds, and could guess you were eating. The footsteps grew farther away, probably because the killer had gone far, followed by a loud door slamming.

He heard you exhale, then there was a rustling sound—probably the folding of wrapping paper. Then the clothes wrapping him were lifted, and you released him, or rather, pulled him out by the back of his neck.

You placed him beside you, then handed him the remaining third of the sandwich, wiped your mouth, and although you felt a bit reluctant, since they were companions, you chose to share—a bit of a painful feeling of letting go, haha.

"Here. That guy really looks like he's raising a pet here...... Hmph, you can eat this remaining sandwich, since we all need to conserve our strength. ”

You said casually, then looked away, restraining yourself from looking at that sandwich. Actually, you weren't full, but of course, you wouldn't be full even if you ate the leftover sandwich, so it's better to share it now. You casually thought about it, listening to the rustling behind you, the sound of wrappers being folded open, the crunching noise, and the subtle chewing noises. It was very light; if the room weren't quiet enough, you wouldn't have been able to catch it.

Then, you hear Spamton's voice, and you realize he's speaking—after all, you were still lost in your own thoughts, and when he spoke, you could barely tell who was speaking.

"[You] did this intentionally? [Deliberately] engaged in [detention]?" "Probably because you were still chewing, the voice was not very clear, but you still easily recognized what it meant. So at this moment, you gave a couple of awkward laughs and scratched your hair. Usually fearless, you actually felt a little guilty at this moment.

“…… Uh, no way. Actually, I just realized I could have just escaped by climbing over the window while he was looking at the bookshelf. I just hadn't thought before, hiding under the bed thinking everything was fine...... Uh, okay, I thought too. He must have noticed the bookshelf was passively touched...... "You turned your head, since you're the one explaining now, so you thought you needed to look at him to make your attitude seem more sincere. But now you see Spamton glaring at you, so your voice quieted, and in the end, you just mumbled. But you still managed to make sure Spamton could hear you clearly."

"Anyway, that's the reality! Hmm—even though I've been caught now, at worst I'll just find a chance to escape. I'm confident too. As long as he lets me out, I can take you far away and he'll never find out." You patted your chest and promised.

But in reality, you don't know how big this forest really is—maybe it's about 30 kilometers or even 100 kilometers away—something beyond human reach. And at that moment, you realize that maybe you need to find a way for those who were arrested before to get here, maybe...... If they have vehicles, you can escape through them. But what about those people? You haven't thought about it. After all, you're selfish. Bringing Spamton along is only because he's considered a companion. If something happens to him, you'll probably be trapped here forever.

He just stared at you, and you could only scratch your hair and give a dry laugh: "Let's not talk about that. Since we're here, let's settle in. We'll think about it when we need to figure it out. Thinking all this is exhausting—oh, I just saw that killer holding a photo. The person in the center is him, and the two people next to him are his parents. That's how it looks." I don't know why the face on the nightstand is blacked out...... "You can't figure this out. Right now, we're just sharing information."

"Maybe, only he [acknowledges] the way his parents [exist], while others do not [acknowledge] the [existence] of those two people." You were halfway through speaking when spamton interrupted you, and you paused, unable not to nod; you were somewhat persuaded, feeling that this made perfect sense.

So you excitedly and happily lifted the little puppet, staring at him: "Wow, you're amazing...... Sigh, I never thought of that! I just thought it was some kind of weird habit...... You muttered to yourself, ignoring Spamton's furious remark about putting him down.

Until he raised his voice again and said, "[Now], immediately, [immediately] re-[put me on the ground]." ”

You only let out two 'ohs' sounds, which is considered awakening. You put him back on the ground, then rested your chin on your hand and looked at him, lost in thought.

"I think the password on the bookshelf and the room hidden behind it must be important—even though I came over yesterday to eat and then sleep, I still noticed that the structure of the house is completely different from the wooden cabins it looks like outside, clearly missing a piece. That place is probably a room...... you muttered.

Spamton, on the other hand, glances at you in surprise. Just minutes ago, he had given up on the possibility of you thinking proactively, but now you are thinking for a moment.

And you noticed his gaze—after all, you just forced him to press his head over your belly, and the glasses were too annoying. You simply took them off through his clothes and didn't put them back on yet. So you complained, "Don't look at me like that. I can think anyway!" You grumbled, but you knew most of the time you couldn't remember that much. After all, your mind was a bit slow and you just couldn't react in time. Over time, you got too lazy to think and acted on instinct, so people wouldn't always say you had a problem with your intelligence. So you might as well admit it generously.

"No, I just thought [you] didn't have any [talent]." He hesitated for a moment, searched for the right words, and finally said.

And you suddenly laughed: "Ah...... It's okay if you don't have talent, as long as you work hard afterward, you can still be good at something. But you can never beat genius. I admit, I don't have talent, and even after trying hard, it's still not enough! So that's what I am...... To some people, I'm just someone worse than trash. "You said this as you looked up. Actually, you didn't care, but at that moment, tears still shimmered in your eyes.

Spamton fell silent. To be honest, the moment he said it, he realized he had said the wrong thing. But now, looking at you, he realized you were just a chance encounter. Even though you had met once before, you don't remember, and he doesn't know you as a potential customer. To be honest, you two have nothing to do with each other. At that moment, he became curious about your past and wanted to connect with you.

And you didn't really care about any of that. After all, it was just a gaming buddy. You just wanted to vent some uncontrollable pain at that moment, so you held back your tears, lowered your head, and smiled at Spamton: "Forget it, just pretend I was just rambling earlier—just pretend you didn't hear me." ”

Spamton hesitated for a moment, then cautiously asked, "So, your [past]?" I'm very curious. [You] are not worse than trash. I want to know your [reason]. ”

And you just scratched your head, because Spamton's question made you feel a bit annoyed. Maybe you're just avoiding it, unwilling to face it all, so you just dodge the question: "Ah, these aren't important, right? After all, the more important thing now is figuring out how to escape this cage and then find more clues...... Hmm, time is tight, there's no time to waste. ”

"It's okay. I've already thought of a possible solution. Maybe ...... we have enough time to discuss." Spamton said.

You opened your mouth, about to argue when you heard the sound of the door handle being turned. You hurriedly stuffed the spamton into your clothes, not forgetting to add, "See, I told you time was tight, that person is back now!" You lowered your voice, speaking very quickly. You felt the spamton probably moved; the environment made him uncomfortable, but you couldn't worry about it anymore. Very time, very much action. You stared intently at the doorway, watching the sullen-faced murderer unscrew the doorknob, push open the heavy wooden door, and walk in. Several people were tied with hemp rope in his hand, then he untied the rope and threw them into the empty cage.

You glanced at the chainsaw in his other hand, still dripping fresh blood. A heavy, fishy smell and rust filled the entire room. To make yourself look more like a pregnant woman, you grabbed the railing and lowered your head to retch, deliberately amplifying the sound so the killer and others could hear.

You feel everyone else looking at the killer with indignant eyes, but the killer doesn't seem to care at all. He just walks straight over, then opens your cage. Just as you're wondering what he's about to do, he roughly pulls you up, stumbles out, and then pushes you out the door. You hear a bang—he's closing the door. Before closing it, you hear a twisted smile, a mad, and unknowable angry voice: "From now on, run." The farther you run, the better. If you can get out of this forest, you'll win the game. ”

You turned around and glanced at the firmly closed door. You knew that if you took Spamton out now, he would surely see him through the window, so you just held your stomach and walked slowly. Only after you entered the woods and were certain that he would no longer notice you did you take Spamton out again and place him on your shoulder.

Of course, before that, you held him by the back of his neck, looked him in the eye, sighed, rubbed your own neck, and tilted your head: 'Alright, it seems we don't need to think about escaping for now… hmm, but I'm not sure what he really has in mind. Let's first find a way to get out of these woods safely.' You said cheerfully, not allowing Spamton to question you, and directly chose a direction to move forward.

He wobbled on your shoulder. The good news was, since you weren’t running nonstop like yesterday, even though your body was swaying, you didn’t have to worry about him falling off. As for the bad news… he thought your sense of direction was probably terrible, really bad.
