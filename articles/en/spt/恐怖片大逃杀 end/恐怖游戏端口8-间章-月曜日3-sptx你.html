Horror Game Port 8 - Interlude - Monday 3 - sptx You

With your hands behind your head, you rest your head on it, staring at the ceiling for a few seconds, pondering your words for a moment before softly speaking: "Mm...... And then, anyway, I didn't get into college, and at my age, I left home. But my parents still call me every month asking for money, meaning I have to support my younger siblings, and since they worked so hard to raise me, I have to give them money too. ”

“…… I don't have much money either, I only have a high school diploma, what can I possibly do? Although I barely found a job...... But right now, I'm just doing some odd jobs, helping move bodies in the hospital morgue......

You pause for a moment, then give a self-deprecating smile: "Forget it, there's really nothing more to say. That odd job is gone now. Even though I'm strong, the boss hired a male helper and fired me. Why is it that girls can't do this kind of work......

“…… In short, that's exactly what happened. I chose to rent in the Dark World because I didn't want them to call me again—by the way, you probably can't get their calls from the Dark World," you remember the first time you came to the Dark World and then left, when you left, you found several missed calls on your phone, so you probably couldn't get through. "So it's best if they don't bother me. I'm really annoyed with them." Hmm...... And I don't plan to do any more odd jobs; I want to do something else. ”

Even if you didn't get into college, you've always wanted to complete the adult self-study exam. You know, even though you're learning slowly, and it might take twice as long as others, it doesn't mean you can't learn. You just didn't have that opportunity before. And now, you think...... Maybe next time, the money from completing the side tasks will be enough to sign up for exams and buy textbooks and study questions. There will probably be some surplus leftover.

After saying that, you didn't care about his reaction, just turned over and yawned: "Hey...... Actually, I didn't do much today, right? I don't know why I'm so tired...... "Actually, it's only close to noon."

But maybe it's to avoid seeing the expression he might show, which is why you choose to turn things around. You don't want others looking at you with a look of maybe pity. You don't need pity, because you know you'll keep moving forward strongly. You always want to prove that you can still do something, achieve certain goals, and that you're not destined to achieve nothing, as your parents say.

Just as you were lost in thought, you heard Spamton's voice behind you: "Maybe [you] could choose another [path]?" Also, maybe [you] need [Sweet Supplement]. ”

You know, this is hoping you'll feel a bit better—after all, there's a saying that eating sweets can make you feel better. Although you think the truth of this saying is still to be verified, since you haven't really eaten sweets.

You're glad you turned away, and he can't see your expression, so now you can still pretend to be relaxed and say, "Ah, no need, just eat something random—I don't mind, I'm used to it!" When I was busy doing odd jobs before, I often had nonstop meals, just having a bite to eat was enough...... You kept rambling, not even knowing what you were talking about.

But actually, the salary they pay you can barely get by, but...... Your parents always ask you for money, and if you don't give enough, they'll call you like crazy. Even if you change your phone number and your account on other apps hasn't changed, they'll still message and call you. With no other choice, you can only keep yourself a small portion of money. So of course, that money isn't enough for your food and drink.

—But just barely managing to survive is already pretty good. You're already amazing! You always comfort yourself like this. Even though you've deeply remembered your parents' story.

Actually, it wasn't like that at first. You still cherish your parents' kindness, so you selectively forget their harsh words and willingly give money. But later, when you get fired, they still chase you for money, the same amount as before. If it's not enough, they'll urge you to ask, and you gradually get tired of it, and that filter slowly breaks down.

"[You] should [take care of] your own [health]." Spamton said this.

…… Although he was only saying this, after all, he probably wasn't particularly protective of his own health. Even if he ended up like this, it wasn't the outcome he had imagined. However...... Perhaps it was also the result of his own actions.

You turn your back to Spamton, just shrugging: "Mm...... Doesn't matter, who cares about these things? I'm the only one who cares......

Before he could finish, Spamton interrupted him, and you heard him speak: "I [care]." ”

You pause, your mind rarely pauses for a moment, or rather, it crashes. You still need to think about what he really means. Of course, you know it's probably not because your relationship is particularly close, since you two are currently related by interests, so there might be many other reasons......

Before you could figure out a solution, listen to him continue: "After all, [we] need to [join] [enter the game] now. I don't want [you] to be in a bad state." ”

Oh, oh...... That's the reason...... You nodded quietly to yourself and casually replied, "Don't worry, my body is strong. Carrying you to complete the task is easy...... But you make a good point—let's go out for lunch!" ”

You were still very proactive; you acted without hesitation. You immediately sat up in bed, grabbed Spamton's scruff, and scrambled out the door.

"[Stop], [Place] me on [the ground]! I can [move free]!" "You feel him struggle twice in your hand.

You casually made a couple of 'ohs', put him down on the ground, and still complained, "Hmm, but you're walking too slowly. Maybe I'd rather just carry you faster...... Uh, or maybe you sit on my shoulder and I'll walk with you? That's way faster than this, right?" "Although that would make your shoulders a bit sore, it's still much better than taking it slow." You may be patient, but you're an impatient person, so this isn't something you have to wait for.

He suddenly stopped walking. You almost kicked Spamton, but this time you didn't complain again, so he wouldn't interrupt you before he could finish his complaints. You just crossed your arms and watched what he was planning. Unexpectedly, Spamton turned around and reached out his hand to you.

You looked at his movements in surprise and met his gaze—uh, well, you still couldn't tell what his mood was. So you just pointed at Spamton, then at your own shoulder: "Uh...... So, you're going to sit on it? ”

Spamton nodded: "Yes, this is your [proposal]. ”

You muttered, "It's not like you have to carry it out just because you made a proposal...... I was just saying it casually...... Even though you complained, you still bent down, took his hand, and then simply picked him up through his arm and sat him on your shoulder. Hmm, your shoulders really do hurt...... But it's more comfortable than holding him, and doing so makes you much faster.

So you walked out like a gust of wind, walking quickly. But after just a short while, you slowed your pace a bit, because you felt a bit lost. You weren't familiar with the layout of the dark world and didn't know where to go right now. So you stood there looking around for a long time, looking at the distant streets on either side, trying hard to recall where the restaurant was located.

At that moment, Spamton pointed to the left and poked your cheek—uh, otherwise you definitely wouldn't be able to see it. Although you think he could just say it directly! But that was indeed very convenient and efficient, so you couldn't be bothered to say anything.

"This [direction] has [dessert shop], [ramen shop], and [barbecue shop]." You follow the direction he pointed, nodding thoughtfully, naturally curious.

"So you actually want dessert more?" Although I don't think eating snacks at noon is bad, wouldn't it be a bit tiring...... "At least you think most desserts are both sweet and greasy, so you tend to keep your distance."

"I think maybe [you] need [sweets]. Also, [sweets] have a rather [light, bitter taste]. ”

"Maybe that's true, but honestly, I don't need sweets! I don't think it matters, it's long gone, though it's still going on...... But I haven't cared for a long time!" You said this in a relaxed tone, still smiling. Of course, you know that smile is fake, maybe even a bit exaggerated...... After all, it's just a disguise.

Spamton turned his head and saw your slightly upturned lips. He didn't say much—after all, you two weren't close enough to take things further. Maintaining the status quo was fine—if you took a step further, maybe anyone would find it hard to endure.

"Alright, that's just because I need [sweets]. After all, I don't have enough [money]." So he said this.

You rub your chin and click, "Alright, if you have money and want to spend some money, I get it, I get it—then I'll take you to the dessert shop!" If it's just dessert, this money is definitely more than enough! ”

As it turns out, although desserts are a bit more expensive than buying daily necessities, they're still within your means. So you wave your hand and order three or five servings of cake. Anyway, you can definitely finish it. If he can't finish it...... Then pack it up and put it in the fridge. Remember, you still have one at home.

So you picked and picked, ordering a tiramisu, a chocolate brownie, a red velvet cake, and a matcha Black Forest cake, and finally after hesitating for a moment, you ordered a honey cake—well, you were just curious about the taste.

The dessert was served to you quickly. You put the Spamton next to you, then noticed he couldn't reach the table—his head was just a little above it. You were silent for a moment, then picked him up and sat him on your lap.

"Uh...... Forget it, you should eat first. "Now he can reach the food on the table, but you always feel this posture is awkward, so you choose to stare blankly at the dessert.

"No, maybe [you] can [eat at the same time]." he said.

You pouted, not daring to agree with the suggestion, but still reluctantly took the fork, brought over the plate of honey cake, and took a bite—you stuck out your tongue and put it down.

It's too sweet. It's not that you can't eat it, but the cloying sweetness isn't for you. You think you should just go back and have some dark chocolate. Just as you're lost in thought, you notice Spamton actually pulls over the plate of honey cake you've just taken one bite and pushed aside, and you can't help but widen your eyes.

"Wait, don't you think it's sweet and sweet...... You raise your eyebrows, their brows twisted into a strange shape.

"[No]." Actually, Spamton hadn't told you either. Now, tasting these foods and savoring these flavors felt like a thin membrane, not fully appreciated. So the sweetness now was also elusive—what he could sense was just the lingering aftertaste of extreme sweetness. So this flavor was just right for him.

You stroked your chin in confusion and muttered to yourself, "So you're a sweet person, the kind who likes very sweet things......"

…… He won't be able to explain this to you. To explain this, he has to talk about how he became what he is now, what he went through—memories he doesn't want to touch right now. Even though all of that has truly happened, and he's still striving to change his current situation and destiny.

So Spamton just tacitly assumes your opinion.

In the end, you actually nodded and said, 'Alright, I understand. But I feel like just eating this piece of cake is enough for you, so the rest is all mine!' So you happily picked up a plate of tiramisu and started eating. Although your current posture was indeed a bit strange, after a whole morning of fussing and then a period of rest, you were genuinely hungry, so you needed to eat now!

'...【Okay】. Then I will only pay for this 【cake】.'

You casually replied twice, 'I know. After all, for fairness's sake, this time I won't ask for it to be split evenly. You eat as much as you pay.' You weren't being unreasonable to demand he pay half in this situation.

By the time he was almost done, you had already finished two portions of cake and were about to eat a third. You looked down at his situation and couldn't help but sigh, '...You're so slow.'

'【Food】 should be 【savored slowly】.' Though in reality, it was just an excuse. Even if it had some flavor, in a state of nearly lost taste, he still felt that eating like this made him miserable.
