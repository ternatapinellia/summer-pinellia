Horror Game Port 13 - Add Fuel to the Fire 4 - sptx you

The charred corpse gradually moves toward you, and you clearly see the raging flames reigniting from downstairs, slowly climbing up the stairs. You can't explain what exactly is happening; it's just a feeling in the unnatural that you can't be chased down—besides, you have nothing on hand right now. You can't use the Spamton as a weapon, after all, it's still a living thing.

Lost in thought, you turned your head and lowered your body to avoid the sharp claws swung at you by the charred corpse, then ran with Spamton toward the stairwell. Although Spamton couldn't see anything, he could still feel the wind that tore through the air for a moment. Watching your movements, Spamton immediately realized—probably some monster was chasing after you. And the flames on the hallway were still rising, almost reaching this floor. Clearly, there was no time for you to ponder these matters any further.

So he spoke very quickly, at least to your ear sounding a bit nervous: "[Speed up], [Flames] are [attacking]." "You grabbed the shaky staircase handle and climbed the steps, not forgetting to reply: "Of course I know—I can see those flames spreading. Getting to this point is a bit strange, but how can I see them? Isn't this supposed to happen from your perspective...... But what you saw was the flames reigniting. If you remember correctly, the entire first floor was burning fiercely before being abandoned, so there probably isn't anything wrong with that."

You were just relieved that the railing in your hand hadn't broken, and the charred corpse was still chasing after you. You took a deep breath and ran upward. For some reason, the speed of the flames suddenly intensified. As your heels advanced, the tongues of fire licked your heels several times. The burning and melting sensation of the flames made you feel unbearable pain for a moment, but you could only endure the pain and keep moving upward.

After running about five or six floors, you finally stopped, clutching your knees and gasping for breath. At that moment, the steps had become smooth and brand new again. Looking up, you saw that the corridor on this floor had about eight rooms, and you didn't even know which one to check out first.

But Spamton had been holding onto your clothes to keep him from falling off. Now he finally stopped to take a good look at the surroundings, then tugged at your cheek, signaling you to listen to him. You turned your head in displeasure to see what he was about to say.

"There's a burnt corpse patrolling ahead." He just whispered this sentence to you, since no one knew that loud noise wouldn't attract the corpse. Fortunately, when you stood on the stairs, the charred corpse didn't seem to have seen you yet.

You immediately held your breath, but in reality, from your perspective, you could see nothing—only brand-new hallways and doors. So you couldn't help but quietly complain, "Now I understand...... It's like you can't see anything." And now, when you look down, the flames have vanished—maybe not completely gone. You just realize that beneath your feet is nothing but pitch black, deep darkness that terrifies you. From that darkness, you can still feel the wind, the scent of blood, and a faint stench of decay, making you wonder what lies below. But you also know you must never go any further; if you do, you might be doomed to endless ruin.

But that wasn't the main point now. You looked up again and looked ahead: "So, roughly where is that corpse?" "You asked, but your voice was still very low, so you couldn't see anything.

"Roughly, between [Five to Eight Doors], conduct [patrol activities]." He glanced at it and gave you an answer.

You rubbed your chin. Indeed, the rooms were all on one side, and the opposite side was full of windows. Actually, this design was pretty good, wasn't it...... Then you carefully observed the doors. To be honest, your eyesight was pretty sharp, so after you looked carefully at the four doors in front, you told Spamton to let him choose.

"Yeah, the first door is completely empty, nothing at all. The second door only has a wreath hanging there, and the third door has some paintings on it...... It seems like there's a Christmas tree or Santa Claus's. The fourth door...... Strange, there's nothing on it either, but someone has stamped countless X symbols. "You muttered to yourself, mumbling as you finished speaking.

Spamton also looked around the rooms. From his perspective, the first door had completely disappeared, meaning the first room could probably be explored directly...... Or maybe only he could enter. If they acted separately, the current situation would be too risky, since you wouldn't see the monster's presence. So he just thought for a moment and said, "Go to the symbolic door." "After all, he can see the symbols above."

To be honest, he didn't even know what those symbols meant...... But overall, the existence of symbols was more directional than others.

You nodded gently, sneaking up to the hallway, walking close to the wall. Suddenly, you heard Spamton say, "[Speed up the pace]." ”

After hesitating for a moment, you finally understood. Maybe the patrolling corpse had just walked away, or maybe it was about to return, so you quickly sped up and slipped through the main door.

Fortunately, the corpse probably wouldn't open the door—you even took a moment to think about it, then looked up because you heard the sound of an argument. You looked up and saw a vision before you—probably a resident here, probably a couple.

So you gently covered Spamton's mouth as he was about to speak, and gestured for him to be quiet.

You quietly listened to the couple arguing, frowning involuntarily, feeling a bit annoyed by the noise. You don't like hearing other people arguing, and of course, you don't like the process of arguing either.

So right now, you're suppressing your impatience while listening.

You heard that man say, "You heard that, right?" You heard that! That woman said she moved somewhere more than ten years ago, and that place is very close to the ocean, so she likes it very much. But after more than ten years, how could she still look so young?! ”

You turn your head and see the woman speak, her tone a bit impatient: "It's only been a little over ten years. Maybe it's just that people take good care of themselves...... Tsk, how do you take care of yourself? I want to learn ...... too," she said, but with a hint of envy.

"But...... Maybe that's the mermaid? You've heard the legend that eating mermaid meat grants immortality!" The man spoke with a hint of madness.

The woman's voice no longer showed the impatience from before, but instead carried a hint of hesitation and restlessness: "If that's really the case, does that mean your beauty will never age?" She seemed to be touching her own face, murmuring something to herself.

"That's right! And it's said that eating mermaid meat makes you immortal. Even if you're injured, you recover quickly, and even if you get sick, there's nothing to worry about—" The man's voice grew louder and louder, and you frowned in discomfort, almost trying to cover one ear. Spamton, though unable to see anything, could only see the empty room—hmm, except for the counter still filled with rotting meat, he could still catch your uncomfortable expression at that moment. After hesitating for a moment, he still covered one ear for you.

Your brows relaxed slightly. Good, at least for now, you only need to accept half the noise. But after saying this, the illusions of the man and woman faded away. It seemed it was finally over. You rubbed the other ear to calm down, then took Spamton's hand away...... I don't know what he was thinking just now, but he probably saw you rubbing your ear. His hand that had been covering your ear gently rubbed it for you. It felt so strange that you almost reacted and threw him off. Luckily, you remembered he was your business partner, so you didn't do that. Instead, you took his hand away and awkwardly said, "Thank you." ”

Spamton also felt a bit embarrassed at this moment; in fact, he didn't know why he had instinctively done that just now. Of course, he didn't regret it either, so he just replied dryly, "It's okay." ”

You scratched your head and cleared your throat twice, then recounted the conversation between the man and woman to Spamton, then said, "It seems that sister let slip something and was discovered as a mermaid, then killed and eaten as meat?" Maybe the entire apartment was involved in this process...... "Combining the previous diary with the current illusionary conversation, you have indeed reached that conclusion."

Spamton sits on your shoulder, nodding as if something is up, shaking his head: "[That's the fact], and [not just that]. Maybe learning the truth about their [communication channels] is the real priority." ”

You nodded, half understanding, half understanding. That sentence...... You think they're probably talking about how this couple actually makes everyone believe in mermaid meat. After all, if it's just a rumor, there will always be people who don't believe it.

"Since they say eating mermaid meat will help you recover quickly, could mermaids do the same?" So you proposed this possibility.

"Maybe it's as it should be, maybe that couple uses [identity] to make others believe in the power of 'mermaid flesh.'" Spamton continued with your words. He had indeed thought of this possibility earlier, but didn't know how this couple would actually make it happen.

You noticed the progress on the screen, which gradually increased from 30% to 50% as you discussed. Not only that, the main quest also showed 15% progress, though you have no idea what was going on.

You let out a long sigh: "Alright, let's first see what's still in the room...... From here, I can see the desk in the living room, the empty, clean stove over there, the wooden sofa against the wall, and the coffee table in front. "You roughly judged the direction of the things and finally confirmed that only the desk was worth investigating.

Spamton glanced around, then shook his head: "I [see nothing]." "He really doesn't have any information left to investigate."

You nodded knowingly and chose to step forward and open the desk drawer. Inside were a few flyers or research reports. You took them out and took a look.

[Welcome to Mermaid Island in Paradise and enjoy a seven-day holiday just for you!

Although there are no real mermaids on the main island, you can still watch mermaid performances!

Legend has it that hundreds of years ago, a mermaid appeared by the ocean. It received a human call, and at their request, gave him a piece of flesh and blood. As a result, the illness in that human body miraculously cured, and from then on, he became immortal. You may have heard the legend of the Eight Hundred Bhikkhunis, who later embarked on a similar path.

The main island is thus named Paradise Mermaid Island. Enjoy this trip to the fullest! ]

You glance at it and feel it looks like some kind of flyer, so you casually toss it aside and start looking at the content of the next research report. Although it's called a research report, the situation and content aren't that serious.

[Investigation Report on Mermaids:

Calling it an investigation report is really just a notebook. But I still want to record it, and I want to tell others: I succeeded, I found the mermaid, I saw the real mermaid, and I was able to share its flesh and blood—I, I don't know, maybe I killed him. ]

You instinctively glance at the page and notice it looks like it has been torn off—maybe from some novel, but you don't know if mermaids really exist.

[I finished that mermaid; the meat was delicious, truly delicious...... I couldn't describe that feeling, only the lingering fragrance on my lips and teeth. I really loved it.

……

…… What a detestable human being, even recording the food and judging its taste? It's detestable.

……

What's wrong with me? I feel like my memory is getting messed up. Today should be the 21st, so why did it turn into the 23rd? This isn't the first time something like this has happened......]

Then the pages of the book contained no other content, and it looked like they had also been torn. You put down the paper with somewhat complicated feelings and relayed to Spamton, 'It seems... people who eat mermaid meat might undergo changes? Could it also carry the consciousness of the mermaid?' As you spoke, you felt a bit uncertain, after all, you couldn't even be sure that this account was true.

Spamton nodded: 'Roughly 【so】. Perhaps eating 【mermaid meat】 and thereby gaining a state of 【mental coexistence】 is precisely what that elder brother 【needs】.'

You nodded thoughtfully. Indeed, in this way, it all might make sense... Then you saw that the side quest's progress bar had reached 100%, and you couldn't help but remark that this progress was incredibly fast.

Afterward, Spamton poked your face, and following the direction of his finger, you noticed that it was an axe.
