Horror Game Port 14 - Add Fuel to the Fire 5 - sptx You

You stared at the axe twice, then picked it up. To be honest, the axe could be seen from both perspectives...... It was truly amazing.

So you can't help but say, "I actually didn't notice this axe just now...... Alright, but with a weapon, I can clear out that charred corpse." "There are other rooms you haven't checked yet! Although there might not be much to see...... But you can't miss this opportunity.

So you open the door and walk out. Spamton sees you coming right into the charred corpse, which spots its target and swings its claws. Just as he tries to speak, he sees you dodging it. Although you can't see your target now, you can still feel the howling wind and the hostility sweeping around you. Of course, that feeling of tearing through the air is very brief, so you rely on your intuition to swing your axe at that spot, chopping down hard.

You did chop something substantial, but...... Because you couldn't see anything, you only heard a faint sound like something falling, and you still couldn't see anything.

Spamton hesitated, wanting to speak. He could see, and even though you couldn't see the monster, it still decisively chopped off the claw that had just attacked you. The arm was broken on the ground, still twitching slightly. No blood flowed from the broken surface, but sparks were still burning in tiny spots.

But you simply raised your axe, turned your head once more to avoid the gust of wind, and a sense of foreboding came from beneath you. You turned to the side to dodge the possible attack, then swung fiercely in the direction you had determined. You heard a crisp cracking sound. This time, you saw the corpse broken in two and lying on the ground. From the cross-section spread grease and burning flames. Perhaps as that small amount of grease spilled out, the tiny spark that was about to go out reignited again. You understood why the fire below had just started.

But before you could make any further guesses, Spamton poked your cheek again. This time, his movements were urgent and his words urgent: "We need to [speed up], [corpses] like [half-price items] piling up." "Just now, he saw...... Those corpses, more and more bodies, opened the doors of other rooms and came out. With you alone and unable to see anything, it would be difficult to deal with these monsters, which is why he seemed so anxious now.

You paused, instinctively glancing behind you. Though you couldn't see or hear, you could still smell the increasingly distinct smell of burnt beard and the stench of rotting blood. Moreover, the flames beneath your feet grew even fiercer.

But those corpses weren't afraid of the flames; they just sped up. At least now you could feel the howling wind again, instinctively swinging your axe and running toward the stairs behind you.

"Why are so many monsters suddenly appearing? Did every resident of every building be burned to death inside their homes?" Isn't that a bit unrealistic? Then why were there no bodies in that room just now...... "You still have time to ramble a few words. You really don't know what's going on here, you can't figure out the answer, so it's better to leave it to Spamton to think about."

Spamton hesitated for a moment: "The symbol on the room door might mean they died first." "Of course, that's just speculation, but you still see the main storyline progress on the screen has changed to 30%. You still don't know how this is calculated."

You ran up the hallway and looked down, but you couldn't see anything—only a burning sensation, as if flames were spreading. You thought this time it was probably because of the flames burning the corpses again.

And in the end, you replied, "I see...... Alright, are there still corpses and flames catching up now? If not, I'll pause for a bit...... "Although you're naturally athletic, you can't handle running five or six floors in a while. You estimated the building is about 15 floors, which means you'll reach the top soon. But at this moment, you still have main quests that haven't been fully resolved."

Perhaps this is a troubling question.

Spamton glanced back and shook his head at you: "Stop [Spread]." ”

Only then did you breathe a sigh of relief and walk up the stairs. The scene before you changed again. What you saw was once more a burned, abandoned scene, but this floor of the hallway was empty, with no monsters around, and for some reason, there was only one door, which looked like just one occupant. Instinctively, you looked up the stairs and belatedly realized you were almost at the end. Beyond that was the rooftop. You felt a sense of urgency—what if you couldn't complete the main quest? But it seemed there was no need to worry too much. You found yourself blindly trusting yourself. Anyway, these thought-intensive tasks would be left to Spamton. You really couldn't praise your thinking ability.

Just in case, you carefully approached the door with your axe and gently pushed it open. After all, if there was another charred corpse lying in wait for you, that would be troublesome. Fortunately, there was nothing in the room, but strangely, there was nothing inside—only a safe placed right in the center.

You closed the door with a full smile of confusion and glanced around at the scene...... Hmm, the walls were surprisingly clean as well. Here, the cement brick walls were just plain ones, but they were so clean it seemed like someone had specially tidied them up.

So you couldn't help but describe the scene before you, and finally asked, "This room doesn't look like it's already abandoned, does it?" ”

Spamton also looked around the room. Unlike you, besides the safe placed in the center, he also saw various instruments on the floor. To be honest, he couldn't quite understand them, only seeing a person in a white lab coat—perhaps a researcher's uniform—busy doing research here, not saying a word.

So Spamton simply said, "This room belongs to the [Researcher]. ”

But you seemed to be thinking, "Researcher...... No wonder, no wonder this room is so clean—maybe the things have already been moved away. So, that means the researcher moved into the building after it was abandoned, right? "You casually shared your own guess.

Spamton glanced at the burn marks on the surrounding walls and nodded: "No [error]." ”

You rubbed your chin, squatted down, and looked at the safe. It was an old-fashioned safe, impossible to open by brute force, only using a password, and it was entered in circles. You had no idea what the password was, so you started asking Spamton for help.

"Spamton, what do you think the safe code is? It looks like it's three digits."

Spamton didn't answer your question; he just watched the researcher stand up, stop fiddling with the instruments, but didn't touch the safe. Instead, he went to his desk and started recording his small ecological box. It looked like an ocean ecological box.

So Spamton said, "The password should be [sea]." ”

You nodded half-understandingly, turned the wheel, and opened the safe—yes, it was the password. Although you didn't know how he got the answer, seeing the perfectly preserved documents inside, you chose to pick them up and check them first.

A thick stack of experimental materials—if you can't understand them, just look at the summaries and summaries attached on the first page.

[File One

Mermaids fear fire and prefer water sources—this has already been proven. The institute once captured a mermaid for experiments, and it turned out that the experiments yielded many conclusions. If there were flames, they wouldn't even try to cross them, or would shrink into a circle of flames, even if they were burned to death.

Once killed, their corpses become monsters completely unafraid of fire, allowing them to be killed again—completely dead. Research has also found that these corpses are extremely afraid of water sources, which is very different from the habits of mermaids during their survival. Perhaps this can be used to manipulate mermaids and their corpses into our tools.

I found this building to be a suitable experimental venue. ]

[File 2

There is something strange about this building. Of course, I don't believe in the existence of immaterial things. It's just that occasional hallucinations and singing of merfolk on the rooftop lure people down, making me suspect they are merfolk acts of revenge. I have no choice but to believe in irrational, non-material existence.

Recently, I found that as long as I eliminated those illusions, I could briefly return to reality, or return to the past. After destroying those illusions using the methods I had developed, what I saw was the scene from the former rooftop.

Here's a simple method: apply the liquid of this recipe to weapons, which can produce effects similar to flame burning and scorching heat, and can also eliminate the mermaid phantoms. ]

You dullly lift your head and find a bottle of liquid in the safe.

You picked up the bottle of crimson liquid and shook it. Although you couldn't see anything, you could still faintly smell the scent of burnt food.

So you repeated the information from earlier and added, "Now, it looks like we can leave here together...... Probably?" "You're not quite sure how you can leave this place after climbing onto the rooftop."

"[That's the fact.] Maybe we can try to stop [the corpse]. ”

You scratched your head in frustration: "I wish I could, but we don't have water here...... Rather than stopping them, I actually think it's much easier to just kill them outright. If it weren't for the way there were so many corpses just now that I couldn't see them...... You muttered to yourself, put away the bottle, and walked out the door. But once you stepped outside, your expression stiffened. You saw three or five corpses who, at some point, had arrived in the corridor, slowly wandering around, looking like they were on patrol.

As soon as they saw you, they quickly swarmed around and began attacking with their claws. At this moment, Spamton couldn't see it, but he could feel the howling wind, and then he saw you start swinging your axe.

Now you were feeling happy, grateful to have seen all of this now. So even though you were surrounded by corpses, you weren't afraid. However, your movements inevitably had to be a bit more intense. So, while leaning back to dodge the incoming claws, you also used your axe to cut off another hand attacking you, and didn't forget to add, "Hold on tight first. I'll deal with these monsters first—"

Spamton didn't have time to say anything more. After all, if he didn't hold onto you firmly, you'd throw him hard to the ground. So now he simply grabbed your arm. In fact, he had already been thrown off by the moment he wasn't paying attention, so he had to hold onto your arm.

And the inexplicable weight on your arm naturally made you frown. Fortunately, it was only your left arm. Your right arm swung as usual, splitting the heads of the corpses blocking your path. Watching their bodies split in half and fall to the ground, and as flames burned on their bodies again, you couldn't help but mutter, "That wasn't mentioned in the documents just now...... Forget it, let's go first!" You casually picked up Spamton, who had just been tightly holding your arm, and put it back onto your shoulder.

With that, you picked up your axe and headed toward the rooftop. After all, the bodies lying together were engulfed in raging flames, and you definitely didn't want to be burned.

Very quickly, you arrived on the rooftop, and indeed, you heard the mermaid's song. It was sitting at the edge of the rooftop, luring you step by step toward it. Perhaps at first, you were truly bewitched, genuinely walking toward that mermaid. But at that moment, Spamton pinched your face hard. Of course, he could tell that something was wrong with you; after all, from his perspective, there was nothing there. But he saw your expression—it was dazed and blank, as if your soul had been stolen, or your mind had been taken over. In short, this state was truly dangerous, and he couldn’t guarantee you would regain consciousness. At that moment, he was even somewhat relieved that you were seeing from a different perspective; otherwise, he might not have had time to bring you back to awareness.

You felt the pain and instantly snapped out of that dreamlike state, subconsciously breaking out in a cold sweat. You poured the crimson liquid onto the axe, and then swung it at the mermaid's illusion.

There was a crisp sound of glass shattering, and the broken noise followed. The illusion in front of you also shattered, the scene burned and fell away as if on fire, and ultimately, what appeared before you was a gloomy sky and the illusion of the brother and sister standing on the rooftop.

You stared blankly at the scene and then looked at Spamton: “Did you also see the sky so dark and the shadows of the brother and sister?”

Spamton confirmed that the change in the sky and the appearance of the illusion were only triggered by the sound of the glass shattering just now. He merely nodded.

You instinctively clenched the axe, and suddenly, a beep sounded. You noticed on the screen that you were congratulated for completing the main quest. The brother and sister turned their heads and mouthed one phrase toward you: Thank you for discovering the truth.

In the next second, as the game ended, a flash of white light passed, and you lost consciousness.
