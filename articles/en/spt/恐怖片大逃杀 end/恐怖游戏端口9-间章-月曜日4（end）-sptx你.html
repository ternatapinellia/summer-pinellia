Horror Game Port 9 - Interlude - Monday 4 (end) - sptx you

After lunch, you spent the whole afternoon fiddling around and spending time tidying up the room. Everything now looked so cozy and livable—at least that's how you felt. So you looked at the furnishings in the room, nodded in satisfaction, and clapped your hands. After a simple dinner, you lay on the bed for a while, daydreaming, staring at the ceiling. At this moment, you completely blocked out any outside noise, including the spamton's sound. After all, you wouldn't respond to anyone who spoke; you were too tired.

It kept dragging things out until you felt it was almost time to sleep—after all, you had already started yawning and getting sleepy. Only then did you slowly get out of bed, finish a simple wash, and then collapse back on, instinctively about to undress and turn off the light.

But before starting this task, you barely remembered Spamton's presence, so you quickly stopped and looked down at him: "Wait, do you still want to sleep here?" "You asked hesitantly, finally putting down the clothes you had half-undressed."

"...... naturally, my room doesn't have suitable furnishings." he said.

You stroked your chin and thought for a moment. Indeed, his room was basically unfurnished. After all, you wouldn't care about any of that! What does his stuff have to do with you—just thinking about the hassle, some things couldn't be stacked, so he simply put them on his bed, like leftover bedding for changing and washing. Thinking about it made you feel guilty again.

Alright, you didn't give anyone a place either. After all, a small puppet doesn't take up much space, so you cleared your throat twice and suggested, "Alright, then you can ...... sleep on the floor?" "Although you were just saying it casually, you never expected him to listen." Sure enough, of course he didn't go down; he just kept lying there, looking at you with a faint, distant gaze.

"Alright, I know this isn't very practical. Maybe you can stay here any longer...... But don't just look at me! Go wash up! I need to change clothes! "You said this as you walked over, grabbed him by the back of the neck, and put him on the ground. After all, you bought him some clothes, of course, he paid for it. So now you're feeling quite justified.

Although Spamton struggled in your hand for a moment, it didn't last long, because you grabbed him by the neck and then threw him to the ground. Then you lay comfortably on the bed, resting your hands on your head, watching his back. For him, all of this might be difficult, but you believe he can handle it on his own!

At this point, you recall what happened that morning. You really ignored the dirty feeling when you first took him out of the trash bin, since in the world of light you saw many beggars living under bridges, begging everywhere, and rummaging through trash bins. Of course, rather than relying on fate in vain, you still trust your own hands and the wealth you create yourself.

…… So, you won't do the same thing as those people, because you believe you always have some ability to change yourself.

After thinking for a moment, you finally remembered what you were supposed to do—you almost forgot! Hurriedly, you changed your clothes, pulled up the blanket, and curled up comfortably. You were almost asleep, after all, you were exhausted all day. The bright lights were so bright and blinding that you wanted to turn them off, but you were still thinking about Spamton, who hadn't slept yet, so you didn't move. Oh my god, you almost feel moved for yourself. After all, you're such a good person, willing to hold on a little longer for this.

Yawning again, you finally saw him come out. After all, you could hear him climbing onto the bed with your eyes closed, and of course, you also felt the bed sink in a lump together. Although it was slow, probably because he spent some time getting into bed. You still didn't open your eyes, just clumsily getting up and feeling for the switch on the wall, then turned off the light. Your vision turned dark again, so you happily and comfortably lay back down.

But after just a short while, you felt the blanket being pulled. You were slow for a moment before realizing something: Oh, you only prepared a quilt, not something for Spamton to cover up. So you awkwardly coughed twice in your heart, then turned over and chose to throw the blanket in that direction. In the darkness, even though you opened your eyes, you still couldn't see anything, so you were just following your instincts. Fortunately, you probably caught it, since you now felt a bit of wind leaking there.

You were a bit speechless, choosing to close your eyes and press against your blanket—well, it didn't help anyway. You simply reached forward and felt around, then pulled the Spamton a little toward you. Good, now there won't be any draft, but you think if you turn around, you'll press on him.

After a moment of silence, you choose to ignore it, just close your eyes and sleep!

You slept deeply and comfortably, a peaceful night without any dreams, sleeping soundly until dawn. You groggily opened your eyes, reacted dully for a moment, and realized Spamton was now in your arms...... Uh, but this was actually warm, and the blanket didn't let air out. But how did things end up like this? You couldn't help but start thinking about it.

After thinking for a long time, you still can't come up with an answer, so you can only guess it might be a subconscious behavior while sleeping. After all, you still like to wrap yourself tightly in a blanket like a zongzi, and you're used to sleeping alone. So it's probably normal that you didn't recognize Spamton as a person while you were asleep.

So you let go of the spamton, scratched your head, and didn't forget to apologize to him: "Uh, sorry, I didn't notice last night...... So I still think if you go to the floor, you'll sleep more freely...... "Of course you wouldn't make another quilt for him, because you're lazy. So thinking about the current situation, it's actually acceptable again.

"...... It's okay. In the game, you're in the same state." "He looks like he's already awake, maybe he didn't sleep well at all. You won't care about that. Since he hasn't complained much, just pretend nothing happened.

So you just coughed dryly: "Haha...... Really? That's really embarrassing. "Of course, you didn't look that embarrassed, just gave a couple of awkward laughs. Indeed, you remembered, it was the same before. You were hugging Spamton, fast asleep, since you had to rest up and recover before that killer returned.

Spamton didn't answer your question, just jumped to the next topic: "Maybe you could [consider] [breakfast time]." He really did wake up long ago. After all, being held by you isn't very comfortable. It's not unacceptable, but he always finds it a bit strange. Ultimately, maybe it's because your relationship isn't that close. So all of this feels a bit strange.

As for the specific strangeness—probably, everything seems indifferent in the game, but once you leave the game world, it feels like you have to look at it separately. You know this, and of course, he understands it too.

But whether you actually do it depends on your feelings. So now everything seems strange, since none of this is separated, yet the same pattern continues.

However, perhaps because they understood the situation, no one mentioned this abnormal condition, only maintaining it.

You let out a couple of 'oh ohs', yawned, and recalled slowly: "By the way, I forgot to buy breakfast...... There's absolutely nothing left at home, so let's go out for breakfast!" "Because it's still a bit cold outside, you don't want to crawl out of bed to change clothes. The blanket is so warm—if you weren't hungry, you'd even want to lie under the covers a little longer."

Now, you chose to change clothes under the covers. Spamton quietly turned around, probably out of self-awareness. Of course, he could still hear the sounds behind him clearly. After a rustling sound, it finally quieted down, and your voice came again.

"Alright, I'm done changing...... Hurry up and finish changing your own clothes. It's not like I didn't buy you new ones, so don't wear the old one anyway...... You muttered, then took out another set of clothes and tossed them next to Spamton, then you went over to wash up first.

Spamton was silent for a moment, then finally got out of bed.

Anyway, after all this was over, you skillfully lifted him onto your shoulder, patted your cheek to sober yourself up, and then walked out the door to look for food. There were quite a few breakfast shops on the neighboring street. At least you happily bought bread and sandwiches, dipped them in jam, and poked Spamton to signal him to pay for you. He split the price evenly with you, you accepted it happily, then stuffed a sandwich into his mouth.

He probably never expected you to do this, and in a flustered panic, he started eating the sandwich with both hands.

Soon, the breakfast was over. Eating bread sandwiches alone was quite choking, so you bought two steaming cups of orange juice. Even if you treated Spamton, you didn't ask him for money. You just thoughtfully handed him a straw when he finally let go of his sandwich halfway through eating. Of course, the force might be a bit strong. If he chokes, it doesn't really concern you. You just happily think you're so thoughtful, and you also drink the orange juice yourself.

After drinking the sweet and sour orange juice, you finally feel alive, your body warm, and you've eaten. So you pat your belly, yawn, and feel a bit sleepy—you pat your cheeks to try to wake up. At that moment, you feel Spamton poking your cheek, and instinctively raise the glass of orange juice, since he hasn't finished it yet.

But when you turn your head, you realize he actually handed you the Dark Coins. You glanced at them—it was exactly the price of two glasses of orange juice. You were a bit speechless and couldn't help but mutter, "I was already planning to treat you, so how did it end up being you inviting me......" Even though you said that, you still accepted the Dark Coins without hesitation and then shoved the orange juice into his hand.

"I want to express my sincere gratitude to you." He held the glass of orange juice in both hands, but didn't drink it. Instead, his tone was filled with seriousness as he spoke.

You waved your hand: "If you were truly thanking me, you wouldn't just treat me to a meal of orange juice, right? At least treat me to a big meal...... You complained, feeling Spamton stiffen on your shoulder. You were heading home, but walking very slowly. Now your hand was about to touch the doorknob, so you couldn't help but turn back to glance at Spamton.

“…… What, are you really going to treat me? Then I'm going to a fancy restaurant, I want to eat roast squab, roast lamb, and fried steak...... What else is there...... "You casually mentioned a few dish names, then couldn't continue. Forgive me, you've never been to a fancy restaurant, so of course you don't recognize those names. You only recognized some dishes when you happened to pass by before.

"No, it's just about [thinking] about [ways of expressing gratitude]." he said.

You shrugged: "Ah, if it were the simplest way...... You just need to help me finish the side quests properly. After all, I can't be bothered to think about the answers to those questions. If it were me, at most I could seriously complete the main quests...... "Just being able to complete the main quests seriously is already impressive!" You're too tired and really don't want to think about the problems anymore.

"I promise, I will complete the side quests seriously." Spamton said. Of course, his willingness to help you complete the side quests wasn't to express gratitude. What he said was just a token gesture...... He thought, he didn't necessarily have to do it all at once. As for completing side quests, half was waiting for you to take him along to complete the main quests, and the other half was that he also needed the reward amounts for side quests. Moreover, he had a feeling that these quests might not appear for nothing.

"Mm hm hm, that's good." Only then did you nod in satisfaction. After all… you didn’t really care whether he was expressing gratitude; you just needed a companion.

Anyway, you were only in a cooperative relationship, nothing more. Back at home, you picked up Spamton and put him on the floor. At least for now, the tasks you needed to do were basically finished. You couldn’t think of anything else, so you simply sat on the sofa and started scrolling through the phone you had just bought in the Dark World yesterday.

Spamton also climbed onto the sofa and sat next to you. You could still hear the sound of his straw, but you didn’t bother with it. You just kept scrolling through the news and updates from the Dark World, and nothing seemed particularly noteworthy.

The one-week free time given by the game system expired after your brief week residing in the Dark World. On this day, you sat on the sofa as usual, but your vision went blank for a moment. After a strong sense of dizziness, you arrived at another place. Of course, Spamton was right there on the floor beside you.
