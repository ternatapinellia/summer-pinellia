Horror Game Port 6 - Interlude - Monday 1 - sptx You

You calmly patted your cheek...... Forget it, not calm at all. You glanced at the time—it was still 3:30 a.m., just as you finished your part-time job and were about to go to bed, and somehow it ended up in that game—it must be Spamton's fault, how could it be your own fault? You firmly believed this, pushed aside the quilt with tore threads leaking through, got out of bed, passed over the stacks of instant noodle boxes and chocolate wrappers on the floor, closed the old computer with its screen lit up, and took a look at the door.

…… There's a bill on the door urging you to pay your water, electricity, and gas. You glance at the old calendar hanging on the wall, think for a moment, count on your fingers, and realize tomorrow is the payment deadline. Of course, since you can also move out of the lease, it doesn't really matter whether you pay or not. This is just next month's fee.

And after you got fired, you don't have money left to pay the collection and rent. You plan to pack up the last bit of things tomorrow and head to the Underworld—mainly to get your money back! Anyway, you think renting in the Underworld is fine!

…… So, let's sleep first. You closed the door. Luckily, in this weather, it was still a bit cold, but it was barely manageable. And you made it through the coldest times, so it didn't matter at all. You just skillfully curled yourself up, wrapped yourself in a quilt with a few holes, and drifted off to sleep.

The next morning, you still woke up punctually at nine, because normally you'd be going to the hospital at ten o'clock to work as a morgue porter, but yesterday you happened to be fired. You opened a box of instant noodles, boiled some water, ate a little, threw away the box, changed into decent clothes, and packed some luggage—all you could pack was just a slightly bigger backpack.

Then you thought about the location, touched your still empty belly, mustered your strength, and headed to the place where you remembered the intersection of the light and dark worlds. You passed through that entrance and entered the dark world. To be honest, you were a bit nervous. After all, last time you had entered by mistake and had no particular purpose, so it didn't matter. But today, you still had other motives, and you knew nothing about the paths of the dark world.

Forget it, since you're here, it's better to settle in. You tugged at the strap of your backpack, looked at the blue-black buildings and the neon signs hanging on the tall buildings, then at the neon lights slowly lighting up as you walked by. The shadows stretched longer and then lit up again with the next light—well, it looked like they were welcoming you. You calmly thought, what is there left to welcome someone like you?

But that thought lasts only a moment. You keep moving forward, forward—and then obviously, you're lost. According to Spamton's previous statement, do you think he probably lives in some kind of garbage station? Surely he can't live among the trash...... But now, looking at the brightly lit streets and the cars speeding by, you stop and look around. Good, it seems there isn't much to stop for and ask for directions, because all the faces you've never seen are quietly watching you. They think you haven't noticed at all, but you have noticed long ago, just pretending not to notice.

Maybe it's because you're a citizen of light. You find joy in hardship, but in fact, you're a failed citizen of light. Patting your cheek, you decide to move in a different direction and stop dwelling on these emotions. But as you take a step forward, you almost slip. Looking down, you see a foot stepping on a billboard. The bill is very old, wrinkled at the edges, and the image is no longer vibrant, but has turned a withered yellow. You've probably been drifting in this city for several days, since you've noticed several footprints on it.

You pulled your foot back, slightly disdainfully lifting a corner of the ad to take a look. Ah...... Actually, the design was pretty good, with the text and images arranged just right...... But that's not the point! You pulled back your drift and glanced at the image on the ad—oh, it was that annoying salesperson you encountered when you accidentally entered the dark world earlier. As for the name...... Your movement froze. Originally, it was just a casual glance, but now your eyes were fixed on that name.

Spamton G. Spamton.

…… Aha, you laughed silently twice inwardly, without any amusement. To be honest, you really did want to laugh out loud, maybe just ...... You wanted to mock yourself, maybe that's how it is? Your hand weakly loosened the flyer, watching it drift away with the wind that appeared at some unknown moment, not knowing whose foot it would land on or whose face it would hit, but you didn't care.

You were just a bit annoyed, but also a bit curious—after all, you were curious, and you never recognized him from start to finish. Now you still find it hard to believe—the gap is just too big.

But who it is and what their real identity is—it doesn't matter to them. It won't stop you from asking them to share the money that should have belonged to you! So you're just heading in the direction you chose. You know, that direction looks rather dull, and the walls are a bit old—maybe the direction of the garbage dump. You guess like this and try to lighten your mood.

Sure enough, but this place should probably be called a garbage heap too...... I really can't think of a good word to describe it. You walk around those piles of trash, then frown and pinch your nose. Honestly, you absolutely don't want to rummage through trash! Your life isn't that down-and-out. So now you hope Spamton can step out on its own, since you haven't found any residents at the garbage station.

You walk forward, and instead of piles of trash ahead, there are trash bins of all sizes. You finally don't have to cover your nose anymore. So you let go and start looking around, then your gaze settles on one of the large trash bins. You stroke your chin.

After all, you saw the lid of the trash bin move twice, and it looked even less tightly closed. If it were just like that, it would be fine, but after that, it moved several times after a while. So guess who's probably inside, and you think that's your target this time.

So you take a deep breath, get ready, suddenly open the lid of the trash bin, and then pull the Spamton out.

He seemed a bit stunned, but upon seeing you, he quickly recovered and said, "It's [you]." ”

You nod, loosen the lid of the trash bin, and reach out to Spamton to make a gesture for money: "Yes, it's me, hand over the money! My five thousand yuan, that's my five thousand!" ”

As you said this, you grabbed the Spamton and glared at him fiercely.

“…… Maybe [you] need [usage recommendations]? "I don't know why, but you felt this little puppet shivering—maybe it's a bit guilty?"

And you just snorted coldly: "No need. Now I know, you're the salesman who cheated all the money from me back then, so this time I won't trust you again! Just give me the money directly!" ”

Then you meet the gaze of the little puppet in front of you. You watch him silently for a moment, then reluctantly nod: "Then, I'll [lead], [you] will follow the action." ”

"That's more like it." You set him down on the ground with satisfaction, nodded, and watched him walk forward. You followed him slowly—to be honest, you hadn't noticed there was a hidden door just now, and it was a shortcut. You silently sighed in your heart.

Seeing that you were about to walk a bit further, you lowered your head to look at the back of the little puppet ahead, hesitated for a moment, and finally asked, "That ...... So I want to know, why did you make a deal with me back then?" ”

Actually, you thought you would forget about it, since it was mysterious back then and sounded like a scammer. You talk about getting what you most want for a tiny price, but you wonder how the other person knows what your dad's wish is, and the trivial price—you say you wouldn't care at all, but who knows what this salesman is really for?

In short, you only wanted to spend money to avoid disaster, so you painfully gave all the money you had to Spamton. Honestly, that was your newly paid salary! So after that, you never wanted to come back to the underworld, and you felt that day's stumble was truly unlucky.

Spamton paused for a moment, and a bit distracted, you almost kicked him in. Luckily, you noticed in time and hurriedly stopped, ready to hear what he was going to say—no, before that, you should complain about why he stopped!

But before you could speak, you already heard his voice: "I believe [you] can [cooperate]...... Or, for [you], I can [provide a use]. I believe [you] [need]. "But he did not specify exactly what he could provide, nor did he say what he hoped to require.

You gazed at his back, in this eerie silence that almost made people panic, in the quiet sound of the wind, you softly spoke: "Are you lying...... No, maybe you're telling the truth, but you're just dodging the issue. You never answered my question directly, and I didn't want to know what you could offer. ”

You follow in his footsteps, and the destination seems just within reach. You see a shop at the end of a garbage heap. Why would a shop be in a place like this? You really can't understand.

"...... [Sky]." When you were distracted, you heard him whisper, probably answering your question.

So you couldn't help but speak, and your curiosity was piqued. You asked, "What?" ”

"I believe we can reach a higher and higher sky through you, observe, or even climb higher. I believe perhaps by touching you, we can fulfill our expectations." You can't see his expression, only hear his somewhat dull and slow voice.

So you fell silent. To be honest, you didn't quite understand—but you could roughly grasp what he meant. Probably hoping you could find something like freedom through you.

But why you? Is it just because you're a Citizen of Light? Does that mean no other Light Folk come to the Dark World? Uh...... Well, maybe he hasn't seen it before.

So you simply chose to remain silent, following in his footsteps, and didn't ask any more questions.

Then you walked into the shop—cramped, with walls painted blue—or maybe blue skies and white clouds. You casually scanned the narrow shop, watched Spamton slip behind the counter, then took out five thousand Dark Coins and handed them to you. You nodded in satisfaction and put away the Dark Coins.

But suddenly, you hear Spamton speak: "How do you plan to [use] these [silver dollars]?" ”

It took you two seconds to realize what he was talking about. Then you looked down at the Dark Coin that had just been put in your bag and hadn't been warmed, and hesitated for a moment: "Hmm, Dark Coins can only be used in the Dark World, right? So I'm planning to rent a place there and stay here for now." ”

Anyway, you've been fired, and you don't have cash on hand right now. You might as well stay in the Dark World for a while.

"Not [return] [Light World] to conduct [Residence Activities]?" Spamton asked.

But you just shook your head, shrugged indifferently: "No, my rental over there just expires today, and I was fired yesterday. The only job I have left was laid off, so it's fine to stay in the dark world for a while." ”

…… What else could it help you escape? But you didn't say these things. After all, you and Spamton are just familiar strangers; you don't think you can talk about these more private matters. If it were just a casual remark, forget it.

For some reason, he suddenly got motivated: "So, I'm very sorry, and maybe you need a [housing recommendation]?" ”

You stared at him with wide eyes, wanting to say that you didn't need it at all—well, actually, you did, after all, you were completely unfamiliar with this dark world and didn't even know the way.

But you still asked cautiously, 'So, how many Darkness Coins do you need as payment?'

'One thousand [Dollars],' he said.

You drew in a sharp breath—so expensive, that's a fifth of the money right now! But perhaps the prices in the dark world really are like that? You had never bought anything here, so you reserved your doubts and just asked one more question: 'Is this the normal price?'

'[Yes], [definitely].'

Hmm… anyway, you'll still go into a shop later to check properly. For now, you half-believingly agreed with Spamton.

'Alright, deal. But I won’t fully trust you; I’ll check it myself!' You reached out your hand, not forgetting to add sharply.
