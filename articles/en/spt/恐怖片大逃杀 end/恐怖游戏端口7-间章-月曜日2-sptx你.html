Horror Game Port 7 - Interlude - Monday 2 - sptx You

…… Well, actually, adding it again is just to avoid forgetting yourself. If you don't remind yourself, you would easily believe Spamton's recommendations. After all, you remember the good but not the bad. Although you might get angry when you first remember, you will forget it later. You only remember the person who took all your money back then, and now he has returned all that money to you! True, anyway, in the underworld, this cash is useless.

You happily followed behind Spamton, walking slowly as you counted the cash. Good, no problem, still the amount you lost that day. But this little money doesn't seem to be enough to do much, hmm, maybe you can consider it yourself......

Before you could finish your thoughts, you almost kicked Spamton again. You couldn't guarantee how much your toes would hurt if you hit the puppet, so you quickly stopped, then looked up and saw rows of houses. Then you knew you'd reached your destination.

It looked like there were some residents, but not many...... You looked around thoughtfully, nodded, and walked toward one of the houses that looked nice. The landlord was still standing there, and it looked like all these were bulk rental properties. Compared to those with agents, these houses were a bit cheaper than those with agents, but their safety and legality weren't guaranteed......

Just as you were thinking this, you noticed Spamton was still following you, because he had just bumped your leg. You lowered your head and looked at him in surprise: "...... Why are you still following me? You've already taken me to our destination. I'm grateful to you, but that's all. ”

"I think maybe your silver dollars aren't enough." But that's all he said.

You pouted, reluctantly accepting his explanation. But to be honest—you don't think five thousand Dark Coins isn't enough!

As it turns out, he was right, and you were overthinking it. The house here also requires a month's deposit and three months' rent, and every month it's exactly 1,500 Dark Coins, meaning it will be used up just right.

After looking around, most of them were two-bedroom apartments, and there were some one-bedroom apartments, but they were really cramped and simple. You might as well go back and renew that room—after all, you're used to living there and getting used to it.

You put your hands on your hips, thought for a moment, then looked at Spamton, making eye contact with him. Spamton was looking at you innocently. In the end, you gritted your teeth: "Alright then...... Then, let's split the rent and take you with us! So you have to pay half the cost, which means splitting the bill evenly! ”

Now you remember you actually wanted to check out the prices here, but you had forgotten about it earlier...... But it's still not too late to check it out later.

Spamton nodded, then took out three thousand Dark Coins and handed them to you. You sighed, walked up to one of the landlords, and handed him the six thousand Dark Coins along with them. Then you picked up Spamton and asked him to add contact information with the landlord—after all, you don't have a phone from the Dark World.

Now, everything is over. You walk in with your bag and casually toss it aside. Honestly, you really don't have much luggage, so you only need to buy some daily necessities and food. After sliding the clothes from your bag onto the bookshelf, you grab your Spamton and walk out.

"Come with me to buy something." You said angrily, but fortunately, there were still grocery stores and convenience stores in the Dark World, unlike his shop, which looked rundown.

"I think that [shop] might be more suitable." Spamton pointed to a shop not far away. You looked toward that direction and found it was also a small, old shop. Then you looked at the convenience store in front of you, which was simple yet clean and hygienic, and fell silent.

You squinted and noticed something interesting: "What, you don't want me to go in?" Could it be that the prices of these two items are different? ”

Spamton quickly shook his head. You couldn't read his expression, and in any case, you couldn't see any signs of lying. You walked into the convenience store in front of you, half-believing—you definitely wouldn't go to that obviously suspicious shop!

…… As it turned out, the prices here seemed quite cheap. Of course, you didn't really understand it. You just knew that when you bought a bunch of things and paid the bill, you only spent a few dozen Dark Coins.

So when you came out carrying a pile of pots and pans, you started staring at Spamton: "...... Be honest, where exactly did you take me just now? Why was it so expensive!" You gritted your teeth, feeling like he had scammed you again. Maybe on purpose, to ride on the house you rented or something. Honestly, can't he just rent a place himself?!

Spamton shook his head and once again avoided answering: "Perhaps [you] need [transportation]?" ”

"I don't need that. If I were to move these things back, it would be easy. I'm very strong—no, don't change the subject, you have to pay me up!" If it weren't for being so unavailable, you would have grabbed his collar and shook it hard.

"Then, maybe [you] misunderstood." The amount of these [houses] is 1,500 [silver dollars] for twelve [months], which means renting [for the whole year]. "When you hear his voice, there is no trace of guilt.

You grit your teeth in frustration: "I don't need to rent for that long...... Ask the landlord if that's the case. If so, then ask if you can cancel early! ”

You feel like you've been inexplicably tricked, and you have to bring this guy along—well, now it's something annoying. You think it's your own fault, forgetting what you said just a few minutes ago, forgetting to remind yourself to check elsewhere, and instead believing Spamton's words.

…… Sigh, you know you're a bit slow to react and easily trust others. This has always been your problem. But you think that after suffering this loss, you'll definitely remember it next time.

Holding a box of things, you watch Spamton open his phone, its glow flickering. Of course, your eyesight is sharp, so you can clearly see what he's chatting—ah, yes, it was a conversation with the landlord, indeed asking how long 1,500 Dark Coins lasted, and you could rent for a year and then return it—it seemed you were used to the price of the Light World, so you didn't ask too much.

You grit your teeth and hear Spamton's casual remark: "[Sure]." ”

Of course you know, but for some reason, you're still holding back a lot of anger—uh, well, maybe you'll forget about it again soon. You're always like this, but this time you swear in your heart, you must remember this!

Anyway, you quicken your pace and return to the place you just rented—ah, now it should be called your home. Uh, or rather, your home...... No, just roommates, you see Spamton climbing up to the sofa and sitting there, which always feels a bit awkward.

Tsk, forget it, better not see him for now, or you'll get mad again. You quietly turned your head and started packing up with a breath of anger.

After cleaning up and placing pots, pans, and the like in various spots, you went out and bought a few more quilts and covers to lay them back on the bed. The originally empty wooden bed instantly became full, and now it was even cozier. You stood with your hands on your hips, looking at your results, and nodded in satisfaction.

Then you look toward another room and fall silent. To be honest, you don't really want to make a bed for Spamton...... Judging by how he looks, it's definitely not okay, and besides, that bed is too big, not suitable, right?

The more work you do, the more work you get, and the more diligent you are, the more you'll take on the work for yourself. You agree with that. Anyway, you can handle it now, so you just collapse onto your own bed.

You heard Spamton's voice: "My [residence]?" ”

You turned over and said indifferently, "Ahh...... No, if you want to sleep, go make your own bed. I bought it for you, and I paid for it myself! ......! But you have to live here too, so you can do some chores yourself! ”

You definitely don't want to take on the work for yourself! And in the end, no one pays you, so why do all this? You muttered to yourself like this.

Then you feel the soft bed sink. You tilt your head to look and see that Spamton has climbed up the bed and is lying down beside you.

You were speechless for a second, but the bed was indeed huge, right? Still, you couldn't help but ask: "So your solution is to share a bed with me?" Then I'd rather have a one-bedroom apartment...... No, if I move to a new place to rent now, I could kick you out......

You muttered, though you had no intention of getting up. Because you were afraid of trouble, so if you wanted to keep things as they were, so be it. You just couldn't be bothered to move.

"[Solution]. One person's [half cost], so it's a [reasonable action]. " he said.

You actually know what he means—because he really did give half the money, so you can't kick him out. But if you see him now, you'll be furious, since he tricked you into handing over all that money, so you can only split it half.

So you turned around, choosing not to see his face again, but your voice was muffled: "Alright, this bed is big, so we can split it half and half...... If you lie there and don't bother me, it's fine......" You muttered to yourself, then felt your face gently poked. You looked up and saw Spamton had somehow crawled over to your side and even poked your cheek.

Raising your eyebrows, you simply turn around, twist his cheeks, and pull him to the sides: "What are you doing—didn't they say not to bother me?" What are you doing—"

Well, the puppet's face isn't very interesting to pull at either. Honestly, you even find it a bit hard to pull, so in the end you let go and just silently stared at Spamton.

Very good, now you're in that strange state of rage, but still very calm—so you just quietly look at Spamton.

"Very [sorry][disturbing behavior]. I think this can [get your attention]; I have some [questions] that need [consult] [you]. "He said, you tilted your head, and you can see Spamton lying right next to you.

You took a deep breath. Honestly, you were suppressing your anger. And why did I have to bother you just to ask you questions! That's what you thought, and of course you asked: "If it's just a question, you can ask like that too. As long as I can answer, I'll say it. So do you think that face-to-face things will make me seem more sincere? No matter what, I won't lie, right...... As you speak, your voice quiets. You look at Spamton, and as he meets your gaze, you find it hard to believe.

Because you feel, yes, that's right, that's exactly what he thinks.

…… Alright then. You take another deep breath: "Uh...... Alright. Alright! Just say what you want to ask, I won't ask so much...... "It feels like asking more won't help, and if you turn around again, Spamton will definitely find a way to make you turn back, so in the end, you just keep your head down and look at him."

Of course, now that you're lying in bed, all of this seems even more unbelievable.

"I want to [know] that [you] don't have a [direct answer] answer to a [question] in the [game]."

You thought about what the problem he meant was...... To be honest, your memory isn't very good, so the room fell into an eerie silence. You really did think hard for a while and finally remembered what the problem was.

So you cough twice: "Ah, I remember now, that question......" Then you fall silent again.

Actually, you're not very opposed to answering that question, because you stopped caring. But for you, answering that question doesn't help much. Still, it's definitely easier to ask a familiar stranger. Maybe...... Actually, you do care a little, but after forgetting it, you think everything is fine.

You rarely remember those things, so now that you're asked to recall them, you actually feel a bit down.

You cleared your throat, lifted your head to look at the ceiling, stared blankly, and fell into your memories: “Ah, actually it's nothing really, it's just that I’ve always been slow to react since I was little, and I’m not very smart, so I’ve always done a little worse than others in school because I usually hand in my paper as soon as I figure out the solution, and it’s the same during exams. Plus, because my family was very poor and had many children, I wasn’t the most important one; I’m the eldest daughter, and I was responsible for taking care of everyone. Maybe it’s because I spent too much time taking care of others. Anyway, my grades were always poor. After I didn’t get into university, my parents scolded me as trash—well, it wasn’t a big deal, they’ve said things like that to me more than once anyway.”

You finished this passage in one breath; you didn’t really need a response, or rather, you just needed someone to hear it, even though you weren’t particularly sad. Then you tilted your head and looked at Spamton, who didn’t seem to react at all, before intending to continue speaking.
