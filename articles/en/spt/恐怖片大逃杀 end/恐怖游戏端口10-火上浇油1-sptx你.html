Horror Game Port 10 - Add Fuel to the Fire 1 - sptx You

[Main Quest: Explore the Building, Head to the Top Floor] Reward: One Month of Rest and Security Protection.

[Side Quest: Explore the Building and Learn the Cause of Its Current State] Reward: 100,000 currency + 100,000 Dark Coins

The light screen appeared before you, and seeing the reward this time, you let out a sigh of relief—if the rewards aren't split yet, you'll really have to settle in the Dark World, but at least for now, you don't have that plan.

You looked around—thick fog, thick fog, endless fog, surrounding and obscuring everything else. Maybe the sunlight was just right, since the sky was clear, with only endless splashes of blue paint and no accidental graffiti of white clouds. Maybe it would be strange if there was fog right now—but you knew, it was just a forbidden place to explore. Only the building before you stood tall, as if it could pierce straight into the clouds. Through the building, you could even see the reflected sunlight, so dazzling that you couldn't help but close your eyes when you looked up—but you still noticed that through the window, you couldn't see inside the building. Of course, perhaps because the sunlight was just right, you shouldn't have seen it anyway.

But—you look at the glass door before you, unable to see what is inside, only through the glass you see pitch black. This is probably the strangest place, since there are glass windows all around. Even if there is darkness without light, it shouldn't be pitch black, but should be filled with bright light, or rather, a bright scene in between.

However, you took a deep breath and swallowed hard. No matter how strange it was, it didn't matter—it just looked strange on the outside. It didn't mean you would encounter anything strange inside, nor did it add any pressure to be paranoid. Persuading yourself like this, you lowered your head, looked at Spamton, then skillfully lifted him up and sat him on your shoulder, saying casually, "Alright, the side quest is still up to you to complete. Reaching the top of the building is still a breeze, isn't it?" ”

Although you say this, you know the building looks neglected for years. Even the lock on the glass door is rusted, forming iron-red rust swirling over the lock. Of course, it probably won't damage it if you touch it—it's just that you always have to be suspicious. And that means the elevator in this building must be broken, meaning you probably have to walk up the stairs. This is probably a test of physical strength and endurance. However, you naturally aren't afraid of this kind of test; in fact, you're eager to try.

"Naturally, this is a cooperation treaty." he said.

You nodded casually and stepped forward to tentatively push open the glass door. You thought that if the main character of the game was to explore the building, they probably wouldn't block you at the door; otherwise, there would be nowhere else to look for the key. Sure enough—you only gently pushed the door, but at first it was unmoving and clumsy, stuck by the lock, so you could still see only complete darkness. But when you instinctively touched the lock and instinctively grabbed the rust, it broke apart, making a crisp clanging sound as it fell to the ground.

You stared silently at the red lock, unsure whether it was an illusion or if it was truly so, but in that instant, you saw a bright red and even orange-yellow glow like a red-hot branding iron. Beneath the lock, on the clean gray-white floor, gushing blood gushed out. It wasn't a sudden gush of blood, but rather ...... It slowly oozed out. Though it was blood, it wasn't exactly fresh; instead, it carried an unsettling black-red color, lingering in that moment with an irritating stench of rot and blood. Widening your eyes, it was as if you were under a nightmare, or perhaps you didn't want to miss such a sight. Although you didn't find it particularly noteworthy, perhaps you found it novel, since you had never seen anything supernatural before.

Until the blood spreads, slowly spreading to your feet, until the black around your calf slowly rises, until—you don't know what will happen next, because you feel your face being poked, it's spamton. You instinctively shiver and blink. Indeed, everything before your eyes is just an illusion, because now, everything before you is clean, except for that broken lock and the rust marks scattered on the floor.

You patted your cheeks, glanced at Spamton, and for once, felt a bit embarrassed: "...... Thank you." ”

He didn't answer your question, but simply asked, "Just now, [you] kept [staring] in the same direction...... Did you find anything? "Spamton also observed for a while and noticed you seemed almost obsessed, staring blankly in the same direction, with eyes wide open, showing no other reaction. Only then did he decide to try to awaken your attention."

You scratched your cheek awkwardly, silently recalled for a moment, and then said, "Ah...... Hmm. Let's just say that's how it is, but are you really not seeing it? "It seems you're the only one who saw what just happened.

You look at Spamton, and as expected, he shakes his head. That's the answer you expected.

Well, it seems like only you saw it. You let out a long sigh: "Ah...... It's nothing, maybe it's a good thing you couldn't see? Could it be that my inspiration was too strong to see this scene...... You muttered to yourself, instinctively avoiding the topic. After all, you didn't know how to describe it, so you took a deep breath and pushed open the door.

Spamton wanted to say more, but now you had already entered the building. The door slammed shut with a bang. You turned around, but all you could see was unsettling black. If you hadn't known, the glass door wasn't painted black—you'd have believed it was black anyway. You tried pulling the doorknob, but it was tightly shut and wouldn't open again.

You exhale, but at that moment you force a smile, the corners of your mouth lifting: "Hmm...... Very good, it looks like you can't get out, so you can only explore forward—is the theme here something you can't look back at? Just like that Greek mythology...... Uh...... At this point, you get stuck, because you've only heard of it but aren't familiar with the name of that legend. Of course, you're just trying to hide that sudden chill.

But Spamton didn't answer you. He wasn't clear about what Greek mythology you mentioned...... Well, maybe he actually knew.

So he hesitated for a moment and replied, "Orps and Eurydice?" ”

You turn around and clapp: "Yes, that's the name!" Hmm...... It's a choice between death and survival, a fate where once you turn back, you'll always face death. Or perhaps, it's also a symbol...... You muttered this phrase in a daze, took two steps forward, and gazed at the building. The white floor seemed as if it had never been abandoned; the floor even reflected the ceiling's reflection in its cleanliness and honesty, and as you walked on it, your reflection was reflected too. Shadows refracted between the tiles, sometimes long, sometimes short, tinged with shadowy hues, and at rare times tinged with sunlight, leaving only unknown light.

You deliberately looked around. Naturally, all the windows were closed, or rather, all the glass was intact. But now, through this glass, all you could see was the outside blue—only blue, endless blue. If that's the case, why was the scent inside the building still so fresh? Could there be another vent, or maybe it was coming down from upstairs? You couldn't help but make this association. And...... Even though the windows were tightly shut and there shouldn't be any sensation, you could still feel the wind blowing from the left side. You were certain that the window on that side was closed.

You sigh and ask, "Do you feel the wind?" ”

Spamton hesitated for a moment, then just shook his head. Actually, he didn't feel anything. When you led him into the building, all he saw was a patch of chipped floors, yellowed gray-yellow cement exposed by broken tiles, peeling paint on the walls, and ugly gray-white or yellow cracks that couldn't be removed. He didn't feel any wind, since all the windows had been nailed shut. What he saw was just a dim yellow, and at that very moment, the door behind him was nailed to the wooden boards.

…… So, he didn't even know how you managed to successfully lead him into the building. At that moment, he didn't ask any additional questions.

You were silent for a moment, then met Spamton's gaze. His gaze looked so innocent, and you couldn't help but ask, "Speaking of which, is it that we actually feel different, or rather, what we see is different?" "You just think it's possible. After all, if only you could sense it, that would be too strange. You wouldn't accept it. So you have to find some reason that can convince you."

"Maybe. What I saw was the [Collapsed Building]." "He had no intention of hiding it from you, after all, you still need to figure out how to get out of here. Reporting to the Intelligence Department isn't a good thing, and it's not a role-playing game where you have to betrayal a teammate.

You widened your eyes, surprised that just a casual remark would turn into a prophecy. So it really is different.

So you couldn't help but murmur twice, "It really is different...... What I saw on my end was a new building? Well, it's not like what you saw. Since you're seeing an abandoned building, what I see is probably a building from its heyday...... You murmured to yourself, curiosity piqued as you began exploring this floor. To be honest, it was empty and nothing worth noting, but you couldn't help but search for the direction the wind was blowing. Your hand involuntarily touched the pristine white wall—and then you were startled.

After all, when you first touched it, it was indeed cold, but it was a slightly slippery sensation, making you want to vomit and even get goosebumps. At least you withdrew your hand and looked at the pristine white wall, but for some reason, you could see some skin color, and even faintly see veins flowing with bluish-purple.

You swallowed twice and instinctively poked Spamton: "Uh...... Honestly, from your perspective, what does this wall look like? ”

After you said that, Spamton took a closer look at the wall. Just now, he had only paid extra attention to the signs of damage and the orange-red bricks peeking out from behind the wall, showing that the restoration hadn't been done very seriously. Now, he looked at the wall again, saw the wall panel, and naturally noticed what was behind the half-peeling, crumbling wall.

"...... [Wall], with [Black] [Hair]. Beneath [Wall Skin] is [Yellow] [Fat]. "Calling it yellow isn't quite accurate; it should be brown, since it's not very fresh anymore. It's probably been a long time since it changed to this color, maybe mixed with flesh and blood." Of course, Spamton didn't say this out loud.

"And, the 'wall shell cracks' are 'burnt marks.'" These are marks of a mix of dark brown and brownish-yellow hues, so maybe the fat had also been roasted. Although at this moment, all that remained was the stench of rot. It was precisely at this moment that he noticed that the dark brown burn marks on the wall weren't just fractures; in fact, the entire wall was more or less mixed with these marks.

You retched twice. Well, you actually can't see or feel anything, so it's just for show. After adjusting your state, you pat your cheek and take a deep breath: "Alright, I'm still stuck here. Let's keep moving forward." You say that, continuing in the direction the wind is coming from.

Until you stopped in front of a wall, you realized that the wind was coming from behind it, though you didn’t know why. You hesitated for a moment, tapped twice on the wall, and heard a hollow echo. After a brief pause, you said, 'There should be a hidden door here… Can you see where the doorknob is from your perspective?'

Spamton saw a door with no markings, even completely dark, of course, it also bore burn marks, which seemed to be spread throughout the entire building. He looked down and saw the doorknob, then reached out to point a direction for you.

"[Over there]."

You followed his direction and indeed felt yourself grasp a doorknob. Every moment you pressed down, opening the door, and the first thing you saw was darkness. Strangely… there was a dim yellow oil lamp hanging on the wall.
