Horror Game Port 5 - Old House in the Forest 5 (end) - sptx you

In that darkness, and in the faint, flickering candlelight that had appeared at some point, you could vaguely see your own shadow. You wondered where the wind came from, why the candlelight was flickering—and where was the flame coming from? Clearly, this place should be pitch black.

You couldn't help but shiver. You clearly didn't feel the cold, but through the faint firelight, you saw yourself—a mirror not far away—or rather, a blackened window glass. On it, only a faint image could be seen. The person who had blacked it didn't want others to notice it. The wind still lingered through the window slit, gently stirring the window lattice, making a clicking sound.

This also explains why the candle flame flickers. Probably so. In short, everything seems explainable. Thinking this, you lower your head to look at the candle flame somewhere low, wondering how the candle flame actually comes from.

Then, you realize you've forgotten something—it's Spamton, who has followed you into this dark room. You open your mouth to call his name, but hesitate, afraid to speak in the oppressive darkness. That unknown darkness and silence envelop you, and in that silence, unexpectedly, you can hear the sound of your heart.

As for why it was pitch dark—after all, when you opened the door and entered the dark room, the door behind you rumbled shut again. So now you still regret it. If you'd known last time, you wouldn't have waited for the last time. You might as well have risked your life to hide in the dark room, especially since there was a window here.

At this very moment, you shake your head to shake off those thoughts and lower your head to look at the candlelight. After your vision adjusts to some darkness, you realize the person holding the candlestick is actually Spamton. He doesn't say a word, just holds the candlestick quietly watching you, lost in thought, so much so that in the darkness, you inexplicably feel timid when looking at his glasses.

As for Spamton? He hadn't spoken at all, after all, he wanted to see how you would react. To be honest, he didn't mean to do this. After noticing your silence, he watched your face by candlelight, and at that moment found you panicked and timid. Your expression sometimes seemed timid, sometimes brave by showing off. Spamton found it interesting to observe your emotional changes. He roughly had a guess...... Maybe in the future, he could make some kind of deal with you in the dark, as if you were afraid of the dark.

Then you cough twice: "Cough, cough...... Where do you get the anger? "That's your biggest confusion."

"I [found] there is a [flint] on the floor of the darkroom." Spamton pointed to the floor not far away. If you look in the direction he pointed, there really is a flint there.

"Alright, it seems you have sharp eyesight in the dark too." You shrugged and said casually, while Spamton neither agreed nor disagreed; he was just subconsciously observing his surroundings.

You bent down and gently took the candlestick from Spamton's hand, but he didn't stop you. Then you walked not far away, looking at the candlesticks of all sizes and heights filling the room, gently approaching with the candlelight in your hand and lighting one candlestick after another. Then, in this dim yet bright room, you stared at the things on the floor and fell silent, then couldn't help but start retching. Now you knew what that faint, lingering scent of blood in the air really was.

Because—lying on the floor were several heads casually thrown there, most of which wore terrified expressions. Some were still fresh, but their faces were a bit swollen, showing an unattractive bluish-purple color. More than that, probably because they had been placed for a long time, red or yellow fluids had flowed out, and the blood mixed together was nearly dried up, the skin shriveled and gradually turned dark brownish-black. The cut surfaces also turned black, and you could see the greasy yellow dripping down before slowly drying up.

You pinched your nose. Besides that, there was a magic circle carved on the ground. These heads looked like offerings, perhaps simply for worship—who were they worshipping? Because when you looked up, you saw two tombstones directly ahead.

Spamton didn't say anything, just closed his eyes, then grabbed the clothes at your pant leg. You felt the pressure on your pant leg and looked down at him—well, you could only see the top of the little puppet's head, so you couldn't be bothered to think further. You just circled around the two tombstones and looked up, only to find that the walls were actually filled with various photos—including cropped printed text.

You get dizzy just looking at these characters. You don't want to decrypt them, so you pick up Spamton and sit on your shoulder. It's still more convenient for Spamton to see directly.

You turned your head to glance at him, pursed your lips, and said, "In that case, I'll just leave these text and photos for you to see. If you say you want to see something, just let me move a seat." "At this moment, you're just a ruthless commuting machine."

Spamton meets your gaze. Well, fine, he can only see sincerity and clear foolishness in your eyes, and it's clear you have no desire to decode—but from his guess, you actually do think anyway, just a bit slow to react. But since you want to, he still looks at the wall.

First are the photos—you saw those too, most of them solo shots, faces you don't recognize. Only two were of two people, and then you thoughtfully nodded at one of the pictures: "Ah...... These two people, I've seen before, are the people who appeared in the group photo that the killer didn't ruin—probably his parents. ”

Spamton nodded and turned to another photo, which also showed a married couple. Between the two photos were several letters, which looked handwritten, though it was unclear how the killer had found him. In short, it looked ...... letters from them pouring out their hearts, with handwriting both male and female.

So Spamton pointed to the photo: "That photo is his [parents''s] [affair partner]." ”

You nod thoughtfully: "Ahh...... I see. So what about these people? "You've given up thinking, and you don't want to look. Let Spamton take a look."

"Then [you] need to move to the [left]." he said.

So you move a few steps to the left to make it easier for Spamton to look. He read the text hanging on the wall. To be honest, it was all commentary—not just printed, but also cropped from newspapers. Most were comments about previous reports, mocking his parents for both cheating on another couple and deserving of their deaths. Some comments were calling for lenient sentences, but it seemed people in this world despised cheating even more. Maybe it was a world where both spouses could kill one of the cheaters.

So he turned his head to you, and possibly to the light screen that appeared at some point, explaining: "After the [death report], the person who appeared on the photo wall mocked the [report's content] and [parents]. Therefore, he decided to [take revenge], and at the same time, [mourning] and [sacrifice] to his parents. And the [hunting behavior] is meant to make the [targeted] and [former perpetrator] feel [fear] and experience [the same confusion they once had]. The only question is how he [chooses the target]......"

You all saw it. With his words, the completion rate of side quests kept rising, only stopping when he reached 80%.

You rubbed your chin, wondering how to pick a target, how to get people to come here...... Then you thought about the car you saw in that clearing and casually said, "This place is a tourist destination...... It used to be the place where his parents drove over here? ”

You see the progress bar for side quests has turned to 88%

So you nod involuntarily, and Spamton quieted down. He quietly watched your profile, realizing that even if he thought he understood you, he could still notice something different the next moment.

And you didn't notice his gaze, just continued talking to yourself, or maybe guessed: "And as long as you spread some rumors and rumors that the couple who died here have become vengeful spirits or something, only by coming here to worship will they avoid being entangled. Those with hidden feelings will fall for it!" ”

But you're not just guessing. In fact, when you arrived at that clearing earlier and hurriedly glanced past the few campervans parked there, you caught sight of candles and other items left in the car, along with bouquets—clearly meant for those coming to pay respects.

So you still combine your previous findings with your speculation, and then you'll find that the side quest progress has reached 100%, with flowers and fireworks appearing on the screen—probably congratulations on completing the quest.

You cheerfully say, "Wow, this task is done—ten thousand dollars, that's ten thousand!" You were excited, but the next second, you realized that ten thousand coins had turned into ten thousand dark coins, and you lost your smile. To be honest, although most of it was done for Spamton, at least 20% was done by you. And without you, Spamton wouldn't even know what would have happened at this moment!

So you glanced at Spamton quietly; he probably felt guilty too. You sensed the little puppet's body was probably stiff, but you didn't care about it. You just grabbed his shoulder and started shaking him.

"I don't care...... You have to give me half of these ten thousand. Dark coins are dark coins, and I want the currency circulating in the dark world too! I can't give you everything, or what would I be! "You grumbled in annoyance, tugging at Spamton, watching him make a surrender gesture, and only then did you feel a bit satisfied.

"So, quickly tell me the address...... When I get back, I'm going to the Dark World to catch you out......" you said in a sinister tone, grabbing him by the back of his neck and lifting him up in front of you. You stared at him fiercely, very clearly. You could tell Spamton was about to tell you something—you felt satisfied.

But before you could get an answer, you all heard the door ringing. Listening in the darkroom would sound dull and blurry, and you instinctively grew nervous. You have to know, the bookshelf hadn't been restored to its original state; the killer would definitely come to check the room, and maybe you two would die here—so you quickly ran to the window trying to open it, not caring whether Spamton answered you or not. You shook it twice and found the window was completely nailed shut, unless ...... Unless you smashed the window and got out.

You gazed quietly at the pitch-black window, then at the Spamton in your hand—instinctively, you reached out and pinched the little puppet's skin. Hmm, it really did look like wood, but it still had some hardness, so smashing the window might be fine! Watching your movements and extensions, Spamton had a bad feeling, and the next second, that feeling came true.

Because you raised him up with both hands, then really smashed a huge hole in the window—the crisp sound of glass shattering. The next second, you could hear the killer quicken his pace, the heavy footsteps approaching the dark room. There was no time to say anything else. You looked at the gap in the window you had smashed and struggled to clutch the Spamton and crawl out. Honestly, the Spamton was actually quite useful, you thought to yourself.

As for Spamton...... Honestly, breaking glass or being scratched by broken glass is really painful, but probably because the force is too large, that long-lasting pain actually fades after a while.

And you didn’t care about so much; who cares what it really looks like when you’re about to die? You just ran forward holding Spamton, then instinctively looked down at him. He silently pointed you in a direction, so you continued running that way until a light suddenly appeared ahead, until the crunching of leaves underfoot stopped, until you stepped once more onto soft soil. You saw vehicles stopped ahead, and you found one with the key still in it, not taken out… well, to be honest, it seemed like the keys of all the cars hadn’t been taken out. Probably the killer put them there? After all, giving a little hope makes the hunt more interesting, and you get to see the human flaws of competing and betraying each other.

You couldn’t think about so much. You quickly climbed into that car, put Spamton in the passenger seat, and then shakily started driving. You could hear the chainsaw getting closer to your direction, and when you looked back, you could still see the killer.

The car kept swaying, and Spamton didn’t have time to fasten his seatbelt, just raised his voice a bit: “【Request: Smooth ride—】”

You also raised your voice: “Ah—I don’t have a driver’s license, I’m just driving blindly, hang in there, anyway, as long as we can get out—” You pressed the accelerator to the max, speeding up to the top, and soon the sound of the chainsaw faded away. The trees ahead gradually thinned, revealing an exit to the outside, faintly showing a few houses and a distant road.

“Oh, right, so where exactly are you—address—”

Before reaching the end, you were out of breath, speaking loudly. You turned your head to look at Spamton’s mouth moving, but unfortunately, you couldn’t hear him. Because as you crossed the finish line, a blinding flash of white occurred, everything around melted in that instant, and you couldn’t help but close your eyes. When you opened them the next second, you were sitting up in your small, dark rented room.

You barely remembered Spamton’s lip movements… garbage, does he live in a dump? No way! You suspected it was just your own dream, but the next second, a strange voice sounded, reminding you that all of what just happened wasn’t a dream.

“Ding dong, main quest completed successfully, congratulations, player.”
