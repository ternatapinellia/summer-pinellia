I don't really want to pry into it." "But Spamton has already taken the initiative to ask about your past. If you ask him, it might be a kind of courtesy, right?" That's what would be fair, right?

You see him stop what he's doing, probably not very willing to speak. Then you wait a moment before hearing his voice break the silence.

His voice was very calm, though you could still hear a slight trembling in it. It was clear he wasn't, or rather, was very unwilling to recall that past, but he still told you. Think about it, this really is a case of reciprocity...... Well, actually, when you talk about your own past, it's not that you don't care at all; it's just that there are more things you should care about. You just shouldn't keep getting caught up in those feelings, so you end up letting the feelings that once erupt be pushed away.

Once a member of Addison, he never sold anything, until one day he received a phone call that changed his fate, even moving into the Queen's mansion...... Until one day, the magic that changed his fate failed, he was no longer a big shot, kicked out, and even thrown into a pool of acid.

You thought of it at an inopportune moment, and it was like some magic that fails at midnight, except the cost was heavy. When he finished speaking, you just patted his head, pondered for a moment, and said, ‘Alright… It seems that fate really is fickle, and you could even say it's unfair. Relatively speaking, you even felt that his voice was solemn, although still mixed with unpleasant static and strangely fluctuating sharp recorded sounds: ‘【Thank】【you】 for your 【effort】. Presenting you with 【super deals】. Perhaps you also need my 【help】.’

‘It’s not 【trouble】.’ It was only when you heard his reply that you realized you had unconsciously said that last sentence as well.
