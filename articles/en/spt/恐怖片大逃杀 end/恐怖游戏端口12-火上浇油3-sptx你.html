You muttered a couple of words: "If they kill and then eat them, I feel like something's off about them. Though it sounds strange that you're just eating at night...... "After all, no matter what, you're a normal person. There's no way you'd think of such bizarre things, let alone carry them out."

But after looking around the room, you did see there was a refrigerator inside, which looked like it had been out of power for a long time. You had no idea how things were inside, just in case. You still pinched your nose and opened the fridge. What you saw was brownish meat—long since spoiled, and it was still reeking of rot. Even pinching your nose couldn't stop the smell's erosion. And there were quite a few bugs crawling around above, all white and shiny, making you even more nauseous. Still, you mentally scanned the inside of the fridge to make sure there was nothing else to look like besides this piece of meat, then closed the fridge door.

From Spamton's perspective, the illusion had already disappeared. He did see you walk over and open the fridge door, but what he saw was still a pile of fresh human flesh, with some uncut bones inside.

You closed the fridge, let out a long breath, rolled up your sleeves, and became full of energy: "Alright, let's check out the other things in this room next! Maybe we'll find something else!" "If there's nothing, entering this room to investigate would actually be a bit of a loss...... It doesn't seem like a big loss, since it's safe here. Thinking this, you circle the room and check the layout. It's a two-bedroom one-living room, but there probably isn't much to see inside, and you can't open the door unless you forcibly unlock it.

"So, what did you see in the [room appearance]?" Spamton began to ask the question. From his perspective, the living room only had a simple table and refrigerator, plus a sofa—it looked like the kitchen function was combined with the living room, since the stove was on the other side of the wall. Other than that, the room was very clean. He also saw that the two bedroom doors were open, so he described them as well.

You frowned, hesitating a bit—after all, the difference was just too big...... But you cleared your throat and said, "Hmm...... What I saw was also the table, the fridge, and the dusty stove. The sofa was already torn, with cotton sticking out, probably nothing to look at...... "By the way, there was a shoe cabinet at the door, but it wasn't anything worth paying attention to. Both you and Spamton deliberately ignored it.

"According to the [First Floor's] [theory], maybe you can [try] [enter the bedroom]?" he said. After all, on the first floor, although you saw the main door fastened with heavy wooden planks, from your perspective you could easily push it open and enter the building. But in that hidden room, you couldn't see anything, yet still opened the door and entered the room at his prompt.

You rubbed your chin thoughtfully and nodded, "You're right, let's try again...... Maybe you can go straight in." At this point, you grabbed the Spamton off your shoulder and set it on the floor, then looked at him with anticipation.

"Then I'll leave it to you!" You made a palm gesture and bowed in goodbye, then squatted on the floor and looked at him. Spamton met your gaze and agreed to press him, but in the end, he said nothing more and just walked to the door—in his eyes, it was indeed the door wide open without any concealment, and from your perspective, Spamton walked straight through the door, so you couldn't help but let out a whimper. Then you sat on the already tattered sofa with cotton spilling out, waiting for Spamton to come out. After all, you didn't have much else to do right now.

But just as you sit down on the sofa, you collapse along the soft surface, and you notice something in the gap between the sofas, so you take it out to take a look. It seems to be a photo...... It looks like a photo of the siblings living in this household? The only strange thing is, why is it a photo by the seaside...... And it's not in a swimsuit. If you had to describe it, it might sound a bit odd, since in the photo you see, the older brother seems to have scales on his neck, and the younger sister has a fish tail growing directly from there. If it weren't for some pretend game, maybe...... Maybe it would be more accurate to say she's a mermaid.

So there is a story about mermaids here? You can't help but feel emotional, but even so, you don't see any difference. At most, you confirm that your sister's death was caused by someone else. But you still don't know the cause of her death or why her brother wanted to eat her corpse.

Just as you were pondering the photo, you saw Spamton walk out of the room, then turn into the next room, and soon come out again.

So you put down that photo and decided to hear what Spamton would actually gain.

He also sat down on the sofa, though probably not the same as yours. From your perspective, he happened to be sitting in that completely ruined area, with the cotton fully exposed. You quietly looked away, intending to focus intently on listening.

He quickly spoke up: "I found a [notebook] in a [bedroom]." As he spoke, he handed over a notebook. From his perspective, it probably already looked very old, but from your perspective, it was worn and damaged but still brand new.

"I hope [you] can do [reading collection]." After all, after opening Spamton, he found that all he could see was vague words, unable to see anything clearly.

Curious, you opened the notebook and casually asked, "Let me see...... By the way, how did you get this notebook?" "After all, if you weren't carrying or holding him, you felt he wouldn't be able to reach most places at all."

Spamton hesitated for a moment. Actually, he didn't really want to explain this question, though there was nothing embarrassing about it...... But if he said it, it might just sound strangely strange. But as if compelled by some fate to speak up: "The [notebook] is on the [table], the [chair] is next to the [table]. I'll [climb] [the chair] first, then [climb] the [table]." Although it wasn't a complicated question, after explaining, he still didn't want to speak for a while.

And you nodded thoughtfully...... To be honest, you hadn't even considered this possibility earlier. It seems your thinking was too narrow, so you patted the little puppet on the shoulder and praised him, "That's amazing! You did a great job, you're just too smart!" "Although you yourself feel that remarks sound a bit like coaxing a child......

Spamton, on the other hand, had no intention of answering your question. He just felt embarrassed and annoyed, so he simply lowered his head directly. You scratched your chin, belatedly feeling a bit embarrassed by that sentence, and simply stopped talking, just turned to look at the notebook seriously.

Calling it a notebook would be better if it were a diary. It looked like it was just that brother's daily diary, and the handwriting seemed exactly the same as in the hidden room on the first floor, which meant ...... It was probably that brother who set fire to the entire building.

You start reading the contents of the diary.

My sister and I moved into a new apartment...... The environment here was nice, and the neighbors were all very friendly. Except that the high floor was a bit inconvenient, but fortunately, our floor was relatively low. My sister was very happy and even took a photo with me at the entrance of the building. Speaking of which, this was the first time in so many years that we took a group photo, so we had to keep it safe. ]

[Below is a photo of the siblings, both looking human, smiling brightly]

Forget it, as long as I kept a low profile and was ordinary, someone else would forget about it someday. ]

[October 15

My sister was a bit hesitant when she came home today...... When I pressed her, she wouldn't tell me why, and she seemed to be in a bad mood. So I decided not to press further, and to cheer her up, I bought her a small cake. She looked very happy—that's good. ]

[November 2

So that's how it is...... I realized why my sister was so stammering that day, and I realized that besides being unhappy, there was a hint of guilt in her expression. My sister didn't come home today, and I began to reflect on myself. Even so...... Maybe it was my fault as her older brother. ]

[November 3

She didn't go home. ]

She didn't go home. Was she missing? Or did she simply forget to come back? I shouldn't be overthinking it......]

For a whole month afterward, the diary entries were all simple. She didn't mention going home, which actually gives you chills. Skipping those words, you look toward the record near the end of your notebook.

[December 2

…… I know what those people are doing behind my back...... I understand...... My sister did nothing wrong, yet you killed her just for immortality...... The whole building is secretly devouring her body, even plotting to kill me too. Luckily, they didn't succeed......

How wonderful it would be if she just disappeared. ]

After a few pages, there are no more notes left. You casually turn the pages back, and on the last page you see several large blood-written characters: They all deserve to die.

You swallowed hard, closed the notebook, then looked at Spamton, who had been waiting for you to explain, and cleared your throat: "The elder brother and sister of this resident are from the merfolk clan...... It seems they don't know much more information yet, only that eating mermaid flesh grants immortality. So the others in this building killed that sister and shared her corpse. As for the human flesh in the fridge, it was probably snatched back by that brother...... "At this point, you find yourself at a loss for words. Even though you've only summarized it, you feel a bit conflicted."

"It's called human flesh, or rather, the meat inside the fridge is mermaid meat. But after all these years, it probably no longer works." You exhaled, stood up, and placed your notebook and photos on the table. You glanced at the screen showing that 25% of the side quests had been completed, which meant your overall prediction was correct.

Although you usually don't bother to think about problems, since only you can see these clues now, you explain: "Uh, by the way, the handwriting on this is the same as on the first floor, so in the end, that guy probably chose to set fire." After saying that, you saw the task progress on the screen drop to 30%. You even felt a bit surprised. Could it be that the quest to uncover the truth is actually quite simple? Anyway, for now, as long as you piece together these clues, you'll know the answer.

You casually picked up Spamton and put it back onto your shoulder, exhaling: "Alright, let's go...... There's probably nothing much to see in this room. ”

"[Careful], there might be [many mysteries] ahead." Spamton still reminded you. It wasn't that he was overthinking it; it was just that while he was exploring the bedroom earlier, he had noticed a bit of something strange. After all, from his perspective, this should be a brand-new building, at least as it looked before it was abandoned, but he saw traces of charred beard growing up the wall...... Or rather, it was the dark brown marks slowly crawling, as if it had come to life.

You casually waved your hand: "Don't worry. If you really need to decrypt it, I'll just leave it all to you to handle." "Anyway, aside from finding clues and then piecing them together, you don't have much other skills.

Before Spamton could recount his earlier discovery, you had already opened the door and walked out, and then your expression turned serious. Because once you stepped out, you noticed traces of flames spreading in the hallway, and charred corpses wandering about.

The skin was completely dark brown, with the only remaining white, tender skin peeling off, dripping down brown and yellow grease. In the exposed area, the fresh red flesh was even pulsating and beating. In some places, along with the peeling skin, the flesh flowed down as well, probably because the tissue had been completely destroyed. In that hollow, it was pitch-dark, and you could see the already decayed organs, though for some reason, they were still pulsating. As the charred corpse slowly moved forward, fresh blood flowed across the ground, but it was dark red, seemingly long dried up, yet for some reason, it still seemed alive. The smell of rot, burnt flesh, and blood all entered your nostrils. Your hands were empty, yet the charred corpse seemed to have found its target and started moving quickly towards you.

You patted Spamton: 'Hold on tight!' Then you began to run quickly, and for some reason, the fire downstairs was also spreading upward at that moment.
