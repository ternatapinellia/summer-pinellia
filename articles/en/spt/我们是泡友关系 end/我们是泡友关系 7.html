Spamtonx, you, OOC belongs to me

Let's go through the plot, so this chapter might have a bit of stomach pain, with three stomachaches and two stomachaches (what does that mean?).

——

…… Uh, fine, then you really are getting revenge, but if you don't care, you'll just be making a fuss. After all, he's already indulged it. So you just glared at him fiercely twice: "Wow, then I don't care. Just endure it a little, okay?" Anyway, this life passes quickly, a lifetime is only this long. You just feel utterly humiliated at this moment, but who knows what you'll be like the next second......

In fact, you were just talking nonsense, but Spamton probably couldn't stand it anymore. He just smiled and bent down to bite your mouth, using a bit more force this time. In short, after being released, you were panting again and glaring fiercely at him.

“…… What are you doing? "You whispered.

"Nothing, I just hope you can be quiet for a while." Spamton was still smiling.

So you just rolled your eyes, finally quieting down, even though you still grunted in annoyance inside.

Anyway, I don't know how much time passed after that. You lost consciousness and only now slowly woke up. When you woke up, you saw Spamton lying beside you, still staring at you. You just sat up as usual, struggling to put on your clothes, then waved at Spamton: "Alright, see you tomorrow." ”

"See you tomorrow." You heard Spamton say. As you walked out the door, you looked back at him one last time. You found him staring at you, probably watching your back. Although you were a bit confused, you still walked out as usual and returned to the world of light. You checked that everything on you was fine before heading toward home in the sunset.

In the mailbox at your doorstep, you find an email inserted right there. You open it and find that a company has responded to your resume and asked you to come for an interview next week.

Although the company wasn't big, the moment you received the email, you were still overjoyed. So you took the reply letter and walked home, opened the door, raised the letter high with your left hand, and announced to your parents that your resume had passed and that you were going to attend the interview next week.

At least for now, you still want to share some happiness with your parents.

But your father studied the letter for a while, then slammed it to the ground: "...... So, I think you should go on a blind date this weekend. Coincidentally, your mother and I have picked a young man together, and he should be pretty good. ”

You blinked, your mouth agape, ignoring the letter lying on the floor, and just anxiously asked, "Why?" ”

My mother shook her head: "No reason. We just think it's time for you to get married. At this age, getting married is just right. If you wait until after 25, no one will care about you then." ”

My father added one more thing: "And if you don't get married now, when the interview comes, when they find out you're single, they might not hire you. As for the reason, I don't think you need to know." ”

Taking a deep breath and taking two steps back, you instinctively suppress your fear of the word 'marriage' and try again to argue your case: "But I think I have the ability—even if some unfavorable conditions are met, the other party can still hire me, and even if I don't get married, it doesn't matter. I don't really need a partner—"

This time your mother interrupted you, just giving you a half-smile: "Ah...... Really? I think you always need someone by your side. You need someone else to love you. We're your parents—how could we not understand our own child? We know you need these things more than you need, and even without emotional needs, you'll have physical needs. What will you do then? ”

Taking a deep breath, you took two steps back. This time, you were almost leaning against the door, your body trembling, but you didn't have the courage to rush out.

“…… I can also avoid physical needs. "You said cautiously.

"So even if you don't have it, you definitely need feelings. You need someone to love you, right? You need someone who can love you all the time, and only love you. I think a partner is the best choice for you. I only propose it out of love and care." ”

You have to admit, your parents really analyzed you thoroughly. Maybe that's why they can control you, use the love you want to meet their needs, and make you the ideal person they want. But now you keep saying that it's all because of love, and you just find it disgusting.

So you just gasped, tears welling up at some point, and you just looked at them helplessly.

My father waited until my mother finished speaking before speaking with authority: "Besides, I hope you won't work out for the next few years. After you've finished your marriage and childbirth, you'll consider going out to work." I think the work environment might not be very friendly to you, especially since your education isn't very high. Marriage is probably the most suitable option. I'm thinking of you all and hope you live a better life, which is why I care so much. ”

You just shook your head and looked at your father in terror: "No...... I don't need your concern...... No matter how harsh the work environment is, I can find a way to overcome it, so please don't say that anymore...... "By the end, your voice had unknowingly choked with tears. You almost pleaded, almost kneeling, but you felt it wouldn't work. They would just look at you with disappointment as always, saying they hadn't met their demands, and they were disappointed.

Sure enough, they just sighed in disappointment: "Sigh, you've already said things this far, and you still don't understand. Let's be disappointed...... We're all doing this for your own good. ”

You didn't speak, tears kept falling down, and you just looked at them in despair.

At that moment, your father seemed to be carefully sizing you up, then his gaze froze. He noticed the ring on your left ring finger, and his voice suddenly became stern, even reaching your ears with a hint of shock: "...... Why are you wearing a ring? Have you pledged yourself to someone else?" ”

You blinked blankly, belatedly recalling the ring you wore, so you hurriedly said, "No, I just thought this ring looked nice, but I didn't know where to wear it, so I imitated you and put it on my ring finger......"

My father was silent for a moment, then spoke with authority: "Then, now, throw away that ring." ”

You widened your eyes and quickly shook your head: "No, I absolutely won't throw it away!" ”

My mother's voice grew sharp, almost turning into a scream: "So, could this ring be a gift from someone else?" Tell Mom, tell us, we want to know how you're doing. It's all our fault, and we've neglected our love and care for you lately......

…… Is it that kind of heavy love and care, that suffocating, mission-like inquiry? You'd rather never have had such an experience.

So you just shake your head again and argue, "I bought this ring myself because I think it's beautiful and expensive. In any case, I won't throw it away." ”

Even though you know all of this is just lying, you know very well that what you say is false.

"But in that case, we can also give you the money, and you can buy another one later. There are plenty of beautiful decorations—earrings, necklaces, bracelets, anything is fine, but it can't be rings. ”

“…… Why do you have to care so much?" "You've lost your smile long ago, and now you seem to be screaming hysterically. In the end, you feel you've had enough of all this. Somehow, the courage that had just disappeared is slowly returning. Your legs, which had been sore and weak and had to lean against the door to stand upright, now regain strength, at least enough to support you as you rush out and run a long distance."

"Why should we? Because we are your parents, because you still live here. If we don't care about you, who will? It's precisely because we love you that we want to restrain you. Everything we say is for your own good. Trust Mom and Dad...... "They were still talking about something, but you finally couldn't take it anymore. Covering your ears, you pushed open the door and ran out desperately.

You seemed to hear the voices behind you abruptly stop, followed by your father's angry voice shouting, "You have to go home, you can't never go back," and your mother's voice trying to persuade you.

…… If you're never going back, then so be it. After running a certain distance, you realize you're out of breath, your lungs twitching like broken bellows, burning pain, and then the soreness in your calves keeps attacking you, telling you to stop. But you don't dare, you don't dare, as if if if the devil will come to seize you if you stop. Even though you're starving and your strength is almost gone, you only dare to keep running forward.

So you just ran to the entrance of the dark world holding your breath, then took a deep breath and jumped inside. If you never go back, then never go back. Maybe you're just sulking, or maybe you're truly disappointed because of it. In any case, you are now desperate to go to another world. You still want to see him.

…… No need to say anything, just quietly hold onto you. All you want is to quietly hold onto something that truly belongs to you.

A halo of light appeared before you, followed by dizziness. You descended into this world once again. This time, you sobbed and revealed your identity. At least the tears had mostly stopped, leaving only ugly tear stains on your face. You roughly wiped away your tears with your hands, took out your phone, and dialed Spamton's contact number, telling him to come to the entrance right now to pick you up.

He didn't ask why, just appeared in a familiar spot after a while. The sobbing that had just stopped was about to revive, but you just stepped forward and suddenly hugged him, holding him tightly and refusing to let go. You just held that position, silently streaming tears. You could feel the suit your head resting on was already soaked with tears.

You didn't say anything, just quietly crying. Spamton fell silent too, and the questions he had prepared were no longer planned to ask. On the way here, he actually had many thoughts and guesses, but after arriving here and seeing you, all those thoughts vanished. So, at this very moment, he just kept holding on to the embrace.

For a moment, it felt as if only you two remained in this quiet space. Of course, you knew that wasn't the case, but you had no other strength now. You just held Spamton and cried helplessly, wanting to release all the emotions from before. You felt his hand gently patting your back, as if comforting you, then rubbing your head, probably telling you not to be sad anymore. But that only made you want to cry out loud even more.

But after venting your emotions, you still let go of Spamton, your voice muffled and hoarse, nasal: "Sorry, it seems like I ruined your clothes......"

Spamton just shook his head: "It doesn't matter. Do you need to [enjoy] dinner time? You've already missed lunch time." ”

You wiped your face haphazardly and nodded: "Mm, then let's go have dinner, but I'm really not in the mood...... Just a little food should be enough to fill you up. ”

"No problem. Then, enjoy some desserts. Maybe it will be good for you."

You nodded aimlessly again. In fact, no matter what Spamton said now, you would just go along. After all, you really had neither the mood nor the energy to think about complicated matters.

He leads you to a tea restaurant. Spamton calls the waiter to bring a pot of black tea and several plates of dessert. You blink at the stacks of strawberry mille-feuille and honey muffins, as well as blueberry chocolate Napoleon cake, slowly opening your mouth wide.

Although you wanted to ask, "That would definitely make you suck," you didn't say anything, just started eating wildly with your tea. Indeed, sweets can lift your mood, at least useful for your current self, completely calming your mood.

Although Spamton ate very little, he still ate a little. Of course, most of the time he just looked at you with his face in his hands, since the most important thing now was to calm your emotions.

As for why you acted like this, he actually had some guesses. It was just about things at home, so Spamton was waiting for you to speak first. He was also a bit nervous because he sensed something, so he had no choice but to say something to you.

But at least not now, he can't deal you any more blows.

Of course, you really don't have the mood to say all this today, so you'll have to wait until tomorrow. After all, you really want to share these things. After finishing the dessert, your mood improved a lot, and you ate your fill. You glanced at Spamton and stammered, "Uh, anyway...... I'm not planning to go back today, but I don't have any other place to stay in the Dark World, so could I stay at your place for now? Hmm, but tomorrow I'll find a way to stay first......"

"Of course, you can. If you can't find another address, you can also stay long-term and become a permanent resident." After hearing this, Spamton spoke—or rather, he was almost impatient—but you ended up giving him a few puzzled glances.

"Alright, anyway...... Then it's settled. But I don't want to stay at your place forever. What's the big deal? Anyway, I still have some dark coins, so I should be able to find a place to live. "At the end, you were still a bit uncertain.

But you still stayed at Spamton's place for a night. The next day, you did rent a place as you said, and Spamton accompanied you to see the place. I don't know if it was because of him, but the landlord offered you a pretty low price—at least enough to cover the first three months' rent.

Having resolved this matter, you felt much more at ease and felt a bit better, though thinking about your parents' issues still made you feel a bit down. In the evening, you ordered a bottle of wine as usual, waiting for the barbecue on the stove. You propped your head up and looked at Spamton, speaking with a somewhat worried expression.

"Sigh...... Yesterday, I clearly received the company notice that I was going to an interview, but after I got home, my parents suddenly demanded that I go on a blind date, saying it was necessary and that I had to get married first......

Spamton quietly watched you. Seeing that you hadn't spoken for a moment and had fallen into silence, he hesitated for a moment before finally saying, "Alright...... I think, for your own good, some things have to be "ended." ”

You suddenly lift your head and stare at him intently: "...... What do you mean by 'for my own good'? What do you want to end with? ”

"As for the so-called 'buddy with benefits' relationship, I think it's because the 'time' should have ended, but we're still in a 'friend added' relationship."

You narrowed your eyes slightly, propped yourself up on the table, and stood up to look at him: "...... Why? Was it me that I was dissatisfied with you? Or was there a condition I hadn't met? Is there something you think I did wrong? Why should you say it's for my own good? Your parents are like this too...... You do the same...... As you spoke, you couldn't help but sob, your voice tinged with tears, growing quieter and quieter.

“…… Don't you love me anymore? "That last sentence was almost a mutter, at least you can be sure he didn't hear it, and you just stared at him intently.
