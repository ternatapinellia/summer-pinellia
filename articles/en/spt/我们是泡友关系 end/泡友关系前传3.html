Casual Friends Relationship: Prequel 3

But he just kept smiling, sitting there and nodding at you: "[Sure]." ”

…… You don't even know why you want to ask for his opinion. Thinking about it, it seems you don't really need to do that. Well, maybe it's because he's the owner of this room?

Anyway, you should quickly get to the bathroom and change into the dried skirt again.

After changing into your own clothes, you finally breathe a sigh of relief and completely relax. You walk out the door and look at him: "Alright, let's go out." ”

Spamton didn't dare look at you anymore. He just stood up with his back to you and walked toward the door, not even saying a word. Although you thought he was acting a bit strange, I'm afraid other beings might have some quirks or something, and you wouldn't disturb them. After all...... Parents always tell you to respect others' decisions, respect their thoughts, and not trouble others. If you must trouble others, you should thank them afterward...... You are still following these words now, since you don't think there's anything wrong with it.

After all, those aren't the people you care about, so what decisions and actions you take are none of your concern. If there really is someone you care about...... Then think about it: at that moment, you absolutely won't respect their thoughts and choices. If they go against your expectations and wishes, if their so-called happiness turns out to be a great misfortune for you, then perhaps you absolutely won't accept it.

But these things are just thoughts; you don't really believe such a person exists, since you seem to care about no one and have no contact with anyone.

But you watch Spamton's back ahead and hesitate for a brief second. Just now, you, who had no contact with anyone, now reconsider this so-called friend. So, maybe you still have contact with others? At least have some connection with him?

So, how exactly is the concept of a friend defined...... But you do long to have a friend, since other relationships are fleeting to you, but only friends are probably permanent.

Your thoughts were a bit chaotic. You didn't say anything along the way, just went to a phone shop. Spamton picked out a phone for you, paid for it, then opened the phone and saved your contact information. He even set it to the pin and gave an asterisk. He also helped you get a SIM card, then stored your number on his phone and quietly pinned it at the top, just giving you a heart when making a note.

Of course, for a long time, your phone actually only stored this one number.

After entering the number, Spamton handed you the phone and gave you a flawless smile: "[Done]." From now on, [you] can [use] this [phone] to your heart's content. ”

You took the phone with both hands, glanced at it, and nodded solemnly to Spamton: "Thank you for your generous help." You said this quite formally, and Spamton couldn't help but laugh. You felt that although the smile wasn't as perfect as before, it carried a touch of authenticity.

"No need to be restrained; this is what I should do for my friends." He simply repeated this point persistently.

You tilted your head to look at Spamton, then at the time, and realized it was almost dinner time. You exclaimed, "Ah, it's already this late...... I have to go first. I don't even know if I can go back to where I came before......" You muttered, giving Spamton an apologetic look.

"Sorry, I should have thought of a way to make it up to you first, but I'm afraid I have to leave first......

He still shook his head: "No need to apologize." ”

In fact, Spamton felt you apologized far too many times...... But he also knew that since your personality was like this, he wouldn't feel uncomfortable, so he just kept correcting you. After all, why should you apologize for something like this? If you need to apologize, that's probably his own thing; it's just that Spamton feels there's absolutely no need to do that.

…… Of course, in some ways, he also hopes you can rely on and trust him a little more. Although Spamton also finds your crying funny and wants to kiss and chew you in that moment, at this moment, he actually hopes you can genuinely smile as well.

And in the next moment, he sees your genuine smile.

You pause and hesitate for a moment. In fact, you think you should apologize if you cause trouble for others, and your parents' reminders are the same...... But maybe friends don't need to think so much about it? You guessed it, but you decided to apologize a few times in the future.

At least, you don't need to be so nervous about friends, right? You guessed it, so at this moment you smiled, probably from the heart, because now you feel your expression is no longer stiff.

You nod firmly: "Alright." ”

Then, you bring Spamton to the place where you appeared in this dark world. Fortunately, the door can now open again, so you wave goodbye to Spamton and return to your original world.

When you returned, your excitement fell to the ground again, turning into anxiety, fear, and sadness. Even though you were very reluctant, you still moved your feet toward home.

Spamton watched your figure disappear through that door, unable to describe it, feeling a sense of loss. In the end, he turned around and went back to his own business, but he couldn't help but wonder when you'd contact him again.

You eventually returned home. Unsurprisingly, it wasn't a good place to return to you, but you still needed to go home, and perhaps you still needed the poison called love from your parents. At least, you really needed these.

Even at this moment, they are merely speaking the truths you must accept in the name of love, and then, when you achieve their goal, give you another reward called love.

But at today's dinner table, it seems a small argument broke out again, probably just your own assumption, because apart from that, there is only silence and repression, only their dark eyes staring at you. After that angry shout, you feel fear again, finally sit back down, say nothing, and end the dinner.

By the next day, you left home early. As for where to go, you hadn't decided yet. Usually, you would just hang out in a nearby park, daydreaming and ending the day. But now, you had a new place to go—you thought of the Underworld, you wanted to chat with the friends you had met yesterday.

Or maybe you just desperately need an outlet, even if you say nothing, just quietly accompany you for a while.

So you crossed that door, and after the familiar dizziness, arrived at the Dark World. Looking at the familiar yet unfamiliar streets, you hesitated for a moment and took out the phone Spamton had bought you yesterday—this phone wouldn't light up again in the world of light. Now it's fine, probably because the worlds are different. At that moment, you even feel a bit lucky to have arrived in another world, grateful to have made your first friend.

So you dial Spamton's phone number and tell him to come to the place where you left yesterday to pick you up.

Spamton was actually a bit surprised. Although he had felt a bit disappointed yesterday, he quickly composed himself. Since he was quite patient, he didn't mind waiting a bit longer. He just didn't expect that you would contact him the very next day.

When you arrived, seeing your expression on the verge of tears, Spamton belatedly realized something. Ah, probably for you, his friend is now the only one you can rely on.

So, it's okay. You can rely on him a bit more. He won't mind accepting your emotions, since all of this has to be devoured to be considered delicious. But right now, he can't act so eager.

But seeing your expression, Spamton couldn't help but suggest, "Maybe ...... [you] need a [place to relax]?" ”

He still doesn't want to see that expression linger on your face for too long.

You paused, instinctively wanting to ask Spamton how he figured it out, but belatedly realized, actually, your expression was written all over your face, but your family probably didn't care at all. After all, they didn't care about your emotions at all; they just thought you were still young, didn't have other emotions, and didn't have much recognition ability, so you could only follow the path they arranged. The choice of major for university was the result of your first rebellious choice...... But they didn't seem to care much about what kind of major you actually had.

So you just took a deep breath, tried hard to turn your brain, but your mind was empty. At this moment, you couldn't think of anything, so you just looked at Spamton as if seeking help: "Ah...... I-I don't know where to relax. Do you have any recommendations?" "And in fact, in such a sad moment, you just want to sit there and sit blankly for a while, not wanting to do anything, because you don't have the energy to do anything."

Spamton observed your confused expression and suggested, "Maybe, an amusement park?" Given your current situation, this should be a good choice. I think maybe you need a meal, then rest well, and finally go to relax. ”

You look at him blankly, then instinctively grab his arm and nod: "Hmm, probably okay. I really do need to rest for a while...... Just do whatever you say. I don't want to think about these things anymore." "You just want to clear your mind."

Spamton glanced at your hand holding his arm, subtly raised it, then held yours and continued smiling: "Well then, today I'll be in charge. I'll make sure you're in a good mood." ”

You feel his hand seems a bit cold, but the moment one hand touches it, you feel a bit of warmth and reassurance, so you can't help but nod.

Spamton took you to the amusement park. He bought two tickets and brought you in. There weren't many people at the amusement park today, but then he took your hand, bought two marshmallows, and slipped one into your hand, pulling you to sit down on a nearby chair.

"Get some good rest first. Maybe you should [enjoy] some sweets." He said, looking at you.

You paused, biting into the marshmallow a bit without appetizing the food. Honestly, you'd never been to an amusement park before, only heard of this place, so the facilities were unfamiliar and uneasy, including the candy-like yet fluffy item in your hand. Actually, you hadn't tried it before.

Then that sweet, cloying taste bursts in your mouth. The tears you've been holding back start to drip from your eyes. You freeze for a moment, then quickly let go of the hand you've been holding and wipe your tears: "...... Sorry." ”

You clearly promised to apologize less, but at this moment, you can't help but say sorry. After all, on a phone you don't use often, typing the right subconscious association is just saying sorry.

"No need to [apologize]. If you want to [cry], [you] certainly have the right to [cry]." You hear Spamton's voice, and at the same time, you feel his hand gently wiping away your tears.

Blinking, you just stammered, "But my parents said you can't cry, that crying looks too ugly...... And seeing it makes others unhappy, so I should be smiling." "Even so, you really can't laugh right now, and in fact, you want to cry even more because of it."

“…… Besides, after I got home yesterday, my mother said the pieces I made were all insignificant, completely meaningless. Rather than continuing to try composing, I should try something else...... She said I had no talent at all...... As you spoke, you couldn't help but lower your head and move the cotton candy a bit farther away. You didn't want your tears to soak the candy.

Only after saying it do you realize you might never have said it at all. Why do you have to reveal these things about yourself? So you just wipe your tears and hurriedly say, "Sorry, I shouldn't have said this......

"No need to apologize. Friends can [listen] everything, so you can [tell me] all of this." Spamton said. At this moment, he didn't have many extra thoughts, just thought that if you were willing to reveal everything, it meant he was getting closer to you, and maybe after saying all this, you could show a genuine smile once again.

You paused for a moment, said nothing, just stared blankly and took a bite of the cotton candy.

Seeing your still sorrowful and anxious expression, he suggested, "Then, maybe we should go visit the [Drop Tower Machine]?" ”

“…… Why? "You haven't heard of this project, but just hearing the name gives you an idea of what it looks like.

"Because if you are at the boundary between excitement and fear in a moment, you might [abandon] all sadness and disappointment, leaving only that moment of experience. I think that might help you temporarily forget your sadness." ”
