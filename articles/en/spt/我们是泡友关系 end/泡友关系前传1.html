…… To be honest, you must have wandered into a different world! To be honest, you were just curious...... After all, you discovered that in a remote alley that happened to pass through, there was actually a door, and it could be opened, even though it was pitch black. But out of curiosity to find out...... Or maybe you were just in a bad mood, and since you were already here, you decided to go take a look.

Whatever it is, you just left a suffocating home, but you know you'll return sooner or later. So even if you escape, your escape isn't complete, because besides that, you have nowhere to go, and no one needs you. But no matter what, even if you suddenly fall into an abyss, even if you might die suddenly, it's still fine.

But none of those things existed—just the howling wind and a sudden wave of dizziness, a flash of intense light that made you close your eyes involuntarily. When you snapped back to reality and opened your eyes again, you found yourself standing on another street.

You stared blankly, instinctively trying to walk forward. It was a deep and dark blue, with almost no lights around—just gloom. Until you felt the first drop of rain on your face, followed by even more raindrops, pouring down almost to soak your whole body. Only then did you panic and realize, you instinctively turned back to try to pull the door, but for some reason, it wouldn't open now.

…… Filled with anxiety and unease, you covered your head with your hands and ran toward the nearest eaves.

Then you felt a bit troubled, just staring blankly at the rain before you. The pouring rain grew heavier and heavier, forming a continuous curtain of rain. Everything before your eyes became hazy and hazy. Here, you could see some neon lights, probably even the twinkling signs hanging above buildings, but you couldn't clearly see what words were written on them. Through the chaotic rain, the neon lights became blurry and distorted. You could vaguely see figures on the street, but they were different from anything you'd seen before. If you had to describe them, it would be more like...... Purely living creatures or something? That's quite novel.

But you have no umbrella, and with such heavy rain, you have nowhere to go. This is just a strange world, so you can only endure the moisture in the air and the gradually dropping temperature, waiting alone for the rain to stop. You clasp your arms tightly, lost and helpless, the only glimmer in your eyes extinguished, just quietly staying there.

Spamton was the one who saw you then. To be honest, he just walked out of the shop as usual, looked at the heavy rain outside, and was about to pull a pull from the umbrella rack nearby, but then his movement froze.

Unlike before, the street across the street was not empty or gray; instead, there was a figure here. Perhaps it was a passing car flashing its flash, illuminating your eyes, while you only closed your eyes and shrank back in discomfort, with droplets still clinging to your cheeks—it was unclear whether it was rain or tears.

He instinctively swallowed hard, and for some reason, he felt as if a fire was burning in his chest. Then, even though he was just standing in such a cold environment, he still felt a burning heat.

He didn't know exactly what he was thinking at that moment. Spamton only knew that at that moment, he had an urge to lick the droplets of water off his cheek, then bite your lip, and then ......

He didn't think any further. He paused and resumed his movements, but his hand changed direction. Originally, he wanted to use a larger umbrella because he didn't want the rain to soak his suit. But now, he picked up a small umbrella nearby, which could barely cover a person. After all, it was a sunshade umbrella and wasn't very spacious by nature.

He held up the umbrella, walked through the rain, and walked toward you, curling his lips into a familiar smile that was most comfortable and reassuring: "Miss, I see you staying here because of the troubles caused by the heavy rain? How about I [hold the umbrella] and send you off? Sharing an umbrella is always better than a damp ending." ”

You had been daydreaming at first, but when you heard that, you finally snapped back to reality. Honestly, although there seemed to be a bit of a pause and something odd in the middle, the voice actually sounded pretty pleasant. And it sounded quite polite...... In short, your previously listless mood improved a bit.

Turning your head, you see a ...... At least someone who really does look like a person is standing there holding an umbrella, reaching out a hand toward you, probably signaling you to put it on it.

You hesitated for a moment, a bit uneasy: "Wouldn't that be too much trouble for you? I'll just wait here for the rain to stop, no need for you to see me off......"

…… And it's just a chance encounter—why help you? You can't help him at all, you have nothing to express gratitude with, and if he feels guilty if he feels indebted, it's better never to have ever done it at all.

So you just shake your head and put on a distant smile—well, maybe it's pretty stiff, since you're completely unused to smiling. Even though your parents often say you have to smile to make everyone happy and happy yourself, you've only learned to smile mechanically.

This is the first time you've tried to make a different expression. From the stiff corners of your mouth, you can sense that you have probably failed.

Spamton naturally noticed your somewhat stiff expression, but he said nothing, just continued reaching out his hand on his own: "It's not [troublesome], I'm very happy to [serve you]." ”

…… Even if you say that, what do you think in your heart? Maybe it's a different approach? Thinking this, you stared blankly at the hand he reached out, then at his still smiling face, tilted your head, and asked, "Uh...... Sir, we have never met. Why do you want to help me? Maybe there won't be any reward at all. ”

"I don't [need] [payment], nor do I have many [reasons]. I just [hope] to provide [help] to [you]." He was still very patient. In fact, he had thought you might be very cautious, and had thought you might reject him once or twice, but definitely not a third time. Spamton was still confident in this.

You take a deep breath and look seriously into his eyes this time, but you can't see them. He has his eyes closed, just squinting and smiling, so you can't tell what emotions he's really feeling. Or maybe you can't understand other people's emotions either.

“…… Then why me? "You murmured, to be honest, you don't know what answer would satisfy you, or maybe you just want to hear an answer. Whatever it is, it doesn't matter.

You hear him chuckle: "It's just because it's [you]." ”

You stopped speaking, but for some reason, tears slid down your eyes. You didn't intend to care anymore. Maybe this sudden downpour was just like your mood—sad and endless, you didn't know where to go, and you had nowhere to go.

…… Maybe you just want to be chosen once, or maybe you just want to know you still have a reason to stand here.

So in the end, you still held his hand and softly said, "Thank you for your trouble. ”

Watching the tears dripping down your cheeks, Spamton's throat swallowed again, and he could clearly hear the sound of swallowing. To be honest, he really wanted to kiss the tears on your cheeks, then kiss your lips, and then swallow everything—whether sweet or bitter, he would accept it with a smile. But he also knew he absolutely couldn't do that now, or else you'd definitely leave far away.

After hearing this, Spamton's smile deepened a little, becoming even more genuine. He always knew that it couldn't be more than three times, and you would still refuse this transaction...... No, this wasn't even a transaction, just a gift. As for the payment for the transaction, it was naturally repaid after the debt was owed.

"In that case, 'ladies first', I'll make sure [you] try not to be attacked by [Rain]." As he said this, he stretched the umbrella over your head, leaving most of his body exposed.

…… The suit can be changed later; what's more important now is the situation. He doesn't want to lose you in a flash and never find you again. It's better to seize the opportunity and make sure you remember him forever.

You looked blankly at the umbrella hanging over your head. To be honest, it was very small—even if just one person held it in such heavy rain, it would be a struggle, so you couldn't help but feel a bit guilty. At that moment, you saw that most of his body was exposed, and quickly pushed the umbrella toward him: "How can this be? If you keep getting wet, you'll get sick...... Besides, you helped out for free, so I shouldn't have let you get wet......

You're really embarrassed. If you hadn't already walked a distance, you'd want to run back under the eaves and just hold the umbrella for yourself.

“…… Alright, then if you want to make me feel comfortable, I'm too embarrassed to see you get caught in the rain......" you whispered, then you saw only innocence in his eyes, and he didn't respond to your words. In any case, you hesitated for a moment, but still gritted your teeth and closed the distance between you.

You don't really like interacting with the opposite sex; it's just an instinctive aversion, but it's not impossible. So now, carrying that restless feeling, you keep the distance between you, and you feel your shoulders bumping into each other as you walk. To be honest, you're not used to this.

And although it had improved a bit, you could still see half of his body exposed. The rain was just a bit lighter than before, so it didn't look so scary.

“…… Why don't you just hold an umbrella by yourself? Just drop me off somewhere I can stand in the rain. I'm really sorry." You muttered as you spoke.

"[No need], I'll take you to an excellent [home]. And it's not [far]. It's just around the corner." And that's all Spamton said.

You hesitated for a moment. There really wasn't much place nearby to take shelter from the rain. This area was all brick walls, not a single eave, and you weren't familiar with the roads of the other world, so you didn't know if he was leading you to some remote road. But there were still cars passing by on the street, and you could see pedestrians passing by, so it was probably just a less busy street.

So in the end, you didn't continue. You just turned to look at him again, hesitated for a moment, and honestly, you thought of an idea, but you didn't know if you could do it. In the end, after hesitating for a moment, you still crawled in front of him, though it was a bit farther than when you were in your arms and close to your chest. You felt you couldn't do that, probably just a hint of intuition...... You didn't know what harm it would bring.

But now, maintaining that posture, your body feels a bit stiff, so you quicken your pace a bit, since you don't want to bump into his footsteps. The umbrella moves along your head, so now it covers both of you. You feel his hand lightly resting on your shoulder, probably not wanting to slip up all at once.

Maybe it was the inexplicable awkwardness, but in any case, both of you fell silent. No one spoke anymore, and you just quickened your pace and moved forward.

Spamton, on the other hand, only looked at the occasional exposure of the fair skin on your neck from your hurried steps, at the wet and transparent white skirt that had been stained by raindrops from earlier haste, his thoughts a bit confused. In the end, he chose to look away a little, otherwise all the heat would probably gather together.

I don't know how much time passed, maybe just a moment, but it was really close, because you were standing under the eaves, with a residence in front of you. Then you watched as Spamton took out his keys, opened the door in front of him, and gestured invitingly toward you.

…… So the perfect place he said was his home? But you really have nowhere else to go here, so you hesitantly walked in, standing at the doorway, then looked at Spamton, who was closing his umbrella: "Hmm...... May I ask your name? I really feel bad for getting wet because you got wet. Maybe someday I'll make it up to you......" your hand nervously clasped together.
