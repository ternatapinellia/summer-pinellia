Otherworld Fantasy Drifting [Part 1] (sptx you)

Uh, there's a P5 fusion, if you don't understand, private Marseille ......

——

You're so enthusiastic for no other reason: you actually know the recently popular leader of the Phantom Thieves Gang—and you naturally know about other worlds and changing your mind. Today, you accidentally discovered that your phone also had a navigation app for another world. You even asked around, and found out they use the same app...... Although you're just an international student studying in the United States.

And last night, in my dream, I even found myself in a room that was all blue, with a long-nosed old man saying his name was Igor, saying he was some kind of unruly person and was about to embark on his own journey of rebirth...... I don't understand, TD!

You don't know why your otherworldly navigation app actually has a location for your address, but you just mark three questions. Anyway, you still open the navigation and arrive at your destination. Then, after a flash of red-black light before your eyes, you arrive at a completely different ...... It looks like a cyber-style city.

Not only that, your clothes have changed. You touch your face—it's a mask with half a butterfly wing that somehow appeared. Your clothes have changed to cyberpunk-like mechanical outfits. You close your eyes and think, then try to summon your own persona just like those senior members of the Phantom Thieves taught you.

So you shouted, "Sodium aluminate!" ”

…… Nearby passersby—perhaps called Shadow or the People of Darkness—saw the Light Folk in bizarre costumes shouting and shouting for no reason, and everyone was so frightened they fled.

And you...... standing here with both hands raised, apart from a gust of wind blowing a flyer that blocked your face, there was no other sound.

You cleared your throat and took the flyer down to see what it was. The flyer was wrinkled, probably folded and thrown away, then blown here by the wind. You carefully examined the blurry handwriting—it probably read SPAMTON G. SPAMTON, apparently some kind of promotion, half-price deal...... Well, you remember that palaces usually have a small ecosystem, and the palace content is generated based on the owner's subconscious. So this Spamton guy must be the target you're looking for this time—in other words, someone who has changed their mind!

No, no, no...... It shouldn't be called a candidate for a change of heart; rather, it's someone who must change his heart!

You carefully folded the flyer in half, tucked it away on your persona, and then walked forward with great enthusiasm. You hadn't summoned a persona, so you had to use the weapons that automatically appeared in the other world—a spear and a revolver. Just as the seniors said, any model can be brought to another world, and those shadows take it seriously—it can be reset and used every time!

You enthusiastically swing your revolver and take out one monster after another. If you really can't take them out, you stun them with your spear first, then poke a few more times. Honestly, you don't even know how these monsters attack you right away; some even rush toward you even after seeing you from afar...... But gradually, monsters stop rushing at you on their own; they even sweat vividly when they see you and then run off into the distance.

You actually just wanted to stop a monster and ask for directions...... At that moment, you frustrated and touched your mask, wondering if you were really that scary. Not a single monster was in sight, and for some reason, the streets were empty. It seemed that snowflakes were drifting down without you noticing.

You took a deep breath. Since it looked like there was only a straight road for now, you quickly followed the road forward and finally reached the entrance of a castle. After entering, you almost tripped over those blue eggs.

No one knows how ...... The ground is covered with these things! As long as you bump into them, you'll enter battle. All you can do is curse and smash the eggs with your gun as you move forward. Finally, you reach the finish line, and your eyes light up. This place is so deep and remote—it must be the place the seniors called the treasure! Once you take the treasure, you'll change your mind smoothly, and from then on, there will be one less evil adult in this world!

—Yeah, you're an adult now, after all, you're an adult.

You rushed toward the finish line with great enthusiasm, but when you pushed open the tightly closed door, all you saw was darkness. Besides that, there seemed to be a creature with a pile of green threads hanging from it. It looked furry but also a bit cold—probably some kind of robot.

To be honest, you're a bit disappointed. After all, you haven't seen the Palace Master on this trip. But if you can get the treasure directly, that would be a great thing—so you just wanted to grab the guy hanging up and steal it. After all, the treasure you're stealing will soon be yours! Heh heh heh!

But you couldn't pull it. Not only could you not pull it, you also heard a crackling sound with electricity, a voice that was a bit hard to hear but tinged with anger: "This [bold and reckless] [People of Light], are you [reckless]?" ”

You didn't quite understand what he was saying, so you paused for a moment. Your hand didn't let go, just blankly asked, "Are you alive?" ”

You've never heard of a secret treasure having living creatures. Did you come across something so rare in a hundred years? You couldn't help but feel overjoyed. Once this otherworldly journey succeeds, you'll have to show off a bit!

"[Pretending to know]. I'm SPAMTON G. SPAMTON! The most popular [1997 salesman]! An [outstanding Dark Citizen]! "He shouted loudly, his voice piercing, but you got it—he was the true master of this palace, the most formidable shadow!

Your expression grew serious, and Spamton raised his hand. The light cannon gradually began to gather energy, aiming at the top of your head, looking like it would leap onto your head at any second. You rolled on the ground, raised your revolver to aim at him, and loudly announced, "In that case...... Ahem, I hereby announce here: SPAMTON G. SPAMTON, who has committed the sin of greed, your crimes are unforgivable. Let me steal that heart of desire and change your soul! ”

Well, actually, you were just making things up on a whim. You don't even know what you're actually saying, and only after saying it do you realize that maybe, probably, maybe...... You should have sent a notice letter to the Palace Master first, and then come to attack him—the order was completely reversed. But that's totally fine!

For some reason, this strange guy's pale face turned bright red, as if it had warmed up. His legs swung with the string as he shouted, "I don't have a [heart-shaped object]! And your [criminal charges] can't be established! ”

You were stunned for a moment. If he had no heart, how could you change his mind? You couldn't just move him out completely...... Could a part of desire really be a living incarnation? So this really is a secret treasure.

Not meeting the palace master is a good thing. But...... It looks like they're about to start fighting the secret treasures, so troublesome......

You pouted, feeling a bit guilty after his rebuttal, but you still waved your revolver and started shooting: "Cut the nonsense! The otherworldly navigation already brought me here, and you even have a palace, so you're the unforgivable sinner!" ”

You pull the trigger, and the big flying bird—or rather, the big bird suspended by a string—shields its wings in front of you, blocking the bullet attack. With a clang, the bullet flew out, but it didn't seem to cause much damage.

You're a little frustrated—this palace master's defense is way too high, isn't it? Wait, wait, it's a secret treasure.

“…… But I don't have a [heart-shaped object], so your [brilliant idea] can't be realized! "His voice seemed a bit calmer, probably wanting to have a good talk with you.

"Why do you always speak with some unknown recording...... Forget it, even if you have no heart in this place, you still show your intentions. Because you are just a shadow formed by your inner subconscious! Even death would only be a shadow that disappeared from the shadow. "You put your hands on your hips and announced. In fact, you didn't know why you were talking with the palace master again. Feeling uneasy, you raised your spear again and thrust it at the wings blocking his way.

“…… I'm not the 'Shadow Creature' you mentioned, I'm the 'People of Darkness'! Spamton shouted wildly.

The good news is, the spear's tip did pierce into those two wings. The bad news is, you tried to pull the spear out, but no matter how hard you tried, you couldn't pull it out.

Could it be...... Losing a weapon like this, and giving up like that? But this is your first visit to the palace, and you always feel you have to achieve something. All you have left is a revolver, you can't draw that long spear, and relying only on mouth and revolver, you know you definitely won't be able to defeat the enemy before you.

You take a deep breath...... You know that if you can't succeed this time, you'll always be the child adults see as accomplished, dependent only on them. You'll always have to ask adults for help, only to receive their disappointed or understanding looks. You don't want to go on like this. You don't want to let yourself fall into this situation. You want to refute those adults who are always stubborn, unwilling to discern or accept change. You—you want to be yourself.

A sharp pain shot through your head, and the revolver was thrown to the ground. You clutched your head with both hands, kneeling on the ground, your face pale. For some reason, another voice popped into your mind. The mask that had been hanging on your face now seemed to stick tightly to yours. Spamton across from you was startled—to be honest, he didn't know how you suddenly ended up like this, but maybe he could have snagged your soul.

He hesitated, swinging the puppet and moving slightly closer to you, but an invisible aura rising from around you forced him to stay at a distance of about a meter and watch you.

You endured the voices in your mind, and then—the mask was torn off, and with it flowed blood that looked like a torn skin, and the wound healed quickly. You gasped for breath, got up from the ground, and called out to the Persona that had just answered your call.

“…… Kukukaner! ”

A huge shadow appeared behind you, her golden hair flowing, but her face was terrifyingly empty. Behind her were emerald-green wings, and she was holding a bundle of golden wheat. At your call, a bolt of lightning struck the ground. If Spamton hadn't flashed so quickly, the lightning might have struck him instead.

He looked at you with a complicated expression, because he saw the yellow heart shining in your chest. The spear finally fell to the ground, and you stopped picking it up. You just clumsily controlled the Persona, first casting a witch's curse—yes, it lowers the opponent's defense, so you won't be unable to hit it again later.

But since this is a turn-based battle, after you act, it's his turn to act. I just don't know why it's a bullet hell attack—the senior never said it would happen!

But it didn't matter. You're from Gotham, and you're used to it. At this moment, you were just easily dodging and neutralizing his attacks. After all, you're a Gotham native, and you're very skilled at dodging others' attacks.

So you just dodge left and right, use the gaps in his attacks to dodge, and do a backflip to dodge his sudden laser cannon, while secretly complaining that this guy can use so many attack moves in one round.

Just as you were about to give yourself another buff that boosts attack and defense, he hurriedly spoke up: "[Go Easy]! I'm not the owner of the [Palace Mansion]! ”

You're a bit surprised, but you still don't forget to give yourself a buff first. You just widen your eyes and look at him: "Aren't you the master of the palace?" Then who exactly are you? ”

You looked him over carefully a few more times. Hmm, according to what the senior said, his body seems to be red, red, black, black, purple, and purple...... This guy seems to barely fit the description, correct. There will be a sound like chains, basically as if something is binding it. This guy has countless green ropes on his body, and when he just moved, he did hear a bit of metal clashing himself. Correct. Then...... After hunting several shadows, because the event lingers too long, it will appear to hunt the Persona Messenger.

So you realize, your left hand clenched into a fist and you pat your right palm: "Oh oh oh, I get it, you're a hunter!" ”

So of course you won't let him off! After all, you've heard that killing a hunter can earn you a lot of experience points, and you can just kill those weak shadows directly! It's not that you find it troublesome, but you just want to save time, so of course you'll take this hunter in front of you seriously!
