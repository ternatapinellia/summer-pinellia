Shi Xing 23 (swapsptx you)

Of course, you wouldn't believe such childish things. You believed that just blowing on the wound would heal it, but seeing Spamton's smug expression, you naturally noticed his hand behind his back—you guess, this must be some kind of spell from the Dark People or something. You didn't say a word to expose him, just pretended to be surprised and widened your eyes: "It's actually healed—Spamton, you're amazing!" How on earth did he do this? Can I learn it too? ”

Spamton just pretended to be mysterious and mysterious, shaking his finger and head: "No, this is my exclusive secret manual, it's a secret! However...... Only you know this little secret." As he spoke, he winked at you mysteriously.

He would never use this kind of spell on other Dark Folk or Lightfolk — but there was no need! Besides, Spamton wouldn't want to cast spells on others. Although this kind of spell was nothing to him, if other Dark Folk insisted on using it, he would definitely take a hefty profit.

You sighed. This time, you genuinely felt a bit regretful. Honestly, you inevitably had some bumps and scrapes. If only there were such spells, how great it would be—you could heal yourself with one click and no longer have to worry about getting hurt.

Spamton, however, seemed to see right through your thoughts and said seriously, "Don't think that having this kind of spell as a backup doesn't mean the extent of the injury doesn't matter...... This usually only works on surface injuries. So, you must be careful, absolutely careful. ”

You looked away a little guiltily, not daring to look at him, and Spamton just turned your head to look straight at him, his tone a bit fierce: "Did you hear that?" ”

"I heard you, I heard you—don't be so harsh with me." You puffed out your cheeks and pretended to be wronged, and Spamton's arrogance quickly softened. He let go, just scratched his head and coughed dryly twice.

"I...... I'm not really being harsh with you!" He emphasized, his voice a bit weak.

"I know. Don't worry......" You tugged at his face and couldn't help but laugh, and he followed behind you, laughing too.

It's a brief moment of affection at home, and after that, you'll still have to work at a gallery, and Spamton will be his big boss at the company. Honestly, he really wants to be with you forever, even if it's just a doll that can only stay in your pocket, or just a parrot that always flies around you—even if he's by your side and does many, many other things.

But he can't. He's the master of the advertising world, so of course he can't just leave this place behind. But if he had to choose between you and his career, his first reaction would be to want both, and if he could only pick one, he'd grit his teeth and pick yours.

…… After all, if you lose your career, you can just try hard later. But if he loses you, there's no way he'll find the exact same Lightfolk again, and there's no chance he'll ever meet you again.

So—for this goal, to stay with you all the time, he has to work hard! Spamton pulled himself together, held another meeting, then stood on the high platform and began to order: "Y/N's solo art exhibition is about to open in the gallery. All the People of Darkness, buy a ticket, and during this period, all advertisements will be replaced with something related to the Y/N exhibition. We want more People of Light to see and click on them, and sell more tickets." ”

The employees looked at each other. Recently, because Spamton has been showing off the paintings you made for her, they've become numb to hearing your name. Admittedly, your paintings are well-chosen in color and beautifully brushed, worth admiring, but no matter how stunning they are, seeing them every day can make them feel a bit bored and irritated.

Yellow twirled his ankle boredly and asked, "What is the theme of this Y/N art exhibition, and what kind of promotional images do we need to use for advertising?" ”

This question left Spamton a bit stunned, since he didn't know exactly what kind of painting would be best to display...... After all, you've already taken the paintings to the gallery for display, and there's nothing left at home.

But he still looked up proudly, and the staff all heard a boastful tone: "The theme of this exhibition is, of course—the most dazzling person in the advertising world, SPAMTON G. SPAMTON!" ”

Moreover...... This time, he ordered his staff to buy tickets and even advertised for you—things you didn't know about. If he asked you for a preview of the artwork, wouldn't that be exposing himself!

Finally, his eyes darted around as he recalled the sketch you had promised to give him and had been hanging it on the wall at home for him to admire. He clapped his hands, and a pop-up appeared showing the images he had taken from all angles of that sketch before.

"As for promotional footage, let's use this!" He sent out countless identical pop-up windows, afraid that the Dark People behind wouldn't notice, and even made several pop-ups bigger.

Orange hesitated and clicked the pop-up window: "...... But, boss, are you sure a sketch is enough? If I remember correctly, Y/N is holding an oil painting exhibition, right? ”

Thanks to Spamton, all Dark People employees know that sooner or later you'll hold an oil painting exhibition.

Spamton hesitated for a moment, then reluctantly pointed to the oil painting behind him: "If that's not enough, let's use this one as promotional material too." ”

"So you never told Miss Y/N about advertising her art exhibition?" The other Dark People hesitated. Although the boss had become much easier lately, they still didn't dare say anything to their faces. If Spamton lost his temper in anger, the consequences would be extremely serious. Still, Blue quietly spoke up to reveal the truth.

…… If he didn't say anything soon, the pink next to him would explode! He definitely didn't want to see what was once a good event turn into a mess and a complete mess.

But now, Pink just stood there with his arms crossed, staring expressionlessly at Spamton.

Spamton coughed twice, probably because this time it wasn't advertising his own product, so Addison was a bit reluctant. Besides, there wasn't much commission for the ad page—he closed his eyes and said painfully, "Here's the deal: I'll give you 520 Dark Coins a day." ”

…… As for why it was 520, he was still too embarrassed to say it.

The chattering noise below had quieted down a lot, and the pippins were all satisfied.

Only Blue was still quietly staring at Spamton, along with his former colleagues.

Seeing this, Spamton was a bit embarrassed and angry, and as if she was losing her temper, she shouted back to them: "Alright—I admit, I advertised her without her knowing! Because I hope more people of light and darkness will come to support her! I didn't charge her advertising fees, so I rented these ads out of my own pocket. Alright! ”

Because his volume suddenly increased, the Dark People below were startled. Pippins and Zapper didn't dare speak and calmed down. Shadow guy originally couldn't speak...... But they didn't dare make a sound anymore, even holding up their saxophones and not daring to move.

"But would she be willing for you to do this?" Pink rolled his eyes, his tone a bit harsh. Logically, he shouldn't have contradicted Spamton's opinion, since whatever the advertiser says goes. But he just couldn't help but want to retort.

Spamton choked up. He wanted to say firmly not to make unnecessary comments and just follow what he said—but before he could speak, he remembered your earlier advice and told him to listen to the opinions of other Dark People.

So in the end, he took back that sentence and said somewhat gloomily, "...... I don't know. ”

He really doesn't know—but no matter how he guesses, it's obvious you definitely wouldn't want him to advertise. Convincing you to start an art exhibition was already quite a challenge.

But right after, he perked up a bit, his eyes beaming as he said, "But...... It's fine, after all, I voluntarily bought the ad for her! I paid for it voluntarily, so you can go ahead and do it! ”

The Dark People below didn't say anything more. With Spamton's wave of his hand, they announced the end of the meeting and had no choice but to leave the meeting room.

Spamton was the last to leave the meeting room. Before leaving, he turned to look at the oil painting hanging on the wall. The painting was simple, just a flower arrangement from the trash can. That flower was real, nurtured with your help. He brought it back to the office and carefully tried to care for it, but with the help of the plant lamp, it only looked wilted.

After your last reminder, Spamton focused on reducing the water content, which actually improved a lot—at least it wasn't as wilted, but it didn't grow too well.

…… He thought, maybe he's just like that flower. Without your love and help, he'll just grow crookedly. Even if he might survive in the end, he won't live a good life.

Spamton lowered his eyes, closed the conference room door, and walked straight to his office without looking back.

Of course, he didn't have much else to do. Back in his office, he just squatted down to observe the growth of those potted flowers. Hmm, it looked better than yesterday.

As for you...... You're running around the gallery to the point of exhaustion. Normally, there wasn't much to do, since during the previous artist's exhibition, you didn't have much work, but the boss insisted on printing your exhibition tickets and selling them early.

You thought there wouldn't be much sales, but who knows what happened? Those tickets were printed out and displayed at the entrance to be distributed, but they sold out not long after.

And now, many Dark People were lining up at the entrance, and the owner was urgently printing more tickets. Anyone who didn't know better would think there were many Dark People coming to visit this art exhibition.

And you can't help but feel a bit blacked. With so many tickets sold, just checking tickets for the exhibition will be a huge task!

But I'm not sure if it's just your imagination...... Most of the Dark People coming to buy tickets are pippins and zappers, plus Shadow Guy, who can't speak and only plays the saxophone to express his joy. You always feel these Dark People ...... A bit like Spamton's employees. After all, he admitted before that trash can flower arranging and art pieces was all his hired subordinates as paid posters. Maybe this time Spamton called them over too.

You start to get suspicious and plan to ask Spamton about it properly when you get home.
