Shi Xing 24 (swapsptx you)

But since the number of tickets increased, the boss urgently printed another batch of tickets—luckily, there was no event review or registration system in the Dark World, which meant that no matter how many Dark People showed up, even if it was packed, it would be normal.

You held your aching back, your face stiffening from selling that batch of newly printed tickets, even later than usual after work. You sighed, packed up your things and went home. Though it was a bit late...... It was still within normal hours. But you still felt a bit guilty, because you had just noticed several missed calls from Spamton—all of them called when you were so busy and dizzy, after you got home normally. Probably worried you wouldn't come home.

In short, you still walked to the door, panting, and then pushed open the door. What caught your eye was Spamton's anxious expression. He looked serious, looking like he was on the phone, seemingly about to give instructions. When he saw you, his expression relaxed, and he put down his phone. Instinctively, he spoke in a coquettish tone.

"You're back...... Why are you only just getting back! The food I made got cold! And I've called you so many times, and you never answered—" He grew more anxious, and you just interrupted him.

“…… Ah, there's no rush about this, right? It's fine if the food gets cold or hot. I didn't answer the call because I'm working overtime...... "You don't know what you're talking about, but you still answered his question. As you walked over, you stood on tiptoe and gently patted his head. He obediently lowered his head, so you don't have to stand on tiptoe anymore."

"As for why I'm only back now, it's because I'm working overtime. As for why I'm working overtime, it's because so many Dark People suddenly came to buy tickets. We've been working overtime to handle ticket sales all this time...... As for why there are so many Dark Citizens, I think those people are a lot like your employees. "You just mentioned this casually, not at all that serious.

But Spamton's face froze. He was relieved that since he was lowering his head now, you couldn't see his expression, and he was also a bit annoyed. Although he knew this was just another round of unreasonable troublemaking—those employees really didn't know their place, even though they were still lining up to buy tickets during their own off-work hours! Because of his Y/N coming home so late, he was worried to death!

However, he still muttered, "You can just dump your work on other Dark People. Why do you have to work overtime? You're a Light Citizen...... after all."

You flicked his head, and Spamton covered his forehead and took two steps back. He didn't weigh much, but I don't know why his eyes suddenly filled with tears, looking so pitiful and aggrieved.

"Even if I am a citizen of light, I can't use my status to oppress other people of darkness." You still leaned in, stood on tiptoe, and gently blew on his forehead. That warm, gentle, and moist breath brushed against Spamton's cheeks, making Spamton's face flush. He hurriedly covered his face, but spoke a little shyly.

“…… Well, Y/N did the right thing, so I want to learn from you too! "Spamton raised his voice as he spoke.

"Just keep being yourself, why are you copying me?" You chuckled, poked his forehead again, then picked up the food on the table that had already cooled down and headed to the kitchen.

"Alright, I'll heat up the food first."

"Me too, I'll do too." Spamton hurriedly picked up another plate and walked into the kitchen as well.

By the time you sit down at the table and start your formal dinner, it will be about ten minutes later.

You didn't press him further, since your words were already there. You know Spamton definitely understands, you've already guessed something, so—he'll definitely speak up later. You don't know why you're so sure, probably...... It's just out of trust and understanding of him.

Spamton's fork had been stirring in the bowl for ages, hesitating and a bit nervous, unsure whether he should speak up. He was a bit afraid to look you in the eye, and a bit too afraid to speak. Because he knew very well that you would never allow this—he was basically using his position for personal gain.

But in the end, he still took a deep breath, lifted his head, and looked into your eyes. He saw all the tolerance and care in your eyes—without any blame. For some reason, his heart suddenly felt lighter; he just felt he could speak more easily.

So Spamton admitted: "...... Alright, I admit I had my staff buy tickets. But, but I paid them advertising fees and commissions! I was just volunteering to advertise for you...... He met your gaze, his voice growing quieter and quieter.

You take a simple bite of pasta, watching him toss the noodles and ketchup in his bowl all over the place, and finally you can't help but chuckle: "So he volunteered to buy ads for me...... Actually, I don't need Spamton to do that. ”

Although in reality they still hire employees as paid posters, it's still a bit different.

Spamton nodded and shook his head again: "I know...... But I did it willingly. I think all the people of light and darkness should see your masterpiece!" He said this with his head held high, as if feeling very proud and honored because of you.

You feel a bit embarrassed by what he says, your cheeks flushing a little. You instinctively scratch your face: "Not that bad...... I'm not that impressive...... "You're still a bit lacking in confidence, or rather, this is your self-perception. Your self-recognition as your own works is pretty average."

Spamton, on the other hand, just shook his finger seriously: "No. Y/N's artwork is really great—actually, when I called in the meeting at the time, only 100 Dark People were called in, so only those 100 Dark People would go buy tickets. Are there still a lot of Dark People going to buy tickets today? ”

You paused, indeed...... About 200 tickets were sold out at once today. Although the advertising might have had a huge effect, and the dark people of the advertising world probably wouldn't buy it after seeing it, you still find it a bit ...... Unbelievable.

Or rather, you're feeling a bit carried away. You didn't expect your paintings to be so popular?

You cleared your throat and told yourself not to get complacent. Maybe there weren't that many Dark People attending the exhibition during those days, and maybe many of them bought tickets just for the sake of the advertisement. Maybe...... Maybe it's because the tickets themselves aren't expensive—just 50 Dark Coins.

“…… Hmm, about two hundred tickets were sold today. Maybe it's the effect of the advertising...... You nodded your chin, your voice still a bit hesitant.

"Of course, I gave them a commission of 520 Dark Coins per day! Their ads must be very thoughtful!" Spamton was very proud. He had taken a look at their ads on purpose; their ads really made people want to buy tickets, but Spamton was very satisfied.

520 Dark Coins......? You blinked, unsure if Spamton meant that, but judging by his expression now, he probably ...... It was probably just a casual remark. Just like the old line "I like you the most," it's just a habit, as if reminding yourself to feel this way.

So you just overlooked the price, but for some reason, at that moment, you felt a slight sense of disappointment, mostly a sense of disappointment.

"Hmm...... But I still hope that those who come to visit the exhibition genuinely want to appreciate the artworks. "You try hard to ignore that feeling, just forcing a smile at the corner of your mouth as you continue to say this.

Spamton could see your reluctance too. He frowned deeply: "Y/N...... What's wrong? Are you unhappy? How about I have them take down the ad......" His voice was a bit cautious, though of course, a bit reluctant. Spamton really wanted to promote you. He wished all the Dark and Light folk could see how beautiful your drawings were, and then proudly announce that he was your only Dark Citizen! And this was drawn by his Y/N, who is the best!

He just thought you were unhappy, and you really did feel a bit uncomfortable for reasons you didn't even understand. You didn't know why you were unhappy, but it definitely wasn't for that, so you just shook your head: "No need to take down the ads...... Actually, I'm actually very happy that so many people of darkness can come and recognize my paintings." "Even if you couldn't believe it at first.

"Ah...... Then I'll add a line in the ad! I'll also tell the other Dark People that if they don't want to go, they don't need to go...... Spamton fiddled with his fingers, still a bit uneasy. He could feel that your mood was still not very pleasant, even though he didn't know why.

"Mm, that's good enough." You finally breathed a sigh of relief and nodded.

Spamton puffed out his cheeks slightly, a bit displeased, but the next day he urgently called a meeting, calling over all the employees who had been reluctant and had met for the past week and solemnly announced, "Ahem, if you really don't want to attend the exhibition, give me the tickets right now. I'll compensate you separately based on the ticket price." ”

The employees looked at each other, unsure what trick the boss was up to—maybe he had reclaimed the tickets and sold them at a high price. But in the end, more than half of the employees gradually stepped forward and exchanged their tickets for genuine Dark Coins.

Who cares? If the boss wants to be a scalper, go ahead. They can't control it! Pippins happily ran off with the dark coin in hand, then set up a new betting game. Zapper jumped a few times in place, hesitated for a moment, but still had some tickets left unsold. Shadow Guy was the same.

However, after painfully exchanging a large number of tickets with dark coins, Spamton just carefully put away the tickets with excerpts from your paintings and exhibition titles. Although collecting these things is useless—but... he just wants to collect everything related to you, collect all traces of you.

Time passed quickly, and the day of the exhibition was almost here. Because you were the artist, you didn't stand at the entrance to check tickets. You just sat there, nervously tugging at the hem of your clothes, waiting for the visitors to come in. If they had any questions, you would introduce the corresponding paintings.

Do you remember...... A few nights ago, Spamton even swore he'd come every day! So you're also waiting for Spamton's arrival. It's understandable that you're a bit nervous, after all...... Spamton probably just thinks you've only drawn a few of his designs.
