When you got home and opened the door, you indeed saw Spamton wearing an apron, placing steaming hot dishes on the table. Seeing you come back, he smiled and said, "Welcome home!" ”

You nodded and couldn't help but smile: "Mm...... I'm back. The surprise I have prepared for you is right. But let's wait until after the meal to come and see for yourself!" Before you went home, you even pulled a piece of cloth to cover the oil painting. After all, surprises always have to leave some suspense. As for the question...... Wait until later. You hope that at least during dinner, he can be happy and looking forward to it, that's all.

Sure enough, you see Spamton's eyes instantly light up, as if he can't wait to get close and circle around you.

"Now...... Can't you know what the surprise really is? "He bent down slightly, looked up at you, and looked at you with a hint of pity.

But you just shook your head and made a gesture to be quiet: "No, that's not allowed. Let's wait until after dinner to see what the surprise is!" ”

Spamton nodded, eagerly returning to his seat, and also invited you to take a seat: "Then let's hurry up and eat!" "He really wanted to know what surprise you had prepared for him."

And you just smiled and placed the oil painting on the sofa. You want ...... You really are happy to show him this surprise, since at least the brand new present and future are something you both walked out together. So treating it as a surprise, as a keepsake and a memento of his past moments, is just right.

…… As for the past ones, you're not ready to reveal them to him. So, this dinner is just a rare quiet meal. Usually, Spamton would dawdle and want to chat with you a few more times, but today he ate quickly for once, because he was eager to find out what the surprise was. On the other hand, you still maintained a slow, almost normal pace, as if deliberately hanging him around, while Spamton could only look at you eagerly, but didn't dare to urge you.

Finally, you finished your meal. He quickly cleared the dishes and headed to the kitchen, then sat beside the oil painting covered with a layer of cloth, looking at you expectantly: "So, do I know what the surprise is now?" ”

You also sat on the sofa, just on the other side of the painting, gently nodding with a smile: "Of course, you can open it yourself and take a look." "Do you think that if he lifted the canvas and saw the truth himself, the sense of surprise and happiness would be even stronger?" You wouldn't be sure.

Spamton swallowed nervously, unusually nervous, but also a bit disappointed and uneasy—after all, he saw you walk in carrying this thing. What if you just casually bought some landscape painting or decoration as a surprise to brush him off? Although—although he really does feel happy no matter what you give him, if you brush it off like that, he would inevitably feel a bit disappointed too.

So at this moment, his hands were trembling a little, and Spamton was even a bit annoyed. Why did he have to reveal the final answer? You could have told him directly—though that surprise wouldn't be a surprise, and he knew he was just making a fuss.

In the end, he closed his eyes, held his breath, lifted the cloth, and then slowly opened them—only to see himself sitting on the sofa that day, focused on his not-so-nimble handiwork. Spamton's eyes widened instantly. He knew only you had seen this scene, and only you could see it—and almost no one in the advertising world dared to paint him, so this painting was probably, no, or rather, definitely by your own hands.

But if you find someone else—and his heart is on high alert—what if you learn something from those Dark Civilians that you didn't know before? Because of this, the joy that came from the surprise was now somewhat washed away by nervousness and unease.

But joy and happiness still prevailed, and precisely because of this, Spamton held the oil painting, his eyes sparkling as he looked at you: "Is this ...... Did you paint this? You painted me! ”

You nodded, and Spamton secretly breathed a sigh of relief in his heart, but for some reason, he still felt uneasy.

"Yes, I drew this...... Actually, I haven't painted in a long time, so I don't know if what I created now suits your taste...... You scratched your head, but before you could finish, Spamton interrupted you.

"No, it's fine, I really like it!" He shook his head and nodded again, then added, trying to make up for it, "I'm not saying your painting isn't good...... It's just that even though I don't know much about painting, I can tell you've put your heart into this work. Whether it's the colors or the shape design, it's excellent...... His hands gestured wildly, but when it came to the moment, he couldn't find the right adjective.

Moreover, he could tell that you had invested emotion into the painting...... Or rather, you had invested emotion into the person in the painting; at least at this moment, he could feel himself surrounded by that warm feeling. Although...... He didn't understand, or rather, couldn't believe what kind of emotion you were projecting in the painting. Maybe it was warm love......? He thought of that word, but then dismissed it himself. Spamton didn't dare to believe these things easily.

You watched him waving his arms and feet and couldn't help but chuckle, while Spamton leaned in and gently rubbed his head against your cheek—fluffy and a bit ticklish, but not uncomfortable. You paused, then gently tugged his cheek with your hand: "As long as you like it...... But I have a few questions I want to ask you too. ”

Spamton's body stiffened for a moment; he didn't realize that his voice had become a bit hoarse and tinged with bitterness: "What is the problem?" Anything I know, I'll tell you. ”

You blink gently, turn your head and only see the furry top of his head, while you simply reach out and gently stroke his cheek: "Hmm...... Do you know who the real owner of this dark world, this advertising world? ”

Spamton wanted to say, of course he didn't know! After all, he was just a lowly addison; how could he possibly have any contact with the lofty master of the advertising world? But then he remembered the lie he told you before, when he said he wanted the owner of the advertising world to back him up, so he could return to the shopping street to do business. If that were the case...... The two would be at odds, and he grew increasingly nervous, wanting to quickly come up with a good excuse to cover up the previous lies. Maybe lies just keep piling up.

But at this moment, he was extremely nervous, his mind completely blank, unable to come up with any good excuse, so he stammered, "N-I don't know." As soon as he finished speaking, his heart sank; he knew he would definitely tell this was a lie.

You just silently stared at the top of his head, knowing he was lying, and you could choose to expose him right now—but you still let it go gently, slowly saying, "Do you really not know who it is?" ”

“…… Alright, I know, but if I say it, you probably wouldn't recognize me either......" His voice grew quieter and his speech quickened, afraid you'd keep pestering this question.

"Do I really not recognize him? What exactly is the name of that advertiser?" You scratched the top of his head, gently rubbed—in other words, you came with an answer—which might not be ideal, but you also want to hear Spamton admit his lie.

Spamton fell silent. At this point, if you call yourself the advertiser, he knows you must know the truth—if you keep hiding it, you might lose all patience and expose his clumsy lies. In the past, he always thought he could easily fool you, but this time, it seemed impossible to fool you no matter what.

He didn't realize that most of those easy things in the past were just your indulgence.

“…… Spamton G. Spamton。 In the end, he reluctantly said his name.

Then he closed his eyes, as if awaiting the final judgment. He was very anxious. After learning the truth, you might have been furious or exhausted, and you might have kicked him out of the house—even though this was already a place he deeply cherished and reluctant to part. But your reactions were completely different from what he expected.

You just gently pat his head: "So...... You are the master of this advertising world. "Your voice is certain, after all, for you...... This is something you already know the answer to.

Spamton silently nodded gently. The calmer your reaction, the more nervous he became—he didn't know exactly what you would do; he just felt it was all over.

"Our Spamton is really something—he actually managed to become the master of the Dark World." And you just cupped his face, rubbing his cheek with the top of your head—fluffy.

Spamton stared blankly open, still a bit incredulous, but hesitantly asked, "You...... Aren't you blaming me? Aren't you angry? ”

You poked his head: "I told you, I will never truly blame you." As for being angry—that is indeed a little. "You dragged out your words, watching his expression turn tense and confused.

But you just poked his forehead again: "Why didn't you tell me you were angry and faced all this alone? You, a little Dark Citizen, have reached such a high position. How exhausting must it be? How lonely it must be to stay there." Angry is that you're still putting on a show in front of me...... Do you want to hide your true self? That must be so exhausting, how exhausting. This is clearly your home, you can relax a little. ”

Spamton stared at you in a daze. You didn't understand why his eyes were red, or why tears were streaming down his cheeks. You couldn't figure out what emotions were flickering in his eyes at that moment. You just gently wiped the tears from his cheeks: "At least...... You're home. I hope you can let go and be yourself, okay? ”

He shook his head and wiped away his tears in a daze: "No...... Everything is real, because...... I ......" He said this but fell silent again. He was too embarrassed to tell you, but in fact, Spamton just wanted to find a place where he could freely release his fear and unease, and say things he hoped for pity without restraint...... Or rather, he could receive your care without restraint.

You touched his cheek and softly asked, "...... Alright. But if that's the case, why hide it from me? Wouldn't it have been better to tell me from the start? ”

His hands held yours and rubbed against them: "...... Because, because I don't want you to throw me away...... Spamton whispered this. And you could feel all the unease and panic in that sentence.

“…… I will never abandon you." You only instinctively promised this, but you yourself were momentarily stunned.
