Shi Xing 21 (swapsptx you)

You leaned back slightly on the sofa, watching as Spamton seriously moved over a new pot, then poured in the soil and carefully transplanted the already wilted tulip. You looked at the flowerpot and couldn't help but get a bit distracted. It was bought when Spamton first became interested in gardening. As for why he was interested, it was probably because you told him at the time you wanted to see a sea of lavender.

Although I don't know why flowerpots are still sold in the Dark World...... Maybe it's because some people of darkness really like to plant fake flowers, and you can't help but smile when you look at the patterns on the pots.

I don't know why he acts so serious, as if afraid the flowers might be hurt in the slightest by his actions. But you look at him and can't help but be a bit lost in thought. In the end, you quietly return to your room, pick up your brush and a piece of drawing paper again, and decide this time to just sketch him simply.

So you simply returned to the sofa and quietly began tracing his figure on paper. For a moment, the room fell silent, with no other sounds.

After sketching and tracing his form, you began to carefully sketch on paper, no longer looking at spamton. You got so absorbed that you quietly walked to your side and admired it for a long time without noticing.

Until you were almost done with the painting and were about to lift your head to stretch out your arms for a lazy stretch, you widened your eyes at Spamton, who had appeared at some point and happened to meet your gaze.

Maybe because he saw you were so focused on drawing, he didn't disturb you. Even if he really did, you wouldn't say anything......

At that moment, he tilted his head, excitedly leaning close to you, his head pressed against yours, staring at the sketch. You instinctively felt a bit embarrassed and wanted to put the sketch away, but then you heard Spamton's voice again, as if he was acting spoiled, his voice a bit sticky: "Y/N." "Can I have this painting?" ”

“…… But it's just a sketch, nothing particularly good. How about I draw a new one for you?" You scratched your head, though giving this painting to Spamton wouldn't be a problem...... No, it does matter. Although Spamton praised your drawing and your colleagues said you did too, you still felt a bit insecure. After all, it was just your improvisation. If possible, you would still hope you could give Spamton the best.

Spamton just felt that whatever you drew was excellent in his eyes, and that didn't stop him from showing off, saying his Y/N had painted a picture for him—it was beautiful. And even if it was just a casual drawing, he could feel a warm emotion in the painting, as if it was enveloping him completely.

"This one is fine, and the previous one is fine too...... Ah, that one is already a gift for me, right? Y/N wants to draw me a new one—no, many, lots and lots of new ones! Y/N must really like drawing me too, right? "Spamton immediately went back to sitting on the sofa, but his eyes sparkled as he looked at you.

And you can't help but laugh: "Yes, of course I'll draw lots and lots of pictures for you...... Or rather, besides you, I probably don't have any other models." When you said this, you hesitated a little, because you couldn't be sure either...... After that, there would only be Spamton, the model. What if you need to draw someone else later?

But Spamton was almost making a fuss, grabbing your arm and shaking it, his eyes shining as he looked at you: "So, it's settled? Whether among the People of Light or Darkness, I'm the only model in Y/N!" ”

You were momentarily stunned, and before you could answer, Spamton puffed out his cheeks, looking a bit angry. You couldn't help but poke his cheek, then ruffle his hair, and softly say, "Of course, I will only have you as a model." ”

At this point, you decide, and indeed you can only paint him. Besides landscape oil paintings, the main character in the painting can only be him. Probably you still don't have the courage to paint other characters; you're afraid your painting will be criticized by those people, but only Spamton accepts it wholeheartedly.

And...... He has become another protagonist in your life. If you don't portray him, who else can you paint? Of course, the first protagonist in life is yourself. Everyone thinks about themselves and lives for themselves. As for him...... Spamton's presence was already present in the original future plans.

He rubbed contentedly against your palm, grabbed your hand, and rubbed it against his cheek, while Spamton just squinted and let out a slight purring sound similar to satisfaction.

"Alright, then Y/N's future paintings will all be mine...... Oh right, maybe Y/N should consider exhibiting them. I guarantee all the Dark People will come to see your masterpiece!" He said excitedly.

You feel a bit troubled. Honestly, seeing his sparkling eyes and hopeful expression, you feel a bit embarrassed to refuse, but...... You think, if it were your own will and choice, you probably wouldn't be so eager for the exhibition.

So in the end, you just quietly replied, "But holding a solo art exhibition is meaningless, and I don't want to be famous in the dark world." "You just need to honestly and steadily get through your lower half. You don't have many desires, nor any desires. You just ...... I just hope you and he can both spend the rest of your lives peacefully."

But Spamton, now the main advertiser, seems to really enjoy liveliness and fame.

Spamton was stunned for a moment, opening his mouth but not knowing what to say. From his perspective, he really did want you to become famous, to hold an art exhibition, so he could proudly announce to everyone that you were extremely, extremely amazing, and that you were his favorite Light Citizen. But...... He also knew that you should respect your wishes. If you didn't want to, although he might feel a bit disappointed and sad, he wouldn't say much.

So in the end, he just lowered his head, the two tufts of hair on the front of his head drooping a little, looking gloomy.

"If Y/N really doesn't want it, then forget it......" the voice was muffled.

You were stunned for a moment, not knowing how your Addison had ended up like this again. To be honest, you could feel that he was eager for you to hold an art exhibition. If it was for him, you would indeed be willing to hold one...... You thought for a moment, patted his head, and softly said, "Don't be sad." But if it were an art exhibition themed around you, I think I'd be willing. ”

You don't want to exhibit your past works. Although confirming this theme means rushing to produce a huge number of paintings, even though you don't know when you'll be able to finish them—you admit, personally speaking, you also hope more people of light and darkness will see Spamton, and perhaps the exhibition is a good opportunity.

Then Spamton's eyes suddenly lit up again. He dawdled down next to you, eagerly saying, "Then Y/N will hold an exhibition tomorrow!" I believe the tickets will definitely sell out overnight! ”

You helplessly flicked his forehead, and Spamton let out a loud "Ouch" as if in pain, then clutched his forehead and looked at you in grievance.

"It's not that easy. Preparing for the exhibition will take at least a week, and ...... I have far fewer paintings. "At most, if you count two paintings, I can only hang them at the entrance. The rest are just empty walls, nothing to look at.

"Then Y/N will treat me as a free model. I'll allow you to leave many portraits of me! There must be many Dark People coming to admire my heroic figure to visit your art exhibition!" Spamton slightly raised his head, speaking proudly.

You sighed and replied somewhat perfunctorily, "Alright, alright." ”

At the same time, you picked up the paper and left a portrait of him sitting upright as if spreading his screen.

Over the past few days, you feel like you can see Spamton striking seductive poses wherever you go. Whenever you pass by with a canvas, Spamton immediately stops and strikes a pose, sneaking glances at you out of the corner of his eye as if he's trying to see what you're doing.

If you really stop to paint, he'll look away and continue with that pose seriously, but after a while, he'll sneak another glance at you. If you're still focused on painting, he'll immediately turn his head and pretend he hasn't done anything. If you've finished, he'll lean in to look at your masterpiece and shout that your painting is beautiful, every time he's sincerely complimenting you, making your face blush and a bit embarrassed.

If his peripheral vision meets your gaze and you happen to meet your gaze, Spamton will blush, turn his head back, and try to be a responsible model.

If you haven't stopped, then Spamton can only look disappointed, tuck away that nonexistent tail feather that seems to be spreading its tail, then slunk behind you, eagerly waiting for your free moment, so he can continue modeling for you.

Recently, the staff in the advertising world have been troubled by the situation. For some reason, when Spamton recently comes to the company, they always look cheerful, with less difficulty and workload, but the boss's rambling noticeably increases.

A pippins, who wished to remain anonymous, shared a story about their own experience: "At the time, I was just delivering some documents to the boss's office. Just because I glanced at the oil painting behind the boss for a couple of seconds, the boss cheerfully shared with me, 'How did you know this was a gift from my Y/N,' and even nagged me for ages, bragging about how Y/N made this painting and how genius Y/N was!" ”

The other Dark People also expressed that what Pippins said was true, and that was exactly how they felt right now.

You didn't know about it; you just carefully left traces of his painting, recording his upright posture behind the dining table waiting for you, his falling asleep after waiting too long, his excitement as he watched blooming flowers, his eager expression as he leaned close to you to admire the painting, his face earnestly praising you, his face showing him lying on the sofa complaining about work, his glasses wearing glasses sitting at the table studying data, his apron around his apron flustered in the kitchen cleaning up dishes, and his skillful stir-frying of dishes......

You record these scenes, and the number of oil paintings accumulates, and this quantity is finally enough to hold an art exhibition.

So you take these oil paintings to discuss with the gallery owner, and the color card manager quickly agrees to your exhibition request.
