Time Walk 30 (end) (swapsptx you)

After dinner, as usual, Spamton went to the kitchen with the dishes to wash them, while you sat on the living room sofa, lost in thought, thinking about the future and what to do next. At that moment, your phone lit up, and you realized it was a message from the gallery owner.

You glanced at your phone screen—it was telling you that the gallery would close in seven days, and you'd have to settle your wages for the month. You know, it's because this dark world is about to disappear that the gallery will close. You put away your phone and let out a gentle breath.

And the next morning, Spamton was syncing with you, sticking with you all the way to the gallery. He said righteously, "Anyway, this dark world is about to disappear, and he's no longer the owner of ads—he's basically an unemployed vagrant. Of course, he can go wherever he wants!"

You followed him into the gallery, and at first he just excitedly shouted, "I'm about to see the painting Y/N drew for me...... I'm so nervous. I wonder if many people of darkness will come to see ......."

It wasn't until he entered the gallery that he fell silent, widening his eyes at the paintings hanging on the walls—he thought there would only be a few, he thought you would show other old paintings too, because Spamton thought those paintings should also be seen by other dark people. But he didn't know—you had painted so many, and all the main characters were him. Looking around, all kinds of ...... His silhouette, the fragments of his life with you.

He quietly walked forward, the first painting ...... He still had some impressions—it was that day when he was tinkering with the flowers, and you drew it on a whim—probably the first painting. Later on, some were scenes of him busy in the kitchen, some dozing off on the sofa, some grinding coffee, and some of his sparkling eyes showing off the newly made desserts. Spamton didn't even know...... When did you record all this? It was only then that he realized that you had seen everything.

After all, there are two more paintings, probably just your imagination—the first shows Spamton in a conference hall, directly commanding the other Dark People. Of course, this meeting room is just your imagination, completely different from the one he was in.

The second painting was of him proudly patrolling the shopping street. The small shop that used to be white Addison had been closed, and he was standing there, seemingly pointing and talking about other Addison shops.

Spamton turned his back to you, he's a bit afraid to turn his head to look at you, but his voice was a bit dry: "...... When did you paint it? How come I have no idea at all? ”

You rubbed your chin and thought for a moment: "Most of it was drawn in my own room...... After all, you've never seen it in my room before, so it's normal not to know, right?" "Except for that time, when you brought Spamton into your room, he hasn't gone in since. You know, that's respecting your privacy. Of course, you never tried to find out what exactly he was preparing, after all...... You would respect his thoughts, no matter how curious you are, you wouldn't sneak a peek!

Spamton remained silent, just walking down the corridor filled with paintings, until he finally stopped in front of that ...... Your family portrait. The term 'family portrait' was actually Spamton's own name. After all, your family only had you as a citizen of light and a person of darkness as well.

He looked at himself in the painting, smiling carefreely, and suddenly smiled too. He turned around and smiled at you: "I have an idea. Right in front of this painting, how about we take another photo together?" ”

You nod as if it were only natural: "Of course you can." ”

You might think, maybe he also wants to leave a little memory in this disappearing advertising world.

The store manager volunteered to step forward, holding an instant camera to take photos of you.

So Spamton puts his arm around your shoulder, and together with you, smiles at the camera, taking a precious photo together—of course, his current height has lost the advantage of being the master of this dark world, and he's reverted to the same state as he did in Addison, so he has to stand on tiptoe just to put his arm around your shoulder.

You suppress your laughter, and when you see other Addisons also come to the gallery to view the exhibition, you wave to them, and they come closer, gathering around you.

Pink looked impatient, but still said, "Alright, you want to take a photo together, right? We happened to be here today." ”

Blue glanced at Pink, then at Spamton: "I was clearly worried Spamton would come to the gallery......" But before he could finish, Yellow covered his mouth.

"Well, looking at it now, it actually seems like a good thing." Orange tried to smooth things over, skillfully sidling with Spamton.

You smiled, hugged Spamton, rested your head on his head, four other Addisons surrounded you, and then with a "click," the group photo was taken and washed out from the polaroid.

Of course, this group photo was copied four times and given to the other four Addisons. Spamton was a bit reluctant but still gave it away. And for your group photo, you used that family portrait as the background, framed in a photo frame, and placed it in the most prominent spot in the living room.

A week isn't long either, but not too short. The art exhibition ended, the gallery closed, and this dark world was about to disappear. In the end, Spamton hesitantly tugged at your sleeve and whispered, "...... Go to the shopping street. I told them that when they arrive at the time of the transaction, a Lightfolk will come looking for them. ”

You nod and head to the shopping street, where waiting for you are two Dark People, one human, and the other ...... Seems like monsters? You're not sure either, since the community you live in is still mostly human.

The purple girl who looked a bit like a dinosaur greeted you cheerfully, while the other blue boy didn't say a word.

She tells you that all the Dark People can live in Castle Town; you just need to move the corresponding objects from the Light World there.

You suddenly realize that you have left this dark world, and they have sealed the dark fountain of this world. You can no longer enter this dark world.

Feeling a bit disappointed? Indeed...... But with a bit of anticipation and a smile, you set out again on the road back to your original home. You no longer cared about those voices; after all, none of them mattered to you anymore.

You push open the door again, and your parents' gazes immediately turn toward you.

His mother was the first to shout: "You've called so many times and you still won't answer. I told you, you're old enough to find a place for your future. I've worried so much that my hair turned white over you, and you don't even glance at me. You must feel sorry for your parents!" ”

My father sighed too: "We've worried about you since we were little. Whatever you want, we haven't given it to you...... Look at this room—aren't these all the things you've been loudly trying to get? And only when you go to a good family can you focus on painting, right? ”

You ignore their voices, just walk into your room, bring out that computer, and you know Spamton survives on the spam stored in your mailbox.

When you walk out of the bedroom, your mother's voice suddenly rises: "Why won't you listen at all! We're doing this for your own good......"

You stopped in your tracks and turned to look at them. Although you tried hard to stay calm, your voice was still choked: "No, you ...... It's not love for me at all. You say it's for my own good, but it's really just to satisfy ...... Your own vanity. ”

Mother opened her mouth, her voice sharp: "Not at all—"

And you interrupted her: "That's it. You don't love me, you're just manipulating me. You don't see me as a child, a child worthy of your love, so I don't need parents like you either; I no longer need your love." ”

Sobbing and choking up, you feel your vision blur, and you just step back toward the door, smiling: "I won't come back. This isn't my home either. I'm going back to my own home now." ”

There is spamton, sunshine, smiles, eyes that always wait for you and anticipate you, voices that affirm you no matter what, your lover and your eternal family.

You closed the door, shutting out your parents' noise, feeling a sense of relief in your heart, and your steps becoming lighter. You beamed with a smile, leaving the past behind. You were about to walk toward your own future. This time, it was a future entirely decided by you—no scolding from your parents, no manipulation after superficial discussion. Now, you were going home.

You arrive at the address the purple girl gave you, put the computer inside, and then arrive in Castle Town. Having just experienced dizziness, you open your eyes when Spamton hugs you tightly.

"Welcome home!" Even though you were just standing on the streets of Castle Town, while other Dark Folk passing by were looking at you strangely, Addison only chuckled. The employees originally from the advertising world passed by, gave you and Spamton a flirtatious look, and then walked off again.

And you just sniffed, hugged Spamton back, and buried your head in his neck: "Mm, I'm back." ”

That day, while you were tidying up your new residence in Castle Town, you found the artwork Spamton had once shown you, along with some unsold fake flowers. You smiled and carefully stored those things away in the cabinet. As for that slightly wilted sunflower...... You took good care of it and placed it on the windowsill. As the sunflower grew and bloomed facing the sun, it was as if a small seed had been planted in your heart, growing and blooming.

Spamton, for some reason, transplanted those flowers to the square in the center of Castle Town, confidently surprised you, and solemnly took out a pot of roses he had grown to confess to you.

That sea of flowers still remains in the square, and you have simply returned home with Spamton. The future is still long, very long, and you will walk into the future together with Spamton.

So...... Welcome home.
