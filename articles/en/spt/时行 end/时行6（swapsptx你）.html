In short, you just sat on the sofa, listening to the sound of running water from the kitchen, treating it as white noise. You casually took a book from the shelf and started flipping through it. After reading tens of pages intently, you yawned, and the sound of running water in the kitchen seemed to fade, and you covered the book with it on your lap. You closed your eyes and slowly drifted off to sleep.

When you wake up again, you find a soft pillow and bed beneath you—and the blanket has been thoughtfully covered, with your outer clothes taken off. No doubt, it's obvious Spamton did this. He could have woken you up, probably thinking you were sleeping too soundly.

You got up, changed your clothes, glanced at the time, and then walked out of the bedroom door, yawning. Of course, Spamton wasn't there, but there was still a steaming hot breakfast on the table. I guess he had hurriedly prepared it for you not long ago before leaving again.

You sat down munching on some bread, a bit annoyed. You had hoped to get up a bit earlier today, at least ...... After all, you had a day off and nothing much to do. Of course, you could have gotten up earlier to see him out and wait for him to come home in the evening. Maybe work had been too exhausting these past few days, and you hadn't been woken up by any noise.

Of course you don't know, in the morning Spamton closed the door and kitchen door as if it were a serious matter, studied it quietly, and lowered the voice, afraid of disturbing your sleep.

So after packing up, you head out, heading to the commercial street.

There, what you see is still the familiar Addison—pink, blue, yellow...... But there is no white one. You strolled casually down the commercial street, a bit surprised—why did every shop display those flower arrangements in trash bins, and ...... There were ads for half price, limited to 250 Dark Coins.

Well, whoever buys this flower arrangement is a fool...... You muttered to yourself, but still walked over, casually walked into the blue Addison shop, picked up a bucket of flower arrangements, and casually said, "I'll buy this...... By the way, I remember there's a white Addison among your Addisons. Where is he? ”

Seeing it was you, Blue forced himself to suppress the urge to complain. After all, Spamton kept insisting that if he saw you, he definitely wouldn't tell you that he was now the master of the advertising world—he even posted your photos for them and would brag about it every few days, pretending to casually show how much you cared about him. Blue felt that he insisted on saying he had something even without it.

But...... Hearing your question, he hesitated for a moment. Spamton's earlier nervousness and panic flashed through his mind. He also knew Spamton had been trying to cultivate flowers and plants in the office every day, but had failed every time. All that resentment and confusion were vented on them. Later, he suddenly made a bunch of fake flowers to take away. Now it seemed it was all for you.

"You mean spamton?" He...... He didn't come to work today. "As he spoke, Blue placed the tub of flower arrangements into your hand.

But unknowingly, you notice that the city in the dark world has also changed. What used to be connected skyscrapers have now somehow turned into island-like cities. Maybe the owners have changed. Thinking this way, a flash of inspiration flashes through you, but you don't catch it, or maybe...... You actually think of something, but you find it a bit ridiculous.

Could such a small, pitiful Addison become the master of this world? And if so, why not tell yourself? You would only feel joy and pride in his success, after all, a domesticated Addison is amazing, able to achieve such great accomplishments.

You suppress your anxiety and still go home with that bucket of flowers. You have no mood to stay on the commercial street any longer...... To be honest, you went there just to secretly check on Spamton's current situation, and now that he's not here, you don't need to go anymore.

And this anxiety peaks when you receive a text saying Spamton will be back late today, so you don't have to wait for him. Where exactly did he go? What exactly was he doing? A mess of thoughts swirled in your mind. He didn't go to work today, so...... Could it be that he actually argued with another Addison? Will he win? Will he be bullied? These thoughts swirl in your mind, and in the end, all you can do is anxiously take out a fast pizza from the freezer, heat one for yourself as dinner, then heat another for Spamton—what if he can come back sooner?

But at first, you sat upright by the table, waiting. Then you got a bit tired, propped up your head, yawned, forced your eyes open, and finally fell asleep on the desk, not even waiting for Spamton to come back.

There was even a moment when you worried he would never return, just like the two people you waited for before—clearly your parents, your family, but never truly listened to you, nor truly fulfilled the request you once promised. Then came arguments, wiping away tears and crying bitterly as you fled, until you didn't know where you were and finally arrived in the dark world, until you found Spamton, the family you now think of.

So you tell yourself, no, after all...... This is your home.

Then, you hear the door open. Instinctively, you look up and see Spamton standing outside, panting heavily. He looks like he's just running back, his hair still bristling. When he opens the door and sees you sleepy, he instinctively scratches his head, returns to his usual self, smiles, closes the door, and says softly, "Didn't I tell you not to wait for me? Look...... I came back very late. ”

After a pause, he added, "But I'm back." "As if emphasizing." After all, this was his home—how could he not come back to rest?

You just rubbed your eyes, forced yourself to perk up, and said, "Welcome home." ”

Then there was a moment of silence between you. He sat nervously in the chair across from him, saw the pizza on the table, and instinctively wanted to pick up a piece and eat it. Even though it was already completely cold.

But you stopped his hand: "It's cold...... Go to the kitchen and warm it up before you eat. ”

Spamton softly replied with an "Oh." Even though he was back so late, you were still waiting for him here, which made him feel a bit guilty.

"I did this voluntarily. I wanted to wait for you to come back." You just forced a smile, now feeling a bit relieved. It turned out you had bet right...... He would come back.

"I'll definitely be back. You're just too tired waiting here all this time......" He instinctively wanted to argue, but before he could finish, you interrupted him.

"No, I'm not tired." You shake your head. He's here waiting here every day...... For him, the daily waiting and restlessness must have been exhausting.

But for some reason, that restless feeling vanished the moment you saw him. You probably think he was the same.

Spamton shut his mouth and said nothing more, just picked up his plate and headed to the kitchen, then noticed the bucket of flowers you placed on the counter.

His expression stiffened for a moment. At that moment, he was relieved to have turned his back to you, so you couldn't see his expression.

Spamton's voice was a bit dry as he asked, "Why did you buy this bucket of flower arrangements?" ”

"Ah...... I wanted to go shopping on the commercial street today. I saw that the shops were all displaying these flower arrangements from trash bins, and they even advertised. "You glanced at the flower arrangement bucket, a bit puzzled as to why Spamton's tone was so stiff now. Maybe he wasn't happy seeing other Addison sell items with the same idea as him?"

"If you don't like me, just put it away...... Oh, right, so where did you go today? That blue Addison said you didn't go to work. I went to the garbage heap to find you, but I didn't see you. ”

You see him shake his head, firmly but with a hint of panic: "No, no need, flower arrangements look pretty nice in the living room...... As for where I went, it's a bit late. Can we talk about this tomorrow?" For some reason, his tone carried a hint of caution.

You nodded matter-of-factly: "Of course...... Speaking of which, the flower arranging business should be pretty good, right? ”

Since it's almost time to sleep, let's casually find some topics to chat.

It's fake—actually, only you—and the only one among the people of light who bought this flower arrangement.
