Time Walk (III)

[3]

Early the next morning, Spamton didn't even bother to count your wake-up time. After preparing breakfast for you, he hurried out and left you a note with a messy handwriting, clearly written in a hurry. The gist was that he had urgent matters to attend to and needed to leave early today, so he couldn't accompany you.

Last night, he was anxious for ages, but when the hot pizza came out, he found you had fallen asleep again, leaving him with mixed emotions. Half guilty and guilty, half a sense of relief—at least he didn't have to think about how to explain it to you on the spot. But seeing you wait for him for so long and probably worry all day, Spamton felt heartbroken.

No matter what, he still carried you to bed, then carefully covered you with the blanket and took off your outer clothes. He didn't want you to catch a cold the next day just because you slept in the living room.

As for what urgent matter he was talking about—naturally, he wanted to hurry up and hold a meeting to have the employees follow his instructions. He had made up his mind. Today, he was going to reopen the shop he hadn't run for a long time. It was covered in quite a bit of dust, which would make his lie seem more true.

So Spamton went to the meeting room and urgently notified his employees to come and hold a meeting.

The other Dark People were all muttering among themselves, wondering what the boss was up to again. They had just held a meeting a couple of days ago, so why was it happening again today?

Spamton calculated the expenses and couldn't help but grit his teeth. Even though maybe it could cost nothing......

He placed his hand on the table, stood up, and looked at the other Dark People announced, "Today, Addison is still working normally on the commercial street, while the other Dark Folk will be going to Addison's shops to buy flower arrangements." ”

The previously noisy Dark People now quieted down. Pippins instinctively wanted to argue, since they didn't have extra money to buy them! Previously, an employee was forced to buy a single flower arrangement and had already used up all their savings...... But that wasn't the case.

Shadow Guy played the saxophone, producing a discordant sound, while Zapper bounced in place, seemingly wanting to say something but holding back.

Immediately after, Spamton displayed a panel labeled 250 Dark Coins: "I'll transfer the corresponding Dark Coins to your accounts. Just go buy them then." ”

He cleared his throat again: "Also, I'll be opening my own shop on the commercial street...... You won't be visiting my shop then." Saying this, he glanced around at the Addison below. The Addisons instinctively tensed up. Judging by the situation, Spamton would probably be making things difficult for them. Blue felt a bit helpless. He had thought yesterday's matter was over, but Spamton looked like he was going all the way to the end.

He clearly dislikes the custom of the People of Darkness instinctively trying to please the People of Light...... But he keeps trying to please you.

If you asked Spamton about this, he'd probably just be stunned, then slowly turn red from his neck to his face.

"So, as my former colleagues and current subordinates, you still have to make things difficult for me just like before." He snorted coldly twice, clearly reluctant, and held his head high.

Pink gritted his teeth a little, but still lowered his head and nodded in agreement: "I understand." ”

Yellow and Orange exchanged glances and could only nod in agreement. Blue, worried, looked up at Spamton, but unfortunately, Spamton was holding his head high, unable to see the expressions of the other employees. Meanwhile, Shadow Guy, who was listening to the boss pay the bill, played a joyful saxophone tune this time. Pippins happily hopped and left when Spamton announced the end of the meeting.

The meeting ended, and the entire room fell silent. Only Spamton was still resting his hands on the table, silent for a moment. In this rare quiet environment, he thought carefully for the first time. Actually, it was fine if he didn't do these things, nor did he need to explain to you. After all, although they lived under the same roof, even though you took him in and raised them from the start, and had maintained peace and harmony until now—but now that he was the advertiser, what could he want? Even if ...... Even if he used his identity as the advertiser to force you, a citizen of light, to stay in this world, it didn't matter.

But whenever he thought of that scene, his heart tightened sharply, even making him feel a bit painful. But can a spam sent to the Dark People have hearts too? He didn't know. Spamton just instinctively clutched his chest. He admitted he was afraid—afraid of seeing your disappointed eyes, hearing your helpless and disappointed sighs, and he was afraid too...... He would never see that pure joy, or pure love, in you.

Yes, your unconditional compassion and love are exactly what he needs, so he would rather spend so much effort doing all this. But he...... Yet he can't believe that all of it is unconditional, yet he fears it will be lost so easily.

Spamton straightened up again, dashed out of the meeting room, and walked to the commercial street. Just like yesterday when he rushed home to see you, he was filled with anxiety and nervousness, the hair on his head standing on end.

He reopened his shop and brought over a few pots of flowers from another Addison. The other Addison was angry but didn't dare speak out. Spamton also changed into the same clothes as the other Addisons, striking Addison's signature gesture and smile, waiting for your arrival.

And you yawned, still a bit groggy when you got out of bed. You knew it was probably because you were asleep, and Spamton carried you in...... You woke up a bit later today than yesterday, maybe because you waited too long yesterday.

After getting dressed and walking to the living room, the breakfast had already cooled down. When you took it to reheat it, you naturally saw the note left by Spamton, and even an emoticon at the end. You couldn't help but chuckle softly before reheating it and eating breakfast.

In the end, after hesitating for a moment, you put on your bag and decide to head to the shopping street again. You don't even know if you'll see Spamton today...... Just to take a look. After all, you definitely don't want your Addison to be bullied again.

By the time you arrived at the commercial street, Spamton had already spotted you. He gave the other Addisons a look, signaling them to start acting. Pink was a bit at his limit, veins on his forehead nearly scrunching up, but he still wore a smile and used the same sales pitch as usual, making deals with the Dark People they all knew were just paid posters hired by Spamton.

Looking left and right, you were surprised to find that the other Addisons' entrances were packed with crowds, crowded with many Dark People. To be honest, you didn't really know these kinds of Dark People...... You only knew it seemed as if they had suddenly appeared in this world one day, and you also saw that the entrance to Spamton was completely empty. He looked noticeably smaller than the other Addisons, and was also shouting loudly, advertising the flower arrangement for 50% off, saying it was now 125 Dark Coins per basin, but still no one paid him any attention, only cold sweat dripping down his forehead.

In fact, the other Dark People were sweating nervously. After all, what you see as a pitiful little guy is, in their eyes, a big boss. They could feel Spamton looking at them with a deathly gaze.

So in your eyes, those Dark Dwellers just glanced at Spamton and then left it alone. You even heard some Dark Dwellers probably talking about pippins saying they had never seen this white Addison before, and now it's still half price. Who knows what they're up to—maybe it's a low-quality product.

You can't stand it anymore and quicken your pace as you walk to Spamton's store. Only then do you realize his shop is more than just rundown—you could say it was just hauled back from a scrap collection station. The shop is run-down, and the goods inside are covered in dust. It's obvious no one has visited for a long time, or rather, it's been cleaned. However...... If you follow what you heard yesterday about the blue Addison, and Spamton hasn't come to work, and hasn't been there for a long time, then his shop really will end up like this.

You swallowed nervously, suddenly timidly approaching, wanting to tell Spamton you didn't mean to watch him make a fool of himself, nor did you mean to see him in such a disgraceful state—you just came with that original intention, hoping to protect his original intention.

So you walk up to his shop, lower your head, not daring to look at his expression, just greeting: "...... Hi." ”

Then you hear Spamton's equally guilty moans, which somehow makes you breathe a sigh of relief.

“…… Y/N, what are you doing here? "He was panicked, and you heard something moving. When you looked up, you were surprised to find that he had placed everything in front of what looked clean and bright, blocking the flower arrangement.

"I'm just casually browsing. Since it's rare to have a day off, I wanted to go out shopping." You were speaking sincerely, since you really were thinking that way. Yesterday, going to the shopping street was an accident, but today you came straight to the commercial street with a clear purpose.

You see the white Addison in front of you open its mouth, and surprisingly, it doesn't come out of the usual sales jargon. He just lowers his head, and you hear his somewhat aggrieved voice: "But...... But I probably don't have anything here worth buying for you...... You can go check out the shop next door......

You interrupted him: "Who said you don't have anything I can buy here?" "As you spoke, you brought out that bucket of flower arrangements, but the fake flowers inside weren't lavender, but sunflowers."

"I want to buy this. 125 Dark Coins, pretty cheap, isn't it?" You smiled and decided to put this bucket of flowers in the living room when you got back. The previous bucket of flower arrangements...... Just casually place them in the corner.

Spamton looked up at you in surprise, his voice stammering: "You...... You don't need to ......"

And you just gently pat his head, wondering why he's gotten shorter, not realizing he's deliberately shortened just to be like before.

"I think it's necessary. If you don't have customers, let me be your first customer." You said casually, paying him 125 Dark Coins.

Spamton opened his mouth, speechless. As the owner of advertising, he had closed countless orders, but when he was addison...... You were indeed the first customer to visit him.

So he could only lower his head, his voice growing muffled as he tried to hold back the tears welling up in his eyes.

"But a whole pot of flowers...... It won't help me meet today's performance requirements......

And you replied without hesitation, "Then I'll buy everything in your shop." ”

As soon as he said this, Spamton was stunned.

He wanted to say something more, flustered and trying to hide his emotions—but unfortunately, it was too late. By then, Pink had already walked up to you with confidence.

You instinctively glanced at the pink Addison, but he didn't look at you. He just put his hands on his hips and looked at Spamton, who was a head below him, his tone fierce: "Give me your flower arrangement. I don't think any Lightfolk will come to buy your stuff. You must have tampered with the flowers." ”

You blinked, just about to stop him, but then Pink swaggered into Spamton's shop, grabbed the remaining two pots of fake flowers, and didn't even glance at you. He gritted his teeth and thought to himself, if it weren't for Spamton's instructions, why would he have come to Spamton's shop to snatch things—but that was also a way to vent his anger.

After all, Spamton suddenly became their direct superior, and Addison had been displeased with him for a long time.

As for you—he doesn't dare look at you. He knows you're the precious Light folk targeted by Spamton. If anything really happens, they're doomed.

Spamton just lowered his head, shrank back, and didn't dare to make a move. He simply whispered, "...... But I didn't tamper with the flower arrangement! This is, this is something I sold myself......" he said, and he didn't dare to fight over the two pots of flowers.

You take two deep breaths, trying to calm yourself. Well, even though you know your Addison sometimes lies—you don't really care about that. After all, what he says is actually secondary; the emotions he reveals are genuine, so you're willing to trust him.

And now, all of this is happening right before you—it's all unbearable. You assess your size. To be honest, you know that once the People of Light enter the Dark World, they can more or less manifest some weapons and abilities, but that doesn't matter.

Confirm that there was probably no problem, and you didn't need to actually start the fight. You stepped forward and snatched the two flower arrangements from Pink's hands.

Your tone was also cold and indifferent: "Doing such a thing to your face...... Are you brazenly thinking I would ignore it? I can't bear to see any injustice happen. ”

Pink glared at you and Spamton in annoyance. He even pretended to try to snatch the flower arrangement you were holding, but he couldn't match your strength. In the end, he just spat and said, "Tch, acting like I care about his flower arrangement...... Even without these, I could still sell very well." With that, he proudly lifted his head and puffed out his chest as he walked back. Indeed, in front of his shop was a long line of Dark People.

You quietly gazed at his back, saying nothing more, just looking back at Spamton. You visibly shivered, and you thought he was scared again, so you quickly spoke up to comfort him: "Spammy, don't worry, I've already snatched your things back...... By the way, do you need me to help you clean up? ”

What you don't know is that Spamton just remembered that he was already the owner of advertising. He realized that under his leadership, there was no fairness at all—more accurate than exploitation. If you knew all this, how would you react?

However, he had long been used to oppressing those employees, so...... He couldn't bring much change because of this. But perhaps there were still things he could accomplish.

Spamton remembered the leave request he had sent back that morning, and swallowed nervously again. Hearing your question, he quickly shook his head, looked up, and gave you a genuine smile: "No, having you here already makes me feel at ease...... No need to trouble you, I can handle all of this! ”

No matter what, as long as he's by your side, all that tension and anxiety vanish like smoke...... Maybe your side is the harbor he's always hoped for.

You stared at Spamton a few times, making sure he was telling the truth, then nodded: "Alright, I understand. But if you have any questions, you have to tell me." As you spoke, you poked Spamton's forehead, and he quickly nodded in agreement.

"Of course—of course I'll tell you! I promise that when I get back today, I'll tell you everything in detail." By the end, his voice gradually faded again.

In the end, you just gently pat his head and walk into his shop to see if there's anything else worth taking. Spamton instinctively tries to stop you, but you just gently push his hand away—in fact, neither of you really does it.

"Let me see what you're really selling...... I should at least get a sense of your work situation, right? "Although you wouldn't ask directly—seeing is believing. You saw other Addison snatching Spamton's stuff today, so you felt a bit uneasy, so you insisted on checking out his shop."

Spamton opened and closed his mouth, unable to argue with you. In fact, he probably couldn't refuse you at all...... In short, you still walked into the store and gently wiped the dust off the painting. It seemed to be a landscape painting, but it was quite similar to the "Sunrise" painting in the world of light. For some reason, it was black and white—maybe because Spamton itself was white...... Or rather, this was actually a counterfeit and inferior product.

But when you take down the painting and prepare to buy it, you'll be surprised to find that the original black-and-white painting has regained its vitality, lifelike and lifelike. Now, it looks almost indistinguishable from the original.

…… Maybe it's simply because you're a citizen of light. You turned to look at the other items in the shop, which were also black and white. Only those two pots of flower arrangements were colorful. You didn't say much, just walked past the goods one by one, gently touched them, and watched as the originally black and white items regained their vitality under your hands, becoming vibrant again. Even the entire shop was now in a dazzling state.

Spamton opened his mouth, suddenly at a loss for words—he didn't know that these things would turn into color again in your hands. And you were thoughtful. No wonder Spamton's ads were out and no Lightmin came to buy them—after all, black-and-white products are obviously fakes, and Lightfolks probably wouldn't click on those junk scam ads.

At this moment, you see some Dark People getting a bit tempted and heading toward Spamton's shop, while Yellow and Orange, for some reason, made them stop and return to their shops to continue waiting in line.

You feel sorry for Spamton, and at the same time, you're disappointed in his Addison colleagues. You count the dark coins on you—buying more things is more than enough. So you roll up the painting, grab another tea set, and go to pay Spamton.

"I'll buy these two things. It's a pity I don't usually come to the shopping street and missed out on a lot of ......," you sigh as you say.

"Ah...... All of this is 500 Dark Coins! If it's Y/N, 250 Dark Coins would be enough...... As he spoke, he lowered his voice again, probably feeling that this number was a bit off.

You sighed and handed him 500 Dark Coins, along with the 125 Dark Coins you had previously discounted: "No, you can't just give me a half-price discount just because of me...... I know, you all have merit. And why keep giving me discounts? A deal is a deal." ”

He puffed out his cheeks, took only 250 Dark Coins, and forced the rest into your hands.

"No...... Exchanges and exchanges are what make a deal. I hope you come again next time. Besides, I've been living at your place for so long, I'm a bit embarrassed...... His voice was still very low, his voice light and airy.

"That's your home too. No need to be shy." You replied matter-of-factly, slipping the money back into the hand.

"And...... Even without discounts, I would still come. I like these things that have been revived because of me, as if they've been revived. "You look into the shop; those items have now returned to black and white, with only the paintings and tea sets in your hands still shining brightly.

Spamton didn't refuse anymore, just silently accepted your money. He ...... Actually, it seemed his eyes were a bit moist again, so he just lowered his head, his voice a bit muffled: "Thank you for your support, and you're welcome to visit again next time!" ”

This is probably addison's unified marketing tactic. You didn't take it too seriously, just smiled and nodded: "Yes, of course I'll come again." ”

Then you slowly left the commercial street holding the things you bought, returned home, and placed the bucket of floral arrangements on the counter. As for the original lavender flower wipe...... You hesitated for a moment, but still placed it in the corner. You hung the painting in a prominent spot in the living room, and you placed the tea set on the coffee table as well.

You glanced at the time, had a quick lunch, and decided to go out for a few more casual walks on the street. After all, it's still not his end time, so you can't just sit here waiting forever.

You decide to check out another snack street. Coincidentally, you're starting to crave something, since you've been off work these past couple of days and have been making a quick lunch for lunch.

Meanwhile, Spamton watched you walk away, finally exhaled, dropped the smile on his face, returned to the height of the advertiser, and put on that exquisite white suit.

Then he swaggered around the Addison stores, and to vent his anger, he started bossing around without hesitation, especially at Pink's shop.

"The items here aren't in line with the rules! You have to put the same kind of items in the same area, and you need to make them look attractive. Otherwise, how can you advertise them? How would those people of light even look at our stuff?" He pointed and pointed without hesitation, while Pink gritted his teeth and nodded in agreement, then placed the same kind of porcelain and tea sets together.

Then Spamton shook his head again: "Still not right!" It looks terribly ugly like this, so rearrange it. ”

Pink gritted his teeth and indeed repositioned those goods to the maximum extent possible according to his aesthetic...... He did his best.

But Spamton rubbed his chin and continued to nitpick: "How could that big tea set be placed on top? Big head and small body, it's terrible—rearrange it!" "Actually, arranging it like this already looks great. Or rather, he shouldn't interfere with how other people's things are placed; he was just looking for an excuse to pick a fight."

Piink resisted the urge to roll his eyes and readjusted his strategy.

Now Spamton was finally satisfied and relieved, so he walked out with his head held high, staying cautious this time and only returning to the office when Addison was about to get off work. If you make a surprise attack on the shopping street and don't see Spamton, things could get a big deal...... Although he could explain it too.

And after work, he just found Blue alone, shoved the leave slip signed with his name into his hand, and said without looking back, "...... I've approved your leave slip." ”

The justice you want...... Let's start from here, okay? Thinking this, he walked home in a cheerful mood.

However, as he hurried home and stood at the doorstep, he became timid again. Spamton swallowed twice, placed his hand on the doorknob but hesitated to press it, and naturally didn't put the key into the lockhole.

He was thinking that if you were waiting for him at home right now, how would he explain it—well, although he quickly came up with the reasons, he felt he couldn't keep deceiving you like this, and he always felt a bit guilty. He also felt uneasy, even feeling that it wasn't worth it for you.

…… But if only by doing this could you always keep your eyes on him. If only doing this would earn your favor.

So he steadied himself and decided to keep going like this. After all...... He wasn't really lying to you about anything. In a sense, everything he said was the truth.

Spamton finally opened the door. As soon as he did, he greeted him with a message: "I'm back!" But all he was greeted was a vast, quiet living room—no you. After a moment of hesitation, he realized you hadn't come home yet. At that moment, he suddenly felt a bit relaxed and relieved. After all, he no longer had to anxiously figure out how to answer your questions. But what followed was a sense of emptiness, loneliness, and loss. He didn't know if you felt this way when you were alone at home, but every day before, waiting for your return, he was always the same loneliness and longing.

And today...... The reason he felt disappointed was because he hadn't seen you. Spamton patted his cheek, opened the fridge to prepare dinner for you, just like every other day. He set the steaming hot food on the table, then went to his room to rummage around—just moments ago, he came up with a new plan, and this time it was sure to sell a fortune!

Of course, ideals are rich, but reality is harsh. He just tied up a mineral water bottle and stuffed it full of fake flower arrangements. Although he thought it was artistic when imagining...... He didn't know how you would view this object.

Spamton just wanted something with an artistic touch to make you happy, and now, looking at the finished product, he felt uncertain.

In the end, he still sat at the dining table, staring blankly at the steaming food, just like every day he always had, waiting for your return.

Then, he heard the door open. Spamton knew it was you coming back, so he straightened up again, smiling: "Welcome home!" ”

And when you open the door, you're still holding snacks you plan to share with Spamton, some CD bagels, and dark candies. You smile too, looking at the exquisite hairstyle he's specially styled for you, but you don't expose him: "Mm, I'm back." ”

You know he styled his hair just to wait for you...... But you clearly came back right on time for Addison's usual work-off time, so you don't know why Spamton came back so early. You were hoping to bump into him at the door.

So naturally, you asked, "Did you get off work early today?" You closed the door, put things on the cabinet, took off your coat, and sat at the table to enjoy dinner. Spamton's dinner was as pleasing as ever, even though you had no idea how many kitchens he had blown up in private.

And he had already prepared the answer to that question, just smiled at you and nodded: "Yes, because Y/N bought something from me...... My performance quickly reached my target, and after you left, several Dark People visited my shop." "However, whenever they touch those things, those objects turn black and white again, but that doesn't stop them from clicking their tongues in amazement—objects touched by the Light Folk actually change color."

"Maybe it's the influence you've left behind...... Y/N is truly my lucky star—I like you the most! "He added with a cheerful smile, then pushed your favorite dish toward you—he had specially prepared it for you today."

Spamton also knew you probably wouldn't take it seriously, so this time he didn't lower his head. He just looked you straight in the eye and said this directly, though he still felt a bit disappointed when he realized you weren't taking it seriously.

You nodded with a smile: "Mm." "I see, so you said Addison actually has performance targets every day...... Those past few days when you came home late was because colleagues made things difficult and you couldn't meet the targets, so you came home late. Just like the previous two days, that's how you thought.

"Well...... What happened a couple of days ago? "You know very well, Spamton would probably say he was bullied by colleagues, robbed a customer, and then stole something. Actually, you know this might not have happened a couple of days ago, but it definitely happened. Otherwise, he wouldn't have described it so vividly, so vividly, so emotionally.

And sure enough, upon hearing that, Spamton immediately started pointing fingers, indignant: "It's just like what you saw today! It was that guy Pink who led the way in moving all the flower arrangements from my shop...... So now their shop is full of those lavender flower arrangements, and many people of darkness and light come to see them." At this point, his tone turned aggrieved again.

"And yellow and orange are great at attracting customers...... So the few Dark People I managed to keep were all tricked by them into going over to buy things. Still, no Dark People came to my shop, nor did any Light Folk click on my ad pop-ups...... As he spoke, he lowered his head. Even if you couldn't see, you could imagine the look of grievance on his face.

"That's why I came home late last night...... It was to deal with this issue. Although I didn't snatch back those few buckets of flower arrangements, I managed to get them!" He looked up again, his tone excited, his eyes sparkling as he looked at you.

You couldn't help but laugh, even though what he said sounded a bit ridiculous...... But you still applauded and chose to offer some encouragement: "Hmm, our Spamton is the best. He actually got the courage to snatch things from those guys who bullied you before. That's amazing." ”

Spamton scratched his cheek a little embarrassedly. Actually, he had come home late last night...... It wasn't because of this, but the flower grabbing was real, since he had secretly tried planting flowers in the office again. This time, he planted sunflowers, and the sunflowers quickly withered. Pink couldn't stand it anymore and snatched the pot away, vowing he would definitely be able to keep them alive. Spamton only brought back a pot because he wanted to plant it in the pots in his shop.

Naturally, the sunflower eventually died, and Yellow and Orange took the opportunity to recruit Pink's customers, leaving Pink furious all day. But seeing Spamton, whose status was so high, could only suppress his anger.

You hesitated for a moment, wanting to ask why you hadn't gone to work one day before, but Spamton answered first: "And then...... I didn't go to work the day before yesterday because I was always bullied by colleagues and was unconvinced, wanting to find the ruler of the dark world to back me up. So I didn't go to work that day but waited under his office building." Spamton's voice grew quieter as he spoke. You saw his fingers resting on the desk restlessly stirring together, looking extremely nervous.

Across the table, you couldn't touch his head, so you just tilted your head and whispered, "I see...... My Spamton is really amazing, and he's brave, daring to fight against injustice." "You smiled warmly. Although you had doubts before, wondering who the master of this world really was, no matter how you looked at it, it was just an absurd guess. The master of this dark world had nothing to do with your own obedient yet frail Addison."

Hearing you say the word 'unfair,' Spamton lowered his head, pressed his lips together, and once again felt glad you couldn't see his expression—after all, at this moment, his face was full of panic and unease. He was afraid you'd know that all that injustice was actually his doing...... And not something that had always been inflicted on him.

So he quickly changed the subject: "Anyway, the next day, the owner of this advertising world backed me up!" That's why I got back to work in the shop...... Oh right, they took away my flower arrangement idea and business, so I came up with a new idea! Let me show you! With that, Spamton ran back to his room.

You opened your mouth to say something but held back, honestly...... You originally didn't think anyone would actually come to this so-called fake flower business, but judging from today's queue of Dark People, it seems business is really booming? You don't know that you are the only real customer; everyone else is just paid trolls hired with Dark Coins.

And ...... The advertising world...... You don't even have time to think about the word before you see Spamton pull out a ...... made up of countless bottles of mineral water connected together. It looks like a work of art, and the bottle is filled with fake flowers.

…… You probably wouldn't buy anything like this from the Dark or Light folk, unless someone has a peculiar taste. At least based on your taste, you wouldn't even glance at it.

But you still wait for Spamton to explain it to you.

He said excitedly, "I was thinking, maybe changing the vessel would be different—although the content is similar, the artistic forms formed by different vessels and corresponding vessels still have visual value!" ”

"So I think...... "You can defeat the other Addisons!" After saying this, his eyes sparkled, as if asking for praise.

You sigh and still reluctantly pat his head, but at his current height, you can only scratch his chin: "Yeah, yes, very beautiful. Spamton can definitely beat other Addisons, sell the most, and become a big shot." ”

You might think, these so-called vessels can be considered works of art by casually tying leftovers and arranging flowers, so why bother buying something to take them home?

However...... Staying almost the same isn't a good thing either, so you still asked, "Speaking of which...... I plan to have a meal with your Addison colleagues and have a good chat. What do you think? ”

Spamton's expression stiffened for a moment, and you tilted your head in confusion as you looked at him.

He quickly shook his head: "Of course there's no problem! It's just ...... The exact time is probably hard to agree on...... "Meeting your gaze, his voice quieted down again, and he dejectedly put down the so-called artwork.

"Then let's do it next weekend. If we really can't spare the time, then let's discuss it again." ”

Spamton nodded, thinking he needed to get his "good colleagues" to finish work early and free up the weekend.
