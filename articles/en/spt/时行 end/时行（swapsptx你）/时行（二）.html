Time Walk (Part 2)

[2]

Spamton left proudly early the next morning. You packed up and went to work. Although you were a bit curious about how Spamton sold the fake flowers and pots he mentioned—you were busy with work today and didn't have time to check out Spamton's shop.

As for Spamton, after arriving at the advertiser's office, he immediately held a meeting. Once the other Dark People had arrived, he boldly announced what he would be selling next: "Next, we're going to heavily promote the artworks." ”

The employees looked around and whispered among themselves, probably curious about how the advertiser, who originally wasn't very interested in art, had started caring about artworks.

In the end, Blue stepped forward, probably because he was the calmest among the Addisons. Like the others, he squinted, but when he looked at Spamton, he had a hint of submissiveness—even though he used to be their colleague, he had now become their superior.

“…… So, what style of artwork should we promote? "Blue asked, and the other employees all looked at him with admiration, making Blue break out in a cold sweat.

When asked this question, Spamton also hesitated a bit. He did know that you really liked art… or things with aesthetic appeal? Otherwise, you wouldn't have placed so many decorations in that somewhat cramped home, nor set up flower arrangements on the living room table, even if they were just fake flowers, and he actually couldn't appreciate such art at all, because he simply didn't understand it.

So the advertiser rarely fell silent for a moment. In the past, no matter what meeting he held, he would talk endlessly with almost no other employees getting a chance to speak. But today, he fell into a rare silence, making the staff below start to feel uneasy. Blue even began to wonder if he had said anything wrong.

In the end, the advertiser just waved his hand: "...... Promote any art! As long as it's beautiful, that's enough! ”

"However, the first thing we want to advertise is flower arrangements and potted plants made from trash cans—those bins are usually designed in artistic styles, so these bins are reusable, serving as artworks or as ordinary trash bins...... He finally began to introduce them enthusiastically, beaming with excitement, much longer than the string I told you about yesterday. At the same time, he opened several pop-ups to display all kinds of trash bins and his so-called flower arrangement artworks.

Blue hesitated, actually wanting to say that these trash bins probably had no real selling value—very few people would actually buy them.

But this time, yellow was the first to raise his hand and speak. He also opened a pop-up window displaying a black-and-white photo, also a work of art shaped like a urinal.

“…… Boss, do we want to promote this kind of art too?" yellow asked, magnifying the pop-up several times and pointing it right in front of Spamton.

Spamton originally wanted to scold Yellow a bit, but when he saw this so-called installation artwork, he fell silent—he was completely! No! He saw a bit of beauty in this thing!

In the end, he waved his hand and flicked the pop-up window with a big cross: "No, don't promote this kind of thing." At this point, he had to admit that maybe he couldn't quite understand the aesthetic of the Lightfolk...... And judging from the data he had, there were even many Lightfolk who pursued this kind of art. Spamton didn't understand, and he also thought you probably wouldn't like these things.

But...... There are still quite a few people of light who pursue these things? So he hesitated for a moment, then changed his mind: "Forget it, if it's art...... It can also be promoted as long as it's art-related. But things that look beautiful to the naked eye should be promoted more enthusiastically, like the flower arrangement I just mentioned." He was quite confident and opened several more pop-up windows. Besides the flower arrangements from the trash can earlier, there were also some paintings he knew were popular among humans, such as "Sunrise," "Sunflowers," and "Starry Night."

…… Of course, there were also some abstract paintings he really couldn't understand, and Spamton wasn't sure why the People of Light liked such things. But since they liked them, they could just promote them.

"If there's no problem, then adjourn. I think you all understood what I just said, right?" He waved his hand, ready to send them away.

At that moment, pink stepped forward: "...... Boss, I don't think flower arranging from trash bins has any promotional value. From what I know, the People of Light usually only use slender bottles for flower arranging. ”

"...But these multipurpose buckets should really suit the tastes of the Light Folk, after all, such slender containers don’t really have any other use." Spamton insisted on his point of view. Not only did he hate being opposed, but he also wanted to use this to prove himself—that the plan he proposed was indeed effective, and you could surely imagine the scene of him making a fortune!

"No, but the People of Light probably wouldn't buy it. After all, their families usually already have similar ......," Pink hadn't finished speaking before Spamton interrupted, who looked very impatient.

"Stop talking, they'll definitely buy it. Also, I think your home could probably use some art to decorate, so here’s what you’ll do: you first buy one flower arrangement per household based on the Dark Folk units." He announced domineeringly, completely ignoring the expressions of the employees below. Some employees gnashed their teeth in anger at him, while others just trembled and lowered their heads. The few Addisons exchanged glances, probably sending signals with their eyes, and eventually just lowered their heads in agreement.

Spamton, on the other hand, didn't care at all. After all, he was the master of the advertising world, and whatever the world wanted was up to him.

So he nodded in satisfaction, though he was a bit annoyed...... No matter what, he hated hearing his words contradicted, especially now with these employees he looked down on. He started to miss you, the taste of home, the warmth of your palm, your faint smile.

…… Now, Spamton suddenly really wants to go home. Just seeing you and talking to you is enough.

But he knows you're not home, so the little bit of anger he's built up can only turn into unspeakable grievance, waiting for you to come home and complain to you.

"Alright, meeting adjourned!" Spamton waved his hand, signaling the other Dark People to leave. He didn't want to see those people for the time being, so he drew a big cross to cover his face, so he wouldn't see those annoying guys again. At the same time, he created another pop-up displaying the price of flower arrangements in the trash can. Each pot was half-price for a limited time, only 250 Dark Coins!

On this day, the advertiser pulled out a card reader and slaughtered the dark coins of his employees. He looked at his income with joy, ready to show off to you when he got home.

Although advertisements for flower arrangements in trash bins are advertised, it seems there aren't many clicks from the people of light.

Meanwhile, you are busy with your daily work in the gallery, cleaning the dusty frames and preparing to display the artworks for the next exhibition. Just then, a colleague nearby seems to notice something and starts coming over to gossip with you.

"Ah, our tickets for this exhibition sold out really fast! It seems like the promotion lasted less than three hours and tickets were already sold out! The store manager is still considering whether to put out more tickets!" It said this while winking and making faces.

Your speed of cleaning the picture frame has slowed down. Actually, selling tickets doesn't really concern you, but the more tickets you sell, the higher your share will be. But usually, the tickets released have to be pulled and given away, and you have to find ways to give away some before you can barely sell out in half a month. How come this time it sold out in less than three hours? You just find this a bit strange.

“…… Is that so? Maybe the artist in this exhibition is very famous? "You answer absentmindedly, starting to wonder how Spamton's work is going and whether other colleagues will make things difficult. Although he tells you he'll definitely sell it, you don't really believe it, and now you're worried he'll be harassed by other colleagues again.

If only I had known...... I would have reminded him a few more times yesterday.

Actually, ever since you first started cleaning the frame, you've been worried something was off, which is why you've been cleaning it for so long.

A colleague clicked her tongue in dissatisfaction: "No, this time it's just an unknown little painter!" Unknown, the store manager was worried that less than half of the tickets might not sell, but now they were sold out. She was overjoyed. ”

Then she tugged at you again: "You've been wiping this frame since earlier, distracted. Tell me, is something wrong?" "She started making faces with you."

You coughed awkwardly twice, released the frame you'd already polished from the past, and started wiping the next one: "...... No, I don't have any issues. You know very well. I'm a citizen of light, and in the dark world, I don't know anyone except you. ”

Lying. You know you're lying, and you know your lies aren't very sophisticated, which makes you a bit nervous.

But your colleague just dragged out her words with an "Oh," seemingly not suspecting you: "I knew it, you don't look like you'd be looking for a boyfriend either." As she spoke, she moved a bit farther away from you and started tidying up the curtains and screens used for the exhibition on that side.

You paused for a moment and stopped what you were doing. She hadn't realized you were lying...... Maybe after spending so much time with Spamton, you've become a bit of a liar? You're not really sure.

But she's right—you don't want to find a boyfriend, nor do you want to be in a romantic relationship. For you...... That's an unlikely thing.

You lowered your eyes, recalling the food that had been placed on the table until it cooled, the argument that day, the moment you couldn't bear it anymore, covering your ears and instinctively running out, wanting to get as far as possible, and in the end, like Alice who had stumbled down a rabbit hole, you fell into this dark world.

To you, the dark world is much better than the light world. Think about it, your family's Addison wouldn't be like this, and when you think of Spamton, the corners of your mouth can't help but lift slightly. If your colleague saw this, they'd be shocked—they'd definitely ask who you're thinking of to make you so happy.

Maybe you want to go home, you want to see Spamton, even knowing he might be lying, you still want to hear his voice. At that moment, you start looking forward to the end of work, as if you can hear Spamton's words "Welcome home" again.

But when he says he likes you the most...... He probably just said it casually. Or maybe it's just that you haven't thought it through.

The store manager happily announced that she could leave early today—since the urgently printed tickets were sold out, she waved her hand and said, "You all got a three-day holiday." The other colleagues were very happy, because sometimes they had to stay until dinner time before leaving work, leaving them starving when they got home.

And you couldn't help but pause. Although you were just as happy, you also felt a bit worried. You won't have to go to work these days...... This means you'll have to wait at home for him to come home in vain, which makes you feel a bit anxious.

But it was also time to get off work. You gave a reluctant greeting to colleagues who were cheerfully saying goodbye, then returned to your expressionless demeanor and headed home. As usual, you pushed open the door and saw Spamton placing trays on the table. He was a bit at a loss seeing you come back, as you never returned this early before, so he quickly set down the tray, wiped his hands on his apron, hurriedly tidied up his slightly messy hair from rushing around the kitchen, and put on a big smiling face for you.

"Welcome back—did you get off work early today?" He warmly came over again, hanging your coat on a nearby coat rack, and placed your freshly taken briefcase on the coffee table.

"Well, because the art exhibition tickets sold quickly, the manager gave us a day off and even gave us three days off." You nodded, instinctively scratching Spamton's chin, and he paused for a moment before resting his chin on your palm and gently nuzzling. Yes, he really did crave this warmth—though, as the advertiser, he shouldn't have these trivial and subtle emotions anymore, or rather...... It wasn't suitable for his current status.

But he knows that in this home, he's just your addison. You tacitly stop talking about extra work matters, because for you, coming home is just a place to relax.

"So, I'm back, and barring any surprises, I'll probably be waiting for you to come home for the next few days." You said this in a pretending lighthearted tone, but the thought of waiting for him made you uneasy. Often, the one waiting holds the greatest hope, and also works the hardest, just like you did before.

So you can't help but pat Spamton's head again: "I know you work hard every day......" You want to say more, but Spamton can't wait to interrupt you.

"It's not hard! Y/N works out every day, so it's hard to support this family...... I've only done a small thing for you." Spamton shook his head. He knew very well...... The reason you were able to leave work early and still have three days off is probably because of the meeting he hosted this morning, which called for vigorous promotion of the art industry. Because of this, many people of darkness and light probably bought tickets for the exhibition.

If ...... It would be great if you could be so relaxed every day. But he also knew very well that he couldn't let you fall into that state. If you had to wait every day for his return with hope and fear—he wouldn't want that. As long as he could feel this feeling, that would be enough.

So even though he was stunned for a moment, he didn't say much, just said with a smile, "Then I absolutely have to finish my day's performance quickly, then come home early to find you!" He said this with absolute confidence, and you didn't take it seriously, just patted his head again.

"I know, but your job is tough too...... If you really have to work late, that's fine. ”

Out of sight, Spamton's eyes darted around as he pondered some questions—finally deciding to wait a few days.

"Well, if you come back late...... I'll tell Y/N." His head rubbed against your palm, then naturally led your hand to sit at the table, sitting across from you.

Spamton naturally complained to you again: "Let me tell you...... Although today's performance was all completed, it's still annoying! My Addison colleagues really are annoying! ”

"Yeah, I know they're annoying, after all, you're competitors too...... But that's already considered unfair competition. "You nodded, picked up a spoonful of pasta with your fork, and ate it.

"They say the plan I proposed is absolutely impossible—nonsense! The advertising plan I proposed will definitely get lots of Light Citizens to click on the pop-ups!" While speaking, he gestured wildly, spamton waving his arms and hands, probably trying to recreate the original scene for you. He even opened several dense pop-ups, filled with many photos of various trash cans and flower arrangements, all priced at 250 Dark Coins.

You looked at the price and held back, but didn't laugh. You just waved the pop-up window in front of you—it was almost crowding in front of you and interrupting your meal. Spamton noticed your actions and hurriedly shrank the pop-up window.

"So, how's the sales?" You just calmly asked, even though through the pop-up window, you couldn't see Spamton's expression.

But his voice sounded hesitant: "...... Today's sales are pretty good!" Although he raised his voice to emphasize this, you still felt a bit guilty about it.

But you didn't press further, since he said it was good...... At least a few pieces were sold, and that's what you know about the white Addison at home. Even though you don't know, he's changed a lot.

Spamton, of course, wouldn't tell you that the only sales today were forcibly sold to employees by selling those flower arrangements, and for now, no Lightminers have checked this ad pop-up. Of course, the rest of the business is pretty good—though some fraudulent practices have led to constant criticism, and many people still fall for it. Of course, the expected effect is still there, just not as flashy as the ads say—and it's much more expensive than ordinary products.

"Yeah, as long as sales are good...... So what your colleague said isn't quite right either. "Across the table, you can't comfort Spamton's head by patting him, but you're already thinking about whether one day you can treat these addisons to a meal...... And teach them a lesson, since at least you need to let these addisons know that their own addisons aren't easy to bully. After all, you're a protective person.

Thinking this, you also asked, "Otherwise, when will you ...... organize a dinner together? Let me meet the other Addisons? ”

Spamton's body stiffened instinctively, those pop-up windows disappeared, and his expression was somewhat blank. His first reaction was, what if you discovered his lie then—the second thought: what if you don't want him and instead keep another Addison at home? At that moment, he suddenly felt an overwhelming sense of panic, and because of this, he planned to hide his identity even better, at least for now...... He still couldn't let you find out.

If you know what Spamton is thinking, you'll probably just smile and say that's fine. You've already guessed he's lied to you, but that's okay. After all, what he says may be fake, but the emotions he expresses are never fake—so the favoritism you give him isn't fake either.

And you noticed Spamton was momentarily stunned, a bit puzzled, and finally waved his hand in front of him: "Hey, Spammy, what's wrong?" "You don't know why he's daydreaming again now, and you're a bit worried about him."

Then you asked, "…So what’s the problem? If you can’t get other Addisons, then forget it." You chose to give him another option first, after all, facing someone you were once afraid of is not easy. If Spamton couldn’t muster the courage to do it, then that’s fine.

Spamton snapped back to reality and quickly shook his head: "No...... No, it's fine. I was just thinking, uh, just thinking about something...... Y/N, you're not blaming me, are you? "He lowered his head, his voice pitiful, making it hard for you to ask what he was really thinking. Maybe he was recalling something related to those Addisons.

You sighed and softened your voice: "Of course I won't blame you...... I will never truly blame you." Your words were very serious, and to you, this was indeed a very sincere promise—you thought you'd never have the chance to say the word 'forever' in your lifetime, but now, it flowed so smoothly that you were momentarily stunned.

…… Probably, it doesn't matter. That's how you think about it.

Spamton nodded vigorously, still keeping his head down, silently rolling up the pasta and eating. He didn't want you to see his eyes suddenly redden—that would be terrible. He just wanted to show you his beautiful face. He didn't say anything more; he was afraid you would notice anything unusual.

You also seemed a bit distracted, and for a moment, the dining table fell silent, with only the sound of knives and forks clashing with ceramics.

After dinner, Spamton got up as usual to start clearing the table. You stood up too, ready to help him tidy up. After all, you hadn't worked hard today, and besides, starting tomorrow, you have a three-day holiday.

But Spamton held your hand down. For some reason, his words even carried a hint of seriousness: "Leave these things to me, you don't need to come to the kitchen." ”

You paused, then looked up at him—yes, now you have to look up at him. You don't even know when it started.

"Why?" You simply asked three words.

Spamton hesitated; he admitted that this did sound a bit like a bit of a presumptuous opinion...... Or rather, he was just making a pure assumption. When he first learned you worked at a gallery, he thought you would also do some painting work, but no matter how he looked at it, he couldn't see the tools or you were doing what he thought he was.

But...... He rarely shows a bit of a childish temper, or maybe he's always had a similar temperament—he just blocks your hand, popping up countless crossed pop-up windows.

"There's no reason! No means it's not allowed!" You heard Spamton's voice, and for some reason, it sounded a bit annoying, making you laugh uncontrollably. It seemed like you heard your laughter, and you felt Spamton glare at you through those pop-up windows.

And you put away your smile: "Alright, alright, I know it's not allowed." ”

Only then did he happily withdraw those pop-up windows. In fact, not letting you go to the kitchen was just a childish excuse—he didn't want you, who had done so much, to have your time occupied by these trivial matters. He also didn't want your smile, your hands, everything about you to be completely altered by the passage of time or other such reasons.

Just maintain the status quo, as long as it stays as it is—at least, that's what Spamton hopes for.

Although you don't know why he won't let you into the kitchen—you still go in a few times without him seeing. Sometimes you see he hasn't come back to prepare dinner for him; other times, it's just for the sake of ...... You're a bit hungry, so you go to the kitchen to see if there's anything else edible. Although the countertop doesn't have many items, when you open the fridge door, you'll always find the fridge door packed to the brim...... Basically, it's all for tomorrow's food and your favorite foods. Chocolate, fruit candy, brownies, tiramisu...... Everything is there, and you can't tell if he bought them from outside or made them himself. In any case, you still casually pick up the candy and toss it into your mouth, the corners of your mouth lifting involuntarily.

Spamton usually isn’t much into eating these cakes… He would just smile and rest his chin while watching you eat, and for a moment, you felt like you saw a peacock spreading its feathers, as he was beaming with pride, craving praise.

And often, you'll laugh and say, "Spamton's craftsmanship is amazing!" Then pat his head again.

And he would lift his head, squint his eyes, looking thoroughly satisfied, while secretly hiding his burned fingers from cooking behind his back. He wouldn't tell you all this was made after studying the recipes for days...... He even blew up several kitchens on his office building's side.

In short, you just sat on the sofa, listening to the sound of running water from the kitchen, treating it as white noise. You casually took a book from the shelf and started flipping through it. After reading tens of pages intently, you yawned, and the sound of running water in the kitchen seemed to disappear, and you covered the book with it on your lap. You closed your eyes and slowly drifted off to sleep.

When you wake up again, you find a soft pillow and bed beneath you—and the blanket has been thoughtfully covered, with your outer clothes taken off. No doubt, it's obvious Spamton did this. He could have woken you up, probably thinking you were sleeping too soundly.

You got up, changed your clothes, glanced at the time, and then walked out of the bedroom door, yawning. Of course, Spamton wasn't there, but there was still a steaming hot breakfast on the table. I guess he had hurriedly prepared it for you not long ago before leaving again.

You sat down munching on some bread, a bit annoyed. You had hoped to get up a bit earlier today, at least ...... After all, you had a day off and nothing much to do. Of course, you could have gotten up earlier to see him out and wait for him to come home in the evening. Maybe work had been too exhausting these past few days, and you hadn't been woken up by any noise.

Of course you don't know, in the morning Spamton closed the door and kitchen door as if it were a serious matter, studied it quietly, and lowered the voice, afraid of disturbing your sleep.

But since it's a holiday and there's nothing to do, why not go shopping? Although I haven't asked about Spamton's work status, it's fine to take a quick look at the shopping street, since Addison works there.

So after packing up, you head out, heading to the commercial street.

There, what you see is still the familiar Addison—pink, blue, yellow...... But there is no white one. You strolled casually down the commercial street, a bit surprised—why did every shop display those flower arrangements in trash bins, and ...... There were ads for half price, limited to 250 Dark Coins.

Well, whoever buys this flower arrangement is a fool...... You muttered to yourself, but still walked over, casually walked into the blue Addison shop, picked up a bucket of flower arrangements, and casually said, "I'll buy this...... By the way, I remember there's a white Addison among your Addisons. Where is he? ”

Seeing that it was you, Blue forced himself to resist the urge to make a sarcastic comment. After all, Spamton had repeatedly ordered that if he saw you, he should definitely not tell you that he was now the master of the advertising world—he even posted your photo for them to see, and every once in a while would show off, pretending to nonchalantly indicate how much you cared about him. Blue felt he was just making things up.

But...... Hearing your question, he hesitated for a moment. Spamton's earlier nervousness and panic flashed through his mind. He also knew Spamton had been trying to cultivate flowers and plants in the office every day, but had failed every time. All that resentment and confusion were vented on them. Later, he suddenly made a bunch of fake flowers to take away. Now it seemed it was all for you.

Maybe out of compassion, or out of pity, Blue decided to keep it secret for Spamton, so he said something insincere, and in the meantime, put another colleague down.

"You mean spamton?" He...... He didn't come to work today. "As he spoke, Blue placed the tub of flower arrangements into your hand.

He didn't say much, but it was actually a lie. The advertiser had already been sitting in his office early today, and was even asking them to hurry out and advertise these things, even if no Light Citizens clicked.

You blinked, and understood automatically. After all, this so-called trash can flower arrangement was now sold by all Addisons and seemed quite popular...... Spamton lost its business, so it was normal it didn't come to the commercial street. But if it wasn't here, where could it go? You suspected this, so you could only thank you anxiously, pay 250 Dark Coins, then wandered around the commercial street holding the tub of floral arrangements, but indeed didn't see Spamton.

And you even went to the trash bin where you had found Spamton earlier, but didn't see him. You started to get a bit anxious, since you didn't know where else Spamton could go besides these places.

But unknowingly, you notice that this city in the dark world has also changed. What used to be connected skyscrapers have now somehow turned into island-like cities. Maybe it's a change of owners. Thinking this way, a flash of inspiration flashes through your mind, but you don't catch it, or maybe...... You actually think of something, but you find it a bit ridiculous.

Could such a small, pitiful Addison become the master of this world? And if so, why not tell yourself? You would only feel joy and pride in his success, after all, a domesticated Addison is amazing, able to achieve such great accomplishments.

You suppress your anxiety and still go home with that bucket of flowers. You have no mood to stay on the commercial street any longer...... To be honest, you went there just to secretly check on Spamton's current situation, and now that he's not here, you don't need to go anymore.

This sense of anxiety reached its peak when you received a text saying Spamton would be home late today and you didn’t need to wait for him. Where had he gone, and what was he doing? All these messy thoughts swirled in your mind. Since he didn’t go to work today… could it be that he was now arguing with other Addisons? Would he win, would he be bullied? These thoughts kept circling in your mind, and you could only anxiously take a frozen pizza from the freezer, heat one for yourself as dinner, and then thought to heat another one for Spamton in case he came back early.

But at first, you sat upright by the table, waiting. Then you got a bit tired, propped up your head, yawned, forced your eyes open, and finally fell asleep on the desk, not even waiting for Spamton to come back.

There was even a moment when you worried he would never return, just like the two people you waited for before—clearly your parents, your family, but never truly listened to you, nor truly fulfilled the request you once promised. Then came arguments, wiping away tears and crying bitterly as you fled, until you didn't know where you were and finally arrived in the dark world, until you found Spamton, the family you now think of.

So you tell yourself, no, after all...... This is your home.

Then, you hear the door open. Instinctively, you look up and see Spamton standing outside, panting heavily. He looks like he's just running back, his hair still bristling. When he opens the door and sees you sleepy, he instinctively scratches his head, returns to his usual self, smiles, closes the door, and says softly, "Didn't I tell you not to wait for me? Look...... I came back very late. ”

After a pause, he added, "But I'm back." "As if emphasizing." After all, this is his home—how could he not come back to rest?

You just rubbed your eyes, forced yourself to perk up, and said, "Welcome home." ”

Then there was a moment of silence between you. He sat nervously in the chair across from him, saw the pizza on the table, and instinctively wanted to pick up a piece and eat it. Even though it was already completely cold.

But you stopped his hand: "It's cold...... Go to the kitchen and warm it up before you eat. ”

Spamton let out a soft "Oh." Even though he was back so late, he was still waiting for him here, and he felt a bit guilty.

"I did this voluntarily. I wanted to wait for you to come back." You just forced a smile, now feeling a bit relieved. It turned out you had bet right...... He would come back.

"I'll definitely be back. You're just too tired waiting here all this time......" He instinctively wanted to argue, but before he could finish, you interrupted him.

"No, I'm not tired." You shake your head. He's here waiting here every day...... For him, the daily waiting and restlessness must have been exhausting.

But for some reason, that restless feeling vanished the moment you saw him. You probably think he was the same.

Spamton shut his mouth and said nothing more, just picked up his plate and headed to the kitchen, then noticed the bucket of flowers you placed on the counter.

His expression stiffened for a moment. At that moment, he was relieved to have turned his back to you, so you couldn't see his expression.

Spamton's voice was a bit dry as he asked, "Why did you buy this bucket of flower arrangements?" ”

"Ah...... I wanted to go shopping on the commercial street today. I saw that the shops were all displaying these flower arrangements from trash bins, and they even advertised. "You glanced at the flower arrangement bucket, a bit puzzled as to why Spamton's tone was so stiff now. Maybe he wasn't happy seeing other Addison sell items with the same idea as him?"

"If you don't like me, just put it away...... Oh, right, so where did you go today? That blue Addison said you didn't go to work. I went to the garbage heap to find you, but I didn't see you. ”

You see him shake his head, firmly but with a hint of panic: "No, no need, flower arrangements look pretty nice in the living room...... As for where I went, it's a bit late. Can we talk about this tomorrow?" For some reason, his tone carried a hint of caution.

You nodded matter-of-factly: "Of course...... Speaking of which, the flower arranging business should be pretty good, right? ”

Since it's almost time to sleep, let's casually find some topics to chat.

"Hmm...... Otherwise, why would they steal my business! Spamton's voice rose again; he was probably in the kitchen, and his voice sounded a bit muffled.

It's fake—actually, only you—and the only one among the people of light who bought this flower arrangement.

But you don't know, just yawn, lie face down on the table, and fall asleep again.
