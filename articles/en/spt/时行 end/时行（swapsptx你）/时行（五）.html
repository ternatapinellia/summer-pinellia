You returned home as usual. Indeed, having to chat with Addison and analyze the truth about past events is exhausting for you. You just leaned wearily on the sofa, watching Spamton unusually serve you tea and pat your shoulders on the sofa, which made you want to laugh a little—though you can't say he was exceptionally attentive; rather, it was usually the same. Spamton’s action of patting your shoulder stiffened for a moment. If he weren’t able to tell that your tone was purely curious, he might have thought that you actually knew something.

"What are you talking about...... Of course I haven't done anything to betray you! If I really did, I'd rather punch myself......" His voice sounded a bit pitiful, sounding aggrieved as he said this, though the first half was pretending to be relaxed. You could tell he was speaking sincerely, but Spamton seemed nervous, so much so you wanted to tease him for a moment.

“Ah, I see…” you dragged out your voice, looking back at Spamton, and Spamton quickly nodded in confirmation.

You cupped his cheek, sighed softly, and gently stroked his cheek: "You don't need to do that. Besides, I trust you. You won't do anything to betray me." "These words come from the heart. You truly believe this. You trust Spamton...... It seems like the first time since that time in the Light World that you've trusted anyone other than yourself so much. You were slightly distracted, but quickly gathered your thoughts and gently stroked his face once more.

"But...... So, you can't tell me you're hiding something from me, are you?" You tilted your head. You're pretty sure Spamton hasn't told you the whole truth, but you don't really care......
