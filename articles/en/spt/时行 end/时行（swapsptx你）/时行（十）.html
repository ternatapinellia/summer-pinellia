Time Practices (Ten)

[10]

Following the surging crowd, you sighed, held your head high, and walked out of the subway station. Looking at the familiar yet unfamiliar street scene, the people passing by were still chatting and gossiping, and the street vendors’ calls were still so attention-grabbing. You just lowered your head, your trembling hands unlocking your phone, looking at the wallpaper which was still a photo of Spamton and you, both smiling broadly. You couldn't help but take a deep breath, reinforce your conviction, and move forward.

Walking through the crowd, your pace quickens and faster. Just a little faster, a little faster, you don't want him to be left alone for too long, and ...... What if you drag on for too long and he can no longer get help?

You fiddled with the keys in your pocket and suddenly started running down the street. Some people looked at you in surprise, and you overheard the neighbors gossiping, saying you must be some kind of young lady, so ill-mannered, and didn't even know to stop and apologize properly.

Indeed, you only had time to quickly say sorry and then dash forward. You only had time to glance around quickly to make sure you hadn't gone the wrong way. Until you, panting and holding your knees, stood at the doorstep—no, that was already your old home. You were panting heavily at the door of your old home, your hand trembling as you reached out, and after several times, you inserted the key into the keyhole, then gently twisted it, and the door creaked open.

First and foremost was the sound of the coffee machine spinning at high speed, along with the constant spinning of the soy milk machine nearby. The noisy noise made you frown, and then you opened the door and walked in.

Your parents were sitting at the table enjoying dinner, then faced your mother at the door and saw you, who was the first to confront you: "You still know to come back? After graduating from college, you ran off without a word, didn't reply to messages or answer your calls. I told you long ago that after graduation, you could just get married right away. Mom has watched over several promising local youths for you, but you didn't say a word. Now look, those promising youths each have their own families. What will Mom do now—"

You frown, a wave of irritation rising in your heart. You look at the vase on the counter at the door, wanting to suddenly throw it at your mother's head. But your good sense of morality still lets you hold back—you tell yourself you don't need to say a word, just endure, just endure and get through it.

And you clench your fists, just walking toward your own room.

My mother suddenly raised her voice: "What's your attitude toward your mom? You just say hello when you get home and don't call her?" So rude—"

Her father quickly spoke up: "Good girl, didn't we agree? After you graduate from college, you can stay at home. You can continue with your hobbies and interests. Our family can afford to support you, but you really should consider other ......."

"And even if you get married, after a long day of work, you can still draw your lousy drawings, do whatever you want. Why don't you even listen to your parents—" your mother grumbled, then followed behind and said another sentence.

You shook your head, a bit annoyed, but didn't plan to talk back. It was never agreed; they just insisted you should go home after graduation, since your major wasn't suitable for working outside anyway.

They never negotiated; they just used the pretense of negotiability to push you to offer an offer you couldn't refuse.

In the end, you just put your hand on the doorknob and replied calmly, "Mm." Of course, it was just a perfunctory response. Then you hurriedly opened your own door, entered, and locked it, blocking out their cursing outside the door.

Soon after, you hear your parents arguing again, but that doesn't matter anymore. You just hurriedly walk over and turn on your computer, feeling a rare sense of comfort and relaxation in the quiet of the room.

While waiting for the computer to turn on, you might daydream, thinking that marrying those promising young men your mother mentioned is definitely not a good choice. They always hope you achieve greater things, yet criticize the small mistakes you make in daily life. Once, you only felt wronged because of this, but later, you listened coldly, skillfully closing the door to briefly block out the noise — until you reached the dark world, and that noise finally faded away.

Think about it, if you really want to get married, your partner might be spamton—wait, why do you think that?

You clutch your chest in confusion, feeling your heart racing up, and you can't care about anything else, because the computer has just turned on.

You skillfully connect to the internet, then open your email, dig through the trash bin, dig through the spam folder, and choose to restore each folder one by one.

Think about it, these emails were probably sent to you by Spamton—after all, the subject lines of these emails all say [Limited Time Offer] [50% OFF].

And some more...... If you accidentally click in, you'll see emails about [valued at only $4.99] and [Bodybuilding Physique], and some are [Only 250 Dark Coins] [Limited-time special offer].

You, unusually patient, calmly clicked through emails one by one, checked them, and then restored them. Fortunately, they weren't deleted forever.

Some emails feature promotions about coffee, some contain fake potted flowers, and others contain all kinds of imitation paintings—without exception, these emails are spam and ignored.

You restored each of these emails one by one, then breathed a sigh of relief, rekindling hope in your heart—you hoped that when you returned, you could see Spamton still alive and well, standing there perfectly fine. If ...... If the Dark People lost their carrier and could no longer survive, then you hoped you could provide them with a small carrier. You hoped that these restored, deleted, and broken spam emails could be pieced together into a complete Spamton.

And just as you recover all your emails and are about to shut down your computer and return to the Dark World, you hear a "ding" sound, indicating that a new email has arrived.

Check your inbox. Time...... It's just now, and the subject of this email is: Hello, do you need a [100% success rate] [Confession card]?

Your hand trembled as you swallowed twice—you knew this email was sent to you by Spamton, probably because your computer was turned on, so you received it. Your heart was beating faster and faster. Did you have some kind of cure-all-in-the-middle illness? Anyway, the mouse moved, and I still clicked on the email.

[Hello, may I ask if you need a [100% success rate] and a [confession card]?

Dear Ms. Y/N, we sincerely thank you for your willingness to order the [SPAM] service.

We are now promoting our store with a 100% positive review rate, a limited-time special confession card!

The content is: [I love you], this love is [known by everyone].

I dedicate all my loyalty and passion to the respected and dear Miss Y/N.

I hope to spend this life and grow old together with you.

This [Confession Card] is currently priced at only 520 Dark Coins! ]

You swallowed hard, staring blankly at the email. Clutching your chest, you felt the pounding of your heart. Suddenly, you understood—

You murmured, "Ah, so ...... I'm not sick at all. ”

You just love him, and it seems you love him very much. You don't really know what love is...... But you think that perhaps this is how it should feel.

You wiped away the tears streaming down your eyes, looked at the letter carefully once again, then smiled slightly and silently answered in your heart, "Yes, I love you too."

Then you shut down your computer, reopened the door, and walked out.

You ignore your parents' angry voices, ignore those voices that seem to be for your own good but actually control and oppress you, ignore the noise in the room, and ignore the noise of the crowd. At this moment, all you can hear is the sound of your heart beating in your chest. All you can think of is that letter, Spamton's smiling face, that he might be carefully but smiling as he prepares this card, his excited expression when the flowers bloom and his joy as he can't help but hug you, his nervous, anticipatory look as he carefully serves you freshly made desserts, his eyes shining as he rubs against your palm, and the occasional time when you come home late, He hugged you tightly, as if afraid of something. Thinking of you sleeping half-awake in the middle of the night with a fever, seeing Spamton sleeping on your bedside, and how he had gone several kilometers away to queue for two hours just to bring you a snack because of a single sentence......

With these memories, you step off the subway, walk against the crowd, ignore their discussions, and return to the boundary between light and darkness, then, following the blinding white light and a wave of dizziness, you return to the dark world.

The roadside addison disappeared, and you hurried back to the place where the garbage pile was, only to find that the road to the icy cliff, filled with withered lavender and heavy snow, had also vanished.

With a mixture of nervousness and slight excitement, you returned home. It was already very late. You opened the door to your house. It was bright inside, and there was Spamton sitting idly at the dining table, writing your name on a piece of paper in boredom, waiting for you.

When he saw you return, his eyes suddenly lit up. He ran over and hugged you, and you instinctively opened your arms to catch him.

Spamton's fluffy head rubbed against your neck, as if complaining and acting spoiled, "Why are you only coming back...... I almost thought you weren't coming back. That scared me, I called you but didn't answer...... And didn't reply to your messages. Hmm, I replied to your texts, you can take a look! ”

He spoke and then raised his head as if seeking approval, his eyes sparkling as he looked at you. Perhaps it was just an illusion, but he seemed a little shorter, more like he was during the earlier Addison period.

You take out your phone and see that he really did send you a message, basically saying he's sorry he was busy and didn't reply. Now he's home, but he hasn't seen you and asks where you've been.

You rubbed his head and softened your voice: "Mm, I'm back...... As for where you asked where I went, I can answer now—I went to the world of light. ”

Spamton's body stiffened, and he stammered, "You...... What are you doing in the Light World? ”

You lowered your gaze and didn't answer his question, instead of asking, "During the time you didn't reply to my messages...... What were you doing? ”

Spamton was a bit flustered. He clasped his hands behind his back and lowered his head, not daring to look at you. He knew very well that he was lying.

"Nothing...... It's just that a few people of light visited the advertising world, and I'll handle them. "Of course, it's not entirely a lie.

You were silent for a while, just lightly and lightly. Spamton couldn't hear the emotion in your words; he only knew you said one sentence: "Is that so?" ”

…… Well, for some reason, he was a bit afraid you'd be angry, he ...... Still afraid you'd abandon him because of those lies? Spamton was a bit stunned, then remembered you had promised him you would never truly reject him, so he finally lifted his head, puffing out his cheeks, a bit aggrieved and angry.

“…… I really was dealing with those Lightfolk, but later they wanted to seal the dark fountain that made up this dark world, so I stopped them...... The Howling Knight doesn't keep his word! I clearly held off those Lightfolks for quite a while! But in the end, he still slashed me twice! "As he spoke, he tugged at his collar and showed you those two wounds, just like the sealed mail seals, but now the wounds had become extremely faint.

Your fingers gently stroked those two wounds, then you lowered your head and softly blew a couple of breaths on them. Spamton was stunned for a moment, his cheeks instinctively flushed, his eyes darting left and right: "I...... It doesn't hurt anymore. ”

So, you don't need to blow on the pain anymore; it can fly away.

But you just touched the wound and said seriously, "But it must have hurt a lot at the time. I hope...... This can soothe the pain in your memories. So what happened afterward? How could your injury be okay and come home? ”

Spamton was stunned for a moment, but still answered instinctively, "Mm...... I don't know what happened either. All I know is that I've been lying in that snowy patch for a very, very long time, and I'm almost losing consciousness...... In a daze, the two separate halves rejoined again, and my mind returned a bit. Although there were still scars, I was much better......

You patted his head: "So in this situation, Spammy insisted on going home to prepare dinner for me?" Spammy is very strong, truly impressive. "You squinted slightly and scratched his chin again. Spamton instinctively squinted in response to your movement, making a purring sound. Then he suddenly opened his eyes, looked up at you, and his tone was a bit fierce: "No, don't think you can change the subject just because of this. You haven't answered my question yet!" ”

But...... You don't seem to need to answer his question? After all, between you two...... Is it necessary to know these details? Spamton was stunned for a moment, his heartbeat suddenly erratic. Once again, he didn't dare look at you, just lowered his head.

You just sighed, patted his head, then tried hard to hold back the tears in your eyes and suddenly hugged Spamton.

Your voice was still tinged with tears: "I saw everything, I saw everything...... You lay alone in the snow. I thought going back to the world of light might help you get better, so I went back. As it turned out, you really did get better." Your words were fairly calm, but Spamton's eyes also turned red.

He hugged you tightly as well, his voice still a bit stammering: "But...... But? Don't you really dislike the Light World...... "After all, although you are a citizen of Light, you have never mentioned the Light World. Spamton naturally assumed you hated the Light World very much, and of course, he never mentioned it in front of you.

“I really dislike the Light World, but you’re more important… so, going back once for you is worth it.” As you said this, you buried your head in Spamton’s fluffy neck and shoulders, rubbing away the tears in your eyes. Spamton could feel a bit of wetness on his shoulder, and your voice was muffled.

He fell silent for a moment, opened his mouth but didn't know what to say, and finally just smiled and said, "Ah, Y/N is really amazing, like saving me again—though I don't know how you did it, the reason I'm standing here fine is because of Y/N! I like you the most! ”

The first ...... was to take him out of the garbage heap. From then on, his destiny changed, like a flower blooming with new vitality. And this time...... Once again, he escaped the environment that only made him cry, helpless, and powerless to escape.

Maybe this sentence was just a casual remark, or maybe it was ...... Every time it was sincere, his heart pounding, every time, every time it was like this. And seeing you just respond with a hum after hearing that, Spamton would only suppress his downward mood, lift his face again, and smile at you.

But this time, you only gently patted his back and softly said, 'Mm, I like spammy the most too, no... I love you the most.' You finally responded to a line that sounded somewhat like a joke, yet also somewhat like a heartfelt confession. This time, you chose to believe what he said, you chose... to believe in an unknown promise.

You are willing to re-entrust this unknown emotion, perhaps called "love," to Spamton.

Spamton's face flushed again. He was relieved that they were embracing at this moment. You couldn't see his expression, but you could still feel his body stiffening slightly.

"Ah...... Huh? Mm, I, I love you. "He felt a burning heat at this moment, his mind almost crashed, and he simply didn't know what he was saying. He thought this time the answer would be another 'mm'...... But now, his heart was blooming with joy, just holding you tightly, feeling the sudden joy and pleasure rising in his chest. The pounding sound in his chest seemed to pierce through his skin, and that sincere and warm emotion enveloped him, so much so that at this moment, Spamton only felt sweetness, even if it was just a simple hug.

"Y/N is the best...... I love you the most. "He murmured, repeating the sentence. His head rubbed against your neck too, fluffy and a bit ticklish.

And you let go of his tight embrace: "I know, I like you the most. Our Spammy is the best—I just tried to restore those spam ads in the trash bin, and now you can stand here again. That's great." ”

As you spoke, you picked up the food on the table, planning to heat it up yourself.

Spamton patted his cheeks, which had not yet fully lost his warmth, and followed behind you into the kitchen: "Wait a moment, let me take care of things!" Y/N, you just need to sit there! ”

His heart was still pounding, although he was also skeptical about whether the Dark People really had such a thing...... But if he said it existed, then it did! The corners of his mouth couldn't help but curl up. Now...... In other words, his existence as a Dark Tribe in the form or carrier of the Light World...... was actually the spam mail you had restored. Only then did he remember that, when he was unconscious, he had once tried sending mass advertising emails to users of the Light World. Of course, he also knew one of them had been sent to your computer. Even though your computer hadn't been turned on for a long time and hadn't been connected to the internet for a long time......

So, he must have voluntarily chosen those spam emails as carriers. Even though it's just spam...... But you really value all of it.

Spamton quietly lowered his head, picked up the tray, and walked toward the kitchen, while you didn’t notice his expression, just raising your voice to respond: “I also need to do something. Spammy should be sitting there and resting properly, you’ve obviously suffered a serious injury…”

Spamton insisted, "But Y/N made a special trip to the Light World just for me, which must have been tough too...... You're tired too, right? Leave these things to me! ”

You two held out for a while, then looked at each other and suddenly burst out laughing.

In the end, you all just did some work as usual.

At the dinner table, Spamton hesitated for a moment, but finally spoke up. This time, his voice was somewhat serious: "Y/N...... Although I was happy and felt it was inappropriate to talk about these sad things at a time like this, I still had to say, this world is about to disappear. ”

You stopped eating, just looking at Spamton with a hint of confusion: "Are you going to disappear?" "But you're still sitting here perfectly fine?"

He cleared his throat: "Not today, not now, but in a week...... I made a deal with those Lightfolk. I won't stop them from sealing the Dark Fountain, but on the other hand, they won't be able to act until a week later. ”

As he spoke, his voice grew quieter: "So...... I'm no longer the advertiser. After seven days, when this dark world completely disappears, I won't be anymore. ”

"But you'll always be spamton, so it's okay." You just curved your eyebrows slightly.

Spamton was stunned for a moment. He lowered his head, not letting you see his reddened eyes: "Mm...... Mm! I promised beforehand that I would come see your art exhibition every day, but I missed my appointment and didn't go today...... So I specifically took a week to properly visit your exhibition! ”

"Alright, I'll introduce everything to you in detail, one by one. But after this week, where are we going to go? ”

Spamton pouted, clearly not very happy: "Specifically, the other two Lightfolk will tell you later. Anyway, from now on, we'll all have to move to Castle Town to live." ”

"Alright, I know...... Then when the time comes, you and I will both have to find new jobs, right? "You think seriously about this question.

Spamton just nudged your arm: "Let's talk about this later. For now, focus on your meal, or the food will get cold later!" ”

You understand, this is just a very forced excuse to change the subject...... Maybe Spamton is still more or less worried about the future? But you haven't exposed this, since in the days to come, you'll always be there for him, no matter what happens.

So you just smiled and softened your voice, replying, "Alright, alright." ”

After dinner, as usual, Spamton went to the kitchen with the dishes to wash them, while you sat on the living room sofa, lost in thought, thinking about the future and what to do next. At that moment, your phone lit up, and you realized it was a message from the gallery owner.

You glanced at your phone screen—it was telling you that the gallery would close in seven days, and you'd have to settle your wages for the month. You know, it's because this dark world is about to disappear that the gallery will close. You put away your phone and let out a gentle breath.

And the next morning, Spamton was syncing with you, sticking with you all the way to the gallery. He said righteously, "Anyway, this dark world is about to disappear, and he's no longer the owner of ads—he's basically an unemployed vagrant. Of course, he can go wherever he wants!"

You followed him into the gallery, and at first he just excitedly shouted, "I'm about to see the painting Y/N drew for me...... I'm so nervous. I wonder if many people of darkness will come to see ......."

It wasn’t until he entered the gallery that he hushed, eyes wide, staring at the paintings on the wall—he thought there would only be a few; he thought you would display the other past works as well, since Spamton believed those paintings should also be seen by the other Dark World residents. But he didn’t know—you had actually made so many, all starring him. As far as the eye could see, there were all kinds of… his silhouettes, pieces of life he shared with you.

He quietly walked forward, the first painting ...... He still had some impressions—it was that day when he was tinkering with the flowers, and you drew it on a whim—probably the first painting. Later on, some were scenes of him busy in the kitchen, some dozing off on the sofa, some grinding coffee, and some of his sparkling eyes showing off the newly made desserts. Spamton didn't even know...... When did you record all this? It was only then that he realized that you had seen everything.

After all, there are two more paintings, probably just your imagination—the first shows Spamton in a conference hall, directly commanding the other Dark People. Of course, this meeting room is just your imagination, completely different from the one he was in.

The second painting was of him proudly patrolling the shopping street. The small shop that used to be white Addison had been closed, and he was standing there, seemingly pointing and talking about other Addison shops.

Spamton turned his back to you, he's a bit afraid to turn his head to look at you, but his voice was a bit dry: "...... When did you paint it? How come I have no idea at all? ”

You rubbed your chin and thought for a moment: "Most of it was drawn in my own room...... After all, you've never seen it in my room before, so it's normal not to know, right?" "Except for that time, when you brought Spamton into your room, he hasn't gone in since. You know, that's respecting your privacy. Of course, you never tried to find out what exactly he was preparing, after all...... You would respect his thoughts, no matter how curious you are, you wouldn't sneak a peek!

Spamton remained silent, just walking down the corridor filled with paintings, until he finally stopped in front of that ...... Your family portrait. The term 'family portrait' was actually Spamton's own name. After all, your family only had you as a citizen of light and a person of darkness as well.

He looked at himself in the painting, smiling carefreely, and suddenly smiled too. He turned around and smiled at you: "I have an idea. Right in front of this painting, how about we take another photo together?" ”

You nod as if it were only natural: "Of course you can." ”

You might think, maybe he also wants to leave a little memory in this disappearing advertising world.

The store manager volunteered to step forward, holding an instant camera to take photos of you.

So Spamton puts his arm around your shoulder, and together with you, smiles at the camera, taking a precious photo together—of course, his current height has lost the advantage of being the master of this dark world, and he's reverted to the same state as he did in Addison, so he has to stand on tiptoe just to put his arm around your shoulder.

You suppress your laughter, and when you see other Addisons also come to the gallery to view the exhibition, you wave to them, and they come closer, gathering around you.

Pink looked impatient, but still said, "Alright, you want to take a photo together, right? We happened to be here today." ”

Blue glanced at Pink, then at Spamton: "I was clearly worried Spamton would come to the gallery......" But before he could finish, Yellow covered his mouth.

"Well, looking at it now, it actually seems like a good thing." Orange tried to smooth things over, skillfully sidling with Spamton.

You smiled, hugged Spamton, resting your head on his, with the other four Addisons surrounding you. Then, click, a photo was taken, captured as a keepsake, developed from the instant camera.

Of course, this group photo was copied four times and given to the other four Addisons. Spamton was a bit reluctant but still gave it away. And for your group photo, you used that family portrait as the background, framed in a photo frame, and placed it in the most prominent spot in the living room.

A week isn't long either, but not too short. The art exhibition ended, the gallery closed, and this dark world was about to disappear. In the end, Spamton hesitantly tugged at your sleeve and whispered, "...... Go to the shopping street. I told them that when they arrive at the time of the transaction, a Lightfolk will come looking for them. ”

You nod and head to the shopping street, where waiting for you are two Dark People, one human, and the other ...... Seems like monsters? You're not sure either, since the community you live in is still mostly human.

The purple girl who looked a bit like a dinosaur greeted you cheerfully, while the other blue boy didn't say a word.

She tells you that all the Dark People can live in Castle Town; you just need to move the corresponding objects from the Light World there.

You suddenly realize that you have left this dark world, and they have sealed the dark fountain of this world. You can no longer enter this dark world.

Feeling a bit disappointed? Indeed...... But with a bit of anticipation and a smile, you set out again on the road back to your original home. You no longer cared about those voices; after all, none of them mattered to you anymore.

You push open the door again, and your parents' gazes immediately turn toward you.

His mother was the first to shout: "You've called so many times and you still won't answer. I told you, you're old enough to find a place for your future. I've worried so much that my hair turned white over you, and you don't even glance at me. You must feel sorry for your parents!" ”

My father sighed too: "We've worried about you since we were little. Whatever you want, we haven't given it to you...... Look at this room—aren't these all the things you've been loudly trying to get? And only when you go to a good family can you focus on painting, right? ”

You ignore their voices, just walk into your room, bring out that computer, and you know Spamton survives on the spam stored in your mailbox.

When you walk out of the bedroom, your mother's voice suddenly rises: "Why won't you listen at all! We're doing this for your own good......"

You stopped in your tracks and turned to look at them. Although you tried hard to stay calm, your voice was still choked: "No, you ...... It's not love for me at all. You say it's for my own good, but it's really just to satisfy ...... Your own vanity. ”

Mother opened her mouth, her voice sharp: "Not at all—"

And you interrupted her: "That's it. You don't love me, you're just manipulating me. You don't see me as a child, a child worthy of your love, so I don't need parents like you either; I no longer need your love." ”

Sobbing and choking up, you feel your vision blur, and you just step back toward the door, smiling: "I won't come back. This isn't my home either. I'm going back to my own home now." ”

There is spamton, sunshine, smiles, eyes that always wait for you and anticipate you, voices that affirm you no matter what, your lover and your eternal family.

You closed the door, shutting out your parents' noise, feeling a sense of relief in your heart, and your steps becoming lighter. You beamed with a smile, leaving the past behind. You were about to walk toward your own future. This time, it was a future entirely decided by you—no scolding from your parents, no manipulation after superficial discussion. Now, you were going home.

You arrive at the address the purple girl gave you, put the computer inside, and then arrive in Castle Town. Having just experienced dizziness, you open your eyes when Spamton hugs you tightly.

"Welcome home!" Even though you were just standing on the streets of Castle Town, while other Dark Folk passing by were looking at you strangely, Addison only chuckled. The employees originally from the advertising world passed by, gave you and Spamton a flirtatious look, and then walked off again.

And you just sniffed, hugged Spamton back, and buried your head in his neck: "Mm, I'm back." ”

That day, while you were tidying up your new residence in Castle Town, you found the artwork Spamton had once shown you, along with some unsold fake flowers. You smiled and carefully stored those things away in the cabinet. As for that slightly wilted sunflower...... You took good care of it and placed it on the windowsill. As the sunflower grew and bloomed facing the sun, it was as if a small seed had been planted in your heart, growing and blooming.

Spamton, for some reason, transplanted those flowers to the square in the center of Castle Town, confidently surprised you, and solemnly took out a pot of roses he had grown to confess to you.

That sea of flowers still remains in the square, and you have simply returned home with Spamton. The future is still long, very long, and you will walk into the future together with Spamton.

So...... Welcome home.
