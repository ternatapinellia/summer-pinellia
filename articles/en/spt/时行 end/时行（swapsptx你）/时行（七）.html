[7]

Spamton had to admit, what you said was indeed somewhat useful—whether it was that full-spectrum plant lamp or the proposed operation to cooperate with other Dark World distributors. After all, he was sitting in his office at this moment, seriously pondering the issue.

…… To be honest, he doesn't want to sell a single copy; he wants to collect everything about you himself. But he also knows that you do this for the sake of his career—there's no real career at all; it's just imitating the Light folk playing house, but he does feel a bit proud and self-satisfied from it.

While muttering to himself, Spamton still set a time for the meeting.

He was the last to enter the meeting room. The other Dark People had already arrived, whispering about what unreasonable request the boss was about to make this time. Then Spamton walked in, and all the Dark People quieted down, looking at each other, afraid that Spamton would catch them speaking up.

After all, Spamton's face was expressionless at the moment, looking quite intimidating. No one knew who had provoked him.

He was still a bit reluctant to share your stuff—but when he took the main seat, his expression immediately changed, looking cheerful or proud: "Now I have some great news to tell you—"

All the Dark People fell silent, each pondering what he intended to say.

“…… That is, we can now cultivate plants from the true Light World in the Dark World!" Spamton said, raising a finger to the sky and proudly announcing the event.

"How is that possible? The environment of the dark world itself isn't suitable for ......," pink instinctively wanted to argue, but Spamton interrupted him.

"Don't argue with me. There's no need to object; what I say is the truth." Spamton raised his head and raised his voice to announce the matter, since he wanted all the Dark People to know.

In fact, this unwritten rule had long been in place. All the Darkfolk under his rule knew it, except for his former colleagues. Addison occasionally argued with him, which greatly displeased Spamton.

Pink fell silent, though he still felt a bit resentful. He remembered it—Spamton was now all high and mighty, nothing had happened, yet he still called on the People of Light to back him up. Now, almost every Citizen of Light in the advertising world knows that Citizen of Light. After all, Spamton likes to show off his own Citizens of Light so what?

"Anyway, my family's Lightfolk are amazing! My Y/N is the best! This full-spectrum plant lamp was developed by her, but only a few of them are available." Spamton enthusiastically introduced it, and took the opportunity to bring out a pot of plants. The plant was thriving—it was a sunflower, but without any light, its petals closed.

When Spamton took out a full-spectrum plant lamp and skillfully turned on a few switches, the sunflower slowly began to bloom, revealing petals that revealed the sun-facing center, even though it was now only directed at that plant lamp.

…… Actually, there were originally ten portions, but due to his selfishness, he withheld several portions. He planned to carefully nurture a few more pots of flowers, and this time he definitely wouldn't let them die!

He wanted to grow some lavender and forget-me-nots, as mentioned before...... Ah, maybe tulips and roses too.

Spamton wants to carefully cultivate a lavender sea again. This time, there are no fake flowers—he's going to give you a real surprise.

Spamton also wants to cultivate some roses well, so he can happily present the pot to you, tell you he's succeeded, tell you...... He probably likes you.

Because Spamton does not want to leave you, just as light and darkness actually coexist; without light, where would darkness come from? So...... Darkness exists by relying on light, irreplaceable, and Spamton depends on you to survive as it is now.

Because he likes how shining you are, because he wants all the Dark People to know you're powerful, that you're strong—but deep down, he doesn't want the other Dark Folk to know just how good you really are. He doesn't want them to steal you away.

What does 'like' mean, and what does 'love' mean? He doesn't know; he just knows that when he's with you, Spamton vaguely thinks that's probably what love feels like, since he feels light and warm throughout.

So he feels that he probably, no, definitely, he definitely likes you.

He lowered his brows and eyes without speaking, while the Dark People below started making a fuss. Spamton deliberately didn't make a sound to stop them; he wanted the noise to last a little longer, even though his heart was unexpectedly quiet now. And none of the outside noise could disturb his calm or the warmth that came back when he thought of you.

…… Maybe it's only when he thinks of you that he becomes so quiet. Spamton's lips couldn't help but lift. Blue, who had been silent all along, noticed this, but only watched.

Spamton remembered that day again. It was the day after he took the full-spectrum plant lamp from you. To be honest, Spamton always thinks you're amazing, and he's genuinely praising you—whether it's gardening or painting—to him, it's just an art aspect he has no access to, something he can't grasp.

But he can feel genuine beauty in it, feel the effort you put in, and feel warmth from it. So Spamton knows it must be beautiful.

But he also knew that only he was the one from the Dark People expressing affirmation...... You probably wouldn't fully believe it, even if he was already the master of the Dark World and the boss of the advertising world, you might not fully believe it. So Spamton wanted you to hold an art exhibition; he wanted more and more Dark People to see you and see how shining you were.

Then he could brag that this was their Y/N, their Light Folk were really powerful!

But at that time, even though he was thinking about all this mess, he didn't say much. He just carefully brought back the already withered flower he had brought back from the office—a pot of flowers he had almost nurtured to death again.

However, when the lamp was turned on, the sunflower unfolded its petals again, revealing a warm and soft inner self. Just like him, in front of you, it also reveals its soft inner self.

He stared blankly at the pot of flowers and asked, "In that case...... Just illuminate all the flowers, or rather, just place a lamp in the middle of a cluster of flowers, wouldn't that be enough? ”

Your mouth twitched, feeling like this was some kind of greenhouse cultivation—well, not entirely, just a bit similar. You cleared your throat, feeling you should answer seriously, but your words inevitably showed a hint of amusement.

Because you find his serious demeanor surprisingly cute.

"Ahem...... Even a single lamp couldn't cover such a large area! If the equipment were big enough, it could indeed be done, but this is just a small lamp—how could it have such a big effect? ”

"And precisely because it's small enough, the coverage isn't that big, so it's easier to sell more." You wink at him innocently and smile.

Spamton was taken aback, quickly understanding what you meant, and couldn't help but laugh along.

"That's perfect—then, for a scale of this size, we'll sell for a thousand Dark Coins. If it's a large ...... Then ten million Dark Coins! Spamton shook his finger and started talking nonsense.

You didn't break his imagination. Even though you thought buying ten million Dark Coins and the Dark People would be a fool, a fantasy, you just clapped and smiled: "That's right, that's exactly what I meant. If it were large, it would definitely be even more expensive." ”

Spamton's thoughts were pulled back again. He was a bit unhappy about the person interrupting his memories, but seeing it was Blue, he felt embarrassed to lose his temper. As the master of advertising, he wouldn't get angry with a patient!

He was already taught by you yesterday, and now he's super proud thinking he's done very well.

"So, what is the price of this lamp?" Blue simply asked calmly.

He felt a bit helpless. If it weren't for the growing voices of the Dark People at the scene, and if some pippins had even sneaked up betting while Spamton was lost in thought or even sneaking in from below, Blue wouldn't know why they were still thinking about gambling at a time like this—especially how long it would take for Spamton to say something next.

…… In fact, the reason he was now calling out for Spamton was because Orange, who loved drama, dragged him into the gamble too. And Blue definitely didn't want to lose.

Orange winked at him, while Pink glared at him, probably because he was also involved. But since Blue spoke up, he probably lost the bet—Blue was just pretending not to notice.

"Only five units...... Each Dark Citizen is limited to buying one. As for the price, let's set it at 10,000 Dark Coins per lamp! Spamton's eyes darted around as he popped up a large pop-up displaying the price, which also showed "50% off."

"Not only that, but I want to tell everyone this is a limited-time offer. If it expires, it will return to the retail price of 20,000 Dark Coins in a week."

Then he popped up another pop-up window, countless arrows pointing to the image of the full-spectrum plant lamp he had imagined.

"If you can't catch the first generation, you can wait for the second, but the second generation's functionality isn't as strong as the first, so it was only sold for 5,000 Dark Coins. It's also a limited-time first-week discount, and after that, the original price will return to 10,000."

He loudly announced that the price he told you was obviously lying...... Now was the retail price he had truly considered!

The employees looked around, glanced at each other, then nodded randomly. After all, the price and such were set by Spamton, the big boss, and had nothing to do with them.

Spamton saw that all the employees below nodded in agreement, with no objections, and nodded in satisfaction. He said so, there would never be anyone among the Dark People who thought their decision was bad!

Of course, there are certainly some Dark People who think this way, but for them, they simply don't have the courage to challenge Spamton's opinion.

But Spamton still remembers what you said. You pinched his cheek and told him, since you're already the master of the advertising world and a big boss, you should carefully ask the employees if they have any opinions. If they just don't dare to say anything—you wouldn't go to work with him, and of course you wouldn't be able to find his mistakes in time. And you always feel that sometimes Spamton will keep things from you and won't tell the truth.

At this moment, he was smugly thinking that he had done quite well, and none of the employees below objected at all! But he still obediently listened to you and decided to ask again.

So he just cleared his throat and swaggered, "Ahem, do you have any suggestions? If there are no objections, the meeting is adjourned. Next up is this promotional plan!" He clapped his hands and announced.

At this moment, Blue raised his hand again: "I think, if it's the second batch, the pricing might be a bit high. Maybe we should lower the price?" Not many Dark People have savings in Dark Coins. ”

Spamton's face immediately fell, knowing he shouldn't have asked. That way, he could go home and show off to you how right his decision was, but not a single Dark Civilian objected.

He opened his mouth, but before he could answer, Orange raised his hand and gave his answer: "I think, if the second batch of lights is set at 10,000 Dark Coins, then we should give them more time to discount, or at least give them an extra month before sales start so those Darkfolk can reserve and purchase, and after the official sale starts, give them another month of promotional time. That way, those Darkfolk can accumulate enough Dark Coins, and then we can all make a profit from them." Orange said this while winking and winking.

Spamton puffed out his cheeks, wanting to arrogantly say that doing exactly as he said would be fine, but he had to admit his former colleague did have some point...... And he remembered what you said—he needed to listen to the opinions of other employees.

To be honest, he actually didn't want to do it; it was only because you said that he was reluctantly willing to give it a try. But now Spamton realized that this did have some benefits, since there were still some things he didn't do well. Although being a dictator is great and comfortable, after all! But he also wants to do better. As long as he becomes more impressive, you'll definitely be drowned in all kinds of boasting and exaggerated praise. Just thinking about this makes Spamton feel delighted.

So at this moment, Spamton just waved his hand impatiently: "You make a good point...... Then let's adjust according to your proposal. First, lower the price from 10,000 Dark Coins to 5,000 Dark Coins. Next, run a one-month promotional event. During the promotion period, pre-orders will be priced at the 1,000 Dark Coins. Once the promotion ends, after the product launches, there will be a month-long promotion, and then the price will be set at 2,500 Dark Coins!" "Anyway, it doesn't really matter how many Dark Coins you set at the price. Everything is up to him," Spamton thought, hands on hips. However, after all, most of the Dark Coins owned by other Dark People in the advertising world are actually under his control, so it doesn't matter if he controls all of this.

Then Spamton waved his hand, signaling the meeting to end. The employees swarmed out of the meeting room, where the pippins were still whispering, discussing how the boss had changed his temper these past few days. Not only did they hardly bother with their gambling—otherwise, they wouldn't have started gambling so brazenly while Spamton was daydreaming. They were sure Spamton must have known, just ignored them.

But it didn't matter—it just meant their lives had gotten better, and they could be more uninhibited. So those pippins just happily jumped out, Shadow Guy was happily playing tunes, extremely excited, and this time it was a stirring saxophone track. Zapper, on the other hand, just bounced a few times without saying much.

Spamton just sat for a while in the now-quiet meeting room, and he started missing you again, because when he was alone, he only felt bored or even afraid, completely alone, as if he had been abandoned by all the Dark People. No, he would never be like before, alone and alone. He knew he had a home, and he knew you were always there waiting for him. He knew...... He probably knew, or rather, he insisted on thinking that way, that you would always be by his side and never abandon him.

He simply got up and walked out of the meeting room, returned to his office, letting his thoughts wander as he wandered, while he squatted down, looking at the roses and tulips being planted with some worry.

Although he secretly bought a few full-spectrum plant lamps from you, under his care, these plants still looked wilted. He just didn't kill them, but they weren't much better either. Spamton was always worried. He wanted to plant them properly and show them to you, to surprise you. But now...... He couldn't possibly give you a withered, wilted flower either.

Spamton frowned worriedly and couldn't help but water a little more, which only made the flowers even more wilted. He closed his eyes for a moment, then finally picked up a pot of tulips—he decided he should go home and ask for your opinion. What exactly are these trade secrets you mentioned? He had no idea! Fortunately, he didn't intend to treat this pot of flowers as a surprise or anything, so it was fine to show you casually.

Today, he was busy in the office with a loud clang. The other employees had no idea what he was doing. Spamton just kept a stern face, holding a listless tulip as he went home. He came home a bit late. To be honest, all those moments ago had been spent flipping through books in the office, trying to find some information to see if there was anything useful among the useless piles of just pretty books.

…… It turned out there weren't. They were all economics books he didn't even read, because he found them boring and only covered key points he had already mastered.

He took a deep breath and opened the door. You were already sitting at the dining table waiting for him. Seeing that he hadn't come home today, you simply reheated the frozen pizza in the fridge. After all, this has quite a lot and is convenient to eat. You really enjoy tinkering with it.

The steaming hot pizza was on the dining table. You wanted to say more, but when you looked up at Spamton, you couldn't help but pause—you had just been anxiously scrolling on your phone because Spamton had come home late, not knowing what to do. In short, you were just constantly refreshing the chat page. After all, if you really wanted to calm down and look at something, you wouldn't be able to stand it anymore.

But you didn't expect Spamton to bring back a potted flower...... It was a wilted sunflower.

Your mouth twitched, and you could tell the flower had been watered too much. Spamton was probably too nervous, and sometimes even too nervous wouldn't work...... But it's hard to maintain a relaxed mood.

You were momentarily distracted, but still smiled and said, "Put that pot down for now. We'll talk about it after we have a good dinner." By the way, it's probably just that you watered too much. Usually, once a day is enough, and some plants require different amounts of watering depending on the variety. ”

You probably explained it to him, and Spamton just nodded half-understandingly; he probably didn't really get it.

You just sighed and chose to break it open to explain: "Anyway, first pour out the water that has accumulated in the basin. Then I suggest you change the soil, to a dry environment, and make sure to irrigate once a day. As long as the soil is slightly moist, it'll be fine." ”

Spamton nodded repeatedly and was about to find a new pot, but you quickly grabbed him: "Plants are very resilient. It's fine to leave them a little longer...... You should eat first. You're coming back so late—you must be really hungry." ”

Spamton scratched his cheek, his face slightly flushed. Indeed, he could hear the rumbling of his stomach, so he finally put down the flowerpot and obediently sat down across from you to start eating.

You nod in satisfaction, glanced at him, and couldn't help but add, "Do you have any other cultivations...... Different flowers have their own cultivation methods. ”

But Spamton just shook his head frantically; for now, he still doesn’t want you to see it. After all, he plans to give you a surprise, and he also intends to make an effort himself—he must grow lavender successfully to give you a surprise, and then watch your eyes light up as you praise him, saying that Spamton is really amazing.

Seeing him like this, you can tell that he’s definitely hiding something from you again, but it doesn’t really matter. After all, you know, and sooner or later you’ll find out exactly what he’s been keeping from you.

After all, what he’s hiding is all meant for you to see. You know this very well, so you can’t help but start looking forward to it.

What might he be preparing? Could it be a carnation, meant to express gratitude and friendship? But you don’t need such things. You just voluntarily, spontaneously, want to be kind to him. It’s not because he has done something that deserves it; you just feel that he also deserves these things, that he is entitled to his own happiness.

After all, no one is born to suffer, even if… your past hasn’t been happy, you’re also willing to make an effort to create what can be considered a warm and beautiful present for him. After all, you believe his future will also be bright and shining—though you haven’t considered this for yourself. You just feel that it doesn’t matter what happens to you.

After you finished dinner, he tidied up the dishes as usual, and now you are both sitting on the sofa.

You leaned back slightly on the sofa, watching as Spamton seriously moved over a new pot, then poured in the soil and carefully transplanted the already wilted tulip. You looked at the flowerpot and couldn't help but get a bit distracted. It was bought when Spamton first became interested in gardening. As for why he was interested, it was probably because you told him at the time you wanted to see a sea of lavender.

Although I don't know why flowerpots are still sold in the Dark World...... Maybe it's because some people of darkness really like to plant fake flowers, and you can't help but smile when you look at the patterns on the pots.

I don't know why he acts so serious, as if afraid the flowers might be hurt in the slightest by his actions. But you look at him and can't help but be a bit lost in thought. In the end, you quietly return to your room, pick up your brush and a piece of drawing paper again, and decide this time to just sketch him simply.

So you simply returned to the sofa and quietly began tracing his figure on paper. For a moment, the room fell silent, with no other sounds.

After sketching and tracing his form, you began to carefully sketch on paper, no longer looking at spamton. You got so absorbed that you quietly walked to your side and admired it for a long time without noticing.

Until you were almost done with the painting and were about to lift your head to stretch out your arms for a lazy stretch, you widened your eyes at Spamton, who had appeared at some point and happened to meet your gaze.

Maybe because he saw you were drawing seriously, he didn't disturb you, though if he really did, you wouldn't say anything......

At that moment, he tilted his head, excitedly leaning close to you, his head pressed against yours, staring at the sketch. You instinctively felt a bit embarrassed and wanted to put the sketch away, but then you heard Spamton's voice again, as if he was acting spoiled, his voice a bit sticky: "Y/N." "Can I have this painting?" ”

“…… But it's just a sketch, nothing particularly good. How about I draw a new one for you?" You scratched your head, though giving this painting to Spamton wouldn't matter...... No, it does matter. Although Spamton praised your drawing and your colleagues said you were great, you still felt a bit insecure. After all, this was just your improvisation. If possible, you would still hope you could give Spamton the best.

"This one works too, and the previous one is fine too...... Ah, that one is already a gift for me, right? Y/N wants to draw me a new one—no, a lot, lots of new ones! Y/N must really like drawing me too, right? "Spamton immediately went back to sitting on the sofa, his eyes sparkling as he looked at you.

"You just need to honestly and steadily spend the rest of your life. You don't have many desires, nor any desires; you just ...... I just hope that you and him can both spend the rest of your lives peacefully."
