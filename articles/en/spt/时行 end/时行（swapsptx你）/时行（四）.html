Time Practices (Part 4)

[4]

The advertiser is holding another meeting. Judging by the frequency of meetings, this isn't that strange, but if you look at the content of the meetings...... At least, no other employees have seen these things.

After all, this time, their boss was cheerfully showing off a piece of art made from countless bottles of mineral water, which was filled with fake flowers.

Spamton just raised an eyebrow and announced, "Next, we're going to advertise this...... The theme is probably that you can grow flower arrangements using different vessels!" As he spoke, he weighed the items in his hand again.

"Anyway, it's probably something like this kind of artwork! If you have any good ideas, you can let me know. If the artwork really succeeds, we can continue promoting it. ”

He definitely didn't think there was anything about the brand he was putting out that couldn't sell—moreover, based on his research on the Lumens, humans do indeed prefer these inexplicable art pieces, just like that urinal Addison showed him last time. As long as he sold it under the banner of 'art' and advertised it, some Lumens would inevitably click to take a look.

…… Even though he himself felt the vase had little aesthetic value. Calling it a vase was just a bunch of mineral water bottles stacked together for flower arrangements, and to prevent fake flowers from loosening, he had filled each bottle with flowers, which made them look even less aesthetic and just bloated.

Pink couldn't hold back and retorted, "But this doesn't look like art at all. There won't be any pop-up pop-ups for the Light folks to buy......

Before he could finish, Spamton interrupted him. Spamton just raised his eyebrows high; his meeting would not allow any other Dark People to object. No matter what, he was the ruler of this world.

"But my Lumens at home said this looks great! Y/N said I'm really amazing, whatever I make will definitely sell well!" Spamton said cheerfully. He didn't care about the wording you originally used; it was basically the same meaning… Although he also felt yesterday that you probably only said those things to flatter him. Yet Spamton didn't care at all; he only heard what he wanted to hear, and indeed, he had heard you say it!

Even if it's just a lie...... He's willing to believe it, because you said it yourself.

The Dark People's eye twitched; no matter how you listened, it sounded like Spamton was showing off. Honestly, they found it hard to believe that was the truth...... These employees had more or less seen you before, so of course they knew you probably said that to appease the advertiser.

As for exposing his identity with you? They wouldn't dare do that. In this world, Spamton determines their fate...... Even if you speak, he won't do anything to your face, but what about behind your back? They wouldn't dare imagine what Spamton would do behind your back.

Yellow's eyes darted around. Since that's the case, it was better to follow Spamton's words. So he created a pop-up window and made up a pattern—probably stacking discarded cardboard boxes and arranging fake flowers on top: "Then I think we can take it further!" For example...... He advertised that all these artworks were recycled from waste, and that some people of light with compassion would definitely click in to take a look. ”

Spamton nodded in satisfaction, while the other Addisons glared angrily at Yellow. Of course, he could think of anything he could think of, but he had already taken the initiative and spoke out first.

"Yeah, Y/N's taste is really great! Our upcoming theme is waste recycling—let's give it a fresh look!" Orange followed along with a cheerful smile, adding a bit of praise just like Spamton.

Spamton's brows relaxed, his mood lifted, and he looked much more beautiful. He waved his hand grandly: "Then let's follow Orange's instructions. Next is the topic!" By the way, each employee should buy one to take home first. ”

As soon as he said this, the staff below started complaining again. Shadow Guy couldn't speak, so he could only play a saxophone tune expressing anger and resentment. The pippins thought for a moment, then their eyes darted around and went to negotiate with the other Addisons. The gist was that if multiple groups bought together, could the final total price be a bit cheaper?

Unfortunately, Spamton also heard the murmurs below. He waved his finger, looking serious: "Group purchases won't offer discounts! Each one can only be paid at the original price." He emphasized the last few words, and the pippins quieted down, still trying to struggle until they heard Spamton's next sentence.

"Besides that, for every artwork promoted to the People of Light, they get a 50 Dark Coin commission. Including the previous trash can flower arranging." "Spamton knows that the Light Tribe never clicked on pop-ups before; they must have treated it as spam. As for now...... He just wants to make the sales look better, so he can show you the number of transactions and the Dark Coins he earned.

After saying this, the meeting was almost over, but before Addison could leave, Spamton suddenly remembered what you said yesterday and added: "Oh right, my dear colleagues, next weekend Y/N is treating you all to a meal, so you have to complete your respective targets in advance." There was a hint of jest and schadenfreude in his tone. He knew these former colleagues wouldn't dare to tell you the truth—if they did, Spamton would let these 'good colleagues' taste the feeling of misery… well, joking, of course.

Addison and the others were all stunned for a moment. They glanced at each other, but for a moment, no one spoke. In the end, it was Blue who spoke: "...... Where will the location be then? ”

This was something neither you nor Spamton had considered, so he stood with his hands on his hips, a bit stunned. Honestly...... Of course, the first option is to pass your place. He definitely doesn't want these Addisons to come over; he always feels a bit unfairly uneasy.

He pondered for a moment, then finally said, "...... Cyber BBQ Restaurant, right? Yes, just go there, it's just right." As he spoke, it sounded like he was murmuring, but in fact...... He actually didn't want to go back to that place either.

But he decided he had to face his past head-on. Although the future now was what he hoped for, he still felt something was missing...... Maybe it was the past he once desperately wanted to deny was missing. But it was precisely because of his unbearable past that you kept him.

But no matter what...... At least, maybe he hopes your involvement can save the lost and helpless self he once was. He can change the past that has been repeated. He can ...... Let him choose to accept himself again, or in other words, be willing to love the person he once was because of your love.

The Addisons exchanged glances again, nodded, and said in unison, "Got it," then walked out of the meeting room. Once again, only Spamton remained in the room, and he felt the coldness and suddenly became genuinely afraid—afraid that when he showed you his achievements and the dark coins he earned, you'd laugh and say he could stand on his own, live on his own, and in a way, kick him out—even if your intentions were just good.

He simply pushed these chaotic and complicated thoughts aside, walked out of the meeting room, and only felt at ease as he watched the rising Dark Coin accumulation. But when he thought of you, he felt a bit empty inside. He transferred the dark coins from the vase art he sold today into another account, planning to show you this account when he got back, show it off to you, and then get a few compliments from you. Thinking of this, Spamton couldn't help but curl his lips, feeling delighted again.

So he returned home with such a happy mood. It wasn't until he stood at the door that he swallowed nervously, feeling nervous again. In the end, he carefully opened the door. You were sitting across the dining table, on which was a freshly made, still steaming pizza.

And seeing Spamton cautiously peek inside as he opened the door for a look, you couldn't help but feel it was somewhat funny, but you just smiled warmly and said, "Welcome home."

Spamton paused for a moment, pushed the door open, hung his coat on the rack, and set down his bag, nervously adjusting his hair that wasn't even messy—he just wanted to maintain a good image in your eyes, always.

You quietly watched him. Spamton cleared his throat and sat down on the chair opposite you, also smiling, 'Hmm, I'm back.'

Then he placed his arm on the table, unintentionally revealing a wound on his forearm—actually, this wound was caused earlier during his inspection on the shopping street, when Yellow accidentally bumped into him and knocked over a vase. The broken ceramic pieces caused this cut. It was a minor wound, easily healable with any healing magic Addison had. But he stopped Yellow from trying to fix it afterward and didn't use magic himself, intending to elicit a bit of your sympathy with this small injury.

So he just covered it with a black shirt, but naturally, when he rested his arm on the table and the shirt pulled back slightly with momentum, the wound became partially visible.

Of course, you noticed too, hurriedly stopped his hand, then carefully lifted up, grabbed his arm, rolled up his sleeve, and saw the wound still bleeding, not yet fully healed. It looked scary, a long wound, but in reality, it wasn't a big deal—after all, it hadn't gone very deep.

Still, you furrowed your brow, understandably feeling concerned because he was hurt.

'...Don't rush to eat yet. Let's take care of the wound first!' you said, running to your room to pull out a first-aid kit from under your bed, something you had prepared long ago but never used. You also glanced at the similarly dust-covered alley nearby, finally lowering your eyes.

…… Those are your old memories, but you have never opened them. You only want to start a brand new life, even avoiding touching on your past. Even if the past is just as important to you.

Shaking your head, you discard those thoughts, run out, take out a cotton swab dipped in iodine to carefully wipe his wound, and with a hint of concern and reproach, say, "What's going on...... Such a long wound looks terrifying. ”

Spamton just lowered his head, softly answering your question, 'It's just... those Addison saw that my items sold well and wanted to take them, then we argued, and we broke a vase...'

You sighed, reaffirming your resolve, vowing to talk to those Addison properly next weekend. You wrapped Spamton's arm in bandages, then ruffled his hair: 'You dare to resist, dare to face these injustices. My Spamton is amazing!'

...No, you had no idea of the truth. Spamton thought about this with rare bitterness.

But he only dared to lower his head, staring blankly at the bandage you had just bandaged, lost in thought. He didn't dare let you see his expression, nor the unease and bitterness he might be revealing. He stared at the bandage, staring at the small wound that could have been healed with just a spell, barely sorting out his tangled and complicated emotions.

Do you regret deceiving you? Maybe not...... Because he's afraid that once you know the truth, he won't show him such genuine care anymore, and he's afraid he won't be able to feel love from you again.

But maybe always deceiving you isn't a good thing, but trying to explain everything from the beginning is hard to begin. Spamton opened his mouth, about to reveal the whole truth, but your voice interrupted him again.

"By the way, how are your fake flower art products selling?" Actually, you didn't really know what to say. You just felt it wasn't good to let the room sink into silence, so you spoke up and randomly picked a question.

Spamton snapped back to reality, perked up again, and answered your question with a big smile: "It sold very well, a full hundred copies!" "And it was exactly the number of employees under his command...... Yes, aside from those he forced to buy and sell, nothing else was sold.

But he believes that with commissions as an attraction, there will always be employees who make sales. As for commissions...... Speaking of which, it should be considered part of the fairness you want, right? Spamton can't be sure.

And the truth he had just instinctively wanted to blurt out was buried by him. He thought, since that's the case, he'll wait until next time, when he's sure he's ready to speak, and then tell you. Unfortunately...... Spamton wasn't sure if there was still such an opportunity.

You tilted your head, a bit puzzled, probably not expecting this so-called artwork to actually sell, nor to expect so many copies to exist.

"All of these...... Are they all sold by you? Do you have that much stock? Do you need my help...... You look a bit worried. Although you haven't done any art or handicraft work in a long time, you think it shouldn't be too hard to pick it up again for him.

Spamton was a bit stuck. He was a bit annoyed—if he'd known, he wouldn't have said 100 servings so directly—his shop didn't have that many!

But he cleared his throat and came up with another lie, unusually feeling guilty because it was completely false: "I sold them all! There are only five or six items left in stock, so the rest are all pre-orders. I'll come pick them up in a few days...... I don't need your help, I can handle it myself!" He smiled to hide his panic.

You stared at him steadily, long enough that Spamton began to wonder if maybe he had said something wrong and you were about to expose his lie, feeling genuinely uneasy, and then you lightly nodded and leaned in to ruffle his hair: "I see… business is booming, Spammy who came up with this is really amazing."

You smiled, despite ...... You had already realized that this sentence was probably a lie. After all, when he spoke, he didn't even dare look you in the eye, constantly glancing to the upper left. It was obvious he was just fabricating the scene from before. But you still chose to follow his words.

Sometimes you can't figure it out—he could have told you the truth, so why lie again? Even if you're willing to believe his lies, because most lies hide genuine emotions, revealing past fears and fears, anxiety, and grievances. You've been waiting for the day he is willing to tell you the truth. If it's always a lie, that's fine.

…… You are willing to favor him, tolerate him, and accept the lies he says.

Maybe at first it was just a simple exploitation...... You guess that's how it is, but for some reason, this somewhat twisted relationship has persisted until now.

You rub his head, and Spamton instinctively rubs his furry head against your palm. You hear his voice full of affection and joy: "I like you the most!" ”

You're a bit distracted—is that really true? But...... But why did things end up like this? You don't understand.

So, this statement is probably false. You immediately put it out of your mind, just a beat too late to respond with a "hmm."

Spamton's movements finally slowed down, though still rubbing slowly against your palm: "By the way, Y/N, we promised you were treating my colleagues to dinner...... They set the venue at Cyber BBQ." By the end, his voice gradually softened.

You nod gently, then remembered Spamton was looking down and couldn't see your movements, so you replied, "Alright, I know." ”

“…… That place is where they once threw a coffee cup at my head. "He grabbed your hand, but this time rested it on your chin, and he looked up at you, showing a pitiful expression.

"So...... The place where they said they would throw you into the trash was also that place? "You paused for a moment, then recalled the first time we met, while Spamton just blinked at you and said nothing.

"I know...... Although I can't guarantee I'll help you get revenge, I can make sure they don't do it again. "You scratched his chin, assuring him.

Actually, even if you hadn't said anything, those Addisons wouldn't dare do anything to him anymore—after all, he was a big boss. But Spamton just blinked and said nothing, after all...... What he hoped for wasn't the present, but the past—the past he once didn't want to face, even hated, to be changed.

You also scratched his cheeks before sitting back across from him. You had been waiting specifically for Spamton to return; dinner hadn't even been eaten yet, and bandaging his wound wasted a bit of time, so the pizza had cooled off slightly.

But it's still edible, so you plan to pick up a piece and eat it directly.

But Spamton held your hand down: "Some have cooled down. I'll heat them up before you eat." ”

You paused for a moment and instinctively said, "No need to go to such trouble......" After all, you've had dinner like this many times in the world of light. Many times, even when dinner got cold, your parents still hadn't come home. Many times, they broke their promise from yesterday.

But Spamton had already walked into the kitchen carrying that plate, and you heard the sound of the heating plate and microwave, so you could only sigh softly. Even though you were sighing, the corners of your mouth couldn't help but lift. You touched the corner of your mouth, a bit dazed.

While you were still dazed, the microwave stopped, and Spamton came out carrying the pizza again, placing it in front of you. You touched it—the temperature was just right.

Spamton secretly felt proud. Previously, just in time for you to come home, he had prepared a steaming dinner just right, and he had studied the heat control of each item. So heating the pizza to this temperature for a long time was just right.

He doesn't think about how you might react; he just instinctively lifts his cheeks and looks at you with sparkling eyes, begging you to praise him.

And you naturally curved your eyes: "The temperature is just right, thank you, spammy...... You're really amazing! ”

You see him lift his head proudly and can't help but laugh too.

And so, dinner ended happily. After dinner, Spamton tidied up the leftover bowls and chopsticks, then sat on the sofa and started making art pieces with those mineral water bottles.

…… He really did regret it a little. If he'd known, he wouldn't have mentioned pre-ordering earlier. He just told you that giving them counterfeit or inferior products would be better than doing handicrafts here...... Alright, you'll definitely say that way.

Although Spamton only plans to make a few for show, leaving the rest to the staff themselves, at worst they just pay and get a picture. After all, the actual product doesn't necessarily match the pictures.

You sighed and sat down next to Spamton. Spamton felt the sofa sink in one spot, and when he turned to look at you, you picked up several bottles of mineral water as well.

"Almost 100 servings? How can you finish it? Let me help you." You said softly, picking up scissors and glue to start working.

Spamton didn't know whether to tell you that what he just said was just deceiving you—but he didn't want to bother you, because under your command, what used to be something he had just haphazardly assembled had now truly become a work of art.

You cut off the base of the bottle, glue the mouth together, and trim some plastic strips from the sides of the bottle as decoration. Soon, a piece of art resembling a sunflower appeared in your hands.

Spamton stares at you blankly, even forgetting to continue what he's doing. You glance at him with some confusion: "Stop staring at me all the time...... Hurry up and do it." ”

"Oh oh…" He lowered his head to fiddle with the 'art piece' he was working on halfway, but his mind wasn't on it. He kept thinking, you clearly had the talent and interest to do other art-related work, yet you just worked as an ordinary gallery employee. He thought he had no right to interfere with your thoughts and choices, but Spamton still felt sorry for you.

You wouldn't think so much about it, just staring at the "sunflower" in your hand and getting a bit lost in thought—you recall your previous painting imitating that sunflower, but unfortunately, that painting is now tucked away at the very bottom of the box, probably never to be republished in this lifetime.

So you pick up a few bottles of mineral water and repeat the previous work, planning to make a few more sunflowers. You think, maybe if something else happens, you won't remember those previous paintings anymore.

But even though you made several more sunflowers, you couldn't hide your earlier thoughts, so you thought, maybe something else could distract you. So you looked at Spamton again, only to find with amusement that he hadn't even finished the one in his hand.

"spammy, don't you know what shape to tie it into?" You put down the scissors and glue, stretched lazily, and said lazily.

Spamton shivered instinctively, feeling a bit distracted and guilty about being caught by you: "Hmm...... Ah, right, I don't know what shape to tie it into." Then he gave you a cautious smile.

You rubbed his head and thought casually: "Alright, then you can do sunflowers like me. Look, the examples are all right here. You just need to do a quick pom." ”

Spamton nodded. Although he didn't do as well as you, he managed to manage a few things.

When he turns his head with sparkling eyes, hoping for your praise, he finds you already asleep on the back of the sofa, your head tilted and resting on his shoulder.

Spamton was silent for a moment, then got up, carefully carried you to bed, tucked you in, and then gazed at your peaceful sleeping face for a long time before leaving.

On the weekend, at the agreed time, you showed up at the Cyber BBQ Restaurant with Spamton. As soon as you entered, you saw the other Addisons. They were sitting by the window, so you grabbed Spamton's hand and sat right across from them. Honestly, I don't know why they happened to make room across from you, but all four Dark Folk had to squeeze in...... You didn't quite understand, but Pink spoke first.

"It's Spamton who first stole my stuff, so of course, I had to take it back," he said vigorously, arms crossed. Of course, because Spamton was right there, Pink couldn't openly say that Spamton had first taken his stuff and then he took it back, conveniently performing this scene for you.

Pink was grinding his teeth in hatred. He kept thinking to himself, Spamton clearly doesn't like flattering the Lightfolk, yet he's so scheming at it—he must have learned it all for you.

You're a step too slow. According to Pink, Spamton was indeed at fault, but you just waved your hand: "I think we should hear from the parties involved and the witnesses around us about this." "You're very serious. Honestly, you feel like a judge in one of the courtroom games you used to love—honestly, it's pretty fun."

Spamton, on the other hand, just cautiously grabbed your arm, not daring to use too much force, just holding it weakly: "Because...... Pink said bad things about you, and I was upset, so I wanted to steal his stuff. I want to get back at you......

You were stunned again, surprised that Spamton would admit to the matter but explain the reason. Honestly, seeing his head lowered and his weak voice, you couldn't help but soften.

Pink was at a loss for words. He really wanted to say that Spamton was just twisting the truth and talking nonsense—back then, he had only mocked Spamton for being good at winning your favor, so why not try to please the other Light Folk and make the advertising world better? Then Spamton targeted him like this.

And you just gently rub Spamton's head, look up at the other Addisons, and ask, "So what did you see at the time?" ”

"I was quite far away. Spamton did indeed steal Pink's stuff, so later Pink went to the Spamton shop to rob something." Blue said.

“…… Well, anyway, what Pink said back then wasn't very pleasant. "yellow hesitated, obviously not expecting Spamton to twist the facts so much.

"But later, all the customers went to Pink, and we were busy grabbing clients, so we didn't pay attention to this matter." Orange added.

You nodded, then patted Spamton's head. Well, now it seems he was indeed at fault—but you chose to favor him, and besides, he acted for you. If you don't support him, who else will?

So you spoke up, slowly saying, 'Okay, I understand… spammy is indeed at fault, but first, pink spoke unpleasantly, so I think, no matter what, spammy’s actions can only be considered self-defense.' You admitted that you were also quite unreasonable, after all, robbery carries much heavier criminal liability than a few words of dispute… but you believe spamton was not wrong.

How could your Spamton possibly have any major issues?

Pink rolled his eyes helplessly. He felt that you and Spamton were both equally unreasonable; he didn’t understand how you two could think the other was just absolutely great.

But then he heard what you said next: "...... So, I want to know, who poured coffee on my Spammy earlier?" You said with a smile, showing no sign of anger, but the other Addisons all shivered.

Then they all looked at Pink, who was a bit annoyed: "Why are you all staring at me!" ”

And you just tilted your head, looking at Pink as well: "So...... Do you know anything? ”

Pink picked up the coffee cup on the table and took a sip, probably feeling it was getting cold. Instinctively, he threw the cup out—right in the direction of Spamton.

…… Well, actually, before coming, Spamton had already reminded them to reenact the scene from that time. Although they didn't understand what Spamton was really trying to do, they had no choice but to comply. After all, Spamton said they would be given extra overtime pay, which was rare. Normally, they would have paid them out of pocket.

You reached out to catch the coffee cup and held it in front of Spamton. Fortunately, only a few drops spilled and splashed onto you. You placed the cup on the table and said calmly, "So, it really was you." ”

Spamton hid behind you, cautiously poking his head out to look at the other Addisons, then pulled his eyelids into a funny face and grinned at the others. Pink was so angry he nearly fainted.

He rolled his eyes again and said irritably: "Yes, it was me. I asked Spamton to make coffee, but he didn't brew it right. The coffee got a bit cold and tasted terrible. What's wrong with me asking him to drink it?"

“…… You shouldn't have poured coffee on him." You just said slowly. Honestly, you're starting to feel sorry for Spamton again...... Although this is ultimately just a small matter, you can't imagine how many such small incidents have happened before. And often, these little things pile up enough to completely change the course of a life.

“…… He doesn't have coffee anyway, so what's the point of me treating him?" Pink crossed his arms and rolled his eyes again.

"At that time, Spamton volunteered to make us coffee. The other temperatures were just right, except for Pink's cup, which had issues." Yellow added a seemingly considerate comment from the side, but Pink always felt he was a bit gloating.

…… So today the only one being the punching bag is him, who was pushed out to be the punching bag? Pink was a bit annoyed, so he quickly added.

"Do you want to know what happened when he was thrown into the trash heap?"

You nodded. Of course you wanted to know about this...... To be honest, you came here just to find a place for your Spamton. You know this barbecue restaurant probably contains all of Spamton's grievances and sad memories, and you have no idea how much courage it took for him to dare to walk in.

"It was that guy Yellow. When they saw Spamton with a coffee cup on his head, they thought he was better suited to staying in the trash heap, and ...... Spamton couldn't sell a single thing, had no results at all, so treating him as trash was more suitable. "Pink squinted, also speaking with a hint of schadenfreude, thinking that the gloating Yellow just now definitely wouldn't get away.

Now yellow fell silent, just lowered his head, not daring to look at you.

You sighed, "No one should stay in a garbage heap, and no one should be treated as trash. Besides, he's a righteous businessman of the Dark. Even if he doesn't do as well as you, you have no reason to target him." ”

The Addison didn't say anything. Honestly, with Spamton's pitiful look...... They hadn't thought he could be that formidable at first. But now, they just lowered their heads and didn't say a word.

And Spamton just carefully poked your waist. You turned to look at him, and he whispered softly: "…That's enough. After all, they are my colleagues too, no need to make things too awkward. We'll still be interacting in the future…" Saying this, he lowered his head again, his voice slightly pitiful.

You gently wiped his cheek with affection, then looked up: "Alright, anyway, I'm here today...... I just want you to know that I'm the one who protects him, so don't think about doing anything bad anymore." As she spoke, she lightly tapped the table with her knuckles.

The Addisons exchanged glances, thinking to themselves, how could they dare...... Now Spamton is the master of the advertising world, above all the Dark People. It's already good if Spamton doesn't turn around and target them.

They just nodded eagerly: "We know, we know." ”

Even though the play wasn't over yet—because Pink had interrupted and revealed Yellow's previous actions—Spamton still slightly lifted his head, looking at your back with a complicated expression—yes, he knew you'd pass by there, which is why he stayed in the garbage heap for days. Back then, he couldn't imagine the current scene or the chaotic, complicated emotions of today.

His eyes reddened, and he silently wiped away the tears swirling in his eyes with his other free hand. Maybe...... From the start, he wished someone could stand before him. After all, who hasn't thought about being hurt? If only someone had stopped it then.

…… Only when there is no hope or hope do you stumble and get up on your own, trying every possible way and crawling to protect yourself. He once thought that once he became strong, he would take revenge, but now, seeing you standing in his way, he felt ...... This isn't so bad.

Because ...... He was no longer alone; he had something to rely on, no longer having to grit his teeth and complete everything on his own.

And ...... Having someone to rely on feels so good. He thought, he could finally face all of this and stop caring, since what he cared about now was you. Spamton thought, he could truly love the once down-and-out self and the self now full of lies. After all...... He also wanted to give you this love.

You sighed, not noticing the change in Spamton's expression behind you, but Blue did, showing a somewhat complicated expression.

"So...... Is there anything else? If not, let Spamton handle it herself. "You nod, and just as you finished speaking, Orange picked up your words.

"Ah, I used to put on a little dress for Spammy...... To be honest, he actually looks pretty good in a little dress." Orange rubbed his hands with a smile. Anyway, he didn't think he did anything wrong, and you paused for a moment, instinctively turning to look at Spamton, and indeed began to assess what he would look like if he wore a dress......

Spamton, rarely blushing, covers your eyes and whispers close to your ear, "Y/N, you...... If you want to see, I'll wear it alone for you later. ”

You want to say you don't want to see this kind of scene, but you honestly nod—well, maybe you still want it, and you remember, some skirts you bought before that you haven't worn are probably just right for spamton. As for why you stop wearing skirts...... It's just for some personal private reasons.

Spamton glanced at you with some awkwardness. Well, he doesn't mind showing you wearing a skirt...... If others see it, that's not acceptable, especially since advertising for the Light People doesn't require him to risk everything and wear a skirt.

Blue didn't speak, only softly adding when the surroundings had quieted: "From now on, I won't just stand there and watch." ”

…… Bystanders are also accomplices.

You felt everything had been clarified, so you nodded approvingly and said, 'Alright, in any case, my spammy also troubles you!'

The meal was basically over. Next, you ordered some barbecue inside the shop. The atmosphere was quite harmonious, and you were very satisfied.
