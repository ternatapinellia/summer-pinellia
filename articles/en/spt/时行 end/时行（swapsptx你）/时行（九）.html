Time Travels (Nine)

[9]

You sat on the chair, thinking you were casually watching the people coming through the door. You wanted to see that figure of Addison that you were especially familiar with, but unfortunately... you waited for a long time, and with every passing minute, the sense of anticipation only grew. You craned your neck in expectation, feeling each second like a year, yet only a few Pippins, all smiling broadly, came in, along with Zapper hopping in joyfully, and Shadow Guy quietly playing the saxophone because loud noises were prohibited in the gallery. Besides them, there were also some Dark Citizens you did not know—indeed, Spamton's advertised tips seemed to be useful, and after entering, these Dark Citizens silently admired your artwork. You could see their expressions shift from indifference to slight shock and admiration—which made you feel a bit elated and considerably better in mood.

You think, yes, that's right, just like Spamton said...... You really are amazing, because the other Darkfolk also greatly appreciate your artwork.

But what about him? You nervously stared at the front door, waiting for that white Addison, waiting for Spamton, but all you saw was Blue walk in.

…… Even when the last Dark Folk walked into the gallery and ticket checks stopped at the entrance, you still hadn't seen that Dark Folk pounding their chests and vowing that they would come to your art exhibition every day.

You suppress your inner disappointment and regret, trying to keep the corners of your mouth upward and avoid showing excessive sadness, but your eyebrows still droop. Anyone can see you're in a bad mood—it's all just forced smiles.

You lowered your head, feeling gloomy. Actually...... It doesn't matter to other Dark People; you just confirmed one thing: you really seem impressive and will earn the admiration of many. But all this silent effort was never seen by the Dark People you most wanted to see...... You were a bit distracted, and at that moment you heard a somewhat familiar voice calling your name.

You look up and see Blue standing in front of you with a smile: "Could you tell me the background of these paintings? Under what circumstances did you create them?" He asked.

But he just saw you were in a bad mood and wanted to pick a random topic to cheer you up. He thought, after all, mentioning Spamton should lift your spirits...... There weren't many Dark People here today, since many employees had exchanged tickets with Spamton for money, and Spamton...... To be honest, he was probably caught up in some matters related to you, which is why he didn't have time to rush over.

But Blue still obediently bought the ticket. First, he was genuinely curious about what your exhibition would look like, and second...

… He also wants to know how your relationship with Spamton is. He's just gossiping—after all, Spamton's attitude is already obvious. He wants everyone to know how amazing you are, how good you are to him, how much he likes you, how much he loves you...... Love you.

So Blue was a bit curious: what exactly do you think about Spamton?

You paused for a moment, but still put away your expression and put on the smile you usually use for business. You stood up and looked at the recent painting—Spamton was carefully tending to a plant.

So you couldn't help but smile: "Ah, this painting is the first one I made for Spamton... At that time, he was fussing with the plants following my instructions, a bit flustered. I thought it was really cute, so I painted it." As you spoke, your gaze dulled slightly as memories of the past returned, and you paid little attention to the guests beside you. When you came back to your senses, you turned to Blue and hastily bowed: "Ah, sorry... I forgot. I should have introduced it to you."

Blue just shook his head: "It's fine, that's it. I really like hearing it." "At least by looking at your expression, seeing the smile you make when you mention Spamton...... He knows you must love Spamton, otherwise why would you instinctively smile when you mention him? At least thinking of him makes you happy.

You then arrived in front of the second painting. You looked up—it depicted Spamton carrying dishes to the dining table, looking at you painting him and subconsciously smiling. Clearing your throat, you said: "This... is the painting I did on the day I decided to start painting Spamton, and decided to make him the theme of this exhibition. Because he was smiling at me, I captured that rare smile."

Because most of the paintings you paint behind his back, he usually doesn't have much of a smile on his face.

The third painting shows Spamton operating a hand-cranked coffee machine, focused on grinding coffee beans, wearing an apron. Your voice stammered for a moment: "This is me...... I secretly drew it for him. I thought this scene was especially warm and beautiful, so I couldn't help but draw it. Because our family has a habit of drinking coffee, he gets up in the morning to grind coffee beans. He doesn't know that I am already awake by this time and is secretly watching him. You couldn't help but laugh as you spoke.

Blue looked at your expression, silent as well. From your tone and your expression, everything revealed joy, as if the gloom that had just wrapped around you had already lifted.

You feel nothing, just keep walking forward. Looking up, you see a fourth painting—a profile of Spamton on the phone. He is talking to someone or something, and you can't even remember what you were feeling at the time: "This painting...... I probably wanted to capture some of his usual work scenes, so I painted this picture of him on the phone. But since he usually doesn't work at home, I only kept this painting. ”

“…… He usually doesn't make phone calls outside of work hours. The only time he did was to talk to us about your situation and to pay more attention to the preferences of the People of Light. "Blue suddenly interrupted.

You paused for a moment, instinctively glancing at the blue Addison beside you, but he only added with a calm expression, "Because recently he heard that other Lightfolk have descended into other dark worlds. Let's keep an eye on them." ”

In fact, he didn't understand that Spamton already had the ability to check the preferences of the Light Tribe...... Or was it that he couldn't check your computer or shopping history at all, or did he simply not want to look?

You pursed your lips and turned your head in a bit of panic. For some reason, you just felt your ears turn red, and you just muttered softly, with a hint of complaint: "...... He just needs to ask me...... "You'll definitely tell him everything."

But does this also prove that he actually likes you very much? You pause for a moment, then suddenly realize: why do you care whether he really likes you, and whether what he says is true?

You haven't thought much about it yet, but you only hear Blue add: "Because he cares about you a lot." He was also smiling the whole time on the phone. ”

You look at that painting...... Indeed, upon closer inspection, you realize the corners of his mouth are indeed upturned, and you unconsciously record it.

Because Spamton cares deeply about all of Y/N's feelings, and cares deeply about whether you are truly happy, he secretly prepares a gift for you. That is probably...... It's not just a thank-you gift, but also his long-standing gratitude, and also his love. He wants to wait until that sea of flowers is truly planted, with flowers blooming, wanting to hold the gift and solemnly tell you about his long-standing feelings, telling you that he really likes you and ...... loves you.

In front of you, although he was already the master of the Dark World, he still approached everything with the same cautiousness and sincerity as the pure white Addison once was.

You paused, wiping away the tears that suddenly welled up in your eyes: "Alright, I'll continue introducing you to the next painting." ”

The next painting...... It shows him in the kitchen, preparing dinner. He didn't notice you, and you only drew his profile. Looking at the painting, you softly said, "This painting...... It's because I wanted to record my daily life, so I went home a bit earlier that day and painted it while he was making dinner. I went home early that day, and he was very happy, and so was I. ”

Because even you can see that he still has a smile on his face.

And so, unconsciously, you reached the end of the introduction. Standing in front of the last painting, you belatedly felt parched, but you looked up at the painting, embarrassed, clearing your throat, because the painting showed you and him smiling: "This painting...... It's basically our family portrait. Yes, our whole family consists only of him and me—one of the people of light and one of the dark. This is to commemorate the photo he left behind for raising the first pot of flowers, and I redrew a picture based on that photo. ”

Think about it, you really don't care so much about your parents tearing up your painting anymore. When you finish the final stroke on this painting, that heavy past just disappears.

But there are still more, more pasts you still don't want to recall.

"Mm, thank you for the introduction." Blue smiled slightly at you and nodded.

He already knows very well, maybe you haven't fully realized it yet, but no matter what...... You also care about him deeply, or rather, love him.

So, Blue thought, he didn't need to say anything more; the rest was up to you guys!

But before turning to leave, he smiled and added, "Then I wish you two a happy family forever, to stay together for a long time." ”

You reply without hesitation, "Of course." ”

Then you paused, not even understanding why you said that, but Blue had already walked far, so you had no choice but to return to your previous chair and sit down, resting your chin on your hand and staring blankly at the door. You know the ticket inspection period is over, but what if? What if...... He will show up at the main gate later, then watch the blocked entrant entering the passage, looking pitifully. Then you will definitely quietly let him in.

But you waited, waited, until most of the Dark People had already left the gallery, until the other colleagues looked at you worriedly and left, the gallery was about to close, and the boss was urging you to go home, but you still didn't see him show up at the door.

You left gloomily, closed the gallery door, and walked out, while you kept looking around. You thought, what if, maybe he was just late? Wouldn't you be sad seeing the gallery closed?

But even when you returned home, you still didn't see him. Carefully opening the door, all you saw was darkness.

…… He wasn't home.

For some reason, your heart sank.

You stood at the doorway, looking at the pitch-black interior. Unconsciously, the hallway sensor light went out. After a few more minutes, your hand trembled slightly as you took out your phone. The screen became the only source of light in the darkness.

For some reason, you had a sense of foreboding—which peaked when you saw Spamton's empty message page. Normally, he would have surely messaged you, surely complained at length, yet today there was nothing.

Nothing at all.

You tentatively dial a number, but the missed ringtone and beeping echo in the stairwell...... Then comes the "number you dialed is temporarily unanswered."

You put down your phone, thinking, maybe it was just delayed for a moment?

But five minutes, ten minutes, half an hour passed, and you were still the only one standing at the door. You called again, but no one answered, and the message sent half an hour ago was still unread.

Taking a deep breath, you closed the door, then turned around and left resolutely.

No matter where he suddenly goes, you have to find him and make sure he comes home with you. You still have so many things left undone...... There are still promises you once made that haven't been fulfilled, and so many things you want to say......

And no matter who it was, everyone needed to go home.

You came to the gallery door again, thinking maybe he had just finished work in a hurry and wanted to hurry to the exhibition to surprise you. But the gallery was empty, very quiet and quiet, and you simply left in silence.

You arrive at the shopping street, thinking that maybe the big boss occasionally feels like reliving the old job—though most of the shops on the shopping street have already closed, and it's about time to close down. But those few Addisons are still standing there, probably maintaining their old habits.

You walk over, and Yellow greets you first, forcing a smile: "Hello. Do you know where Spamton is now?" ”

Yellow was stunned for a moment, because he actually didn't know where Spamton had gone. All day today, he felt the boss was acting all secretive, but he still gave you his answer: "I'm not sure, but you can go ask Pink at the company. Spamton invited us to a meeting this morning." ”

And he was only responsible for the shopping street side...... He didn't know how things were going elsewhere.

"A meeting?" "Another ...... Did something happen?" You kept feeling that Spamton's absence from home might be related to this, so you couldn't help but press further, your face showing nervousness and anxiety, and your body instinctively leaning forward.

Yellow couldn't help but lean back, his tone a bit awkward: "I'm not sure either, but today two people of light and one of darkness did come by...... They stayed on the shopping street for a while before leaving. ”

As for what happened back then, he doesn't need to repeat it to you.

You nodded, anxiously twisted your ankle, and rubbed it on the ground: "Alright, I understand." ”

Then you hurried toward Spamton, the advertising company. You know, as long as you keep going like this, you'll always find more clues, piece together the truth, and find his final whereabouts.

Even though you're now deeply nervous and worried, your brows furrowed, and you haven't even noticed the small stone under your feet, you almost stepped on it and tripped.

You stumble a bit, but don't care about that. Looking at the company so close, you can't help but run. You suddenly realize that if Spamton were by your side right now, he'd definitely grab you, carefully check if you're hurt, and then ramble on for a long time—but you don't have time to think further.

You just grabbed Pink's arm as he was about to leave. Pink looked at you in confusion, panting: "What's wrong? Why aren't you home with Spamton? What are you doing here?" He couldn't help but retort with a hint of sarcasm.

You took a few breaths before you finally felt a bit better, closed your eyes for a moment, then opened them again: "Spamton...... Not at home. You...... You really don't know...... Where is he now? ”

Pink's mouth twitched. He wanted to say, if Spamton hadn't gone to find you, then where else could he be? But then he remembered those three guys who appeared in the advertising world today, and finally withdrew his words, stammering, "...... I don't know! He disappeared after giving us the order this morning! ”

Then he shook off your arm and hurried off, somehow feeling like he was fleeing in panic.

The trail seemed to end here, and you stood there dazed for a moment, blinking lightly. In the end, without holding much hope, you decided to go back to the other end of the garbage heap where you initially found spamton to look again.

Sure enough...... There were no Dark People on this side, and the garbage heap was just a garbage heap. You crouched down, letting the frustration, distress, and anxiety sweep over you. You took out your phone to check—the message was still unread.

Spamton…… Where exactly is he? Is he hoping he can suddenly appear before him right now? Is he feeling helpless? Does he also want to go home? You take a deep breath, and this belief supports your body again. You stand up, rub your eyes, which are dark and blurred from the sudden up, and then you notice that behind the pile of garbage, there is actually a crooked little path...... Usually, when you take a shortcut this way, you never notice this path; maybe this path only appeared later.

After all, it's a rare ...... You see a slightly wilted lavender growing crookedly beside the path. You don't know how this path came to be, but you still walk up with a bit of curiosity.

Then, the flowers lining the road gradually disappeared, replaced by ...... A thin layer of snow. The further you went, the thicker the snow accumulated, the road became cold, and when the wind blew by, there was a bone-chilling cold.

You grit your teeth and walk forward. Think about it, what if...... After all, this is a place you've never been before. What if, what if, what if you can see Spamton? What if it's just a heavy snowstorm and poor signal?

Until you reach the end of the road, squinting to block a howling cold wind, you open your eyes again and see ...... Spamton, lying there, head and body apart, with his back to you—somehow, like the opening of an email, his upper body was severed.

You widened your eyes and instinctively took a step forward, about to run toward him, but his next words froze your steps in place.

He was crying—you couldn't see his expression, but through his sobbing voice, through his tearful words, you could still feel it.

"No—don't leave me alone—" he shouted loudly in the blank void, the snow-covered clearing, perhaps even knowing that no one could hear him.

You see, the phone screen is lit up and falls not far away, and he struggles to move a small part of his body, which has no limbs, only a swirling viscous liquid flowing from the cut, probably trying to touch the phone.

"I can ...... I can reveal all my business secrets...... Don't just leave me here...... Please...... "Ugh......" Then came another round of sobbing.

You were silent for a moment, wanting to lift your foot and walk toward him again, but then you heard his next words: "I want to ...... I want to call Y/N...... Yes, she definitely won't just ignore me......

He was trying hard to move toward the phone, but after struggling for a long time, he only managed to move a short distance. The slime that had flowed down had gathered in the snow and disappeared without a trace.

You silently lowered your footsteps, but fortunately, your footsteps were light and light. He couldn't hear your footsteps, probably because he was muttering to himself.

"But...... But Y/N will definitely dislike me when she sees me like this...... I'm useless, I don't look good anymore...... Maybe she'll soon abandon me and find another addison...... "No, no, no, no......" he nervously repeated.

You open your mouth, you want to say you'll never despise him, you'll never leave him, you'll only feel heartache for it—but you know he definitely wouldn't want you to see you like this, and you know he definitely wouldn't want you to hear those words. So you just stand there, saying nothing.

Only then do you suddenly realize that all along...... That's what he was thinking. Maybe he acted too relaxed on the surface, so you overlooked all this.

Your heart tightens, and you regret it. If only you'd found out sooner...... You'll tell him that no matter what, it doesn't matter, as long as he's still him.

Then you hear him mutter to himself, whispering softly, "No...... If it were Y/N, he would definitely be heartbroken...... She ...... She wouldn't despise my ...... She probably just thinks those Lightfolk are bad, and the Howling Knight is bad, that's why I ended up like this. Sorry...... Me...... I'll tell you all the business secrets, okay...... Let me go home...... Go home...... By the end, his voice was just intermittent sobbing, and he stopped trying to touch the phone that had fallen to the ground.

"Y/N must be very worried...... Let me ...... Go home...... he said softly, intermittently.

You lowered your brows and eyes, silently wiped away the tears that had fallen from your eyes, gave him one last deep look, and then turned to walk back the way you came.

You vaguely remember that the People of Darkness are all made up of objects from the Light World...... So, there is definitely a way to save Spamton in the Light World, and a way to bring him home safely. You plan to return to the Light World, to that home you haven't visited in a long time, and when you think back, it only feels darker than the Dark World.

You quickly left the trail, then quickened your pace, gradually started running, faster and faster. You knew that if you just went faster, faster, Spamton could get home sooner.

Near the entrance to the world of light, there was also a pile of garbage, and your steps gradually slowed because you saw Addison standing on one side of the road, with his back to you.

You were silent for a moment, not knowing what to say—they probably had a reason to stand here. But you just slowly stepped forward and asked, "...... What are you doing here? ”

None of the Addisons answered your question; you just saw Pink, standing at the very front and closest to you, glance at you lightly before speaking.

"I still remember that Spamton guy, really quite annoying... Ah, though I admit, I used to deliberately bully him, but that was only because he could sell nothing, bringing shame to us Addison’s—The café incident did happen before, but that time you came with him, he made me act, which is why the coffee cup was thrown at you." Although Pink speaking carried traces of anger, he was already very calm, almost maintaining a neutral tone. Even with his back turned, you could sense his clenched teeth in frustration.

And you just nod gently at his back: "Mm. I know...... I get it, it's probably just a performance. But he really went through those things, so I really feel heartache too. ”

You know very well what is true and what is false.

Pink was momentarily stunned, but then added, "And back when we were on the shopping street before—he also forced me to act, making me steal his flowers to show off, so you could play the hero and save the damsel." As he spoke, he snorted coldly again.

You recall how he might be arrogant outside, and that his high and mighty attitude was just to cover up some vulnerabilities inside...... You couldn't help but smile.

Whether it's pretending or real, you're willing to accept that he may be hypocritical or weak, not that strong, nor that formidable.

So you just nodded again: "Mm." I know, it's okay. ”

You walked a few steps past Pink's shadow, and at the same time, Orange, standing a bit ahead, shrugged and spoke, his tone a bit careless: "That Spamton guy does a lot of things behind your back just to please you. He's cooking really well now, right? ”

You paused: "Indeed." ”

When he was still that small, pitiful Addison, he could cook, but simply. You wondered when exactly... his skills suddenly improved.

Orange snorted coldly: "That guy, because he's the big boss of the company, relying on no one daring to say no, he tried out in the company's kitchen. He blew up four or five kitchens and always came out looking disheveled." ”

You want ...... You remember the look on his guilty face after blowing up the kitchen, and you also imagine his ecstatic look after he might succeed. You recall a few times when he would nervously and guiltily serve you food, twisting his fingers and saying this was his new creation, asking you to try it. And every time, after trying a few bites, you would give him a thumbs-up and say Spamton was amazing. Whenever that happened, he would put on a genuine smile. So you let out a gentle laugh.

"Is that so...... I thought he could do it after just one try. "Trying so clumsily several times actually makes him stubborn and endearing.

You stepped forward, but Orange's groan stopped you: "It's not that he's not smart. He knows how to appeal to the preferences of the Light Citizens and comes up with ways to make them watch the ads—When he helped with your exhibition's advertisement, he even thought of brilliant slogans. However, these fake flowers and tickets related to you, Spamton forced us to buy them, only this exhibition refunded us."

You lowered your eyes, recalling Spamton's reluctant expression after telling him that he wanted to refund the ticket money for someone who didn't want to attend the exhibition. He was throwing a little tantrum with you again.

You think, whether clumsy or clever, whether forced...... or gentle, it's all a reflection of a side of his personality. You don't care about these things, you know them...... Of course, you're willing to accept the truth.

So you just nod and shake your head: "I know, but it's okay...... No matter what, Spamton is the best in my heart. ”

You murmured, not knowing why you said that.

And you just keep walking forward, Orange, with his back to you, stops talking, while Yellow, standing not far ahead, speaks.

"Because the Dark People can hardly grow real flowers, Spamton would prepare fake flowers for you, right? That guy secretly planted flowers in the office before, and even planted several pots—but every one he planted died, and in the end, I secretly took the pot and threw it away." He complained, though it wasn't entirely a complaint.

Your smile stiffened for a moment—you didn't know, you only knew that the pot of lavender he planted at home almost died, and in the end, you quietly helped out a little, so the pot didn't die.

Suddenly, you recall that pure white Addison from back then...... Without your help, I might have just been obscure, withering in the darkness, like a flower left uncared for.

And you only murmured softly, "I don't even know...... He had tried so many times in secret. "If only I'd told you earlier, you definitely would have helped him...... But maybe that would break the special nature of surprises.

Yellow whistled, "Of course you don't know, that Spamton kid never intended to tell you." Speaking of which, he's been preparing a surprise again lately, but—" Just before he could say it, he hurriedly stopped the car again. What was supposed to be a surprise was spamton repeatedly reminding them not to say it, so Yellow thought, better give Spamton some face and stop talking.

"Surprise...... "I'll look forward to it." You instinctively want to ask, but you also know you need to keep the mystery of the surprise.

You know, whatever the surprise is, you'll love it, because it's prepared by Spamton. Whatever it is, whatever it looks like, or whether good or bad, you'll love it.

So, whether good or bad, success or failure, you care about Spamton, because you're willing to give it complete favoritism.

Thinking of this, you notice the beating in your chest seems to be becoming more obvious. You cover your chest in confusion, feeling your heart beating, and wonder, could it be that you're sick, which is why your heart is beating so fast?

You just press your chest and keep moving forward; the last Addison is blue.

He also turned his back to you, but spoke as if telling a story: "That day, I gave Spamton my leave request without much hope. I thought he wouldn't approve it, because he often says there's nothing to take leave for the Dark People—he never gets sick, he's in great health, and nothing else matters." ”

"Yet, at the end of that day, Spamton silently returned the leave note to me, stamped with the approval seal for the first time... Later, Spamton gradually changed, becoming less overbearing, less reckless, no longer demanding complete obedience from his staff. He began to consider employees’ opinions, though his lips always curved downward. I think... he probably wanted to get closer to you again."

You also reflected, thinking... perhaps Spamton hoped to become as just as you. No, he surely thought so, and that’s why he awkwardly went about doing it.

And you just choked up once, then softly replied with a soft "Mm."

"Lately, that guy has been showing off your paintings to us, intentionally or not. He hangs them on the meeting room wall and also on his own office wall. Before we report to him, we always have to hear his praise a few times before he gets back to the current topic."

You imagine him showing off and can't help but laugh again, your thoughts growing even stronger. You want him to go home, you want him to bounce around again, you want to hear his sticky voices...... Even if it means returning to the home you once ran away from, to that noisy, noisy world that constantly makes you feel restless and noisy.

Whether it's his childish past, or now he's a bit mature but still very childish, you still want to see him again, to keep witnessing and accompany him through the journey of change and growth. You want to see him, whether he changes or not, he's doing well. After all, that's your family's Addison.

"Yeah, I get it...... He's really adorable. "You sighed softly and kept walking forward.

Then you hear Blue's voice: "Hurry up and go...... I still remember your family photo. I still remember, when I threw him into the trash heap, I turned to look at him, and he was silently hugging his knees and crying. And in that family photo, he was laughing carefree...... I hope Spamton can do the same in the future, so go find him. Go get his smile back. ”

When he smiles carefreely, he is secretly watching you; Spamton's heart and eyes seem to be filled with you.

You silently wiped away the tears at the corner of your eyes, hurriedly responded with a "Mm," then stepped into the light. After a wave of dizziness, you uncomfortably covered your ears, blocking out the noisy crowd.

You step out and see people waiting at the bus stop. The noisy sounds mixed with occasional curses surround you, but you frown, avoid the crowd, step onto the pedestrian bridge, and squeeze into the subway station...... The ticking of machines and the dense footsteps of the crowd hit your eardrums, and you hurriedly check your phone and enter the station, standing at the edge, waiting for the subway to arrive.

Accompanied by the sound of the subway passing through the tunnel and the announcement announcement, countless people get off the train, and you squeeze onto the train with the crowd. Although no one is making a loud noise on the subway, the constant announcement of arrival still makes you instinctively want to cover your ears, just to clearly hear the next destination, yet you still do nothing.

Gradually, your heart, irritated by the city's clamor, calmed a little. You recalled pieces from Addison's words and slowly pieced together Spamton’s image outside. You couldn’t help but quietly smile.

You thought, perhaps all of this no longer seemed so terrifying. You’ve grown up. You can protect Spamton, and you can protect your past self, no longer helplessly crying amid everyone’s strange gazes in the noisy market, no longer dragged forcefully only to face harsh, scolding voices.

You look up, your expression clear, and as the announcement of the subway arrives at the station, you follow the crowd, walking off the subway with head held high.
