Time Practices (Six)

[6]

He just lowered his head and didn't look at you anymore, almost hugging his knees—after all, his body was trembling at this moment, and his voice was soft: "I don't know...... I don't know what you're thinking. I'm just afraid, afraid you'll hate me...... I'm afraid you'll think I'm terrible, bad, afraid you'll be afraid of me...... Spamton didn't know what he was talking about; maybe he was just ...... afraid you'd stop projecting your own love onto him. Even though he wasn't sure if that counted as love, since it made him feel warmth and attachment, reluctance, and happiness...... Then it should be.

You were silent for a while, and because you hadn't received your answer, Spamton nervously lifted his head. At that moment, you cupped his cheek and shook your head solemnly: "No, why would I be afraid of you, hate you, or hate you? You're my favorite addison...... You're really amazing. You've become the master of the advertising world and managed to keep it from me for so long, which shows you've worked really hard! "You smile brightly, even though you don't know yourself...... Why did you say that word so smoothly?

What is liking? And what is love? You have no idea; you only judge based on your only rational logic that you should give your home the addison and other things.

He stared at you blankly. For some reason, you felt the spot where your palm touched seemed a bit hot, and you could only tilt your head in confusion. Spamton broke free from your hand, hurriedly turned his head, and seemed to pat his cheek with his hand, stammering, "Mm, I like you the most, Y/N!" ”

You touch your chest, not knowing why...... Every time you hear him say that, you can actually feel the heart in your chest beating faster. But this time, that feeling becomes especially obvious. You can't help but wonder: could he actually have an infectious disease, which is why his heart is beating so fast?

Thinking this, you couldn't help but ask, your tone very serious: "Do you really have some infectious disease bacteria on you? Otherwise, why would my heart be beating so fast?" ”

And for some reason, your heart seems to be beating faster, and you feel like it's about to jump out of your throat the next second—of course, that's probably just your imagination.

Spamton turned to look at you, looking straight into your eyes and biting his lip—after all, when he heard what you said, his first reaction was ecstasy, but then he just found it a bit funny. After all, it turns out you don't understand anything either.

He simply nodded solemnly: "Yes, I probably have an infectious disease on me, because my heart is beating very fast." "Even if—even as a Dark Citizen, he couldn't be sure there was this organ in his body—Spamton could feel that mysterious object in his chest was thumping and thumping.

You hurriedly asked, "What is the name of this infectious disease? I can buy some medicine to cure both of us. And since you have an infectious disease...... Then you've definitely been sick for even longer, so you need to get treatment sooner...... You grumbled, your brows furrowed tightly, leaving only anxiety.

But Spamton just shook his head: "The name of this infectious disease...... It's Y/N. ”

You froze when you heard this answer, the anxious look on your face faded away, and you stared at him in a daze. You didn't expect to hear this answer—and you only felt your cheeks start to burn, your heart seemed to beat faster. You always felt as if you had already received some answer, but you didn't dare to ask further—even though the answers were right in front of you, you just found it somewhat ridiculous.

So you hastily changed the subject, planning to show Spamton your past later, but now your resolve is even stronger. He has already let you know his past, present, and future, so to be fair, you should let him know too.

Or rather, you originally wanted him to know.

So you cleared your throat, just turned your head, got up, and walked toward your own room: "...... Ahem, ahem, I—the surprise I prepared still has the second half. Come to my room and take a look." Your voice became a bit stammered, but in your panic, you quickened your pace.

Spamton scratched his cheek, which had just cooled down, and couldn't help but laugh at your flustered back. He felt a bit pleased, but also a little disappointed—maybe because you hadn't given him a direct answer, but he longed to hear those words from you? Maybe it was because he longed for it. Even though he had already gained so much by your side, he was insatiable and only wanted more. Spamton himself knew this wasn't a good habit, but under your indulgence, he had become what he was today.

He followed you into your room. To be honest, he had never set foot before, only sneaking glances when you were there. Of course, Spamton was just there to see you.

And just today, he finally stepped into your room and followed you down by the bed. Spamton didn't know what you were trying to do until he saw you dragging a dusty box out from under the bed.

That box...... It was covered in dust, with no marks or patterns on it—just a pure white box, as if waiting for someone to dye it. Spamton had never seen this box before, not even once since he started living in your house.

His voice was also a bit dry, probably carrying a fear even he didn't know: "This...... What's inside the box? "Maybe it's like Pandora's box—once opened, there's no going back.

You didn't look at Spamton, just quietly lowered your head to look at the box and softly said, "...... It's my past, the past I once buried with my own hands. ”

"Do you hate your past? Well, you don't have to treat these as part of the surprise to show me—" Spamton's eyes widened, hurriedly waving his hand, while you simply held his hand and placed it on the lid.

You gently shook your head: "...... Come open the box. It's okay, I ...... too. You don't really hate your past, do you?" Your voice was dry. You knew it was a heartfelt remark, and you believed Spamton could tell through it.

He blinked at you, his hand resting on the lid of the box without moving: "...... But why? ”

You propped your chin on your hand and thought seriously for a moment, then spoke uncertainly: "Hmm...... Because you actually showed me your past too? I think I should show you mine too. And...... After all this time, I should let go, and I should let go of my obsession with the past. "Your voice was soft, but it sounded very heavy to Spamton's ears. He turned his head blankly to look at you.

“…… Because I should also move toward the future. I want to walk into the future with you." You still didn't look at him, and you didn't know why, at this moment, you didn't dare turn around to look at him. Spamton stared at your profile, but in the end said nothing, just opened the box.

What greeted him was a complete set of painting supplies, a dozen oil paintings, unfinished works, and several graffiti-like artworks—the brushstrokes were immature, yet full of creativity and creativity.

You didn't say a word, nor did you watch Spamton's movements; you just listened to the rustling sound beside you, like turning pages, and nervously swallowed hard.

He flipped through your paintings one by one, quietly admiring each one, his eyes growing brighter and brighter.

Then you hear his voice: "This painting is imitating that 'Sunflowers', right?" The brushwork and coloring are very delicate...... Also, the shapes are more vivid. Although I'm not sure how to comment on the painting, this painting feels very warm and cozy, and I think it's even better than the original! ”

You opened your mouth, wanting to say, how is that possible? The original is so famous, but he just flipped to the next oil painting: "This one...... Is this your self-portrait? The painting definitely doesn't look as good as yours...... But it's quite interesting—breaking into many petals like this—is it a way to observe yourself from different angles? ”

You closed your mouth and quietly watched him flip through the next oil painting: "Is this a still life sketch...... I'm not sure, but that's probably the title. Y/N's fundamentals are really good! "He said with a cheerful smile.

You want to interrupt him, but Spamton just keeps going, "And this one...... Ah, it looks like just a depiction of natural scenery—is it a maple forest? Or yellow or red leaves, giving off a very bleak autumn atmosphere! ”

It's clear he doesn't analyze the artwork; he's just speaking based on his feelings. Yet Spamton still flips through your artworks one by one, earnestly giving feedback, until he finally finds your childhood doodles.

You instinctively want to snatch that painting away, since showing your childhood doodles is somewhat embarrassing—you just couldn't help but take away this graffiti, which once represented your imagination and creativity, while packing up at home.

Spamton tilted his head to look at the painting and also seriously commented, "Countless cars traveling through space...... Some cars carry the sun, some carry the moon, and some carry stars? It looks very much like a modern version of a mythological story! The brushstrokes in this painting are quite immature, but they still perfectly capture the unique characteristics of each vehicle...... "Even if it's just a doodle."

You silently looked at him, suddenly remembering—that day, your parents tore your painting in half, saying they wanted to keep half as a keepsake, and only half remained in the box, only ...... Only the half of you was drawn, and besides that, later you added a white shadow beside it. You don't know who this is, nor do you know...... When will you finish this incomplete painting?

And the day your painting is torn in half is the day before you go to college to register.

You swallowed hard and didn't stop Spamton, but flipped to the last half-incomplete painting. He stared blankly at the white shadow, then picked up a black crayon from the box and wobbly smeared it on a pair of sunglasses.

"Only Y/N...... That's too lonely. That white shadow standing next to you must be me! But without sunglasses, it doesn't really look like ......," he muttered, admiring his work with great pride.

But you didn't say anything, just reached out and hugged him tightly. You didn't want him to see you crying, didn't want him to see the redness in your eyes. Even though tears were streaming down your cheeks, they still soaked his clothes.

Spamton was momentarily stunned, but just gently patted your back with his hand.

At that moment, you silently embraced. Spamton lowered his brows, thinking—since you've treated him so well, he should repay you with equal respect. He thought it was only natural, but hadn't expected you to cry—after all, warm tears were now soaking your clothes.

And at this moment...... It seemed a strange emotion surged in his chest—no, not entirely unfamiliar—he knew it was heartache for you. Besides that, Spamton didn't know much about your past, but from your half-torn painting, he could tell your past was bleak...... Or rather, it was similar to his? What followed immediately was an inexplicable sense of emotion.

…… After all, your past was probably not beautiful. For you, finding him, deciding to bring him home, and deciding to live with you—are those things truly wonderful? Even so...... He can still sense a love from you that isn't very smooth, even a bit awkward and sluggish.

He didn't know...... No, Spamton can now be sure your life must have changed a lot, definitely become better! After all, you just said he's your favorite Addison! Spamton perked up again, and he also wanted to show off to the other Dark People by showing them his own Y/N paintings—although he couldn't see anything extra, he could feel that every painting carried your emotions, and each one only made him marvel at that kind of beauty.

So he gently patted your back again and asked, "...... Y/N, have you considered holding a solo exhibition of your paintings? ”

You were stunned for a moment, lowered your eyes, and said nothing. You never imagined your paintings could be held for exhibitions—after all, you're not a famous artist; these are just your hobbies. Just like your parents said, it's just a hobby.

So you said it too: "...... It's just a hobby. If you take it out, there's no one to look at, right?" Your voice was a bit hoarse.

Moreover, although you let Spamton see these paintings, you weren't prepared to let other Darkfolk come to see them as well.

"So what if it's a hobby? As long as you want, you can display these paintings for everyone to see!" Spamton shouted, almost imagining that if someone didn't come to see the painting, it would be a loss for them.

You let go, just sat on the ground and looked him in the eye, hesitating for a moment before saying, "Alright...... Then shall I think about it? ”

Spamton's eyes lit up, and he persistently said, "Hurry up and think about it! And if you say these paintings are created by the People of Light, there will definitely be a lot of people of Darkness coming to see them!" "He shakes your arm, probably acting spoiled, but you can see his eyes sparkling as he looks at you."

You coughed awkwardly twice, a bit embarrassed, and changed the subject: "...... If other Dark People came over eagerly to take a look, only to find that the Light Folk only had this level of painting, they'd be disappointed...... "Before he could finish, Spamton interrupted him.

"No, your painting is beautiful. Not a single Dark Folk would find it ugly!" Spamton promised you with absolute confidence.

He'd already thought it through, and when the time came...... At worst, he'd just spend some dark coins to buy tickets himself, have his employees go, and just praise your artwork!

And you just quietly watched him, as if you had already guessed what he was thinking: "Don't even think about hiring your employees to come to my art exhibition...... No, I haven't even agreed to hold an art exhibition yet." "No matter how you feel like I've already been drawn into it...... You rubbed your forehead in frustration.

“…… Me. I'm not hiring employees......" His gaze drifted, clearly showing that Spamton had been thinking about this just now.

You sighed, just rubbed his head, and pulled him up from the ground: "Alright, let's talk about this another time." "If we keep discussing, you'll really have to blindly follow his words and start an art exhibition."

"No. I think Y/N, you really should hold an art exhibition and show all these paintings! I'll help you advertise a bit, and then all the Dark People will be able to see them—"How wonderful it is to see their own Light Folk then!" Thinking of this, Spamton couldn't help but feel proud again.

And you interrupted him again: "No...... No need. I don't need you to help with advertising. "Advertising is not like hiring employees to come to an art exhibition; it's all about forcing your own dark people to come.

The hair on his head drooped again, and his head drooped as he said gloomily, "...... Oh. ”

You couldn't help but rub his head again, then grabbed his hand and walked out, softly asking, "Alright...... So, what you told me before, you sold 100 ...... Hmm, artworks—are they real? "You think you'd better quickly change the subject, so Spamton doesn't keep looking gloomy, and he won't keep getting hung up on the art exhibition. After all...... You haven't decided yet. You want to wait until you can truly make your own choices, and don't want Spamton to get coaxed and push you into holding an art exhibition—that won't make you happy.

Only the rewards you receive after sincere choices will make you happy—of course, you chose to pick up this Addison from the trash, brought him home to live together, and made this place his home too, which was naturally a heartfelt choice.

The hair on his head stood up again, and he patted his chest to promise you, "Of course! I really sold a hundred portions!" "Although all of them were sold to his employees."

You just whispered softly, "Oh?" and he simply said mysteriously, "This ...... is all business secrets! ”

You stopped, turned around, and quietly stared at him. Spamton, because you suddenly stopped, almost bumped into your arms, his nose just a tiny step away from your chest. He instinctively widened his eyes, stopped abruptly, and stammered as he asked, "Suddenly ...... Why did you suddenly stop......" He felt a bit uncertain, his voice gradually fading, and he lowered his head, not daring to look at you anymore.

You hold his face, making him lift his head to meet your gaze, while his eyes just roll around, not daring to look at you.

You just repeat once more: "Is it really true?" ”

He raised his voice: "Of course it's true! And I told them that if they sell an extra copy, they'll get a commission of 50 Dark Coins!" ”

"Our Spamton is really great...... But those 100 servings—aren't you really the one selling them to your employees? "Your voice was light, after all, last time it was 100, and this time it's 100, which naturally arouses your suspicion.

“…… It really is sold to the employees." Spamton's voice was muffled, and his voice lowered. He still admitted to you about it, then raised his voice again, "But—but as long as they sell a few more copies, they can make it back, since there's a commission anyway!" ”

"Yeah, I know there's a commission...... You should also reward outstanding employees. But besides this hundred, do you have any other achievements? ”

You pulled Spamton to sit on the sofa and asked softly. Spamton pulled his hand back and began fiddling with the fingers of both his hands, his voice a bit dry: "...... That's all. ”

This answer is within your expectations. You don't think this so-called artwork will sell much, but looking at it this way...... Spamton actually didn't tell many truths, although he did tell part of it, but only about half.

But you just rubbed his head: "Hmm...... Are you planning to do these business again, or rather, to advertise these artworks? Ah, including the previous trash can flower arrangements and such. ”

Spamton nodded sharply. Of course he wanted to, after all, he thought you actually liked it, so he naturally assumed that the other Dark and Light folks would like it too—though that was just unreasonable. He just rubbed the top of his head back and forth against your palm, muttering softly, "But with all the ads flying everywhere, there are still hardly any customers...... It feels like it's completely useless." "But he doesn't want to give up just like that. He even plans to show you the jaw-dropping sales figures to make you happy."

You reached out and hooked his chin again, watching Spamton squint his eyes, resting his chin in your palm, tilting his head slightly up, looking thoroughly enjoying it. After thinking for a moment, you said, "In that case...... Have you considered changing the promotional approach, or simply switching to a different product? ”

He shook his head and nodded: "I've changed my marketing approach, even tried bundled sales or price reductions, but still, no Dark or Light folks have seen these products...... As for switching to another product, Spamton never considered it.

You narrowed your eyes slightly and smiled as you offered your suggestion: "Is that so...... Then, how about selling real flowers directly? ”

Spamton was taken aback. He recalled the few pots of flowers he had raised dead in his office before, and it seemed he had never heard of any cases of keeping flowers alive...... As for the trees that existed in the Dark World Plains, they seemed to have existed for a long time as well. No one knew how those trees came to be or how they grew, since they could still bear dark candy.

"But...... If it's a real flower, it's hard to keep it alive......" he whispered. For example, he never succeeded; only that pot of lavender you occasionally tended to it managed to grow and produce clusters of tiny purple flowers.

You waved your fingers and made a wink: "Ah...... Because the Dark World lacks sunlight, it's normal that they can't survive. But I have a special formula here. ”

Saying that, you walked back to your room. When you came back, you had a touch light in your hand—a simple full-spectrum plant lamp you developed long ago to successfully plant flowers in the Dark World...... Although you don't need it now.

You shoved the full-spectrum plant lamp into Spamton's hand, resting your chin on your hand: "With this, you can keep it alive." But I don't make much of this myself, so it's limited—first come, first served! ”

You seem to have some talent for business, or maybe after spending a lot of time with Spamton, you've learned a bit more.

Then you waved your hand again: "But if you want plants to grow better, you'll need a secret recipe...... And different plants need special treatment, and each plant has its own requirements." But at this point, you didn't say anything more.

Spamton blinked. Honestly, your words had piqued his interest, so he couldn't help but ask, "And then?" ”

And you just smile and wave your hand: "Well...... Business secret! ”

Spamton puffed out his cheeks: "Alright...... Secret is confidential!" Although he didn't press further, he still felt a bit unwilling. Spamton fiddled with the full-spectrum plant lamp, studied it roughly, and figured his staff should be able to replicate it too—whether it works or not, it has to be done. Although he couldn't understand it, he believed that among all those Dark People, there must be someone who could figure it out.

So he asked, "Exactly...... Y/N, if I take this thing and replicate it, it should be fine, right?" "After all, no matter what, it's still something you created...... He can't just copy it casually. If it's someone else's thing, he doesn't care; if he uses it freely, he won't blame him for any trouble. But this is yours, Spamton believes he should show you enough respect.

You paused for a moment, touched your chin, and said, "Hmm...... Are you thinking of doing some promotions? For example, these are the first batch of limited edition treasures, and after that are regular batches or something? ”

Spamton nodded heavily. That's exactly what he thought—after all, doing this would earn him more Dark Coins, so why not do it?

You nodded thoughtfully: "It's possible, but after all, this isn't my invention. I just replicated it using materials available in the Dark World. So if you want to recreate it, just go ahead and do it!" ”

Spamton raised both hands and cheered like a child: "Yay—"

You couldn't help but chuckle, patted his head, and he nuzzled your palm against it.

Now, he no longer spoke in a pitiful tone, and simply said directly: "Y/N!" Let me tell you—the queen of the Dark World next door is really annoying. Clearly, our advertising world wouldn't steal business from the cyber world, yet she still wants a piece of the pie, thinking our flower arranging business must have made a lot of money, and even wants to secretly learn our craft and sell it. "Although Addison really did come from the cyber world...... But Spamton didn't care. When he felt wronged, he wanted to tell you, even though sometimes he thought it was just unreasonable trouble.

You just scratched his chin thoughtfully: "So, how do you want to solve this?" ”

Spamton closed his eyes comfortably, even making snoring sounds. He just mumbled a little unclearly, "Well, of course we hid our exclusive business secrets. Anyway, she definitely won't be able to sell them!" He said confidently.

Then he opened his eyes and saw your smiling face, his voice involuntarily lowered: "But we don't have any exclusive business secrets...... But only after you suggested did he finally master the trade secrets again. Before this, it was only because he pressured employees to buy, and those employees pressured the Dark People of the advertising world to buy them, which was why sales seemed higher.

You just tilted your head and scratched the soft, sparse hair beside his cheek: "Hmm, don't we already have some business secrets?" "If that's the case, you should keep the trade secrets safe." ”

Spamton nodded vigorously again: "I understand—" He dragged out his voice, as if being playful.

"Hmm...... But if the neighboring dark world wants a piece of the pie, it might not be a bad idea. "You thought it over for a moment.

Spamton just puffed out his cheeks again: "Isn't it only a limited edition fashion item from the Dark World that looks better? That way, I can raise the price too!" ”

You gently flicked his forehead, and even without force, Spamton pretended to be in pain, clutching his forehead tightly, then looked at you with tears in his eyes, making you laugh again.

"Think about it, if there's only one Dark World to sell, then if the Dark People from other Dark Worlds want to get these limited fashion items, they'll either have to go to this Dark World to buy them, or have to rely on Dark People who can travel between them...... Most Dark People probably don't have the ability to do that, right? That would just let middlemen profit and resell them, wouldn't they? ”

"Then I'll just have the Dark People with that ability sell in other dark worlds......" Anyway, the Dark Coins he earned ended up in his own pocket, Spamton thought, without much conscience.

You shake your fingers and shake your head seriously: "Not good. Even if your employees hold up huge banners and advertise, it's hard to gain the trust of other Dark People...... Besides, even more Dark People won't see it." ”

Spamton raised his voice: "The ad pop-up will appear on all the Lightfolk computers—no one will miss seeing it!" He swore confidently.

Seeing him like this, you get a bit distracted and suddenly recall the ad pop-ups and all those random spam emails with all kinds of ads on your computer before. You can't help but be curious: "By the way, do you manage all the ad pop-ups and spam emails?" ”

Spamton immediately fell silent. He cautiously looked up at you, suddenly feeling a bit embarrassed to speak...... Back when he was just an ordinary addison, just a regular spam-sending addison, he really did like to visit your computer. Although the cyber world is built on another laptop—in a sense, they can roam among the computers of the Lightfolk.

And you...... Your computer inbox is never emptied, so it can fool itself by telling yourself maybe you saw those emails, maybe you saw those ads. You just don't know when you deleted all those emails.

Probably the day before he heard that a Light Folk had arrived in the Dark World.

Spamton just shook his head and nodded: "Pretty much, so all the spam and ads are managed by us...... I'm really something!" He was feeling proud again, though he couldn't help but feel a bit guilty. If he wasn't online, he wouldn't be able to touch that computer either.

You just smiled and patted his forehead: "Spamton is amazing—it can manage ads on so many computers!" ”

Spamton also looked up. Looking at him now, he looked a bit like a peacock happily spreading its feathers, and you couldn't help but laugh again.

"So...... Are those spam emails on my computer also sent by you? "You asked softly.

Spamton was a bit stuck. He glanced at you timidly, then lowered his head again: "I'm not going to post ads anymore! It's all the Dark People under my command who are posting them—I just need to set up the overall strategy!" ”

…… Now, no one should be placing ads in your inbox anymore. After all, ever since you arrived in the Dark World, Spamton has never been able to enter that familiar computer anymore.

You nodded, drowning out your "Oh—" sound. Seeing his somewhat guilty expression, you couldn't help but ask again: "So, which Dark Citizen is actually responsible for placing ads in my email?" ”

Spamton lowered his head, unusually a bit afraid to look at you, and just whispered, "...... Back when I was still Addison, I always sent mail to your mailbox...... After you came to the Dark World, I never sent mail again. "I can't send it anymore."

You blink, then your pupils widen. You don't realize that before that, you had already encountered Spamton—though you don't clear those emails, you also don't click in to view them. It wasn't until you decided to leave home and erased all traces of the past that you shredded and deleted all those emails, because you never intended to recover them.

At this moment, Spamton started drawing again: "But I guess I'm just a Dark People who exist because of concepts...... Probably not because of some physical object? But I think, since I've sent so many emails to your mailbox, in a way, it can be considered a concept that makes up my body! But I'm the master of advertising! I definitely don't lack these conceptual components! ”

You couldn't help but nod and patted his head again. You probably understand...... In a way, he exists because of you. In a way, it's precisely because of you that he has been able to get through the years gone by.

"Alright, alright, I get it—I feel like the topic is a bit off-topic, but regarding the other dark worlds just trying to learn from your methods, I can offer some of my opinion." You cleared your throat and said seriously.

"So what advice does Y/N have to give? I'll definitely listen!" Spamton said eagerly, sounding a bit eager to answer. If other Dark People gave him advice, he definitely wouldn't even pay attention! But he would listen to you...... Even if he occasionally found it unpleasant, he would keep listening. Of course, you wouldn't take it seriously or say anything unpleasant.

Because he knows you care about him, you care for him, and that's why he says those things. It's also because you don't want him to be sad that you won't say anything hurtful.

And he...... He cares about you and cares about your words, so no matter what you say, he listens. After all, Spamton knows this is something you painstakingly came up with for him.

Bad or unpleasant words or sad things can be discussed properly. Of course, he believes you won't do this, and he can't do it either, because he cares about you the most.

"Hmm...... Although I think having your employees sell in other dark worlds is a good idea, it's still a bit troublesome, and if too many people buy it, your staff won't be able to keep up, right? So you can cooperate with other dark worlds—you handle supply, they handle sales—and if it sells well, then you get a commission! "You waved your finger.

Spamton simply leaned on your shoulder, playing with your fingers, acting affectionately, and neither of you thought it was a problem. He just said excitedly, "Y/N is so smart! I never even thought of such a solution!" ”

…… Actually, when we were chatting and discussing earlier, he thought of this too, but didn't say it out loud. Well, he just wanted to compliment you.

You cleared your throat, your cheeks rarely flushed, and just waved your hand: "...... I don't think I'm anything special. You must have thought about that just now, right? ”

You still know your Addison very well...... Of course, he can think of such a simple thing as well.

Spamton just shook his head: "I did think of it...... But you're still amazing. Look, you know gardening, you can paint, and you can develop this kind of plant lamp...... You know a lot! Even if you don't do well in some areas, you're already an impressive person! "He was counting on his fingers and carefully listing your strengths.

You blinked sluggishly, then rubbed your slightly reddened eyes, and just muffled a "Mm."

You never thought that before, your parents only scolded you for having a hobby that wasted your spirit...... Now you can be praised as amazing.
