Shi Xing 4 (swapsptx you)

Spamton left proudly early the next morning. You packed up and went to work. Although you were a bit curious about how Spamton sold the fake flowers and pots he mentioned—you were busy with work today and didn't have time to check out Spamton's shop.

As for Spamton, after arriving at the advertiser's office, he immediately held a meeting. Once the other Dark People had arrived, he boldly announced what he would be selling next: "Next, we're going to heavily promote the artworks." ”

The employees looked around and whispered among themselves, probably curious about how the advertiser, who originally wasn't very interested in art, had started caring about artworks.

In the end, Blue stepped forward, probably because he was the calmest among the Addisons. Like the others, he squinted, but when he looked at Spamton, he had a hint of submissiveness—even though he used to be their colleague, he had now become their superior.

“…… So, what style of artwork should we promote? "Blue asked, and the other employees all looked at him with admiration, making Blue break out in a cold sweat.

When asked this question, Spamton was a bit stuck. He really knew that you really like art...... Or something with aesthetic appeal? Otherwise, he wouldn't have placed so many decorations in that cramped home, nor put flower arrangements on the living room table, even if they were just fake flowers. He actually couldn't appreciate those arts because he simply couldn't understand them.

So the advertiser rarely fell silent for a moment. In the past, no matter what meeting he held, he would talk endlessly with almost no other employees getting a chance to speak. But today, he fell into a rare silence, making the staff below start to feel uneasy. Blue even began to wonder if he had said anything wrong.

In the end, the advertiser just waved his hand: "...... Promote any art! As long as it's beautiful, that's enough! ”

"But first, what we want to advertise is the flower-arranging pots made from trash cans—those bins are designed in artistic styles, so these bins are repurposed, can be used as artworks or as ordinary trash bins......" He finally began to introduce them enthusiastically, beaming with excitement, much longer than the string I mentioned yesterday. At the same time, he opened several pop-ups and began displaying all kinds of trash bins and his so-called flower arrangement artworks.

Blue hesitated, actually wanting to say that these trash bins probably had no real selling value—very few people would actually buy them.

But this time, yellow was the first to raise his hand and speak. He also opened a pop-up window displaying a black-and-white photo, also a work of art shaped like a urinal.

“…… Boss, do we want to promote this kind of art too?" yellow asked, magnifying the pop-up several times and pointing it right in front of Spamton.

Spamton originally wanted to scold Yellow a bit, but when he saw this so-called installation artwork, he fell silent—he was completely! No! He saw a bit of beauty in this thing!

In the end, he waved his hand and flicked the pop-up window with a big cross: "No, don't promote this kind of thing." At this point, he had to admit that maybe he couldn't quite understand the aesthetic of the Lightfolk...... And judging from the data he had, there were even many Lightfolk who pursued this kind of art. Spamton didn't understand, and he also thought you probably wouldn't like these things.

But...... There are still quite a few people of light who chase after these things? So he hesitated for a moment, then changed his tune: "Forget it, if it's art...... We can also promote it, as long as it's art-related. But things that look beautiful to the naked eye should be heavily promoted, like the flower arranging I just mentioned." He was quite justified, opening several more pop-up windows. Besides the flower arrangements from the trash can earlier, there were also some paintings he knew were popular among humans, such as "Sunrise," "Sunflowers," and "Starry Night."

…… Of course, there were also some abstract paintings he really couldn't understand, and Spamton wasn't sure why the People of Light liked such things. But since they liked them, they could just promote them.

"If there's no problem, then adjourn. I think you all understood what I just said, right?" He waved his hand, ready to send them away.

At that moment, pink stepped forward: "...... Boss, I don't think flower arranging from trash bins has any promotional value. From what I know, the People of Light usually only use slender bottles for flower arranging. ”

“…… But this kind of dual-purpose bucket should suit the preferences of the Lightfolk, since such a slender container has no other use. Spamton stuck to his point. Not only did he hate being disobeyed, but he also wanted to prove himself—his proposal was indeed effective, and you'd definitely see him make a fortune!

"No, but the People of Light probably wouldn't buy it. After all, their families usually already have similar ......," Pink hadn't finished speaking before Spamton interrupted, who looked very impatient.

"Stop talking, they'll definitely buy it. Also, I think your family probably lacks art decorations too. How about this: for now, buy a pot of flowers for each household based on the Dark Citizen unit." He declared fiercely, completely unconcerned about the expressions of the employees below. Some employees glared at him through gritted teeth, while others just trembled and lowered their heads. The addisons exchanged glances, probably sending some signal with their eyes, but in the end, they just lowered their heads in agreement.

Spamton, on the other hand, didn't care at all. After all, he was the master of the advertising world, and whatever the world wanted was up to him.

So he nodded in satisfaction, though he was a bit annoyed...... No matter what, he hated hearing his words contradicted, especially now with these employees he looked down on. He started to miss you, the taste of home, the warmth of your palm, your faint smile.

…… Now, Spamton suddenly really wants to go home. Just seeing you and talking to you is enough.

But he knows you're not home, so the little bit of anger he's built up can only turn into unspeakable grievance, waiting for you to come home and complain to you.

"Alright, meeting adjourned!" Spamton waved his hand, signaling the other Dark People to leave. He didn't want to see those people for the time being, so he drew a big cross to cover his face, so he wouldn't see those annoying guys again. At the same time, he created another pop-up displaying the price of flower arrangements in the trash can. Each pot was half-price for a limited time, only 250 Dark Coins!

On this day, the advertiser pulled out a card reader and slaughtered the dark coins of his employees. He looked at his income with joy, ready to show off to you when he got home.

Even though the flower arrangement in the trash can ads were broadcast, it seemed there were no pop-ups clicking on the Light Citizens.

Meanwhile, you are busy with your daily work in the gallery, cleaning the dusty frames and preparing to display the artworks for the next exhibition. Just then, a colleague nearby seems to notice something and starts coming over to gossip with you.

"Ah, our tickets for this exhibition sold out really fast! It seems like the promotion lasted less than three hours and tickets were already sold out! The store manager is still considering whether to put out more tickets!" It said this while winking and making faces.

Your speed of cleaning the frame has slowed down. Actually, selling tickets doesn't really concern you, but the more tickets you sell, the higher your share will be. But usually, tickets sold out have to be pulled and given away, and you have to figure out how to give away some before you can barely sell out in half a month. How come this time it sold out in less than three hours? You always find this a bit strange.

“…… Is that so? Maybe the artist in this exhibition is very famous? "You answer absentmindedly, starting to wonder how Spamton's work is going and whether other colleagues will make things difficult. Although he tells you he'll definitely sell it, you don't really believe it, and now you're worried he'll be harassed by other colleagues again.

If only I had known...... I would have reminded him a few more times yesterday.

Actually, ever since you first started cleaning the frame, you've been worried something was off, which is why you've been cleaning it for so long.

The colleague smacked her lips in dissatisfaction: "No, this time it's just an unknown little painter!" Extremely unknown, the store manager was worried that less than half of the tickets might not sell, but now they were sold out. She was overjoyed. ”

Then she tugged at you again: "You've been wiping this frame since earlier, distracted. Tell me, is something wrong?" "She started making faces with you."

You coughed awkwardly twice, released the frame you'd already polished from the past, and started wiping the next one: "...... No, I don't have any issues. You know very well. I'm a citizen of light, and in the dark world, I don't know anyone except you. ”

Lying. You know you're lying, and you know your lies aren't very sophisticated, which makes you a bit nervous.

But your colleague just dragged out her words with an "Oh," seemingly not suspecting you: "I knew it, you don't look like you'd be looking for a boyfriend either." As she spoke, she moved a bit farther away from you and started tidying up the curtains and screens used for the exhibition on that side.

You paused for a moment and stopped what you were doing. She hadn't realized you were lying...... Maybe after spending so much time with Spamton, you've become a bit of a liar? You're not really sure.

But she's right—you don't want to find a boyfriend, nor do you want to be in a romantic relationship. For you...... That's an unlikely thing.

You lowered your eyes, recalling the food that had been placed on the table until it cooled, the argument that day, the moment you couldn't bear it anymore, covering your ears and instinctively running out, wanting to get as far as possible, and in the end, like Alice who had stumbled down a rabbit hole, you fell into this dark world.

To you, the dark world is much better than the light world. Think about it, your family's Addison wouldn't be like this, and when you think of Spamton, the corners of your mouth can't help but lift slightly. If your colleague saw this, they'd be shocked—they'd definitely ask who you're thinking of to make you so happy.

Maybe you want to go home, you want to see Spamton, even knowing he might be lying, you still want to hear his voice. At that moment, you start looking forward to the end of work, as if you can hear Spamton's words "Welcome home" again.

But when he says he likes you the most...... He probably just said it casually. Or maybe it's just that you haven't thought it through.
