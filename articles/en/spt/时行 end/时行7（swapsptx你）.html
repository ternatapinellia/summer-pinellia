Shi Xing 7 (swapsptx you)

Early the next morning, Spamton didn't even bother to count your wake-up time. After preparing breakfast for you, he hurried out and left you a note with a messy handwriting, clearly written in a hurry. The gist was that he had urgent matters to attend to and had to leave early today and couldn't accompany you.

Last night, he was anxious for ages, but when the hot pizza came out, he found you had fallen asleep again, leaving him with mixed emotions. Half guilty and guilty, half a sense of relief—at least he didn't have to think about how to explain it to you on the spot. But seeing you wait for him for so long and probably worry all day, Spamton felt heartbroken.

No matter what, he still carried you to bed, then carefully covered you with the blanket and took off your outer clothes. He didn't want you to catch a cold the next day just because you slept in the living room.

As for what he meant by urgency—naturally, he was making the most of the time to hold a meeting and have the employees follow his instructions. He had made up his mind. Today, he was going to reopen the shop he hadn't run for a long time. It was covered in quite a bit of dust, which would make his lie seem a bit more credible.

So Spamton went to the meeting room and urgently notified his employees to come and hold a meeting.

The other Dark People were all muttering among themselves, wondering what the boss was up to again. They had just held a meeting a couple of days ago, so why was it happening again today?

Spamton calculated the expenses and couldn't help but grit his teeth. Even though maybe it could cost nothing......

He placed his hand on the table, stood up, and looked at the other Dark People announced, "Today, Addison is still working normally on the commercial street, while the other Dark Folk will be going to Addison's shops to buy flower arrangements." ”

The previously noisy Dark People now quieted down. Pippins instinctively wanted to argue, since they didn't have extra money to buy them! Previously, an employee was forced to buy a single flower arrangement and had already used up all their savings...... But that wasn't the case.

Shadow Guy played the saxophone, producing a discordant sound, while Zapper bounced in place, seemingly wanting to say something but holding back.

Immediately after, Spamton displayed a panel labeled 250 Dark Coins: "I'll transfer the corresponding Dark Coins to your accounts. Just go buy them then." ”

He cleared his throat again: "Also, I'll be opening my own shop on the commercial street...... You won't be visiting my shop then." Saying this, he glanced around at the Addison below. The Addisons instinctively tensed up. Judging by the situation, Spamton would probably be making things difficult for them. Blue felt a bit helpless. He had thought yesterday's matter was over, but Spamton looked like he was going all the way to the end.

He clearly dislikes the custom of the People of Darkness instinctively trying to please the People of Light...... But he keeps trying to please you.

If you asked Spamton about this, he'd probably just be stunned, then slowly turn red from his neck to his face.

"So, as my former colleagues and current subordinates, you still have to make things difficult for me just like before." He snorted coldly twice, clearly reluctant, and held his head high.

Pink gritted his teeth a little, but still lowered his head and nodded in agreement: "I understand." ”

Yellow and Orange exchanged glances and could only nod in agreement. Blue, worried, looked up at Spamton, but unfortunately, Spamton was holding his head high, unable to see the expressions of the other employees. Meanwhile, Shadow Guy, who was listening to the boss pay the bill, played a joyful saxophone tune this time. Pippins happily hopped and left when Spamton announced the end of the meeting.

The meeting ended, and the entire room fell silent. Only Spamton was still resting his hands on the table, silent for a moment. In this rare quiet environment, he thought carefully for the first time. Actually, it was fine if he didn't do these things, nor did he need to explain to you. After all, although they lived under the same roof, even though you took him in and raised them from the start, and had maintained peace and harmony until now—but now that he was the advertiser, what could he want? Even if ...... Even if he used his identity as the advertiser to force you, a citizen of light, to stay in this world, it didn't matter.

But whenever he thought of that scene, his heart tightened sharply, even making him feel a bit painful. But can a dark citizen turned from spam have a heart too? He didn't know. Spamton just instinctively clutched his chest. He admitted he was afraid—afraid of seeing your disappointed eyes, hearing your helpless and disappointed sighs, and he was afraid too...... He would never see that pure joy, or pure love, in you.

Yes, your unconditional compassion and love are exactly what he needs, so he is willing to spend so much effort doing all this. But he...... Yet he can't believe that all of it is unconditional, yet he fears it will be lost so easily.

Spamton straightened up again, dashed out of the meeting room, and walked to the commercial street. Just like yesterday when he rushed home to see you, he was filled with anxiety and nervousness, the hair on his head standing on end.

He reopened his shop and brought over a few pots of flowers from another Addison. The other Addison was angry but didn't dare speak out. Spamton also changed into the same clothes as the other Addisons, striking Addison's signature gesture and smile, waiting for your arrival.

And you yawned, still a bit groggy when you got out of bed. You knew it was probably because you were asleep, and Spamton carried you in...... You woke up a bit later today than yesterday, maybe because you waited too long yesterday.

After getting dressed and walking to the living room, the breakfast had already cooled down. When you took it to reheat it, you naturally saw the note left by Spamton, and even an emoticon at the end. You couldn't help but chuckle softly before reheating it and eating breakfast.

In the end, after hesitating for a moment, you put on your bag and decide to head to the shopping street again. You don't even know if you'll see Spamton today...... Just to take a look. After all, you definitely don't want your Addison to be bullied again.

By the time you arrived at the commercial street, Spamton had already spotted you. He gave the other Addisons a look, signaling them to start acting. Pink was a bit at his limit, veins on his forehead nearly scrunching up, but he still wore a smile and used the same sales pitch as usual, making deals with the Dark People they all knew were just paid posters hired by Spamton.

Looking left and right, you were surprised to find that the other Addisons' entrances were packed with crowds, crowded with many Dark People. To be honest, you didn't really know these kinds of Dark People...... You only knew it seemed as if they had suddenly appeared in this world one day, and you also saw that the entrance to Spamton was completely empty. He looked noticeably smaller than the other Addisons, and was also shouting loudly, advertising the flower arrangement for 50% off, saying it was now 125 Dark Coins per basin, but still no one paid him any attention, only cold sweat dripping down his forehead.

In fact, the other Dark People were starting to break out in a cold sweat. After all, what you see as a pitiful little brat is, in their eyes, a big boss. They could feel Spamton's gaze sweeping over them with a deathly gaze.

So in your eyes, it's just those Dark People just glancing at Spamton and then ignoring it. You even hear some Dark People probably talking about pippins saying they've never seen this white Addison before, and now it's still half price. Who knows what they're up to—maybe it's just a low-quality product.

You can't stand it anymore and quicken your pace as you walk to Spamton's store. Only then do you realize his shop is more than just rundown—you could say it was just hauled back from a scrap collection station. The shop is run-down, and the goods inside are covered in dust. It's obvious no one has visited for a long time, or rather, it's been cleaned. However...... If you follow what you heard yesterday about the blue Addison, and Spamton hasn't come to work, and hasn't been there for a long time, then his shop really will end up like this.

You swallowed nervously, suddenly timidly approaching, wanting to tell Spamton you didn't mean to watch him make a fool of himself, nor did you mean to see him in such a disgraceful state—you just came with that original intention, hoping to protect his original intention.

So you walk up to his shop, lower your head, not daring to look at his expression, just greeting: "...... Hi." ”

Then you hear Spamton's equally guilty moans, which somehow makes you breathe a sigh of relief.

“…… Y/N, what are you doing here?" He was panicking, and you heard something moving. When you looked up, you were surprised to find he had placed everything in front of everything that looked clean and shiny, blocking the flower arrangement.

"I'm just casually browsing. Since it's rare to have a day off, I wanted to go out shopping." You were speaking sincerely, since you really were thinking that way. Yesterday, going to the shopping street was an accident, but today you came straight to the commercial street with a clear purpose.

You see this white Addison open his mouth in front of you, and surprisingly, he doesn't use the usual sales jargon. He just lowers his head, and you hear his somewhat aggrieved moan: "But...... But I probably don't have anything here worth buying for you...... You can go check out the shop next door......

You interrupted him: "Who said you don't have anything I can buy here?" "As you spoke, you brought out that bucket of flower arrangements, but the fake flowers inside weren't lavender, but sunflowers."

"I want to buy this. 125 Dark Coins, pretty cheap, isn't it?" You smiled and decided to put this bucket of flowers in the living room when you got back. The previous bucket of flower arrangements...... Just casually place them in the corner.

Spamton looked up at you in surprise, his voice stammering: "You...... You don't need to ......"

And you just gently pat his head, wondering why he's gotten shorter, not realizing he's deliberately shortened just to be like before.

"I think it's necessary. If you don't have customers, let me be your first customer." You said casually, paying him 125 Dark Coins.

Spamton opened his mouth, speechless. As the owner of advertising, he had closed countless orders, but when he was addison...... You were indeed the first customer to visit him.

So he could only lower his head, his voice growing muffled as he tried to hold back the tears welling up in his eyes.

"But a whole pot of flowers...... It won't help me meet today's performance requirements......

And you replied without hesitation, "Then I'll buy everything in your shop." ”

As soon as he said this, Spamton was stunned.
