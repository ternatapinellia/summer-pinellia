Shi Xing 13 (swapsptx you)

You returned home as usual. Indeed, having to chat with Addison and analyze the truth about past events is exhausting for you. You just leaned wearily on the sofa, watching Spamton unusually serve you tea and pat your shoulders on the sofa, which made you want to laugh a little—though you can't say he was exceptionally attentive; rather, it was usually the same. It was just that today's excessive attention made you feel a bit flattered.

So much so that even though you took a sip of tea, you couldn't help but marvel at whether it was just the right temperature or just the right flavor to your liking. You couldn't help but ask out of curiosity: "...... Why are you so attentive today? Did you do something to let me down?" ”

Speaking of which...... The temperature of the tea was just right. He wondered how many cups of coffee he had brewed before, and how many cups of coffee had been thrown by those Addisons. Thinking of this, you seemed a bit distracted, but quickly snapped back to reality, just shook your head, and tossed those thoughts out of your mind.

Spamton's pat on your shoulder stiffened for a moment. If he hadn't recognized that your tone was just pure curiosity, he might have thought you really knew something.

You felt the movement behind you stop, and no sound came from you. You turned to look at Spamton in confusion, and he quickly turned your face away, cleared his throat, and pretended to be deep.

"What are you talking about...... Of course I haven't done anything to betray you! If I really did, I'd rather punch myself......" His voice sounded a bit pitiful, sounding aggrieved as he said this, though the first half was pretending to be relaxed. You could tell he was speaking sincerely, but Spamton seemed nervous, so much so you wanted to tease him for a moment.

"So that's how it is...... you dragged out your words, turned to look at Spamton, and Spamton quickly nodded in agreement.

You cupped his cheek, sighed softly, and gently stroked his cheek: "You don't need to do that. Besides, I trust you. You won't do anything to betray me." "These words come from the heart. You truly believe this. You trust Spamton...... It seems like the first time since that time in the Light World that you've trusted anyone other than yourself so much. You were slightly distracted, but quickly gathered your thoughts and gently stroked his face once more.

"But...... So, you can't tell me you're hiding something from me, are you?" You tilted your head. You're pretty sure Spamton hasn't told you the whole truth, but you don't really care...... After all, sometimes, when he's saying this and that, even if you see him showing a fake smile or a pitiful, miserable expression, beneath that, in his voice, you hear the hidden lines—no matter what, he looks like he's about to cry, and you can only hear and feel his sadness.

So no matter what he said, this sadness was real...... You were willing to believe him. Although the facts may be fabricated, the emotions behind them are definitely not fake.

Spamton just blinked twice, suddenly afraid to look at you. But when you cupped his face and met his gaze, he couldn't avoid it and could only look at you.

“…… Of course not, it's all sincere and sincere. "He stammered, looking a bit guilty, his eyes darting around, but he didn't dare look you in the eye. Even as he said that, his heart was pounding wildly, because...... From the moment we first met, he hadn't really told you the truth.

Spamton wasn't sure what he'd think if he told you the truth...... Would you be disappointed, would you be sad? Thinking of this, he couldn't help but feel heartache...... He couldn't figure out whether he was afraid of being exposed, that he lost his home, or that he didn't want to see your disappointed and sad face, or that he didn't want to see you hurt.

You stared at Spamton, watching his eyes roll around, but in the end, you didn't expose him. You thought, fine, then wait until he will tell you—or when you have to face the truth. And until then, just pretend you know nothing.

So you just patted Spamton's head, your eyes slightly curved: "I see...... Then our Spammy is so well-behaved! What reward do you want? "Actually, you were just saying it offhand, and for some reason, it always feels like you're coaxing a kid...... Well, it's understandable.

Spamton rubbed his head against your hand, his voice a bit muffled again. In fact, he was a bit flattered—after all, before this, he had never thought that doing well would come with rewards. It seemed like doing well was just a given. Now he made the same demands for his subordinates, so Spamton reflected and decided he should give employees more rewards and the like...... He was just copying your methods.

"I...... I don't want anything, just hope you can stay with me a little longer. His voice was muffled and a bit light, just holding your hand and pressing it against his cheek, then gently rubbing against it.

You looked at him and scratched his cheek: "Mm...... Then I'll try to come home a little earlier. Also, I'll be with you all day today. ”

He still didn't look at you, just nodded firmly, then hurriedly changed the subject and said, "By the way, I haven't finished the artworks those people ordered yet—I have to get busy with this first!" ”

At this moment, he felt a bit relieved about the reservation he had told you earlier; otherwise, he wouldn't have known what to say at this moment.

You nodded and let go: "Alright, you go ahead and get back to work. I'll go do my own things first...... But I'll always be at home." You emphasized the last sentence, then moved a little away, just sitting on the sofa resting your chin on your hand and watching Spamton.

Spamton let out a light breath. He took a few bottles of mineral water from the coffee table, cut them open with scissors to look proper, then poured water and glued them together to make something resembling a sunflower. However, his craftsmanship wasn't very good, so the results were a bit crooked.

You look at him boredly for a while, then swallow hard...... You suddenly feel an impulse, but this impulse makes you nervous and uneasy. You haven't done this in a long time, so you don't know how your craft is now—you've given up so long that you think you'll never pick up a paintbrush again.

But if that's the case, why did you find a way to buy art supplies on your very first day in the Dark World, but only lock those old paintings along with the supplies in a small box? Maybe you didn't have the courage before, but now you've gathered a bit of courage and are willing to open that box again.

You squatted by the bed, looked up at Spamton, and gazed into the distance...... So focused, undistracted. You know, it was because of him that you decided to open the box again and take out your painting supplies.

So you quietly opened the box, set up the oil rack, and held the canvas—since you only wanted to paint a quick one, you didn't use a very large canvas. Then, you took out brushes of different sizes, squeezed out the still-dried paint, squinted your eyes, pointed the pen at Spamton to quietly measure the proportions, and then used a small piece of paper and pencil to sketch a small draft—your technique hadn't faded; it was still as vivid and lifelike as ever.

After finishing your draft, you sneak a glance at Spamton, seeing that he's still absorbed in the so-called artwork, clearly uninterested in what you're doing. Only then do you quietly breathe a sigh of relief, grab your brush and palette, and begin smearing and sketching on the canvas, sketching his silhouette.

You don't know how much time has passed. By the time you come to your senses, your figure has already appeared hastily on the canvas, just waiting to dry it completely. You feel a bit guilty, glance at the time as dinner approaches, hurriedly put down your brush, hurriedly walk out, and deliberately close the door, then sit down on the sofa.

The sofa collapsed in one spot. Of course, Spamton felt it. He looked at you, and at the same time regretted it—he really hadn't paid attention to your whereabouts that afternoon! Still, it's that so-called artwork that drew his attention, but...... Your craftsmanship and creativity are truly impressive. Even though the sunflowers you made are so lifelike, Spamton doesn't understand why you're only satisfied with your current small job, but he's too embarrassed to ask you more—what if you happen to be asking about something you dislike?

If you hear it, you'll just smile and answer, and that's okay. Even if you ask about something you dislike, you won't blame Spamton—after all, it's just that you didn't make it clear at first.

At this moment, he panicked and looked up, checked the time, then patted his head in frustration: "...... Oh no, I forgot to prepare dinner for you in advance......" After saying that, he carefully watched your expression and noticed your tightly closed door, pausing for a moment.

You just wave your hand: "It's fine, just eat something casually...... There's still instant pizza in the fridge anyway, how about that? ”

Spamton puffed out his cheeks: "Eating fast food all the time isn't healthy......" Then you lean in and poke his cheek. The puffed cheeks quickly shrink back, and he just touches his own cheek and glances at you with a hint of complaint.

"But it's just an occasional meal, so it's fine!" He understood what you meant, so Spamton announced loudly.

You nod in satisfaction and got up to head to the kitchen for a hot pizza, but Spamton's next sentence made you stop in your tracks.

"Well...... I'm a bit curious, just curious! "This sentence sounds like some kind of supplement, "Exactly, why did you close your door?" ”

You don't even need to look back to know Spamton is probably looking a bit guilty at her finger at this moment, which makes you laugh a little. But you still hold back and slowly reply, "Ah, my room...... Because you prepared a surprise for me recently, I plan to prepare a surprise for you too. ”

Actually, this is the reason you just came up with: you feel proud of your brilliant whims—well, it's just that you don't dare show it to him right now. You don't want anyone to know or be involved in your past, for now...... It's enough for you to face those things alone.

Or maybe one day, you'll be able to openly reveal your past to him. But at least not now.

So, before you're fully prepared to show him, isn't that a surprise? One day you'll let him see it, and you believe in that.

Spamton was momentarily stunned, his voice rising: "Surprise?! I—I'm really looking forward to it!" He really started imagining what kind of surprise you'd prepare for him.
