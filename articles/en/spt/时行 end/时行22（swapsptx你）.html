The exhibition is tentatively scheduled for half a month later, for a week-long show, so you have plenty of time to prepare before then. There's still another artist's exhibition going on, about a week away, and in a week, you'll be preparing to set up. At the same time, tickets will be available for reservation and go on sale.

You went home and shared this with Spamton. His eyes sparkled as he listened, then he excitedly waved his arms and legs: "I knew it—Y/N, you were absolutely right to hold this exhibition, especially great!! And I believe many dark people from the advertising world come to the exhibition just to admire my style, and maybe many dark folk from other worlds visit the exhibition out of curiosity about advertising!" ”

He kept shouting to himself, "You always see something behind him, like there's a tail, and it's already raised." You try to hold it in but still can't laugh, and you're too embarrassed to expose Spamton's beautiful imagination. After all, you think you're just an unknown artist, and printing so many tickets probably won't get you out. But the gallery owner insists on holding a week-long exhibition, and has printed a lot of tickets—twice the number of tickets from your last week-long exhibition...... You're worried about whether these tickets will even sell.

Besides, the advertising world probably wouldn't be particularly interested in seeing what the owner of this world is like in daily life. After all, almost all the people of darkness below are his employees, and they rarely see each other in daily life.

"Hmm...... Not really, my paintings are really just average, so don't flatter me like that. "You scratched your cheek a little shyly.

Spamton suddenly leaned in, his face enlarging in front of your eyes, making you involuntarily lean back: "...... What's wrong? ”

But he just looked at you seriously, then cupped your face with both hands: "Y/N, you need to be confident." Of course...... It's okay if you're not confident, I'll help you feel confident! I'll help you love your own ......." As he spoke, he covered his mouth, looking at you a bit helplessly, since he hadn't intended to say that out loud at a time like this.

But you just smiled and gently rubbed his head: "...... Yeah, I know. After all, it's because of you that I'm willing to accept my past." "Otherwise...... You'd probably never be able to pick up a paintbrush. The moment you do, you'll see that torn painting in half—one half of your parents, the other half your painting.

You can also hear the voices of those days—pointing at your paintings, looking at you with disappointment, saying that even if you spend your time on these hobbies, they wouldn't mind, but you can't achieve exquisite realms in those hobbies as well.

But now, when you pick up your brush, all you see is the half-painted spamton's shadow and the painting holding your hand together, and all you hear is his noisy chatter, saying that even if it's just a hobby, it's something impressive.

A bit noisy and noisy, always playing around, making the house a bit noisy, but you don't find it annoying; instead, you find him very cute.

"That's right, so Y/N is really impressive! Don't always be so humble. When you should be proud, just admit it. As long as you're proud, that's enough!" As he spoke, he first lifted his head proudly. You looked at him with a smile, and tried lifting your head yourself.

"You see, as long as you accept praise proudly, everything will become beautiful, right? And...... Just accept others' praise proudly! Because that's definitely what you deserve! ”

This statement sounds a bit unreasonable, after all...... Not everyone comes to praise your work with a completely appreciative gaze; maybe they're just being polite and giving a perfunctory answer.

But you still nodded: "...... I understand, I'll try to do this, right?" Your words carried a hint of hesitation.

Spamton just leaned in and leaned on your shoulder: "It's not about trying, it's about making sure — but if you're not used to it, I'll listen to what they say and then relay it to you!" He waved his finger, but of course, if he relayed it—he'd definitely praise you to the brim!

“…… Alright, but if you hear anything nasty, you have to tell me. You don't have to help me listen to these things—I'll handle it all myself." You fiddled with his fingers, playing with them casually, muttering this line under your breath.

He just waved his other hand in front of your eyes, mumbling unhappily, "What? If it makes you sad, you definitely shouldn't listen! Don't worry, no one will ever say anything bad about you!" Spamton patted his chest and promised you.

Even though you want to say more, seeing him make such a firm promise, you end up holding back your words. Think about it, maybe it's better not to spoil the mood, even though you don't think many people will praise you or anything...... Most of the time, aren't they just casually glanced around and then left?

But right after, you hear Spamton say, "But...... Y/N, you still need to build some confidence. How about this, I'll just praise you a few times every day from now on. After a month, you'll definitely be fine!" He nodded proudly as he spoke.

"Do you need it? But I think maybe you don't need it anymore, after all, you've always been like a proud peacock." You couldn't help but smile and gently rub your head against Spamton's head—fluffy and a little ticklish.

Spamton choked up, then suddenly raised his voice: "Of course I need—"

Of course, he really needs Y/N's praise! Although he usually seems very confident—part of the reason is that, as the creator of the advertising world, he can't be without confidence. Of course, there are also other reasons...... Maybe he's been by your side too long. Although Spamton still remembers the past, the pain of the past has long been something he can let go of, so gradually, he has become more confident.

But of course he needed it, or rather, needed even more!

You blink, rub his head with both hands, and his head obediently sways in direction with your movements.

"Mm...... Good, Spamton is amazing—it raised its own flowers! That's awesome! "You said with a cheerful smile.

"Y/N is amazing too! You've painted so many pictures...... For me, even being able to draw a single picture is already pretty rare. "Spamton immediately jumps in after you.

You coughed lightly, feeling your cheeks flushed a little, feeling a bit embarrassed...... So you patted your cheeks and quietly turned your head: "I...... I'll go get you a drawing paper. You try drawing too." You were just clumsily changing the subject, and then you hurriedly and somewhat in a panic went into the room to fetch a sheet of drawing paper and a set of crayons.

Spamton, looking at your background, also patted his slightly flushed face. He muttered softly twice, relieved that you probably couldn't hear what he was saying—even though he was just watching your back and whispering, 'I like you the most.'

Then, he saw you come out again. At that moment, the warmth on your faces had faded, and you handed Spamton both the drawing paper and crayons.

Spamton glanced at you cautiously, and you just nodded gently, smiled, and gave him an encouraging look.

Spamton took a deep breath and began to paint. He decided to paint that day, when you stand in front of him and take his coffee cup—that would be just so—cool!

Unfortunately, whether from excitement or nervousness, his hand trembled a little, but he still tried hard to draw his figure—a white Addison's shadow, your figure, the heroic figure standing in front of him to take the coffee cup—and the less important NPC opposite, who just sketched those shadows with crayons of different colors, yet carefully traced you. Even though you didn't have those, Spamton forcibly added luxurious clothes and exquisite decorations to you. In his eyes, you deserved the best of everything.

And after finishing the last stroke, he excitedly raised the drawing paper to show you. Although his lines were a bit crooked and not very good, you immediately recognized it — this was what it looked like that day at the cyber barbecue restaurant—you still remember that day, after all, you remember everything your white Addison had suffered.

You took the painting, stroking the brushstrokes on it. Out of the corner of your eye, you saw Spamton sitting upright, nervously waiting for your comment, while you suddenly laughed: "Spamton is really amazing!" Although it might be his first time painting or something...... But this painting is very vivid, and I really like it! It must have been that day at the Cyber Barbecue Restaurant. You've worked hard all along. "As you spoke, you bent down slightly and rubbed Spamton's head. He squinted slightly and rubbed against your palm, snoring twice before answering your question.

“Y/N…… That's impressive too! That day was so brave, standing in front of me without hesitation! If only one day, I could become a Dark Citizen as brave as Y/N! He patted his chest and said enthusiastically.

You always feel a bit embarrassed and turn your face away, but the slightly reddened tips of your ears still reveal your emotions.

"Ahem...... Y-I know...... But feeling scared is normal, and you don't have to be that brave. I'll protect you. "You sat back down on the sofa, and Spamton leaned closer to your side, pressing close to yours. Hearing this, he shook his head sternly.

"You can't always protect me...... I'm the advertiser now! Don't worry, maybe I'm protecting you more often! "Spamton promised.

"Then we'll just wait and see. But in the best...... I still hope you don't get hurt. "As for you...... If you get hurt, it's fine; you don't really care much about these things.

"You can't get hurt either. If you are...... Then I—I'll help you get revenge! ”

"Alright, alright. But I've heard that if you're injured, just a breath and the pain will fly away...... Of course, that's probably just for kids." You shook your head helplessly, then nodded, casually changing the subject.

"So you're hurt." Spamton's voice suddenly tensed and turned serious.

You were stunned for a moment, then had to roll up your sleeve to reveal that long line of wounds. It looked scary, but in reality, it was just a little bleeding at the time.

"It's okay, this little wound is just a minor issue. It'll heal in a couple of days...... And it was also my own carelessness that cut the doorframe. ”

But in the next second, Spamton cupped your arm and carefully blew on your wound, while his other hand behind his back quietly cast a healing spell. The wound healed quickly with his soft, warm breath, regrowing fair skin.

You widened your eyes in surprise, while Spamton just raised an eyebrow and looked at you proudly: "Look, just blow on it and the pain will fly away!" ”
