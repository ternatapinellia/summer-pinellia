You scratched your cheek, feeling a bit embarrassed seeing him so excited and his eyes sparkling. You just cleared your throat, cleared your throat, cleared your throat, and added, "You said it was a surprise...... Maybe, not that surprising? I don't know if you'll like it...... As you spoke, your tone was a bit hesitant. You really didn't know what Spamton's preferences were. It seemed like he liked everything you gave him, but in contrast, he seemed to know your preferences quite well.

Spamton just shook his head: "It's fine, as long as it's prepared by you, I'll like whatever it is." He swore with absolute certainty, and he also believed that you wouldn't prepare anything that was truly hard for him to accept—he believed that what you would bring him would definitely be something wonderful and a wonderful experience.

You cleared your throat twice, a bit embarrassed: "...... Not necessarily...... But before you could finish, Spamton interrupted you.

"Definitely!" He raised his voice, "I think what Y/N prepared is the best!" ”

And he looked so proud, as if his feathers had lifted up.

You blink and glance at him a few times, then can't help but smile and nod: "Alright, I know. Then I'll look forward to seeing you react with such a surprise." "You think it's because Spamton is here that you start looking forward to tomorrow, the day after, and every day to come...... Originally, you just thought this day was just passing through casually, so what else was there to look forward to, or what meaning was there? But...... Maybe from the very first day you found him, the gears started turning again, and you started looking forward to the next day.

Spamton nodded vigorously, his face beaming with a smile: "Of course, I'm looking forward to that surprise too!" ”

Because of your and his expectations, after the meal you hurriedly finished that painting—but you hesitated looking at it, still not daring to show it directly to Spamton. Because, after all this time, you still don't know how your drawing skills have become. You think maybe you should show it to the other Dark People first, confirm that the painting is truly impressive, and then show Spamton this surprise.

If ...... If everyone thinks it's not good, then just draw a new one. It doesn't matter how many you make—after all, you have to prepare well for this surprise for your own Spamton. So until the other Dark People sigh, you won't take out your painting.

But if the other Dark People have seen the painting, it's not just a surprise for Spamton. Even though you didn't say it at first, you still felt it wasn't very good. After thinking it over...... Maybe you'll have the courage to take out the remaining paintings later. No one but you and your parents has seen them, so giving them to Spamton as a surprise is just right.

Thinking this, you can't help but smile, pack up the other art supplies, and step out with the oil painting—coincidentally, the gallery is recently holding an exhibition of works about different Dark People's perceptions of beauty, and many Dark People have submitted their works. Think about it, you can submit too. This painting...... It probably represents your understanding of beauty.

So you hang this painting on an empty spot on the wall, step back a couple of steps to admire it, and indeed, it looks quite nice here, not out of place at all.

Your colleague noticed your actions and came over curiously, glanced at the painting on the wall, then stiffened a bit, and his voice was dry: "Uh...... Who is the artist of this painting? ”

You tilted your head in confusion: "I drew it...... What's wrong? ”

You're still a bit nervous, wondering what to do if your paintings aren't good—but then you hear something unexpected about it.

“…… Do you know who you painted? "My colleague hesitated, just staring at you.

You tilted your head in confusion: "Hmm...... Spamton, huh? What's so special? Oh, right, he's not very famous for Addison, right? After all, he can't sell anything, so it's normal you don't know about him...... You can't help but start muttering.

"But he's a very good Dark Citizen. Although he can't grow flowers, he still seriously prepared a surprise for me—even though it was made with fake flowers, and he even said he wants to sell the idea of flower arranging from the trash can. It seems like business is going well, and his colleagues have even come to snatch his stuff......" As you continue, you don't notice your colleague quieting down, just looking at you with a hesitant expression.

"Recently, he suddenly had an idea to make a creative flowerpot or something. I made a demonstration of making a sunflower for him, and he imitated it, so that's why I recorded this scene...... I don't even know if the painting looks good, but anyway, this is my understanding of beauty...... Because of him, I started looking forward to tomorrow, I began to ......" You opened your mouth, but felt too shy to say the rest. Honestly, you didn't dare say things to Spamton, but here you boasted and told others...... You blamed yourself in your heart.

"Anyway, I want to give this painting to Spamton as a return gift, but I don't know how my painting skills have improved after all this time, so I put it on the gallery for exhibition. I want to hear what other Dark People have to say." You cleared your throat twice and summed up for yourself. After all, you can't keep talking about it forever. Although you could probably talk about Spamton for a few days and nights, even if your mouth is dry, that's fine.

The colleague sighed, then stepped forward to cover your mouth, nervously looking left and right. You tried to pry his hand away, but your colleague just lowered his voice and said, "...... Don't you know that name can't be mentioned casually in the Underworld? ”

You were confused for a moment, then instinctively shook your head. You'd never heard such a thing before. Could it be that Spamton was asking other Dark People to use other names to address him? Or was his reputation so big that even the other Dark People didn't dare call him by that name? Somehow, you subconsciously rejected that possibility—maybe you just didn't want to believe it.

A colleague's voice was tense: "It's Spamton......! His name can't be called casually, otherwise he'd be very upset. I can't even say what methods he might use to punish the other Dark People...... But it's fine to whisper a few words occasionally; he can't hear you. "He finally let go, but still stared at you, probably afraid you'd say something inappropriate again.

You hesitate for a moment, but finally ask, "So how do you usually address him?" ”

“…… The master of advertising, or the king of advertising." The colleague's voice was soft, but still enough for you to hear those two words clearly. You were silent for a moment, and although you quickly understood—but you still found it hard to believe, how could things turn out like this, how could the truth be like this?

Your voice became a bit dry: "So...... Is he the master of this advertising world?" "When you ask this question, although you feel a bit nervous, you also feel a bit relieved...... You think about it, you really have already guessed it, but you just haven't been sure of this answer.

The colleague nodded solemnly, looked at the painting again, and scratched his cheek: "I don't dare comment on anything else...... Anyway, your brushwork is excellent, your color choices are just right, and the whole painting feels warm and inviting. That's all there is to it. ”

Then he hurriedly left his place. You silently watched the back of this Dark Citizen, then glanced at the oil painting hanging on the wall, staring at Spamton in a daze. At that moment, you felt very close to him, yet somehow inexplicably far away. No matter what kind of Dark Citizen he was, no matter what identity he was, to you he was just a weak, pure white Addison who needed care.

…… Alright, maybe he doesn't really need care. But you think he still needs it. Otherwise, if he, a Dark Commoner, is so high and mighty, how lonely must that be? It's okay to pretend you know nothing and just go back like this, right?

But you still want to ask him, that's just ...... Simply ask him, even if you think this matter isn't important, since he's kept it from you for so long, there must be a reason. You just want to know the reason.

On this day, you were working in the gallery as usual, but...... No Dark People came to admire the painting; most only glanced at it from afar and then walked away in fright. Only a few dared to come over and take a closer look, but they left in a hurry after leaving with a few words of praise. You had hoped to hear some sincere advice, but now you can't help but worry.

When the gallery closed for hours, the last few Dark Folk who came in were left with nothing but praise. You sighed and took down the oil painting to take home. Since that's the case, you can only show it directly to Spamton.

At that moment, another colleague came over again. She didn't seem so afraid of this advertiser—he was a Renwu management girl. You glanced at her twice, and she smiled warmly: "I want to know, what's his relationship with you?" ”

You were stumped by that question. Honestly, you hadn't considered this question at all. Maybe you've been together for so long—you've gotten used to it, and you're used to spamton being with you.

"Uh...... It's just ordinary friends, right? But since we live together and know each other well, it's roughly ...... ...... like family? "You say this, but for some reason, you can't help but feel a bit melancholy, as if you're completely dissatisfied with this answer.

She let out a meaningful "Oh—" and you couldn't help but shiver. Not knowing what else she wanted to say, you could only widen your eyes and watch her warily.

"Then...... What do you think of him? "She asked.

You tilt your head and answer without thinking, "My Spamton is definitely the best." After saying this, you belatedly cover your mouth, remembering that his name shouldn't be said carelessly...... Of course, no other dark people heard it either.

She just said meaningfully, "Oh—" twice, waved the braid she was holding, and smiled: "Then...... I hope you two can stay together for a long time. ”

You blink and nod: "Of course...... Yes, we will. After all, that's our home. "So of course you both will go home.

And she didn't say anything more, just walked away. You hugged the painting and also walked toward home. You began to ponder: should you show Spamton this surprise first, or ask him the advertiser's question first, so he could find out why he's been hiding it from you all along?

In the end, you decide to show him the surprise first—after all, what he hides from you isn't really important; the surprise is what matters.
