Pink Adventure Journey 6 (ggdwx you)

As you walked through the underground city's gate, the stone gate behind you closed. Before you could see what exactly you were in, the ground beneath your feet suddenly collapsed, and both you and Spamton were falling downward. You instinctively widened your eyes, and then you grabbed Spamton's wrist—after all, you didn't want to fall into some messy place! Instead, Spamton tightly held your hand. After all, someone had no idea where they would end up and could only grab you. His eyes darted around nervously, clearly very uneasy, and even his voice grew louder, almost like a roar: "Y/N—are we trying to ...... Where are we going—"

You shouted just as loudly, "I don't know—let's talk about it if we fall—" So you both fell for a while in the darkness. To be honest, this darkness made you uneasy, but you still managed to see each other clearly. No matter what, in the end, you both landed on a soft grassy patch. Luckily, it wasn't solid ground, or you'd have been bruised and bruised. You rubbed your temples and sat up from the ground. At this moment, you were indeed in the middle of a forest...... But for some reason, it was still a crossroads, with directions to move forward in all directions.

"Is this the dungeon...... Good thing I've never been to a place like this before...... Spamton beside you is still holding your hand tightly. Now, he has also sat up from the floor, looking around and muttering to himself. You shake your head and nod again.

"Yes, uh, welcome to the dungeon...... At this point, it seems we can only walk through all four directions once. Looks like we need to get keys from other places or something...... "You muttered to yourself, got up from the ground, and pulled Spamton up too. Only then did you notice you were still holding hands. You hurriedly let go, cleared your throat, and then walked forward, though a bit in sync: "Anyway, let's pick a direction and go wherever we want. Based on my experience, once you clear a path, that side will be blocked......"

"What kind of checkpoints are they usually like?" Spamton quickly stood up and followed behind you. To be honest, when you let go, he actually felt a bit disappointed for a moment...... Yes, it was a feeling he felt he shouldn't say out loud. He was the lord of the advertising world—how could he be so easily influenced by a Lightkin! At least, that's how he felt.

"Normally, a path should be entirely mechanical, meaning you just need to pass through all the roads to reach the end. Some roads are filled with monsters, and you just need to defeat all the monsters on that path...... However, I've only encountered a three-way junction before. This is my first time encountering such a crossroads, so I don't know what else might be on the remaining road. "You explained in detail the experience you gained from exploring dungeons. Spamton followed behind you, and to be honest, he was lost in thought...... So, all four paths have a certain possibility, but he couldn't identify what kind of existence they were. Of course, you probably couldn't tell either.

But he still stubbornly asked one more question: "Can't your compass find the way either? Also, did we take the wrong path and go straight to the exit leading to the road ahead? Can't we get through?" ”

You just shook your head: "My compass can only be used to locate the dungeon. There's nothing more to do inside. Normally, exploring the dungeon is just me doing it alone...... This is the first time I've brought others to this place together. As for reaching the exit or anything like that, you can't continue forward, because usually that exit is locked and requires keys from other places to open it...... "Saying this, you randomly picked a path and walked in."

The trees along this road had already withered, with only withered yellow leaves spreading across the ground. When stepped on, they even cracked and rustled. The surrounding trees looked as if they had been scorched, showing only a withered, grayish hue. Occasionally, some branches fell to the ground. To be honest, at first glance, it looked desolate, even carrying a somewhat terrifying atmosphere, because at that moment, wisps of cold wind were blowing from behind you, messing up your hair. Of course, you didn't care much about this feeling. But just then, Spamton suddenly tightly wrapped his arms around yours.

You looked at Spamton in confusion: "What's wrong?" At present, it seems there haven't been any mechanisms or anything on this road yet...... There's no need to be so nervous. ”

Spamton couldn't really say it was because this place was a bit creepy and scary; he was afraid of something might be hiding and that's why he stuck to you—he really couldn't bring himself to say that! He just hugged your arm tightly and said solemnly, "Don't you think this place is a bit cold? Maybe it'll be warmer for us to lean together." ”

You shake your head in confusion: "...... I don't think so." "At most, this place just blew some cold wind, not exactly extreme weather. If it were really that extreme, you would have already taken out your warm clothes." But since this was Spamton's first time in the dungeon, he probably really felt that way......

You opened your backpack, took out your usual cotton jacket, and draped it over Spamton. Your expression was very serious: "If you really feel cold, you need to put on more clothes. Leaning on me won't help much...... Also, let me tell you in advance, although this is an underground city, some places mimic extreme weather in the surface world. If that already makes you feel cold...... You fell into deep thought, and Spamton, suddenly covered with heavy clothes, was also stunned. Of course, the very second he wrapped himself in the coat felt hot—oh my god, what is it...... He wasn't really that cold! He was just a little scared. Of course, he wouldn't tell you this. He just took off the clothes covering himself and handed them to you, looking a bit embarrassed: "I'm not...... I don't feel particularly cold, wearing this is too hot...... Uh...... He stammered, not knowing what to say. You put your clothes back in confusion, and then Spamton just followed behind you as you walked forward. He was a bit distracted, his footsteps landing on the ground. The crackling sound continued, then he paused, feeling the chilling wind behind him suddenly grow stronger. Spamton's expression changed as well, and he began to panic: "Ghosts, there's a [Ghost Attack]!" "A dignified lord is actually afraid of ghosts—if the other Dark People found out about this, his dignity would surely disappear!" But at this moment, he was still afraid, since he didn't even know if his life would be in danger—immediately after, Spamton's steps faltered. In panic, he stepped on a mechanism. As the surrounding dead leaves fell, a net suddenly appeared from the ground and grabbed him. Thus, the lord of the advertising world was now defeated by a small trap.

Of course, you also heard Spamton's shouting and his sudden running. You were about to turn to tell him there are no ghosts in this world and that you must be a bit materialistic, but when you turned around, all you saw was a pale face—although he was originally only white, his peach-pink eyes had now shrunk in fright, helplessly clutching the wire fence. Then he looked at you, his voice strange: "Y/N, Y/N, quick, get me out! I'll definitely give you a [silver dollar reward] later......," with a hint of panic and confusion, and a bit trembling.

The net was very soft, and because Spamton was still moving around inside, trying to struggle out, he couldn't stand up at all, falling heavily onto the net each time. You looked at him and could only shake your head helplessly and sigh.

"Don't move. Once I find the mechanism behind this net, I'll put you down......" As you say this, you start circling the net to search for the tree binding it. Spamton, following your instructions, lies motionless inside the net, feeling utterly hopeless—he's lost all face...... Damn it, the dungeon is actually such a sinister and cunning existence!

You finally found where the net was hanging. You pulled a dagger out of your body, then cut the thread connecting the net. With a thud, Spamton fell straight to the ground. He rubbed his aching waist and stood up. You had tidied yourself up and stood in front of him again.

Spamton, looking serious, starts shaking your shoulder: "Y/N, pretend nothing just happened. You must remember, I am the most powerful Addison in this dark world, the most powerful lord!" ”

You think to yourself, judging from the scene where he just shouted 'There was a ghost' and then fell straight into the trap, he didn't seem that formidable...... You just thought this guy was a bit silly, and actually quite funny, and you had no idea how he became a lord. But you still gave him a lot of respect, just nodding and hmming 'hmm ah ah': "Mm, I get it. Spamton is the most powerful Addison in the world!" "As for how sincere that is, only you know."

Of course, Spamton didn't care about that. With your guarantee, he happily kept moving forward.
