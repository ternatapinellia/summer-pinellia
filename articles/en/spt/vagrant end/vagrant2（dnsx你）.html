He hesitated for a moment, then secretly peeked at you again—just looking at you like this made him genuinely happy. He watched you innocently nibble on that piece of bread, frown slightly at the messages on your phone. He watched your black hair floating with the wind, messy, and you casually brushed the hair that floated in front of your face back behind your head. Spamton felt that all of this was beautiful, extremely beautiful, making him involuntarily hold his breath, gazing at you intently, also watching your skirt fluttering in the wind.

And you seemed to notice something; you felt as if some gaze was fixed on you, so you couldn't help but lift your head and look in that direction. But Spamton just withdrew his gaze, hiding behind the wall, and continued typing and sending messages to you with some excitement and slight trembling in his hands: [Very beautiful, extremely beautiful... Baby, you are like a sprite standing in the wind, as if you could ride the wind away from all of this... Yes, dear, you don't seem to belong to this world, and you can no longer eat bread... I will prepare for you what you deserve.]

Hmm, but in a couple of days I'm going to get a haircut, so you won't be able to see my black hair flowing in the wind.

After sending this message, you put down your phone, looked around the room, sighed, and honestly... although some areas are still messy, given your current physical condition, you simply don't have the strength to organize everything. You start thinking about whether you can hire someone to help tidy up the room—after all, you don't mind spending a bit more money now, and those things, for you, really aren't a big deal.

[I'm begging you TAT]

[Baby, why aren't you replying to me? Do you really want to cut it off right now TAT]

[Then I will squat outside the baby's room, ready to pick up the hair you throw away ^^]

So you casually sent him a few messages.

[Replied to you]

[Not cut off]

[I won't give you the chance to pick it up either]

This time the message from the other side also came very quickly.

[Baby has come back to me again awa]

[This time it's actually three messages! Dear... dear, are you willing to chat with me?]

[Darling, please don't abandon me TAT……]

[Could it be that my dear doesn't like me sending so many messages? TAT]

[If you really don't like it... OTTO]

"If you don't like it, will you just keep crying?" You admit that chatting with this guy is quite interesting, but... you can't go on like this for much longer. It's fine to tease him online, but you certainly don't want to suffer in real life.

[I will hide in a place where my darling can't see me and keep crying until my darling is willing to pay attention to me ><]

After seeing this sentence, you turned off your phone. You plan to ignore him for a while, believing that he won't be able to resist bothering you again after some time.

Meanwhile, Spamton communicated a bit, and then moved into the house next to yours. To be honest, this entire street was originally an asset that the partners intended to give him—so naturally he also got the keys to your house. However, he just quietly tidied up the house and then prepared to give you a surprise when he meets you tomorrow. Maybe you'll be very happy—maybe you'll just think it's a coincidence. Spamton, fantasizing about your reaction when you see him tomorrow, couldn't help humming a couple of notes. He was in a really good mood, or rather, a bit too good of a mood.

As for you... you lay on the sofa resting for a while before realizing that your phone hadn't rung all afternoon. You picked it up and looked, only to find that the last message was still the one he sent. Rarely, you moved your fingers and tapped a reply: [Really crying?]

[Really cried TTTTTT]

Spamton is sitting on the sofa in the house next to yours, smiling, wiping the tears that flow from his swollen eyes, pretending. He doesn't know if this trick will work on you, but this is all he can do.

You pressed your lips together, and for some reason felt a bit guilty: [Alright, go ahead and send it, I'm fine]

After all, you also feel indifferent; if he wants to post, then let him post.

Great——then I will send lots of messages to my baby every day! It doesn't matter if my baby replies or not, as long as my baby sees them~

You clicked your mouth once, and couldn't help but send him another message: [Are you a pervert, acting like this?]

[///^^///Yes, I am the pervert belonging to the baby alone///^^///]

…To be honest, you were a little disgusted, so you simply put down your phone, walked into your bedroom, tidied up the books you had brought from the world of light, hesitated for a moment, and then casually picked up a book to start flipping through it. You were only trying to pass the time; you didn't know what you should do. Maybe you should find something to do in the dark world?

You were daydreaming about these things while looking at the book. To be honest, you didn't actually absorb much, since you had been distracted the whole time. When you came back to your senses, the book was already flipped to the last page. You sighed, put the book back on the shelf, and then picked up your phone again, only to realize that he had sent you a lot of messages again.

[Awa, I knew the baby's heart is very soft... but it's fine as long as you're soft-hearted only towards me! Baby must not be this soft-hearted towards other men... I would be very unhappy TAT]

[But I'm not happy, and the baby probably wouldn't feel anything either... TAT, can you care about me a little more, dear?]

[Darling, look at me, look at me... I know you haven't seen any of this news, when will I get the baby's favor?]

[The movie that baby watched with someone else today should be really good, right? I'm so jealous of the person who watched the movie with you... I also want to watch a movie with baby.]

[Dear, look at me, look at me, look at me...]

You unlocked your phone and swiped the screen, and all these messages were marked as read, but the next second, his message popped up again.

[Dear, you finally saw my message ///><///]

You let out a sigh and yawned, looking at the time—it’s actually only about half past nine, but you really do need to get a good rest.

So you quickly sent him a message: [Alright, little pervert, I'm going to sleep, good night]

[Awa, my dear gave me a nickname… I like it so much, good night my dear! I hope I'm in your dreams, baby! ///><///]

You stared at the message in silence for a moment, rubbed your forehead, and then turned off your phone, also setting it to silent so that if he sent messages in the middle of the night, it wouldn't wake you. Then you curled up, hugging yourself, and fell asleep in the blanket. To be honest, you arrived in a strange world today, and there was one more person bothering you. You slept somewhat unsteadily, often half-asleep and half-awake during the night. When you sat up in the morning, facing the dim light of this world, you were still a bit bewildered. Then you remembered, you are in another world, and you have welcomed the morning of the second day.

You stared blankly outside, then reopened your phone — only to find that the little pervert hadn't sent you any new messages. Instead, you felt a bit inexplicably disappointed, so after hesitating for a moment, you tapped on the keyboard and sent him a message: [Good morning]

[Good morning, my dear—I waited for you for so long, and you finally woke up awa.] The person on the other side almost replied to you instantly. You suspect that they might have been staring at the phone screen all the time, or maybe they even set a special notification tone for you, otherwise how could they respond so quickly?

What does baby plan to eat for breakfast? Do you need me to prepare it for you! I want to know what baby likes to eat more~

You scratched your somewhat messy hair. Because you were going out, you changed your clothes and tied up your hair. You planned to go find something to do; always staying at home alone, waiting for death... that feeling wasn't pleasant either. So you put on a blue and white dress with lace and tied your hair into a high ponytail.

You typed on your phone keyboard: [Haven’t decided yet. Probably I'll go to the convenience store to buy a sandwich to eat.]

[Only eating convenience store food is unhealthy TAT, baby look at me, I will prepare breakfast for you every morning from now on!]

You raised your eyebrows and walked to the door. To be honest, you didn't know where he would put the breakfast, but when you opened the door, you saw a plate at the doorway, with steaming sandwiches on it, along with a steaming sausage and a fried egg.

You subconsciously widened your eyes and looked around, but there wasn't a single figure in sight, and this sudden breakfast was exactly as written in the message earlier—it would be prepared for you every morning.

You hesitated for a moment, carried this breakfast back to the living room, closed the bedroom door, and started eating. Honestly, this breakfast really tastes good.
