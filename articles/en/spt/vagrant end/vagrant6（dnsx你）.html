He looks like he is happy... You slowly sip this cup of milk tea, switch pages on your phone, and feel a bit worried about what you should do next. Although the illness has been confirmed... you can still handle some small daily tasks, and you are thinking about whether you should go out to work.

But just as you listened, the phone vibrated again, and that little pervert's message popped up.

[Dear, do you want to go to work? >X<, I think dear doesn't need to go to the office awa, I can support the baby! Believe me! WWW] You don't really know how he came to this conclusion, but based on your recent activities, it's indeed possible to infer this news.

But you still sent him a message asking back: [How did you know I was looking for a job?] You didn't tell anyone about this, and you don't know where he got the information from.

On the other end of the phone, Spamton, who received the message, had just switched back to the monitoring interface. He watched you frown slightly as you began examining your phone. He couldn't help but cup his face in both hands, muttering to himself, 'Ah... my dear, you look so good even with a frown... I wish I could lean in and give you a kiss...' Knowing that you frowned because of the text he sent, he couldn't help feeling even happier. He liked seeing your emotions change because of his words.

Spamton laughed hee-hee, without revealing anything about the surveillance, otherwise it would be easy to associate it with him. He just expertly typed a line of text for you: [awwww because I pay close attention to everything my dear! I noticed this morning when baby was paying at the mall, baby was a bit reluctant to spend money... is it because savings aren't enough awa, if baby needs it, just say the word and I will send the dark coins my dear needs right to your door T^T]

You looked a bit silent at this message, and I have to say, his observation is remarkable—well, you did hesitate for a moment when you were paying, because buying this house had already spent most of your savings. The remainder is enough to support you comfortably for the next few years, as long as you don't overspend. But you want the remaining time to be a bit happier.

But you were still a bit curious, so you sent him another message: [Hmm, your observation skills are quite good. Then why don’t you tell me, what was my expression like back then?] You thought maybe you should just tease him a bit, after all, you have nothing to do while sitting here now.

[//^^// Baby praised me awa! I'm so happy——but dear, weren't you also giving me a cold look at that time? If I were standing next to you, I definitely wouldn't let you keep that expression! OO(>x<)oo]

You stared at this message in silence for a while. Indeed, your expression really wasn't very good when watching the cashier check the prices... Where exactly was he looking at you from? Or did he just hack into the supermarket's surveillance system? Otherwise, how could he see it so clearly?

You felt that you couldn't come up with an answer to this question for the time being, so you simply opened the door and walked out. You planned to casually look for a job, after all, it was only the afternoon, so there was still a bit of time — as for lunch, you just ate some bread casually to get by.

You had no idea that, not long after you walked out, Spamton quietly opened the door to take a look at your back, and then used a spare key to open your room door, closing it tightly behind him. He eagerly lay down on the sofa, rolled around, closed his eyes, as if this could allow him to enjoy your scent, as if your scent could completely envelop him—of course, he also had some selfishness; he hoped that the lingering scent that belonged to him could also completely envelop you.

Then he finally got up from the sofa, picked up the milk tea bottle you haven’t thrown away yet, and then absently licked the straw, with a lingering sweet taste, as if he was savoring the sweet flavor in your mouth, as if... he were kissing you.

Spamton took a deep breath and reluctantly put down the cup. He glanced at the cleaning tools left in your house, and although it wasn't necessary, he still helped tidy up a bit. He also took a look at your fridge, and then stuffed into it what he thought should be in it. He checked the time, took out some ingredients, and helped prepare dinner for you. After everything was cleaned up, he placed the dinner on the dining table and even put on a food cover. Then, Spamton quietly left your house and returned to his own. He opened his phone and, trying to contain his excitement, started messaging you again.

[Dear, why don't you ever clean up the trash T^T, it's so messy... I helped the baby clean up and throw away the trash, praise me quickly~~(-V-)~~]

[The milk tea the baby drank is also so sweet~~ I nibbled on the straw my dear used, it's sweet, I really like it, this is the baby's flavor~~(-V-)~~]

By the way, dear, have you gone out… I prepared dinner for you, so you don't have to tire yourself out awa. I hope you like the taste of the dinner!

Spamton was still excited to send you something else, but a phone call came through on his mobile. Although he maintained a smiling expression, the aura around him cooled down. He certainly didn't want the call to disturb his sweet time with you—of course, this was only his one-sided sweetness.

Spamton picked up the phone, and it seemed that the person on the other end said something, while the smile on Spamton's face widened: 'Of course, let's complete this 【deal】! 【Fair for all】, you 【deserve it】!' This was an item that had been on display for a long time without selling, and now it finally had an audience—well, and you could get a lot of Dark Coins. If you agree, he would give you all the Dark Coins.

It seemed that the other side said something again, and Spamton just chuckled lightly: 'Well then, [Deal's a go].'

The call was disconnected, and he stared blankly at the dark screen, thinking about his current situation and then recalling the past.

At that time, he was just an ordinary Addison staying on the commercial street—a white one, looking thinner and weaker than the other Addisons, and unable to complete any deal. He originally thought that everything would just continue like this, but he could not bear the bullying from the other Addisons, could not endure their insults... until that moment, you appeared in front of him.

You are a being of light, and at that moment, Spamton admitted that he, too, seemed to have captured the only light from this dim and lifeless existence. You simply stood in front of him, frowning as you scolded the other Addisons, and then awkwardly bought goods from his stall with the currency of the world of light. That was the first item sold at his stall—and it was precisely because of your visit as a being of light that other beings of darkness also flocked to his stall, until he received that phone call—until he completely fell under the queen's authority. When moving into her villa, Spamton also knew that he was getting closer and closer to you.

He once did not dare to hope that you could stay by his side... but now he can finally watch you day and night, every moment, he can finally... finally let you see him again.

Of course, you also noticed those few text messages and couldn't help frowning. You didn't expect that he would actually take the opportunity to sneak into your home when you weren't there. But after thinking about it, there really isn't much in the house anyway, so if he wants to go, let him go.

And it just happened that you didn't have the energy to tidy up, he was willing to help you clean the room and even made dinner for you... you were quite surprised.

So you casually sent him messages: [Ah, thank you, I'll try the taste of the dinner you made]

After sending this message, you originally intended to put your phone away, but then the phone vibrated several more times, and you saw several more messages flash on the screen.

[If the baby likes it, I will make dinner for the baby every day awa]

[By the way, dear, you won't be mad at me for sneaking into your room, right… I just missed you so much, so I lay on your bed for a while \>X<//]

[If my dear doesn't like it, I'll try not to do it next time awa... Don't worry, my dear, your bed is absolutely clean! All the dust has already been swept away by me~~-V-~~]

You watched these few messages and the corner of your mouth twitched, then you couldn't help but laugh a little, and typed a reply to him quickly: [You are really a pervert, a lunatic, wicked to death, sneaking into my room and even sucking my straw, sleeping in my bed, and yet you are so self-righteous?]

[awa Baby scold me a few more times~~] As a result, the person on the other side replied instantly, but it just looked like they sent you this message in a very playful way. The corner of your mouth twitched, and you decided to ignore them for the time being. You happened to walk into a flower shop; the dark world doesn't have many types of flowers that bloom—but the owner of this shop, upon hearing that you are a being of light, welcomed you warmly and also said that most of the time you wouldn't need to work, as long as you sat here and attracted customers. It seems that the business of this flower shop isn't very good either.

But you happened to be studying design — you casually picked a few flowers and performed a bit of the art of flower arranging for the shop owner, which happened to match your field of expertise. The shop owner nearly begged you on your knees to stay. Your mouth twitched; anyway, you were planning to stay at this shop. After all… the work was easy, and although the salary wasn’t much, it was enough for you to do things at your leisure.

On the other side, after receiving the text you sent him insulting him, Spamton simply held his face with both hands, his expression swirling, and his cheeks were a bit red. He held his phone, waiting for your reply, but... it was as if you didn't see the message; the message still showed as unread. So his originally turbulent emotions came to a stop. He turned on your home camera to check and confirmed that you hadn't come home yet—he was anxiously waiting.
