Employment Syndrome 2 (sptx you)

Sptx, you, OOC counts me as me. And the full title of this story is 'Wasn't it supposed to be a thug? Why don't you want money and want to sleep with me?!' 》

I wonder how long it will be, just writing for fun.

——

You scratched your head, wanting to say something but holding back, and glanced at Spamton—well, to be honest, you're hesitating about whether to tell others. If you just swallow your pride and swallow your anger, that's fine, just a little longer......

Anyway, this kind of thing isn't the first or second time.

So now, you just looked away a little guiltily...... Hmm, hmm, you didn't dare meet his gaze for now, but you could still feel his gaze fixed on you.

In the end, you cleared your throat twice: "Alright, I'll organize my words and tell you. Before that, can you change back! It's too high, I'm tired from tilting my head back!" You started pointing and whispering, automatically ignoring his hesitant expression.

In the end, Spamton returned to his little puppet form. I don't know what he'd been messing with again. You looked down and saw him running to your leg. After thinking it over, you grabbed him by the back of the neck and went upstairs. You planned to put him down at the table before talking—just as long as you could stall, so you actually walked very slowly.

But Spamton struggled in your hands, and you almost lost your grip: "[Stop]! I can [move free], I don't need [futile] [help]!" ”

You let out a regretful 'ah' and had no choice but to put him on the steps, watching him climb up the steps. After all, he was much faster than you walked slowly, so you just watched his back with regret.

…… In fact, from your slow pace, Spamton could tell you probably didn't want to speak, or maybe you just didn't know what to say, so you wanted to stall for time—after all, you just said you wanted to tell him.

So he just climbed onto his seat, not in a hurry, just waiting for you to walk out slowly.

No matter how reluctant you are, this path still has an end, and you will eventually reach the end. So when you peek out from the basement, you spot Spamton sitting there—honestly, he looks like a tiny one, but why does he seem so oppressive? You only feel nervous and uneasy, a feeling similar to when you stammered with your parents about your mistakes before...... Or maybe you feel a bit uneasy about saying such things to strangers. After all, you can't possibly treat the person you just hired as your own parent!

…… It's just that I feel a bit guilty for no apparent reason. After all, you know that if you ignore those matters, you'll look very weak. Anyone who sees you will be disappointed that you haven't lived up to your talents.

But you gave him money, so as long as he doesn't tell anyone, it doesn't matter, right...... Thinking this, you finally dawdled and sat down opposite him.

You rested your chin on your hand, hesitated for a moment, and said, "Uh...... First, you must make sure that what you hear from me must not be leaked outside. "You coughed lightly twice, and your voice grew serious. After all, confirming this matter is still very important.

Spamton nodded matter-of-factly: "Of course." "He wouldn't do something so disgraceful, and spreading your story wouldn't do him any good."

So you took a deep breath, your fingers restlessly fiddling with each other. Probably conflicted, the room suddenly fell silent, leaving only the sound of light breathing. Only then did you finally make up your mind to speak.

“…… Actually, it's nothing much. I just had a friend I used to get along with—at least I think they were good friends—but recently they fell out with me and even turned around and accused me of bullying her behind their backs...... Honestly, I don't have that much time. Every day after finishing my work, I eat and sleep and I'm already very busy...... "Well, it's just that you're so slow." After all, you don't have much to do, so you take your time on everything.

“…… Moreover, she leaked what I had told her before to others, which led to many rumors about me outside. But because of my status, those rumors didn't dare come to me ...... "As you spoke, you pressed your temples in frustration. To be honest, what you told her back then wasn't a big deal, but she exaggerated the rumors, mixing those rumors with half-truths and falsehoods. You were a bit lazy to explain." At this point, your eyes reddened. After all, you had no idea that an old friend would do such a thing after turning against you.

After all, self-proving is the most troublesome part and often hard to explain.

"That's why I said from the start that you can't spread this matter outside...... Even if I pay a little more, it doesn't matter! After all, oh, we're just strangers who met by chance, and old friends could turn against each other...... "You muttered to yourself."

"I have [professional ethics], so I naturally wouldn't engage in such [behavior]. Also...... I think [you] shouldn't [let go so easily]. Maybe the [beating] behind the scenes is necessary for [you]. "Spamton finished speaking without changing his expression. To be honest, he didn't know how you managed to endure it. Could it be that you don't like going out, so naturally you can't hear those things?

You scratched your head and awkwardly chuckled twice: "Ahaha...... Well, after all, I might have had issues too, otherwise we might not have fallen out back then...... After all, maybe I did say something wrong and then spoke inappropriate...... You were still racking your brains over your own reasons when Spamton interrupted you.

"No, maybe [you] have a problem, but it's not the main reason. It's the other party who caused you [harm] and caused you [distress]. Moreover, the [behavior] itself is [wrong], so I believe [revenge] is [reasonable]. ”

…… You scratched your hair again. The already messy hairstyle was completely messed up by you. Looking over through Spamton's glasses, you couldn't see his expression, so in the end, you took a deep breath, though still a bit hesitant.

“…… But precisely because I probably have issues too, I feel I can't fully blame her......" you hesitate and whisper softly, but the room is so quiet that Spamton can hear everything clearly.

He almost wants to knock on your head to see what kind of brain your brain is actually constructed. Of course, at first, it's just for money, so he plans to ask a few more symbolic questions. Even if you might be at fault, he can hype you up and make you look like a victim.

But now, it sounds like you're both the victims—just not perfect, you have your own problems.

Spamton just sighed: "I think your only [problem] is that you gave your heart inappropriately. That person is clearly [not worthy] [you] [giving your heart], and obviously, [you] shouldn't casually [tell outsiders] certain things. ”

You listened in a daze, wanting to argue something, but then Spamton paused and continued: "Besides, when facing a [problem], [you] shouldn't be too harsh on yourself, but rather reflect on the other person's [problems]. ”

You blinked, though you didn't quite agree, but you still felt a guilty sense of being taught, so you instinctively placed your hands on your knees and sat up straight: "Oh oh...... Oh oh oh......

"[Understood]?" You heard him ask, probably feeling somewhat satisfied seeing your reaction.

You hesitated for a moment, then shook your head, hesitated for a moment, and then nodded again: "Uh...... Then could you please go beat her up? ”

You heard him sigh heavily. So you nervously smiled instinctively, pressing your lips together. Although you probably understood something, in reality you still didn't, so you felt embarrassed to continue.

After all, even if he says that, you'll probably still be like this next time...... Maybe you never learn.

But at this point, you actually became more alert and glanced at Spamton: "By the way, I just told you about it...... You can't tell anyone about it! Besides, I paid you—I'm your employer. If you tell anyone, I'll ...... ...... Forget it, I really can't do anything about it. "As you spoke, your shoulders slumped too, feeling a bit deflated.

Spamton thought to himself that if you really told others, it would make you look like a victim. Public opinion was overwhelmingly one-sided, unless he cleverly tweaked his wording, saying it was just because he had a falling out with a friend that he planned to hire thugs to get revenge on an ex-friend...... But that didn't help him at all.

So he simply added, "[Don't worry], enough [money] can keep me 'tight-lipped.'" ”

"Oh...... Then I'll give you a little later!" You clenched your fist and high-fived excitedly, thinking you understood something.

…… Actually, that's not what he meant, but if you gave him more money, of course he wouldn't mind—in fact, he was quite pleased.

"So, continue with the [Thug Agreement]?" he suggested. That topic just now was settled.

"Oh...... Yes, actually, I had the butler investigate too. There's information about where she appears whenever she comes in, and she usually appears at fixed times every day. So you just need to use the information to sack her and beat her up! "Speaking of which, you were quite interested, feeling around the cabinet nearby, then took out a plate of documents and handed it to Spamton.

He roughly glanced at your file, knowing where you needed to take action, and that you could get it done this afternoon. So Spamton closed the file. But then he looked up at you again: "Do you need a [photo as a keepsake]?" ”

You hesitated for a moment. To be honest, you didn't really want to see the other party's miserable state, but having photos would definitely confirm that Spamton had really done the work...... Hmm, so your expression finally turned serious: "Ah, then let's take a photo as a keepsake!" After all, I need to confirm your mission status so I can pay you...... Oh right, regarding what I just said, I'll give you 100,000 Dark Coin hush money......

Muttering to yourself, you somehow pull out a checkbook and write a check slip to Spamton.

He put the check away, nodded, then suddenly took out a phone—hmm, it looks like an old model? It probably hasn't been replaced in many years.

"So, [Add] [Contact Information]?" He asked.

You thought for a moment and gave a string of your phone numbers, watching Spamton type them into your phone. For some reason, you felt relieved, probably just ...... Confirm that everything is going smoothly.

Although you are still hesitating about whether you should hit the other person, at this point, you should fight! Otherwise, you will feel uncomfortable whenever you think about this matter, and you should try something else to distract your attention!

Just as you were thinking, you heard his voice again: "Maybe, the afternoon timing is just right." ”

You blinked sluggishly and looked at Spamton. Fine, you still couldn't figure out his expression, so you cautiously asked, "Uh...... Is it really that urgent? ”

"It's not an emergency, it's just that I think this kind of matter should be handled first."

You nodded half-understandingly, half-understanding. Actually, you thought it wouldn't matter to delay it until later, since you had been delaying for a while anyway, so you just muttered quietly, "Well...... Actually, I also delayed about half a month before finding your ......" But looking into his eyes, you don't know why you don't dare to continue.

Actually, you can't see his gaze clearly; in that moment, you feel as if he's throwing you a sharp, cold look—probably a reproach, so much so that you shut your mouth.

"No other [questions]?" he asked.

And you quickly shake your head: "No, no, no." ”

Spamton nodded in satisfaction, jumped off his seat, and headed toward the basement. You leaned on the table watching him walk out, put on that mech or something like that, and then staggered out the door. You yawned, probably busy with some things to handle. After lunch, just as you lay drowsy in bed, your phone suddenly vibrated.

You held back the yawn you were about to let out, checked your phone, and found that it was a photo sent by Spamton. It was the former friend you mentioned in the morning, and at that moment she was lying on the ground with her face bruised.

Somehow, although you felt a little vindicated, you also felt a bit wistful. At the same time, you had a sense of relief that someone had finally stood up for you—although that, too, had been hired with money.

So you just sent Spamton a string of thumbs-up emojis, threw your phone aside, and completely fell asleep.

At dinner, Spamton turned into that little puppet again and sat at the table, probably considering that you weren’t used to it. In any case, you ate this dinner feeling refreshed, and afterward, the rumors died down; it’s said that the person went out to clarify or something like that.

Spamton wouldn’t tell you that he had threatened the other person into doing it. He was just providing you with some extra services, after all, you had paid way too much money.

End.
