Employment Syndrome 3 (sptx you)

You just feel a bit relieved, thinking that person will eventually repent......

So one day at dinner, you couldn't help but bring this up to Spamton: "Speaking of which, that person hasn't been spreading rumors lately. It seems he's repented...... Hmm, or maybe he changed his mind or something? "Your mind wandered, recalling a game you played when you were bored.

Seeing you so enthusiastic, Spamton didn't dare interrupt you, just kept munching on his sandwich and continued talking excitedly.

"I knew it, even though I gave my heart a bit wrong, at least I have a decent eye for people. She's not someone who admits her mistakes and doesn't change...... "You're in a pretty good mood, and it's obvious you're happy."

Spamton couldn't help but interject: "No, I [requested] her [to keep quiet.] ”

You blinked, like a deflated balloon inflated, and slumped unhappily on the table: "Ah...... Alright. Actually, I don't have many friends; I've only made friends with sincerity. I didn't expect she would be that kind of person...... "You're a bit down."

But you still want to insist you're not someone who judges people, after all...... You've only made such a good friend! You don't know anyone else. You don't like having a wide social circle, so aside from that, you only have a few acquaintances.

Spamton looked at you speechlessly: "Maybe you could consider making friends through [Money Transactions]. ”

You tilted your head, still leaning on the table, watching the little puppet across from you slowly gnaw on a sandwich, and just sneered, "Buying friends with money?" I'm not stupid. How could a friend bought with money be genuine...... Most of the time, it's just for the money. "You muttered to yourself.

"I will be 'sincere.'" You wanted to say more when you heard Spamton's voice, and you couldn't help but widen your eyes in surprise at him.

His voice became stammering: "W-what are you saying, you mean I can pay you to buy you as a friend?" As you spoke, you couldn't help but size up Spamton again, but all you could tell was that he was serious.

You hesitated for a moment: "...... Are you sure? You probably don't know me very well, but you can be friends just because of money. How valuable do you think that is?" Although you were a little tempted, you still forced yourself to suppress the urge inside.

Spamton looked at you, put down his sandwich, and immediately said, "You hate [trouble], hate [handling affairs], and prefer [getting things without effort]. You hate [conflict], because you hesitated for half a month before deciding to [hire] someone to resolve [the issue]. In summary, [you] are somewhat [weak] in personality, or rather, [gentle in temperament]. You are [introverted], dislike [crowds], and [you] do not have other [friends]...... At the same time, you should [prefer] [minimalist style], but at the same time, [you] will try other [styles] out of your own [preference]. You prefer [sweets]......"

Before you could finish, you made a gesture for me to be quiet, making you a bit embarrassed: "Stop, stop, stop...... How can you analyze so much? I've confessed, I've confessed. That's it. I agree you can be my friend. I'll give you the money later." "You'd better use the money to shut him up."

Spamton closed his mouth, looking very enthusiastic. Actually, he said so much not because he wanted more money; it was only at the moment he left the mouth that he realized he already understood you—and so he was confident.

Well, at least for now, he simply wants to be friends with you.

You sighed, resting one hand on your chin, wondering how much money to give, but then saw Spamton suddenly jump off the table and run toward you. You stared at him, then saw him pick up the hand you had put down and seriously kiss the back of your hand—well, actually, you didn't feel any wetness, only heard the sound of a kiss, and you somewhat dumbly realized it was a hand-kissing gesture. Your mind was a bit confused for a moment, because you didn't know why he was doing it.

"Then, you and I will always be [friends added] [relationship]. I will help you clear away [troubles], resolve [conflicts], and listen to [troubles]. "You listened in a daze, not knowing why he said that.

So you can only nod awkwardly: "Oh oh...... Oh oh oh...... Uh, how much money do you need? "In the end, you still get cautious. After all, if you're that loyal, it definitely requires a lot of money, right?

You see Spamton look up at you. Somehow, you catch a bit of a bit of speechlessness through his glasses, so you rub your nose a little guiltily: "After all...... This is just friendship bought with money, or something like that. ”

Besides, this was your hired enforcer. Although to make it easier for you to have him live under the same roof and see you every day, looking up and down every day...... Ah, wait, it's only when you look down every day that you see him, but you think you're far from that close, and you can't naturally become friends.

Spamton was silent for a moment. Well, he knew you were just that kind of person...... After all, a couple of days ago he heard you complain about how you fell out with your previous friend. He said he liked you very much, but you didn't understand and said you liked him too. So he thought you had agreed to confess and planned to kiss you, but instead, you blamed him for not doing such things because you were just friends......

So in a way, he thinks you should be purebred wood.

So at this moment, he just looked at you speechlessly: "...... [Friend Fee]?" Then, one hundred [silver dollars] per day. "Actually, one hundred silver dollars isn't much to him, since you've given me too much other money. That one hundred silver dollars is just an extra tip to answer your question. He was seriously suggesting becoming friends with you, but in the end, he didn't want you to overthink it.

You nod thoughtfully, but somehow, you feel a bit disappointed because of it...... You start to blame yourself, thinking you shouldn't have such feelings. After all, you're just making a cold monetary transaction, so friendship and loyalty that can be gained with just money aren't necessarily bad.

"Alright...... Then just treat it as pocket money! Hey, is that right...... It doesn't seem quite right...... "You mutter, then pull out a 100-dollar dark coin bill from your pocket and hand it to Spamton.

Then you cheerfully say, "Here, today's friend fee is yours!" Let's be friends! "But this way, you have a real friend too—even though it was bought with money, thinking about it lifts your spirits again.

Spamton took the pocket money, placed it on his body, then looked up at you: "So, what about today's dinner?" "What do you need? I'll notify the butler." "Since Spamton arrived at your house, you all ate together during mealtime, so you simply handed Spamton the instructions for the butler's next meal. Anyway, this wasn't a trouble for him.

You rubbed your chin and let out an 'ouch' before you remembered the incident.

"Ah...... Well, I won't be having dinner at home. There's a banquet tonight, you know, it's the kind of gathering where the upper class hangs out. I have to go. "You shrugged.

After all, the family's status is there, so it's hard to refuse to go, and this gathering was organized by the queen.

Spamton nodded: "Understood, then I will wait for you to return." ”

You nodded in satisfaction. After finishing this lunch, you carefully picked out your evening dress and put on some light makeup, then took the vehicle that came to pick you up and headed to the banquet venue.

Dinner was enjoyed by Spamton sitting alone at the table. Usually, he would add a bit of what he needed after listing the dishes he needed. And since your appetite wasn't large, he usually only ate part of it and then had to take care of the rest, so Spamton didn't dare to order too much. In short, every meal was tailored to your taste, which made him very familiar with what tastes you liked and what dishes you liked.

But today, since you went out, it was rare that Spamton ordered only your favorite flavors. He actually enjoyed dinner, then casually found a spot in the living room to wait for you to come back.

You were still excited before leaving, but when you came back, you were puffed up, your eyes red, and you even wiped your eyes, looking like you weren't about to cry. You stormed in, then threw yourself into the sofa and sat down hard. The sofa caved in and bounced back up several times, and Spamton started sliding as you sat on it—after all, it was a very smooth leather sofa—and then it slid beside you.

You were still feeling a bit stifled, feeling a cool sensation beside your arm. Looking down, it was Spamton. You simply picked him up and held him in your arms, while your other hand started ruffling his hair, a bit displeased as you said, "I'm really stuffed with anger...... I barely ate anything! Maybe I'll have to go have a late-night snack later...... Ah, forget it, none of that matters. Go beat him up for me!" You started showing off your fangs and claws, lying on the sofa and gesturing casually.

"What happened at the gala tonight?" Spamton asked.

…… There was nothing either, so he just jumped down to the coffee table, grabbed a candy, handed it to you, and then climbed back onto your knees.

You feel his movements tickle you, so you simply lift him up and sit on your lap.

"Sigh...... There have been rumors about it recently, but most people know about it to some extent. It's just that most people don't care—at most, it's just gossip behind the scenes. And there are plenty of people who simply don't believe it...... "As you chewed the candy, your tone finally calmed down a bit, though your words were a bit unclear.

As you talk, you get a little angry and want to cry: "Besides, I was in a bad mood before, which is why I told my ex-friend that I dislike the opposite sex...... After all, when I used to go out occasionally, I would run into guys staring at my chest with lecherous eyes. That would be one thing, but some people even asked me why I wore a bit of something and even wanted to touch them!" As you spoke, you pressed Spamton's head hard again.

Spamton's body stiffened for a moment. Actually, he hadn't paid attention to this before...... It was only when you mentioned it that he realized your chest was indeed soft, and ...... Uh, maybe he just leaned back and touched it?

"Anyway, I really don't know what the rumors are exaggerating to the point...... But today I met a man who first looked me up and down with a critical attitude, then said that considering my family background and figure, he could barely marry me? And he said that with those rumors around, probably no one would be interested in me? "You didn't care about these things and just kept rambling about your experience tonight, since you were really about to be furious.

And saying such things in public is almost an insult, which is why you're so upset.

"Oh my god...... Actually, I had genuinely liked that person before. After all, I thought his manners were elegant and interesting, and we got along well before. But I never expected him to say that in public...... "You still find it hard to believe. But after you said all this, you felt much better, and in the end, you just let out a heavy breath.

"Ha...... Phew, forget it, maybe he didn't mean it sincerely. Anyway, I saw clearly what kind of scumbag that person was. I probably don't need you to beat him up; I just need to be a bit more careful with my words and actions...... You muttered.

"So. [Sincerity] again [misplaced]?" "After hearing your rambling, he asked this question first.

Your smile stiffened for a moment, then you chuckled awkwardly: "Alright, I admit I misjudged people. I swear I've learned my lesson." ”

…Actually, making a deliberate pact with him is probably also a case of misjudging people, after all, his intentions aren’t simple. Thinking this way, Spamton, of course, didn’t speak; he just continued the topic: “Well, I think your ‘beating plan’ has no ‘problems,’ or rather… is ‘exceptionally reasonable.’”

Hearing this, you actually hesitated a bit: “But at the very least, it’s a noble family, right? Unlike before, it won’t be easy to handle, and they won’t just let it slide without investigation. This time, if they investigate, it’ll be troublesome…”

“Don’t worry, I won’t give them any chance to investigate,” you hear Spamton say.

You blink dully, about to say something, but then you see Spamton turn around, and the puppet’s finger touches your forehead. The force isn’t heavy, but your head instinctively tilts backward.

“Don’t hesitate, and don’t feel sad. This is completely their problem; there’s no need to blame yourself.” Saying this, Spamton actually just hopes that you won’t hesitate. Watching you get excited at first to teach the other party a lesson but then hesitate to go through with it, he can’t help but feel a little disappointed at your lack of resolve.
