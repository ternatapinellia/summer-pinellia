Employment Syndrome 1 (sptx you)

Sptx, you, OOC counts me as me. And the full title of this story is 'Wasn't it supposed to be a thug? Why don't you want money and want to sleep with me?!' 》

I wonder how long it will be, just writing for fun.

——

Take a deep breath, and you roll around on your big bed. Turns out the saying 'don't want lots of money, lots and lots of love' isn't a joke...... No! You don't need love either. To you, love is useless; it can be changed at will, impossible to keep forever, love is completely unimportant. You only worry about how to use a pile of money, and even if you have money, you can't resist being foolish and insisting on getting close to it...... At least what you think is brainless.

After two seconds of contemplation, you suddenly sat up from the bed. You figured out how to deal with the money, and also how to stop the idiots from getting close to you and give you some peace. Why not just hire thugs? At worst, just beat up those idiots who came over and beat them up. If it really doesn't work out, sneak around and put a sack on them to beat them up. If anyone you dislike, beat them up too. That way, you'd feel refreshed.

You start to feel happy, but despite the wonderful idea, you don't really know what the call for hiring a messenger is. So reality is harsh. After two seconds of hesitation, you decide to post the commission, believing that as long as you pay enough, no one will come.

So you hastily wrote a recruitment commission for enforcers. As for the salary...... Uh, then one million Dark Coins per month will be fine. If that's not enough, add more. If no one comes in the next few days, you decide to add more Dark Coins.

Then write down your contact number and address. Make a few copies of this commission, since you don't want to leave home too far, so you only post a few near your home and a few in the alley behind. What if someone desperate wants to come and take a look...... Although you also have to be careful about murder and robbery.

Then you go home and lie in bed, happily imagining what happens next. You've made up your mind: look through the peephole, and if someone thinks it's no good at first glance, just don't open the door.

But before you can finish imagining the next wonderful scene, there is a knock at the door. You have no choice but to get out of bed in annoyance and peer through the peephole to see who is knocking. But when you first look outside, you can't see a single person there—surely it can't be haunted...... Then you hear another knock at the door, sounding like it's below the main door. So your gaze looks down and you really see a little short man through the peephole, and you also notice he is holding a stack of power of attorney letters...... Counting to ten, aren't these the ones you just printed?

But a little dwarf probably wouldn't pose much threat to you, so you comforted yourself like that and then opened the door.

And he tore down all ten commission letters—what kind of perseverance is that? Are you going to reprint and post them yourself? If so, you'd have to send this guy away first. You don't want to wait until he thinks no one has left, only to have all the new commissions he posted be torn up and knocked on your door again.

And his clothes were all sloppy, and there seemed to be some kind of smell from him...... In short, you quietly took a few steps away from him.

You put your hands on your hips, trying to look a bit more imposing, then look down at him from above: "...... Is there something you need?" ”

He waved the power of attorney and showed it to you: '[Hello], [self-introduction], I am Spamton.G.Spamton, and I am now applying for [your] [hitter position].'

You sighed. Sure enough, what is meant to happen will come. But what could such a tiny puppet possibly do? Could it be to go up and bite someone...... Anyway, you couldn't help but start laughing, first denying another thing: "Alright, even if you tore up ten authorization letters, I still can't give you ten times the price of one million." ”

Spamton nodded; actually, he knew about this. But when you posted your commission in the alley, he happened to be in the trash bin and saw you. After you left, he came out to see what was written on your commission, and then he was tempted. After all, he wouldn't give up a million silver dollars.

Tearing off all other commissions is obviously to prevent competitors from coming in. Since he has his eye on this position, it can only belong to him.

Now you stand with your hands on your hips, looking at him from head to toe, and finally shake your head regretfully: "But you're too small, you don't look good at all. I feel like I can't hire you...... Since I'm a hired enforcer, I have to find someone who looks really tough." ”

"No [relationship]. I'm not just [maintaining the status quo]. Before I fully become a [enforcer], as long as I obtain the [Abyss] [Body] of the [Queen's Mansion]'s [Fitness Body], I can become the [perfect enforcer]. "He sounds like he's trying hard to promote himself, while you just look at him with a strange expression, suspicious in your heart.

In short, you still know that the queen's basement really does have a set of mechas locked away...... Probably abandoned, you and her are pretty close—at least you're acquainted. After all, your family is so wealthy now partly because the queen appreciated it, so just spending money to buy a set of mechas she might not need isn't a big deal.

But...... You've only just met, and you don't even know this employee. Well, maybe he's a future employee. How will he work? Is he suited for the job? Why should you spend money on him?

That's what you think, so naturally you asked. You said, "I might have gotten that thing you mentioned by spending some money...... But you haven't done anything yet. I don't know how capable you really are, so why should I spend money for you?" You genuinely have this doubt.

"No [relationship]. [You] will find this is a [value for money] deal that will definitely make you [kill]. Besides, I don't think [you] care about this [money]. " he said.

You scratched your head. You couldn't see his expression clearly, so you didn't know what he was feeling right now. But Spamton was right—you really didn't care much about money, just afraid of trouble. So most things you could solve with money were too lazy to handle yourself. Now, being willing to chat with Spamton a bit longer was just boring.

So after thinking it over, you finally waved your hand grandly: "Alright, let's start with a one-month probation period. I won't pay you for this month, but you have to work hard...... Also, looking so sloppy, you can stay at my place first. Oh, there's a guest room there." As you spoke, you pointed toward another direction in the corridor.

Then you closed the door, grabbed the authorization letters from his hands and threw them on the table, returned to your room, lay down on the bed, and after a moment of dazing, remembered to call the queen. In any case, you paid and agreed to treat her to acid pie for a month. The queen generously agreed to your request, telling you to have the serocard butler deliver the mech to your residence the next day.

You hung up satisfied, rolled around on the bed a few times, then fell asleep again. It wasn't until dinner time that you got up and walked outside, rubbing your eyes as the butler brought you all kinds of delicious food. Honestly, you didn't have much of an appetite, so you just bored your fork with chicken in your fork. Then you saw Spamton climb over and sit across from you. You glanced at him and saw that the puppet's head couldn't even go above the table; you could only see the top of his head. You had to quickly call the butler to bring him a few more cushions, and now his head was visible on the table.

You didn't pay him any mind, just finished the dinner by yourself. In the spacious room, you did some walking and digestion, then went to the shower on time to wash up and rest...... But before you could rest, you were stopped, your leg was grabbed, and when you looked down, it was Spamton.

“…… I don't have a suitable [change of clothes]." He thought it over for a long time before coming to tell you. In fact, he really didn't have any clothes to change into. Even in this situation, he couldn't guarantee his own clothing or food.

You glanced at him in disbelief, your mind spinning twice, realizing that he had previously been rummaging through trash, so it was normal not to have it.

"Ah, you can talk to the butler about this kind of thing, and just ask me for reimbursement when the time comes." You thought for a moment and said. Thinking carefully, you don't really need that money, and you're genuinely curious about what kind of surprise this little puppet is making with such confidence.

"Anyway, if you need anything, just ask the butler to buy it. There's no need to come to me specifically for this kind of thing—I don't even know what to buy...... Unless you want to wear a doll dress, then I have a lot piled up over there." As you spoke, you couldn't help but yawn, because this was already your usual rest time.

As for why there are so many doll dresses...... Yeah, you used to really enjoy dress-up dolls. Of course, you've tried all kinds of games, and a bunch of dresses are just a collector's habit. In fact, you don't even bother to swap them—you just buy them and just toss them away.

"[Okay], I'm [already known]." Spamton nodded to show he understood. In fact, he was just looking for some opportunities to talk to you. He noticed that after half a day, he hadn't found any chance to talk to you, and instead talked the most when he first came in. After half a day, he decided to use this topic to test your attitude...... Hmm, it seems you don't really care.

And you just nodded randomly and went back to rest. Early the next morning, sure enough, the Sekka butler brought over that set of mechs. The Sekka butler said it was called Neo, but you didn't care what it was called—after all, the Queen had left them in the basement and abandoned them.

Then you watch Spamton tinker with the mech set. You have no idea what he's doing, you can't understand. In short, you insert the disk he mentioned into the Neo and see if nothing has changed. You simply ignore it and stand up, planning to head into the room.

But after just a few steps, you hear an even worse sound behind you. Of course, compared to Spamton's previous voice, it's rougher, and for some reason, the electric current sounds even louder. You can't stand it and can't help but turn back to see what Spamton is like now.

…… Wow, indeed, he looks quite tall now. At least you need to look up to see him now. You sigh a little, unable to help but smack your lips and ask, "So...... Looks like you can start working tomorrow?" "You even get a little excited."

Although he didn't know why his hands and feet were still shaking...... He also didn't know why there were some strange strings and puppet stands on top, but it seemed like there was nothing wrong with them.

You don't care about that; you just want more enforcers.

"Of course, no problem. Maybe you have a training ground, which allows me to show off my power?" He looked up, probably laughing heartily. You hesitated for a moment, thought about your home's layout, and finally spoke sincerely.

"Hmm...... As for the training ground, it does exist, but it's in the basement of my house. It's just that it's been a long time since I used it, and it's gathering dust. "You don't even know why your family has such a training ground. You don't even need that thing......

Spamton nodded; he didn't care where it was. So you hesitated for a moment, but eventually led the way, leading him to the basement and watching him raise his hand...... Ah, no, you could say you could have raised the laser cannon, but in any case, you used that shell to blast the wall.

“…… Wow. You rubbed your chin and couldn't help but sigh, starting to ponder the question. To be honest, you didn't really think a single thug could cause much harm. At most, you just got so angry you called the thug to beat him up. But now you feel that even has some killing power, which makes you hesitate.

And once you lose your temper, you can't help but wonder: do you really want the thug to deal with that person? Maybe it's just your own actions that went wrong at the time, or did you get angry just during the conversation with that person? Maybe there was a better way to solve it at that time? In short, you basically do this every time. After your initial anger passes, start reflecting on whether it was really your fault.

So you watch as Spamton, who has turned to face you, hesitantly starts to speak: 'I think... maybe that person, looking back now, doesn't necessarily need to be severely beaten...'

For some reason, the little puppet who you could originally only look down upon now advances with a sense of pressure. He is closing in on you, closing in on you, until you are forced to lift your head and look at him. And now he looks down at you, speaking in that hoarse, electric-blended voice: 'Do you really think that a severe beating is no longer necessary?'

You swallow and explain: 'It's just... maybe, I also did something wrong before, it's not purely that person's fault...'

'...Then, [tell] me, the full [picture of the whole matter].'
