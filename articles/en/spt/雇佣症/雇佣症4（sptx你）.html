Employment Syndrome 4 (sptx you)

You hesitated for a moment, not knowing why you felt you couldn't deny it again, otherwise you might be the one who got beaten first—no, you're the employer yourself! Though you probably wouldn't hit that hard—no! In the end, you just feel guilty, right?

Why are you feeling guilty? You're the one who pays! Thinking of this, you felt a bit more justified, but when you saw Spamton staring into your eyes, your shoulders still slumped. You really couldn't muster up the courage to come.

So you just lowered your voice and whispered, "Uh...... Well then, then I'll leave it to you to give him a beating. But don't get caught...... "Your voice was very soft. Honestly, if the living room weren't quiet enough, Spamton wouldn't have been able to catch your voice."

"Don't worry, there won't be any mistakes. I will perfectly complete the mission." Spamton said this.

After hesitating for a moment, you finally nodded, responding to his answer.

The butler quickly handed over the intelligence you and Spamton had gathered. In fact, since the other party was from the upper class, the investigation was easier, since many of the information about your whereabouts had been leaked. Early the next morning, after breakfast, you watched Spamton go to the basement, then drifted out wearing that NEO outfit. You watched him leave, gazing at his back, lost in thought.

Well, you don't even know what you're really thinking. It's just that once you've made up your mind, some things shouldn't be said unless they've been carefully considered, or they'll only bring trouble upon yourself. For those small things, at worst, just endure them......

Well, that's what it is. You decided on your own and collapsed back onto the sofa.

It was probably around lunchtime when Spamton came back. He didn't look badly hurt, just went back to the basement on his own, then reverted to that little puppet climbing onto your leg. Watching him crawl around was really troublesome, so you simply scooped him up and put him on your lap.

"What's wrong...... Oh right, the agreed payment—I'll give you a check later, plus today's friend bonus...... You muttered to yourself, but first slipped a 100-denomination Dark Coin to Spamton. He accepted it without hesitation.

"[Compensation][Never in a hurry]. I just need to [report] [today's situation]. "Because you hold him so you can't see his expression clearly, you only hear him say that, so you nod twice, oh, and you can't help but feel a bit confused—isn't it better to just tell yourself when reporting? Why do you have to sit down?"

"Then go ahead, I want to see what kind of fate that person really has." You said this somewhat indifferently, but in fact, you didn't really care what kind of outcome the other person would get...... Hmm, you feel like you've already come up with a better solution, and you even think you're pretty smart!

His chin rested on Spamton's head, and you watch him open his phone, lingering on a few photos, even videos—though I don't know how it was filmed, but the video shows him putting that person in a sack and then beating him, while the remaining photos show him crying and begging for mercy.

You glanced at it twice, and you have to admit, seeing the other person getting beaten and crying and begging for mercy was incredibly happy. But...... I was still a bit worried.

"Come to think of it, did he see your face? Isn't it a bit troublesome to fix things like this...... "You're worried about that."

"It's okay. If the injury doesn't leave a trace, he can't [sue]. ”

You're still a bit worried, but if he explains it like that, you probably don't need to worry anymore—after all, if the other party really wants to sue you, you don't know how to keep your own henchman. Come on, at least he's pretty useful, right?

But at this point, you suddenly remembered something and felt energized: "By the way—actually, I think just getting beaten up isn't satisfying. People like this may look glamorous on the surface, but probably aren't that great in private. So this morning, I carefully reviewed the intelligence I got from yesterday's investigation—"

Before you could finish, Spamton interrupted you: "[You] plan to [attack the other party] with public opinion?" ”

You pouted, a bit unhappy: "At least let me finish speaking...... But that's the point—I'm planning to use public opinion to attack him! Hmph, don't you think I'm a certain kind of person just because you hear rumors? Now that his rumors are spreading, he'll probably be scared to death first. "As you spoke, you hummed in satisfaction.

And this also helps divert attention, but if you spread rumors...... You have to carefully select the candidates. If the other side traces and finds out the rumors are on your side, it's a disaster.

Of course, if you can bring down the other party's company, you don't need to worry about that, but it's probably still a bit difficult......

Spamton didn't say anything, because you often give a long sentence and want to explain every aspect clearly, so he interrupted you first to summarize for you. But he still listened carefully to what you said next, and of course, he understood your intention.

However, before that, he chose to confirm again: "So, what kind of [public opinion] do you intend to [fabricate]?" ”

You thought for a moment. This morning, you came up with such an idea, thinking you were clever and went off in high spirits, but of course, you hadn't figured out what to do next. So now you just stammered and scratched your head: "Uh...... Exactly, I read those reports and found that he often goes to private with civilian women...... Are I planning to make a move from that angle? But before that, I should first send a statement to his family, explaining what happened to me and strongly demanding an apology. "As you spoke, you let out a deep sigh.

Spamton turned to look at you: "So, how do you plan to [write] the [Declaration Letter]?" ”

Actually, you only just remembered this incident. Yesterday, you got so angry that you even sent Spamton to hit someone. Today, after calming down a bit and regaining your ability to think, you only realized that you should have seriously declared that you should cut ties with his family. After all, before that, your two families had a pretty good relationship.

And you've already thought about how to organize your words this morning, so now you're confidently pulling over a piece of paper: "I'll write it for you right now!" ”

Spamton watched your movements, watching your hand slip past him and start writing the letter on the coffee table, watching the ink you dripped onto the paper without much hesitation, but the more he looked, the more his brows furrowed.

As for how you wrote it—

[Dear XX Family:

At last night's ball, I was insulted by xxx and felt I could not bear it. I ask your family to stay away from me from now on. I am sorry that I cannot continue to associate with your family. If any additional losses are caused, I will naturally compensate you, but I sincerely ask that you and your family not disturb you in the future. In return, I will also stay away from your family. I do not wish to associate with your family anymore, and only wish to cut off all contact from now on. I hope we will meet again and never get to know each other. ]

To be honest, the letter wasn't very long, and you were quite satisfied with it. You thought your tone was polite and distant, so it probably didn't matter.

"It's okay, but honestly, this is my first time writing a letter like this. If it weren't for the relationship between the other party and my family, it would've been okay...... You were a bit confident at first, but by the end, you started to feel uneasy again. To be honest, if you hadn't been really angry last night, maybe you could have just tolerated something within the normal range. After all, you didn't plan to fall out with them, and besides, our relationship was decent—seeing each other all the time, and if you saw it and it was a falling out, how awkward would that be?

You were still wondering whether to change your words when you felt a stab on your forehead—not gently, but with a bit of force. You instinctively covered your forehead, looking at Spamton with teary eyes. You saw it clearly—he had just withdrawn his finger!

So you couldn't help but start complaining to him: "What are you doing...... I didn't, and I never said I wouldn't send this letter! I've already done very well, haven't I!" After you finished, you paused. You felt something was off. What are you feeling guilty about now?

Spamton sighed silently. He finally understood that, to some extent, you could actually be considered weak and easy to bully...... Or rather, you were too soft, always subconsciously thinking of people in the positive light.

He turned his back to look at your letter, cleared his throat: "[No.] [Honest Explanation:] [Very Problematic]. [You] used [overly euphemistic.] "He commented on your letter.

You pouted, still holding your forehead. Even though it didn't hurt anymore, you still had to pretend to pitifully for a while. So now, you covered your forehead and leaned in to read the letter, your chin once again resting on Spamton's head: "Alright, so where exactly is the problem?" ”

"First, you shouldn't be seeking compensation; you should make a claim. Second, you can change your honorifics; you don't need to be so respectful. You're the victim, so you naturally have higher authority and don't need to be so cautious." Spamton said this, rarely giving you a long explanation.

And you just nod half-understandingly. To be honest, you don't really get it—uh, but you probably already know how to revise it!

So you confidently pulled another piece of paper and started writing another letter, assuring yourself, "I get it, I roughly know how to write it!" ”

Spamton leaned in again to see how you had written it, and then fell silent again.

[xxx family:

I want to cut off all contact with your family. I believe I cannot tolerate the humiliation from your noble son, xxx, so I earnestly ask your family not to associate with mine in the future, and I will also avoid seeing anyone from your family. ]

This letter was much shorter, and the wording was less tactful. You wiped the unexpected cold sweat from your forehead, then asked, almost as if asking for praise: "...... What do you think of this letter? ”

Oh my god, you really are asking for praise, your tone carrying a barely noticeable nervousness and anticipation. Honestly, before this, you never imagined you would turn out like this.

"[Barely passed]. However, [delete] your [plea]. This should be your [request], not [pleading]...... Forget it. [You] [took the letter again] and [began writing according to what I [informed]. ”

You let out a couple of disappointed 'oh-ohs', though that initial sentence barely got through helped lift your mood a little—but now, you just gloomily put the paper aside, pull out another one, listen to Spamton's report, and sullenly finish writing the letter according to his dictation.

[To xxx Family:

I am very doubtful about your family's education, or rather, the overall quality of your family. At yesterday's ball, your esteemed son xxx blatantly and openly insulted me, and countless outsiders witnessed this farce. ...

I demand compensation from your side, and after that, we sever all contact. Whether it is close visits or business cooperation, all will be suspended or even terminated. ]

You have to admit, this letter is indeed written much better than yours, mixed with sarcasm and some belittling, while also trying to match your tone...... Well, maybe you really did lower your stance when writing the letter.

So after writing this letter, you just mumble quietly, "Well, it seems I don't have much talent for this matter......

"No, it's just that your talent isn't here. If you were [writing poetry or literature], or [expressing respect], your [letter content] would be extraordinary." Then you noticed Spamton turned and sat facing you.

So you rekindled your hope, even if it wasn't real, and it didn't matter: "Oh, oh...... Then I'll give it a try!" You pulled yourself together again.

“Moreover, you control the business and have the management rights of the company.” He added this, and you couldn't help but nod. Indeed, to be honest, you are quite experienced in managing a company. You don’t have any innate talent; it’s just that you worked hard to learn and mastered these skills.

“Learning and striving to take control, and persisting, can also be a kind of talent.” Before you could say anything else, he added.

So you exhaled: “Sorry… I clearly didn’t need it, yet I still let you comfort me. Actually, in this kind of situation, I just need to process it myself…” After all, it’s normal not to have talent, and it’s normal to feel disappointed; you just need to adjust. It’s something you really shouldn’t have said out loud.

“It’s okay, we are now friends. Also, the special report on public opinion will be handled by me.”

Then your mood, which had just started to improve, sank again, and you gloomily said, “Oh… got it!”
