Gold Medal Pet 1-sptx you

The name could also be endorphins. 3k+, estimated to finish in three or four chapters, not long. sptx you, OOC apologies

——

You yawn, rub your eyes, and straighten yourself in front of the mirror—well, it seems no cosmetics can hide the dark circles under your eyelids anymore.

You haven't had a good night's sleep for two or three months in a row. Maybe it's because your anxiety is too severe. When you went to the sleep disorder department for a checkup, the doctor gently suggested you look for emotional support and build connections with others, whether animals or people—whether they're people of light or darkness. Overall, you're deficient in a series of factors, with endorphins being the most severe.

After hesitating for a moment, you decide to accept the doctor's suggestion. Indeed, you are solitary and haven't had any contact with the outside world for a long time. Yet you hate people, hate talking to people, whether the People of Light or the People of Darkness—it's pretty much the same concept to you: people.

But the Dark People are always a bit better, since to some extent, they're more like objects in disguise. So you decide to stay in the Dark World for a while, buy a property on the edge of Cyber City, and then go to the pet store to get a bunch of cats and dogs, along with a few birds—cuckoos, parrots, and even a few hamsters—and a squid mice. For some reason, as soon as those little animals see you, they come over and refuse to leave. But when you see those soft, smooth fur, your heart softens. You don't want any of the animals to be sad, so in the end, you grit your teeth and buy them all.

Fortunately, you still have some savings and used all the universal currency to exchange a large pile of dark coins before coming here.

You hold a cat in your arms, a hamster cage in the other, and a bunch of cats and dogs running around your feet, along with a few chipmare mice—birds flying in the sky following you. You sigh and pass through the alley you must pass home. You hear something from the trash can, but you don't have time to look, because you still have to groom the cats and dogs in your arms and feet. Listening to their soothing purring, your mood gradually relaxes.

Back home, you put the cage on the floor. The once quiet house was now filled with the chatter of animals, but you didn't find it noisy. Instead, you half-knelt and gently groomed the kitten at your feet, your fingers running through the smooth fur. Parrots and similar creatures flew over, stopped, perched on your shoulder, and refused to leave.

You prepare to count how many small animals you bought today, taking a deep breath as you gently hum a song to them. The melodious tune makes the previously noisy animals all quiet down. You stroke the cats and dogs beside you, silently counting in your mind until your hand unconsciously touches the greasy, seemingly bristly brittle fur—not only that, you also touch down to something that seems smooth, even somewhat cold...... Skin?

You glanced down, your pupils dilating sharply, and for a moment, you panicked. The reason was simple: the animal in your hand didn't seem like a small animal you bought—it was a puppet that had come from somewhere.

It's your fault—the size difference between the other animals is so big, and you didn't notice...... He looked roughly about one meter tall. The other little animals were a bit displeased at this, snoring angrily and suppressedly. Even the parrots perched on his shoulder began flapping their wings, and a few tried to peck him. You quickly reached out to stop them, gently stroked their fur, and then they quieted down again, perching on a nearby rack, though still staring at him like tigers.

The cats and dogs kept clasping their hands, telling you to stop petting them and instead pet yourself. You suddenly snapped back to reality, withdrew your hand, and stroked a ragdoll cat that had rubbed against your feet. Its eyes widened in fright, and you instinctively curled up to look at it: "You...... Are you the pet I bought too?" "You say that, but you don't believe it at all. Besides, if you ask about it, you'll regret it—you know you've asked the wrong question yourself.

But he nodded as if it were only natural, even introducing himself to you, proudly puffing out his chest: "Yes, I am [you] [the Gold Medal Salesman] Spamton G. Spamton, who is determined to pay in full!" ”

You stroked the goosebumps rising on your arm. To be honest...... You didn't even expect his voice to be like this. It's not that it was terrible to accept; you just felt that this kind of sound, mixed with electric currents and recorded audio, was the first time you'd heard it. The little animals around you were growing even louder, snoring in dissatisfaction and anger the day before yesterday, so you could only stroke their soft fur to comfort them.

"Well...... But what I need to buy is a pet, not a salesperson. "In the end, you decided to explain quietly." For some reason, the smile on his face seemed stiff, but then it curved even further.

"No, I am exactly the perfect pet model you need. If missed, there will be no more." His words left you stunned, making you take a while to understand.

You and it stared silently for a while, and the parrot on its shoulder began repeating your words, as if mocking Spamton: "What you need to buy is a pet!" "Not a salesperson!"

Spamton, however, reached out, then placed your hand on his head, pressed your hand and stroked your hair, smiling: "I believe [you] will give a [100% positive review]." ”

…… In fact, he only changed his tune after hearing your request. At first, he didn't know why you brought so many small animals back, but he felt he couldn't miss this chance to meet the People of Light, so he came along with them. And now, he knew this was probably his only chance to interact with the People of Light—he couldn't give up. After living in the trash for so long, he desperately hoped everything could change, desperately wished he could return to how things were before.

That's why he made up his mind to put your hand on his head. Even though this wasn't something he wanted to do. But soon, he changed his mind, realizing that being touched by you was actually a very pleasant thing.

After hesitating for a moment, you still leaned in, gently stroked his hair, scratched his face, and finally scratched his chin. Although you were a bit unfamiliar with this human-like form...... It wasn't much like petting a cat or something, so you scratched his chin again, while Spamton tilted his head back, squinting slightly, even making a faint purr.

He was even a bit shocked—it was purely a subconscious reaction, but he really did like your touch. This was unimaginable for Spamton.

Listening to his purring, your eyes curved into a smile, and you couldn't help but laugh. This really is no different from other pets...... Moreover, this pet can talk, making it seem more convenient than others to hear requests.

"Alright then...... Maybe you really are the pet I need. But before that, shouldn't you take a bath first? "You stroked his chin again and asked softly. The other little animals were all very clean, clearly having been thoroughly cleaned by the pet shop. But Spamton's body was dirty—or rather, he was no different from someone who had just crawled out of a pile of trash—and maybe that was true.

Spamton was stunned, instinctively shrinking back. He didn't want to take a hot bath—that would always bring back some unpleasant memories for him. But after sizing him up for a while, you finally handed him something like a towel you had just bought. Of course, he seemed to need a change of clothes, but you definitely didn't—you just glanced at it and didn't dare look again. His lower half wasn't even wearing pants, which means...... He was openly exposing his genitals, and in that sense, he seemed no different from other pets. So you think, maybe your previous clothes would have been suitable for him too, and in the end, you took out a white cotton nightdress.

When you look up again, he's about to sneak out to your doorstep. You tilt your head, puzzled: "Isn't it my top pet?" Why is it ...... Trying to sneak away? "Though you wouldn't be that angry, since you know most small animals are afraid of water, or rather, afraid of bathing. You could understand Spamton's fear, but still walked over, grabbed him by the back of the neck, and pulled him back into the living room.

Spamton continued to argue: "A Gold Pet doesn't necessarily need a Bathing job." ”

And you replied seriously, "But all my pets need to be cleaned thoroughly...... After all, I don't want to sleep with a dirty pet tonight." You complained, but didn't hear the puppet's response. You turned to look at him and saw that the puppet's face had inexplicably turned bright red, with wisps of white smoke...... Or rather, steam was rising from the top of his head.

You find it a bit funny, but indeed, because of sleep disorders, you've already planned to have these little animals sleep with you. After all, having them by your side visibly calms your anxiety.

You push Spamton, then bring the change of towels and clothes into the bathroom, and finally close the door. You turn on the faucet, and hot water starts spraying from it, while Spamton is sitting in the bathtub. He looks at you in surprise: "[You] want to participate in [bathing]?" ”

“…… Isn't it normal to help your pet bathe?" You tilted your head too, and while you were at it, you tidied up your shampoo, body wash, and pet bath oils. Hot water quickly filled half the tub. Spamton instinctively wanted to jump out, but you scratched his chin, stuffed it back in, and even pulled off his coat.

For some reason, his face seemed even hotter—at least the temperature you accidentally touched while scratching your chin told you.

And you just blinked innocently at him, splashing hot water on him, then squeezing out pet bath oil and applying it to his hair and upper body...... Just as you were about to reach out again, Spamton hurriedly held your hand down. For some reason, his voice seemed a bit stuttered.

"I...... I can switch the operating mode myself! "As he spoke, he hurriedly squeezed out some shower gel and applied it to his body, while you focused on ruffling his hair. After confirming that the grease was almost completely washed off, you finally drenched it little by little with hot water to wash off the foam.

Spamton's body seemed to relax too, or maybe your movements made him feel comfortable. He started snoring again, then suddenly stopped.

Although there was a little incident, the bathing process was still over. In the end, you put that white nightgown on him and pushed him out of the bathroom.

*Endorphins, also known as endorphins or endorphins. Endorphins are neuromodulatory chemicals secreted by the pituitary gland. They are spontaneously produced by the body after persistence in doing something for a long time, making people feel euphoric, satisfied, and calm. Endorphins play an important role in regulating emotional behaviors (including anxiety, fear, tension, and pleasure) and in pain control. They are closely related to drug addiction, post-exercise excitement, appetite control, sexual response, as well as mental illnesses such as depression and schizophrenia.

Endorphins are a class of endogenous opioid peptides that specifically bind to morphine receptors in the brain, acting similarly to morphine, hence the name "endogenous morphin-like peptide." Besides analgesic functions, these peptides also have many other physiological functions, such as regulating body temperature, cardiovascular health, and respiratory function.

Endorphins are a general term for neuropeptides with morphine-like activity.
