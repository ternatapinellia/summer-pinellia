He just shook his head and repeated, "[Sorry], I didn't provide [perfect service]." ”

After saying that, he continued eating breakfast. You glanced at him tentatively, but still couldn't get an answer, so in the end you had to give up. He won't tell you now, but someday he'll will...... Probably?

But over time, you gradually forget about it. You just still treat him as a slightly different pet—he's just a bit bigger and speaks things you can understand. Other than that, he doesn't seem much different from other pets.

After all, when you occasionally subconsciously pat his head, he squints and lifts his head high, looking proud, since the little animals surrounding you are all purring in dissatisfaction.

Sometimes, when you reach out to touch his head, Spamton will actively lean in, rubbing his cheek against your palm a few times, making you blush with emotion. In the end, you can only stumble your head away, not daring to look into his innocent yet indescribable eyes.

Every morning, you still maintain that state. Sometimes, even if you instinctively curl up and bury your head in his belly to fall asleep at night, the next morning you find Spamton's head buried inside your belly, and you end up in that strange posture, sharing the bed for who knows how long.

In short, you feel refreshed and your mental state gradually improves. You no longer have to toss and turn at night unable to sleep; you just need to lie down for a while and fall asleep, and you won't wake up suddenly in the middle of the night. You really enjoy this feeling.

Spamton, on the other hand, felt he couldn't go on like this. He admitted he really liked the environment at your home, and admitted that being in such a warm setting made him not even want to leave. He even felt a bit guilty, since bringing him back wouldn't do you any good—on the contrary, you spent a lot on him.

So during meals, he always looks at you with a complicated expression, and you glance at Spamton somewhat puzzled: "Why do you keep staring at me?" "Do you think he wants to say something...... Probably.

"Thank you for the angel's generosity." He just shook his head and said this.

You stared at him for a few seconds, not knowing if what you meant matched what he meant, so you just shook your head: "It's nothing...... After all, raising one pet is still a raise, and raising a bunch is still raising. ”

Your words were understated, but Spamton lowered his head. He knew he couldn't go on like this. You have your own goals, and he has his own ambitions and ideals—and he knows very well that he is not content to remain just a pet forever. Even though you may not be able to tell them apart.

But since he's still considered a pet to you—then you should agree to any excessive requests, right? Anyway, Spamton decided to use you, and of course, that was the first and last time.

He unconsciously stirs the bacon twice with his fork, and while you're dipping your bread in jam, you suddenly hear Spamton talking again.

"The Queen's Mansion is the Underground Abyss...... There is a [Fit Body], and I wish to have the right to use it. "This time, he lifts his head, gazing at you with intense intent."

You hesitated for a moment...... Honestly, other pets still have to guess their thoughts. His thoughts are just for him to say, which is really convenient. But for the queen's mansion, or the basement, it really is a bit difficult for you.

But if the pet wants it and the pet gets it, you'll still try. So you nodded: "Alright." "You didn't ask further. You didn't want to go and get this body—what exactly does it want—well, maybe you're still curious, but for you, knowing the answer doesn't mean much."

Spamton was a bit surprised. He had been nervous just now, after all, he didn't know if you'd agree, or if you'd ask anything more. But now...... He actually felt a sense of emptiness, since you hadn't asked anything.

So he hands you something like a USB and adds a sentence: "In front of the [Underground Abyss], you can use the [Obstacle Blockade] to solve the problem with the key cracker." ”

You accepted the USB, glanced at Spamton again, and patiently asked, "Besides that, you probably have other requests, right?" ”

“…… Hand me the [Hard Drive Eject] inside the [Bodybuilding Body]. "He hesitated for a moment, but still answered your question.

You nodded, got up, put the finished dishes into the dishwasher, checked the eating habits of the other animals, then waved to Spamton and left. Spamton also waved absentmindedly at you.

You didn't expect that when you went over today, there were no guards inside the Queen's mansion. The slot card butlers you found when you passed by before were nowhere to be found, so you entered the mansion very smoothly. But when you were searching for the basement, you had some trouble, since it was a bit hard to find.

But after a long search, you finally found a hidden room, went to the basement, and tried to avoid those inexplicably complex mechanisms. Finally, you obtained the hard drive inside that so-called well-built body.

…… Well, the so-called well-built body was just a piece of armor hanging on the wall, covered in dust and even covered in moss. I don't know why it's so heavily guarded.

You returned home and handed the hard drive to Spamton, speaking succinctly: "The hard drive you want." ”

Spamton didn't know you could be this fast...... He was already prepared—maybe you wouldn't be coming back today, maybe he'd wait for you to come back and apologize to him for the moment you do. You really couldn't fulfill his request, couldn't go to the basement, and couldn't get this hard drive.

Or rather, precisely because of this, he didn't have high expectations at first, which is why he was now somewhat overjoyed.

He took the disk, and this time looked at you with great seriousness—even his voice grew serious, making you look at him intently as well.

“…… Next, I will make a [deal] with you. ”

"What?" Clearly, you didn't understand what he was saying, but since the pet was so serious, you decided to answer him seriously.

"Become a [big shot]. The condition is [hard drive inserted]. "He just said.

…… You don't want to become a so-called big shot. Honestly, it's better to let your mental health issues go early. Thinking this, you can't help but complain, "Of course I'll put the hard drive back as you ask...... But I don't want to be a big shot. I wish you could solve my mental health problems. ”

Spamton was silent for a moment, then couldn't help but stare at you again. He realized that every time he spent time with you, he always made some new discoveries.

"Then I'll find a way to [become your professional psychologist], with the condition of [inserting a hard drive]."

"No deal, I'll do as you requested." You just said that, watching Spamton disappear on the spot, while the hard drive in your hand twisted a few times. It looked like Spamton had stored the data inside. You found it a bit novel, not sure how he managed to do it.

Though curious, you still chose to go and put the hard drive back first. With the experience from last time, you easily arrived at your destination this time. You put the hard drive where Spamton was located back in, but there was no extra reaction. You tilted your head and decided to head back first.

Then, on the way back, a body suddenly appeared before you. You were startled, and when you saw it was Spamton wearing that set of armor, you curiously touched the feathers on the armor before burying your face in it. You never expected that the armor that had just been hanging on the wall that looked like a cold, mechanical piece of armor was now covered in fluffy feathers.

And when your face touches it, it not only feels soft but also carries a warmth that doesn't belong to mechanics. Maybe Spamton is moving at that moment, and the feathers covering it have become warm.

This made Spamton's body stiffen for a moment. He probably hadn't expected that asking him to help solve your psychological problems would be so simple. But his body still had a string hanging from his body, and he was still not free—even though he was rightfully entitled to be called a big shot, what he truly wanted still hadn't been found.

You didn't care much, just hugged Spamton Neo's waist, then buried your face in his body and rubbed back and forth. His tone was a bit muffled, and when it reached his ears, it was still a bit vague, but he still heard your words clearly. After all, in the quiet basement now, there were only the two of you.

"Ah...... So it's Mao Furong. Then I guess you don't need to work hard to become a professional psychologist. You are now. "You seem a bit intoxicated, rubbing your head against it as you speak."

Spamton neo hesitated for a moment as he looked at you, who now seemed insignificant to him, or could be easily restrained by hand. Naturally, a verbal agreement doesn't have to count—after all, it's not some written contract. Moreover...... He not only wants to break the agreement, he also wants something else from you.

Because he knows you are a citizen of light, and he also knows that you probably definitely have a heart-shaped object, or rather, a soul.

So you heard a voice coming from above, belonging to Spamton: "I will become your [Gold Pet Psychological Mentor], on the condition that you have a [heart-shaped object]." His voice even carried a hint of coaxing; he knew how important souls were to you people of light. But maybe having a soul would allow him to achieve his desired goals; perhaps having a soul would mean he would no longer be bound by these strings and manipulated by these strings. Maybe......

But before he could think further, he heard your answer first. You lifted your head and looked up at Spamton Neo, your tone a bit light: "Sure." ”

He was stunned, unable to react for a moment. But for you, this really wasn't much—just handing over control of your life and part of your body to a Dark Commoner. Compared to the dull, day-to-day life and incurable psychological problems, this was nothing.

But you just smiled, your voice a bit soft: "Sure...... As long as you're still my pet, that's enough." You emphasized this, since if he broke the contract again, you wouldn't have much to do.

For a moment, silence fell again. You felt those furry wings encircle you, and your field of vision was pitch black, with only the heart still radiating light—the soul.

Right after, you lose consciousness. You don't know what happened afterward, only that when you wake up groggily, you feel yourself lying on furry wings, and when you slightly lift your head, you see the ceiling of your home and the Spamton Neo sleeping across from you.

Even though your chest seemed a bit empty—you were especially content, simply lying back down and falling asleep again in the furry yet warm embrace.
