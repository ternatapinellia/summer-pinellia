Dark World Bound Spirit 7 (sptx you)

Spamton hesitated as he put away the movie tickets—or rather, the photos. He was the only one in the theater. He walked out in silence. There weren't many Dark People outside, probably because at this hour, no Dark People would come. Spamton was also puzzled why the cinema would have a movie about you, or rather...... It was a memory.

So he walked up to the ticket counter, stood on tiptoe slightly, looked at the color card butler, and asked, "May I ask, is there the [screening logic] for this movie?" As he spoke, he showed the movie ticket in his hand. Strangely enough, the ticket didn't even have a name on it.

The color card butler glanced at the ticket and patiently explained, "This movie ...... Our cinema was generated according to the Queen's film screening requirements, and the movie content comes from the storage and download of the People of Light. The related videos of this movie are stored in the movie-related folders, so the screening of this movie is probably about the lives of the People of Light. ”

Spamton nodded. In fact, he didn't understand the relationship between computer storage and you, but he did know the general principle behind the creation of the Dark People...... So, this might be a clip of audio casually tossed into a computer folder.

He took the movie ticket back, deciding to bring it to you. Although it wasn't too early and most of the Dark People had already gone home, only he went against the flow and returned to the commercial street. Most of the Dark People had left, including his colleagues and a few addisons.

And you're bored sitting on top of a trash can, with no one to play with, so you can only entertain yourself playing Gomoku with the board—playing by yourself, which is quite fun.

When you see Spamton arrive, your eyes light up. You shake the chessboard aside, jump off the trash can, and run toward him, grabbing his hand: "Did you find a new photo?" ”

Without hesitation, Spamton handed you the photo—or rather, a movie ticket, with only the image on the back. Curious, you took the colored ticket, then froze. Instinctively, you covered your head, feeling a slight pain and blurred vision. For a moment, you couldn't process what was happening—maybe it was the same as last time. Spamton instinctively supported you. You leaned against Spamton's chest, his body stiff, his eyes darting left and right, but he didn't know what to do.

Just like the last photo, you've recovered part of your memory, at least from after your high school years...... You seem to have figured it all out, but unfortunately, that jar of origami cranes seems to have disappeared in the end. It seems that after you handed it over that day, no one took the jar, nor did it capture your affection. So the jar fell to the ground and shattered, along with your heart. You finally admitted that that figure, that affection, might just be your illusion.

And now, looking at Spamton standing in front of you and holding you, unconsciously, tears streamed down your face. You clearly knew that now he was truly there; compared to that, you felt more like an illusion.

“…… So, are we lovers, spammy? "You sobbed a few times, and finally asked the question with a trembling voice.

And Spamton can't answer your question. In fact, when he hugs you, he feels his chest pounding, but...... He can't clearly say, what is a lover, and what is love? But if it's this feeling of holding you, if the complex emotions flowing in his heart are called love, then he can't deny you.

So in the end, he just shook his head and nodded, his voice a bit dry: "I ...... [I don't know], [system failure], unable to provide a [stable answer]." "He couldn't give a specific answer, nor did he dare to make any promises to you." In fact, at this very moment, he was actually afraid, but even he couldn't explain what he was afraid of.

You lift your head and silently look at him. Spamton instinctively reaches out to wipe the tears from the corner of your eyes: "Maybe ...... [soon] there will be a [definite answer]." ”

He wouldn't dare to say 'definitely to you' either.

"Yeah, I know. After all, it's just a phantom in my memory...... But still, thank you for helping me find this memory, and thank you for walking with me through that embarrassing time." You let go, let go of that embrace, and smiled at him. You only felt a little bit of loss, just a little bit.

"Speaking of which, you helped me recover two memories. So, do you look any different about me now?" As you spoke, you lifted your skirt and spun around in front of him twice, then looked at Spamton with anticipation.

"Your body...... It no longer looks like a "semi-transparent state," but rather a "physical form." "Spamton carefully observed you for a while before giving you this answer. He doesn't know if touching it will make any difference, since he has been able to touch you from beginning to end."

"Is that so? I wonder if the other Dark People will be able to see me...... If they could see me, wouldn't that be a bit boring? "You sighed and shook your head.

"You...... "Preset behavior?" "Spamton was a bit curious, what are you planning to do?"

“…… Hmm, if they couldn't see me, I could tease them a bit more. But if they did, there would be far fewer ways to tease them. "As you spoke, you shrugged, as if talking about something unrelated to yourself. Only Spamton knew how important this matter was to you.

…… If you are no longer invisible in Cyber City, then you will have more possibilities. You will definitely find ways to recover more memories, and perhaps you will find other dark people to help. What about him? Will his life change as well? If he does nothing, will he just watch you walk away? Spamton felt unwilling to accept this. Spamton didn't know what his future would become, but he knew that if he did nothing, nothing would change.

So this time, looking at you, he didn't hesitate but directly expressed his thoughts: "I think...... If a [People of Light] came to my stall to buy goods, it would cause a huge [public opinion effect]. ”

"So what do you mean by ......?" You followed his question and asked.

"What I mean is, come to my [stall] to buy [related goods], and the appearance of the [People of Light] and [goods procurement] can create a [public opinion effect], maybe even change my [class position]." He took a deep breath and spoke the plan he had just come up with. He really ...... The moment he realized your change, he made this decision. Not just to change his own life, but to deepen his entanglement with you. He hoped that the relationship between him and you would become indistinguishable.

He is not an ordinary addison who has achieved nothing; perhaps all addisons are ambitious dark people, but he is willing to seize the opportunity that appears before him and fight hard for it.

You were momentarily stunned, looking at him in disbelief and honestly...... You didn't expect the People of Light to be so important to the People of Darkness. But Spamton had already helped you a lot, and you had no reason not to help him, so you just smiled and nodded in agreement.

"Alright, then I'll just stroll around your shop tomorrow, buy some things, and leave some dark coins. That should be enough, right?" You confirmed his plan with Spamton again. You didn't mind that he was using your identity as a Light Clan to achieve his own goals, after all...... You also used him to achieve your own goals and recover more memories.

It's hard to say whether love or interests are more important at this moment, but either way, neither of you will give up what benefits you. Anyway...... Your future is already tangled at this moment.

Spamton nodded, agreeing with your point, and added a few more words: "And then...... You don't need to be "deliberately," just "spontaneous." "Actually, it doesn't matter what you buy; as long as public opinion is present, that's enough.

You tilted your head, not knowing why he said that, but still nodded. You couldn't leave the commercial street, so you just propped your chin up on the trash can, watching Spamton's back, watching him gradually walk away.

Then you yawn, casually lean against the wall to rest.

The commercial street grew silent as night fell. When you heard the slowly growing noise on the street, you slowly opened your eyes. You knew a new day had begun, and your spot was a blind spot—none of the Darkfolk could see it. You quietly poked your head out to observe them, curious—well, Spamton had already arrived at his shop early.

You fumbled through the remaining dark coins, tidied yourself up, and felt a bit nervous—after all, this was the first time you'd appeared before everyone since your death. Then, Chao casually walked out of the alley and strolled leisurely down the commercial street. All the Addisons, including the other Dark People on the street, stared at you, the suddenly appearing Lightkin, in shock, while you discreetly avoided the touches of passing Dark Folk to prevent them from discovering that your body could pass through.

You just pretend to be an ordinary customer, looking around the shopping street, then stop precisely in front of Spamton's corner shop, casually pick up a vase and start reading.

You and Spamton exchange an understanding glance. He starts smiling and recommends all sorts of things, including but not limited to antiques and daily necessities in his shop, or just some miscellaneous clothes and accessories. After searching for a while, you finally pick up a pretty goldfish-shaped accessory and put it on your head. Satisfied, you hand Spamton a dark coin, then walk away leisurely.

Other than that, you did nothing, and Spamton did nothing. Yet the passing Dark People all saw this scene and began whispering among themselves.

"A Citizen of Light actually visited his shop!"

"I saw it. The things that Light Tribe picked out look pretty nice. Maybe the other items in his shop are pretty nice too."

"Hmm...... Yeah, that shop is way too tucked away. No wonder I didn't notice it before. I have to go check it out. Are you going or not? ”

"I'm going too. I'm tired of browsing other shops...... I wonder if there's anything new or interesting in his shop. ”

For a moment, the Dark People wandering the streets and other Dark People outside the Addison store all gathered at the entrance of Spamton's store. These Addisons were almost twisted from anger, but could only try to keep smiling and continue shouting, hoping that the customers who had gathered at their door would return.

Meanwhile, the entrance of Spamton's shop was packed with people. He was so busy he barely touched the ground, his smile nearly stiffening. He thought, as expected—having you, a citizen of light, make an appearance is the perfect signboard. Although you don't have a physical presence yet, it's enough to at least pretend to be his customer and attract the attention of others.

He watched his sales keep rising, beaming with joy, his smile growing even more.

For several days in a row, Spamton's shop was in this state. Besides the initial Dark People, there were also those who came to join in the celebration after hearing about the Visit of the Light People. During this time, you saw Spamton seem to have answered a phone call. You don't know what they said, but all you know is that Spamton looks very happy. In the following days, the number of Dark People outside the Spamton shop increases, even forming long queues.

You know Spamton is happy. You watch his originally worn-out black short-sleeve shirt change into a refined black suit, and you see several other Addisons staring discontentedly in the direction of Spamton's store. Of course, you're happy for him—after all, you want your friend to be well.

You only have a little, just a little bit of frustration. You really want to talk a few more words with your friends; you miss them a little, and you want to ...... to ask them to reclaim more of their memories, but you watch him smiling and busy with his feet, while you just can't bring yourself to say the words.

Finally, that afternoon, you noticed there weren't many customers at the entrance of his shop. You tidied yourself up, found a spot, and stepped out. Your appearance as a Light Clan immediately drew the attention of other Dark Clowns, while you quietly lined up at the back of the line at Spamton's entrance.

The Dark People ahead were all very fast, probably knowing that you, the Citizen of Light, were there, and there were no other Dark Folk lining up behind you. So, you soon found yourself standing in front of Addison, now dressed in a brand-new black suit, a pure white figure.

He seemed a bit surprised to see you, stammering for a long time without getting the words to go, while you quietly took out a few dark coins and handed them to him, your voice sincere: "These are ...... payment. I hope you can help me find more photos, which are my memories. ”

Spamton was a bit surprised. He pushed your money back and said, "No [in-kind reward] is needed! You and I are already [Friend Added], and I'll provide [free support]. "At this point, he did feel a bit guilty. Although the time to leave after entering the photo doesn't change, there is still time to be spent searching for the photo."

And...... Indeed, he forgot to help you find photos. He's been so busy lately that he almost forgot about you. All he can do is think his sales are rising, and his views are increasing accordingly. He thinks that as long as things keep going like this, he'll eventually become the big shot he once was, just bold and ambitious. But...... But after that, he forgot about you. He couldn't help but feel a bit guilty.

You just shook your head, persisted, and stuffed those few dark coins into Spamton's hand: "Not a reward...... Or you can treat it as a commission fee. Anyway, I hope you'll accept it, because this is a transaction. "You said seriously.

Spamton opened his mouth, his expression complicated. He really didn't know what to do, but inexplicably, he had a feeling that if he accepted these dark coins today, if he treated everything between you as mere transactions, then the friendship he had only recently gained would be broken like this.

So he insisted and returned those few dark coins to you, his expression serious: "I won't charge any [commission fees] or [commission fees]. I will provide you with [free assistance] because you and I are already [added as friends], and I [care deeply] about you. This is sincere." Spamton held your hand, staring at you. You were stunned for a moment, but in the end, you put those dark coins back, speaking with a hint of complaint.

"What? I thought you'd completely forgotten me, so I wanted to use the dark coin to commission you with a task. I think you'll definitely accept it—but turns out you still remember me." You started making sarcastic remarks with a cheerful smile.

Spamton coughed dryly a few times, his gaze shifting slightly: "Yes, 'Never Forget.' I won't charge you any 'silver dollars.'" He said solemnly. Though he had been afraid of the promise before, at this moment, he could say it with ease. He thought maybe it was because he now had the confidence to do so. At the very least, he was no longer the useless Dark Folk he once was.

"Really? That's great. So I can take whatever stuff from your shop as you like?" You tilted your head slightly and began to look over the items in the shop. Although you weren't very interested in these things, some ...... Indeed, they really look pretty good. Maybe I could buy them myself and admire them properly. At worst, I could buy them and resell them to Spamton. You thought somewhat indifferently.

Spamton hesitated for a moment, then realized he shouldn't, so he quickly nodded at you: "Yes, you can [take it freely]." ”

He has already taken a chance because of you—he thinks, he really needs to repay you.
