Dark World Bound Spirit 1 (sptx You)

You sat boredly on a trash can, swinging your legs. This was a remote alley, usually no one passed by, and as for you...... You couldn't remember what was going on. Or rather, you didn't know who you really were or why you were in this place. You only remembered that you seemed to have been sitting there for many days. For you, every day was just looking at that old advertisement posted on the wall across the street, with that blue ...... It seemed to be someone called the Queen, and you could recite the words written on that advertisement backwards.

In short, you only know you sit in the same place every day. You seem unable to get far from this place, because you have searched the entire commercial street, trying to find an exit, but...... You reach the edge but can only touch the wall of air. You cannot, nor can you leave this place. It seems you absolutely cannot leave this place...... This is what your intuition tells you.

…… You don't know what's the point of staying here forever. People on the commercial street can hardly see you. Sometimes you just walk by and sneak away things just thrown away by those addisons—sometimes unfinished barbecue, sometimes ice cream, sometimes ...... Some clothes that can't be sold and look cheaply made.

You enjoy it because no one here can see you; sometimes you swagger past them, watching these addisons, spitting, sweating, and introducing products to others.

You don't quite understand why you don't look much like these people. You also don't understand what you really are.

Most of the time, you sit on a trash can, lost in thought, until you see an Addison you've never seen come to the shopping street. He looks shorter than the other Addisons, and not only that, he's pure white—or rather—a bit translucent. Just looking at him makes you feel a bit closer, since you've even looked at yourself in the puddle during rain—otherwise, how would you know what you look like? In your impression, you seem semi-transparent too, a shadowless person.

So for once, you got a bit more interested, resting your chin on your hand and watching the shopping street, wanting to see what this new Addison would do. But...... Just a few minutes later, that guy who seemed to be called Pink disdainfully poured the coffee in his hand onto White's head. The originally mixed white and semi-transparent color turned coffee, startling you, who quickly jumped out of the trash can.

To be honest, this is the first time you've encountered something like this. Although you don't understand what it is, subconsciously you feel you deeply hate it all. Even if you don't know what it is, looking at the confusion and fear on that white Addison's face, you know this must be something bad. It's uncomfortable, so you have to find a way to stop it.

Before you could figure out what you should do, you saw Pink grab White's collar and raise his fist, clearly trying to vent his anger by treating his subordinate Addison like a human punching bag. Startled, you crawled over and put Orange's clothes on your head, picked up a pot of freshly brewed tea Pink had just brewed in the shop, and prepared to go play tricks and get back at Pink. Anyway, they can't see you, so you can pretend it's haunted. The pot isn't very hot, so pouring cold on Pink's head is just right to refresh and wake him up!

You planned all this, but when you turned around, you saw Pink had already slapped White. His originally white face now had a red handprint, and Pink was raising his hand, seemingly wanting to give him another blow. You quickly propped up the blue skirt and slipped it between the two Addisons. From Pink's perspective, it looked like a blue dress had flown over and blocked his view. Then, you tried hard to stand on tiptoe and poured the pot of tea at Pink.

He let out a sharp crackling sound, running back to his shop with the tea leaves on his head, and even snatched the teapot and the blue skirt away. You seemed to hear Pink and Orange arguing, apparently talking about the blue skirt. Then you watched as Pink reluctantly pulled out a dark coin and gave it to the smug-faced Orange.

You breathe a sigh of relief and plan to go back to the trash can and sit there, doing good deeds without leaving a name, especially since they can't even see you.

Just as you were about to leave, a timid voice rang out: "[Honored Miss], thank you for your generous offer. May I know your name?" ”

He seemed a bit incoherent, speaking anxiously, with a crackling sound of electricity. Even so, you could still understand what he was saying. Because of this, you turned your head in surprise to look at the little Addison in front of you. It was the first time you realized someone could see you.

So you instinctively pointed to yourself: "Are you talking about me?" "You also glanced around. Well, it seemed like there was no one else around here besides you. Probably because Pink was causing trouble here earlier, most people had dispersed."

He nodded without hesitation: "Honored Miss, that's you." ”

"I...... I don't have a name. Ah, I can't say that, I don't know what my name is. "You scratched your head, feeling a bit curious. This was the first time someone had ever seen you and talked to you. To be honest, you were pretty excited.

So you couldn't help but circle around him several times: "By the way, what's your name? Do you know those Addisons? Why are they bullying you? Are you unable to beat them? Why don't they fight back?" And then...... And if you want to open a shop on this commercial street, I can be your bodyguard! Look, I was just trying to scare him earlier, and he immediately ran off because of me. I'm very useful! ”

He looked like he was startled by you, his body stiff, and his expression very tense, but he still seriously answered your questions one by one: 'My [only title] is SPAMTON G.SPAMTON! I... am also a addison, and I have a [dwelling] similar to theirs. Probably because my [sales are zero] and... [body issues]? My [strength is unmatched], and I once tried [self-defense], but only ended up with [double losses]. Lastly... thank you, miss, for your [kind intentions]! But I cannot [award a salary], I... [have zero sales], [no one is interested].' He spoke very quickly as he explained to you, perhaps his education level wasn’t very high? Although it was a bit hard to understand, you could barely grasp what he was saying, though it left you a bit stunned.

After saying these words, he took a few deep breaths, his face flushed from holding back. It seemed that saying those words just now was quite difficult for him.

You quickly shook your head and waved your hand: "I don't need a salary! Even if you pay me, I can't spend it...... As you can see, after all, now only you can see me. Maybe I'm just a ghost!" After saying that, you laughed heartlessly.

Actually, you also want to know who you really are, you want to leave this place, you don't want to stay here forever. Actually, when you say this, your mood feels inexplicably heavy. You just instinctively let yourself smile carefreely.

Spamton looks at you, opens his mouth, hesitates, and finally instinctively reaches out to grab yours. And you widen your eyes because as he moves, you notice your hand slowly turning into a physical form.

You were so scared you pulled your hand back, and then it became semi-transparent again. You breathed a sigh of relief and patted your chest: "You scared me to death. I thought I had turned into a human." "Once you become human, it's not easy to grab things or run around freely. You have to find a place to live, a place to sleep, find something to eat, and spend money to buy clothes."

…… You definitely don't want to live like that, and besides, you don't know anything yet—how are you supposed to go out and find a job!

Spamton slightly raised his head to look at you, seeming somewhat puzzled: "I don't understand, [materialization] has many benefits." "He wanted to repay you, but didn't know how, which is why he said such things."

But you just shook your head seriously: "You don't get it, being a ghost is so much easier! If it materializes, then I'd have to think about a lot every day like you, you see." You pursed your lips and pointed toward Pink and Yellow walking toward you.

Pink probably had already snapped out of his earlier shock. He looked at Spamton and laughed, "Look at you. I told you, poor little rascal, you can't accomplish anything big. You still want to be a big shot, but now you're so crazy you're talking to thin air." ”

Spamton's eyes widened instinctively. He glanced at you, standing nearby with an innocent expression but a bit guilty, then at Pink standing in front of him, took a deep breath, and said, "I'm not in a 'mad state'; she is a real [Dark People]. ”

Yellow laughed too: "There's no other Dark Folk here, just the three of us standing here. Spamton, you're not just a drunken barbecue, are you? Why don't you go smell it in the trash heap and sober up?" ”

Spamton understood. Although he didn't know why, only he could see you; the other Dark People probably couldn't see you.

He took a deep breath and grabbed your hand again. You were watching the show and thinking about how to get these two Addisons to go, only to notice your hand materializing once more.

You blinked, and the hand you touched appeared in the air—for those two Addisons, it was a ghost hand suddenly appearing in the air.

Spamton just blinked innocently: "Hmm...... I said the person beside me is [real] and the evidence is conclusive." "As he spoke, he raised that hand.

Pink's expression stiffened. He poked yellow with his hand, his voice trembling slightly: "See, I told you there must be some hauntings next to Spamton, and you still don't believe me? You insist on having me confront you together." ”

Yellow didn't dare either: "What are you talking about, pink?" Wasn't it just now that you said you were upset by Spamton's counterattack and insisted on coming over to help you find a scene? Now I'm getting shot right in the middle, right? ”

You hesitated for a moment, saw the two Addisons arguing so fiercely, and decided to add fuel to the fire. So you leaned in and pressed your cheek against Spamton's. His face was icy cold, so much so that you, the cold ghost, couldn't help but shiver. Then you smiled and said to them, "Nice to meet them." ”

In their eyes, it was just a person with only half a face and a hand greeting them. Pink's teeth chattered, but he still dragged Yellow back and returned in defeat.

You found it interesting, turned to look at Spamton, and for some reason his cheeks flushed: "Do you get bullied by them all the time? Don't worry, as long as you're here on the shopping street, I won't let you get bullied!" You patted your chest as you spoke.

"What about the [Exploration Area] outside the [Shopping Street]?" He knew he shouldn't have that much curiosity, but he couldn't help but ask.

You pretended to be troubled, resting your chin on your hand and thinking for a moment: "Hmm...... But I've tried, I can't leave the commercial street. I remember...... This should be called an Earthbound Spirit? "You're not really sure, because you don't have much memory. Just now, the word 'Earthbound Spirit' just happened to pop into your mind.

"Speaking of which, what exactly are the People of Darkness? Aren't we all human?" As you spoke, you poked Spamton's cheek, a bit curious. After all...... You really don't know what this is. Ever since you woke up, you've been stuck here.

"...... [Honored Miss], don't you know about the [People of Darkness]?" He was also swept off the topic by you, looking at you in disbelief.

You stick out your tongue, a little embarrassed: "I really don't know...... I have no idea what kind of place this is, who am I, why am I here, or what do I need to do? ”

As you spoke, you reached out your finger again, poking his soft, slightly fluffy face again and again. Your fingers also flashed again and again, which was quite amusing.

Spamton hesitated for a moment, then helplessly grabbed your hand and held it in his hand. Then you reached out with your other hand to poke him, and his other hand grabbed yours too. Now you were executed on the spot, puffing out your cheeks and looking at Spamton.

He cleared his throat. Although he felt a bit guilty, he pretended not to notice your angry expression and continued to himself, "Ahem...... The [People of Darkness] are objects that reflect the existence of [Light] in the midst of [extreme darkness]. The [People of Light] are ordinary beings that exist in the [Artificial World]. This world is the [Dark World], called the [Cyber World], a [world] constructed by the ruler [Queen]. This place is part of the [Cyber World]. ”

You listen carefully to what he finishes, and after Spamton's finish, his eyes sparkle as he looks at you. You smack your lips, trying hard to understand what he means, so you respond with a hum.

He replied with a 'hmm', still staring at you with sparkling eyes, eagerly awaiting your next answer.

"I didn't understand." You honestly shook your head. Honestly, this passage is a bit abstract, and it's normal for you not to understand.

Spamton sighed, his head drooping, looking quite disappointed. You couldn't help but break free from your hand, went up to pat his head, and after racking your brain, said, "I really didn't understand...... But I'm not saying you said it badly! It's just that I really haven't seen those so-called Lightfolk either......

"The People of Light are the objects of the [People of Darkness]'s pursuit, the users of the [Avatars of the Dark People], and the monsters and humans of [flesh and blood.'" Spamton tried to break down the meaning and explain it to you again.

You scratched your head, still a bit confused: "Are the bodies of the Dark People different?" ”

He nodded: "The [Dark People] do not have [true flesh and blood]. ”

You don't know exactly what the body structure of the Dark People looks like, and you can't have Spamton cut off a piece for you to study now, so you have to awkwardly change the subject.

"Then do you think I'm a Dark Clan or a Clan of Light?"

This time, Spamton sized you up for a long time, looking at you from head to toe. You cleared your throat twice, feeling a bit embarrassed by the stares.

“…… You should be the [People of Darkness]; you don't have [Unique Light] or [Heart-shaped Objects]. But you are an unprecedented [Human Type] of the [People of Darkness], only among the [People of Light] are humans." He said, frowning, clearly unable to figure out what exactly you should be regarded as.

You cough twice: "So you don't know?" ”

He nodded honestly, then asked, "[Honored Miss], you don't have any [reference answer] at all?" ”

“…… I don't know. I've always stayed here. I thought...... I'm just like those others—no, the Dark People." "You slumped your shoulders in despair, and now you have no idea who you really are.

"Not a single [clues]?" Spamton asked again. He didn't think that for a person, they would have no status at all.

You scratched your head: "Ah...... As for clues, I think there's a bit of it." "By now, you've gotten used to Spamton's slightly odd way of speaking—you're quite accustomed to it." You fumbled around and pulled out a black-and-white photo from your pocket. It was your profile picture—black and white, or maybe a memorial portrait to be more accurate.

"When I woke up, this was by my side, and only this was left. It was just a photo......" As you spoke, your voice quieted, since there were no other clues besides that.

Your face is printed on the photo, expressionless, and the black-and-white portrait leaves only a trace of sorrow.

“…… Maybe this represents a [death declaration]." Spamton said. After all, the black-and-white photo ...... still looks like this kind of expression, looking like a memorial portrait.

He reached out to take the photo and take a look, but just as he did, a black-and-white vortex appeared from the photo. Spamton was powerless to resist, feeling dizzy and swept into the vortex. You stared blankly at the scene, and only a blank photo fell from the spot. You didn't know what had happened. You just nervously picked up the photo and sat dazed on top of the trash can, staring blankly at the photo.
