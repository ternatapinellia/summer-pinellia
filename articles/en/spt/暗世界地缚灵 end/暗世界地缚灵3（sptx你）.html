Dark World Bound Spirit 3 (sptx You)

You blinked, looking at Spamton's confused face, then looked at the photo: "Then let's take it and study it." As you spoke, you took the photo from Spamton, but as soon as you took it, you frowned, covered your head, pressed your temples, and for some reason, you felt dizzy and had a headache, and your vision turned gray.

In Spamton's eyes, it's like you suddenly freeze after taking the photo, then your face turns pale—of course, for a ghost, no matter how pale it is, it's not all that pale. But you stumble and fall from the trash can. Spamton panics, rushes over to catch you, and the ghost starts to feel light at first, but as soon as it touches Spamton, it begins to take shape. Your heavy weight presses down on Spamton, and he tries to catch you, but you pin him to the ground as well. Still, he successfully acts as a cushion beneath you.

Your body was fully materialized, exactly like in the photo. The falling black hair brushed against Spamton's face, tickling a little. You truly felt a bit of pain. Only after realizing you were pressing on Spamton did you hurriedly get up, propping up your arms and looking down at Spamton. At this moment, your body returned to a semi-transparent state. For some reason, Spamton actually felt a bit regretful, but he swallowed hard and just instinctively called your name.

“…… Y/N……”

And you cheerfully chimed in: "Yes, I just remembered and saw that my name is this!" So I'm not someone without a name—ah, sorry, did I hurt you when I pressed down just now? I'm standing up now. "As you spoke, you quickly returned to floating there,

"After touching this photo again, I actually remembered my own name! And the memory I just recovered actually includes your presence. It's really amazing—ah, so you really care about me?" "At first, you were a bit excited because you had recovered some memories, but when you said the last sentence, you felt a bit uneasy. You used to ...... probably didn't know each other, so you know that in the memories that suddenly flashed through your mind, those words he said might have been just words to comfort you. Even if they were fake, you wouldn't be too surprised.

You will just feel a little bit of loss, just a little.

Spamton was stunned for a moment, then hurriedly waved his hand: "No [pain signals]—no need for [extra apologies], I just need to [thank you], and ...... [genuine and solid evidence]." ”

You listened in a daze, touched your chin, and nodded to show you understood: "Okay, but I still have to apologize to you. After all, it's because of me...... You fell to the ground. You actually don't need to catch me. After all, a ghost falling to the ground doesn't feel anything. "You smiled, bent down slightly, and looked at Spamton, who was sitting up straight. Unable to resist, you reached out and poked his face. Your hand flickered with each touch.

"...... the [body] doesn't have [pain perception], and the [heart-shaped object] doesn't have it either?" Spamton said somewhat gloomily.

You paused. Honestly, you didn't understand what the heart-shaped object he meant. Was it your heart?

So you asked, "Of course my body doesn't hurt, but what the hell is that heart-shaped object...... My heart? ”

Spamton only nodded slightly, then began shaking his head vigorously. He hesitated and said, "Your ...... [Perception System]?" ”

"Oh...... You mean, my soul? "You guess. If that's the case, it should be something that isn't material, but something that exists in consciousness.

This time, Spamton nodded vigorously, agreeing with your point.

You think for a moment, then just nod and shake your head: "Of course the body doesn't hurt, and neither does the soul, after all, I'm already used to it......" But why do you feel used to it? Why is that? You rarely have a question, but you just can't think of an answer.

“…… Don't get used to [pain perception]; [heart-shaped objects] might also be [damaged and degenerate]. "Spamton stood up, seriously shaking your shoulders, and raised both hands in a surrender gesture.

"I know, I know—alright, I can't just endure pain and say nothing. If I don't fight back at all and let others slaughter me, then what's that even then?" "You say you know, since he's been shaking your shoulders the whole time, and even ghosts still feel a bit dizzy.

But now that you're talking about it, you start to remember a bit and hurriedly ask, "But...... In my memory, you fell with me, it must have hurt a lot, sorry...... You looked at him with a hint of guilt. Knowing he truly cares about you, you're happy, but at the same time, you also feel guilty. You don't want to drag anyone else's ...... into it. Ah, wait, that's not a person—he's just a Dark Citizen.

Spamton just shook his head: "I had already [detached my memories] before, so it wasn't affected." ”

You patted your chest and finally let out a sigh: "It's okay, it's fine. If it hurts that much too, I'd be heartbroken......" You muttered softly, but Spamton didn't catch it. He couldn't help but want you to repeat it, but you just crossed your hands and made a big numbness sign and refused his suggestion.

"It's nothing important, why repeat it again......" you said this, your expression not very natural. Spamton slightly lifted his head to look at you, frowning as well. He didn't think it was a trivial matter.

He just tugged at the hem of your clothes and started playing the victim: "...... But I want to know, Y/N, can't you really [tell me a little]?" As he spoke, he slightly lifted his head to look at you, showing a slightly pitiful expression. You looked at him, opened your mouth for a moment, then closed it again. Honestly, because of Spamton's expression at that moment, you felt an inexplicable pang of guilt, but then you hardened your heart.

"No, no!" "I've already said it's not important, and if I don't want to talk, it means it's my privacy. You can't ask further, understand?" As you spoke, you poked Spamton's long nose. He covered his nose and instinctively took two steps back. Seeing him like that, you couldn't help but burst out laughing.

"Alright, anyway...... Thank you so much for helping me recover a piece of my memory. "You said softly.

Even though those were memories from before you died, and even though all that remained for you was sadness and helplessness, in that memory you saw Spamton, you heard his words, and you chose to believe him. At least, you realized that your existence still had some meaning, some necessity, and at least a small Dark Folk was watching over you.

Your voice was light and floaty: "Thank you too...... So you really care about me. But I don't understand—why? We just met today, so we're not that familiar. ”

Spamton was taken aback. He thought about the question for a moment, hesitated for a moment, and then answered you: "Because you are my [savior], and you are [full of kindness], I...... I want your [happy ending]. ”

As he spoke, he swallowed hard. Saying such things made him a little nervous, his chest seemingly pounding nonstop, while he just lowered his head, not daring to look up at you for a moment, his voice muffled: "I...... I think you're worth it, your [Heart-shaped Object] is worth it. ”

You paused, then sat back down on the trash can, looking down at Spamton. Actually, when you first asked why, you didn't expect him to give you a satisfactory answer, but...... But his answer made tears wellup in your eyes for no reason. You don't know why, but at that moment, you actually felt like crying. You felt it shouldn't be right, it seemed a bit weak, and you wanted to hold back your tears, but you didn't know why.

In the end, you only had two lines of tears on your cheeks, and nodded blankly: "I understand...... Thank you, thank you. ”

You didn't move; instead, that white, slightly furry hand reached out, gently touching you and wiping away the tears that had flowed down your face. Only then did you realize that these tears were not those of a ghost that no one cared about nor could see, but of the weakness and tears of the soul. At that moment, you were being treated with great care as a human.

“…… No, always switch to [crying mode]. "Spamton stammered, a little gloomily said. He doesn't really want to see you cry; you should always be smiling joyfully.

"I won't always be like this—but you, since you say so, you shouldn't keep crying. I saw you standing in the shop crying secretly because you couldn't sell them." You started pointing and whispering seriously, your legs swinging back and forth. To be honest, when you're stuck on the commercial street, you occasionally pick up dark coins or similar items left behind by the Dark People. After a while, you still have quite a few dark coins on you, but buying things is useless to you. So even though you watch them open their shop and this pure white Addison crying, you still haven't taken any action. And now, you feel a bit regretful.

Anyway, buying things doesn't really help you; you can buy anything you want...... You grumbled in your heart, thinking that you could just grab some things and toss the dark coins down. All you wanted was to see a smile on his face.

"I ......," Spamton scratched his face a little embarrassedly. When he was hiding there and secretly crying, he thought no one would notice, but unexpectedly, you did. Well, before this, no one knew there was a ghost on the commercial street—a groundbound spirit trapped here.

"Stop talking about you, huh? You still need to go back to your business, right? I'll go to your shop to get some stuff and leave you some dark coins to support the event." "You made the decision so happily with a wave of your hand, not caring whether Spamton agreed or not. Anyway, you said you would do it—who could stop a ghost like you?"

Spamton could only sigh helplessly. Calculating the time, it was about time for the shop to open in the afternoon, so he had to go back. But before leaving, he still turned around and said seriously, "I don't know the [pain index] of jumping from a building...... But you have the [autonomy] to cry. ”

You were stunned for a moment. Although you were still smiling, tears quietly fell down. You lifted the corners of your mouth and nodded heavily, your voice a bit muffled: "Mm." ”

You watch his back, watch him open the shop door, display some old, unremarkable items, and then shout out loudly, hoping even one of the Darkfolk will stop and buy his stuff.

But...... Not a single Dark Citizen stopped. They simply took what they wanted from other shops, paid the bill, and then left, not lingering even a single step in front of Spamton's shop, nor did they glance at the white Addison shop.

Spamton reluctantly withdrew his hand, then opened several large pop-ups, trying hard to attract attention. But other addisons did exactly that, because all addisons were trying to attract customers.

You pursed your lips, wondering whether to go over at this moment, but then noticed Addison walking over. It was Blue. He seemed to have handed Spamton a cup of tea. Spamton took it, nodded repeatedly, and then slowly began to drink his tea.

To be honest, you ...... You originally thought Blue was just looking for trouble, so you could only reluctantly withdraw your eager hand and continue observing his shop.

He sat at the shop entrance, sipping that cup of tea. For the moment, he probably didn't want to shout. Of course, his shop was also in a remote area, so...... With no more shouts, no Dark People would come to his shop door. You hesitated for a moment, but still grabbed the dark coins you had and got up, slowly walking toward his shop.

Spamton clearly noticed your actions too. He stopped drinking his tea and waved slightly at you, as if wanting to say something. But before he could speak, Pink, who had been staring at the nearby shop for a long time, walked over again, looking arrogantly at Spamton, and said arrogantly, "Oh, are you saying hello to some ghost friend of yours?" "I mean, you can't even sell your stuff. You're really embarrassing us addisons. If you ask me, you'd better pair up with your ghost friend. Just picking up trash every day is enough for you to survive." His words carried a hint of schadenfreude. When you heard that, your fists clenched and you aimed straight at Pink. Unfortunately, pink couldn't touch you; your hand passed right through Pink's body. He knew nothing, only Spamton saw it all. He covered his mouth, but in the end, he accidentally laughed.

Pink was a bit dissatisfied and confused, and couldn't help but want to kick him: "What are you laughing at? Do you think I'm funny, or do you think what I say is funny?" ”

You rush to the front of Spamton, holding a vase to block Pink's way. From Pink's perspective, it's just a vase mysteriously floating in midair, exactly like in the morning, as if haunted.

He shivered, his voice starting to tremble: "Let me tell you, I saw this thing this morning. Stop trying to scare me with tricks......" But before he finished speaking, you searched Spamton's stall for anything else and happened to find a dusty rag. You directly covered his face, and Pink, still barely silent, swallowed a mouthful of dust. He stammered, his face flushed, and took the rag off. But since he was already pink, his face was still flushed, but it wasn't very obvious.

Pink just snapped angrily, "What exactly did you do?! How did this dirty rag end up in my face? Apologize to me right now! ”

Spamton watched the whole thing. He didn't stop your actions, since he had long wanted to get back at these addisons. Now Pink angrily demanded an explanation, and only then did Spamton, innocently and somewhat pitifully, say, "I don't have [evidence of my actions]...... Other 【Dark People】 can [provide proof], like Blue. "As he spoke, he tilted his head back, still squinting. You could vaguely see a few tears, and you couldn't help but marvel. In fact, you don't think Spamton is that wronged or sad...... But at this moment, he still showed it.

So you simply held your vase and watched from the side. You saw blue rushing over, and then separating pink, who wanted to go up and punch spamton a few times, from pink, who was still sitting motionless in his original spot, and started playing the peacemaker: 'I saw everything, pink, you were the first to make a move, spammy just sat there and did nothing.'

"Then how do you explain the rag that fell on my face?" Pink pointed at the rag that had fallen on the floor, angrily lowering his voice into a low voice.

You can't help but start biting your nails, and for a moment, you suddenly realize that his nickname is spammy.

Blue got stuck for a moment, glanced at Pink, then at Spamton's innocent face, and started rambling: "Well...... It was probably just a sudden gust of wind blowing by, lifting that rag, and it just happened to fall on your face. ”

Pink rolled his eyes: "How could something that just happen? Besides, there was clearly no wind just now. Where would the wind come from in the Dark World?" ”

“…… Maybe it's because the weather is hot, and Yellow's shop has an electric hair dryer." Spamton said. He started to shift the traction, since Yellow's shop wasn't far from him.

You casually browse the shop where there are no Dark People guarding the time, then grab a piece of watermelon and sneak behind Spamton, watching the show while eating the melon. Spamton subtly shifts his seat, trying not to let them see the watermelon floating in the air.

Pink glared at Spamton with anger and walked back to his shop, while Blue sighed, picked up the rag, and placed it on Spamton's stall, then walked back to his shop as well.
