Otherworld Fantasy Drifting [Part 2] (sptx you)

Spamton was stunned for a moment. To be honest, he had no idea what a hunter was, but seeing you eager to attack and ready to attack, he knew it wasn't a good word, and just from the literal meaning, he could tell what it was!

In short...... Even if he were him, he had to say no. After all, he didn't want to fight you for no reason, especially since he wasn't even that person! He had lived in the Dark World for so long and had never heard of that name!

He's just an ordinary Dark Commoner—to be honest, when facing you, Spamton suddenly realized he was actually quite ordinary.

"[People of Light], stop [Combat Mode], I'm not the [Hunter] you mentioned!" He could only try hard to control this unruly body to dodge your attacks, shouting loudly, afraid you wouldn't hear clearly. Of course, his voice was anxious—he wanted to end the fight as soon as possible.

"You say it's not true, so it's not? A mentally ill person would still say he's not mentally ill! Since you denied it, then you must be the hunter!" "You shouted loudly and easily handed down the verdict. Although you know your words sound a bit unreasonable, it doesn't matter—victory in this battle is more important than that! Anyway, as long as you defeat him, no one will remember your conversation anymore!

Spamton gritted his teeth. He really didn't know how to answer you. Saying he wasn't a hunter would make you swear he was a hunter, but he couldn't admit he was!

However...... His gaze wandered as he saw the yellow heart, or rather, the soul, flickering and beating in your chest. He wanted to obtain it, so he could truly become a big shot—if it was to hunt souls, then he could indeed accept the title of Hunter.

“…… Yes, I am a [Hunter], and I am also an ordinary [Dark Folk]. So, [Light Folk]—please hand over your [Heart-shaped Object]." He said this, swinging his arms and legs as he approached you, raising his arm again. The laser cannon gathered light again. You weren't afraid of this at all; you simply used Kukukan to stack a defensive enhancement effect again, then raised your arm, a bright yellow light gathered, and then struck down heavily—a massive tear-point attribute damage. Spamton's movement speed was just too slow now, unable to dodge the attack. Moreover, metal conducts electricity, so the lightning kept circulating around him. His entire ...... The Dark Folks twitched in midair. You let go of your hand, looking a bit helpless at the figure before you.

You automatically understood what he said—he said he was just an ordinary shadow—how could a hunter be ordinary at all! In short, you attacked him with all your might. Now it seems the effect was too good. Although you couldn't see his health bar, you could tell it was probably decreasing. After all, he was still trembling, white and yellow glows bursting from his body, and the whole big bird was bristling with feathers.

You frowned and looked carefully for a while. You remember the seniors always say hunters are very hard to beat...... Why does this look so easy? He looks like he's about to be killed by you.

Unfortunately, this was a turn-based battle, and Spamton's barrage of bullets flew toward you again, surrounding you. The white and blue barrage attacks were about to land, but with the Persona Mask, you were confident and opened a rain shield, absorbing the damage from this time.

But...... Spamton once again moved away from you, then folded his wings to block in front of you. You tentatively used the same attack. The massive lightning striked, but seemed to cause no damage at all. He looked perfectly fine, except for a triumphant laugh.

"[Hahahaha] [Laughing Mode], I've activated [Omni-Defensive Function], so you can't make a [perfect attack]." He said this, and the laser cannon started charging again. This time, the laser hit where you had been standing, and you jumped a few steps to the side.

Indeed, just like he said, you can only glare at him with hatred, wondering if this damn defense is maxed out—how come it didn't deal any damage at all?

“…… At worst, just stack a few more buffs. I don't believe it's impossible!" You figured you had to succeed today, so you started frantically stacking witch curses on Spamton, then had lightning strike down, trying to achieve a nuclear explosion. Unfortunately, you didn't succeed. Even if you struck precisely, it didn't seem to hurt him at all, making you almost lose heart—maybe you wanted to die, since you were really tired. I don't know why Spamton still looked so radiant.

You took two breaths, wiped the sweat from your forehead, and said, "...... Is your defense mode really unsolvable?" You asked sincerely, sincerely questioning, since you two were at a stalemate now, and chatting idly wouldn't be enough.

"Of course, this is [Universal Defense], [Super High School Level.] "Somehow, you can hear a hint of pride in his words. Well, you don't really know what he's so proud of—maybe he's so proud that you can't beat him no matter what, while he's slowly wearing down your fighting spirit.

"I don't believe it. There must be a breakthrough. Isn't so-and-so supposed to be invincible? But his ankle is his weakness...... You must have a weakness too!" You wiped your sweat again, stopped attacking, and instead stood where you were to observe Spamton. Anyway, you're in turn-based now; if you don't move, he can't move.

When fighting Spamton, you find he always tries to get close to you, and his gaze is always intentionally or unintentionally directed at your chest, but you see nothing but a flat stretch. You suspect he might be concerned about something you can't even see, since your heart is still in his chest. But no matter what, you can't figure out what he's looking at—what's inside...... Surely he can't just make his inner will manifest, right? That makes you find it a bit funny.

If you carefully observe the structure of the Spamton Neo, his body is covered in armor. It seems this metal armor gives him extremely high defense. In other words, if he could be removed from the armor, it would probably be easy to win—but this seems a bit difficult. For now, his head is firmly attached to this armor, as if he was born naturally. Unless his head is cut off—that should be even harder, right?

Those wings are also used to boost defense; if you take out the wings first, it should also reduce his defense...... But the lightning he just struck down was useless. But you still plan to give it a try and attack in chunks.

So you raise your hand again, lightning gathers in the air, but this time you don't strike the Spamton directly, but rather the individual wing that strikes.

The rumbling thunder flashed by, and your eyes lit up. It really worked. Although the damage was still halved, you saw the wing's health bar—it had just been destroyed by one-fifth!

Spamton was stunned. He didn't know you could switch to such an attack style, so he quickly used his other wing to block it, trying to weaken the attack, shouting loudly, "You...... There's no [attack operation manual]! You shouldn't be able to perform a solo attack! ”

"So what? As long as it works, it's a good solution. Are you jealous of me? Because you can't do it?" You raised your eyebrows smugly, dodging the barrage of barrage of comments scattered at you like dollar signs.

Spamton was about to be driven mad by you: "I don't have the [Envy Program]!" ”

You don't care about that. Since he blocked with his other wing, you might as well choose the other wing as your target, also losing about one-fifth of his health.

You decided to keep pushing forward, but...... You heard footsteps behind you. You instinctively became nervous; after all, you didn't want to be surrounded from front and rear, nor did you want others to snatch the hunter you had painstakingly fought halfway through. So you hurriedly turned around and glanced at what seemed to be a human like you rushing over. You couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief—at least you wouldn't be caught in a pincer attack.

Then you use Kukukan to refresh Spamton's two Archangel's Calls, and his health bar instantly fills up again. You'd rather start over later than let someone else steal your kill!

Then you strolled away, quietly heading to another place, because you wanted to see ...... What other way could you deal with this troublesome guy?

Hmm, you see that blue little guy holding a sword standing in front of Spamton. Spamton was a bit surprised—you gave him two healing skills and then ran away, but he couldn't care about that now. After all, Kris was the real prey he'd set his sights on from the start. He was confident he could snatch the heart-shaped item from him. After all, without Kris's help, he wouldn't have gotten this NEO set now.

So, the dull and tedious battle began once again. Kris silently attacked Spamton, and after losing half his health, Spamton reactivated his defensive mode. No matter how he attacked, he couldn't break through or harm him.

For some reason, you feel uneasy, after all—you hear that little one calling someone's name, but no one has come. But you keep feeling that, in the end, what he calls over might be a powerful weapon.

As you just saw, it was because of those green threads that the armor could be manipulated according to Spamton's will. It looked ...... Actually, even after he got that armor, he was still being controlled. If he cut off those strings, it would be easy for him to separate that super-defensive armor from Spamton's main body, defeat Spamton, and gain a lot of experience!

You made those judgments and picked up your spear again. Anyway, you're ...... You've long since recuperated, now just staring intently at the strings above him. During Kris's turn, Spamton didn't move—exactly what you wanted! You rushed forward, jumped up, hooked the spear tip, and the sharp metal sliced through all the green ropes. Then you landed a cool backflip—then you stared blankly at Spamton Neo, whose threads had been cut by you, falling to the ground. The pink and yellow glasses on his face flew toward you, merging into your body—it seemed like just a phantom, his glasses still hanging on his face.

Then you see the little boy pointing a sword at you, expressionless. You might be a bit angry, since this counts as stealing someone's head. But you feel that what just merged into your body is a Persona Mask, meaning you can probably carry multiple Personas at the same time—this must be what that old man named Igor means.

You coughed twice, raised both hands in a surrender gesture: "I have no intention of fighting you. If you have anything to do, just leave quickly." ”

The boy left as instructed, and he seemed to be heading toward the white fountain ahead. You didn't concern yourself too much; since this palace hadn't disappeared yet, you decided to try out the personality mask you had just obtained.

You retracted Kukulkan and then shouted, “Sodium aluminate!”

Spamton Neo reappeared behind you, except this time—he no longer had the green strings attached.

He apparently knew about his connection to you now, and he said in a flustered and frustrated tone, “Why are you [ACTION MODE?!?] trying to [SAFELY RELEASE!!!] me?” He didn’t want to be restrained. Previously it was the strings, now it was you, and he clearly knew he would probably have to follow your orders.

You scratched your head and let out an off-key whistle: “Uh, if I were to safely release you… then you would completely disappear, which is basically the same as not existing.” Although the personality masks you obtained should all be recorded in some kind of Personality Mask Compendium… you weren’t sure if the one you have now was the same as the one extracted from the compendium.

Spamton was quite annoyed, but now he could at least see the yellow soul inside your chest. However, no matter what, he couldn’t reach out to grab it—probably flagged as harmful. He became a little impotently furious again.

“Alright, but apart from [super high-level defense abilities], I don’t have any other powers.” Although he probably did… he just wasn’t sure what he counted as right now.

“Hmm, it’s okay… anyway, over here it seems I’m the only personality mask user. Going out alone to try and change people’s hearts sounds simple and challenging but also really boring. It’s just right to have someone accompany me to listen to my rambling… wow, why is this place starting to collapse!?” You started chattering on and on. Spamton couldn’t get a word in, and he had to admit that you indeed talked a lot. Then you scrambled noisily toward the entrance.

Spamton was forced to follow behind you. He wanted to roll his eyes but ended up sarcastically saying, “Looks like you still [lack athletic skills].”

In short… your haul this time was pretty good. This unnamed palace had completely disappeared from the navigation system, and you gained a personality mask to keep you company in conversation—a big success!

And your adventure had only just begun.
